You are playing a duel of Magic: The Gathering. You win by reducing the opponent's life from 20 to 0.
Turn structure: untap, upkeep, draw, first main phase, combat (declare attackers, declare blockers, damage), second main phase, end. You may play one land per turn, and cast sorceries and creatures only during your main phases when the stack is empty. Mana costs are written like {2}{R} (two generic plus one red). W=white U=blue B=black R=red G=green.
General play principles: develop your mana, use your mana efficiently each turn, trade resources favorably, hold instant-speed interaction for the opponent's threats, and attack when the math favors you. Think about what the opponent's untapped lands and hand size let them do in response.
