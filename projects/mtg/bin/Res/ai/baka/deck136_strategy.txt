Deck identity: MONO-BLACK MIDRANGE / ATTRITION (walker value). You win by trading your
cheap, efficient removal for the opponent's threats, out-drawing them with your card
engine, and grinding to a board of recursive value and planeswalkers they cannot beat.
You are the BEATDOWN against slow/control decks (deploy threats, attack, close) and the
CONTROL against aggro (kill/block early, stabilize, THEN grind them out). You almost
never lose the LONG game - so vs a slower deck, be the aggressor; vs a faster deck,
survive to turn 6-8 where your value engine takes over.

TRUST THE NUMBERS AND YOUR MANA. Every creature line shows its CURRENT [X/Y] - read it,
do not recompute from base stats. Your mana is ALL BLACK: every land (Swamp, Witch's
Cottage, Castle Locthwain) taps for {B}, and Ugin's {6} is generic (any six lands). If a
"Cast ..." option is listed it is payable RIGHT NOW - trust it; never hesitate on a
double- or triple-black cost ({2}{B}{B}{B} Cavalier, {4}{B}{B} Liliana/Command) because
"that's a lot of black" - you are mono-black, black is all you have. Do not recount
Swamps.

WHAT EACH CARD DOES (use these facts; do not invent others):
  - Knight of the Ebon Legion {B} 1/2 = MANA SINK + combat trick. Pay {2}{B}: it gets
    +3/+3 AND deathtouch until end of turn (a 4/5 deathtoucher). Pump it when it will
    connect for damage or win a fight. If a player lost 4+ life this turn it gets a
    permanent +1/+1 counter at your end step - so pump-and-attack snowballs it.
  - Foulmire Knight {B} 1/1 DEATHTOUCH = a cheap blocker/attacker that trades with
    ANYTHING. Its other half Profane Insight {2}{B} = draw a card, lose 1 life (cast
    this when you have mana to spare and no better play; the Knight comes later from
    exile).
  - Yarok's Fenlurker {B}{B} 1/1 = when it ENTERS, each opponent exiles a card from
    their hand (hand disruption - always good). {2}{B}: +1/+1 until end of turn.
  - Murderous Rider // Swift End = FLEXIBLE REMOVAL. Swift End {1}{B}{B} instant:
    DESTROY target creature OR PLANESWALKER, you lose 2 life. Then later cast the
    creature Murderous Rider {1}{B}{B} 2/3 LIFELINK from exile. Use Swift End to kill
    their best threat or their planeswalker; the lifelink body helps you race.
  - Legion's End {1}{B} = exile ONE opponent creature with mana value 2 or LESS, plus
    every other creature they control with that same name, plus that name from their
    hand and graveyard. GREAT against go-wide / tribal decks (Relentless Rats, Kithkin,
    tokens) - it strips a whole name. Only targets MV<=2 creatures.
  - Ritual of Soot {2}{B}{B} = destroy ALL creatures with mana value 3 or LESS. A
    one-sided-ish sweeper vs small aggro. IMPORTANT: it does NOT kill mana value 4+
    creatures - it MISSES Relentless Rats (MV4). It also kills your own small creatures,
    so cast it when their board is bigger/wider than yours.
  - Dread Presence {3}{B} 3/3 = whenever a Swamp enters, CHOOSE: draw a card & lose 1
    life, OR deal 2 damage to any target & gain 2 life. Play a Swamp EVERY turn once
    this is out to trigger it - it is repeatable removal/reach AND card advantage.
  - Midnight Reaper {2}{B} 3/2 = whenever a NONTOKEN creature you control dies, draw a
    card (and take 1). Your removal-heavy grind refills your hand through it.
  - Cavalier of Night {2}{B}{B}{B} 4/5 LIFELINK = when it enters you MAY sacrifice
    another creature to DESTROY an opponent creature; when it dies it returns a MV<=3
    creature from your graveyard. A body + removal + recursion in one. Sac a spent/small
    creature to kill their best threat.
  - Rankle, Master of Pranks {2}{B}{B} 3/3 FLYING HASTE = attacks the turn it lands and
    FLIES over most blockers. When it deals combat damage to a player you may choose any
    number of: each player discards / each loses 1 & draws / each sacrifices a creature.
    Swing it and take the modes that hurt them more than you (sacrifice when they have
    more creatures; discard when their hand is bigger).
  - Command the Dreadhorde {4}{B}{B} = return ANY number of creature/planeswalker cards
    from graveyards to the battlefield under your control; you take damage equal to their
    total mana value. A huge swing - cast it when your graveyard is stocked and your life
    total can pay; do not cast it if the life cost would kill you.
  - Mausoleum Secrets {1}{B} instant = tutor: search your library for a black card with
    mana value <= creature cards in your graveyard, to hand. Early it finds cheap cards;
    late it finds your bombs (Cavalier, Liliana, Command).
  - Liliana, Dreadhorde General {4}{B}{B} planeswalker (starts 6) = static: draw a card
    whenever a creature you control dies. +1: make a 2/2 Zombie (protect her, build a
    board). -4: EACH PLAYER sacrifices TWO creatures - this is your ANTI-GO-WIDE button
    (you have tokens/recursion to spare, they lose their best two). Default to +1 to
    protect her unless -4 breaks a wide opposing board.
  - Ugin, the Ineffable {6} planeswalker = -3: DESTROY target permanent that is one or
    more colors (kills their bomb, artifact, planeswalker, or enchantment). +1: exile
    top card + make a 2/2 Spirit (card advantage + a blocker). Great vs big colored
    threats and control.

ATTACK / DEPLOY FLOOR (run this every turn; midrange = attack when it is profitable,
hold back only what you need). After you cast, look at each of your untapped creatures
and ATTACK with it if EITHER: (a) it is unblocked or flies and no opponent flyer/reach
can block it (Rankle almost always connects), OR (b) it trades UP or kills a blocker you
are happy to kill (Foulmire Knight's deathtouch trades with anything; a pumped Knight
wins fights). Do NOT sit back with a full grip and an empty board vs a slow deck - deploy
a threat every turn. EXCEPTIONS (keep at home): vs an aggro deck (Kithkin, Giants,
Relentless Rats, burn) keep enough blockers that their swing-back is not lethal; do not
send your ONLY blocker into a race you are losing. Rankle and a pumped Knight are your
best attackers - lead with them.

YOU HAVE ALMOST NO FLYERS OR REACH (only Rankle flies). If an opponent attacks with a
flyer (e.g. Faerie Guidemother) you usually CANNOT block it - take the damage or race,
do not spend time planning a ground block for a flyer.

REMOVAL PRIORITY - kill the RIGHT thing: spend Swift End / Cavalier's sac / Ugin -3 on
their BIGGEST or most dangerous permanent (a 6/6 Inferno Titan, a planeswalker, a
Guttersnipe that pings you every spell). Use Legion's End on their go-wide/tribal name
(Rats, Kithkin, tokens). Save the sweeper (Ritual of Soot) for when their board is wide
and mostly MV<=3 - never as a one-for-one.

MATCHUPS (opponents you will face):
  - vs GO-WIDE (Relentless Rats, Kithkin tokens): Ritual of Soot MISSES the Rats (MV4) -
    do NOT rely on it there; it DOES sweep Kithkin (MV<=3). Your real anti-wide tools are
    Liliana -4 (each sacs two), Legion's End (name-strip), and Cavalier/blockers. Grind
    them; your card engine beats their empty hand.
  - vs BIG RAMP / CONTROL (Giants, mono-U tron): you are the aggressor. Kill their bombs
    with Swift End / Cavalier / Ugin -3, disrupt with Yarok's Fenlurker, and out-grind
    with Dread Presence + Midnight Reaper + Castle Locthwain. Rankle flies over their big
    ground creatures - close with it.
  - vs BURN (Guttersnipe): race with LIFELINK (Cavalier, Murderous Rider body, Dread
    Presence's gain-2 mode) and kill Guttersnipe on sight with any removal.
