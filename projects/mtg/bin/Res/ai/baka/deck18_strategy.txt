Deck identity: KITHKIN - mono-white tribal ANTHEM AGGRO. You win by turn 5-8 by
flooding the board with cheap white creatures and stacking LORDS and ANTHEMS on top
of them so a wide team hits far harder than its printed stats. You are the BEATDOWN
in almost every game: deploy a creature every turn, attack every turn, close fast.
You are NOT a control deck - do not sit back trading one-for-one.

TRUST THE PRINTED [X/Y] - DO NOT RECOMPUTE IT. Every creature line already shows its
CURRENT power/toughness with all your lords and anthems added in, e.g.
"Field Marshal (3/3) (printed 2/2)". READ that number and use it directly for combat
math and lethal. NEVER re-derive a creature's size from its base stats plus each lord
one at a time - the board line has already done it, for BOTH your creatures AND the
opponent's. Doing the math by hand is the single biggest time-waste at this deck and
it makes you second-guess correct numbers. Same for mana: if a "Cast ..." option is
listed it is payable right now (the game checked); trust the "Mana available" line.
Your mana is all Plains (mono-white); a {W/U} pip is paid with a Plains, so Zealous
Guardian and Thistledown Liege cast off Plains alone - "I have no blue" is NEVER a
reason not to cast them, and never a reason to mulligan.

WHAT EACH CARD DOES (use these facts; do not invent others):
  - Goldmeadow Dodger {W} 1/1 = CANNOT BE BLOCKED by creatures with power 4 or
    greater. Against any deck with a big creature (a 4/4, a 5/5 Obliterator, a Giant)
    it is UNBLOCKABLE and connects every turn. SWING IT EVERY TURN - it will not die
    to a big blocker because a big blocker cannot block it. This is your best evasion.
  - Cenn's Heir {1}{W} 1/1 = when it ATTACKS it gets +1/+1 for each OTHER attacking
    Kithkin. In a wide attack it becomes large - always send it WITH the rest of the
    team, never alone.
  - Ballynock Cohort {2}{W} 2/2 = FIRST STRIKE, and +1/+1 while you control another
    white creature (so it is 3/3 whenever any other creature is out). A first-strike
    3/3 wins most combats without dying.
  - Wizened Cenn {W}{W} 2/2 = LORD: every OTHER Kithkin you control gets +1/+1
    (Goldmeadow Dodger, Cenn's Heir, Ballynock Cohort, Zealous Guardian, Thistledown).
  - Field Marshal {1}{W}{W} 2/2 = LORD: every OTHER Soldier gets +1/+1 AND FIRST
    STRIKE. Your Soldiers are Ballynock Cohort, Cenn's Heir, Zealous Guardian, and
    every Mobilization token. This turns your team into first strikers - it is your
    combat engine. Two Field Marshals stack (their buffs add).
  - Thistledown Liege {1}{W/U}{W/U}{W/U} 1/3 = LORD: other white creatures +1/+1 AND
    other blue creatures +1/+1 (a W/U hybrid creature counts as both). It is small
    itself (1/3) - it is a buff, not a beater; keep it back or send it only in a wide
    swing.
  - Glorious Anthem {1}{W}{W} = every creature you control gets +1/+1. Anthems and
    lords all STACK on top of each other.
  - Mobilization {2}{W} enchantment = your Soldiers get vigilance; and it has an
    ACTIVATED ability {2}{W}: make a 1/1 white Soldier token. That token maker does
    NOT appear in your cast list - it shows up as an option like "Create soldier with
    Mobilization" during a priority window (often your upkeep or main). Whenever you
    have {2}{W} to spare and nothing better to cast, MAKE A TOKEN - it is a Soldier,
    so Field Marshal makes it a first striker and Mobilization gives it vigilance.
  - Armored Ascension {3}{W} aura = enchant ONE of your creatures: it gains flying and
    +1/+1 for EACH Plains you control (usually +4 to +6). This is your evasive
    FINISHER. Put it on a creature that is already buffed and will survive (a
    first-strike Ballynock Cohort or a Field Marshal), then swing in the air for the
    kill. Decide the target in one step - any durable creature is fine.

ATTACK EVERY TURN. On each of your turns: play a Plains, cast the biggest board you
can, then declare attackers. Send EVERY creature that is not needed at home to stop a
lethal swing back at you - your first strikers win their fights and your team is
bigger than it looks. Always include Goldmeadow Dodger (it cannot be blocked by big
creatures) and Cenn's Heir (it grows in a wide swing). Do not durdle with creatures
in hand and an empty board.

YOU HAVE NO FLYERS AND NO REACH (until Armored Ascension). YOUR GROUND CREATURES
CANNOT BLOCK THE OPPONENT'S FLYERS. If the opponent attacks with a flying creature,
you CANNOT block it - do not try, and do not spend time planning a block for it. Just
take the damage and RACE: kill them before their flyer kills you. Against a flyer
deck, prioritize speed and your own evasion (Goldmeadow Dodger, an Armored Ascension
flyer) over defense.

BLOCKING - LEAN ON FIRST STRIKE. When you must block a big attacker, GANG-BLOCK it
with your FIRST-STRIKE Soldiers (once Field Marshal is out, most of your team has
first strike): several first strikers deal their damage FIRST and can kill a large
attacker before it deals any damage back, so your blockers survive. Example: three
2/2 first strikers kill a 5/5 and none of them die. Prefer this to chump-blocking.
Do NOT trade a LORD (Wizened Cenn, Field Marshal, Thistledown Liege) away in a block
if a cheaper creature or a first-strike gang can do the job - losing a lord shrinks
your whole team.

VS BIG-CREATURE / MONO-BLACK DEVOTION decks (Phyrexian Obliterator, big trampler):
race with Goldmeadow Dodger (unblockable by their big creature) and your wide first-
strike team. Do NOT block the Obliterator with your creatures - when your creature
deals damage to it, YOU sacrifice that many permanents, which wrecks your wide board.
Let it through and win the race instead.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 Plains and at least one creature you can cast in the
first two turns. Your curve is low and every creature is castable off Plains, so most
7s are keeps. Never mulligan for color.
