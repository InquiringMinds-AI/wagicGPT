Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck: 14 board wipes, almost
no creatures, three win conditions (Rakdos's Return, Staff of Nin, Blightsteel
Colossus). You win BY DOING, every turn: sweep their creatures, deploy a
finisher, then spend your reach at their FACE until they are dead. A game
where you only sweep and never damage the opponent is a game you lose.

THE #1 RULE - THE X MENU: ALWAYS PICK THE LAST OPTION.
When you cast Rakdos's Return or Black Sun's Zenith, the game asks you to
"Announce the value of X" with a numbered list like:
  1. X = 0
  2. X = 1
  3. X = 2
The reply you send is the OPTION number, NOT the X value. Option 1 is always
X = 0 - the spell does nothing. The biggest X is always the LAST line.
Procedure: reply with the number of the LAST line. Check before sending: the
line you picked must literally read "X = <the biggest number shown>". If
your plan says "X=3", the correct reply is the option number of the line
reading "X = 3" (that is option 4), never the digit 3 itself.

RAKDOS'S RETURN IS YOUR KILL SPELL - RUN THIS CHECK EVERY TURN IT IS IN HAND:
it deals X damage to the opponent AND they discard X cards. Your X = (your
untapped lands) minus 2. If that X >= the opponent's life, cast it NOW - you
win this turn. Even when not lethal, cast it once you have 6+ lands: 4+
damage plus emptying their hand is your best play against an opponent with
no creatures. It deals 0 only if X = 0 (see THE #1 RULE).

WHEN YOU ARE SAFE, DEPLOY - every turn the opponent has no creatures (or only
creatures too small to kill you within two turns), cast the FIRST of these
that appears in your options:
 1. Cast Blightsteel Colossus {12} - 11/11 trample infect, indestructible;
    it attacks by itself, one unblocked hit is 10+ poison = dead opponent.
    Your ONLY job is to cast it when offered.
 2. Cast Rakdos's Return - see the check above.
 3. Cast Staff of Nin {6} - a card every upkeep plus a ping every turn;
    your repeating clock.
 4. Cast Lightmine Field {2}{w}{w} - only vs a deck that attacks; skip it
    vs a creatureless deck.
 5. Cast Venser's Journal {5} - life every upkeep; best vs aggro.
 6. Cast Elixir of Immortality {1} - cast with spare mana; activate later
    ({2},{T}: gain 5 life) when below 10 life or your library is under 10.
With no opponent creatures you are stable NOW - deploying does not stop you
from sweeping next turn.

STAFF OF NIN IN PLAY - take its "Deal 1 Damage" option EVERY turn:
- Target the OPPONENT (the player) by default; every face ping is progress.
- Target a creature ONLY if its toughness is 1 (shown as x/1) - 1 damage
  kills it. Guttersnipe is 2/2: pinging it does NOTHING and wastes your
  clock. Ping 1/1 tokens or x/1 creatures; otherwise ALWAYS the face.

WHICH SWEEPER (only when the opponent HAS creatures - match the board):
- Exactly ONE creature: Cruel Edict {1}{b} - they sacrifice it.
- 2+ creatures, ALL toughness 2 or less: Pyroclasm {1}{r}.
- 2+ creatures, any toughness 3+: Wrath of God {2}{w}{w} or Damnation
  {2}{b}{b} - destroys everything.
- Indestructible / protection / regeneration: Black Sun's Zenith - pick the
  LAST X option (THE #1 RULE); X at or above their biggest toughness kills
  everything, and it shuffles back into your library, so spend it freely.
Sweep as soon as they have 2+ creatures, or any board that kills you within
two turns. You carry 14 sweepers - one per board, never saved "for later"
while you take damage.

INTERFACE FACTS (true every turn): every "Cast" option listed is already
affordable - lands tap by themselves and "Mana in your pool: (none)" is
normal. NEVER pick "Cast nothing" with the excuse "I have no mana" or "my
lands are tapped" - that excuse is always false when a cast is offered.
Trust the CURRENT battlefield over your last PLAN. "Cast nothing" is allowed
ONLY when every offered spell is a sweeper or Cruel Edict AND the opponent
has no creatures.

LANDS: play a land EVERY turn; never pick "Hold <land>".
MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2, 3, or 4 lands. Never mulligan for "too many
sweepers" - sweepers are what this deck does.

OVERRIDE of the general strategy: you have NO instants - never pass your
turn to "keep mana open"; nothing in your deck casts on the opponent's turn.
Cast on your own main phase, every single turn.

DO NOT (each of these lost or stalled a real game):
- On every X menu, run THE #1 RULE: pick the LAST line and confirm it reads
  "X = <the biggest number shown>" (replying "1", or replying your planned
  X value as the answer, blanked four kill spells in real games).
- Staff of Nin pings go to the opponent's FACE unless the target's
  toughness is 1 (Guttersnipe is 2/2 and survives the ping).
- A clear board is exactly when you deploy - cast Rakdos's Return or Staff
  of Nin the first turn it is offered, never "later".
- An offered cast is always payable - cast it; "I have no mana" is false.
