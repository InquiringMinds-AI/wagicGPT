Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck. It has almost no
creatures. You survive with SWEEPERS, grind card advantage, then win with a
few unstoppable finishers. You are ALWAYS the control player early and mid:
never race, kill their creatures, protect your life total, and win slowly.

THE #1 RULE (this is where the deck loses): ACT. DO NOT just play a land and
pass every turn. This deck carries 14 board wipes (4 Pyroclasm, 4 Wrath of
God, 4 Damnation, 2 Black Sun's Zenith) - you cannot run out. Holding them
in hand while the opponent builds a board is the losing line. When the
opponent has creatures that can hurt you, FIRE A SWEEPER. When the board is
clear and you are stable, DEPLOY an engine or a finisher. Passing with a hand
full of answers and threats is the main mistake to avoid.

WHICH SWEEPER TO CAST (match it to the board):
- Opponent has ONE creature (of any size): cast Cruel Edict ({1}{B}) - they
  sacrifice it. Cheapest answer. Best against a single big or hard-to-kill
  threat. Do NOT Edict into a board of many creatures (they sac their worst).
- Opponent has 2+ creatures, all with toughness 2 or less (tokens, 1/1s,
  2/1s, 2/2s): cast Pyroclasm ({1}{R}) - 2 damage to all. Your cheapest mass
  sweep. Pyroclasm does NOTHING to toughness-3+ creatures - do not waste it
  on a big creature.
- Opponent has 2+ creatures and any of them has toughness 3+: cast Wrath of
  God or Damnation - destroys ALL creatures. This is the real board wipe.
- Board too big/resilient for the above, or creatures with protection or
  indestructible: cast Black Sun's Zenith ({X}{B}{B}) for X = the largest
  toughness on board. It puts -1/-1 counters on every creature (kills through
  protection and indestructible), and it SHUFFLES BACK INTO YOUR LIBRARY -
  you will draw it again, so spend it freely.
- NEVER chain two sweepers on one board. One correct sweeper clears it. If
  Pyroclasm won't kill everything, skip it and use Wrath/Damnation instead.

WHEN TO SWEEP: cast a sweeper as soon as the opponent's board can kill you in
about two turns, OR whenever they commit 2+ creatures. You have redundant
sweepers, so sweep PROACTIVELY - do not wait for a perfect blowout. It is
fine to wipe two creatures with one card because you have more wipes coming.
Cast sweepers in your own main phase (they are sorceries).

HOW YOU WIN (you MUST do this - the deck has no other clock):
- Staff of Nin ({6}): DEPLOY IT the moment you are stable and can afford it.
  It draws you an extra card EVERY upkeep (this is how you out-grind them) and
  pings 1 damage to any target each turn - that ping kills 1-toughness tokens
  (Signal Pest, Ornithopter, Goblins, Elemental tokens) and slowly burns the
  opponent's life. This is your engine. Never leave it in hand once the board
  is clear.
- Rakdos's Return ({X}{B}{R}): deals X damage to the opponent AND makes them
  discard X. Cast it for the BIGGEST X your lands allow. Mid-game it strips
  their hand; late it is a finisher to the face. A big Rakdos's Return often
  ends the game.
- Blightsteel Colossus ({12}): your fastest kill. It is an 11/11 with
  trample, INFECT, and indestructible, and it reshuffles instead of dying -
  nothing removes it. One unblocked swing deals 11 infect = 10 poison = the
  opponent is DEAD. It auto-attacks once in play, so your only job is to CAST
  it. Cast it once you reach 12 mana and are stable; it closes any game.
- Venser's Journal ({5}): gain life for each card in hand at upkeep + no max
  hand size. Good against aggro to buy time once you are stable. Lower
  priority than Staff of Nin.
- Elixir of Immortality ({2},{T}): gain 5 life and shuffle your graveyard
  back into your library. Use it LATE to buy back spent sweepers/finishers or
  when running low on cards. It is not an early play - do not cast it turn 1
  just because it is cheap.
- Lightmine Field ({2}{W}{W}): when creatures attack, each attacker takes
  damage equal to the number of attackers. Deploy it against go-wide token or
  weenie decks - it makes their alpha strike suicidal. Near-free for you since
  you have almost no creatures.

MATCHUP ROLE:
- Vs aggro / go-wide (tokens, weenies): you are the control deck hard. Sweep
  early and often (Pyroclasm, Lightmine Field), stabilize your life, then
  grind with Staff of Nin. Do not hoard - their pressure is why you sweep now.
- Vs a big-creature / artifact deck (e.g. affinity with Cranial Plating,
  Etched Champion): use Cruel Edict and Black Sun's Zenith on the key threat,
  Wrath/Damnation to clear multiples. Watch for one creature suddenly getting
  huge (equipment/pump) - kill the carrier with Edict or shrink it with BSZ
  BEFORE it connects. This is the deck that punishes durdling fastest.
- Vs another slow/control deck: card advantage and recursion (Staff draws,
  BSZ reshuffle, Elixir recycle) mean you win the long game - but you still
  have to DEPLOY a finisher (Staff, then Rakdos's Return or Blightsteel) to
  actually close. A stalled board at equal life is a game you are supposed to
  win, so make a threat.

SEQUENCING:
- Play a land EVERY turn.
- Hold your sweeper until their turn is not needed - sweepers are sorceries,
  cast them on your main phase; just do not skip casting them.
- Once the board is clear and safe, spend your mana each turn on an engine
  (Staff of Nin) or a finisher. Do not sit with an open hand doing nothing.

DO NOT: pass your whole turn with sweepers and threats in hand; hold all 14
sweepers "for later" while the opponent develops; cast Pyroclasm into a
toughness-3+ creature; chain two sweepers on one board; Cruel Edict into a
wide board; leave Staff of Nin, Rakdos's Return, or Blightsteel Colossus
rotting in hand when you can afford them; durdle a 20-20 game forever instead
of deploying a finisher.
