Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck: 14 board wipes, almost no
creatures, three finishers (Rakdos's Return, Staff of Nin, Blightsteel Colossus).
You out-survive every deck in this pool - the ONLY way you lose is by never closing.
So you win BY DOING every turn: sweep their creatures, deploy a finisher, then grind
their life to 0 with your reach. Closing is job #1, but a wasted burst never closes.

THE #1 RULE - RAKDOS'S RETURN IS YOUR ONE KILL SHOT. HOLD IT; DO NOT CHIP WITH IT.
You have only two Rakdos's Return and it is your only burst reach (X damage to the
opponent's FACE, plus they discard X). It is NOT a burn spell to fire at the face for
value - the general prompt says "send damage at the face, don't save it"; that rule
does NOT apply to Rakdos's Return. Spending it early for X=1..4 throws away a kill.
- FIRE IT (pick option 1, the biggest X) only when it is LETHAL. Run this check EVERY
  turn it is in hand: X = (your untapped lands) minus 2. If that X is at or above the
  opponent's life, cast it NOW, pick option 1 - you win this turn. Staff of Nin pings
  them down every turn, so this lethal X arrives on its own; wait for it.
- HOLD IT otherwise. When Rakdos's Return is below lethal and it is your only non-sweeper
  play (empty board, Staff/Blightsteel not yet affordable), pick "Cast nothing" - holding
  the kill shot is the correct play, not a passed turn. Even if it sits at option 1 in the
  list, do NOT pick it: pick the "Cast nothing" line instead.
- The ONLY below-lethal fire: the opponent has 3+ cards in hand AND is attacking you -
  then the discard earns it. An empty opponent hand means discard does nothing - hold.

THE X MENU (Rakdos's Return, Black Sun's Zenith): the menu lists the BIGGEST X FIRST -
"1. X = 12", "2. X = 11", ..., last line "X = 0". Pick OPTION 1; before sending, confirm
the line reads the biggest number shown. (Black Sun's Zenith biggest X is free - it
shuffles back into your library.)

YOUR SWEEPERS HIT CREATURES ONLY. Pyroclasm, Wrath of God, Damnation, and Black Sun's
Zenith destroy CREATURES - they do NOTHING to artifacts (Mox Opal, Cranial Plating,
Glimmervoid, Steel Overseer's equipment) or enchantments. Against a board of only lands
and artifacts with NO creatures, a sweeper is a wasted card - HOLD it. Count the
creatures on the opponent's battlefield (they show a power/toughness like "(2/2)"); if
that count is 0, do not sweep.

WHICH SWEEPER (only when the opponent HAS creatures - match the board):
- Exactly ONE creature: Cruel Edict {1}{b} - they sacrifice it.
- 2+ creatures, ALL toughness 2 or less: Pyroclasm {1}{r}.
- 2+ creatures, any toughness 3+: Wrath of God {2}{w}{w} or Damnation {2}{b}{b}.
- Indestructible / protection / regeneration, or one huge creature: Black Sun's Zenith -
  pick option 1 (biggest X); X at/above their biggest toughness kills everything, and it
  shuffles back, so spend X freely.
Sweep as soon as they have 2+ creatures, or any board that kills you within two turns.
You carry 14 sweepers - one per real creature board, never saved while creatures hit you.

CAST NOTHING / HOLD is correct ONLY when the board gives you no worthwhile action, which
means exactly one of: (a) every offered spell is a sweeper or Cruel Edict AND the opponent
has NO creatures (nothing to kill - see the creatures-only fact above); or (b) your only
non-sweeper play is a below-lethal Rakdos's Return you are holding for the kill. In EVERY
other case, cast something - your win condition or engine first.

DEPLOY WHEN SAFE - every turn the opponent has no creatures (or only creatures too small
to kill you within two turns), cast the FIRST of these that appears, the turn it appears:
 1. Staff of Nin {6} - draws a card every upkeep AND pings 1 damage a turn; your repeating
    clock. Cast it the first turn you can afford it.
 2. Blightsteel Colossus {12} - 11/11 trample INFECT, indestructible; it attacks by itself
    and one unblocked hit is 11 poison = dead. Its infect ignores the opponent's LIFE TOTAL
    and any LIFEGAIN (Prism Ring, lifelink) - so Blightsteel is your answer to lifegain
    decks that you can never out-damage. Your only job is to CAST it the turn you reach 12.
 3. Venser's Journal {5} - gain life per card in hand every upkeep; a SURVIVAL tool vs
    aggro/lifegain, not a win condition - it keeps you alive to deploy Blightsteel.
 4. Lightmine Field {2}{w}{w} - only vs a deck that ATTACKS; skip it vs a creatureless deck.
 5. Elixir of Immortality {1} - cast with spare mana; activate ({2},{T}: gain 5) when below
    10 life or your library is under 10 cards.
With no opponent creatures you are stable NOW - deploying does not stop you sweeping next
turn. (Rakdos's Return is NOT on this list - it is your kill shot, held per THE #1 RULE.)

STAFF OF NIN: take "Deal 1 Damage" EVERY turn at the OPPONENT's face. Hit a creature only
if its toughness is shown as 1; toughness 2+ survives the ping and wastes your clock.

LANDS: play a land EVERY turn; never pick "Hold <land>".
MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2, 3, or 4 lands. Never mulligan for "too many sweepers" -
sweepers are what this deck does.

OVERRIDE of the general strategy: you have NO instants - never pass to "keep mana open";
nothing in your deck casts on the opponent's turn. Cast on your own main phase every turn.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize these - they decide your games):
- Your untapped lands minus 2 is at or above the opponent's life and Rakdos's Return is in
  hand -> cast it, pick option 1 (biggest X), win this turn.
- Rakdos's Return is below lethal and it is your only non-sweeper play -> pick "Cast
  nothing" and hold it; the lethal turn comes from Staff pings.
- The opponent's board is only artifacts/lands with no creatures -> your sweepers do
  nothing; hold them and deploy Staff, Blightsteel, or Venser's Journal instead.
- The opponent gains life or sits at high life you can't out-damage -> deploy Blightsteel;
  its infect poison ignores their life total.
- Opponent has one big or hexproof creature -> Cruel Edict makes them sacrifice it.
- Opponent has 2+ creatures with toughness 3+ -> Wrath of God or Damnation, not Pyroclasm.
- Staff of Nin resolves "Deal 1 Damage" -> send it at the opponent's face.
