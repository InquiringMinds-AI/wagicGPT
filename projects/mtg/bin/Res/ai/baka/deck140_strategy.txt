Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck: 14 board wipes, almost no
creatures, three finishers (Rakdos's Return, Staff of Nin, Blightsteel Colossus).
You out-survive every deck in this pool - the ONLY way you lose is by never closing,
by letting creatures sit and grow while you HOLD a sweeper you could have cast, or by
throwing away your own sweepers when an opponent forces you to discard. You win BY DOING
every turn: kill their creatures on sight, deploy a finisher, then grind their face to 0.

SWEEP TRIGGER - THIS IS THE RULE THAT DECIDES YOUR GAMES.
The opponent's battlefield line prints its creature count for you, like
"Opponent battlefield (creatures: 2)". That number is C - you do not have to count.
- If C is 1 or more, and (your life is 16 or LOWER - 16 itself counts, so at exactly 16
  you sweep - OR they have attacked you on any recent turn), CAST A SWEEPER THIS TURN.
  Do not wait. Pick the first sweeper you can PAY for now (see WHICH SWEEPER). Casting
  nothing here is how you lose.
- If C is 0 (only lands/artifacts/enchantments), holding the sweeper is correct -
  deploy a finisher instead (see DEPLOY WHEN SAFE).
The printed C already counts everything that matters, so do not talk yourself out of a
sweep. Two reminders the count settles for you:
- A creature marked "[tapped - cannot attack or block this turn]" is STILL in C. Tapped is
  never a reason to wait, and neither is "it isn't attacking THIS turn" - it hit you last
  turn and swings again next turn; kill it now on your main phase.
- ARTIFACTS do not lower C and do not shield a creature. If C is 1+, your sweeper kills
  every creature - cast it.
- SHROUD / "can't be targeted" does NOT stop a sweeper. Pyroclasm, Wrath of God,
  Damnation, and Black Sun's Zenith hit ALL creatures without targeting - shroud does
  nothing to them. Cruel Edict also works (it makes the PLAYER sacrifice, it does not
  target the creature). Shroud only stops single-target spells you do not run. This is the
  one thing the creature count does NOT tell you - remember it.

WHICH SWEEPER - the cast menu only lists sweepers you can PAY FOR right now, so cast the
FIRST one that fits the board (do not pass because your one "favorite" sweeper is missing
from the list; another one is almost always offered):
- Exactly ONE creature: Cruel Edict {1}{b} (black) - they sacrifice it.
- 2+ creatures, ALL toughness 2 or less: Pyroclasm {1}{r} (needs RED).
- 2+ creatures, any toughness 3+: Wrath of God {2}{w}{w} (white) or Damnation {2}{b}{b} (black).
- Indestructible / regeneration / one huge creature: Black Sun's Zenith {b}{b}{x} (black) -
  pick option 1 (biggest X); X at/above their biggest toughness kills everything, and it
  shuffles back, so spend X freely. But only cast Black Sun's Zenith when the biggest X you
  can AFFORD is at least the toughness you need dead - a smaller X leaves the creature alive
  and wastes the whole turn; if X would fall short AND a Wrath or Damnation is offered, cast
  that instead (they kill any toughness). If no other sweeper is offered, cast the biggest
  Black Sun's Zenith you can - partial removal still beats casting nothing.
If you have no RED, Pyroclasm is out - use Wrath (white), Damnation (black), or Black
Sun's Zenith (black) instead. You carry 14 sweepers - one per real creature board.
Your two black sweepers (Damnation, Black Sun's Zenith) both need TWO black sources; if
the cast menu does not offer them, you are short a black source, not out of sweepers -
play a black-producing land and cast Wrath or Cruel Edict meanwhile.

THE #1 RULE - RAKDOS'S RETURN IS YOUR ONE KILL SHOT. HOLD IT; DO NOT CHIP WITH IT.
You have only two Rakdos's Return and it is your only burst reach (X damage to the
opponent's FACE, plus they discard X). The general prompt says "send burn at the face" -
that does NOT apply to Rakdos's Return.
- FIRE IT (option 1, biggest X) only when it is LETHAL. EVERY turn it is in hand:
  X = (your untapped lands) minus 2; if X is at or above the opponent's life, cast it
  NOW, pick option 1 - you win this turn. Staff of Nin pings them down, so this arrives.
  Trust the X MENU's offered values for lethal math - do not assume you can afford a
  bigger X than the menu lists (the menu caps X at what you can actually pay).
- Otherwise HOLD it. A below-lethal Rakdos's Return is a legal "Cast nothing" - holding
  the kill shot is correct, not a passed turn. Do not fire it for a small X even if it
  sits at option 1.
- The ONLY below-lethal fire: the opponent has 3+ cards in hand AND is attacking you (or
  is a burn deck about to kill you and you are already losing) - OR the game is grinding
  to the turn cap, you have no other play, and firing the biggest X now wins the life
  race. Then the damage (and any discard) earns it.

THE X MENU (Rakdos's Return, Black Sun's Zenith): the menu lists the BIGGEST X FIRST and
tells you every listed value is affordable. Pick OPTION 1. (Black Sun's Zenith's biggest X
is free - it shuffles back into your library.) Answer with the option NUMBER; the mana was
already committed when you chose to cast, so do not re-argue whether you can pay.

FORCED TO DISCARD OR SACRIFICE: when a prompt headed "FORCED discard/sacrifice OF YOUR OWN
CARD" makes you pick one of your OWN cards to LOSE, the engine already tells you to give up
your least valuable - follow it. For THIS deck the order is: a LAND first (you flood
lands); then your most redundant sweeper (Pyroclasm before Wrath / Damnation / Black Sun's
Zenith); NEVER a finisher (Staff of Nin, Blightsteel Colossus, Rakdos's Return) while any
win path is left, and never your last board wipe while a real creature board is live.

DEPLOY WHEN SAFE - every turn the opponent has C = 0 creatures (or only creatures too
small to kill you within two turns), cast the FIRST of these that appears, that turn:
 1. Staff of Nin {6} - draws a card AND pings 1 every upkeep; your repeating clock.
    Cast it the first turn you can afford it.
 2. Blightsteel Colossus {12} - 11/11 trample INFECT, indestructible; attacks by itself,
    one unblocked hit = 11 poison = dead. Its infect ignores the opponent's LIFE TOTAL
    and any LIFEGAIN (Prism Ring, lifelink) - so Blightsteel is your ONLY answer to
    lifegain/high-life decks you can never out-damage. Cast it the turn you reach 12 mana.
 3. Lightmine Field {2}{w}{w} - vs any deck that ATTACKS YOU, deploy this on sight: it
    damages EVERY attacking creature each combat, punishing go-wide aggro your one-shot
    sweepers cannot keep up with. Skip it vs a creatureless deck, and do not deploy a
    second copy while one is already on the battlefield.
 4. Venser's Journal {5} - gain life per card in hand each upkeep; a SURVIVAL tool vs
    aggro/lifegain to keep you alive to deploy Blightsteel, NOT a win condition.
 5. Elixir of Immortality {1} - cast with spare mana; activate ({2},{T}: gain 5) below
    10 life or when your library is under 10 cards.
(Rakdos's Return is NOT on this list - it is your kill shot, held per THE #1 RULE.)

STAFF OF NIN: take "Deal 1 Damage" EVERY turn at the OPPONENT's FACE. Hit a creature only
if its toughness is shown as 1; toughness 2+ survives the ping and wastes your clock.

LANDS: play a land EVERY turn; never pick "Hold <land>".
MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2, 3, or 4 lands. Never mulligan for "too many sweepers" -
sweepers are what this deck does.

OVERRIDE of the general strategy: you have NO instants - never pass to "keep mana open";
nothing casts on the opponent's turn. Cast on your own main phase every turn.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize these - they decide your games):
- Opponent shows "(creatures: 1)" or more and you are at 16 or LOWER (16 counts) or were
  attacked recently -> cast a payable sweeper THIS turn; tapped/artifacts/shroud are NOT
  reasons to wait, and "it isn't attacking this turn" is not a reason to wait.
- Opponent board is "(creatures: 0)" -> hold sweepers; deploy Staff, Blightsteel,
  Venser's Journal, or Lightmine Field.
- A deck is ATTACKING you every turn -> deploy Lightmine Field; sweep their creatures;
  it is fine to keep sweeping one board after another.
- The opponent gains life or sits at high life you can't out-damage -> deploy Blightsteel;
  its infect poison ignores their life total.
- A "FORCED discard/sacrifice OF YOUR OWN CARD" prompt -> pitch a land, else your most
  redundant sweeper; never a finisher, never your last wipe for a live board.
- Your untapped lands minus 2 is at or above the opponent's life and Rakdos's Return is
  in hand -> cast it, pick option 1 (biggest X), win this turn.
- Rakdos's Return is below lethal and your only other play is a sweeper on an empty
  board -> pick "Cast nothing" and hold both; the lethal turn comes from Staff pings.
- Staff of Nin resolves "Deal 1 Damage" -> send it at the opponent's face.
