Deck identity: "Etched Affinity" is a fast artifact-aggro deck. Your cards cost
0-3 mana. Dump your whole hand onto the battlefield in the first few turns,
equip Cranial Plating ONCE, attack every turn, and finish with Galvanic Blast.
Kill by turn 5-6. A turn where you cast nothing is a lost turn. You are the
beatdown by default; against another fast attack deck, see THE RACE below.

#1 RULE - EQUIP CRANIAL PLATING EXACTLY ONCE, THEN LEAVE THE EQUIP LINE ALONE.
The game keeps offering "Equip with Cranial Plating" every turn even after the
Plating is attached. Taking it again is paying {1} for nothing. Run this check
BEFORE any Equip line:
1. Read the target creature's stats on YOUR battlefield. Two power numbers -
   like "Etched Champion (6/2) (printed 2/2)" - mean a bonus is ALREADY on it:
   the Plating is attached, the equip is DONE. Spend that mana on a creature
   or a Blast instead, then attack.
2. One power number - like "(2/2)" or "(0/1)" - means no Plating on it. Equip
   it now if it is your best carrier.
3. Take at most ONE Equip in a whole turn. After one Equip, every further
   Equip line this turn is a re-offer of work already done - your remaining
   mana belongs to creatures and Blasts.
Equip again in a later turn only when your equipped creature DIED or a strictly
better carrier arrived. The "attach with Cranial Plating [cost: {b}{b}]" line
is a black-mana copy of the same thing - the {1} Equip is always the one to take.

CASTING: every "Cast X" in your options is fully payable right now - the game
taps your lands for you, and "Mana in your pool: (none)" is normal. The thought
"I have no mana for this" is always false when the cast is listed. Memnite and
Ornithopter cost {0} - cast them the moment you see them, always. Cast a
creature every turn one is listed; it is fine to tap out.

THE ENGINE - CRANIAL PLATING gives its creature +1/+0 for EACH artifact you
control (your artifact count is shown on the board - usually +4 or more).
1. CAST Cranial Plating the first turn it appears - even if you cannot equip
   the same turn. One copy on the battlefield is enough; cast a creature
   before a 2nd Plating when you can only afford one of them.
2. EQUIP it with your first spare {1} - same turn if you can, otherwise the
   very next turn. An unattached Plating adds nothing but its artifact count.
3. Then run rule #1: one equip, done, attack.
Carrier order: 1) Etched Champion (with 3+ artifacts it has protection from
all colors - colored creatures cannot block or kill it), 2) Vault Skirge or
Ornithopter (fliers), 3) Signal Pest, 4) Memnite.

THE RACE - when the opponent is also fast aggro (red decks with Rakdos
Cackler, Ash Zealot, Legion Loyalist, Goblin tokens, Hellrider), win by going
WIDER, not taller: cast EVERY creature in your hand before spending mana on
Equip lines - two bodies beat one +4 bonus; they add damage AND can block.
Keep attacking, but when their attack on you would drop you to 8 life or
less, block with every creature you do not need for your own lethal next
turn - chump-blocking to stay alive wins the race.

ATTACK EVERY TURN with every creature - including the turn after your best
attacker died: send the rest and rebuild. Signal Pest always attacks even at
0 power (battle cry gives every OTHER attacker +1/+0, and only fliers/reach
can block it). Etched Champion with 3+ artifacts walks past colored blockers
- always send it. One exception: if the opponent controls Lightmine Field
(it damages each attacker by the number of attackers), attack with ONLY
Etched Champion or your single equipped creature.

LETHAL CHECK - run it every turn:
damage = total power of your attackers (+1 each if Signal Pest attacks too)
       + 4 per Galvanic Blast in hand (metalcraft: 3+ artifacts = 4 damage).
If damage >= opponent's life: attack with everyone and fire every Blast at
the opponent's face. When the opponent is at 8 or less, Blasts always go to
the face. Earlier, a Blast may kill a blocker stopping your equipped attacker.

OTHER KEY CARDS:
- Steel Overseer: take "Counter with Steel Overseer [cost: Tap]" in Main
  Phase 1 EVERY turn it is untapped - permanent +1/+1 counters on ALL your
  artifact creatures beat one 1/1 attack. Skip the tap only when Overseer
  itself must attack for lethal THIS turn.
- Master of Etherium: power = your artifact count, and it pumps your other
  artifact creatures +1/+1. Cast it on sight.
- Thoughtcast: affinity - it usually costs just {U}. Cast it whenever you
  have 2 or fewer other cards worth casting - refuel, do not hoard it.
- Mox Opal / Darksteel Citadel / Glimmervoid: free mana and lands; Mox Opal
  and Citadel also count as artifacts - you hit metalcraft (3+ artifacts)
  almost immediately, assume it is ON. A "Cast Card Normally with Mox Opal"
  or "Play Land with Mox Opal" line just makes mana with Mox Opal - your
  creatures and spells come first.

MULLIGAN: keep 2+ lands and 2+ creatures (Mox Opal counts as a land here).
Ship hands with 0-1 land or no creatures.

DO NOT (all seen in real games with this deck):
- Do NOT take an Equip line targeting a creature that already shows two power
  numbers like "(6/2) (printed 2/2)" - that Plating is attached; the mana
  belongs to creatures and Blasts.
- Do NOT spend turns on one creature + equip while holding castable creatures
  - the games this deck lost, it attacked with ONE creature while the
  opponent built five.
- Do NOT stop declaring attackers because your equipped creature died - the
  game this deck timed out, it stopped attacking on turn 4 with creatures in
  play.
