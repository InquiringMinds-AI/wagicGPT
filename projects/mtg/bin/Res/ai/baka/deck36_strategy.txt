MASTER OF ETHER - UB ARTIFACT AGGRO

You win by turn 8-11 by putting cheap artifacts onto the battlefield EVERY turn and swinging with
bodies that grow as the count grows - small on paper, large in play. Every turn is the same three
actions: play a land, deploy every artifact you can, attack with everything the list offers. You are
the beatdown in every matchup. Answer fast: this deck loses games to the clock, not to bad plays.

=== SACRIFICING YOUR OWN CARDS - ANSWER THIS IN ONE LINE, EVERY TIME ===
Some opponents force you to sacrifice. The prompt says "SACRIFICE ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS" and "each
option is a card YOU will LOSE". It also says "Choose card 3 of exactly 6" - the 6 is how many
SACRIFICES the effect demands, not how long the list is. You will be asked again for each one. The
list is not truncated and the prompt is not broken.
Answer by this order, top first, and stop reading as soon as you find one:
1. A Pest token.
2. A TAPPED basic Island or Swamp.
3. Any other tapped land that is not Tolarian Academy.
4. A Mox Jet or Mox Sapphire.
5. An untapped basic Island or Swamp.
6. A Nuisance Engine, then Steel Wall.
KEEP, in this order and to the very last: any creature that can attack or block, Master of Etherium,
Tolarian Academy, Academy Ruins, Akroma's Memorial.
Every one of these is a one-line answer. Give the number and the name and go.

=== THE ARTIFACT COUNT IS YOUR ENGINE - READ IT, DO NOT COUNT IT ===
The prompt prints "Artifacts in play: you N | opponent N". That N is the real number. Never recount
the battlefield list and never argue with N - if your count and N disagree, N is right and your count
is wrong, every time. In particular, Tolarian Academy and Academy Ruins appear on the battlefield line
with no tag at all and they are NOT artifacts; Island and Swamp are not either. The five artifact
lands - Ancient Den, Great Furnace, Seat of the Synod, Tree of Tales, Vault of Whispers - ARE
artifacts, so playing one is a free +1.
Vedalken Archmage {2}{U}{U} is the one nonland exception in your deck - it is NOT an artifact, so it
does not raise N and Master does not pump it. It draws a card each time you CAST an artifact spell,
so your free Moxes and Ornithopters replace themselves. Playing an artifact LAND is not casting a
spell and draws nothing.

=== SEQUENCING INSIDE ONE TURN - THIS IS WORTH DAMAGE ===
1. Play your land first, and take an ARTIFACT land over Island or Swamp when both are offered - it
   is a mana source AND a count.
2. Then cast artifacts cheapest first until the list has none left. Mox Jet, Mox Sapphire and
   Ornithopter cost {0} - their lines say so, "Cast Ornithopter {0} (0/2)" - so they are always
   first and always worth taking, even with zero mana and nothing else going on. Then Steel Wall
   {1}, Glaze Fiend {1}{B}, Silver Myr {2}, then the {3}s.
3. THEN attack - everything from step 2 has already grown your attackers.
Deploy in Main Phase 1, before combat: Glaze Fiend's bonus lasts only until end of turn, so an
artifact cast in Main Phase 2 grew nothing.
{1}, {2} and {3} are generic and ANY mana pays them - the {W} of Ancient Den and the {R} of Great
Furnace each pay the {2} of Silver Myr as well as a Swamp does. A printed "Cast Silver Myr {2}"
option is one the game has already checked you can afford.

=== MASTER OF ETHERIUM - YOUR BEST CARD, AND ITS NUMBER IS ONE TOO SMALL ===
"Cast Master of Etherium {2}{u} (N/N)" prints your CURRENT artifact count. Master is itself an
artifact, so it LANDS ONE BIGGER than the line says - cast at (8/8) and a 9/9 arrives. Add 1 to the
printed number when you plan damage.
Cast it the first turn "Cast Master of Etherium" appears; do not wait for a bigger number, it grows
on the battlefield too. It also gives every OTHER artifact creature +1/+1, so your Ornithopters
start attacking for 1 and your Silver Myrs for 2 the moment it resolves. You run four copies and a
second one makes the first bigger.

=== TOLARIAN ACADEMY - ONE QUESTION BEFORE YOU TAP IT ===
The option reads "add blue mana for each artifact with Tolarian Academy [cost: Tap]" - one blue per
artifact, a huge number late. Before taking it, ask ONE question: is this YOUR Main Phase 1, AND am
I casting something this turn? Unless BOTH answers are yes, answer 0 (pass) - the offer comes back
at every window, you lose nothing by passing. Playing a LAND is NOT a cast - lands cost no mana, so
Academy mana can never help a land drop; if your only play this turn is a land, the answer is 0.
Why: mana disappears at the end of the step it was made in. "Tap it now for later this turn or next
turn" is ALWAYS wrong - mana made at an upkeep, in combat, or on the opponent's turn is thrown away.
When both answers are yes: create any Nuisance Engine Pest FIRST, so the Pest counts toward the
mana. You never need to tap Academy by hand to afford a Cast option - a listed option is already
payable.

=== ACADEMY RUINS - READ THE NAME AFTER "targeting", THEN DECIDE ===
The recursion option reads "Put in Library with Academy Ruins targeting <card>". Before anything
else, read the card name after "targeting":
- Master of Etherium or Akroma's Memorial: take it.
- ANY other name - Cathodion, Scarecrone, a Mox, an artifact land, anything: answer 0 (pass).
Why: the card goes on TOP of your library, so it REPLACES your next draw. Re-drawing a Cathodion
instead of a fresh card is a net loss. "It helps my plan" or "it sets up lethal" does not override
this - only the two names do. Academy Ruins also just taps for {1}; using it as a land is always
fine.

=== KEY CARDS ===
Glaze Fiend {1}{B} - printed 0/1 flying, +2/+2 for each OTHER artifact that enters, until end of
  turn. Its line shows the live number, e.g. "(4/5) (printed 0/1)" - attack with that number. It is
  back to 0/1 on the opponent's turn, so it attacks and never blocks.
Salvage Slasher {1}{B} - 1/1 plus +1/+0 per artifact card in YOUR graveyard; your artifacts dying
  makes it bigger and the line shows the current power.
Cathodion {3} - 3/3, and when it dies it adds {3} to your pool. Attack and block with it freely; the
  mana comes back for you to spend that turn.
Steel Wall {1} - 0/4 defender: it never attacks and never appears on an attacker list. A wall and a
  free +1 to the count; cast it turn 1 with nothing better to do.
Yotian Soldier {3} - 1/4 vigilance: it attacks AND stays back to block the same turn. Send it.
Nuisance Engine {3} - "{2}, {T}: Put a 0/1 colorless Pest artifact creature token onto the
  battlefield." Each Pest is +1 to the count and a free chump blocker. Activate in YOUR Main Phase 1
  when you have {2} spare, before you tap Tolarian Academy.
Coiled Tinviper {3} - 2/1 first strike. An attacker.
Scarecrone {3} - 1/2 with two abilities, and the game offers them separately:
  "Draw 1 with Scarecrone [cost: {1}, Sacrifice]" sacrifices Scarecrone itself to draw a card. Take
  it when you are behind and need a card, NOT while Scarecrone is your only untapped blocker.
  "{4}, {T}: Return target artifact creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield" - take it
  when a real body (Cathodion, Master, Yotian Soldier) is in your graveyard.
Academy Ruins - taps for {1}; its "Put in Library" recursion has its own rule above - two names
  only, pass on everything else.
Akroma's Memorial {7} - gives ALL your creatures flying, first strike, vigilance, trample, haste and
  protection from black and red. It ends games: your 3/3 stops trading with their 1/1, and everything
  you cast can attack the turn it lands. Cast it the first turn it is listed, ahead of any 3-drop.
  Until then it is your worst card in hand and the first card to bottom after a mulligan.

=== ATTACKING ===
Keep declaring every creature the attacker list offers - the list has already dropped everything
that cannot attack. The one exception is written on the line itself: a creature tagged "[deals 0 -
this attack deals no damage to the opponent]" adds nothing, so leave it home and send the rest.
An attack taps that creature for the opponent's turn. Before you swing, read their battlefield line:
a creature tagged "[tapped - cannot attack or block this turn]" or "[summoning sick - cannot attack
this turn, but CAN block]" cannot hit you back this turn, so it does not hold anything home. When
your life is 12 or less, add up what their genuinely UNTAPPED, non-sick creatures swing back for: if
that number is at or above your life, keep enough bodies home to block it and attack with the rest.
Above 12 life, send everything offered.

=== BLOCKING ===
Every blocker line prints the outcome of the 1-on-1 in parentheses - "(you kill it, your blocker
lives)", "(both die)", "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)". That parenthetical is the computed
outcome: BELIEVE IT over your own math, both ways.
- "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" is a FREE KILL: take that block every time, even when the
  attack is small and the header says NOT lethal. "Taking damage while ahead" never means passing
  up a free kill.
- Steel Wall (0/4) and Yotian Soldier (1/4 vigilance) are your blockers - put them in front of the
  biggest attacker whose line says they survive. When every block line on an attacker reads "your
  blocker dies, attacker lives" and the header says NOT lethal, taking the damage is fine - but at
  12 or less life, chump the biggest attacker anyway.
Cathodion blocks and trades happily, its {3} comes back. Glaze Fiend stays out of blocks; on their
turn it is a 0/1.
POISON IS NOT LIFE. When an attacker's line says "[infect]" it does not touch your life total - the
header will say "these attackers put up to N POISON COUNTER on you - you would be at N of 10 poison".
Ten poison loses you the game no matter what your life is, and a poison counter never goes away.
So BLOCK EVERY INFECT ATTACKER YOU CAN, at any life total, even at 20, and even with a body that
gains nothing by it - the block is what stops the counter. Block with your BIGGEST body, not a chump:
infect kills creatures with -1/-1 counters, so a 9/9 Master comes back as an 8/8 and kills the 1/1,
while an Ornithopter absorbs the hit and stays useless. "Your poison counters: N of 10" is printed
for you; read it, never count it.

=== MULLIGAN ===
KEEP any 7-card hand with two or more lands. The prompt counts them for you on its own line - "Your
hand (7 cards), counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells" - so read that number, do not count the
hand yourself. Two lands is enough because 12 of your artifacts cost {0} or {1} and the line
"Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of:" names exactly what you can already cast.
Tiebreakers only, once the two-land floor is met: prefer a hand holding a Mox, an Ornithopter or an
artifact land; prefer a hand without Akroma's Memorial. Colour is never a reason to mulligan - every
land you own makes mana and almost everything you cast is generic.
Never think "I will keep whatever I draw next". Mulligan at most twice, then KEEP.
After a mulligan, the card you bottom is Akroma's Memorial if you have one, otherwise your second
copy of the most expensive card in the hand.

=== DECIDING SITUATIONS ===
- "SACRIFICE ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS": answer in one line off the order above. Pest, then a tapped
  basic, then a tapped land, then a Mox. Never a creature, never Tolarian Academy.
- A Mox or an Ornithopter is in your cast list: take it, this ask, whatever else is listed.
- "Cast Master of Etherium" appears: take it, and plan for one more power than the line shows.
- "Cast Akroma's Memorial" appears: take it, ahead of any other spell you could cast instead.
- An artifact land and a basic are both offered: play the artifact land.
- Your cast list still holds a payable artifact: cast it, then attack - not the reverse.
- Tolarian Academy is offered and it is not your Main Phase 1 with a cast planned: answer 0.
- "Put in Library with Academy Ruins targeting X": X is Master of Etherium or Akroma's Memorial,
  take it; ANY other name, answer 0.
- A block line ends "(you kill it, your blocker lives)": take that block, every time.
- The attacker list shows creatures with no "[deals 0 ...]" tag: send all of them.
- An attacker's line says "[infect]": block it, whatever your life total is.
- Your count of the board disagrees with "Artifacts in play: you N": N wins, move on.
- You are at 12 or less and their untapped creatures swing for your life total: hold blockers back
  and attack with the rest.
