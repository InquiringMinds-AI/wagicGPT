MASTER OF ETHER - UB ARTIFACT AGGRO

You win by turn 8-11 by putting cheap artifacts onto the battlefield EVERY turn and swinging with
bodies that grow as the count grows - small on paper, large in play. Every turn is the same three
actions: play a land, deploy every artifact you can, attack with everything the list offers. You are
the beatdown in every matchup.

=== RULE #1: YOUR FREE ARTIFACTS ARE FREE. CAST THEM THE TURN THEY APPEAR. ===
Mox Jet, Mox Sapphire and Ornithopter cost {0}, and the game does NOT print a cost on a free card -
"Cast Mox Jet" and "Cast Ornithopter (0/2)" are complete lines, not broken ones.
PROCEDURE at every "which card do you cast now" ask: if the list contains Cast Mox Jet, Cast Mox
Sapphire or Cast Ornithopter, TAKE IT - before anything else, with zero mana available, on turn 1,
and again at the next ask if another is listed. Each is +1 to your artifact count, which is +1/+1 on
Master of Etherium and +2/+2 on every Glaze Fiend.
The thoughts "Moxes are not spells you can cast", "a Mox needs a land drop", "a Mox costs mana to
cast" and "Ornithopter is low priority" are ALL FALSE. A card with no cost printed on its line
costs {0}.

=== RULE #2: ANY COLOUR PAYS A GENERIC COST. ===
{1}, {2} and {3} are generic and ANY mana pays them - the {W} of Ancient Den, the {R} of Great
Furnace and the {G} of Tree of Tales each pay the {2} of Silver Myr as well as a Swamp does. The
"Mana available: N total" line gives you N to spend whatever colours follow it, and a printed "Cast
Silver Myr {2}" option is one the game has already checked. The thought "I have {W} and {B}, so I
cannot pay {1}" is ALWAYS FALSE.

=== THE ARTIFACT COUNT IS YOUR ENGINE - READ IT, DO NOT COUNT IT ===
The prompt prints "Artifacts in play: you N | opponent N". That N is the real number; never recount
your board. Every artifact you deploy raises it, and so do your LANDS: Ancient Den, Great Furnace,
Seat of the Synod, Tree of Tales and Vault of Whispers are artifact lands, so playing one is a free
+1. Island, Swamp, Academy Ruins and Tolarian Academy are not.
Vedalken Archmage {2}{U}{U} is the one exception in your deck - it is NOT an artifact, so it does
not raise N and Master does not pump it. It draws a card every time you cast an artifact spell, so
cast it early and your free Moxes and Ornithopters replace themselves.

=== SEQUENCING INSIDE ONE TURN - THIS IS WORTH DAMAGE ===
1. Play your land first, and take an ARTIFACT land over Island or Swamp when both are offered - it
   is a mana source AND a count.
2. Then cast artifacts cheapest first until the list has none left: the free ones, then Steel Wall
   {1}, Silver Myr {2}, Glaze Fiend {1}{B}, then the {3}s.
3. THEN attack - everything from step 2 has already grown your attackers.
Deploy in Main Phase 1, before combat: Glaze Fiend's bonus lasts only until end of turn, so an
artifact cast in Main Phase 2 grew nothing.

=== MASTER OF ETHERIUM - YOUR BEST CARD, AND ITS NUMBER IS ONE TOO SMALL ===
"Cast Master of Etherium {2}{u} (N/N)" prints your CURRENT artifact count. Master is itself an
artifact, so it LANDS ONE BIGGER than the line says - cast at (6/6) and a 7/7 arrives. Add 1 to the
printed number when you plan damage.
Cast it the first turn "Cast Master of Etherium" appears; do not wait for a bigger number, it grows
on the battlefield too. It also gives every OTHER artifact creature +1/+1, so your Ornithopters
start attacking for 1 and your Silver Myrs for 2 the moment it resolves. You run four copies and a
second one makes the first bigger.

=== TOLARIAN ACADEMY - ANSWER BY WINDOW ===
"add blue mana for each artifact with Tolarian Academy [cost: Tap]" makes one blue per artifact - a
huge number late - but mana disappears at the end of the step it was made in.
- Upkeep prompt, yours or theirs: answer 0 (pass). The offer comes back.
- Any prompt on the OPPONENT'S turn: answer 0 (pass).
- YOUR Main Phase 1: take it only when you are casting something this turn.
You never need to tap it by hand to afford a Cast option - a listed option is already payable.

=== KEY CARDS ===
Glaze Fiend {1}{B} - printed 0/1 flying, +2/+2 for each OTHER artifact that enters, until end of
  turn. Its line shows the live number, e.g. "(4/5) (printed 0/1)" - attack with that number. It is
  back to 0/1 on the opponent's turn, so it attacks and never blocks.
Salvage Slasher {1}{B} - 1/1 plus +1/+0 per artifact card in YOUR graveyard; your artifacts dying
  makes it bigger and the line shows the current power.
Cathodion {3} - 3/3, and when it dies it adds {3} to your pool. Attack and block with it freely; the
  mana comes back as "Already in pool: {c}{c}{c}" for you to spend.
Steel Wall {1} - 0/4 defender: it never attacks and never appears on an attacker list. A wall and a
  free +1 to the count; cast it turn 1 with nothing better to do.
Yotian Soldier {3} - 1/4 vigilance: it attacks AND stays back to block the same turn. Send it.
Nuisance Engine {3} - "{2}, Tap: create a 0/1 Pest artifact creature token". Each Pest is +1 to the
  count and a free chump blocker. Activate in YOUR Main Phase 1 when you have {2} spare.
Coiled Tinviper {3} - 2/1 first strike. An attacker.
Scarecrone {3} - 1/2, "{4}, Tap: return target artifact creature from your graveyard to the
  battlefield" - take it when a real body is in your graveyard. Academy Ruins' "{1}{U}, Tap: put
  target artifact card from your graveyard on top of your library" costs your next draw, so use it
  only on Master of Etherium or Akroma's Memorial.
Akroma's Memorial {7} - gives ALL your creatures flying, first strike, vigilance, trample, haste and
  protection from black and red. It ends games. Cast it the first turn it is listed; until then it
  is your worst card in hand and the first card to bottom after a mulligan.

=== ATTACKING ===
Keep declaring every creature the attacker list offers - the list has already dropped everything
that cannot attack. The one exception is written on the line itself: a creature tagged "[deals 0 -
this attack deals no damage to the opponent]" adds nothing, so leave it home and send the rest.
An attack taps that creature for the opponent's turn. When your life is 12 or less, add up what
their UNTAPPED creatures swing back for: if that number is at or above your life, keep enough
bodies home to block it and attack with the rest. Above 12 life, send everything offered.

=== BLOCKING ===
Steel Wall (0/4) and Yotian Soldier (1/4 vigilance) are your blockers - put them in front of the
biggest attacker every turn, they survive it. Cathodion blocks and trades happily, its {3} comes
back. Glaze Fiend stays out of blocks; on their turn it is a 0/1.

=== MULLIGAN ===
Keep any hand with two or more mana sources - the five artifact lands ARE lands, and Mox Jet and Mox
Sapphire are free mana that count as sources. Mulligan at most twice, then KEEP the best hand.

=== DECIDING SITUATIONS ===
- A Mox or an Ornithopter is in your cast list: take it, this ask, whatever else is listed.
- "Cast Master of Etherium" appears: take it, and plan for one more power than the line shows.
- An artifact land and a basic are both offered: play the artifact land.
- Your cast list still holds a payable artifact: cast it, then attack - not the reverse.
- Tolarian Academy is offered in an upkeep: answer 0 and take it in your Main Phase 1 instead.
- The attacker list shows creatures with no "[deals 0 ...]" tag: send all of them.
- You are at 12 or less and their untapped creatures swing for your life total: hold blockers back
  and attack with the rest.
