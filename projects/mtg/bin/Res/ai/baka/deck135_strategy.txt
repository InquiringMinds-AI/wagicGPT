Deck identity: "Modern Snow" is a base-GREEN tempo-value deck splashing blue and
white. It wins by deploying cheap, efficient snow creatures that REPLACE THEMSELVES
with cards (Ice-Fang Coatl, Ohran Viper), grinding the opponent out on card
advantage, and closing with Abominable Treefolk. You are PROACTIVE: develop your
board and attack. This is NOT a durdle deck - do something almost every turn.

=======================================================================
RULE #1 - CRACK YOUR FETCHLANDS. THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT LINE.
=======================================================================
Prismatic Vista, Flooded Strand, Misty Rainforest, and Windswept Heath are
FETCHLANDS. They produce NO MANA on their own. They are useless until you
SACRIFICE them to fetch a real land.
- The turn you play a fetchland, IMMEDIATELY choose the action that reads
  "search basic land ..." or "Put in Play with <fetchland> targeting
  Snow-Covered ___". Its cost line says [cost: Life, Tap, Sacrifice] - paying
  that life and sacrificing the fetch is THE ENTIRE POINT. Always do it.
- NEVER pass (0) while you have an uncracked fetchland and an empty mana pool.
  A fetch left on the battlefield is a wasted land drop and leaves you unable
  to cast anything. Leaving fetches uncracked is the single biggest way this
  deck loses - it screws yourself out of all your mana.
- What to fetch: Snow-Covered FOREST by default (you need green mana most).
  Fetch Snow-Covered ISLAND when you need blue (for Ice-Fang Coatl, Search for
  Azcanta, or especially Force of Negation which needs {U}{U}). You rarely need
  Snow-Covered Plains. Every snow basic you fetch also grows your snow count,
  which powers the whole deck (see below).

=======================================================================
DEVELOP EVERY TURN - do not pass with mana and castable cards.
=======================================================================
Each of your turns: play a land (crack fetches), then SPEND YOUR MANA - cast a
creature, cast Arcum's Astrolabe, or fire a card-draw spell. Only choose "Cast
nothing / pass" when you are deliberately holding mana up for Force of Negation
or to flash in Ice-Fang Coatl on the opponent's turn. Sitting with untapped
lands and spells in hand is a misplay unless you are bluffing interaction.

SNOW IS THE ENGINE. The more snow permanents you control (snow lands, Arcum's
Astrolabe, snow creatures), the better everything gets:
- Abominable Treefolk's power/toughness = your snow permanent count.
- Ice-Fang Coatl gains deathtouch with 3+ OTHER snow permanents.
- Scrying Sheets and Glacial Revelation reward snow.
So fetch snow basics, ramp with Into the North (fetches a snow land), and cast
Arcum's Astrolabe on sight.

=======================================================================
KEY CARDS (roughly in order of how often they decide the game):
=======================================================================
- ARCUM'S ASTROLABE {1}: Cast it on sight, every copy. It draws a card when it
  enters (so it's FREE - it replaces itself), taps for any color to fix your
  mana, and counts as a snow permanent. There is almost never a reason to hold it.
- ICE-FANG COATL {G}{U}: 1/1 flyer with FLASH that draws a card on entry. Prefer
  to cast it at the END of the opponent's turn (flash) so you keep mana up for
  Force of Negation and get information first. Cast it on your own turn only if
  you need the blocker/attacker right now. With 3+ other snow permanents it has
  deathtouch - then it blocks and kills anything.
- OHRAN VIPER {1}{G}{G}: your value engine and a wall no one wants to touch. When
  it deals combat damage to a PLAYER you draw a card; when it damages a CREATURE
  that creature is destroyed. So opponents hate blocking it and hate being hit by
  it. Attack with it for cards; block bigger creatures with it to kill them. Do
  not trade it away cheaply.
- ABOMINABLE TREEFOLK {2}{G}{U}: your finisher and stabilizer. It's a trampler
  whose size equals your snow count (usually 5-8). When it enters it TAPS and
  FREEZES an opponent's creature (that creature won't untap next turn) - use this
  to tap their best blocker before you swing, or their best attacker when you're
  defending. Cast it once you have a healthy pile of snow permanents.
- INTO THE NORTH {1}{G}: ramp + fixing. Fetches a snow land onto the battlefield.
  Cast it early to accelerate a turn ahead and grow your snow count.
- GLACIAL REVELATION {2}{G} / SEARCH FOR AZCANTA {1}{U} / SCRYING SHEETS: card
  advantage. Use them to refuel and out-grind the opponent. (Glacial Revelation
  only keeps SNOW cards from what it reveals.)
- BOREAL DRUID {G}: turn-1 mana dork. Play it early to ramp.
- ICEHIDE GOLEM {1}: cheap 2/2 snow body. Play it to apply early pressure and add
  to your snow count.
- DIAMOND FAERIE {2}{G}{W}{U}: 3/3 flyer that can pump ALL your snow creatures
  +1/+1 (repeatable, needs mana). Use its pump to win a combat or push a lethal
  alpha strike.
- FORCE OF NEGATION {1}{U}{U}: counters NONCREATURE spells only. On the OPPONENT'S
  turn you can cast it for free by exiling a blue card from hand. Hold it for
  their most dangerous noncreature spell - a board wipe, removal aimed at your
  key creature, burn to your face when you're low, or a planeswalker. Countering
  a burn spell or a forced-sacrifice spell is fine; just remember the free mode
  costs you the Force AND a blue card, so don't spend it on something trivial.
- GELID SHACKLES {W}: cheap aura. Put it on a key blocker to push your damage
  through, or on a key attacker to neuter it.

=======================================================================
ROLE - who is the beatdown:
=======================================================================
- DEFAULT you are the proactive value deck: deploy threats, attack, draw cards,
  and win with better cards over time.
- VS AGGRO (mono-red, fast small creatures): shift to defense. Ohran Viper and a
  deathtouch Ice-Fang Coatl are premium blockers - use them to trade UP. Hold
  Force of Negation for their burn. Stabilize behind Abominable Treefolk, keep
  your card engines running, and protect your life total. Don't tap out your
  blockers into a swing-back.
- VS CONTROL / slow decks: be the aggressor. Land threats, protect them with
  Force of Negation, and never stop drawing cards.

COMBAT: your flyers (Ice-Fang Coatl, Diamond Faerie) and Ohran Viper want to
attack for value - swing when it's safe and profitable. Before attacking, check
the opponent's untapped mana for a trick. When blocking, use deathtouch/Viper to
kill bigger creatures; take the damage when your creature is worth more alive.

=======================================================================
MISTAKES TO AVOID (observed in real games with this deck):
=======================================================================
- DO NOT leave a fetchland uncracked. Crack it the turn you play it. (This lost
  whole games - fetches sat unused and the deck made no mana and cast nothing.)
- DO NOT pass your turn with open mana and castable spells in hand. Develop.
- DO NOT hold Arcum's Astrolabe - it's a free card, cast it.
- DO NOT dump Ice-Fang Coatl on your own main phase if you can flash it at the
  end of the opponent's turn instead (unless you need the body immediately).
