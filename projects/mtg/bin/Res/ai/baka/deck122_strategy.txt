YOUR DECK: Orzhov (white-black) LIFEGAIN. You have no removal and no tricks - you win by
turning LIFE INTO A WEAPON. Cheap creatures and lands GAIN you life; a second set of cards
gets BIGGER, meaner, or WINS THE GAME the more life you have. Your life total is not a cushion
you spend down to zero - it is the ENGINE that powers your best cards. Every point of life you
gain is progress toward winning. You have TWO ways to win: (1) beat down with lifelink fliers
and drain the opponent out with Blood Artist, and (2) climb to 40 life and WIN OUTRIGHT with
Felidar Sovereign. Both plans want the same thing: gain life and keep it.

#1 RULE - LIFE IS YOUR ENGINE. STAY ABOVE YOUR THRESHOLDS. Several of your creatures are HUGE
only while your life is high, and shrink the moment you drop below the line. Before you attack
or take damage, check whether the play will push you UNDER a threshold and turn your own
monsters back into 1/1s.
- SERRA ASCENDANT ({W}, 1/1): at 30 OR MORE life she is a 6/6 FLYER with lifelink. Below 30 she
  is a 1/1 with no flying. The board shows her CURRENT size (e.g. "(6/6) (printed 1/1)") - trust
  that number. Do NOT attack yourself below 30 and throw away a 6/6 flying blocker to swing for
  1. Above 30, a Serra Ascendant is your best attacker AND your best blocker at once.
- DIVINITY OF PRIDE ({W/B}x5, 4/4): at 25 OR MORE life she is an 8/8 FLYER with lifelink. Below
  25 she is a 4/4. Same rule - the 25 line is worth protecting.
- The single biggest mistake this deck can make is RACING an aggressive opponent by spending its
  own life. When you burn your life to trade or to push damage, you shrink Serra and Divinity
  AND you walk into their next attack. Against a fast deck your job is to STABILIZE: gain life,
  block, and let your lifelink bodies climb you back up - not to trade life for tempo.

#2 RULE - FELIDAR SOVEREIGN CAN JUST WIN THE GAME. Felidar Sovereign ({4}{W}{W}, 4/6 vigilance
lifelink) has: "At the beginning of your upkeep, if you have 40 or more life, you win the game."
This is a REAL win condition, not a bonus. Once you have stabilized behind lifelink and Felidar
is on the battlefield, your plan is simple: get to 40 life, survive to your next upkeep, WIN. A
4/6 vigilant lifelink body also attacks AND blocks while you climb. Do not bottom Felidar on a
mulligan unless the rest of your hand truly cannot function - in a grind it is your cleanest out.
When you are at 30+ life with a stable board, actively look for the 40-life line: gain triggers,
lifelink attacks, and Kabira/Lone Missionary/Kitchen Finks all push you there.

#3 RULE - BUILD THE LIFEGAIN ENGINE EARLY, THEN CASH IT IN.
- SOUL WARDEN ({W}, 1/1): gain 1 life whenever ANY creature enters - yours or the opponent's.
  Cast it TURN 1 if you can; every creature all game then feeds you.
- SUTURE PRIEST ({1}{W}, 1/1): may gain 1 when YOUR creature enters; may make the OPPONENT lose
  1 when THEIRS enters. Always take both ("Life" and "Life Loss" when asked) unless a rare
  reason not to. Great early against a creature deck.
- LONE MISSIONARY ({1}{W}, 2/1): gain 4 on enter. A big single burst - even better with Soul
  Warden/Suture Priest also triggering, and it grows Ajani's Pridemate.
- KITCHEN FINKS ({1}{G/W}{G/W}, 3/2, persist): gain 2 on enter, and PERSIST means it comes back
  once when it dies (as a 2/1) and gains you 2 AGAIN. A premier blocker vs aggro - it eats two
  attackers and gains you 4 life total. The {G/W} pips pay with white.
- KABIRA CROSSROADS: a land that enters tapped and gains you 2 life. Lead on it when you do not
  need untapped mana - free life every game.
- AJANI'S PRIDEMATE ({1}{W}, 2/2): puts a +1/+1 counter on ITSELF every time you gain life
  (once per life-gain event). Deploy it EARLY behind your gainers and it snowballs into a huge
  body. It is a payoff, so play your gainers around it.

#4 RULE - YOUR LIFELINK BEATERS ARE THE PRIMARY CLOCK, AND THEY GAIN YOU LIFE WHILE THEY WORK.
- VAMPIRE NIGHTHAWK ({1}{B}{B}, 2/3 flying, deathtouch, lifelink): your best all-rounder. As a
  BLOCKER, deathtouch means it kills ANYTHING it blocks and (flying) it can block fliers; you
  gain 2 when it deals damage. Hold it back vs aggro; attack with it when the coast is clear.
- SERRA ASCENDANT (above 30) and DIVINITY OF PRIDE (above 25) are lifelink FLIERS - attacking
  with them gains you life AND pushes you toward Felidar's 40. Evasive lifelink is exactly what
  both of your win plans want.

#5 RULE - BLOOD ARTIST IS A DRAIN ENGINE - DO NOT ATTACK WITH IT. Blood Artist ({1}{B}, 0/1):
whenever ANY creature dies (yours OR the opponent's), the opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1.
It is a 0/1 - never attack with it. Its value is in grindy, trade-heavy boards: every death,
including chump blocks and your own dying creatures, drains the opponent and gains you life.
Keep it alive; it makes every combat swing the life totals your way.

#6 RULE - SORIN, LORD OF INNISTRAD ({2}{W}{B}, planeswalker, loyalty 3). USE HIM EVERY TURN.
- +1: create a 1/1 black Vampire with LIFELINK. This is your default almost every turn - it
  ticks Sorin up (protecting him), adds a lifelink body (more attacks, more Blood Artist fodder,
  triggers Soul Warden/Suture Priest/Pridemate), and builds a board for free. Chaining +1 is
  correct and strong.
- -2: emblem "creatures you control get +1/+0" (permanent anthem). Only when a wide board makes
  the extra power lethal or race-winning; usually +1 is better.
- -6: destroy up to three creatures/planeswalkers and reanimate them under YOUR control. A
  finisher - only when there are real targets worth taking. Do NOT -6 for a small or worthless
  creature; keep +1ing until the -6 is a blowout.

MATCHUP POSTURE.
- vs AGGRO (fast creature decks): you are the CONTROL. Do not race. Deploy blockers (Kitchen
  Finks, Nighthawk, Serra as a 6/6 above 30), take the good blocks, gain life, keep Serra above
  30 and yourself climbing. Let lifelink and Blood Artist grind them out. Your life total going
  UP is you winning.
- vs MIDRANGE / SLOWER decks: you are the BEATDOWN. Grind value with the lifegain engine, apply
  the lifelink fliers, use Sorin +1 every turn, and go over the top - either drain them out or
  ride the life lead to Felidar's 40.

MANA: you need white and black. Scrubland and Isolated Chapel make both; Urborg turns all your
lands into Swamps for black; Kabira Crossroads and Plains give white, Swamp gives black. If a
Cast option is listed, you CAN pay for it - never decline a spell thinking you lack the color.
Play a land every turn.
