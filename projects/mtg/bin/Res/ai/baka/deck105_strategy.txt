INFECTED WOUNDS - BG INFECT (poison kill)

YOU DO NOT KILL WITH LIFE. Every creature here has INFECT: its combat damage puts POISON
COUNTERS on the opponent instead of reducing their life. 10 poison counters and they LOSE, at
any life total. The general prompt's "you win by reducing the opponent's life from 20 to 0"
DOES NOT APPLY TO THIS DECK - ignore it. Their life is not your target and their lifegain does
nothing to you. You need TEN points of unblocked attack damage, total, ever. Every turn: add an
infect body and swing with everything.

== #1 RULE - AT THE BLOCKERS SEAM, YOUR CREATURES ARE THE CLOCK, NOT THE DEFENCE ==
Every infect creature you lose blocking is poison you will never deal. Your life is a 20-point
resource you are SPENDING; your creatures are the only thing that wins.
At every "declare blockers" ask, read the engine's own line - "Your life: 20. Unblocked, these
attackers deal up to 5 - you would be at 15 - NOT lethal". That "you would be at N" number is
accurate for what your opponents do to you. One step:
  * N is 9 or more -> answer "BLOCKS: none". Take it all; every body stays alive and untapped
    for your attack.
  * N is 8 or less, or the line says LETHAL -> block now: chump or trade with your SMALLEST
    bodies, as many as it takes to survive the turn.
Skithiryx and Putrefax are attackers only - never name them as blockers at any life total.
The thought "blocking to remove a big threat is good" is FALSE here while you are at 9 or more
life: killing their creature advances your clock by zero counters and costs you a body that
would have advanced it. Their board only matters when it is about to kill you, and that line
tells you when.

== THE CLOCK - COUNT THE POISON ONCE, THEN STOP ==
The board does not print the poison count. Work it out ONCE, in ONE clause, then move on:
  OPPONENT'S POISON = every "Damage: N dealt by <one of YOUR creatures> to the opponent" line
  in the GAME LOG, added up, + 1 for each of your Ichor Rats that entered the battlefield.
  (A "Damage" line with no "Opponent's life -N" beside it is poison, not life.)
Say "they are on 7 poison, 3 to go" and stop. Do not re-check it, do not reconcile it with
their life total, and do not decide your count is wrong because their life is still 20 - THEIR
LIFE STAYING AT 20 IS WHAT INFECT LOOKS LIKE WORKING. If the arithmetic is ever unclear, assume
you are CLOSER than you think and attack with everything; that is the right play at any count.

Standing loop, every one of your turns, in order:
 1. Play a land if a "Play <land>" option is listed.
 2. Cast the biggest infect creature the Cast list offers. If a Cast option is listed the engine
    has already checked you can pay it - take it, do not recount your lands. Cast two if the
    mana allows, before combat.
 3. Attack with every creature listed under "Your creatures that can attack" - that list is
    already the eligible set, summoning-sick bodies are dropped for you. "ATTACK: none" only
    when the list is empty.
 4. Mana left at end of turn is wasted. Spend it on step 2.

== YOUR CARDS ==
Plague Stinger {1}{B} 1/1 flying - unblockable unless they have a flyer or reach; 1 poison a
  turn, all game. Your most reliable counter.
Blight Mamba {1}{G} 1/1 - {1}{G}: Regenerate. Pay it rather than let it die in combat.
Necropede {2} 1/1 artifact - when it dies you MAY put a -1/-1 counter on target creature: point
  it at one of THEIRS, never your own.
Ichorclaw Myr {2} 1/1 artifact - gets +2/+2 whenever it becomes BLOCKED, so it attacks as a
  1/1 and becomes a 3/3 the moment they block it - it kills the blocker instead of dying.
  Always attack with it; blocking it is bad for them.
Contagious Nim {2}{B} 2/2 - 2 poison a swing. Cystbearer {2}{G} 2/3 - 2 poison, hardest of the
  cheap bodies to kill.
Ichor Rats {1}{B}{B} 2/1 - on entry EACH player gets a poison counter, you included. Cast it
  anyway: +1 on them now plus 2 a turn beats 1 on you, and only you are racing to 10. Neither
  counter is announced in the log - add both by hand.
Tel-Jilad Fallen {2}{G}{G} 3/1, protection from artifacts - 3 poison a swing, and no artifact
  creature can block it. Your best body against an artifact deck.
Blackcleave Goblin {3}{B} 2/1 HASTE - attacks for 2 the turn you cast it.
Corpse Cur {4} 2/2 - on entry you MAY return an infect creature from your graveyard to hand.
  Always take it; take the HIGHEST POWER one offered.
Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon {3}{B}{B} 4/4 flying - 4 of your 10 counters in one swing, and
  almost nothing blocks a flyer. {B}: gains haste until end of turn, so on the turn you cast it,
  if you have one more black mana, PAY IT AND ATTACK THAT TURN. {B}{B}: regenerate - pay it
  rather than lose it. Skithiryx alive for two more turns is the game.
Putrefax {3}{G}{G} 5/3 trample haste - it is SACRIFICED at the beginning of the end step, so it
  exists for exactly one attack. Cast it and attack with it the SAME TURN, always: 5 poison is
  half the game and trample means a chump block barely slows it.
Hand of the Praetors {3}{B} 3/2 - your other infect creatures get +1/+1 (every attacker deals
  one more poison), AND whenever you CAST an infect creature spell target player gets a poison
  counter: point that at the OPPONENT every time. Cast it the turn you can, then keep casting
  creatures. If it is not in your hand, nothing above changes.
Relic Putrescence {2}{B} - enchant an ARTIFACT; when that artifact becomes tapped its controller
  gets a poison counter. Put it on an OPPONENT'S artifact, preferring one that taps every turn
  (a mana artifact or an artifact land). If the only legal targets are your own artifacts, cast
  a creature instead - do not agonise.

== MULLIGAN ==
Your cheapest card costs TWO mana; this deck has no 1-drops at all, so "no one-drop" is never a
reason to mulligan - you will never find one.
KEEP any hand with 2, 3 or 4 lands and at least one creature. Every creature in the deck is a
threat, so any creature counts. Mulligan only 0-1 lands, or 5+ lands, or no creature at all.
AT MOST TWO MULLIGANS, then KEEP the best hand you are shown.
Bottoming on a keep: bottom LANDS first - there are 35 in the deck and you will draw more. Keep
every creature.

== SITUATIONS THAT DECIDE THESE GAMES ==
* At 20 life, they swing for 5 with one big creature -> "BLOCKS: none"; every body stays home
  untapped and attacks on your turn.
* At 12 life, the line says "you would be at 6 - NOT lethal" -> BLOCK; chump with your smallest
  bodies until you survive. Below 9 life you need the turns.
* You cast Skithiryx with one black mana left -> pay {B} for haste and attack for 4 now.
* Putrefax is castable -> cast it and attack with it this turn; there is no next turn for it.
* Corpse Cur enters with infect creatures in your graveyard -> take the return, biggest power.
* Their life is going UP, they are on 23 -> nothing changed. Keep attacking; life is not the
  number you are moving.
* You are on 3 life and they are on 8 poison -> attack with everything. You win by landing two
  more counters, not by holding a blocker back.
