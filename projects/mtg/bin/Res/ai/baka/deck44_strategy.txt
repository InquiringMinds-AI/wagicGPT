Deck identity: UB FAERIE TEMPO. Every creature FLIES. You win by casting a faerie
EVERY turn and attacking in the air EVERY turn - get them dead before turn 10. You
are the BEATDOWN in every matchup. Against a control deck that keeps wiping your
board (Wrath of God, Pyroclasm) or bouncing it (Cyclonic Rift), countering that
sweeper IS your attack - a resolved sweeper erases the clock you just built.

THE #1 RULE - CAST A CREATURE EVERY TURN:
- On EVERY one of your turns: play a land, then CAST A CREATURE if any "Cast"
  option shows a creature. If the engine OFFERS "Cast X", the cost IS payable -
  Oona's Gatewarden {u/b} casts off a single Island OR Swamp.
- Scion of Oona and Obyra have flash, but cast them in your main phase like any
  other creature.
- CAST ARCHMAGE OF ECHOES the first turn you have 5 mana - a 4/4 flying ward-2
  threat that copies every Faerie you cast after it. Board first, counters with
  leftover mana.

THE ENGINE - HOW YOU KILL:
- Obyra, Dreaming Duelist ({u}{b} 2/2 flash flyer): whenever ANOTHER Faerie enters
  under your control, the opponent loses 1. Every creature in this deck is a Faerie,
  so after Obyra is out every creature you cast, every token, and every Archmage
  copy drains 1. Cast Obyra early.
- Archmage of Echoes ({4}{u} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): copies each Faerie or Wizard you
  cast (it copies permanents/creatures, not instants). Archmage + any faerie = two
  bodies and, with Obyra out, two drains.
- Faerie Bladecrafter ({2}{b} 2/2 flyer): gets a +1/+1 counter each turn your
  faeries hit the opponent, and when it DIES the opponent loses life equal to its
  power and you gain that much. The "{right now: life -2}" on its Cast option is the
  OPPONENT'S loss when it later dies - a bonus for you, never a cost. Always cast it.

ATTACK EVERY TURN: your flyers are usually unblockable. Attack with EVERY creature
except Oona's Gatewarden (defender). LETHAL CHECK: total power of your untapped
flyers + 1 per faerie you can still cast with Obyra out; if >= opponent life, attack
with everyone and cast the faeries.

BLOCKING - YOUR FLYERS ARE YOUR CLOCK, NEVER CHUMP THEM:
- When your life is 9 or more, decline blocks with your attackers - Faerie Miscreant,
  Scion of Oona, Surveilling Sprite, every flyer are your clock and your anthem, and
  a 2-power attacker at high life is not worth one of them. Take the damage and keep
  attacking.
- Oona's Gatewarden is your ONLY blocker: defender (never attacks) with wither, so
  blocking with it puts -1/-1 counters on the attacker for free. Block an incoming
  attacker with Oona's Gatewarden whenever it is on your battlefield.
- When your life is 8 or less, chump the biggest attacker with your least valuable
  body - a Surveilling Sprite draws you a card when it dies - to stay out of lethal
  range. Do NOT block Phyrexian Obliterator: any damage dealt to it makes YOU
  sacrifice permanents.

COUNTERS - READ THE STACK, HOLD THE MANA:
- When spells are waiting to resolve, the CURRENT SITUATION shows an "ON THE STACK"
  section naming each pending spell, its controller, and its targets, and your
  counter option names what it can hit ("Cast Counterspell - can target on the
  stack: ..."). Read those lines and counter the named spell when it is any of, in
  this order:
   1. A BOARD SWEEPER - Wrath of God, Pyroclasm, Black Sun's Zenith, Cyclonic Rift.
      These erase every creature you deployed; countering one is your whole turn.
   2. An ARTIFACT creature or equipment you cannot Go for the Throat - Master of
      Etherium, Etched Champion, Cranial Plating, Steel Overseer.
   3. A growing engine - Young Pyromancer, Guttersnipe.
   4. Any creature or equipment while your life is 12 or less, or any spell costing
      4+ mana.
- Counters are for the list above ONLY. LET RESOLVE and keep your mana for: mana
  rocks and cantrips (Mox Opal, Arcum's Astrolabe), Elixir of Immortality, Prism
  Ring, land searches (Into the North), and small utility spells. Never spend a
  counter on a 1-2 mana cantrip or mana rock - Arcane Denial draws THEM two cards,
  so it is worth far more than the one card an Astrolabe replaces.
- A counter can only be USED when the game opens a window, and it opens one only
  while you can PAY {U}{U}: if you tap out on your own turn, no window opens on the
  opponent's turn and the counter rots in your hand. When you are holding a
  Counterspell or Arcane Denial for the opponent's turn, LEAVE two blue sources
  untapped - deploy the rest of your hand with your other mana.
- When you decide to counter, your reply NUMBER must be the Cast Counterspell or
  Cast Arcane Denial line. If your PLAN says "counter it," pick that number - not
  "Cast nothing."
- Casting your creatures on your own turn ALWAYS comes before holding counter mana.

GO FOR THE THROAT ({1}{b}): destroys any NONARTIFACT creature. Fire it on their
best attacker, especially ones that grow (Stromkirk Noble, Abominable Treefolk). It
CANNOT hit artifact creatures (Master of Etherium, Memnite, Icehide Golem, Vault
Skirge) and CANNOT hit enchantments (Lightmine Field), planeswalkers, or lands. If
the opponent has NO nonartifact creature on their battlefield, Go for the Throat has
no target even though "Cast Go for the Throat" is listed - do NOT pick it (it does
nothing): cast a creature, or hold and counter instead.

CARD NOTES:
- Sleep-Cursed Faerie ({u} 3/3 flyer, ward 2): enters tapped with 3 stun counters
  and wakes on its own in 3 turns. Cast it turn 1-2 and let it wake for free. Pay
  the {1}{u} untap only in your Main Phase 1 on a turn it will attack this combat;
  pass every untap prompt in Upkeep, combat, and the opponent's turn.
- Surveilling Sprite ({1}{u} 1/1 flyer): draws you a card when it dies - a fine
  chump-blocker at 8 life or less, and its entry drains 1 with Obyra out. Cast it as a body.
- Faerie Miscreant ({u} 1/1 flyer): the second copy draws a card - cast both.
- Oona's Gatewarden ({u/b} 2/1 flying defender, wither): your best blocker; wither
  shrinks what it blocks. Cast it turn 1 off ANY land. It can never attack, but it
  IS a creature and a Faerie (its entry drains 1 with Obyra out).
- Scion of Oona ({2}{u} 1/1 flash flyer): other faeries get +1/+1 and shroud - cast
  before attacking to pump the team. Keep it attacking; do not trade it away blocking.
- Shadow Puppeteers ({6}{u} 4/4 flyer): makes two 1/1 flying Faerie Rogues on entry
  (each drains 1 with Obyra out). Always say YES to turning an attacking flyer into a
  4/4 dragon.

MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2-4 lands and at least one creature - and Oona's
Gatewarden and Sleep-Cursed Faerie COUNT as your creature. Ship a hand that has no
castable creature, or one whose lands cannot cast its spells (e.g. all Swamps with a
blue-heavy hand), or 0-1 or 5+ lands. A cheap flyer plus lands beats a hand of
counters and removal - you will draw the flyers.

SITUATIONS THAT DECIDED GAMES - THE CORRECT PLAY:
- A pending spell shows in ON THE STACK and a Counterspell/Arcane Denial option names
  it: counter it per the priority list, and make your reply number the counter line,
  never "Cast nothing." (vs140 and vs110 were WON this way - countering Pyroclasm,
  Wrath of God, and Etched Champion.)
- The opponent casts a board sweeper (Wrath of God, Pyroclasm, Black Sun's Zenith,
  Cyclonic Rift): counter it - it is worth more than any single creature.
- A creature is castable on your turn: cast it. Archmage of Echoes hits the board
  the first turn you have 5 mana.
- An attacker comes at you and you are at 9+ life: take it, keep your flyers attacking
  - only Oona's Gatewarden blocks.
- Your opening has Oona's Gatewarden or Sleep-Cursed Faerie plus 2-4 lands: KEEP it -
  that is your creature.
