Deck identity: UB FAERIE TEMPO. Nearly every threat FLIES. You win by deploying
cheap evasive faeries early, attacking in the air EVERY turn, and protecting that
clock with cheap counters and removal. You are the BEATDOWN by default - your job
is to get the opponent dead, not to durdle.

THE #1 RULE - DEPLOY, DO NOT SIT:
- A hand of counters and removal with NO creatures on the board is LOSING. Get
  faeries down. If you can cast a faerie this turn, cast it. Do not "hold up
  interaction" turn after turn while your board stays empty and your life ticks
  down. You must build a flying clock FIRST; interaction protects the clock, it is
  not the plan by itself.
- Play a land every turn. Then cast the cheapest faerie you can.

CURVE / SEQUENCING (deploy on this priority):
- T1: Faerie Miscreant ({U}, 1/1 flyer) or Sleep-Cursed Faerie ({U}, 3/3 flyer).
- Sleep-Cursed Faerie enters TAPPED with 3 stun counters - it can't attack yet. It
  loses one counter each turn instead of untapping, so it comes online on its own
  in ~3 turns. Play it EARLY so it's ready by mid-game. Only pay {1}{U} to untap it
  when doing so lets it ATTACK or BLOCK this turn; do not burn that mana on an empty
  board.
- Keep casting a flyer each turn (Miscreant, Surveilling Sprite, Bladecrafter,
  Obyra, Scion). Attack with everything not needed to block.

ATTACK IN THE AIR EVERY TURN: your creatures fly; most opponents can't block them.
When you declare attackers, attack with every flyer that isn't a defender and isn't
holding back for a critical block. Chipping 1-4 in the air every turn is how this
deck wins. Do not leave attackers home for no reason.

KEY CARDS - USE THEM RIGHT:
- Scion of Oona ({2}{U}, flash flyer): gives your OTHER faeries +1/+1 AND SHROUD
  (they can't be targeted by removal). It both boosts your damage and makes your
  board removal-proof. Great to cast - and because it has flash you can drop it at
  end of the opponent's turn or mid-combat as an ambush blocker/pump.
- Obyra, Dreaming Duelist ({U}{B}, flash flyer): every OTHER faerie that enters
  drains the opponent 1 life. Get Obyra down, THEN pour out your other faeries -
  each one is free damage. Flash lets it ambush-block a flyer too.
- Faerie Bladecrafter ({2}{B} flyer): grows +1/+1 whenever your faeries hit the
  opponent, and when it DIES the opponent loses life equal to its power (and you
  gain it). Attack with it freely - trading it away still drains them.
- Faerie Miscreant: if you already control one, the second draws a card. Play both.
- Archmage of Echoes ({4}{U}, 4/4 flyer, ward 2): COPIES each Faerie or Wizard
  spell you cast after it's out. Land Archmage FIRST, then cast your faeries and
  counterspells to double them. A big evasive body that's hard to remove (ward 2).
- Oona's Gatewarden ({U/B}, 2/1 flying DEFENDER, wither): can't attack - it's your
  best BLOCKER. Wither means it kills bigger creatures (deals -1/-1 counters). Keep
  it home to wall the ground and trade up against aggro.
- Shadow Puppeteers ({6}{U}): top end. Makes two 1/1 flyers on entry and can turn an
  attacking flyer into a 4/4 dragon. A huge tempo/damage swing when you reach 7 mana.

INTERACTION - SPEND IT ON THREATS THAT MATTER:
- Go for the Throat and Counterspell/Arcane Denial are PREMIUM. Save them for the
  opponent's real threats - big creatures, bombs, must-answer spells. Do NOT blow
  removal on a random 2/2; you can just race it in the air.
- EXCEPTION vs fast aggro: killing an attacker to survive is correct. Trade freely
  then.
- Arcane Denial counters anything and replaces itself (you draw) - it's your safest
  counter; use it on the first threat worth stopping.
- Hold counters up on the opponent's turn only once you already have a board.

ROLE BY MATCHUP:
- Vs slow/control/midrange: you are the aggressor. Deploy flyers, protect them with
  Scion's shroud, counter their key removal/sweeper, and race.
- Vs faster aggro (you're getting raced): shift toward defense - deploy Oona's
  Gatewarden to block, use removal on their biggest attacker, and only keep attacking
  in the air if you're winning the race. Don't trade your whole board away chumping;
  your flyers still need to close the game.

MISTAKES TO AVOID (seen in play):
- Sitting on a full grip of counters/removal with an empty board - DEPLOY faeries.
- Firing Go for the Throat at a small, irrelevant creature - save it.
- Leaving flyers at home instead of attacking when the air is open.
- Paying {1}{U} to untap Sleep-Cursed Faerie with nothing to do that turn.
- Forgetting to play a land, or passing your main phase without developing.
