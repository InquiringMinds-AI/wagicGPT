YOUR DECK: mono-red LAND DESTRUCTION. Take their lands while they are still cheap to take, kill
every creature they deploy, then land one real body - Siege-Gang Commander or Rorix Bladewing - and
attack until they are dead. You are NOT a burn deck: every damage card is REMOVAL that can also hit
a face, and all of it totals 11 damage, which is not a clock. Every turn has a job - destroy a
land, kill a creature, or add a body. The kill is Rorix (6 a turn, flying) or Siege-Gang plus
Goblin sacrifices. All 24 lands tap for {R}, plus Talisman of Impulse: colour
never justifies a mulligan or a declined spell, and "Mana available:" is correct.

=== ORDER OF OPERATIONS ===
Play a land every turn. At each "which card do you cast" ask take the FIRST entry below matching a
row on your menu and stop there, then take the ask again - more than one spell a turn is allowed.
 1. STARSTORM, when STARSTORM'S X below clears it. Sweep BEFORE you deploy: the affordable X
    shrinks with each spell cast first, and it kills your own bodies too.
 2. A single-target damage spell (Pyrite Spellbomb, Spark Spray, Hammer of Bogardan) aimed by
    TARGETING below. A token-MAKER on their line (Thraben Doomsayer 2/2, Bloodline Keeper 3/3) is
    what this entry exists for and outranks entry 4.
 3. STONE RAIN, MOLTEN RAIN or LAY WASTE at one of their lands, under THE LAND PLAN's two numbers
    (their lands 4 or fewer AND your life 9 or more); either failing ends the land plan for good.
 4. RORIX at six lands, or SIEGE-GANG at five - never into a board where 1 still applies, and never
    as the FIRST spell of a turn while THEY COUNTER below is true.
 5. DWARVEN BLASTMINER or TALISMAN OF IMPULSE early, when nothing above matched.
 6. A cycling row, by CYCLING below, with mana you were not going to spend.
 7. "Cast nothing right now".
By land count: 2 Blastminer / Talisman / Spellbomb; 3 Stone Rain or Molten Rain (never a Spellbomb
"first" with the land destruction left in hand); 4 a second land-kill plus a Blastminer activation;
5 Siege-Gang; 6+ Rorix, or a land-kill plus an activation. "Land drop: NOT yet used this turn" with
a land in hand -> play it first, ahead of any cycling row. In your own upkeep answer CHOICE: 0
(pass) unless the row destroys a land, kills a creature, or is the Hammer return under EMPTY-HAND;
cycle in second main phase.

=== DRAW PUNISHERS - READ BEFORE ANY OPTIONAL DRAW ===
The frame prints a summed line when they are out: "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield ... Every card
YOU draw costs you 1 life to theirs." That number is N; no line, no punisher.
 - N is 1 and "Your life:" above 5 -> you may cycle.
 - N is 2 or more, OR N at or above your life minus 5 -> take NO optional draw: no cycling row, no
   Forgotten Cave cycle, no Spellbomb draw mode. Pass.
YOUR DRAW STEP IS MANDATORY AND NO ROW SKIPS IT - not "Cast nothing right now", not "Pass
priority", not the Hold-priority row; "DRAW FORECAST:" says what it costs, not that you may skip
it.
PUNISHERS PUNISH DRAWS AND NOTHING ELSE - casting, playing a land, attacking, blocking, activating
and a token entering cost you nothing there. A row marked "[castable now]" is not a draw: cast it,
especially when the crack-back line says their next attack would kill you. Killing the punisher
outranks casting a creature: Fate Unraveler is a 3/4, out of range of Hammer (3) and Spellbomb (2)
but not of Starstorm at X=4.

=== CYCLING - LAY WASTE AND STARSTORM ARE NOT CARD DRAW ===
A cycle is an option beginning "cycling with ", or row 1 ("1. cycling") of a "Choose an option for"
menu. Costs: Spark Spray and Forgotten Cave {R}, Lay Waste {2}, Starstorm {3}.
 Forgotten Cave -> it enters tapped; while the land drop is unused, or a second land sits in hand,
   PLAY it, and after that cycle freely with spare mana.
 Spark Spray -> cycle when its target list shows no opponent creature whose row reads "takes 1
   damage - DIES". A SURVIVES row is not one, nor is the face row.
 Lay Waste -> CAST it at a land; the gate is the MANA line, not a land count, so at "Mana
   available:" of 4 or more it is castable. Cycle only when it is uncastable this turn AND you hold
   another land-kill, or once the land plan's two numbers are dead. Never twice in one turn.
 Starstorm -> do not cycle while the "Opponent battlefield" line names ANY creature. The release is
   that line's count: "of which 0 are creatures" and no Forgotten Cave in hand -> cycle it; with a
   Cave in hand, cycle the Cave first.
CASTING AT X=0 IS NOT CYCLING: only the "cycling" row draws a card, while "Cast Card Normally" at
X=0 deals 0 and bins the card; on a "Choose an option" menu reached by mistake, "Decline - do
nothing" is the way back. ONCE THE LAND PLAN IS OVER THE CYCLE IS YOUR ONLY LINE: at "Mana
available:" 10 or more against 5 or more of their lands, with only a cycle or a pass, CYCLE.

=== TARGETING DAMAGE ===
Spellbomb, Spark Spray, Starstorm, Hammer and Siege-Gang's sacrifice list their choices, the face
row reading "The opponent (player, life N)". Top down:
 0. LETHAL FIRST. Read "Opponent life:" on every decision. If one row deals that number or more to
    a player - Hammer 3, Spellbomb 2, Spark Spray 1, each Goblin sacrificed 2 - take the face row
    NOW; nothing below applies and nothing killing you applies. Its kill count counts CREATURES
    only, so "{kills 0 of the 2 legal targets}" can still be lethal, and a repeatable cost counts
    once per payment you can afford: three Goblins at {1}{R} with 8 mana is 6 damage.
 1. An opponent PLANESWALKER, or a creature named on the DRAW PUNISHERS line -> aim there, ahead of
    casting any creature. Its row prints "[counters: Nx loyalty]": when N is at or below a spell's
    damage, that cast comes before any body.
 2. An opponent CREATURE whose printed CURRENT toughness the damage reaches -> aim there. Spark
    Spray kills 1 toughness, Spellbomb 2, Hammer 3. THE ROW'S VERDICT OUTRANKS YOUR PLAN'S VERB:
    "- DIES" is the row you take, "- SURVIVES (toughness N)" the row you decline, and if EVERY row
    says SURVIVES the answer one screen earlier was "Cast nothing right now". Exceptions: "SURVIVES
    (loyalty 3, 1 left)" is a planeswalker and IS a take, and after combat damage a row can flip.
 3. A creature this cannot kill but that attacks every turn -> aim there rather than bank the card.
 4. Only then the face, and only when "Opponent life:" is 6 or less; at 7 or more that row is not
    on your menu, and an empty opposing board is a reason to HOLD.
THE 6-LIFE FLOOR RELEASES ON TWO PRINTED NUMBERS - their line reading 0 creatures AND 2 or fewer
lands - and only once the log shows a land-kill of yours that resolved. Once released the face row
is the clock at any life total.
LIFEGAIN CROSS-CHECK: read the sign in "Opponent life trend: ... now 31 (+3 since turn 34)" before
numbering any face row. A one-off gain does not stop your burn; a gain that REPEATS at or above the
damage you make per turn latches, and every point then goes at creatures and planeswalkers unless
step 0 fires. BOTH HALVES OF A LIFE LOOP (Sanguine Bond + Exquisite Blood, named in the frame's
"LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER" block) is the one board where DEPLOYING is wrong - their gain repeats
until you are at 0, and their edicts gain them life equal to your creature's toughness. Hold
everything and take the hold row. ON THAT BOARD EVERY PRINTED LIFE PRICE IS LETHAL, NOT EXPENSIVE:
a row bracketed "paying this costs you N life" - the Talisman's own 1 among them - closes the loop
and kills you from ANY life total, so take a row priced at no life, or the hold row. Read the same
way the moment the frame prints "LOOP HALF PENDING".
HAMMER, SPELLBOMB AND SPARK SPRAY NEVER TOUCH A LAND - their menus list players and creatures only.
A ROW TAGGED [your battlefield] IS NEVER THE ANSWER; when it is the only row, "CHOICE: 0 (pass)".
Never target "Yourself (player, life N)".

=== STARSTORM'S X ===
DECIDE WHETHER AT THE CAST ROW, NOT AT THE X MENU: it prints the same two lists and still offers
"Cast nothing right now". Once you answer "Cast Card Normally" the mana is paid and pooled, and the
X menu's Decline cancels the cast without refunding it - declining there loses the turn's mana.
DO NOT CAST when either holds: (a) the YOURS list at that X names Rorix or Siege-Gang, the only two
cards that end a game for you; (b) the THEIRS list names only "[defender]" creatures - carve-out,
if a GROUNDED attacker of yours is being held back by exactly those defenders, the sweep buys your
clock (never for Rorix, who flies over them). Also do not cast on "your mana affords only X=0" or
"even at X=N NOTHING on the board dies".
SURVIVAL OVERRIDE: add the printed power of every creature on their line; if that sum is at or
above "Your life:", take the longest THEIRS list whatever YOURS names - unless what it leaves alive
still totals your life or more, when it buys nothing and you keep the blocker.
At every "Cast Starstorm" window write one PLAN line of actions:
  PLAN: <Starstorm now / hold it>, then <next action>
Answer "Cast nothing right now" when THEIRS holds no non-defender, or YOURS names Rorix or
your commander.
ON THE X MENU: option 1 is the LARGEST X, never X=1, and any X your plan names is older than the
menu. The menu MARKS one row ("[<- most kills at any affordable X ...]" or "[<- best trade: ...]"):
answer that row's number and read nothing else - a marker answers WHICH X, never WHETHER. With no
marker take the LARGEST "kills THEIRS" list whose "YOURS:" reads "none", then walk DOWN through any
"same kills as X=N, for K less mana" row to the LOWEST of that run. Never take a row whose "kills
THEIRS" reads "none".

=== THE LAND PLAN ===
Land destruction is TEMPO: worth a card while their battlefield shows FOUR OR FEWER lands and
nearly worthless after, so spend it EARLY and never hold it for a better target.
WRITE TWO NUMBERS BEFORE EVERY SUCH CAST: "Their lands <N>; my life <L>". N comes off their line's
tail, and collapsed handles count in full ("Island #1-#4 x4" is four lands, one row). At N of 5 or
more, or L of 8 or less, the spell
is dead for the game and does not revive because you have nothing else to do with the mana: "Cast
nothing right now", and the card is a Cleanup discard. The plan also ends the moment "Opponent
life:" is at or below the damage you can point at a player this turn.
WHICH LAND: prefer a nonbasic (Molten Rain deals 2 extra to its controller for one, and nonbasics
fix their colours); a tapped land is a fine target.

=== CARD FACTS THE PROMPT DOES NOT PRINT ===
DWARVEN BLASTMINER (1/1) is a machine, not a creature: "{2}{R}, Tap: destroy target NONBASIC land"
- basics are not legal targets, and it needs him untapped. Activate only while their board shows
FOUR OR FEWER lands and your life is above 8; with a DRAW PUNISHERS line up and a body in "Your
hand" the activation is OFF at any life and land count, because that {2}{R} is the body's mana.
TALISMAN OF IMPULSE is ramp; its {1} mode is free and its coloured mode costs 1 life, which the cast
row prices as "paying this costs you 1 life". At 3 life or less, and on any life-loop board above,
that row is "Cast nothing right now", whatever the spell does.
SIEGE-GANG COMMANDER brings FOUR BODIES (a 2/2 plus three 1/1 Goblins) - your board and blockers.
"{1}{R}, Sacrifice a Goblin: 2 damage" is a REMOVAL row, not a clock: take it only when the row
reads "- DIES" against one of their creatures, or when its "they would be at N" is 0 or less. At any
other N the Goblin is worth more as a body, and a row tagged "[repeat: activated this turn N times
already]" means you have already answered this question this turn - stop and move the turn on.
Attack with all four the turn they can.
RORIX BLADEWING is a 6/5 with FLYING and HASTE: cast him and attack that turn and every turn
after. HE IS LEGENDARY AND YOU RUN TWO - casting the second ends the turn with the same board for
six mana, so keep it for the turn the first dies.
HAMMER OF BOGARDAN is a sorcery dealing 3; from the graveyard your upkeep offers "Put a card into
hand with Hammer of Bogardan [cost: {2}{R}{R}{R}]". Five to return plus three to recast is EIGHT
MANA for 3 damage - never let it push a Siege-Gang or a Stone Rain out of a turn, and with either
body in hand and the lands to cast it, pass on the return. THE EMPTY-HAND RULE is the half
that wins games: when "Your hand" reads "(0 cards): (none)" - or holds only a land spell the land
plan's two numbers have killed - and "Mana available:" is 8 or more, TAKE THE RETURN EVERY UPKEEP
and recast in main phase 1 - the only rate you have then.
PYRITE SPELLBOMB: cast for {1} on any spare-mana turn and leave it as a loaded gun for their first
2-toughness creature; draw mode only when their line reads 0 creatures.
THEY COUNTER when "Their graveyard" names Cancel, Essence Scatter, Dream Fracture or Fall of the
Gavel, "Their untapped sources:" reads 2 or more and "Opponent hand size:" is 1 or more. They answer
the FIRST spell of your turn, so with mana for two spells cast the CHEAP one FIRST as bait and the
threat SECOND: RORIX AND SIEGE-GANG ARE NEVER THE FIRST SPELL OF A TURN there, whatever the cast
order says, and a turn with only one castable spell holds the body instead. "Hold mana for their
turn" is not a play here -
nothing but Starstorm is an instant.

=== COMBAT: ATTACKING ===
RACE CHECK first, and only when your attack list holds exactly ONE creature and it is Rorix or
Siege-Gang. Their turns to kill you = "Your life:" divided by the "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN" line's own
"for up to N" (that figure, never a power you add up); yours = "Opponent life:" divided by your
attacker's power. If THEIR number is smaller or equal, "ATTACK: none" and keep the body home to
block - unless "for up to N" exceeds your life PLUS that creature's toughness, when holding it home
does not change the turn you die and you send it.
WALL CHECK, before the floor. A "[defender]" creature CANNOT ATTACK BUT BLOCKS NORMALLY, and one on
their line does block - it is named in the "their untapped blockers" tag of every attacker row it
can take. While those rows read "(blocking trigger: they may gain 2)", each creature you declare
hands them up to 2 life per blocker they use and your 1/1s and 2/2 kill nothing through a 0/4:
write "Wall: <n> attackers x 2 = <2n> life to them; ATTACK: none", and kill Perimeter Captain or
Pride Guardian first. With a LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER line up as well, every one of those points
comes off YOUR total too.
ATTACK FLOOR, first case that applies:
 - Wall check fired -> "ATTACK: none".
 - Blastminer alone on the list -> he stays home while a nonbasic "[opponent's battlefield]" land
   sits on his target rows; otherwise read HIS OWN tag and SEND HIM unless a parenthesis says he
   dies (every blocker priced "(neither dies)" means he loses nothing).
 - Any other creature listed -> send EVERY creature listed except Blastminer. The list already
   dropped everything that cannot legally attack, so do not re-check summoning sickness or tapped
   status, and never hold back because their creatures read "[tapped ...]".
 - Otherwise "ATTACK: none" only when every listed attacker would die and kill nothing, or under
   the crack-back exception below.
BLOCKER TAGS: a name-by-name list of three blockers is N=3; the collapsed form reads "they have N
untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest X", sometimes with a GANG BLOCK clause. Each
parenthesis prices ONE blocker; GANG BLOCK measures your toughness against the SUM.
 - A GANG BLOCK clause, or 2 or more blockers named -> that creature stays home. A GOBLIN token
   goes anyway; RORIX goes only when every blocker named is grounded; SIEGE-GANG never goes, its
   sacrifice ability needs it alive.
 - N is 1 and no GANG BLOCK clause -> the parenthesis is the whole story; use it.
CRACK-BACK EXCEPTION, outranking "send every creature listed": when "for up to N" is at or above
"Your life:" (it says "that would KILL you"), every creature you send is a blocker you will not
have - attacking taps it, and that line does not know what you are about to declare. Keep home
whatever blocks the biggest attackers; send only vigilance, or a Rorix whose 6 is lethal THIS turn.
A one-row attack list after your own Starstorm is your last body: if its tag says it dies and kills
nothing, "ATTACK: none".

=== COMBAT: BLOCKING ===
Take any block where your creature kills theirs and lives, and a "(both die)" with a GOBLIN token
always. Do NOT take "(both die)" with SIEGE-GANG or RORIX - your only bodies, and Siege-Gang's
ability needs it alive; send a Goblin, or take the damage while "you would be at N" is above 5. The
one "(both die)" the Commander does take is against a token-MAKER.
ONE BLOCKER STOPS THE WHOLE OF ONE ATTACKER'S DAMAGE; a second and third on the same attacker stop
nothing more and die with the first. Read "you would be at N" first:
 - N above 5 and every row on that attacker reads "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> "BLOCKS:
   none".
 - N at 5 or below -> ONE Goblin on the attacker with the largest "deals N", a second only on a
   DIFFERENT attacker; never two on one unless the row says "(both die)" for the pair.
WHEN THE HEADER SAYS LETHAL the arithmetic is the whole question, not the trade. Narrow first: an
attacker tagged "[NONE of your available blockers can block this ...]", or missing from your
blocker's "may block" line, is not on the menu. Among the rest, block the LARGEST "deals N" even
when the trade line says your blocker dies; if an attacker IS on a "may block" line you CAN block
it.
A "[GANG BLOCK: ...enough to kill this attacker]" clause on the header is a different question from
damage: when that attacker is the only one hitting you or is named on the DRAW PUNISHERS line, and
your life minus its "deals N" is 5 or less, put every blocker the clause counts on it, Goblins
first.

=== MULLIGAN AND DISCARD ===
"counted by the engine: N lands, M spells" is the land count - never recount. "Playing every land
in this hand would cover the cost of: ..." is the mana check: if it names a card, this hand ACTS.
 - Two or more lands and at least one spell -> keep at every "(keeping N)", UNLESS the header reads
   5 or more lands and exactly 1 spell: ship at (keeping 6) and (keeping 5), keep at 4 or lower.
 - One land -> keep when the coverage line names a card; ship at (keeping 6) or (keeping 5) when it
   reads "would not cover any spell", keep at (keeping 4) or lower.
 - Zero lands ships at any N; all lands and no spells ships at any N above 4.
BOTTOMING keeps in this PREFERENCE ORDER: the lands covering the cheapest spells you keep (three if
the N allows, two at 4 or less); then the one Starstorm; then your cheapest spells; then the rest
by cost. What is last in it goes under, even a land or the Starstorm. CLEANUP DISCARD reads
it from the end - "PUT: n, m", most expensive first: the priciest spell your lands cannot cast in
two turns, then a duplicate already in hand (marked "(copy 2 of 3 in your hand)"), then a land
beyond the third. Never the only Starstorm, never a castable card while a duplicate is listed.

=== PLAN LINES AND THE HOLD ROW ===
A PLAN line names the NEXT ACTION - a land to destroy, a creature to kill, a body to add; there is
no "the game is lost". When the last menu row begins "Hold priority: pass now, and do not ask me
again ...", take THAT row at the FIRST such ask of the turn: "Cast nothing right now" answers one
window and the list returns at the next seam; the hold row answers all of them.

=== SITUATIONS ===
- Opponent at 16, their board empty, a Spellbomb loaded, no creature to shoot -> hold it; if their
  line reads 0 creatures and 2 lands and a land-kill of yours resolved, the face IS the clock.
- Two non-defender creatures on their line, Starstorm in hand, neither Rorix nor Siege-Gang on your
  battlefield -> Starstorm, before the Commander. If Rorix is already attacking for 6, hold it.
