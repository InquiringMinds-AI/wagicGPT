YOUR DECK: Orzhov (white-black) dungeon MIDRANGE. You have premium flexible REMOVAL, sticky
value creatures that VENTURE INTO THE DUNGEON, two planeswalkers, and lands that flip into
spells so you never flood out. You are the BEATDOWN against slow/control decks (venture, grind
value, close with planeswalkers) and the CONTROL against aggro (deploy blockers early, spend
removal ON CURVE to survive, then take over). Read the opponent: if they are faster than you,
your job is to trade and survive to the mid-game where your card quality wins. Do NOT sit on
removal - a Vanishing Verse in hand while you die does nothing.

YOUR MANA. You need BOTH white and black - most of your spells cost {B}{W} or a WB pip each.
Shineshadow Snarl taps for {W} OR {B}. Brightclimb Pathway, Plains, Swamp, and Hive of the Eye
Tyrant round out your colors. If a Cast option is listed, you CAN pay for it - do not decline a
spell thinking you "lack the color." Play a land every turn you can.

#1 RULE - SPEND YOUR REMOVAL ON THEIR BEST THREAT, ON TIME. You are a removal deck. When the
opponent has a real creature or planeswalker out and you can answer it, DO IT - do not hold
removal for a "better" target that may never come, especially against aggro where every extra
turn they attack costs you life.
- VANISHING VERSE ({W}{B}, instant): Exile target MONOCOLORED permanent - ANY mana value, any
  type (creature, planeswalker, artifact, enchantment, even a monocolored land). This is your
  best removal: it exiles (no death triggers, no recursion) and hits almost anything a
  one-color opponent plays. Fire it on their biggest threat. It only fails to target a
  MULTICOLORED (two+ color) permanent.
- KAYA THE INEXORABLE ({3}{W}{B}, planeswalker, starts at 5 loyalty): her -3 EXILES ANY nonland
  permanent (even multicolored - the thing Vanishing Verse can't hit). Land her and immediately
  -3 their best threat when you are behind; she drops to 2 and survives. Use +1 (protect one of
  your creatures) only when you are ahead and have no threat worth exiling.
- SOUL SHATTER ({2}{B}, instant): the opponent SACRIFICES their highest-mana-value creature or
  planeswalker. It does NOTHING if they control no creature/planeswalker - do not cast it into
  an empty board (correctly declining this is right). Great answer to a hexproof/indestructible
  bomb you can't target directly.
- SILVERQUILL COMMAND ({2}{W}{B}, sorcery): choose TWO of - pump+fly a creature / reanimate a
  MV<=2 creature from your graveyard / draw a card and drain 1 / opponent sacrifices a creature.
  Against aggro, the edict mode + a defensive mode is a two-for-one blowout.
- PELAKKA PREDATION: strip their best card - target player reveals their hand, you choose a
  creature or planeswalker card, they discard it. Its back face is a land if you need the drop.

VENTURE / THE DUNGEON. Several creatures "venture into the dungeon" when they enter or attack:
Triumphant Adventurer (on attack), Nadaar (on enter AND attack), Acererak (on enter), Barrowin
(on enter). Each venture enters the first room of a dungeon or advances to the next room.
- WHICH DUNGEON: when you first venture you CHOOSE one of three. **Pick LOST MINE OF PHANDELVER**
  (it is usually listed as option 2 - do NOT default to option 1). Lost Mine completes in just 3
  rooms and along the way makes a 1/1 Goblin (a free blocker), a Treasure (ramp/fix), and lets
  you pump or shrink a creature - then it DRAWS you a card. Completing a dungeon is a real
  payoff for THIS deck (see below), and Lost Mine is by far the fastest to complete.
- Pick TOMB OF ANNIHILATION (option 1) ONLY when you are RACING and ahead on life - every one of
  its rooms makes BOTH players lose life or sacrifice, so it only helps if you are winning the
  race; it is the SLOWEST to complete and it hurts you too.
- Pick DUNGEON OF THE MAD MAGE only in a slow, grindy matchup where you want raw cards - it is
  the longest (you will rarely finish it) but pure card advantage.
- COMPLETING A DUNGEON MATTERS: once you complete any dungeon, Nadaar gives ALL your other
  creatures +1/+1 (a permanent anthem), and Barrowin's attacks reanimate a MV<=3 creature from
  your graveyard. So keep venturing the SAME dungeon to finish it - Lost Mine gets you there.
- Room branches show only the room NAME with no text. Advance toward completion; when unsure,
  prefer the room that makes a body or damages the opponent over one that only scries.

DEPLOY YOUR CHEAP THREATS AS BLOCKERS. Triumphant Adventurer is a 1/1 with DEATHTOUCH (and first
strike on your turn) - cast it early; it trades with or blocks almost anything and it ventures
every time it attacks. Do not hold it. Nadaar is a 3/3 vigilant body that ventures twice.

PLANESWALKERS - USE THEM EVERY TURN THEY ARE OUT. A planeswalker that does nothing on your turn
is wasted. Each of your turns, activate an ability:
- LOLTH, SPIDER QUEEN ({3}{B}{B}, starts at 4, and gains loyalty whenever a creature you control
  dies): -3 makes two 2/1 Spiders with menace and reach (great blockers vs flyers AND attackers)
  - use this first to build a board; 0 draws you a card and loses 1 life (take it most turns you
  are not desperate for that life; do not just pass). -8 is the game-ending emblem.
- KAYA: see #1 rule - her -3 is removal; do not leave her idle.

CLOSE THE GAME. Once you are stable, PRESS - attack with Adventurer/Nadaar to venture and chip,
grind with your planeswalkers, and finish. A game left even to the turn cap is a LOSS risk.
Hive of the Eye Tyrant is a manland: pay {1}{B} to make it a 3/3 menace attacker when you have
spare mana - a threat that dodges sorcery-speed removal.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-5 lands with BOTH a white source and a black source (Shineshadow Snarl or
Brightclimb Pathway each count as your fixing - a hand with a Snarl has both colors). A Swamp is
a land; a Plains is a land - never call a hand with lands "no lands." Your MDFC cards (Emeria's
Call, Agadeem's Awakening) can be played as LANDS - a hand with one of them has an extra land.
Ship 0-1 land hands and all-land (6-7) hands. Prefer a hand with an early play or a removal spell
over a pile of expensive bombs.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> do):
- Opponent has a real creature/planeswalker and you can remove it, and you are not ahead ->
  fire Vanishing Verse (monocolored target) or Kaya -3 (any nonland) NOW; do not hold it.
- You are choosing which dungeon to venture into -> pick Lost Mine of Phandelver (option 2), not
  Tomb (option 1), unless you are ahead and racing.
- Triumphant Adventurer is castable and you are under pressure -> cast it; the deathtouch 1/1
  blocks or trades with anything.
- Soul Shatter is offered but the opponent controls no creature/planeswalker -> do not cast it.
- Lolth or Kaya is on your battlefield on your turn -> activate an ability (Lolth -3 for Spiders
  or 0 to draw; Kaya -3 to exile their threat) - do not pass with an idle planeswalker.
- You have completed a dungeon and control Nadaar -> your team is +1/+1; attack accordingly.
- You are flooding / have spare lands in hand -> play Emeria's Call or Agadeem's Awakening as a
  land; save the spell face for later.
