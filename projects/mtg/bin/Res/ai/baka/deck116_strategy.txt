TIME OF NEED - Simic (green-blue) CHEAT-INTO-PLAY. You win by putting a giant creature onto the
battlefield WITHOUT casting it, then attacking with it.

IDENTITY AND ROLE
Your deck has two halves and they are useless apart. The ENABLERS (Elvish Piper, Quicksilver
Amulet) put creatures onto the battlefield from your hand for almost no mana. The PAYOFFS
(Kozilek, Ulamog, Emrakul, Sheoldred, Jin-Gitaxias) are far too expensive to ever cast. Your job
every game is: get an enabler onto the battlefield, keep it alive, then dump a monster with it and
swing. Everything else in the deck exists to buy the turns that takes.

RULE #1 - THE FIVE LEGENDS CAN NEVER BE CAST. THEY ARE CHEAT-ONLY CARDS.
Kozilek {10}, Ulamog {11}, Emrakul {15}, Jin-Gitaxias {8}{U}{U}, Sheoldred {5}{B}{B}.
You have 24 lands and no rituals. You will never reach ten mana, and you have NO black source at
all, so Sheoldred is literally uncastable. A legend in your hand does NOTHING until Elvish Piper
or Quicksilver Amulet is on the battlefield.
THE FORBIDDEN THOUGHT: "I will ramp toward casting Ulamog / Kozilek / Emrakul next turn." That
plan never completes. Playing lands and casting Farseek to "get closer" to {11} is a wasted turn.
DO THIS INSTEAD: whenever you hold a legend and have no enabler on the battlefield, spend the turn
on an ENABLER or a real body - Cast Quicksilver Amulet, Cast Elvish Piper, Cast Kitchen Finks,
Cast Thragtusk - never on another search spell.

RULE #2 - ENABLER FIRST, MONSTER SECOND. THIS ORDER IS THE WHOLE DECK.
When your list offers both "Cast Quicksilver Amulet {4}" (or "Cast Elvish Piper {3}{g}") AND a
search spell (Time of Need, Fabricate, Farseek), ALWAYS CAST THE ENABLER. Reasons, in your words:
- The enabler is live with any of your 20 creatures. The monster is a dead card without it.
- The enabler must survive a full turn cycle before you can use it, so every turn you delay
  casting it is a turn added to your clock.
Corollary you must apply literally: DO NOT cast Fabricate to search for a Quicksilver Amulet when
a Quicksilver Amulet is ALREADY IN YOUR HAND. Cast the one you are holding.
Corollary: once ONE enabler is on the battlefield, a second Fabricate/Amulet is low value - cast a
creature instead.

RULE #3 - HOW THE CHEAT ACTUALLY WORKS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT DO.
Elvish Piper: {G}, Tap. Quicksilver Amulet: {4}, Tap. Both read "put a creature card from your
hand onto the battlefield." The option appears in your numbered list as
"Put in Play with Elvish Piper targeting <creature>". When you see that line, TAKE IT, and target
the biggest creature in your hand.
- The creature is PUT onto the battlefield, NOT CAST. Its "When you cast this spell" trigger DOES
  NOT HAPPEN. Kozilek does NOT draw you four cards. Ulamog does NOT destroy a permanent. Emrakul
  does NOT give you an extra turn. Do not plan around those triggers - you will never see them.
- Everything else works normally. ENTERS-THE-BATTLEFIELD triggers fire (Thragtusk gains you 5,
  Kitchen Finks gains you 2, Pelakka Wurm gains you 7). ANNIHILATOR fires when the creature
  ATTACKS, and that is the win: Kozilek and Ulamog make the defender sacrifice FOUR permanents,
  Emrakul SIX, before damage.
- The creature can attack the turn after it arrives. So the plan is: cheat it in, then next turn
  ATTACK WITH IT EVERY TURN. A 12/12 with annihilator 4 wins in two swings.
- Both enablers need to have been on the battlefield since your last turn began before you can use
  them. The activation is a PRIORITY option, not a main-phase cast option - it can appear in your
  upkeep or in your main phase, so read every priority list you are given instead of passing.

RULE #4 - ELVISH PIPER NEVER ATTACKS AND NEVER BLOCKS. NOT EVEN ONCE.
Elvish Piper is a 1/1 body and your best engine card. In a block list it will be offered to you
like this: "B1. Elvish Piper (1/1) - may block A1 (both die), A2 (your blocker dies, attacker
lives)". BOTH of those outcomes lose you the game, because a Piper alive next turn puts a 12/12
onto the battlefield and a Piper in the graveyard does nothing.
DO THIS: when Elvish Piper is your only possible blocker, answer "no blockers" and TAKE THE
DAMAGE. This holds even against infect creatures and poison counters. It holds at any life total
above the incoming damage. The ONLY exception: the incoming damage kills you THIS combat and the
block prevents it - then chump and survive.
Same rule at the attack seam: never declare Elvish Piper as an attacker. Quicksilver Amulet is an
artifact and cannot be attacked into, which is why it is the safer enabler when you hold both.

RULE #5 - DEPLOY A BODY EVERY TURN YOU CAN. A SEARCH SPELL IS NOT A TURN.
Your losses all look the same: turn 8, six lands, zero creatures on your battlefield, a hand full
of tutored monsters. Time of Need, Fabricate, Farseek and Explore do not affect the board.
THE FLOOR: in any casting window where a CREATURE or an ENABLER is offered - "Cast Kitchen Finks
{1}{g/w}{g/w} (3/2)", "Cast Thragtusk {4}{g} (5/3)", "Cast Pelakka Wurm {4}{g}{g}{g} (7/7)",
"Cast Elvish Piper {3}{g} (1/1)", "Cast Quicksilver Amulet {4}" - CAST IT. Prefer it over any
search spell, at any life total, on any turn.
Cast a search spell only when the window offers NO creature and NO enabler you can pay for.
Kitchen Finks is your best early play: {1}{g/w}{g/w} is payable entirely with green mana (each
{g/w} takes either colour), it is a 3/2 that gains you 2 life, and it comes BACK as a 2/1 the
first time it dies. Never hold it.

RULE #6 - COUNT YOUR LANDS BEFORE YOU MULLIGAN. YOUR LAND NAMES ARE NOT ALL "LAND".
You lost a whole game to seven mulligans and a zero-card hand, every time saying "this hand has
zero lands" about hands that held two or three.
These are ALL LANDS and they all appear on your hand line with the word "land" in brackets:
  Forest (land: taps for {G}) - green
  Island (land: taps for {U}) - blue
  Tropical Island (land: taps for {U}{G}) - BOTH colours, your best land
  Hinterland Harbor - taps for {G} or {U}; enters untapped if you already control a Forest or
    Island, otherwise tapped
  Glimmerpost (land) - taps for {1} colourless, and gains you 1 life for each Glimmerpost/Locus
    on the battlefield when it enters. It is a REAL LAND. It just makes colourless mana.
DO THIS at the mulligan: count every hand card whose line contains "(land". Two or more lands with
at least one green source (Forest, Tropical Island, Hinterland Harbor) is a KEEP. Three lands is
always a KEEP. Mulligan only a hand with one land or none, and NEVER mulligan more than twice -
a 5-card hand with lands beats a 7-card hand you shuffle away.

RULE #7 - WHAT TO SEARCH FOR, WHEN YOU DO SEARCH.
- FABRICATE {2}{U} finds an artifact. The only artifact in your deck is Quicksilver Amulet. Cast
  it ONLY when you hold no enabler at all.
- TIME OF NEED {1}{G} finds a legendary creature. Cast it ONLY when an enabler is ALREADY on your
  battlefield or in your hand. Pick the biggest one you can dump: Emrakul (15/15 flying,
  annihilator 6, and coloured removal spells cannot touch it) > Kozilek (12/12, annihilator 4) >
  Ulamog (10/10 indestructible, annihilator 4) > Sheoldred (6/6, steals your creatures back from
  the graveyard every upkeep and makes them sacrifice) > Jin-Gitaxias.
- FARSEEK {1}{G} puts an Island or Tropical Island onto the battlefield tapped. It cannot find a
  Forest. It does NOT draw a card.
- EXPLORE {1}{G} draws a card and lets you play an extra land. URBAN EVOLUTION {3}{G}{U} draws
  three and an extra land. Cast these when you have nothing better - never over a creature.
- In a search list you will be shown your whole library, most of it marked
  "[does NOT qualify]". Pick ONE card, from the lines marked [eligible]. Do not list several.

RULE #8 - TRUST THE NUMBERS THE GAME PRINTS FOR YOU.
The board shows "Mana available: N total (N untapped sources ...; colours you can make: {g}{u})".
That number N is how much mana you have. Do not recount your lands and do not re-derive whether
you can pay - if a Cast line is in your numbered list, the game has already checked it and will
tap your lands for you. Read the printed (X/Y) on a creature and swing with it.

SITUATION -> PLAY
- Enabler on the battlefield, monster in hand -> take the Put in Play option, target the biggest.
- Monster on the battlefield -> attack with it every turn. Annihilator strips their board first.
- Enabler in hand, monster in hand -> cast the ENABLER.
- No enabler anywhere, creature in hand -> cast the CREATURE.
- No enabler and no creature -> cast Fabricate (for the Amulet) or Explore/Urban Evolution.
- Elvish Piper is your only blocker -> no blockers, take the damage.
- Big blocker available (Kozilek, Thragtusk, Pelakka Wurm) and the line says "you kill it, your
  blocker lives" -> block.
- Opening hand with two or more lands including a green source -> keep.
