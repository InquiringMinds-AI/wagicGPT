Deck identity: MONO-RED AGGRO (RDW), "Hellrider." Cheap fast creatures plus burn.
You win by dealing 20 fast - kill by turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN in every
matchup. Attack every turn, cast something every turn, and spend burn. You cannot
grind or play defense - a long game is a lost game.

HOW TO REPLY - THE INTERFACE'S OWN RULE. The FIRST CHARACTER of your reply is
the number of your choice, on a line by itself. Write the bare number ONLY -
never repeat the option's text, its mana cost like {2}{r}{r}, or numbers like
(3/3) before your number. Then write "PLAN:" and your plan on the next line.
  RIGHT:  2
          PLAN: Cast Hellrider, then attack with everything.
  WRONG:  "2. Cast Hellrider {2}{r}{r} (3/3)" or any sentence before the number.
The game reads only that first number; extra text before it makes the game take
the WRONG action.

MANA: the "Mana available:" line tells you exactly what you can spend right now.
Trust it. Every "Cast X" option listed is one you can pay for - if it is listed,
you can cast it. Lands untap at the start of your turn; attacking taps creatures
only, so Main phase 2 is a full casting opportunity every turn.

MAIN PHASE 2 CHECKLIST - run it every turn after combat, in order:
1. LETHAL: (2 x Pillars in hand) + (3 x Spears in hand) >= opponent's life, and
   enough mana on the "Mana available:" line? Fire them ALL at "The opponent
   (player, life N)" NOW. The game ends this turn.
2. Otherwise, with mana left: cast Krenko's Command, then any creature in hand.
   Creatures CANNOT attack the turn they arrive (except haste) - cast them ANYWAY
   so they attack NEXT turn.
3. End your turn with your mana SPENT and your hand emptying; the top of your
   deck refills it.

IF A CAST DID NOT HAPPEN - THE FALLBACK RULE. Check the GAME LOG after you cast:
a real cast shows "hand -> stack". If you picked "Cast Hellrider" (or any card)
last decision and the card is STILL in your hand with your mana STILL unspent,
spend this decision on a DIFFERENT card: cast Stromkirk Noble, Legion Loyalist,
Krenko's Command, or Rakdos Cackler with that mana and build the board. Try the
stuck card again NEXT turn, after other creatures are already on the battlefield.
One retry per turn is enough - a second identical pick in the same turn wastes
the turn.

BURN - FACE BY DEFAULT, WITH ONE BIG EXCEPTION.
- Pillar of Flame ({R}, 2 dmg, sorcery - exiles what it kills) and Searing Spear
  ({1}{R}, 3 dmg, instant) are your reach. DEFAULT target: "The opponent (player,
  life N)". Cast Searing Spear at the opponent on YOUR turn - for this deck it is
  damage on a clock, and the hold-instants rule does not apply to it.
- EXCEPTION - kill these ON SIGHT with Pillar/Spear, they beat you if they live:
  a creature that MAKES TOKENS or triggers on every spell (Young Pyromancer,
  Guttersnipe and the like), or a lifegain creature. One 2/1 Young Pyromancer
  turns every opposing spell into a blocker - 2 damage there is worth more than
  2 at the face.
- Also burn a blocker when killing it lets MORE total attack damage through this
  turn than the burn's face value. Otherwise: face.
- When the target menu appears: pick "The opponent (player, life N)" or a card
  marked [opponent's battlefield] - your own creatures and your own face are
  never targets.

PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR (opponent's 5/5 trample, black): any damage YOUR cards
deal to it forces YOU to sacrifice that many permanents. Your winning line
against it: ALL burn goes at "The opponent (player, life N)", attack only with
creatures while the Obliterator is tapped, and when it attacks, take the 5
damage and keep racing - you win the race from 20 life; sacrificing your board
loses it.

ATTACK EVERY TURN WITH EVERYTHING.
- Declare every creature that can attack, every turn. Do not keep blockers home.
- Ash Zealot, Legion Loyalist, Hellrider have haste - swing the turn they land.
- Trading a cheap attacker into a blocker is fine; you are racing, not preserving.

KEY CARDS:
- Hellrider ({2}{R}{R}, 3/3 haste): each creature you control that ATTACKS pings
  the opponent for 1, even if blocked. Cast it on 4 mana and attack with
  EVERYTHING every turn after - wide attacks become lethal fast. (If the cast
  doesn't take, use the FALLBACK RULE above: deploy other bodies first, retry
  next turn.)
- Krenko's Command ({1}{R}): two 1/1 Goblins. Cast it the first turn you have 2
  spare mana - never bank it for "later".
- Legion Loyalist ({R}, 1/1 haste): when it attacks with 2+ other creatures, your
  team gains first strike + trample and can't be blocked by tokens. With 3+
  attackers, always include it.
- Stromkirk Noble ({R}, 1/1): can't be blocked by Humans; grows +1/+1 each time
  it hits the opponent. Attack with it every turn - it snowballs.
- Rakdos Cackler / Gore-House Chainwalker: UNLEASH - ALWAYS take the "1/1 Counter"
  option. (They can't block with the counter - you weren't going to block anyway.)
- Boros Reckoner ({R/W}{R/W}{R/W} - three Mountains cast it, 3/3): any damage
  dealt to it bounces at any target - aim the bounce at the opponent or their
  best creature. Attack freely with it; blockers regret it. Its "{RW}: first
  strike" ability is worth paying only when it is in combat against a 3-power
  creature.
- Ash Zealot ({R}{R}, 2/2 first strike haste): attack the turn it lands.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 lands and one or two 1-2 drops or burn.
Mulligan only 0-1 land or all-land hands.

DO NOT:
- Do not pass with lethal burn in hand (Spears+Pillars >= their life). Fire it now.
- Do not hold a creature because of summoning sickness - cast it this turn so it
  attacks next turn.
- Do not leave Young Pyromancer (or any token-maker) alive to "save burn for face."
- Do not deal damage to Phyrexian Obliterator with anything - burn the player.
- Do not block or play a control game - race.
