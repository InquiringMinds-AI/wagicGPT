#subsequent time."
#
#ROOT CAUSE (card script, not engine). The primitive's `afterrevealed` leg fired
#the moment option two emptied the reveal zone and granted the source five
#conditional abilities of the shape
#   if type(<TYPE>[tobecast]|mylibrary)~morethan~0 then ability$! ...
#      target(<TYPE>[tobecast]|mylibrary) moveto(hand) !$ controller
#`tobecast` does not exist anywhere in the engine (grep src/ include/: zero hits),
#so `becomes(tobecast)` marked nothing and `[tobecast]` was ignored by the target
#chooser - the guard therefore counted EVERY matching card in the library and
#stayed true forever, re-arming a forced, non-cancelable "put <type> in hand" menu
#whose target chooser points at LIBRARY cards the player cannot click. Answering it
#re-armed it; the phase could never advance (menuObject + targetChooser hold every
#advance path, incl. userRequestNextGamePhase). "Worked every subsequent time" =
#any run in which no revealed card of a cast type was selected leaves the guard at
#zero and the leg never arms.
#
#Fix: express the Oracle with constructs the player can answer - the "put into
#hand" happens from the REVEAL display (where the cards are visible/clickable),
#not by fishing them back out of the library afterwards; the afterrevealed leg and
#the identically-broken aicode= substitute are dropped.
#
#The fixture drives a comma-free clone of the real card (suite zone lists split on
#",", so "Hurkyl, Master Wizard" cannot be placed by name): Lexicon Hurkyl Wizard,
#id 2100000986 in test/lexicon/test_primitives.txt, carrying the same auto= line.
#realgame = real-play automation, so a wedged phase shows up as a phase mismatch.
#Top of library = last listed. Mind Stone is the noncreature spell that arms the
#end-step restriction; Sol Ring is taken to hand, the other four go to the bottom.
#RED on the pre-fix script: "phase problem. Expected [Cleanup](12), got [End](11)"
#plus Sol Ring still in the library - the exact soft lock the owner hit.
