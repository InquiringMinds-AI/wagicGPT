#W42-D6 regression. The [repeat: activated this turn N times already]
#countable AIPlayerGPT renders on an activation option reads
#ActivatedAbility::counters, and the emit floor is 2 - so the annotation only
#exists if a repeatable ability's counters really advance, ON THE SAME
#permanent, across a whole turn.
#The wave-41 docket claimed counters does NOT advance for the land-animation
#class (Hive of the Eye Tyrant's {3}{B}: transforms((...)) ueot). It does:
#the layer object is a GenericActivatedAbility and ActivatedAbility::
#activateAbility() increments it on every activation, resetting only at
#MTG_PHASE_AFTER_EOT. This fixture pins that, and would go red if a future
#change made the second or third activation land on a different ability
#object, refuse outright, or reset the count mid-turn.
#Three animations of the SAME Hive in one upkeep: exactly {9}{B}{B}{B} is
#seeded, so a run that manages only two leaves mana behind and the manapool
#assertion fails. `choice 1` picks the animation off the two-item menu
#({T}: Add {B} is item 0).
[INIT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:hive of the eye tyrant
manapool:{9}{B}{B}{B}
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
hive of the eye tyrant
choice 1
hive of the eye tyrant
choice 1
hive of the eye tyrant
choice 1
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:hive of the eye tyrant
manapool:{0}
ability:hive of the eye tyrant|menace
[PLAYER2]
[END]
