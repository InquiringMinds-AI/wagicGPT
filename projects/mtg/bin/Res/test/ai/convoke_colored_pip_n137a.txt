# LANDS AT ALL. The five white convokers are the entire payment: one of them must
# pay the {W} pip and the other four the {4}. There is no mana anywhere on the
# board, so if the {W} pip cannot be convoked the spell is simply unpayable.
#
# PRE-FIX this FAILS. Convoke's per-creature colour scan broke on the FIRST index
# in all three of its copies (Convoke::getReduction forward pass, its backward

# Cadets stripped the {4} (and then removed from an already-empty generic
# component - the shape behind N-137f's "ManaCost was not properly initialized"
# trace), left {W} unpaid with an empty pool, offerable() returned false on both
# passes and the Loxodon was never offered.
#
# POST-FIX the scan runs every colour: Cadet #1 matches and removes the {W} pip,
# Cadets #2-5 find no coloured pip left and take one generic each, toReduce
# reaches {0}, the cast is offered, paid entirely with creatures, and resolves.
#
# THE OFFER PROBE AND THE PAYMENT NOW SHARE ONE FUNCTION
# (convokeApplyOneCreature), which is the point: three hand-kept copies is how
# they drifted, and offer-vs-payment agreement is the legality oracle's whole
# invariant. This fixture exercises BOTH - offerable() to get the option, and
# getReduction()/doPay() to actually resolve it - so a fix landing on only one
# copy cannot pass it.
