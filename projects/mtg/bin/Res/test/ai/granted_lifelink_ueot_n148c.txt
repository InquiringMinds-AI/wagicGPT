# appears 21x in borderline.txt and 29x in mtg.txt.
#
# Stone Haven Pilgrim (2/2, `auto=@combat(attacking) source(this)

# && lifelink ueot`) plus Darksteel Citadel as the artifact that satisfies the
# restriction. The Pilgrim attacks an empty board: the pump fires (it deals 3,
# not 2), so the FIRST clause of the `&&` chain is live; the question this
# fixture answers is whether the SECOND clause - the granted lifelink - reaches
# the combat-damage life-gain path.
#
# EXPECTED IF THE ENGINE IS CORRECT: opponent 20 -> 17 and the Pilgrim's
# controller 20 -> 23.
# WITNESS FOR THE DEFECT (deck148 corpus 20260727): turn 5 Pilgrim deals 4
# (= 2 + 1 + 1, confirming the pump) and life 19 goes DOWN next, never +4;
# turn 7 deals 5 with no +5; vs146 turn 12 deals 4 to a Goblin, life 13 -> 12.
# Opponent lifegain lines DO render in those same logs, so the renderer is not
# the gap.

# candidate mechanisms the ledger names: if the opponent is at 17 but the
# controller is at 20, the `&&` chain's SECOND clause never applied or the
