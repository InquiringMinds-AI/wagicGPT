#Black Vise is a CHOOSE, not a TARGET. Oracle: "As Black Vise enters, choose
#an opponent." (The printed 1993 face says "target opponent" - pre-errata.)
#The script encodes that with notaTarget(opponent), which the ability factory
#treats as a choice: it sets bypassTC and NULLS the chooser's targetter, so
#PlayerTargetChooser::canTarget skips the whole protection/shroud/hexproof
#block. A plain target(opponent) would keep those checks.
#This fixture DISCRIMINATES the two. The opponent controls True Believer
#("You have shroud", abilities=playershroud, the engine's player-level
#untargetability). Under target() semantics the opponent would be an illegal
#candidate, MayAbility::Update would bail on validTargetsExist(), playerTarget
#would stay NULL and the Vise would be inert forever. Under choose semantics
#- which is what Oracle says and what the script says - the choice resolves
#onto the shrouded player anyway and the upkeep damage lands.
#Opponent holds 7 at their upkeep -> 7 - 4 = 3 damage, 20 -> 17.
#(Nothing here answers the chooser; it auto-resolves, one legal candidate.)
[INIT]
SECONDMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:black vise
inplay:mountain
[PLAYER2]
hand:act of treason,raging goblin,plains,swamp,forest,island,mind stone
inplay:true believer
[DO]
mountain
black vise
eot
next
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:mountain,black vise
[PLAYER2]
hand:act of treason,raging goblin,plains,swamp,forest,island,mind stone
inplay:true believer
life:17
[END]
