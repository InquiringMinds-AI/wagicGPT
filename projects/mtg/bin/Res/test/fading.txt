# Testing (keyword:fading)
# Permanent with fading must be sacrificed at the upkeep after its last counter is removed.
# Re-authored: the fade-counter-removal ability is activated during a MAIN phase (a
# priority-bearing step), not the untap step. No player has priority in untap
# (CR 500.3/502.4), so the old untap-step activation was illegal. Phyrexian Prowler's
# fade counters are emptied on turn 1; on the controller's NEXT upkeep the fading
# trigger cannot remove a counter and sacrifices it (proven at the following firstmain).
[INIT]
UNTAP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Phyrexian Prowler
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
next
next
next
Phyrexian Prowler
Phyrexian Prowler
Phyrexian Prowler
goto upkeep
next
goto upkeep
goto firstmain
[ASSERT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:Phyrexian Prowler
[PLAYER2]
[END]
