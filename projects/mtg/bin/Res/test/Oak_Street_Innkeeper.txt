#Testing Oak Street Innkeeper hexproof during opponent turn
#Re-authored: the opponent's Lightning Bolt is now cast on the OPPONENT's turn
#(player2's main phase), not during player1's cleanup step. No player has priority
#in cleanup (CR 514.3), so the old cleanup-step cast was illegal. Oak stays tapped
#from its turn-1 attack (creatures only untap in their controller's untap step), so
#on player2's turn Oak's 'not your turn' hexproof is active and the Bolt cannot
#target it -> it is redirected to player1.
[INIT]
combatattackers
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Oak Street Innkeeper
[PLAYER2]
hand:Lightning Bolt
inplay:Mountain
life:20
[DO]
Oak Street Innkeeper
eot
#advance into the opponent's (player2's) turn; Oak remains tapped -> hexproof on
goto firstmain
Mountain
Lightning Bolt
Oak Street Innkeeper
#hexproof: cannot target Oak, choose player1 instead
p1
goto untap
[ASSERT]
untap
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Oak Street Innkeeper
life:17
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Mountain
graveyard:Lightning Bolt
life:19
[END]
