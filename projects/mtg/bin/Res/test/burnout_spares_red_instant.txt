#Oracle (Scryfall, verified 2026-08-24): "Counter target instant spell IF IT'S
#BLUE" - the colour is a RESOLUTION condition on the effect, not a targeting
#restriction (CR 601.2c; the intervening-if rides the effect). So a red instant
#is a legal target and Burnout still resolves, is still paid for, and still goes
#to the graveyard, while the red instant resolves normally.
#This is the negative half of burnout_counters_blue_instant: it pins that the
#zone-explicit teach(instant[blue]|stack) did NOT become a colour-restricted
#chooser, which would have made the red spell untargetable instead of unaffected.
#Discriminator: Dangerous Wager is {1}{R} "Discard your hand, then draw two
#cards" - player 1's hand is empty after casting it, so the discard is a no-op
