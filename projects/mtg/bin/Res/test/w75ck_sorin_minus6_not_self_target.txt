#NAME: Sorin, Lord of Innistrad's -6 cannot take SORIN as one of its targets (#W75-CK P4)
#DESC: Wave-74 known-bugs P4 / deck126 HIGH-2, `126v152` deck126 seqs 38-40 (t18).
#DESC: The report read the board deltas as "the -6 charged THREE loyalty". The
#DESC: seat's own NARRATION is the ground truth and says otherwise:
#DESC:   "You used: -6: destroy up to three and reanimate with Sorin, Lord of
#DESC:    Innistrad targeting Sigarda, Champion of Light"
#DESC:   "Your Sorin, Lord of Innistrad lost 6 loyalty counters (now 0)"
#DESC:   "Your Sorin, Lord of Innistrad was put into your graveyard from the battlefield"
#DESC:   "Your Sorin, Lord of Innistrad entered the battlefield from your graveyard"
#DESC:   "Your Sorin, Lord of Innistrad got 3 loyalty counters (now 3)"
#DESC: The loyalty COST parse is therefore CORRECT - six charged, and the walker
#DESC: died as a state-based action. What produced the 6 -> 3 board reading, and
#DESC: the second loyalty ability the same turn, is that SORIN WAS ONE OF ITS
#DESC: OWN TARGETS: the script's `target(<upto:3>*[creature;planeswalker]|
#DESC: battlefield)` does not exclude the source, targets are chosen BEFORE the
#DESC: cost is paid, and `destroy and!(moveto(mybattlefield))!` then returned
#DESC: the dead walker as a FRESH card instance carrying its printed 3 loyalty.
#DESC: That also explains the -2 that followed: the once-per-turn latch
#DESC: (MTGAbility::isReactingToClick, which refuses when an ActivatedAbility
#DESC: whose `source == card` already has counters) is keyed on the OLD
#DESC: instance's ability objects, and the returned Sorin is a new object with
#DESC: fresh ones. That latch hole is general to any walker that returns itself
#DESC: and is NOT fixed here - see strategy-design/wave75/lane-CK.md.
#DESC: Oracle (Scryfall + the card's own text= line): "-6: Destroy up to three
#DESC: target creatures and/or OTHER planeswalkers." The missing word is `other`
#DESC: - card data, no engine change.
#DESC: The option SET a target spec produces is invisible to every zone
#DESC: assertion, so this pins it directly (assertcantarget, #W75-CK): Serra
#DESC: Angel must stay targetable - the fix must not shrink the legal set - and
#DESC: Sorin must not. RED on the same binary with the word removed again:
#DESC: "assertcantarget: 'sorin, lord of innistrad' expected 0 got 1".
