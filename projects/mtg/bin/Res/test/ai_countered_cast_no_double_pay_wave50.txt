#Wave-50 lane V (wave-49 engine seat D-4) -- a cast that gets COUNTERED must not
#be paid for a second time.
#
#SYMPTOM (corpus 20260827-094106, 12 of 234 non-X casts; 20260827-072004, 7):
#the AI seat casts a spell in its main phase, the opponent answers with a
#counterspell, and by the seat's next window TWICE the spell's pips are tapped
#with the difference floating in the pool ("Already in pool: {b}{w}") - the
#response mana is gone in the exact window the counter is on the stack.
#
#MECHANISM: AIPlayerBaka::computeActions' main-phase branch queues the cast's
#payment + click and returns WITHOUT clearing the nextCardToPlay member. The
#interrupt branch (entered when the opponent's counter opens a response window
#for this seat) reads a non-NULL nextCardToPlay as "already chosen this window",
#skips FindCardToPlay, and pays for it AGAIN from potential mana - the card is
#on the stack, so the cast click is a no-op and the mana floats until end of
#phase. Only visible when the remaining untapped sources can afford the cost a
#second time, which is why it correlated with counters on rich boards.
#
#WITNESS (both seats AI): P1 holds Grizzly Bears {1}{G} and a Giant Growth over
#four Forests; P2 holds Cancel over three Islands and controls a Grizzly Bears.
#Giant Growth is what makes P1's response window EXIST (its only legal target
#is the opponent's bear, so the oracle offers the window but the heuristic
#never casts it - a Fog here gets cast in that window and muddies the count).
#P1's Bears is cast (2 Forests tapped) and countered. PRE-FIX the response
#window re-pays the countered spell: all four Forests tapped, {G}{G} floating.
#POST-FIX exactly two Forests are tapped after the counter resolves.
