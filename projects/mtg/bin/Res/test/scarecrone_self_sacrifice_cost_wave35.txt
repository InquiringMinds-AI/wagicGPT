#Wave-35 trace audit b6 F1 -- an extra-cost payment whose ONLY legal payment is the
#ability's own source is never made, so the activation silently no-ops forever.
#
#SYMPTOM (corpus 20260820, trace 1787200425): the engine offered
#"Draw 1 with Scarecrone [cost: {1}, Sacrifice]" 12+ TIMES across turns 3-7. The model
#took it every time, the narration recorded it every time, and Scarecrone never left the
#battlefield (it even attacked). Free repeatable draw from the model's point of view, and
#18 of that file's 30 decisions were spent litigating the contradiction.
#
#ORACLE (scryfall, verified 2026-08-20): Scarecrone {3} Artifact Creature - Scarecrow 1/2,
#"{1}, Sacrifice a Scarecrow: Draw a card." / "{4}, {T}: Return target artifact creature
#card from your graveyard to the battlefield." The card SCRIPT is FAITHFUL -- the audit's
#companion claim (b2 C1 / b6 F1b: "real Oracle says an artifact creature") is WRONG, and
#no card-script change is warranted. Sacrificing Scarecrone ITSELF to its own ability is
#rules-legal: it is a Scarecrow, and the cost carries no "another".
#
#ROOT CAUSE: AIPlayerBaka::chooseCard -- the scan that AIPlayerBaka::chooseCostTarget and
#CanHandleCost both use -- skips `card == source` and `card == tc->source`. That exclusion
#is right for chooseCard's normal job (never target the spell being cast with its own
#effect) but wrong for a COST payment: when the ability's source is the only permanent the
#cost's TargetChooser accepts, the scan returns NULL, payTheManaCost returns false, no
#AIAction is queued, and the whole activation disappears with no fallback and no defer --
#while SacrificeCost::canPay() (which does NOT exclude the source) keeps reporting the
#activation as payable, so it is re-offered every priority window. AIPlayerGPT::
#chooseCostTarget mirrored the same exclusion, so both seats fizzled.
#
#FIX: cost payment legality is the TargetChooser's call. If the ordinary scan finds no
#payment, offer the source itself when it is on the battlefield (i.e. an activated ability
#of a permanent, never a spell paying with itself from hand) and the chooser accepts it.
#Applied at chooseCostTarget AND at the CanHandleCost probe, so Baka's efficiency gate
#stops zeroing these abilities out.
#
