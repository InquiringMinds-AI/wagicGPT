#W54-I (A11): dead tokens must survive TWO turn boundaries, not one.
#Raise the Alarm makes two Soldier tokens; Pyroclasm kills them the same
#turn (MTGTokensCleanup moves them graveyard -> garbage). Two `eot`s then
#cross two BEFORE_BEGIN boundaries. Before A11 `garbageLastTurn` aliased
#`garbage`, so the first boundary freed the tokens while every raw pointer
#taken during the turn (aura/equipment targets, imprints, AI-remembered
#cards, LKI) still named them; now the zone that was `garbage` lives on as
#`garbageLastTurn` for one more turn. The plain suite sees identical zones
#either way (the garbage zones are not asserted); the discriminating run is
#the ASAN suite, where any read through such a pointer is a use-after-free
#on the old binary.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:raise the alarm,pyroclasm
manapool:{1}{W}{1}{R}
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
raise the alarm
goto firstmain
goto firstmain
pyroclasm
goto firstmain
goto firstmain
eot
eot
[ASSERT]
UNTAP
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:raise the alarm,pyroclasm
[PLAYER2]
[END]
