#entered, instead of via a triggered ability that uses the stack. That stole
#the window to respond -- e.g. flickering the creature with Ephemerate to save
#it for a second ETB. Here P1 evokes Mulldrifter, then in response to the
#evoke-sacrifice trigger casts Ephemerate targeting it. Correct rules: the
#Mulldrifter is exiled+returned (a new object), the sacrifice trigger has no
#legal target, and the creature SURVIVES on the battlefield.
# https://github.com/WagicProject/wagic/issues/1129
[INIT]

no
no
yes

endinterruption
