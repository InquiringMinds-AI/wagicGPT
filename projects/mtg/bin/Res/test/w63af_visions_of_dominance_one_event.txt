#NAME: Visions of Dominance - the doubling is ONE counter event
#Oracle (Scryfall, 2026-09-05): "Put a +1/+1 counter on target creature, then double the number of
#+1/+1 counters on it."
#Same loop, same fix as Invigorating Surge: `thisforeach(counter{1/1.1}) counter(1/1)` (N one-counter
#events) -> `counter(1/1.counter{1/1})` (one event of N). Hardened Scales is the discriminator.
#Grizzly Bears 2/2: first counter -> 2 (4/4); doubling N=2 -> one event of 2, +1 -> 5 -> 7/7.
#RED on the old script: 6 counters -> 8/8.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Hardened Scales,Grizzly Bears
hand:Visions of Dominance
manapool:{2}{G}
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
Visions of Dominance
Grizzly Bears
assertpt 7/7 grizzly bears
[ASSERT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Hardened Scales,Grizzly Bears
graveyard:Visions of Dominance
[PLAYER2]
[END]
