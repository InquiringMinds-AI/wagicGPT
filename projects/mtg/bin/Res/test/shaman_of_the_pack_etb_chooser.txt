#Shaman of the Pack - the ETB one-shot half of the as-enters-player-chooser
#class (Black Vise / The Rack are the persistent half). Oracle: "When Shaman
#of the Pack enters, target opponent loses life equal to the number of Elves
#you control." Script: auto=life:-type:elf:mybattlefield target(opponent)
#The parser gives that line the same catch-all wrap as Black Vise's chooser
#(GenericTargetAbility inside MayAbility(must)), so it is the same mechanism:
#a MANDATORY player choice with exactly one legal candidate. It auto-resolves
#- no menu answer, no avatar click scripted below - and the effect still lands.
#Shaman is itself an Elf, so X = 1: the opponent goes 20 -> 19.
[INIT]
SECONDMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:shaman of the pack
inplay:swamp,forest,mountain
[PLAYER2]
hand:act of treason,raging goblin
[DO]
swamp
forest
mountain
shaman of the pack
eot
next
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:swamp,forest,mountain,shaman of the pack
life:20
[PLAYER2]
hand:act of treason,raging goblin
life:19
[END]
