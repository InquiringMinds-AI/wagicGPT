#NAME: A MODAL spell's counter mode raises the countered register (W41 #W41-9)
#The wave-40 ledger left #3 (the "was COUNTERED by <spell>" narration register)
#unvalidated against a MODAL counter, and nominated Silverquill Command as the
#untested case.  That nomination is WRONG: Silverquill Command has no counter
#mode at all - its Oracle (Scryfall, verified 2026-08-23) is choose two of
#{+3/+3 and flying, reanimate mv<=2, target player draws and loses 1, target
#opponent sacrifices}, and the primitive in borderline.txt matches.  So the gap
#is closed here with a card that really is a modal counter: Blue Elemental
#Blast, whose choose-one menu was built in wave 40 (choice fizzle | choice
#destroy).  Mode index 0 is the counter leg.
#The control is in frame on purpose: the counterspell's OWN resolution must
#still read "resolved", so a fix that labels everything countered cannot pass.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:Raging Goblin
manapool:{R}
[PLAYER2]
hand:Blue Elemental Blast
manapool:{U}
[DO]
Raging Goblin
yes
Blue Elemental Blast
choice 0
Raging Goblin
endinterruption
[ASSERT]
