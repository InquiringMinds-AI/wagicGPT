#Wave-22 ledger ENGINE-F1 -- fetchland activations intermittently FIZZLE for the AI.
#
#SYMPTOM (corpus 20260723 deck135, primary driver of its 4/6 -> 1/6 record swing): the
#AI chose "Put in Play with <fetchland> targeting <land>" (a {T},Pay 1 life,Sacrifice
#activated ability), the decision was narrated as taken, but NOTHING resolved -- no life
#paid, no sacrifice, no land fetched. The fetchland stayed on the battlefield and the
#option re-armed next turn. Silent to the model (its own plan asserts the fetch succeeded),
#so it never re-cracks. deck135's whole plan is fetch-a-color-then-cast, so a fizzled fetch
#green-screws it for the game.
#
#ROOT CAUSE (predates wave-20/21; the check dates to 2016, commit 00adb20dc): a fetchland
#crack has no MANA cost ({T}/Life/Sacrifice are extra costs), so AIPlayerBaka::payTheManaCost
#reaches its zero-cost branch. That branch refused any LAND whose land-per-turn play was
#spent (canPutIntoZone == CANT_PLAY) -- meant to stop the AI replaying a land, but it also
#matched a fetchland ALREADY IN PLAY whose ability is being ACTIVATED. So whenever the AI
#had spent its land drop earlier that turn, payTheManaCost returned false, the crack
#AIAction was never queued, and the whole activation vanished. Intermittent because it only
#bites when a land was played before the crack ON THE SAME TURN.
#
#FIX: the land-per-turn restriction applies only to PLAYING a land from hand -- gate it on
#!target->isInPlay(observer) so activating an in-play land's ability is never refused.
#

#(spending the turn's land drop), then cracks Misty. PRE-FIX Misty was never sacrificed
#(life stays 20, graveyard empty, Snow-Covered Forest stays in library = 6 cards). POST-FIX
