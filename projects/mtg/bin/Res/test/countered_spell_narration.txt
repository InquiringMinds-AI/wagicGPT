#NAME: Countered spell narration (W40 #3)
#DESC: A countered spell and a resolved one make the SAME stack->graveyard
#DESC: move, so no zone assertion can tell them apart - the whole defect lived
#DESC: in the words. Three wave-39 seats independently read
#DESC: "<card> resolved and went to ... graveyard" on spells that were
#DESC: COUNTERED, and the pilot is instructed to believe the narration.
#DESC: Root cause: MTGGameZone::removeCard clones the card on every zone move
#DESC: and putInZone raises WEventZoneChange with the NEW COPY, so the W35 gate
#DESC: mCounteredSpell == e->card compared a stack instance with its graveyard
#DESC: clone and never fired once in 21 corpus games.
#DESC: This fixture asserts the WORDS on both seats, and keeps a positive
#DESC: control in frame: the counterspell's OWN resolution must still read
#DESC: "resolved", so the fix cannot pass by labelling everything countered.
#DESC: narration:/nonarration: match case-INSENSITIVELY (the fixture parser
#DESC: lowercases every line it reads); the capitals in "was COUNTERED" are
#DESC: pinned exactly by the W40#3 PARSETEST block instead.

yes
