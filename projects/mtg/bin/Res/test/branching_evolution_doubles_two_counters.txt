#NAME: Branching Evolution doubles a TWO-counter event to four
#DESC: Oracle (Scryfall): "If one or more +1/+1 counters would be put on a creature you
#DESC: control, twice that many +1/+1 counters are put on that creature instead."
#DESC: "Twice that many" is the whole event, not one extra counter: a single event that
#DESC: puts two +1/+1 counters on a 2/2 you control must yield four counters (6/6).
#DESC: The old trigger script added exactly ONE counter regardless of the event size.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Branching Evolution,Lexicon Two Counter Witness,Grizzly Bears
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Runeclaw Bear
life:20
[DO]
Lexicon Two Counter Witness
Grizzly Bears
assertpt 6/6 grizzly bears
assertpt 2/2 runeclaw bear
[ASSERT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Branching Evolution,Lexicon Two Counter Witness,Grizzly Bears
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Runeclaw Bear
