#SYMPTOM (corpus 20260719 deck35 vs49): the engine "never issued" the declare-attackers
#decision on own-turns despite creatures that looked untapped and unsick in the board

#false and GameObserver::pendingCombatDecision correctly returned NONE. The wave-20 fix
#spared attackers only ALL-OR-NOTHING -- it protected every attacker iff the swing-neutral

#ONE would have paid the bill.

#used, so the payment holds the surplus attackers back for combat.
