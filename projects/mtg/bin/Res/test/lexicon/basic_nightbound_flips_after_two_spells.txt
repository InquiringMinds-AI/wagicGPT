# Nightbound: a permanent on its back (night) face transforms to its front (day)
# face when it becomes day. Ballista Wielder (back, nightbound) // Ballista
# Watcher (front, daybound).
#
# Timeline (empirically traced):
#   Turn 1  establishment - Wielder's auto sees no marker and no nonight and
#           makes it NIGHT, casting the "Night" marker. Player 1 (the active
#           player) then casts TWO spells this turn (the two vanilla creatures),
#           which arms the "it becomes day next turn" transition.
#   Turn 2  it becomes DAY (the Night marker flips to "Day"); the nightbound
#           Wielder transforms to its day face, Ballista Watcher.
# We assert on turn 2 while it is day - one more empty turn would flip it back
# to night, so the assert lands immediately after the transition.
#
# Each vanilla costs {1}; the pool funds exactly {1}{1}. A "no" after each cast
# passes priority through the response window. The two vanillas remain on the
# battlefield and are counted. The Day/Night marker bounces to player 2's
# battlefield (Day's "@each end moveto opponentbattlefield") and sits there as
# "Day". The Ballista card is asserted by its CURRENT face (Ballista Watcher).
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Ballista Wielder
hand:Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla,Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla
manapool:{1}{1}
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla
no
Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla
no
goto end
next
goto firstmain
[ASSERT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Ballista Watcher,Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla,Lexicon Synthetic Vanilla
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Day
[END]
