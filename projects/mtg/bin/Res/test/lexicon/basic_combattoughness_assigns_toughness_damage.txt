# combattoughness makes each affected creature assign combat damage equal to
# its toughness rather than its power. Assault Formation
# (auto=lord(creature|mybattlefield) combattoughness -- "Each creature you
# control assigns combat damage equal to its toughness rather than its power")
# grants it to player 1's creatures. The 1/3 witness attacks unblocked and
# should deal 3 (its toughness) to the defending player: life 20 -> 17.
#
# CARRIER NOTE: the original carrier was "Doran, the Siege Tower". Its name
# contains a comma, and [INIT] zone lists (MTGGameZone::parseLine) are
# comma-separated, so "Doran, the Siege Tower" split into "Doran" + "the Siege
# Tower" -- neither a real card -- and Doran never entered play. The witness
# then had no combattoughness grant and assigned its power (1) instead of its
# toughness (3). The engine's combattoughness handling (stepPower +
# GuiCombat::resolve, including the unblocked-to-player path) is correct; a
# comma-free carrier exercises it faithfully.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Assault Formation,Lexicon Combat Toughness Witness
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[DO]
goto attackers
Lexicon Combat Toughness Witness
goto end
[ASSERT]
END
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Assault Formation,Lexicon Combat Toughness Witness
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:17
[END]
