# Wilting makes damage dealt to the witness arrive as -1/-1 counters. The
# active player deals 1 damage (Lava Dart) to its own 2/2 wilting witness,
# which becomes a permanent 1/1 (one -1/-1 counter) rather than dying. It then
# attacks for 1 (life 20 -> 19). Without wilting the 2/2 would still be a 2/2
# and deal 2, so life 19 is the wilting observation (power was reduced by the
# counter).
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Lexicon Wilting Witness
hand:Lava Dart
manapool:{R}
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[DO]
Lava Dart
Lexicon Wilting Witness
goto combatattackers
Lexicon Wilting Witness
next
next
next
[ASSERT]
COMBATEND
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Lexicon Wilting Witness
graveyard:Lava Dart
tappedinplay:1
[PLAYER2]
life:19
[END]
