# Tergrid, God of Fright: "Whenever an opponent ... discards a permanent card,
# you may put that card from a graveyard onto the battlefield under your
# control." Player2 controls Tergrid. Player1 casts Lexicon Forced Selfdiscard
# and discards Grizzly Bears (a permanent card) from its own hand. From Tergrid's
# controller (Player2) that is an opponent's discard, so Tergrid's discard
# may-trigger fires; accepting it (choice 0) moves Grizzly Bears from Player1's
# graveyard onto Player2's battlefield under Player2's control.
# ('^' stands in for the comma inside "Tergrid, God of Fright".)
[INIT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
hand:Lexicon Forced Selfdiscard,Grizzly Bears
manapool:{1}
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Tergrid^ God of Fright
[DO]
Lexicon Forced Selfdiscard
Grizzly Bears
choice 0
no
no
[ASSERT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:Lexicon Forced Selfdiscard
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Tergrid^ God of Fright,Grizzly Bears
[END]
