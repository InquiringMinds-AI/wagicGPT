# Daybound: a permanent on its front (day) face transforms to its back (night)
# face when it becomes night. Ballista Watcher (front, daybound) // Ballista
# Wielder (back, nightbound).
#
# Timeline (empirically traced):
#   Turn 1  establishment - Watcher's auto sees no day/night marker and makes it
#           DAY, casting the "Day" marker onto the battlefield.
#   Turn 2  one clean empty turn (no spells cast) keeps day but arms the
#           transition.
#   Turn 3  it becomes NIGHT (the Day marker flips to "Night"); the daybound
#           Watcher transforms to its night face, Ballista Wielder.
# So a day->night flip needs the establishment turn PLUS one clean empty turn.
#
# The Day/Night marker is a real card ON the battlefield and BOUNCES to the
# opponent's battlefield at each end step (Day's "@each end moveto
# opponentbattlefield"); by turn 3 it sits on player 2's battlefield as "Night".
# An omitted zone line asserts zero cards, so both faces are accounted for.
# The Ballista card is asserted by its CURRENT face (Ballista Wielder) - the
# face-aware zone matcher makes asserting the old face (Ballista Watcher) fail.
