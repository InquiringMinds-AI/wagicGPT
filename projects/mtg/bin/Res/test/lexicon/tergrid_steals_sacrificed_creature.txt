# Tergrid, God of Fright: "Whenever an opponent sacrifices a nontoken permanent
# ... you may put that card from a graveyard onto the battlefield under your
# control." Player2 controls Tergrid. Player1 casts Lexicon Forced Sacrifice and
# sacrifices its own Grizzly Bears (a nontoken creature). From Tergrid's
# controller (Player2) that is an opponent's sacrifice, so Tergrid's may-trigger
# fires; accepting it (choice 0) moves Grizzly Bears from Player1's graveyard
# onto Player2's battlefield under Player2's control.
# (P1 is the scripted actor so the sacrifice is a discrete action and the may
# arms with choice-0 pending -- the phase-progression cancel trap is avoided.)
# ('^' stands in for the comma inside "Tergrid, God of Fright".)
[INIT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Grizzly Bears
hand:Lexicon Forced Sacrifice
manapool:{1}
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Tergrid^ God of Fright
[DO]
Lexicon Forced Sacrifice
Grizzly Bears
choice 0
no
no
[ASSERT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:Lexicon Forced Sacrifice
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Tergrid^ God of Fright,Grizzly Bears
[END]
