# CR 702.19b: a trampling attacker may assign damage to the defending player
# only "once all those blocking creatures are assigned lethal damage", and
# "when checking for assigned lethal damage, take into account damage already
# marked on the creature ... but not any abilities or effects that might change
# the amount of damage that's actually dealt." Prevention is exactly such an
# effect, so a blocker with protection is still assigned its full lethal cut.
# CR 702.16e then prevents that damage when it is DEALT, so the blocker
# survives and only the EXCESS tramples through. Protection EATS trample damage.
#
# Rootbreaker Wurm (green 6/6 trample) blocked by Cerulean Wyvern (3/3 flier,
# protection from green). Lethal for the Wyvern is 3, ignoring prevention, so
# exactly 3 tramples through: defender goes 20 -> 17, NOT 20 -> 14.
# The Wyvern survives (its 3 assigned damage is prevented) and deals its own 3
# back to the 6/6 Wurm, which lives.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Rootbreaker Wurm
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Cerulean Wyvern
life:20
[DO]
goto attackers
Rootbreaker Wurm
next
Cerulean Wyvern
next
next
next
goto end
[ASSERT]
ENDOFTURN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Rootbreaker Wurm
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Cerulean Wyvern
life:17
[END]
