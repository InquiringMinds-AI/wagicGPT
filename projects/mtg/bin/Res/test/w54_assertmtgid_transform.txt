#W54-F (D38): pin the MTG ID a transformed double-faced card carries.
#Wagic card ids are Gatherer multiverse ids and a transform DFC gets ONE PER
#FACE, in the same printing. The id is what WResourceManager::RetrieveCard asks
#art for, so the wrong one is a visibly different card on the console - which is
#exactly the owner's wave-53 Vita report (a transformed Heliod showing another
#printing's UNtransformed art). Lane V fixed the resolution
#(MTGAllCards::getOtherFaceCard) but could pin only its pure core in PARSETEST:
#PARSETEST runs before the card database loads, and the suite SKIPPED setMTGId
#on flip (playMode != MODE_TEST_SUITE), so no harness could see a post-flip
#printing at all. #W54-F lifts that skip and adds `assertmtgid`.
#Delver of Secrets (ISD 226749) transforms on its first upkeep after entering,
#when the revealed top card is an instant - Insectile Aberration is ISD 226755,
#the SAME printing's back face.
#Top of library = last listed.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:delver of secrets
inplay:island
library:Hill Giant,Juggernaut,Lightning Bolt
[PLAYER2]
[DO]
interactivereveal
assertmtgid 226749 delver of secrets
island
delver of secrets
goto end
goto upkeep
goto end
goto upkeep
goto upkeep
lightning bolt
revealok
assertmtgid 226755 insectile aberration
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:island,insectile aberration
library:Hill Giant,Juggernaut,Lightning Bolt
[PLAYER2]
[END]
