#REGRESSION (owner live-play report, Vita build, 2026-08-23): two 2/2 slivers
#gang-blocked his 2/2 Frost Lynx, BOTH lived, the Lynx died - the slivers still
#carried first strike from a lord that had died on an earlier turn.
#
#ONE dead first-strike lord does not do it (see striking_sliver_grant_revoked).
#TWO do.  Keyword grants used to be snapshot-and-restore per ability object:
#the first lord's grant saved "first strike was OFF", the second saved
#"first strike was ON", and whichever teardown ran LAST wrote its own snapshot
#back.  Kill the first-granting lord first and the second lord's teardown
#re-set the keyword ON with no lord left alive - permanently.  MTGCardInstance
#now counts modifiers instead, so the keyword falls away with the last of them.
#
#Talon Sliver and Striking Sliver both read "All Sliver creatures (you control)
#have first strike"; Mighty Slivers (deck35) runs 3 Striking Slivers, so two
#live first-strike lords is an ordinary board there.  Both are bolted on turn 1,
#Talon (the first grant) first; a full turn cycle passes; then a 2/2 Grizzly
#Bears attacks into the surviving 2/2 Spinneret Sliver.  With no lord left they
#must TRADE.  If a dead lord's first strike lingers, Spinneret walks away.
