#that DIED on an earlier turn was still granting first strike to the surviving
#slivers - two 2/2 slivers gang-blocked a 2/2 and BOTH lived.
#
#Striking Sliver reads "Sliver creatures you control have first strike."  Once
#it is off the battlefield the grant must be gone.  Here it is Lightning Bolted
#on turn 1, a full turn cycle passes, and only then does a 2/2 Grizzly Bears
#attack into the surviving 2/2 Spinneret Sliver.  With the grant correctly
#revoked the two 2/2s TRADE; if the dead lord's first strike lingers the
#Spinneret survives and the fixture goes red.
#Mox Ruby pays for the Bolt (basic lands cannot appear in INIT/ASSERT).
[INIT]

[PLAYER1]

[PLAYER2]
inplay:Striking Sliver,Spinneret Sliver
[DO]
Mox Ruby
Lightning Bolt
Striking Sliver
goto end
goto firstmain
goto end
goto firstmain
next

next
