#NAME: An ANIMATED Sorin is a legal creature target of his own -6 (#W75-CM F1)
#DESC: Astra wave-75 adversarial review, finding 1 (HIGH), against wave-75 lane
#DESC: CK's P4 fix. Oracle: "-6: Destroy up to three target creatures and/or
#DESC: OTHER planeswalkers." The word `other` restricts ONE alternative - the
#DESC: planeswalker one. Lane CK expressed it as `other *[creature;planeswalker]`,
#DESC: and TargetChooser::other is a CHOOSER-level flag checked in canTarget
#DESC: BEFORE any type is evaluated, so it excluded Sorin from the CREATURE
#DESC: alternative too. With Sarkhan the Masterless's +1 in effect ("each
#DESC: planeswalker you control becomes a 4/4 Dragon creature") Sorin IS a
#DESC: creature, and a creature is a legal target of his own -6.
#DESC: The target DSL cannot scope `other` per alternative: the whole spec builds
#DESC: ONE chooser (TargetChooser.cpp createTargetChooser: the comma loop folds
#DESC: every typeName into a single tc, and a second bracketed descriptor returns
#DESC: NULL), and `other ` is parsed off the whole string into one bool. So the
#DESC: engine gained the scoped form `other!<type>[;<type>] ` (#W75-CM F1) and
#DESC: Sorin's -6 now reads `other!creature *[creature;planeswalker]`: the
#DESC: source stays a legal target while it is a creature, and is refused while
#DESC: it is only a planeswalker. No legal target is lost either way.
#DESC: RED on base 301a30a37 (the shipped `other ` form):
