#NAME: Thoughtlace can be CAST at a spell on the stack (W41 #W41-9)
#This is the root defect the whole item is about.  With alias=1227 the card
#built a SpellOrPermanentTargetChooser, which declares only the two battlefield
#zones, so TargetChooser::validTargetsExist() (TargetChooser.cpp:1658 - it
#walks the zones the chooser declares and matches MTGCardInstance entries)
#could never see a spell on the stack.  With nothing else on the battlefield
#the cast was REFUSED outright: the card stayed in hand with its mana unspent
#and the AI was never offered the response.
#Here the stack spell is the ONLY thing on the board, so the fixture rides
#exactly that branch: the pre-fix behaviour is Thoughtlace still in hand.

endinterruption
