# W54-G companion: the OTHER aura-move path, `retarget` (Detainment Spell:
# "{1}{W}: Attach Detainment Spell to target creature"). Unlike rehook, retarget
# pushes the Aura through a fresh Spell resolution, so its static effect is
# re-parsed against the new host. This fixture pins that: while the Spell is on
# Prodigal Sorcerer the Sorcerer's activated ability is off; after moving the
# Spell to the Grizzly Bears the Sorcerer can ping again (P2 to 19).
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Prodigal Sorcerer,Grizzly Bears
hand:Detainment Spell
manapool:{2}{W}{W}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[DO]
Detainment Spell
Prodigal Sorcerer
Detainment Spell
Grizzly Bears
Prodigal Sorcerer
p2
goto secondmain
[ASSERT]
secondmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Prodigal Sorcerer,Grizzly Bears,Detainment Spell
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:19
[END]
