#NAME: Burnout counters a BLUE instant, and its cantrip rider draws for its own controller
#Oracle (Scryfall, verified 2026-08-24): "Counter target instant spell if it's
#blue. -- Draw a card at the beginning of the next turn's upkeep."
#TWO wave-42 defects, both in the primitive:
# (1) the teach clause was ZONELESS - "teach(instant[blue]) fizzle" defaults to
#     the BATTLEFIELD, where an instant SPELL can never be, so the fizzle never
#     attached and Burnout countered nothing. Fixed zone-explicitly, the same
#     shape wave-41 gave Hydroblast/Pyroblast: "teach(instant[blue]|stack)".
# (2) the rider was "@next upkeep:draw:1" with no "controller" - every other
#     targeted cantrip-next-upkeep card in the corpus (Balduvian Rage, Clairvoyance,
#     Enervate, Feral Instinct, ...) writes "draw:1 controller", and Burnout has a
#     target= line, so the bare form is the outlier. The rider is UNCONDITIONAL in
#     the Oracle - it draws whether or not anything was countered.
#Discriminator: Obsessive Search is {U} "Draw a card". Countered, player 1 must NOT
#draw; the Burnout caster (player 2) draws at ITS OWN upkeep, the next turn's.
#Top of library = last listed.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:Obsessive Search
manapool:{U}
library:Hill Giant,Juggernaut
[PLAYER2]
hand:Burnout
manapool:{1}{R}
library:Mind Stone,Darksteel Citadel
[DO]
Obsessive Search
yes
Burnout
Obsessive Search
endinterruption
goto end
goto upkeep
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:Obsessive Search
library:Hill Giant,Juggernaut
manapool:{0}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
graveyard:Burnout
hand:Darksteel Citadel
library:Mind Stone
manapool:{0}
life:20
[END]
