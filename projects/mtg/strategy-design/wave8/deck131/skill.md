## 1. [PROMOTE - completes the wave-7 line] A representation fix that shipped for an UNOFFERABLE
option is verified the same way as any other, and its confirmation DROPS the interim stopgap.
Wave 7's deck131 headline was a NEW class: the guide named an option ("Play Mountain over Play
Island") that the engine NEVER OFFERED (0/6 games co-offered two lands). Wave 8 the land-drop
enumeration shipped, and the verification is the composition-seam grep pointed at the option list:
count decisions whose option list contains BOTH "Play Mountain" and "Play Island" (12 this wave, 0
last wave), then count how many chose Mountain (12/12 - the pilot picks by NAME regardless of list
position). This closes the loop the wave-7 method opened: an unofferable-option fix is confirmed by
the option APPEARING, not by a board/reply read, and its confirmation is the DEMOTION trigger for the
interim guide stopgap (deck131 dropped the "hold non-Mountain lands to reach the Mountain" workaround
AND the now-stale "one-land-at-a-time menu" explanation - a stale interface caution reinforces the
wrong posture, Step 0). Same terminus as deck110's re-equip marker demotion (wave 7), now proven for
an ABSENT-OPTION fix, not just a WEAK-MARKER fix.

## 2. [PROMOTE - refines the wave-7 mulligan refinement] A keep-side GUARDRAIL added to stop a
false-mulligan can itself INVERT into a false KEEP - the over-generalization jumps to whichever side
carries the salient token. Wave 6 sharpened deck131's mulligan to a countable ("count the Mountains;
zero = Mulligan") and it FALSE-mulliganed a 2-Mountain keeper (wave 7). Wave 7 fixed that with a
literal-string trigger PLUS a keep-side guardrail ("a hand with a Mountain and a Young Pyromancer is
ALWAYS a keep"). Wave 8: the false-mulligan is GONE (keeps 5/6, the guardrail held) BUT the guardrail
sentence itself caused a false KEEP - the pilot quoted it, DROPPED the "Mountain and," and kept a
0-Mountain no-draw hand on the lone "Young Pyromancer ... ALWAYS a keep" token. METHOD: when you add a
guardrail to a gate, the executor can read the guardrail as the WHOLE rule and drop its precondition;
make the gate STRICTLY ORDERED - the disqualifying step (no red source) must decide ON ITS OWN and be
un-overridable by any keep-side clause, and no keep-side sentence may name a card (Young Pyromancer)
that could stand alone as a trigger. State the SUBJECT of the keep as the scarce resource
(Mountain-in-list), never the payoff. This is the false-belief-bridge lesson (deck140 wave 7) applied
to a two-sided decision: kill the bridge by structural ordering, not by more words.

rules-fact candidate but SINGLE-SEAT here - route as a watch, do not cut core (single-seat-can't-cut-
core epistemics).
