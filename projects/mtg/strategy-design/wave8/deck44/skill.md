## P1 (ADD to method) — score a rule with a VALUE FLOOR by DECISION-CORRECTNESS, not raw usage-rate

**Observation.** The wave-7 handoff tracked the counter seam as "13/14 offer windows" (a raw take-count).
This wave the raw take-rate FELL to 9/15 — which reads as a regression but is **15/15 CORRECT decisions**:
6 of the "declines" were correct value-floor let-resolves (land search, mana rock, discard, a recursive
Bloodghast, a 0/2 Ornithopter) and all 9 takes were correct. The counter-value floor MAKES correct
restraint show up as a lower take-rate, so a raw usage tally penalizes exactly the discipline the guide
installed.

**Proposed method line (extends Step 2 "Tally offered-vs-taken" + the re-offer measurement guidance):**
When a reactive/removal rule carries a VALUE FLOOR (decline the cheap targets), do NOT score it by raw
take-rate or by consecutive-take counts. Classify EACH offered window as correct-take / correct-let /
error, and report DECISION-CORRECTNESS (n-correct / n-windows). A falling take-rate under a value floor is
consistent with IMPROVED play; only the error count is the quality signal. This is the confirm-before-accuse
rule applied to a usage tally, and the mirror of wave-7's "count OFFER windows, not just re-picks" — here
the trap is the opposite direction (reading correct restraint as under-use).
