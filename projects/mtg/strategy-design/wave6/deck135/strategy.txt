Deck identity: "Modern Snow" is a base-GREEN snow tempo deck splashing blue and
white. You win by putting a real CREATURE on the board almost every turn and
attacking with it: Ohran Viper and Ice-Fang Coatl draw you cards as they hit,
Abominable Treefolk (power = your snow permanents, trample) is your one big
finisher, Icehide Golem adds an early body. Your fetches, Arcum's Astrolabe,
Search for Azcanta, Glacial Revelation, and Into the North are FUEL that helps
you cast creatures - they are not a turn's play by themselves. Every game you win,
real creatures hit the board and keep attacking; every game you lose, your
creatures sit in hand while the opponent's life never moves.

=======================================================================
RULE #1 - EACH MAIN PHASE, DEPLOY A CREATURE BEFORE SPENDING MANA ON ANYTHING ELSE.
=======================================================================
Run this over your cast list every main phase and stop at the first you can take:
1. If any of Icehide Golem, Ohran Viper, Ice-Fang Coatl, or Abominable Treefolk is
   a Cast option, cast it THIS turn. A creature in the list ALWAYS beats "Cast
   nothing," a fetch, an Astrolabe, a Search, or a Glacial Revelation.
2. Only after a creature is cast (or none is castable) do you spend leftover mana
   on Astrolabe / a fetch crack / Glacial Revelation / Into the North.
The thought "I will develop next turn, first I set up mana and card draw" is the
losing thought - the value cards only help you cast creatures; the creatures win.
DO NOT skip a creature in your list because you believe you already have lethal on
board next turn: read your OWN battlefield line first - if a creature is not
printed there, it is NOT in play, and every turn you add another body is a turn
closer to winning.

=======================================================================
RULE #2 - DECLARE AN ATTACKER EVERY TURN YOU HAVE ONE THAT PROFITS.
=======================================================================
When the Attackers list appears, one line of math per creature. A creature
attacks if ANY holds: (a) no untapped opponent creature can block it, (b) its
toughness > the biggest blocker's power, or (c) it trades up or draws a card.
Ohran Viper passes (c) EVERY time - anything blocking it dies at end of combat
and a connect draws a card; Ice-Fang Coatl (flying) flies over ground blockers.
"No attackers" is right ONLY when every listed attacker dies for zero gain - "the
opponent might block" is your math working, not a reason to stay home.
- Boreal Druid is a MANA creature (1/1, {T}: add {1}): keep it back for mana and
  blocking; attack with it ONLY when it is your last body and the 1 damage matters.
- AT 10 LIFE OR LESS, before declaring, sum the power of every opponent creature
  that could swing back next turn; keep enough blockers home to survive it, attack
  with the rest.

WHO IS THE BEATDOWN - check EVERY turn from the board, not the matchup:
- If the opponent attacked you on two of their first three turns, OR your life is
  14 or less before turn 5, you are the DEFENDER. Rule #1 still applies, but deploy
  for DEFENSE: cast Icehide Golem and Ohran Viper (a 1/3 wall that kills what it
  blocks) and KEEP them home, Gelid Shackles their best attacker, flash Ice-Fang
  Coatl in as a surprise blocker - all before any fetch or dig. Stabilize first,
  then turn the corner. You cast a creature every turn in BOTH roles.
- Otherwise you are the aggressor: deploy and attack every turn.

MANA (solved - two decisions, not your game):
- Your first search that can take Snow-Covered ISLAND takes it. ONE Island, or one
  Astrolabe in play, is ENOUGH blue - after that every fetch and Into the North
  takes Snow-Covered Forest and your turns go to creatures. You own only 2 Islands.
- Crack a fetch the first time its option appears, then MOVE ON - the 1 life and
  sacrifice ARE the card working. Do not re-pick a fetch-crack you already took: if
  the same "search/Put in Play with <fetch>" line reappears, your land already
  resolved - pass and spend the turn on a creature.

KEY CARDS (grounded in engine text):
- ICEHIDE GOLEM {1}: a 2/2 for one mana, your cheapest real body - best turn-2
  play and best early blocker. Cast it on sight.
- OHRAN VIPER {1}{g}{g} (1/3): attacks profitably into anything and walls on
  defense. Deploy and swing it freely.
- ICE-FANG COATL {g}{u} (1/1 flash flying): ETB draws a card; deathtouch with 3+
  other snow permanents. Cast as a normal creature to build your board; save the
  flash for a surprise block only when defending. Needs GREEN and BLUE - if it is
  in the list, you can pay; if it is NOT, you are short a color, so fetch toward it.
- ABOMINABLE TREEFOLK {2}{g}{u}: your finisher - power = your snow permanents
  (usually 5-7), trample; ETB taps and freezes one opponent creature. This is the
  body that actually closes games; cast it the first turn it appears, freeze their
  best blocker, and swing next turn. Small creatures alone will not race - land
  Treefolk.
- FORCE OF NEGATION {1}{u}{u}: counters NONCREATURE spells, free on the OPPONENT'S
  turn (exile a blue card). It costs you TWO cards (Force + the exiled blue one), so
  spend it ONLY on a board wipe (Damnation, Pyroclasm) or a spell that would kill
  your ONLY threat on board. Against fast aggro, do NOT exile a blue creature you
  still need to cast just to stop a single small burn or token spell - take the hit
  and keep developing. Cast your creatures anyway - Force is still free on their turn.
- GELID SHACKLES {w}: on the opponent's best blocker before you attack, or their
  best attacker to shut it off on defense.
- DIAMOND FAERIE {2}{g}{w}{u} (3/3 flying): needs G, W, AND U at once - you rarely
  assemble it, so do NOT plan around it. If it ever appears in your list, cast it and
  use its end-of-turn team pump in combat to push the last points of lethal.
- ASTROLABE / SEARCH FOR AZCANTA / GLACIAL REVELATION / INTO THE NORTH: card
  advantage and fixing, not threats. Cast them with mana LEFT OVER after a creature,
  never instead of one.

LETHAL CHECK each combat: count ONLY the creatures printed on YOUR battlefield line
and offered in the Attackers list - never a creature you merely planned to have.
Treefolk power (snow permanent count) + other attackers' power; if that >= opponent
life and their untapped creatures can't stop it, attack with everyone. If your only
body is a 2/2 Golem, you are NOT at lethal - keep casting creatures.

MULLIGAN: keep 7 with 2-4 lands (a fetch or Into the North counts) AND at least
one creature or a way to find one. Ship 0-1 or 6+ lands, or zero creatures. A hand
whose only creatures are Ice-Fang Coatl / Ohran Viper with no green source is a
mulligan - you need to make G plus a second color early.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> play):
- A creature AND a fetch/Astrolabe/Search are both in your list -> cast the
  creature this turn; the value card waits for leftover mana.
- Your plan says you have lethal but your battlefield line shows only a small body
  -> the big creature is NOT in play; cast the creature in your list now and keep
  building - a planned Treefolk you never cast deals zero damage.
- Opponent is racing you (two attacks by turn 3, or your life under 14 early) ->
  deploy Golem/Viper as blockers and hold them home, Shackles their best attacker,
  before any fetch or dig.
- Untapped Ohran Viper + the opponent has creatures -> attack; a block trades up,
  a connect draws a card.
- 5+ snow permanents and Abominable Treefolk castable -> cast it, freeze their
  best creature, swing next turn.
- A fetch-crack line you already used reappears -> pass it; the land resolved,
  spend the turn on a creature.
- Only Boreal Druid could attack and you have spells to cast -> hold the Druid for
  mana and cast a creature instead.
