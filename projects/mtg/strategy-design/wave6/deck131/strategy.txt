Deck identity: BLUE-RED SPELLSLINGER. You kill TWO ways, and the FIRST is the one to
lean on: with Guttersnipe on your battlefield, every instant or sorcery you cast deals
2 to the opponent's face - no attack needed. The second is attacking with your
creatures. Pick your gear each turn from one visible fact - is a Guttersnipe or Young
Pyromancer on your battlefield, or in your cast list right now?
- YES -> RACE GEAR: deploy the payoff, then chain spells and attack every turn. Every
  turn in this gear deals damage.
- NO -> DIG GEAR: cast a DRAW spell (Artificer's Epiphany, Opportunity) to find a
  payoff, or a counter at a target worth stopping. A dig turn ends with cards drawn and
  your mana spent on finding threats - not on lifegain. Switch to RACE GEAR the first
  turn a payoff creature appears in your cast list.

THE #1 RULE - WITH GUTTERSNIPE ON YOUR BATTLEFIELD, CAST A SPELL EVERY TURN. Each
instant or sorcery you cast is 2 damage to their face, so the CAST ITSELF is the payoff
- you do not need a good target for it.
- On YOUR main phase with Guttersnipe out, cast any of these for 2 damage even if they
  do nothing else: Artificer's Epiphany (draw 2, deal 2), Opportunity (draw 4, deal 2),
  Hydrolash, Aetherize (deal 2 even with no attackers to bounce), and Downsize or
  Cyclonic Rift pointed at any opponent permanent. If any of these is in your cast list,
  CAST IT - the "Cast nothing" option is wrong while Guttersnipe is out.
- The general rule "do not fire a reaction with no target" is OFF while Guttersnipe is
  on your battlefield: with Guttersnipe out there is no such thing as a wasted spell,
  because the 2 damage is the point. Chain until your mana is gone.

DEPLOY THE PAYOFFS - curve them out as fast as red mana allows:
- Guttersnipe ({2}{R}, 2/2): every instant/sorcery YOU cast deals 2 to the opponent.
  This is your kill. CAST IT THE TURN YOU CAN.
- Young Pyromancer ({1}{R}, 2/1): every instant/sorcery YOU cast makes a 1/1 Elemental
  token. Cast it the turn you can.
- Cast the payoff creature FIRST, then your other spells the same turn - a spell cast
  before the creature lands makes no token and no damage.

YOUR PROVEN WIN LINE (the exact sequence that won you the last mirror-race - repeat it):
1. Turn 1: Play Mountain. 2. Turn 3: Play Island, cast Young Pyromancer. 3. Turn 5: Play
Mountain, cast Guttersnipe; attack with Young Pyromancer. 4. Every turn after: cast one
spell (Cyclonic Rift/Downsize at any target = 2 to the face via Guttersnipe), then attack
with EVERY creature, and answer "no blockers" on defense. That chain took the opponent
20 -> 16 -> 12 -> 7 -> dead while you stayed above 12. Cast + attack + never block = the win.

LANDS - play the MOUNTAIN first. Whenever a Mountain and an Island are both in your
hand, choose "Play Mountain" until you have two Mountains on the battlefield; Islands
after that. Play the Mountain EVEN on a turn you cast nothing red - getting the red
source down early is what lets your creature come a turn sooner, and both payoffs need
red. (Playing an Island first has cost this deck whole games by delaying the creature.)

MULLIGAN - COUNT THE MOUNTAINS IN YOUR OPENING HAND. Zero Mountains -> choose Mulligan.
Both your win conditions are red (Guttersnipe {2}{R}, Young Pyromancer {1}{R}); a hand
with no red source cannot cast a single threat and does nothing for turns. This OVERRIDES
the general "keep any hand with 2-5 lands" rule: three Islands plus a Young Pyromancer is
a KEEP by land count but a MULLIGAN for THIS deck, because the threat is uncastable. The
thought "I will keep this and cast my creature once I draw a Mountain" is a TRAP - you
keep a hand that stalls, and off 8 Mountains in 60 cards you often never draw the red.
The ONLY no-Mountain keep: a hand holding Artificer's Epiphany OR Opportunity (a draw
spell to dig for red) with enough blue lands to actually cast it this turn or next. WITH a
Mountain in hand, keep any 2-5 land hand that also has a payoff creature or a draw spell;
ship a hand that is only counters and Downsizes.

YOUR CREATURES ATTACK. THEY DO THEIR BLOCKING BY RACING.
- When the game asks you to DECLARE ATTACKERS, name EVERY creature in the list:
  Guttersnipe, every Young Pyromancer, every Elemental token. Count the list; your reply
  names all of them. Keep a creature home only when the opponent's untapped creatures
  could kill you on the swing back.
- When the game asks you to DECLARE BLOCKERS, your answer is "no blockers": take the
  damage and keep your creatures alive for your own attack - a creature that dies
  blocking is damage missing from your race every later turn. The one exception: when the
  incoming attack would put you at 0 (add the attackers' power, compare to your life),
  chump-block with an Elemental token first, Young Pyromancer second. Guttersnipe stays
  alive in every case - it is your kill condition.

REACTIVE SPELLS - support cards, not your plan (except as Guttersnipe fuel, above):
- Counterspell ({U}{U}) / Dissipate ({1}{U}{U}) / Essence Scatter ({1}{U}): point these
  at removal aimed at Guttersnipe or Young Pyromancer, and at a threat that beats you on
  its own. Let the rest resolve and develop instead.
- Downsize ({U}) / Hydrolash ({2}{U}) / Aetherize ({3}{U}): fire during combat when you
  are actually being attacked - and any time as Guttersnipe fuel.
- Cyclonic Rift ({1}{U}): bounce the opponent's single best permanent (Overload {6}{U}
  when their board has 3+ nonland permanents) - and it is Guttersnipe fuel too.

DRAW spells win this deck - it cannot win without finding its payoffs. Cast Artificer's
Epiphany and Opportunity on YOUR main phase the turn you can afford them.

PRISM RING / ELIXIR are lifegain only - they do NOT advance your clock. Cast a creature,
a draw spell, or (with Guttersnipe out) any spell FIRST. Play Prism Ring or Elixir only
with leftover mana on a turn you have nothing better to cast, never over a threat and
never in the first turns of a race. Elixir also stops Artificer's Epiphany making you
discard; activate it ({2},tap: gain 5) only when you are below 12 life.

LETHAL CHECK each turn: (total power of your unblocked attackers) + (2 per instant or
sorcery you can still cast this turn, per Guttersnipe on your battlefield). If that
reaches the opponent's life, attack with everyone and cast every spell you can pay for.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> play):
- Your opening hand has zero Mountains and no draw spell -> Mulligan; a no-red hand cannot
  cast your threats.
- Guttersnipe is on your battlefield and a spell is castable -> cast the spell; it is 2
  to their face this turn.
- It is turn 1 and you hold a Mountain -> play the Mountain, so your creature is castable
  next turn.
- You drew no creature -> cast a draw spell to find one; that IS your turn's action.
- Your creatures are being blocked or removed and the opponent's life is not moving ->
  stop relying on combat and win with Guttersnipe: keep it alive and chain spells.
