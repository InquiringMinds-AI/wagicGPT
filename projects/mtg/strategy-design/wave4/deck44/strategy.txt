Deck identity: UB FAERIE TEMPO. Nearly every creature FLIES. You win by casting
faeries EVERY turn and attacking in the air EVERY turn. You are the BEATDOWN in
every matchup: your job is to get the opponent dead before turn 10, not to sit
on a hand of spells.

THE #1 RULE - CAST A CREATURE EVERY TURN. NO EXCEPTIONS:
- On EVERY one of your turns: play a land, then CAST A CREATURE if any "Cast"
  option shows a creature. If the engine OFFERS "Cast X", the cost IS payable -
  Oona's Gatewarden {U/B} casts off a single Island OR Swamp.
- Scion of Oona and Obyra have flash, but treat them as NORMAL creatures: cast
  them in your main phase.
- CAST ARCHMAGE OF ECHOES the first turn you have 5 mana. It is a 4/4 flying,
  ward-2 threat that copies every faerie you cast after it. Board first,
  counters with leftovers.

THE ENGINE - HOW YOU ACTUALLY KILL:
- Obyra, Dreaming Duelist ({U}{B} 2/2 flash flyer): every OTHER faerie that
  enters drains the opponent 1. Cast Obyra early, then every faerie is damage.
- Archmage of Echoes ({4}{U} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): copies each Faerie or Wizard
  CREATURE spell you cast (it does not copy instants). Archmage + any faerie =
  two bodies, and two Obyra drains.
- Faerie Bladecrafter ({2}{B} 2/2 flyer): gets a +1/+1 counter whenever your
  faeries hit the opponent, and when it DIES the opponent loses life equal to
  its power (you gain that much). Cast it and ATTACK with it every turn. The
  "{right now: life -2}" note on its Cast option is the OPPONENT'S life loss if
  it dies - a bonus for you, never a cost. Always cast it.

ATTACK EVERY TURN: your flyers are usually unblockable. Attack with EVERY
creature except Oona's Gatewarden (defender, can't attack) and anything needed
to block a lethal swing back. LETHAL CHECK each combat: total power of your
untapped flyers + 1 per faerie you can still cast with Obyra out; if that is
>= opponent life, attack with everyone and cast the faeries.

COUNTERSPELLS - THE OPTION ITSELF IS THE SIGNAL:
- The engine offers "Cast Counterspell" or "Cast Arcane Denial" ONLY when an
  opponent spell is on the stack RIGHT NOW waiting to resolve. When you see
  that option on the opponent's turn, a spell IS pending - the thought "there
  is no spell on the stack to counter" is ALWAYS FALSE in that moment. The
  pending spell is the LAST game-log line that says "Opponent's X: hand ->
  stack" with no "stack ->" line after it. That X is what your counter stops.
- When a counter option appears, COUNTER the pending spell if it is any of:
  Master of Etherium, Cranial Plating, Guttersnipe, Young Pyromancer, Steel
  Overseer, any creature or equipment while your life is 12 or less, or any
  spell costing 4+ mana. These cheap cards are the actual killers - a 2-mana
  Master of Etherium becomes a 9/5.
- LET RESOLVE and keep your mana: mana artifacts (Mox Opal), Elixir of
  Immortality, Prism Ring, and small utility spells while your life is 13+.
- Casting your creatures on your own turn ALWAYS comes before holding counter
  mana. Counters are for the mana left over.
- Go for the Throat: destroys any NONARTIFACT creature. Fire it the turn you
  see their best attacker - especially creatures that GROW (+1/+1 counters:
  Stromkirk Noble, Steel Overseer). It cannot hit artifact creatures (Master
  of Etherium, Ornithopter, Vault Skirge) - against those, counter instead.

WHEN YOU ARE BEING RACED (opponent has dealt you 6+ damage by turn 6): keep
attacking - you still win by racing - and spend EVERY reactive card at the
first legal moment: Go for the Throat their biggest nonartifact attacker,
counter every creature and equipment per the list above, and block with
Oona's Gatewarden every combat (2 toughness of wither shrinks what it blocks).
At 10 life or less, spend ALL interaction NOW on whatever is hitting you.

CARD NOTES:
- Sleep-Cursed Faerie ({U} 3/3 flyer, ward 2): enters tapped with 3 stun
  counters and wakes up by itself in 3 turns. Cast it turn 1-2 and let it wake.
  Pay the {1}{U} untap AT MOST ONCE per turn, and ONLY in your Main Phase 1 on
  a turn it will attack THIS combat. The same untap prompt also appears in
  Upkeep and on the opponent's turn - in those windows always pass; paying
  there is wasted mana and it will still be offered in Main Phase 1.
- Faerie Miscreant ({U} 1/1 flyer): the second copy draws a card - cast both.
- Oona's Gatewarden ({U/B} 2/1 flying defender, wither): best blocker; wither
  shrinks what it blocks. Cast it turn 1 off ANY land; it can never attack.
- Scion of Oona ({2}{U} 1/1 flash flyer): other faeries get +1/+1 and shroud.
  Cast before attacking to pump the team.
- Shadow Puppeteers ({6}{U} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): makes two 1/1 flyers on entry.
  When asked, ALWAYS say YES to turning an attacking flyer into a 4/4 dragon.

MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2-4 lands and at least one creature. Also KEEP
3+ lands with removal/counters and no creature - half your deck is cheap
flyers, you will draw them; a 6-card hand is worse than a slow 7. Ship only
0-1 land or 5+ land hands.

THREE SITUATIONS THAT DECIDED PAST GAMES - THE CORRECT PLAY:
- A counter option appears on the opponent's turn while your hand holds
  counters: a spell IS pending - counter it per the list above. (Games were
  lost with all four counters still in hand while killers resolved.)
- The Sleep-Cursed Faerie untap prompt appears in Upkeep or on the opponent's
  turn: pass. Pay it once, in your Main Phase 1, on a turn it attacks.
- A creature is castable: cast it. Archmage of Echoes always hits the
  battlefield the first turn 5 mana is available.
