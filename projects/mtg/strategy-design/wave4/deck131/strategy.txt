Deck identity: BLUE-RED SPELLSLINGER with a small kill package. You win when
Guttersnipe or Young Pyromancer is on the battlefield and you cast spells and
attack every single turn. The deck has TWO GEARS, and one visible fact picks
the gear each turn - is a Guttersnipe or Young Pyromancer on your battlefield,
or in your cast list right now?
- YES -> RACE GEAR: cast the payoff first, then your other spells, and attack
  with every creature. Every turn in this gear deals damage.
- NO -> DIG GEAR: you still cast every turn - draw spells to FIND a payoff,
  Prism Ring or Elixir with spare mana, a counter at the right target. A dig
  turn ends with your mana spent and cards drawn. Switch to RACE GEAR the
  first turn a payoff creature appears in your cast list.

THE #1 RULE - YOUR CREATURES ATTACK. THEY DO THEIR BLOCKING BY RACING.
- When the game asks you to DECLARE ATTACKERS, name EVERY creature in the
  list: Guttersnipe, every Young Pyromancer, every Elemental token. Count the
  list; your reply names all of them. Sending one attacker while three sit
  home is how this deck loses winnable games. Keep a creature home only when
  the opponent's untapped creatures could kill you on the swing back.
- When the game asks you to DECLARE BLOCKERS, your answer is "no blockers":
  take the damage and keep your creatures alive for your own attack - a
  creature that dies blocking is damage missing from your race every later
  turn. The one exception: when the incoming attack would put you at 0 (add
  the attackers' power, compare to your life), chump-block with an Elemental
  token first, Young Pyromancer second. Guttersnipe stays alive in every
  case - it is your kill condition, and a 2/2 blocking trade is never worth it.

CAST A SPELL EVERY TURN. If you have unused mana and any castable spell,
casting it is your action this main phase - each cast is Guttersnipe damage
and a Pyromancer token.
- Artificer's Epiphany (2U, draw 2) and Opportunity (4UU, draw 4): cast them
  on YOUR main phase the turn you can afford them. They find your
  Guttersnipes; the deck cannot win without drawing cards, and with
  Guttersnipe out the cast itself is 2 damage.
- Tapping out to develop is right for this deck. Keep counter mana open only
  when you hold a counter AND expect a spell worth stopping this turn cycle.

THE ENGINE - order matters:
- Guttersnipe (2R, 2/2): every instant/sorcery YOU cast deals 2 damage to the
  opponent. This is your kill. CAST IT THE TURN YOU CAN.
- Young Pyromancer (1R, 2/1): every instant/sorcery YOU cast makes a 1/1
  Elemental token.
- Cast the payoff creature FIRST, then your other spells the same turn - a
  spell cast before the creature lands makes no token and no damage.
- With Guttersnipe out, EVERY spell is 2 to the face: Epiphany is "draw 2,
  deal 2"; Downsize at any opponent creature is 2 damage. Spend spare mana on
  a cheap instant every turn Guttersnipe is out.

LANDS - play the MOUNTAIN first. Whenever a Mountain and an Island are both
in your hand, choose "Play Mountain" until you have two Mountains on the
battlefield; Islands after that. Your payoff creatures both need red.

LETHAL CHECK each turn: (total power of your attackers) + (2 per instant you
can cast this turn per Guttersnipe on your battlefield). If that reaches the
opponent's life, attack with everyone and cast every instant you can pay for.

REACTIVE SPELLS - when a response is possible the game opens a window and
asks you; the choice is whether the reaction beats casting the same card
proactively for Guttersnipe damage:
- Counterspell (UU) / Dissipate (1UU) / Essence Scatter (1U): point these at
  removal aimed at Guttersnipe or Young Pyromancer, and at a threat that
  beats you on its own. Let everything else resolve and develop instead -
  these are support cards, never your plan.
- Downsize (U) / Hydrolash (2U) / Aetherize (3U): fire them during combat
  when you are actually being attacked. With Guttersnipe on the battlefield,
  Downsize at any opponent creature on your own turn is also a fine 2 damage.
- Cyclonic Rift (1U): bounce the opponent's single best permanent when it
  slows their attack. Overload (6U) when their board has 3+ nonland permanents.
- Prism Ring ({1}): cast it turn 1, choose BLUE.
- Elixir of Immortality ({1} artifact): cast it early with leftover mana - as
  an artifact it stops Artificer's Epiphany making you discard. Activate
  ({2},tap: gain 5) when you are below 12 life.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands plus a payoff creature or a draw spell. Mulligan a
hand whose only action is a red creature with no Mountain, and any hand that
is only counters and Downsizes.
