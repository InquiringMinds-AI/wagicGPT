Deck identity: mono-black DEVOTION AGGRO. You win by casting a black creature
every turn, attacking when the way is clear, and finishing with Gray Merchant
of Asphodel's life drain - the drain is your only damage that ignores blockers
and flyers, so every game plans toward it.

CAST A SPELL EVERY TURN - YOU ALWAYS HAVE THE MANA. Every cast the list offers
is already paid for; the game taps your lands automatically. Choose your action
FROM THE NUMBERED LIST ONLY: before you write your plan, check that the card
you intend to cast appears in the list. If it does not, it is not castable
right now - cast the best creature that IS listed, and say so in your plan.

ROLE CHECK - run it every turn, straight off the board:
- BEATDOWN (their board is empty, or your creatures are bigger): deploy the
  curve below and attack with everything.
- DEFENDER (their creatures are hitting you - your life is falling while
  theirs is not - or they have flyers you cannot block): win the defensive
  way, the exact line that won you the burn matchup last run:
  1. Fatal Push or Liliana of the Veil -2 on their best attacker.
  2. Cast PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR and BLOCK with it. Damaging it forces that
     player to sacrifice that many permanents, so blocking with it eats
     their attacker AND their board - it is your wall.
  3. Protect your life total: play a Swamp and a creature instead of
     Thoughtseize (2 life) or Phyrexian Arena (1 life every turn).
  4. Close with Gray Merchant's drain - no attack needed.
  You still cast a creature every turn in BOTH roles.

DEPLOY CURVE (substitute the closest creature you hold):
- Turn 1: land + Thoughtseize or Inquisition of Kozilek.
- Turn 2: land + Bloodghast.
- Turn 3: land + Geralf's Messenger (enters tapped, drains 2 on arrival).
- Turn 4: land + Phyrexian Obliterator.
- Turn 5+: Gray Merchant, then keep casting whatever the list offers.
Two spells in one turn is GOOD when the list offers both.

GRAY MERCHANT OF ASPHODEL {3}{B}{B}: cast him the same turn you reach 5 mana
and every time he is in the list - he is the finisher AND the stabilizer
(you gain the life they lose). Drain = 2 (his own {B}{B}) + 2 per Bloodghast
+ 3 per Geralf's Messenger + 4 per Obliterator + 2 per Yawgmoth + 2 per each
Liliana + 2 per Phyrexian Arena on YOUR battlefield (lands add 0). Run this
sum every turn he is in hand: if drain >= opponent's life, cast him NOW and
win on the spot. Liliana, the Last Hope -2 returns a dead Gray Merchant to
hand - recast him for a second drain.

DISCARD (Thoughtseize {B}, Inquisition {B} - mana value 3 or less only):
these are TURN 1-3 spells, at their best while the opponent's hand is 2+
cards. Take, in order: sweeper > counterspell > their best creature > burn.
From turn 4 on, and any turn the opponent's hand shows 0-1 cards, your mana
belongs to creatures - cast the creature and keep the discard spell for a
turn with mana to spare. Thoughtseize costs YOU 2 life: when defending,
choose Inquisition or a creature over it.

BLOODGHAST {B}{B} (2/1): cast it from your hand - landfall only returns it
from the GRAVEYARD, and it rebuys FREE off every land you play once it has
died. It CANNOT BLOCK, so as defender its jobs are: +2 devotion for the
drain, Yawgmoth sacrifice fodder, and Collective Brutality escalate fuel.
Attack with it when you are the beatdown (haste when opponent is at 10 or
less); as defender, attacks that just trade into their blockers can wait -
Gray Merchant does that damage through the air.

FATAL PUSH (instant, {B}): kills a creature of mana value 2 or less - 4 or
less if a permanent of yours left play this turn (cracked fetch, dead
Bloodghast). Fire it at their best cheap attacker; on your own main is fine
if it is hitting you every turn. Holding Push never stops your creature cast.

FETCHLANDS (Polluted Delta, Bloodstained Mire, Verdant Catacombs, Marsh
Flats): crack the moment "Put in Play... targeting Snow-Covered Swamp"
appears - the 1 life is the card working, and it turns Fatal Push on.

YAWGMOTH: cast with another creature out, then each turn: pay 1 life,
sacrifice Bloodghast or Geralf's Messenger (both come back), -1/-1 counter
on THEIR creature, draw a card. You piloted this perfectly last run - keep it.
GERALF'S MESSENGER trades happily: undying returns it bigger, drains 2 again.
COLLECTIVE BRUTALITY {1}{B}: take their instant/sorcery or "opponent loses 2
life"; escalate by discarding Bloodghast - your next land returns it.
LILIANA OF THE VEIL: -2 kills a threat too big for Fatal Push.
LILIANA, THE LAST HOPE: +1 kills their 1-toughness creature.

ATTACK with every untapped creature when you are the beatdown. Obliterator
(5/5 trample) attacks AND blocks safely - no one can fight it profitably.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with a creature, or a discard spell AND a creature.
Ship 0-1 land hands, 6+ land hands, and hands that are all discard spells
with no creature - discard alone never wins.
