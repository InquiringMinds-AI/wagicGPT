Deck identity: "Modern Snow" is a base-GREEN snow tempo-value deck splashing blue
and white. You win by casting a snow creature every turn AND attacking every turn:
Ohran Viper and Ice-Fang Coatl draw you cards while they hit, Abominable Treefolk
(power = your snow permanent count, trample) and Diamond Faerie (3/3 flying, pumps
the team) finish. Every game you have won, you attacked nearly every turn. Every
game you have lost or timed out, you sat back "waiting" while the opponent's life
never moved. Attacking IS this deck's defense, card-draw, and win condition.

=======================================================================
RULE #1 - DECLARE AN ATTACKER EVERY TURN. Run this check, then swing.
=======================================================================
When the Attackers list appears, pick attackers with this check - it takes one
line of math per creature:
- A creature attacks if ANY of these is true: (a) the opponent has no untapped
  creature that can block it, (b) it survives the biggest blocker (its toughness
  > blocker's power), or (c) it trades up (kills something bigger or draws you a
  card). Ohran Viper passes (c) EVERY time: any creature that blocks it is
  destroyed at end of combat, and if it hits the player you draw a card.
- Declaring "no attackers" is allowed ONLY when every listed attacker dies for
  zero gain. "The opponent might block" is not a reason - blocks feed Viper and
  Coatl's deathtouch.
- OPPONENT HAS LIGHTMINE FIELD ("deals damage to each attacker equal to the
  number of attackers"): you still attack - with exactly ONE creature whose
  toughness is 2 or more. One attacker means 1 damage total: Ohran Viper (1/3)
  takes 1, lives at 1/2, and still draws you a card when it connects. Abominable
  Treefolk and Diamond Faerie also attack solo through it easily. Do the math
  (attackers = damage to each), send the survivor, every single turn.
- AT 10 LIFE OR LESS, run the swing-back sum BEFORE declaring: add the power of
  every opponent creature that could attack you next turn. Keep enough blockers
  home that you survive that total; the rest still attack.

=======================================================================
RULE #2 - A LISTED CAST IS YOURS TO TAKE. DIAMOND FAERIE ESPECIALLY.
=======================================================================
The action list only ever offers spells you CAN pay for right now, on a turn you
CAN cast them. So when "Cast Diamond Faerie" is in your list, casting it IS your
turn - a 3/3 flyer that ends games ({1} + one snow mana: ALL your snow creatures
get +1/+1). You have skipped this exact cast 22 times in one game and lost.
These two thoughts are ALWAYS FALSE when a Cast option is listed, never act on
them: "I have no mana" (the engine already checked - you can pay) and "it is the
opponent's turn / wrong phase" (sorcery-speed casts are only offered on YOUR
main phase). Same rule for Abominable Treefolk, Ohran Viper, Ice-Fang Coatl,
Icehide Golem, and Arcum's Astrolabe: if a creature or Astrolabe is in the cast
list, casting one BEATS "Cast nothing". If an option is still in your list, it
has NOT been done yet - believe the list, not your remembered plan.

MANA (this is solved - spend two decisions on it, not the game):
- Your FIRST land search that can take Snow-Covered ISLAND takes it (Flooded
  Strand, Misty Rainforest, Prismatic Vista, Into the North can; Windswept Heath
  cannot - it takes Snow-Covered Forest). ONE Island, or one Arcum's Astrolabe
  in play, is ENOUGH blue: after that, every fetch takes Snow-Covered FOREST and
  your turns go to casting creatures and attacking. You only own 2 Islands -
  hunting a second one is how you durdled a game away at 20 opponent life.
- Crack fetchlands the turn you play them: when you see "search basic land ..."
  or "Put in Play with <fetch> ... [cost: Life, Tap, Sacrifice]", take it -
  the life and sacrifice ARE the card working.
- Cast EVERY Arcum's Astrolabe on sight: it costs one snow mana, draws a card,
  fixes all colors, and adds to your snow count. Copies are never "redundant".

KEY CARDS:
- ABOMINABLE TREEFOLK {2}{G}{U}: your biggest body (power = snow permanents,
  trample). On entry it taps and freezes one opponent creature - target their
  best untapped blocker when you are about to swing, or their biggest attacker
  when defending. It cannot attack the turn you cast it; it swings NEXT turn.
- DIAMOND FAERIE {2}{G}{W}{U}: cast on sight (Rule #2). When your attack is 1-3
  damage short of lethal, pay {1} + one snow mana: all snow creatures +1/+1.
- ICE-FANG COATL {G}{U}: 1/1 flash flyer, draws a card on entry; deathtouch with
  3+ other snow permanents. If it is castable and you would otherwise cast
  nothing, cast it NOW - never hold it across whole turns.
- GLACIAL REVELATION {2}{G}: reveals 6, keeps every snow card. Cast it when you
  have spare mana after deploying - never instead of a creature you could cast.
- FORCE OF NEGATION {1}{U}{U}: counters NONCREATURE spells; free (exile a blue
  card) only on the OPPONENT'S turn. Fire it at a board wipe or mass removal -
  you have used it exactly right on Damnation and Pyroclasm; keep doing that.
  Never hold your whole turn for it: cast your creatures, it is still free on
  their turn. If the opponent has cast no noncreature spell for two of their
  turns, stop waiting - develop and attack.
- GELID SHACKLES {W}: put it on their best blocker before you attack.

LETHAL CHECK each combat: Treefolk power (count your snow permanents) + your
other attackers' power + Diamond Faerie pump (+1 per snow creature for {1}+snow);
if that total >= opponent life and their untapped creatures can't stop it,
attack with EVERYONE and pump.

WHEN YOU ARE THE DEFENDER (check the board, not the deck's colors): if the
opponent has attacked you on two of their first three turns, OR your life is 14
or less before turn 5, you are the defender until their board is contained -
against ANY opponent, red or not. Turns 1-3 cast Icehide Golem {1} and Boreal
Druid as blockers BEFORE Into the North or Search for Azcanta. At 10 life or
less keep Golem and Druid home to block; Treefolk, Viper, and Faerie still
attack (Rule #1's swing-back sum decides how many stay).

MULLIGAN: keep 7 with 2-4 lands (a fetchland or Into the North counts as a
land). Ship hands with 0-1 lands or 6+ lands.
