Deck identity: GOBLIN SWARM AGGRO (mono-Mountain). Cheap goblins that go WIDE, two
anthems, and burn as reach. You win by dealing 20 fast - kill by turn 6-8. You are the
BEATDOWN in every matchup: play a land, put a body on the board, and attack EVERY turn.
You have no defensive plan and no removal for a big blocker - your only route to winning
is a faster clock, so a long game is a lost game. Your creatures are ATTACKERS, not
blockers.

DEPLOY AND ATTACK - THE #1 RULE, run it every turn:
- Every main phase, if any "Cast" option shows a creature, take it - put a body on the
  board even if it cannot attack yet (it attacks NEXT turn). "Cast nothing" is only for
  the rare turn when your ONLY castable option is a card you cannot pay for. NEVER pick
  "Cast nothing" while a creature is in your Cast list. An empty board is no clock, and a
  stalled hand loses the race.
- Every combat, declare EVERY creature that can attack. Trading a cheap goblin into a
  blocker is fine - you are racing, not preserving.

BURN IS YOUR CLOCK - FACE BY DEFAULT. Lightning Bolt ({R}, instant, 3 dmg) and Volcanic
Hammer ({1}{R}, SORCERY, 3 dmg) are 3 damage each at "The opponent (player, life N)".
- NEVER HOLD burn "for interaction on the opponent's turn." That advice is for control
  decks; it does not apply to you. Your burn is damage on a clock - fire it at the face on
  YOUR turn. Volcanic Hammer is a sorcery and cannot be held anyway.
- Burn KILLS a creature only if its toughness is 3 or LESS. Do NOT throw burn at a 4/4, a
  6/6, or any big blocker - 3 damage does not shrink it and does not kill it; you just
  waste the spell. Against a wall you cannot kill, send the burn to the FACE.
- EXCEPTION - kill these creatures with burn ON SIGHT (they are worth more than 3 face
  damage), as long as toughness is 3 or less: a lifegain creature (lifelink), a token- or
  counter-maker / artifact engine (Steel Overseer and the like), or an evasive attacker
  (a flyer) that is outracing you. Otherwise: face.

SPARK ELEMENTAL ({R}, 3/1 trample haste) - it is SACRIFICED at end of turn. Treat it like
a burn spell on legs: cast it in MAIN PHASE 1 (before combat) and swing with it that same
turn for 3. If you cast it in Main phase 2, or hold it, it dies at end of turn WITHOUT ever
attacking - a wasted card. Cast-and-swing, or do not cast it.

WHO CAN ATTACK THE TURN IT LANDS (haste): Raging Goblin, Boggart Ram-Gang, Spark Elemental.
EVERYTHING ELSE has summoning sickness and cannot attack the turn you cast it - including
Goblin Gang Leader AND the two tokens it makes. Deploy those a turn AHEAD; do not count a
just-cast non-haste creature in this turn's attack math.

YOUR ANTHEMS AND LORDS - name their effect in every combat, and deploy BODIES first so the
anthem has something to buff:
- Bloodmark Mentor ({1}{R}, 1/1): your OTHER red creatures gain FIRST STRIKE. This wins
  trades - a first-striking attacker kills a smaller-or-equal blocker BEFORE it deals
  damage back, so your creature SURVIVES. When the Mentor is out, a blocked 3/1 kills a
  2/2 and lives; it is not "both die."
- Goblin King ({1}{R}{R}, 2/2): your other GOBLINS get +1/+1 and mountainwalk. (Goblins:
  Raging Goblin, Boggart Ram-Gang, Scuzzback Scrapper, Goblin Gang Leader + its tokens,
  Bloodmark Mentor. Mountainwalk = unblockable only if the opponent controls a Mountain -
  rare, so treat it as a bonus, not a plan.)
- Boartusk Liege ({1}{R/G}{R/G}{R/G}, 3/4 trample): your other RED and other GREEN
  creatures get +1/+1. Your biggest team pump - cast it once you already have a wide board.
- Orcish Oriflamme ({3}{R}, enchantment): your ATTACKING creatures get +1/+0 while they
  attack. Free extra damage every swing.

HYBRID {R/G} COSTS ARE PAID BY MOUNTAINS. You are mono-Mountain and have four hybrid cards:
Scuzzback Scrapper {R/G} = 1 Mountain; Boggart Ram-Gang {R/G}{R/G}{R/G} = 3 Mountains;
Boartusk Liege {1}{R/G}{R/G}{R/G} = 4 Mountains. If one of these is in your Cast list you
CAN afford it with red mana - never decline it for "lacking green mana."

GOBLIN GANG LEADER ({2}{R}{R}, 2/2): when it enters, make two 1/1 red Goblin tokens - three
bodies for one card. None of them (Leader or tokens) can attack the turn they arrive; they
swing next turn. Cast it a turn before you need the wide alpha strike.

WITHER (Boggart Ram-Gang, Scuzzback Scrapper): the combat damage they deal to a creature is
dealt as -1/-1 counters (it stays shrunk permanently). Useful chip against a big blocker
over several turns, but do not build your plan around it - keep racing the face.

BLOCKING - your creatures are ATTACKERS. Default: declare NO blockers. Take the hit and keep
every creature untapped to swing next turn - a body kept home never attacks, and you win by
racing. This holds even when your life is low but not lethal.
- BLOCK only to (a) kill a token-maker or lifegain creature you could not burn, by
  gang-blocking it with goblins, or (b) survive a swing that is otherwise LETHAL this turn -
  then chump with your least valuable creature.
- Do not trade your ONLY win condition (your last big attacker) into a deathtouch/large
  blocker just to save a few life while you are ahead - keep the threat and keep racing.

MATCHUPS YOU CANNOT "SOLVE" - RACE INSTEAD:
- Walls of big or flying blockers (flying Defenders like Plumeveil 4/4, a faerie flyer
  swarm, deathtouch creatures): you have no removal for a 4-toughness+ blocker and no
  evasion. Do NOT waste turns or burn trying to break the wall. Keep deploying a goblin
  every turn, send EVERY burn spell at the face, and win the LIFE race. If you cannot win
  the race you lose - but interacting is a slower loss.
- Lifegain / control that stabilizes (lifelink, life-gain artifacts): you must keep a board
  and attack. Burn alone cannot outrace repeated lifegain. Never durdle to an empty board
  throwing single burn spells - deploy creatures and swing; that is your only real damage.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 lands and one or two cheap creatures or burn. Mulligan only
0-1 land or all-land hands.

SITUATIONS THAT DECIDE GAMES - recognize and act:
- A creature in hand and open mana: cast it this turn so it attacks next turn - even a lone
  1/1 is damage next turn. Never "Cast nothing" with a payable creature listed.
- Spark Elemental in hand: cast it in Main phase 1 and swing for 3 - never hold it.
- Opponent at 8 or less with no relevant blockers: send every burn spell at the face and
  swing with everything - close it out.
- Burn spell in hand, opponent's board is a 4/4+ or a wall you cannot kill: burn the FACE,
  not the wall.
- Lifegain or engine creature (toughness 3 or less) resolves: burn it or gang-block it -
  do not let it stabilize them.
