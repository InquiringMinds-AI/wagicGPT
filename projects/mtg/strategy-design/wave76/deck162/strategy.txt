DRAW AND DIE - UB FORCED-DRAW PUNISH

The opponent's draw step is your kill. Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler and Ob Nixilis each deal 1
damage every time that opponent draws a card, and they stack; Howling Mine, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's
Puzzle Box and Forced Fruition make them draw MORE. Put the damage on the battlefield FIRST, then hand
them cards. Every turn: land a punisher, wall off their creatures, or - once a punisher is out - stack
another engine. You win on their draw step whether or not you attack.
THE ARITHMETIC: with P punishers and D engines out their draw step costs P x (D + 1), so the next
punisher adds D + 1 a turn and the next engine only P - past your first two engines, a punisher in hand
outranks every engine in it.

=== CAST ORDER ===
At every "which card do you cast now" ask, read the options top to bottom and take the FIRST match, then
take the ask again if offered - you may cast more than one spell a turn. BEFORE ANY RUNG: a row whose
VERDICT begins "taking this row STRANDS" is NEVER YOUR ANSWER. It is printed because it is LEGAL, not
because it is a play; "the engine put it on the list" is not a licence to take it, and neither is a count.
Read on past it. No rung reaches it, and nothing in ATTACKING scopes this - it is a CASTING rule.
0. BODY TURN, READ THIS RUNG FIRST ON EVERY CASTING ASK, outranking everything below. N = your line's
   "of which N are creatures"; M = their line's "M of them without a restriction against attacking".
   If M > N and the CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN
   line reads "that would KILL you": take every creature row you can afford in ONE window, cheapest
   per body first (Shield Sphere {0}, Fog Bank, FATE UNRAVELER, Master of the Feast), and no
   noncreature row. Spend the whole window; for only ONE paid body take Fate Unraveler.
1a. FATE UNRAVELER whenever your line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and theirs shows 1 or more
   "without a restriction against attacking".
1b. UNDERWORLD DREAMS or OB NIXILIS otherwise. Any punisher in the list -> cast it THIS ask, ahead of
   every other card, at every life total; a printed row is payable, and "it doesn't matter which order I
   cast these in" is always false here. Mana runs out mid-turn: buy the punisher first. ONE exception -
   at 8 life or less with a creature on their line, "Cast Fog Bank {1}{u}" goes first.
2. PEER INTO THE ABYSS whenever its row is payable - its "life -N" clause needs NO punisher. Only a
   punisher row on the SAME menu goes ahead of it; never hold it waiting for a punisher.
3. SHIELD SPHERE {0} - free, take it every time it is listed UNLESS its row prints "{their converter:".
4. FOG BANK {1}{u}, when they control any creature.
4b. MASTER OF THE FEAST {1}{b}{b} whenever their line reads 1 or more creatures. A 5/5 flier is a BODY and
   K does not brake a body; the card it hands them is the price of the only real blocker this deck casts.
5. LILIANA'S CARESS - no "{feeds:" clause, so no brake applies; with an engine row on the same menu and
   only one payable, the CARESS wins.
6. HOWLING MINE, DICTATE, PUZZLE BOX, FORCED FRUITION - only when the row's "draw converters ... on your
   battlefield:" number is 1 or more, or it is your first Mine.
7. "Cast nothing right now".

=== LAND DROP AND MANA ===
A "Land drop:" question offering a land -> PLAY THE LAND, every turn, no exceptions, whatever you plan
to cast after. It is its own decision and spends nothing: play the land AND cast the spell, same turn,
that order. Never answer "Play no land right now" or "Hold ..." while a land is playable; given several
take the colour your hand needs, black first.
"Mana available: N total (... colours you can make: {u}{b})" is correct; do not re-derive it, and neither
is a row's "{leaves N of your M ...}" count or any VERDICT inside it - they are computed from your board,
so an answer that rests on your own count disagreeing with one of them is wrong. GENERIC IS NOT A
COLOUR, and the hand line names the half a card fails on.

=== THE BRAKE ON DRAW ENGINES ===
Every card that hands the opponent a card prints "{feeds: ...}" holding K as "draw converters ... on your
battlefield: K". K counts UNDERWORLD DREAMS, FATE UNRAVELER and OB
NIXILIS only; read it off the row you are taking, never off memory or hand.
A CARESS IS NEVER IN K; it converts only at their CLEANUP, for cards held over seven.
- K (A BATTLEFIELD COUNT, NEVER A HAND COUNT) OF 1 OR MORE: cast every engine offered, every turn; a
  punisher ALSO in the list is cast first, engines with what is left. "EVERY engine" never reaches a row
  whose VERDICT begins "taking this row STRANDS" - that row is refused at every K, on every board, and
  with the game already won; "K is 1, so cast every engine" is not an answer to a strand.
- K (BATTLEFIELD) OF 0: THE BRAKE HOLDS EXACTLY THREE ROWS HERE - DICTATE, PUZZLE BOX, FORCED FRUITION.
  Those three arm THEIR deck and not yours, and "the guide does not explicitly forbid the first engine"
  is wrong about them. YOUR FIRST HOWLING MINE IS NOT ONE OF THEM: take its row at K of 0, at any life,
  as the one card on a dead menu that can still find you a punisher. A Mine row carrying "[second copy:
  you already control Howling Mine ...]" is a second Mine and the brake does hold it. MASTER OF THE FEAST
  IS NOT ON THIS BRAKE AT ALL - it is a body, and their line reading 1 or more creatures casts it at any
  K, this ask; only "of which 0 are creatures" on their line holds it. First Mine AND a punisher on one
  unaffordable ask: take the punisher.
  Every other K-of-0 row: take a wall, a punisher, a Caress, or "Cast nothing right now".
RELEASE. "draw converters in your hand: 0" -> the brake stands, however much mana is idle; "1" or more ->
the converter is one turn away, unless
   THE BOARD says otherwise: their "M ... without a restriction against attacking" over your "of which
   N are creatures" AND your life 15 or less -> no release at all. Take a wall row.
LAST CHECK ON EVERY CASTING ANSWER. A row you picked whose VERDICT begins "taking this row STRANDS" ->
your answer is WRONG, however the count reads and however certain the win looks. And: K of 0 on the
row you picked AND your
hand naming none of the three punishers
AND (for Master) their line reading "of which 0 are creatures" -> your answer is WRONG. A bad board is
not a release, and a plan reading "stack draw engines" or "cast Master of the Feast for board presence"
over a K-of-0 row describes a board you do not have.
A ROW WITHOUT "{feeds:" IS FREE OF ALL THIS - Caress, Fog Bank, Shield Sphere, a punisher, a land: cast
them when offered, since "cast a punisher as soon as possible" names a later window. "Cast nothing right
now" answers a menu of GIFTS, never one cheap permanent.

=== THE {reserve:} CLAUSE AND FLASHED ENGINES ===
A row offered OUTSIDE your own main phase may print a "{reserve: ...}" clause. A STRANDS row is struck:
answer "Cast nothing right now" and cast the stranded card in your main phase. This outranks BOTH K
branches - it is about MANA.
THE RESERVATION MUST SURVIVE THE RE-ASK: any row moving re-opens that window, and none of that gives you
mana, so the SAME row returning gets the SAME answer - your upkeep, your draw step and your main phase are
one turn and reach the SAME opponent draw step, so a flashed engine taken early collects NOT ONE extra
point of damage and pays the strand in full. Declining is not delay. "I can still cast it in Main Phase 1"
is what "your main phase this turn is its last window" DENIES: the row has just told you the mana will not
be there.
THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW is that same pass, plus the identical list stops being re-asked, and it ends on its
own at the start of your next turn - so it can never cost you a turn, and any row that moves re-opens it.
Your answer is a pass AND every row above it is braked, stranded or unaffordable -> answer the HOLD row:
it is free and it stops a dead list being put to you all turn. Keep "Cast nothing right now" for a menu
you expect to improve this turn - your land drop, or a spell resolving into the mana a row above needs.
A row tagged "[legendary: you already control one ...]" is not a play - ONE exception: your Ob Nixilis
row while the Ob Nixilis on your battlefield shows loyalty 2 or less. Cast it, and on the LEGEND RULE ask
name the LOW-loyalty copy as the one you GIVE UP; you keep a fresh 5 and two more -2 activations.

=== WALLS ===
Shield Sphere and Fog Bank are your entire defence - no removal except Ob Nixilis's -2 - and a wall in
hand blocks nothing. Shield Sphere {0} costs ZERO: with no life-to-damage LOOP of theirs on the
battlefield, take it every single time it is listed, with a full board, with no other play, on any
turn. Fog Bank {1}{u} (0/2 flier, prevents all combat damage to and
from itself): their line shows ANY creature -> cast it the first ask it is offered, even as the only
row, even when it taps you out.

=== BLOCKING ===
This overrides the combat prompt's general hint about trades and about taking damage while ahead on
life. Every blocker line prints a "STOPS all ..." clause and a parenthesis naming who dies; both true.
A TRAMPLER IS NOT STOPPED BY BLOCKING IT. With [trample] on the A-row, your B-row prints the leftover in
the same parenthesis ("2 tramples to your face") and it still hits you; for Fog Bank the trample-through
is every point past your blocker's printed toughness.
HEADER SAYS THE ATTACK IS LETHAL -> arithmetic first. Add up every "deals N" (TOTAL) and block enough
that TOTAL minus what you stop is LESS than your printed life; at 0 you lose. With fewer blockers than
attackers, assign each to the biggest amount ACTUALLY ERASED - for a trampler, "deals N" minus the
leftover on your B-row - so a 5-power non-trampler outranks a 4-power trampler leaving 2 behind. Among
surviving assignments take the one leaving you the HIGHEST life; if none survives, block the biggest.
TWO PRICES THE RUNGS DO NOT COUNT. (a) When "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: yours - ..." names exactly
ONE card and that card is the blocker offered (Fate Unraveler, almost always), "(both die)" and "your
blocker dies, attacker lives" are OFF THE MENU for it below the lethal check: block on a "(you kill it,
your blocker lives)" line or answer BLOCKS: none. (b) A "(lifelink: ... they gain N)" clause is damage
you cannot undo - add that N to the line's cost.
Then the rungs, in order. WHILE (a) IS LIVE - your ONE punisher is the blocker offered - NO RUNG BELOW
reaches its "(both die)" or "your blocker dies, attacker lives" line: that blocker takes its "(you kill
it, your blocker lives)" line or stays home, and the attacker it declines to eat goes to another blocker
or to nobody, whatever the unblocked total comes to.
0. Subtract what you would stop from the total. If what is left would put your printed life at 8 OR LESS,
   block the BIGGEST "deals N" you may block, and take that line when it kills your blocker. Bounded by
   (a) and (b). Above 8, rung 1 decides.
1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" - take it, every time.
2. "(neither dies)" - take it. One wall per attacker, biggest "deals N" first; never two on one attacker
   while another goes unblocked.
3. One blocker, several attackers: prefer a line where your blocker LIVES; take the dying line only if
   leaving that attacker unblocked puts you at 8 or below.
4. "your blocker dies, attacker lives" - only when the header says LETHAL, your life is 8 or less, or it
   is that blocker's only line.
5. "BLOCKS: none" only when every blocker is already assigned, or every line offered is rung 4 at a
   healthy life total.

=== ATTACKING ===
Read each attacker's "their untapped blockers" tag and answer from it; it is computed, not a starting
point for argument. SEND when the A-line prints NO tag at all - nothing can block it, subject only to
the last-body rule below - and SEND when EVERY name on the tag reads "(you kill it, your attacker
lives)" with no "(blocking trigger:" and no "(lifelink:" in it.
ONE FATAL NAME IS A STOP, however many friendly names sit beside it: THEY pick the blocker and pick the
worst for you, so "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" anywhere in a tag -> ATTACK: none for that
creature, and FATE UNRAVELER additionally never goes into "(both die)".
A "- GANG BLOCK: ... enough to kill this attacker" CLAUSE ANYWHERE IN A TAG IS A STOP for that attacker
below the lethal check, including at the END of a tag of friendly parentheses; "they will probably block
with just one" is what it refutes, and "each result above is a LONE blocker only" says the same. Same for
a COLLAPSED tag - "they have N untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest X (you kill it, your
attacker lives)" prices ONE blocker, so N of 1 makes the parenthesis the story while N of 2 or more
measures your attacker against the SUM of their bodies: ATTACK: none.
LIFE THEY GAIN IS A PRICE: "(neither dies (blocking trigger: they may gain 2))" and "(you kill it, your
attacker lives (lifelink: they gain 1))" are not all-free tags and the send rungs do not fire on them.
With "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" printed those numbers double and point at
you: send ONLY attackers whose A-line prints neither clause, else ATTACK: none.
A LOW LIFE TOTAL KEEPS IT HOME: count your untapped, non-summoning-sick creatures, and a count of ONE
with printed life 8 or less does not attack, whatever its tag says. A CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN line reading
"that would KILL you" is the REASON that body stays home: it blocks one of those attackers. "I am dead
next turn anyway" is not an argument, and rung 0 of CAST ORDER answers a CASTING ask about creature ROWS
- it never answers an ATTACK question. Master of the Feast also does not untap under Intruder Alarm.

=== LETHAL CHECK - RUN IT EVERY TURN ===
EVERYTHING GOES once this clears: swing with everything, Fate Unraveler included.
P = the punisher names on your battlefield. FIRST, THE BURST: Ob Nixilis out with 2 or more loyalty and
any creature of theirs on his -2 list -> that -2 is 2 x P damage NOW; at or above their printed life take
it and stop counting, since that window precedes combat and their next draw step. THEN THE SLOW COUNT:
each Mine and each Dictate is 1 extra card per turn, a Puzzle Box is their hand size off the "Opponent
hand size:" line. Multiply extra cards by P, add 1 x P for their normal draw, add your unblocked power;
at or above their printed life, attack with everything and cast every engine offered. A COUNT THAT LEAVES
THEM AT 1 OR MORE IS NOT LETHAL, and the engine that produced it is the wrong card this turn.

=== FORCED SACRIFICE - THE WALL GOES, NEVER A PUNISHER ===
Every row is a creature you LOSE, there is no decline, each prints "[you SACRIFICE this; they gain N life
(its toughness)]", and the header's tie-break says to pay the least - IGNORE THAT TIE-BREAK WHEN IT
POINTS AT A PUNISHER. ORDER: SHIELD SPHERE first (one carrying -0/-1 counters is the best row of all),
then FOG BANK, then MASTER OF THE FEAST, and FATE UNRAVELER only as the sole row. WITH NO LIFE-TO-DAMAGE
CONVERTER OF THEIRS IN PLAY, THE LIFE THEY GAIN NEVER DECIDES THIS ROW, in either direction: the wall
goes when it pays them MORE, and the punisher stays when it would pay them LESS - "the wall pays them
more, so the punisher is the cheaper row" is the trade you are refusing.
A CONVERTER OF THEIRS REORDERS THE WALLS. Then "[they gain N life]" is also N off YOUR total in the same
event, so among the WALLS take the LOWEST N on the board - Fog Bank over a fresh Shield Sphere - and read
the number as damage to you. A punisher still goes last at every N.

=== CARD FACTS ===
UNDERWORLD DREAMS {B}{B}{B} - enchantment; 1 damage per card they draw.
FATE UNRAVELER {3}{B} - 3/4 enchantment creature, same trigger; punisher AND blocker.
OB NIXILIS, THE HATE-TWISTED {3}{B}{B} - legendary planeswalker, 5 loyalty, same trigger, plus "-2:
  Destroy target creature. Its controller draws two cards." That -2 is removal AND a draw step you
  control - the two cards fire every punisher (2 x P damage) on any creature of theirs, wall or token.
  Aim it FIRST at a creature whose text makes more creatures ("create", "put a ... token"), then at the
  biggest body; never a token while its maker is on the list. Offered only your own creature, pass.
HOWLING MINE {2} - artifact; each player draws an extra card each draw step while it is UNTAPPED.
DICTATE OF KRUPHIX {1}{U}{U} - FLASH enchantment; each player draws an extra card each draw step. Flash
  only explains why its row appears outside your main phase; it is NOT a licence to cast it. A Dictate row
  is an ENGINE row - the brake and the {reserve:} rule govern it exactly as they govern a Mine.
TEFERI'S PUZZLE BOX {4} - artifact; at EACH player's draw step that player bottoms their hand and redraws
  that many, so their hand size is damage every turn - and YOUR hand goes with it. With one of yours out,
  a card in hand does not survive to your next turn: cast it this turn or lose it.
FORCED FRUITION {4}{U}{U} - whenever the opponent casts a spell they draw SEVEN cards: 7 damage a spell
  at one punisher. A FEEDER, not a converter - holding it never releases the brake.
MASTER OF THE FEAST {1}{B}{B} - 5/5 flier; at YOUR upkeep each opponent draws a card.
LILIANA'S CARESS {1}{B} - they lose 2 life per card THEY discard, and your Mines and Puzzle Box make them
  overdraw and discard at cleanup. A DISCARD punisher, never in K.
SHIELD SPHERE {0} - 0/6 defender, free; takes a -0/-1 counter per block, so a fresh one blocks six times.
PEER INTO THE ABYSS {4}{B}{B}{B} - target player draws half their library and loses half their life, then
  asks "target opponent" or "target controller": answer "target opponent", NEVER "target controller",
  which points the spell at YOU.

=== SANGUINE BOND / EXQUISITE BLOOD, THEIRS ===
Both halves out -> KEEP CASTING PUNISHERS AND KEEP RACING: their chain is entered by YOUR life going DOWN
and THEIRS going UP, so your punisher damage enters it NOWHERE. THE SAME GOES FOR OB NIXILIS'S -2 AND
EVERY CARD YOU HAND THEM: making them DRAW is life THEY LOSE, which starts no chain - keep activating the
-2 and keep the engines coming.
WHILE BOTH HALVES STAND, DO NOT PUT A BODY ON THE BOARD FOR THEM TO CONVERT. A creature row of yours
printing "{their converter: ... ANY nonzero payment here is fatal}" is refused at EVERY price, {0}
included: that clause is not about what the row costs YOU to cast, it is about what THEY collect when
that body dies or is sacrificed - a forced-sacrifice effect gains them its toughness, and that gain
enters the chain and does not stop. With no creature of yours on the battlefield their edict gains them
nothing. Take a noncreature row, or decline.
ONE half out only makes life they gain worth more, so the LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER rule still
governs attacking.
=== MULLIGAN, BOTTOMING, DISCARD ===
The prompt counts the hand for you ("counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells") - read that, never count
it yourself. You run 26 lands and no one-mana card, so a shipped six is most often a two-lander again and
"no cheap play" never justifies a ship.
- TWO OR MORE LANDS AND AT LEAST ONE SPELL: KEEP.
- ALL LANDS or ZERO LANDS: ship.
- ONE LAND ON THE UNTOUCHED SEVEN: ship; every card that advances the plan costs three or more.
- ONE LAND AT "(keeping 6)" OR LOWER: keep - the ship costs a card for the same odds - and build it at
  the bottom ask.
BOTTOMING AND CLEANUP DISCARD BOTH SEND THE MOST EXPENSIVE SPELL IN HAND FIRST - Forced Fruition, Peer
into the Abyss, Puzzle Box, a second copy of an engine - and a CREATURE goes only once every costlier
noncreature has gone. KEEP in this order so any mana count is buildable: Shield Sphere (zero mana), your
only punisher, lands up to four, then spells by cost; bottom a land only if the keep still holds FOUR OR
MORE. Cleanup is answered "PUT: n, m" - the numbers ONLY, nothing in brackets.
