DRAW AND DIE - UB FORCED-DRAW PUNISH

The opponent's draw step is your kill. Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler and Ob Nixilis each deal 1
damage every time that opponent draws a card, and they stack; Howling Mine, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's
Puzzle Box and Forced Fruition make them draw MORE. Put the damage on the battlefield FIRST, then hand
them cards. Every turn: land a punisher, wall off their creatures, or - once a punisher is out - stack
another engine. You win on their draw step whether or not you attack.
THE ARITHMETIC: with P punishers and D engines out their draw step costs P x (D + 1), so the next
punisher adds D + 1 a turn and the next engine only P - past your first two engines, a punisher in hand
outranks every engine in it.

=== CAST ORDER ===
At every "which card do you cast now" ask, read the options top to bottom and take the FIRST match, then
take the ask again if offered - you may cast more than one spell a turn.
0. BODY TURN, outranking everything below. N = your line's "of which N are creatures"; M = their
   line's "M of them without a restriction against attacking". If M > N and the CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN
   line reads "that would KILL you": take every creature row you can afford in ONE window, cheapest
   per body first (Shield Sphere {0}, Fog Bank, FATE UNRAVELER, Master of the Feast), and no
   noncreature row. Read each row's "{leaves N of your M untapped mana sources untapped}" clause and
   spend the whole window. For only ONE paid body take Fate Unraveler.
1a. FATE UNRAVELER whenever your line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and theirs shows 1 or more
   "without a restriction against attacking".
1b. UNDERWORLD DREAMS or OB NIXILIS otherwise. Any punisher in the list -> cast it THIS ask, ahead of
   every other card, at every life total; a printed row is payable, and "it doesn't matter which order I
   cast these in" is always false here. Mana runs out mid-turn: buy the punisher first. ONE exception -
   at 8 life or less with a creature on their line, "Cast Fog Bank {1}{u}" goes first.
2. PEER INTO THE ABYSS, with a punisher already out.
3. SHIELD SPHERE {0} - free, take it every time it is listed.
4. FOG BANK {1}{u}, when they control any creature.
5. LILIANA'S CARESS - no "{feeds:" clause, so no brake applies; with an engine row on the same menu and
   only one payable, the CARESS wins.
6. HOWLING MINE, DICTATE, PUZZLE BOX, FORCED FRUITION, MASTER OF THE FEAST - only when the row's "draw
   converters ... on your battlefield:" number is 1 or more, or it is your first Mine.
7. "Cast nothing right now".

=== LAND DROP AND MANA ===
A "Land drop:" question offering a land -> PLAY THE LAND, every turn, no exceptions, whatever you plan
to cast after. It is its own decision and spends nothing: play the land AND cast the spell, same turn,
that order. Never answer "Play no land right now" or "Hold ..." while a land is playable; given several
take the colour your hand needs, black first.
"Mana available: N total (... colours you can make: {u}{b})" is correct; do not re-derive it. GENERIC IS
NOT A COLOUR: Underworld Dreams {B}{B}{B} needs three black sources, while Howling Mine {2} and Fog Bank
{1}{U} take any source for their generic. You cannot hold mana on your own main for your own sorcery-
speed card - punishers, Mines, Dictates and Caress are all sorcery speed, so a castable one has a row
already.

=== THE BRAKE ON DRAW ENGINES ===
Every card that hands the opponent a card prints "{feeds: ...}" holding K as "draw converters (they fire
when the opponent DRAWS) on your battlefield: K". K counts UNDERWORLD DREAMS, FATE UNRAVELER and OB
NIXILIS only; read it off the row you are taking, never off memory or hand. The row prints "in your
hand:" TWICE: "draw converters in your hand: N" counts those three and is what the release reads, while
a bare "in your hand: 0" at the END of a row is the "discard punishers" count of Liliana's Caress.
A CARESS IS NEVER IN K; it converts only at their CLEANUP, for cards held over seven.
- K OF 1 OR MORE: cast every engine offered, every turn; a punisher ALSO in the list is cast first,
  engines with what is left.
- K OF 0: your FIRST Howling Mine is still cast, on a two-row menu, on turn 4 or turn 11 - a SECOND
  Mine's row carries "[second copy: you already control Howling Mine ...]" and a first does not. First
  Mine AND a punisher on one unaffordable ask: take the punisher. With a Mine already out, any further
  K-of-0 row is BRAKED - take a wall, a punisher, a Caress, or "Cast nothing right now".
RELEASE. "draw converters in your hand: 0" -> the brake stands, however much mana is idle; "1" or more ->
the converter is one turn away, but only if BOTH hold:
 (i) COLOUR. The mana line prints "sources that can make each: {U} n, {B} n"; Underworld Dreams needs
   {B} 3, Ob Nixilis {B} 2 and 5 total, Fate Unraveler {B} 1 and 4 total. Needing more black than that,
   with no land in hand to add one, is NOT one turn away: brake stands.
 (ii) THE BOARD. Their "M ... without a restriction against attacking" over your "of which N are
   creatures" AND your life 15 or less -> no release at all. Take a wall row.
LAST-DITCH RELEASE, when ALL FOUR are printed: the row says "and so do YOU: 1 extra card per turn (this
engine is SYMMETRIC - it feeds both players)"; "draw converters in your hand: 0"; the menu has NO wall
row and NO punisher row at any price; CRACK-BACK puts you at 10 or lower. Then cast that engine - the
only card there that can find a punisher. Mine and Dictate print SYMMETRIC; Master of the Feast and
Forced Fruition never release this way. MASTER OF THE FEAST is the other exception: with no punisher
out, cast it only when their line reads 1 or more creatures.
LAST CHECK BEFORE YOU SEND: K of 0 on the row you picked AND your hand naming none of the three punishers
AND (for Master) their line reading "of which 0 are creatures" -> your answer is WRONG. A bad board is
not a release, and a plan reading "stack draw engines" or "cast Master of the Feast for board presence"
over a K-of-0 row describes a board you do not have.
A ROW WITHOUT "{feeds:" IS FREE OF ALL THIS - Caress, Fog Bank, Shield Sphere, a punisher, a land: cast
them when offered, since "cast a punisher as soon as possible" names a later window. "Cast nothing right
now" answers a menu of GIFTS, never one cheap permanent.

=== THE {reserve:} CLAUSE AND FLASHED ENGINES ===
A row offered OUTSIDE your own main phase may print "{reserve: this row is INSTANT SPEED ... leaves N
sources, and <PUNISHER> in your hand needs M - it is SORCERY SPEED, so your main phase this turn is its
last window}". When it names a punisher, DO NOT TAKE THAT ROW: answer "Cast nothing right now" and cast
that punisher in your main phase. This outranks BOTH K branches - it is about MANA - and a plan that
takes the row then says "in Main Phase 1, cast <that punisher>" is one the row printed impossible.
NO {reserve:} CLAUSE PRINTED -> subtract yourself: "Mana available: N total", ADD 1 if "Land drop:" reads
NOT yet used and your hand holds a land, SUBTRACT what the row's "{leaves N of your M ...}" clause spends.
Below the cheapest punisher your hand line shows -> "Cast nothing right now". Your hand line prints those
punishers as "[no cast row now: sorcery speed - only in your own main phase ...]".
THE RESERVATION MUST SURVIVE THE RE-ASK: the engine re-opens that window whenever any row moves - a new
card, a changed count in a "{feeds:}" clause, one more card in a forecast - and none of that gives you
mana, so the SAME row returning gets the SAME answer.
THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW ("Hold priority: pass now, and do not ask me again - this turn or later -
until one of the rows above changes ...") is that same pass, plus the identical list stops being re-asked. Passing AND "[you
declined this exact list N times already this turn]" printed -> answer the HOLD row; that line absent ->
"Cast nothing right now", because the next window may differ.
A row tagged "[legendary: you already control one ...]" is likewise not a play.

=== WALLS ===
Shield Sphere and Fog Bank are your entire defence - no removal except Ob Nixilis's -2 - and a wall in
hand blocks nothing. Shield Sphere {0} costs ZERO: take it every single time it is listed, with a full
board, with no other play, on any turn. Fog Bank {1}{u} (0/2 flier, prevents all combat damage to and
from itself): their line shows ANY creature -> cast it the first ask it is offered, even as the only
row, even when it taps you out.

=== BLOCKING ===
This overrides the combat prompt's general hint about trades and about taking damage while ahead on
life. Every blocker line prints a "STOPS all ..." clause and a parenthesis naming who dies; both true.
A TRAMPLER IS NOT STOPPED BY BLOCKING IT. With [trample] on the A-row, your B-row prints the leftover in
the same parenthesis ("2 tramples to your face") and it still hits you; for Fog Bank the trample-through
is every point past your blocker's printed toughness. Never put your last blocker on a trampler while a
NON-trampler of equal or larger "deals N" is in the list.
HEADER SAYS THE ATTACK IS LETHAL -> arithmetic first. Add up every "deals N" (TOTAL) and block enough
that TOTAL minus what you stop is LESS than your printed life; at 0 you lose. With fewer blockers than
attackers, assign each to the biggest amount ACTUALLY ERASED - for a trampler, "deals N" minus the
leftover on your B-row - so a 5-power non-trampler outranks a 4-power trampler leaving 2 behind. Among
surviving assignments take the one leaving you the HIGHEST life; if none survives, block the biggest.
TWO PRICES THE RUNGS DO NOT COUNT. (a) When "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: yours - ..." names exactly
ONE card and that card is the blocker offered (Fate Unraveler, almost always), "(both die)" and "your
blocker dies, attacker lives" are OFF THE MENU for it below the lethal check: block on a "(you kill it,
your blocker lives)" line or answer BLOCKS: none. (b) A "(lifelink: ... they gain N)" clause is damage
you cannot undo - add that N to the line's cost.
Then the rungs, in order:
0. Subtract what you would stop from the total. If what is left would put your printed life at 8 OR LESS,
   block the BIGGEST "deals N" you may block - even when that line kills your blocker and a free kill on
   a small attacker sits there. Bounded by (a) and (b). Above 8, rung 1 decides.
1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" - take it, every time.
2. "(neither dies)" - take it. One wall per attacker, biggest "deals N" first; never two on one attacker
   while another goes unblocked.
3. One blocker, several attackers: prefer a line where your blocker LIVES over one that kills it, even
   when the dying line stops more - take the dying line only if leaving that attacker unblocked puts
   you at 8 or below.
4. "your blocker dies, attacker lives" - only when the header says LETHAL, your life is 8 or less, or it
   is that blocker's only line.
5. "BLOCKS: none" only when every blocker is already assigned, or every line offered is rung 4 at a
   healthy life total. Fate Unraveler and Master of the Feast block well on a "you kill it, your blocker
   lives" line, when not needed for a lethal swing.

=== ATTACKING ===
Read each attacker's "their untapped blockers" tag and answer from it; it is computed, not a starting
point for argument. SEND when the A-line prints NO tag at all - nothing can block it, subject only to
the last-body rule below - and SEND when EVERY name on the tag reads "(you kill it, your attacker
lives)" with no "(blocking trigger:" and no "(lifelink:" in it.
ONE FATAL NAME IS A STOP, however many friendly names sit beside it: THEY pick the blocker and pick the
worst for you, so "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" anywhere in a tag -> ATTACK: none for that
creature, and FATE UNRAVELER additionally never goes into "(both die)".
A "- GANG BLOCK: ... enough to kill this attacker" CLAUSE ANYWHERE IN A TAG IS A STOP for that attacker
below the lethal check, including at the END of a tag of friendly parentheses; "they will probably block
with just one" is what it refutes, and "each result above is a LONE blocker only" says the same. Same for
a COLLAPSED tag - "they have N untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest X (you kill it, your
attacker lives)" prices ONE blocker, so N of 1 makes the parenthesis the story while N of 2 or more
measures your attacker against the SUM of their bodies: ATTACK: none.
LIFE THEY GAIN IS A PRICE: "(neither dies (blocking trigger: they may gain 2))" and "(you kill it, your
attacker lives (lifelink: they gain 1))" are not all-free tags and the send rungs do not fire on them.
With "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" printed those numbers double and point at
you: send ONLY attackers whose A-line prints neither clause, else ATTACK: none.
A LOW LIFE TOTAL KEEPS IT HOME: count your untapped, non-summoning-sick creatures, and a count of ONE
with printed life 8 or less does not attack, whatever its tag says - usually Master of the Feast, which
also does not untap under Intruder Alarm.

=== LETHAL CHECK - RUN IT EVERY TURN ===
EVERYTHING GOES once this clears: swing with everything, Fate Unraveler included.
P = the punisher names on your battlefield. FIRST, THE BURST: Ob Nixilis out with 2 or more loyalty and
any creature of theirs on his -2 list -> that -2 is 2 x P damage NOW; at or above their printed life take
it and stop counting, since that window precedes combat and their next draw step. THEN THE SLOW COUNT:
each Mine and each Dictate is 1 extra card per turn, a Puzzle Box is their hand size off the "Opponent
hand size:" line. Multiply extra cards by P, add 1 x P for their normal draw, add your unblocked power;
at or above their printed life, attack with everything and cast every engine offered.

=== FORCED SACRIFICE - THE WALL GOES, NEVER A PUNISHER ===
Every row is a creature you LOSE, there is no decline, each prints "[you SACRIFICE this; they gain N life
(its toughness)]", and the header's tie-break says to pay the least - IGNORE THAT TIE-BREAK WHEN IT
POINTS AT A PUNISHER. ORDER: SHIELD SPHERE first (one carrying -0/-1 counters is the best row of all),
then FOG BANK, then MASTER OF THE FEAST, and FATE UNRAVELER only as the sole row - take the wall even
when its toughness pays them MORE life. Any row named on that prompt's "DRAW PUNISHERS ... yours" line
goes last.

=== CARD FACTS ===
UNDERWORLD DREAMS {B}{B}{B} - enchantment; 1 damage per card they draw.
FATE UNRAVELER {3}{B} - 3/4 enchantment creature, same trigger; punisher AND blocker.
OB NIXILIS, THE HATE-TWISTED {3}{B}{B} - legendary planeswalker, 5 loyalty, same trigger, plus "-2:
  Destroy target creature. Its controller draws two cards." That -2 is removal AND a draw step you
  control - the two cards fire every punisher (2 x P damage) on any creature of theirs, wall or token.
  Aim it FIRST at a creature whose text makes more creatures ("create", "put a ... token"), then at the
  biggest body; never a token while its maker is on the list. Offered only your own creature, pass.
HOWLING MINE {2} - artifact; each player draws an extra card each draw step while it is UNTAPPED.
DICTATE OF KRUPHIX {1}{U}{U} - FLASH enchantment; each player draws an extra card each draw step, and its
  flash is why its row can appear in your upkeep or draw step where no punisher can be beside it.
TEFERI'S PUZZLE BOX {4} - artifact; at each player's draw step that player bottoms their hand and redraws
  that many, so their hand size is damage every turn.
FORCED FRUITION {4}{U}{U} - whenever the opponent casts a spell they draw SEVEN cards: 7 damage a spell
  at one punisher. A FEEDER, not a converter - holding it never releases the brake.
MASTER OF THE FEAST {1}{B}{B} - 5/5 flier; at YOUR upkeep each opponent draws a card.
LILIANA'S CARESS {1}{B} - they lose 2 life per card THEY discard, and your Mines and Puzzle Box make them
  overdraw and discard at cleanup. A DISCARD punisher, never in K.
SHIELD SPHERE {0} - 0/6 defender, free; takes a -0/-1 counter per block, so a fresh one blocks six times.
PEER INTO THE ABYSS {4}{B}{B}{B} - target player draws half their library and loses half their life, then
  asks "target opponent" or "target controller": answer "target opponent", NEVER "target controller",
  which points the spell at YOU.

=== SANGUINE BOND / EXQUISITE BLOOD, THEIRS ===
Both halves out -> KEEP CASTING PUNISHERS AND KEEP RACING. Their pair is entered by YOUR life going DOWN
and THEIR life going UP, so your punisher damage enters the chain NOWHERE, exactly as the forecast's
"LOOP SCOPE: ... THIS number does not enter it" says; "LOOP CAUTION: any life you pay is fatal rather
than merely expensive" is about life YOU lose and is also true. Cast punishers and engines, use the -2,
and do not let your own life move. ONE half out only makes life they gain worth more, so the
LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER rule still governs attacking.

=== MULLIGAN, BOTTOMING, DISCARD ===
The prompt counts the hand for you ("counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells") - read that, never count
it yourself. The question is whether this seven is likelier to win than a fresh six. You run 26 lands, so
a third land by turn three and a fourth by turn five are both likely and a shipped six is most often a
two-lander again; punishers cost three to five; no one-mana card exists, so "no cheap play" never
justifies a ship, and "I will keep whatever I draw next" runs a chain to four.
- TWO OR MORE LANDS AND AT LEAST ONE SPELL: KEEP.
- ALL LANDS or ZERO LANDS: ship.
- ONE LAND ON THE UNTOUCHED SEVEN: ship; every card that advances the plan costs three or more.
- ONE LAND AT "(keeping 6)": the ship now costs TWO cards for the same odds. Keep when the "would cover
  the cost of:" line names a card or the hand holds a punisher you reach on the third land (Fate
  Unraveler at four); ship when it reads "would not cover any spell in it" with no punisher in hand.
- ONE LAND AT "(keeping 5)" OR LOWER: keep, and build it at the bottom ask.
Tiebreakers between hands that already clear the land count, never a reason to ship: prefer a punisher or
a Fog Bank, and a coverage line naming a card. Colour is a tiebreaker, never a gate - 18 of your 26 lands
make black, 18 make blue, ten make both.
BOTTOMING sends your most EXPENSIVE spell first - Forced Fruition, Peer into the Abyss, Puzzle Box, a
second copy of an engine - and KEEPS in this order so any mana count is buildable: Shield Sphere (zero
mana), your only punisher, lands up to four, then spells by cost; bottom a land only if the keep still
holds FOUR OR MORE. CLEANUP DISCARD is that order from the end: the ask prints your hand NUMBERED and you
answer "PUT: n, m" - the numbers ONLY, nothing in brackets - most expensive first, then a land when your
line prints six or more, never Shield Sphere or your only punisher until nothing else is left.

=== SITUATIONS ===
- A crack-back line reading "that would KILL you", no creatures of yours, and a menu of Ob Nixilis {3}{b}{b} "{leaves 0
  of your 5 ...}", Master of the Feast "{leaves 2 of your 5 ...}" and Shield Sphere {0}: body turn, so
  take Master AND Sphere.
- 3 life, Master of the Feast (5/5) and Fate Unraveler (3/4) on the same fatal crack-back verdict, and
  Master's row at K of 0: take FATE UNRAVELER - neither body buys survival, only one buys damage.
- Your DRAW step, "Mana available: 4 total", "Land drop: NOT yet used", a flashed Dictate row for 3 and
  Ob Nixilis {3}{b}{b} in hand: 4 plus the land drop is 5, so answer "Cast nothing right now", cast Ob
  Nixilis in main phase, and answer the same if that row returns.
- A two-row ask whose only spell is a second Howling Mine at K of 0: "Cast nothing right now"; a two-row
  ask whose only spell is a Liliana's Caress: cast it, whatever your plan says.
- Their life 3, four punishers out, Ob Nixilis's -2 offered with creatures on its list: take it, since
  2 x 4 = 8 ends it.
