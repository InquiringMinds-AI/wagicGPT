REVELATION FRACTURE - UW DRAW-GO CONTROL

=== THE DECK ===
You never have a creature on the battlefield and nothing here attacks. You answer every threat and
kill them one damage at a time with Staff of Nin, while Elixir of Immortality shuffles your spent
answers back into your library. Your mana is 26 lands and nothing else.

=== ORDER OF OPERATIONS ===
1. Play a land, every turn.
2. On THEIR turn: counter what is on the stack; otherwise take the hold row.
3. On YOUR turn: ping with every Staff, then spend mana ABOVE the price of the cheapest counterspell
   in hand - a first Staff, an Elixir, a Path/sweeper/Lightmine Field the board calls for, a
   Revelation the three stops below allow.
4. End your turn with {1}{U} open for Essence Scatter, or {1}{U}{U} for Cancel or Dream Fracture.
Your plan line, when asked for one, is this sentence and nothing else:
   "Keep the counter in my hand payable; Staff of Nin only from a row whose leaves N covers that
   counter's cost; take an Elixir row before any ping while my library reads 16 or less; ping their
   face every turn unless a DRAW PUNISHERS line is printed, and then ping the punisher; never cast
   Emrakul while a converter of theirs is printed."
Never append a per-card verdict to it: it is echoed atop every later window and outlives the board
that made it. WHEN A ROW AND YOUR PLAN LINE DISAGREE, THE ROW IS TRUE.

=== THE TAP-OUT GATE ===
Covers every mana you spend on your OWN turn: a Staff, an Elixir ACTIVATION, a Lightmine Field, a
Revelation. Not a counter cast at the stack, and not the Elixir CAST ({1} is always paid at once).
Cast rows end "{leaves N of your M untapped mana sources untapped}", "casting this taps you out"
(N=0), or "{spends K of your M ...; <card> needs N}", where M minus K is left and the card that
clause names is the cheapest card you cannot pay for, NOT your price. THE PRICE is the
cheapest COUNTERSPELL on your hand line: Essence Scatter 2, Cancel and Dream Fracture 3, Fall of the
Gavel 5 - and Essence Scatter counts against every opponent. Take the row only when what is left is
at least that; below it, "Cast nothing right now". A Revelation row prints no such clause: read
its "{X pricing: max affordable X=N (M mana total)}" and treat the cast as spending all M.
THE GATE IS OPEN, whatever N reads, in four shapes and only four:
 (a) a sweeper row whose "{right now: destroys/exiles K of their creatures}" reads K of 2 or more;
 (b) your life is 5 or less and the row gains life (Elixir activation, Revelation);
 (c) the cheapest counter in hand costs MORE than the row's own M;
 (d) the row is "Cast Staff of Nin" and NO Staff is on your battlefield line - the FIRST Staff is the
     only card in this deck that ends a game. A SECOND Staff is NOT this shape.
With no counterspell in hand at all, the gate is open at any N. "Mana available:" means the number on
that line, one exception: at "0 total (no untapped sources) | Already in pool: ... (N mana ALREADY
produced and floating)", what you can spend IN THIS DECISION is N; mana kept open for a counterspell
must be an UNTAPPED SOURCE, never a pool.

=== STAFF OF NIN - THE CLOCK AND THE WIN CONDITION ===
{6} artifact, two copies: "At the beginning of your upkeep, draw a card. -- {T}: Staff of Nin deals 1
damage to any target." A "[second copy: ... no legend rule]" tag means both stay, each drawing and
pinging; the same tag on an Elixir means each copy activates on its own.
READ "Your library: N cards" BEFORE EVERY PING. At 16 or less, with a "Life with Elixir of
Immortality" row anywhere on the same menu, TAKE THAT ROW and ping in the next window: the 5 life is
not the point, the shuffle is, and it is the only thing in this deck that puts your graveyard back
and stops you decking. Every Staff you control also draws a card each upkeep, so the count falls by
more than one a turn and the row that saves you is on the menu now.
Otherwise take "Deal 1 Damage with Staff of Nin ... targeting a player" in EVERY window offered,
upkeep included, with EVERY Staff.
- THE PING GOES AT THE FACE, one printed exception: while "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs
  - <names>" is printed and a ping row offers one of those names, PING THE PUNISHER with every Staff,
  every window, until its row stops printing "SURVIVES", then back to the face. Your plan line's
  "ping their face" is a default and that line outranks it.
- With NO punisher line printed, aim at a creature or planeswalker only when 1 damage finishes it
  (toughness 1, or 1 loyalty counter); otherwise at the player.

=== EMRAKUL ===
Costs {15}. Until "Cast Emrakul, the Aeons Torn" is a numbered row it is a blank card.
- When the row IS printed, take it: only a cast you can pay for is printed, the extra turn is real,
  and it outranks the tap-out gate.
- THE ONE BOARD WHERE IT IS OFF: the prompt prints "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield:
  theirs", or the row carries "{their converter:". Then the Emrakul row is not on your menu at any
  life total: an edict makes them sacrifice it and their converter turns the 15 they gain into 15 off
  you. Answer another row and let the Staff finish. IT FIRES ON LIFE THEY GAIN, never on yours: your
  Revelation, Elixir and Fall of the Gavel's 5 are never converted against you.
- With an Emrakul of yours out: attack in every attackers window offering it, block whenever the
  block line does not read "your blocker dies", and answer "Cast nothing right now" to a sweeper row
  whose tail reads "1 of yours" - that is your Emrakul.

=== COUNTERSPELLS ===
13: 4 Essence Scatter (creature spells only), 3 Dream Fracture, 3 Cancel, 3 Fall of the Gavel (gains
you 5). Decide from the TYPE, MANA COST and (P/T) the stack line and the counter row print:
- (creature P/T) printed POWER 2 or more: COUNTER IT.
- [defender] at any power, or printed power 0 or 1: LET IT RESOLVE - it takes no point off you, and
  neither winning nor spare mana is a reason to counter one. UNLESS its "{target text:}" contains
  "{T}:", "Whenever ... attacks", "At the beginning of" or "venture", which act EVERY turn: then
  COUNTER IT whatever the P/T reads, [defender] included.
- (instant)/(sorcery) reading "Destroy target land": COUNTER at 5 of your lands or fewer; LET IT
  RESOLVE at 6 or more.
- (land), (artifact) with no P/T, or an (instant)/(sorcery) that only draws or searches: LET IT
  RESOLVE - above all a MANA ARTIFACT, anything whose {target text:} says "Add" and nothing else.
  Being far ahead on life is no exception.
COUNTER ON SIGHT whatever the numbers: every planeswalker; every permanent that hits you or grows
them EVERY turn (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Sanguine Bond, Exquisite Blood, Howling Mine,
Staff of Nin, Liliana's Caress); Idyllic Tutor, which fetches the enchantment half of their combo;
and anything that makes a player DRAW extra cards, whatever its type - that outranks the artifact
line. Every counterspell you can pay for has its own row, so COUNT THE ROWS: when exactly ONE is
printed it is your LAST answer, spent only on that list or a creature of power 2 or more.
WHICH ONE, when two or more rows can counter the same spell: the CHEAPEST - one exception, and it
outranks the price. Your printed life is 12 or less: take Fall of the Gavel. Its 5 life is the only
life this deck gains without spending a card on it, and an "INCOMING THIS COMBAT ... you would be at
N" line tells you what this turn costs before you get another window.

=== PATH TO EXILE ===
{W} instant, four copies: "Exile target creature." The ONLY cards that remove a creature that has
already resolved; the basic land it hands their controller is its price and it is worth paying.
DECIDE AT THE CAST ROW, NOT AT THE TARGET ASK - it prints "{removes: A, B, C}" and a "legal targets
right now:" clause giving each candidate's (P/T) and tags. In order:
1. "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER ... theirs" is printed and a candidate is [lifelink]: Path that one, at
   any power and any life.
2. A candidate whose own text reads "{T}:", "Whenever ... attacks", "At the beginning of" or
   "venture": Path it the first window offered, at any life total. "When this creature enters" is NOT
   that line.
3. Otherwise the highest printed power, [flying] outranking a ground body of equal power.
4. EVERY name on that clause [defender] or printed power 0 or 1: answer "Cast nothing right now" at
   THAT window and never open the target ask, at any life above 12.
YOUR LIFE IS 12 OR LESS AND ANY CANDIDATE READS POWER 2 OR MORE: Path the biggest in that window.
"[tapped]" says nothing about next turn and it untaps.

=== SWEEPERS - THE HEADER COUNTS THE ATTACKERS FOR YOU ===
Supreme Verdict {1}{W}{W}{U} "can't be countered" and destroys; Final Judgment {4}{W}{W} EXILES - use
it against anything that returns from a graveyard. The opponent header prints TWO numbers and only
the SECOND matters: "N are creatures, K of them without a restriction against attacking" (on the
active player's board, "K of them able to attack right now"). Walls are already off K, a [tapped]
creature is still counted, and the sweeper row repeats K.
- K IS 0: Supreme Verdict and Final Judgment are NOT castable plays this turn, in either main phase;
  "Cast nothing right now" and keep them. This outranks being ahead, being BEHIND, spare mana, a plan
  naming a wipe, the sweeper being your only cast row or your last card, and the phase.
- K of 2 or more: cast Supreme Verdict, or Final Judgment if anything of theirs recurs.
- K of 1, and that creature is [defender] or power 0-1 with none of the engine text the Path section
  names: cast NOTHING, no sweeper and no Path, at any life total.
- K of 1 with power 2 or more and Path in your list: take the Path, keep the sweeper.
- K of 1 with power 2 or more and no Path: cast the sweeper at power 4 or more; otherwise keep it,
  unless your life is 10 or less and nothing else in your list answers it.

=== LIGHTMINE FIELD ===
{2}{W}{W}: "Whenever one or more creatures attack, Lightmine Field deals damage to each of those
creatures equal to the number of attacking creatures." Its row prints "{right now: they control N
creatures able to attack - deals N to each}", and N is also the damage each takes, so it kills every
counted creature whose printed TOUGHNESS - the second number in "(2/1)" - is N or less.
 - N of 0: "Cast nothing right now", always.
 - N of 1 or more with a counted creature at printed toughness N or less: CAST IT, subject only to
   the tap-out gate. "[tapped]" is no reason to wait - the row counted them and they untap.
 - N of 1 or more and every counted creature is tougher: hold it, read the row next window.
SURVIVAL OUTRANKS THAT KILL TEST: when the printed power of their creatures able to attack totals at
or above your printed life, any row with N of 1 or more is a cast.

=== PUNISHER BOARDS (read the printed line, not the battlefield listing) ===
- "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Ob Nixilis): every
  card you draw costs 1 life per such permanent and they stack. Revelation stop (1) covers it; take
  no other draw you are taking for the cards either, and take life from an Elixir, which draws none.
- "DISCARD PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs": Revelation stop (2) covers it, and that tail
  UNDERSTATES the bill, since draw triggers on the stack add cards it did not count.
- Forced Fruition {4}{U}{U}: every spell YOU cast draws you seven first, and only CASTING triggers
  it - not an Elixir activation, not a land drop. Counter it on the stack. Beside a draw punisher,
  cast nothing on your own turn; alone, cast nothing while "Your library: N cards" reads 14 or less.
- A COUNTERSPELL IS NOT AN OPTIONAL DRAW and no stop here reaches one: under Forced Fruition or a
  draw punisher, cast a counter off a "[DRAW PRICE: ... - you would be at K]" row reading K of 15 or
  more - and at any K above 1 when the spell countered has draw text of its own.

=== SPHINX'S REVELATION ===
{X}{W}{U}{U} instant: "You gain X life and draw X cards." X=0 does nothing.
THREE PRINTED STOPS COME FIRST. Answer "Cast nothing right now" while ANY of these is on the screen,
at any mana, any X and any life:
 (1) a "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" paragraph;
 (2) a "{... CLEANUP PRICE at X=N ...}" tail on the row;
 (3) their printed life is 10 or less AND a Staff of Nin is on YOUR battlefield line - the Staffs
     finish from there, and every card you draw past that point is one your library cannot spare.
THE LOW-LIFE FLOOR LIFTS STOPS (2) AND (3), never (1), off two printed strings: your life is 6 OR
LESS and the prompt carries no "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" paragraph. Then the row
is your answer at ANY "max affordable X" of 1 or more - ahead of the tap-out gate, the size floor,
"Cast nothing right now" and the hold row - and you take it in the FIRST window printing it. Same
override when the printed power of their creatures able to attack totals at or above your life.
Then, in order:
- THE TAP-OUT GATE: take the row only when what it leaves covers the cheapest counter in your hand.
- THE SIZE FLOOR: cast only from a row whose "{X pricing: max affordable X=N ...}" reads 3 or more;
  X=1 and X=2 are both "Cast nothing right now" at any life above 10. Exception: your life is 10 or
  less and nothing else in your list is worth casting, then cast at the largest X offered. The
  "[<- best X for this cast: ...]" badge ranks X values and does not know this floor; when they
  disagree, the floor wins. Spare mana is never a reason, and a menu of only rows this guide already
  answers - a dead Lightmine Field, a sweeper at K=0, a Path at a [defender] - is an EMPTY menu.
THE X MENU. The ask prints "... X=K is the largest value on this menu the library pays for." THAT K
HOLDS BACK ONE DRAW ONLY - your draw step, as the sentence says. EACH Staff of Nin on your
battlefield line draws you one more card at your next upkeep, and that K does not count them, so
announce K MINUS ONE PER STAFF, never larger; at 0 or less take the "Decline - do not cast this
after all" row. With no such sentence printed, use "library, minus 2, minus one per Staff". A row's
own "{library: ... - L-N left}" counts the library ALONE, so "0 left" with no warning can deck you.
When option 1 carries a life bill naming a surviving X, announce that X; when it says no listed X
leaves you alive, answer
"Cast nothing right now" and do not open the menu. Otherwise announce the largest X the ceiling
allows; the menu prints largest-first and may collapse its middle.

=== ELIXIR OF IMMORTALITY ===
{1} artifact, two copies: "{2},{T}: You gain 5 life. Shuffle Elixir of Immortality and your graveyard
into library." Every counterspell and Path you have spent goes back into your deck. CAST it the turn
it is listed whenever you have a spare {1}. ACTIVATE it whenever your life is 15 or less OR "Your
library: N cards" reads 16 or less - in that window, ahead of a Staff ping and ahead of any spell.
Sixteen, not twelve, and the library half is not about your life: at 90 life with 12 cards left you
are losing. Above 15 life AND above 16 library, activate only when the activation row leaves at or
above the cheapest counter in hand, unless gate shape (a), (b) or (c) applies.

=== YOUR WINDOWS, AND THE HOLD ROW ===
A window with nothing under "ON THE STACK" has one right answer: decline it - a spell they cast later
opens its own window.
THE LAST ROW OF EVERY MENU is "Hold priority: pass now, and do not ask me again ...". When the prompt
carries "[you declined this exact list N times already this turn]", THAT ROW IS THE ANSWER and "Cast
nothing right now" is the wrong one - take it at the FIRST such window, and again when the same rows
come back. Only it stops the next identical ask, and never cast something because a window opened.

=== LAND DROPS ===
When the prompt asks "Land drop:" and lists "Play <land>" options, TAKE ONE - every turn, at any life
total, with any hand, however many lands you have, TAPPED OR NOT (a tapped land taps for mana from
next turn on). "Play no land right now" is never your answer while a land is listed. WHICH LAND: with
an UNTAPPED row and a TAPPED row both offered take the UNTAPPED one - a Seachrome Coast past your
third land makes no mana this turn.
Otherwise take the colour you have fewest of.

=== CLEANUP DISCARD ===
Above seven at end of turn the ask prints your hand as a NUMBERED list and says how many to send
("PUT: n, m"). What you choose is the hand you KEEP - counterspells you can pay for, and a Path. Send
in this order:
 1. Lightmine Field, unless the opponent header's K reads 3 or more.
 2. EVERY LAND on the list, while numbers still need sending, whenever your own battlefield line
    reads "and N are lands" with N of 6 or more. Read N there; do not count your hand.
 3. A second Supreme Verdict or Final Judgment - and a LONE sweeper too when K reads 0 AND every
    creature they have cast is a wall.
 4. Emrakul, unless your battlefield line prints 13 or more lands.
 5. A Sphinx's Revelation beyond the first.
 6. A Staff of Nin, ONLY when a Staff of yours is already on your battlefield line.
 7. Only then a counterspell, MOST EXPENSIVE FIRST - Gavel before Cancel, Cancel before Scatter,
    reversed to Scatters first when every creature they have cast is a wall.
STEPS 1 TO 3 ARE A TIER, NOT A SEQUENCE - any order, and only when it is exhausted read on to 4-7.
WHILE ANY LAND, OR A LIGHTMINE FIELD STEP 1 WANTS, IS ON THE LIST, A COUNTERSPELL IS NOT YOUR ANSWER.
THE ROW TAGS SORT THE LIST: send "{dead right now:}" and "{spare:}" rows first, then untagged rows by
the tier list, and a "{right now:}" row LAST - untagged means the engine could not price it, NOT that
the card is dead, and a LONE sweeper or Path against a deck that has cast a real creature is never
sent for carrying "{dead right now:}".
HAND ALREADY OVER SEVEN WITH A DISCARD PUNISHER PRINTED: cleanup bills 2 life per card above seven
and you cannot decline it, so while "Your hand (N cards)" is above seven on YOUR turn "Cast nothing
right now" is the one row you may not take - cast one castable card per card you are over.

=== MULLIGAN ===
Your job is REACTIVE, so a slow start costs only the threats that land before you can answer. Read
the engine's own counts and its "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of: ..." line.
 - 2 to 5 lands and a coverage line naming a card: KEEP, at any price.
 - "would not cover any spell in it": ship a seven and a six; at five or smaller keep a land plus a
   spell castable in two draws.
 - ONE LAND: SHIP a seven and a six - that coverage line names your one-mana cards because they cost
   one mana, not because the hand does anything. At five or smaller keep it if it names a card.
 - Seven lands and no spell, or seven spells and no land: ship at any price.
BOTTOM IN THIS ORDER: Emrakul; then any "(copy 2 of 2 in your hand)" duplicate, a duplicate LAND
included; then a land beyond the fifth; then your most expensive spell; then the cheapest card that
is neither a land nor an answer. Keep the LANDS and at least ONE answer; Glacial Fortress "enters
tapped unless you control a Plains or an Island".

=== SITUATIONS ===
- "Your library: 13 cards", you are at 44 to their 12, two Staffs are out, and the menu offers a ping
  and a "Life with Elixir of Immortality" row. Take the Elixir: the life is beside the point, the
  ping can wait a window, and the shuffle is the whole game.
- The X ask reads "X=7 is the largest value on this menu the library pays for" and two Staffs are on
  your battlefield line. Announce X = 5.
- Their life is 8, two Staffs are out, and a Revelation row is offered at max affordable X=12. "Cast
  nothing right now": the Staffs need four more upkeeps and your library has to reach them.
- The same rows have been offered four times this turn and the prompt says so: take the hold priority
  row, and again when they come back.
