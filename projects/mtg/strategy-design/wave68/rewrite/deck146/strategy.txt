YOUR DECK: Orzhov (white-black) dungeon MIDRANGE - flexible REMOVAL, creatures that VENTURE INTO
THE DUNGEON, two planeswalkers, a manland. BEATDOWN against slow decks, CONTROL against aggro.
Never sit on removal. ENGINE, in order: a land every turn -> a cheap body or removal on curve -> a
planeswalker the turn you can pay for her -> venture whenever a venture body attacks -> close with
attacks, Kaya's -3, Lolth's Spiders and the drains. Life you pay must buy a cast THIS window.

THE WHOLE CAST ORDER. At every "which card do you cast now" ask on your turn, read the options top
to bottom and take the FIRST that matches. Stop there.
 1. Removal (Vanishing Verse, Soul Shatter) when their line shows a creature or planeswalker you
    can hit - for Verse, only when its target list names one of THEIRS; Soul Shatter leaves this
    entry when its row names more than about three tied cards.
 2. LOLTH or KAYA if listed. Cast her NOW; activate from next turn.
 3. Nadaar, Barrowin or Triumphant Adventurer - a body that ventures.
 4. Silverquill Silencer, whenever no entry-3 body is listed.
 5. Silverquill Command, when its "{modes live right now: ...; dead ...}" clause names a mode you
    want BEFORE the semicolon.
 6. Emeria's Call, when the option is actually listed.
 7. Pelakka Predation, when their hand size is 3 or more.
 8. Acererak; 8b. Silverquill Command with nothing live before the semicolon (a card for 1 life).
 9. "Cast nothing right now".
Entry 9 is the LAST line, not a tiebreaker: unless the engine marked a row dead ("at 0 this does
nothing", "[legendary: you already control"), the reason a row does not match must be a fact on
THIS screen. Take the ask again if offered - you may cast more than one spell a turn.

SPELLS
- VANISHING VERSE ({W}{B}, instant): exiles ONE MONOCOLORED permanent cleanly - no death trigger.
  Not multicolored, not colorless (so most artifacts and lands), but the test is
  COLOR, not type: an artifact creature costing {1}{B} is legal. ITS TARGET LIST IS THE ONLY TRUTH:
  fire at the best name on "legal targets right now: X, Y"; "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN
  right now" -> do not cast it, exile is not a bounce.
- SOUL SHATTER ({2}{B}, instant): they SACRIFICE their highest-mana-value creature or planeswalker.
  It does not target, so it answers hexproof, indestructible and the artifact creatures Verse
  cannot. IT TAKES EXACTLY ONE CARD: the row's "{right now: N tied at MV 0 (their highest): ...}"
  is the list they choose ONE from, not the list that dies. No creature and no walker there -> do
  not cast it.
- KAYA THE INEXORABLE ({3}{W}{B}): her -3 EXILES ANY nonland permanent - multicolored, colorless,
  artifact, planeswalker - so she answers what Verse cannot. Land her and -3 at once when behind.
  THE TARGET IS THE BIGGEST BODY THAT WILL CONNECT: top of their power line, flier or trampler
  first, never a lord or anthem. Every row on her -3 list reading "[your battlefield]" -> take the
  +1 or pass. Never -3 your own permanent.
- SILVERQUILL COMMAND ({2}{W}{B}, SORCERY - your main phases only): choose TWO of its four modes.
  * The CAST ROW's live/dead clause is the truth about which modes exist ("return creature" before
    its semicolon = return is live). The mode ask re-lists dead pairs UNMARKED and prices only the
    half it can price, so a half-dead pair reads whole: carry that dead list into the ask.
  * TIMING: want the pump in combat -> cast in MAIN 1, before attackers; the +3/+3 ends this turn,
    so "pump now, attack next turn" and any main-2 pump is wrong. After combat, or with nothing
    able to attack, pair sacrifice with draw.
  * TARGET: asked to pick ONE creature, pick YOUR OWN - that one gets the +3/+3.
  * DRAW DIRECTION: that player draws AND loses 1. Point it at YOURSELF; at the opponent only when
    that 1 life finishes them.
- SILVERQUILL SILENCER ({W}{B}): the name is a bet on a card they have NOT cast: (1) one Pelakka
  Predation showed you, or the missing half of a pair on their line (Sanguine Bond -> name
  Exquisite Blood); (2) the card their deck replays - a counterspell the log prints as "was
  COUNTERED by <name>", a punisher enchantment, a token maker, a lord; (3) never a card on their
  line, and never a defender.
- PELAKKA PREDATION ({2}{B}): a discard of mana value 3 OR MORE only - a bomb, never a curve. Their line's "of which N are creatures" at least three above your own creature
  count -> it is not a play at all: cast the biggest body you can afford, or the removal that kills
  their engine permanent.
- EMERIA'S CALL also gives your NON-ANGEL creatures indestructible until your next turn - a blowout
  against a board wipe.
- DO NOT CAST AGADEEM'S AWAKENING - EVER. It returns CREATURE CARDS only; Kaya and Lolth are
  planeswalkers, so no X returns them and the cast taps you out for nothing. Here it is a land.

LANDS AND MANA. Shineshadow Snarl taps for {W} OR {B} by itself. If a Cast option is listed you CAN
pay for it - never decline a spell for its colour. Play a land every turn. Snarl enters TAPPED
unless you reveal a Plains or Swamp; Hive enters TAPPED once you control two or more other lands.
- EMERIA'S CALL, AGADEEM'S AWAKENING AND PELAKKA PREDATION ARE LANDS (Emeria taps {W}; Agadeem and
  Pelakka Caverns tap {B}); their row "<land name> with <spell name> -> PLAY THIS AS A LAND" costs
  no mana. Play a real land first and hold the land-backed card; take its land row when the
  alternative is no land drop, or when its spell face is dead (their hand empty, for Predation).
- The hand header counts these three TWICE, on purpose, and says so. The LAND number is true - use
  it unchanged; the SPELL number is one too high per such card, so "3 lands, 5 spells" on a seven
  is three lands and four spells. "Mana sources among those lands" counts the back face already:
  never bottom or discard one while it is a needed colour's only source.
- THE 3-LIFE MENU AFTER AN EMERIA OR AGADEEM ("{taking this row then ASKS you to pay 3 life ...}"):
  ANSWER "tap" BY DEFAULT. Pay only when BOTH hold: (a) the untapped land turns a card in hand into
  a cast you make THIS window - check "Mana available: N total" counting the new land; (b) the row's
  "{this payment puts you at N}" is still above the total power on their line. NEVER PAY AT 3 LIFE
  OR LESS. Pelakka Caverns never shows this menu.
- HIVE OF THE EYE TYRANT: {3}{B} - FOUR mana - for a 3/3 menace attacker until end of turn. Before
  answering "becomes beholder" run these in order, stopping at the first that fires.
  CHECK 0 - Add the power of every untapped creature of yours that could attack, plus 3, and WRITE
    THAT SUM AND THEIR PRINTED LIFE INTO YOUR REPLY; without both numbers CHECK 0 did not fire. At
    or above their life -> ANIMATE and swing, whatever the phase.
  CHECK 0b - Their line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and your line shows 0 able to attack ->
    ANIMATE at any phase (your only clock), unless the row is tagged [this land is TAPPED] or that
    mana pays a cast you will make.
  CHECK 1 - "Phase:" says Upkeep and CHECK 0 did not fire -> PASS.
  CHECK 2 - that Hive already carries a (P/T) on your line, or its entry reads "[tapped]" -> PASS.
  CHECK 3 - read "Those sources, one per untapped card:": the only {b} source being a Hive already
    at (3/3) -> PASS; animating this taps that one and neither attacks.
  "[repeat: activated this turn N times already]" -> PASS unconditionally, read before anything
  else on the row; a second offer in a turn you animated is the same 3/3.

COMBAT - ATTACKING TAPS YOUR BLOCKERS for their whole next turn (Nadaar has vigilance and attacks
free). Never plan to block with a creature you are attacking with.
- A-line prints "[no creature they control can block this attacker]" -> SEND IT, at every life
  total, ahead of every rule below except CHECK A. No tag, wall or converter rule reaches that.
- CHECK 0 - "[doesn't untap during its controller's untap step]" taps it for the REST OF THE GAME.
  Exemptions: that tag also contains "vigilance" -> send always; the header reads "They have 0
  untapped creatures able to block" AND an untapped creature remains on your line after your
  declaration -> send those that get through. Otherwise send only when the power you send is at or
  above their printed life THIS turn; else ATTACK: none.
- CHECK A - printed life 8 or less and exactly ONE creature on your line carrying neither "[tapped"
  nor "[summoning sick" -> it does not attack, whatever its tag says and even when empty. Nadaar
  (vigilance) is exempt.
- CHECK B - an all-"(neither dies)" tag: sending exactly ONE creature -> ATTACK: none (wall damage
  does not carry between turns); with more, send only when your attackers OUTNUMBER their untapped
  blockers, or it has flying or menace. THEY choose which attacker each blocker stops, so a surplus
  protects the swing, not a particular card.
- A collapsed tag ("they have N untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest X (you kill
  it, your attacker lives)") prices ONE blocker; a "- GANG BLOCK: ... enough to kill this attacker"
  clause prices what happens, your toughness against the SUM. GANG BLOCK clause present, or N of 2
  or more -> that creature STAYS HOME if it is a CARD; a dungeon GOBLIN TOKEN goes anyway, free
  bait. N of 1 and no GANG BLOCK clause -> the parenthesis is the story.
- ANY tag name reads "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" -> that creature stays home even
  while the team attacks (tokens still go). Price the WORST name; other names, outnumbering them,
  and a friendlier A-line do not cancel it.
- SHIELD SPHERE takes a permanent -0/-1 counter every time it blocks, so "(neither dies)" against
  it is how you kill it: send in regardless of numbers - but only when EVERY name on that tag is a
  Shield Sphere. A collapsed tag never qualifies; a stop-tag name beats this.
- ANY blocker prints "(blocking trigger: they gain N)", "(blocking trigger: they may gain N)" or
  "(lifelink: they gain N)" -> that swing PAYS them: send only what gets through and win with
  removal, Kaya and the drains. A tag line that KILLS one of them is no exception, and the prices
  ADD across a gang block. No such clause -> their walls are free to attack.
- "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" printed AND any priced clause on a tag ->
  that price comes off YOUR life ("their converter takes N off you"), so that attacker STAYS HOME
  however many attackers you have; only no-blocker bodies go. A plan containing "net 0", "net
  zero", "drained from them" or "the game ends before that damage matters" is wrong. A collapsed
  tag's "most expensive to attack into: <name>" prices that swing, not its "biggest".
- LETHAL OVERRIDES EVERY STOP ABOVE: their printed life at or below the total power of everything
  you send -> send it all.
- THE RENDER DID YOUR COMBAT MATH: "Barrowin of Clan Undurr (5/5) (printed 3/3)" hits for 5 now.
  Add the printed numbers; never rebuild them from base stats.

BLOCK LADDER - one parenthesis per offered block, and the parenthesis is the answer. SURVIVAL
FIRST: the header says the attack IS LETHAL -> assign blockers to the biggest "deals N" attackers
until the damage still coming through is below your printed life, whatever the parentheses say;
only then spend spare blockers by the ladder.
 0. "(neither dies)" -> TAKE IT. Strictly free.
 1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" -> TAKE IT, always, at every life total.
 2. "(both die)" -> TAKE IT unless the attacker it kills is worth less than your blocker.
 3. "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> the chump line, the ONLY one the forecast decides.
    Read "you would be at N": block at 10 or less, or when the header says lethal; above 10 leave
    it unblocked. A parenthesis ending "...N tramples to your face" does not stop that N - chump a
    trampler only at lethal, and never two blockers on one.
ONE BLOCKER STOPS ALL of a non-trampler's damage: a blocked non-trampler deals you ZERO, so "I take
the damage either way" and "preserve it for future blocks" are false at N of 10 or less. "BLOCKS:
none" is right only when every offered block is rung 3 above your floor. Triumphant Adventurer (1/1
deathtouch) is your best defensive body; keep it untapped under pressure.

VENTURE. Adventurer ventures on attack, Nadaar on enter AND attack, Acererak and Barrowin on enter.

  complete, and you never pick Tomb - so he bounces every time. He is never there at
  declare-attackers or declare-blockers, his attack trigger never fires, and his printed 5 power is
  not in any damage math. He IS a {2}{B} repeatable venture: cast him only with spare mana.
- WHICH DUNGEON: whenever the three-dungeon list appears - first venture and after each completion
  - answer LOST MINE OF PHANDELVER by NAME (option numbers are not fixed; write a name off the

  racing and clearly ahead on life (its rooms cost both players); DUNGEON OF THE MAD MAGE only in a
  slow grind where you want raw cards.
- COMPLETION MATTERS: once any dungeon is complete, Nadaar gives your creatures a permanent +1/+1
  anthem and Barrowin's attacks reanimate a mana-value-3-or-less creature from the graveyard.
- ROOM BRANCHES print each room's effect ("{room effect: Create a 1/1 red Goblin creature token}").
  DECIDE ON THE EFFECT TEXT ALONE: take the room that MAKES A BODY or DRAINS them over one that
  only makes a Treasure, adds a counter, or scries. Never work out which room you are on.

PLANESWALKERS - USE THEM EVERY TURN THEY ARE OUT; an idle walker on your turn is wasted.

THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW ("Hold priority for the rest of this turn: pass now ...") is the same pass as
"Cast nothing right now", plus it stops the identical list re-asking this turn. THE TEST IS ONE
LITERAL: you have decided on no action AND the prompt carries "[you declined this exact list N
times already this turn]" -> answer the HOLD row; that line absent -> "Cast nothing right now".

THE DRAW-PRICE BRACKET. A row printing "[DRAW PRICE: casting this draws YOU N cards ... you would
be at K]" prices CASTING AT ALL, not that card: every row on the menu pays that N. K at or below 1
-> "Cast nothing right now" and win the turn another way; the tax is on SPELLS, so your land drop,
attack, dungeon room, Hive animation and Kaya loyalty ability are free.

MULLIGAN. THE ENGINE COUNTS YOUR HAND FOR YOU - "counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells", "Mana
sources among those lands ...", "Cheapest spell in this hand ...", "Playing every land in this hand
would cover the cost of: ...". Those counts are correct and are the whole basis of the decision;
never recount. Where you are is the ask's "(keeping N)" number - each hand is a fresh 7.
- (keeping 5) or lower with even ONE land in front of you -> KEEP; shipping buys one card fewer at
  the same land odds. Zero lands, or all lands, is the only fresh look worth a card.
- On the untouched 7 or at (keeping 6), a mulligan is right for exactly three hands: ZERO lands; an
  all-land hand (6-7); or ONE land under "Playing every land in this hand would not cover any spell
  in it" WITH the bracketed COUNT reason ("(1 land is not enough for your cheapest spell at mana
  value 2)"). That line is a ONE-LAND TEST AND NOTHING ELSE - at TWO OR MORE lands it is never a
  ship. Any other hand is a KEEP, ONE land under "would cover the cost of:" included.
- COLOUR IS NEVER A REASON TO SHIP, AT ANY LOOK. Every bracketed COLOUR reason ("castable off {B}
  alone on turn one", "off {W} alone even with every land in this hand in play") is the reason this
  rule forbids: 14 of your 21 lands make white, 15 black, 8 either.
- THE CARRIED PLAN IS NOT EVIDENCE ABOUT THIS HAND. "YOUR PLAN (as you last stated it)" saying
  "mulligan to find a white source" is about a hand you already threw away: decide from this
  header and the price line, and say the plan is out of date.
- "KEEP a 7 or a 6 with 2-5 lands" and "prefer a hand with an early play" are tiebreakers, not
  tests: never mulligan for too many lands, for lacking a one-drop, or for colour.

THE BOTTOM ASK IS WHERE THE HAND IS BUILT. "You will be left with an N-card hand" -> keep the lands
you need to cast what you keep, then the cheapest spells those lands cover, and bottom the rest most
expensive first (a second planeswalker, then the priciest card left). Hive, Emeria's Call, Agadeem's
Awakening and Pelakka Predation count as LANDS here, and A LAND GOES UNDER ONLY FROM A KEEP OF FIVE
OR MORE LANDS: at two lands or fewer the PUT line holds spell numbers only, so at N of 3 it is two
lands and your cheapest spell. A card named in your plan whose number is in your PUT line is the
plan bottoming itself.

CLEANUP DISCARD ("your hand has N cards and your maximum hand size is 7"). Answer "PUT: n, m" with
the numbers you SEND. SCAN the numbered list in this order, stopping at the first name you find -
never answer row 1 just because it is first:
 (1) A card that is NEITHER A LAND NOR A LAND-BACKED SPELL, at the highest printed mana value on
     the list. Emeria's Call, Agadeem's Awakening and Pelakka Predation are LAND-BACKED and not
     eligible - AND THE ROW WILL NOT TELL YOU: at every window that culls your hand all three print
     as plain sorceries. Recognise them yourself.
 (2) A second copy of a card already on the list whose first copy you are keeping.
 (3) A second copy of a planeswalker already on the list or on your battlefield line.
 (4) A LAND, once your line prints four or more ("{spare: you control N lands already}"); below
     four lands the PUT line holds spell numbers only.
 (5) Otherwise the HIGHEST PRINTED MANA VALUE left - read the {..} cost off each row: a {3}{b}{b}
     Lolth before a {2}{b} Acererak, whatever position it sits in.
Never send your only removal while their line shows a creature or a planeswalker.
