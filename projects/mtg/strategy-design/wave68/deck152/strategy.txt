BANT HUMAN MIDRANGE (green-white, light blue splash)

THE DECK. Put Humans and Wolves on the board, grow them with lords and counters, and ATTACK. Removal
(Fateful Absence, Brutal Cathar) clears blockers; lords (Intrepid Adversary, Sigarda, Katilda) turn a modest
board lethal. Beatdown that also grinds: deploy a creature every turn you can and swing wider each combat -
one body, then two, then the whole team. Your board is your clock.

ORDER OF OPERATIONS. Play a land; run the LETHAL COUNT and, if it clears, cast nothing and go to combat;
otherwise cast the best creature you can (removal only to clear a blocker you need gone now); attack with
everything the tags price as profitable; then in MAIN 2 take the creature-tapping actions - Katilda's counter
ability, Ranger Class levels, Adversary counters you could not afford earlier.

MANA
- "Mana available: N total (... colours you can make: {g}{w})": N is what you can spend, the colour list is
  which COLORED pips you can pay. Trust both; never re-derive your mana.
- GENERIC IS NOT A COLOUR: Ranger Class {1}{G} is one green pip plus one mana of any kind, so "2 total ...
  colours: {g}" pays it in full. If N covers the cost and every coloured pip is listed, you can pay it.
- A numbered "Cast ..." row IS payable - pick it, and never answer a number not on the list.
- ONE LAND PAYS ONE PIP: Deserted Beach makes {W} or {U}, not both; Overgrown Farmland {G} or {W}, not both.
  So Teferi {2}{W}{U} off one Beach is unpayable and Sigarda {1}{G}{W}{W} needs TWO white sources, and a card
  can be absent from the list even when its colours show - then take the best row offered and move on.

LAND DROP
- A "Land drop:" question offering a land -> PLAY IT. It costs no mana and does not reduce what you cast this
  turn, so never "Play no land right now" and never hold a land for a colour you want later; with several
  offered, take the colour your hand needs most.
- PATHWAYS have two faces, both with a row on the same menu: "Play Land" plays the shown face and "<other face
  name> [PLAY THIS AS A LAND: ...]" plays the other. Either is one land drop, costs nothing, and is the only
  route to that face - Branchloft {G}/Boulderloft {W}, Barkchannel {G}/Tidechannel {U}, Hengegate {W}/Mistgate
  {U}. Take the face that turns an uncastable card in hand into a castable one.

LETHAL COUNT, BEFORE SPENDING ANYTHING IN UPKEEP OR MAIN 1
Add the power of every creature on YOUR battlefield line carrying neither "[tapped" nor "[summoning sick". If
that sum is at or above their printed life AND their line reads "of which 0 are creatures", answer "Cast
nothing right now", pass to the attackers ask, and send everything; this outranks the deploy floor.
YOUR HUMANS ARE MANA SOURCES - Katilda gives every Human "{T}: add mana", so once your lands are tapped the
engine pays with your creatures and prints the bill on the row: "{paying this taps: Katilda, Luminarch
Aspirant - they cannot attack this turn}". COUNT THE NAMES: two or more in Upkeep or Main 1 is a pass,
whatever the row is; with exactly ONE name take the row only if it puts a body of printed power 2 or more onto
the battlefield, and pass if it is a level, an activation or a removal spell leaving no body. If the "Those
sources, one per untapped card:" list disagrees with the clause on your row, believe the clause.

DEPLOY FLOOR - ON YOUR MAIN PHASE, IF A CREATURE IS OFFERED, CAST ONE
"Cast nothing right now" is right in exactly three cases: (a) the only offer is Fateful Absence and you hold
it as an instant for their turn; (b) the lethal count has cleared; (c) every creature row is tagged
"[legendary: you already control <name> ... casting this sends one copy to your graveyard]" and your copy is
healthy - that row is not a body, and is a real cast only when your copy is about to die anyway or is worse
than the fresh one. Otherwise pick a creature, and the fewer you have the more urgent it is. A quiet board is not a reason to
wait, low life is a reason to deploy rather than hold, and creatures are never held to dodge removal.

ATTACKING
Send each listed creature with any of: no untapped blocker opposite it; flying they cannot block (Elite
Spellbinder and Sigarda FLY); toughness beating the biggest thing that can block it; an even-or-better trade.
"ATTACK: none" only when every attacker would die for nothing or do nothing. An A-line proves that creature is
untapped, unsick and legal now - tapped creatures print "[tapped]" and get no A-line - so never hold a name
back on memory or on your plan. Its "their untapped blockers" tag is a per-creature verdict:
- "(you kill it, your attacker lives)" -> send it.
- "(neither dies)" stops every point and kills nothing - the opposite of free damage. If EVERY outcome on a
  tag reads that, send only when your declared attackers OUTNUMBER their untapped blockers or it has evasion;
  one attacker never outnumbers one blocker. EXCEPTION: SHIELD SPHERE takes a permanent -0/-1 whenever it
  blocks, so send into it either way.
- "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" ANYWHERE in the tag is a per-creature STOP: that creature stays
  home even while the team attacks, unless the lethal check clears. Price the WORST name listed, since they
  pick the block they win. Vigilance is no licence, and lifelink is not payment - "you gain N from this block
  only, and this attacker deals nothing to their life" means one or the other, never both. Tokens are exempt:
  a stop-tagged Wolf goes with the team as free bait.
- GANG BLOCK: when the tag collapses and ends "GANG BLOCK: ... enough to kill this attacker", that creature
  stays home unless the lethal check has cleared, whatever the friendly parentheses beside it say. FLYING DOES
  NOT EXEMPT SIGARDA - a collapsed tag counts only creatures that CAN block her. No gang clause -> use the
  parenthesis printed.
- BLOCKING TRIGGERS: Perimeter Captain gives them 2 life when any defender they control blocks and Pride
  Guardian 3, printed as "(blocking trigger: they gain 3)" inside the outcome. First strike does NOT cancel it
  - it fires on the BLOCK, and the tag already applied first strike. Two or more trigger-printing blockers on
  one A-line -> the outnumber rule does not apply; send only your fliers and grind with removal.

BLOCKING
Each blocker prints what it may stop: "B1. Intrepid Adversary (3/1) - may block A1 (both die)". An attacker
missing from that list cannot be blocked by that creature; the usual reason is FLYING, and an all-illegal
answer is thrown away with the engine blocking for you.
- ONE BLOCKER, AT MOST ONE ATTACKER: count the left-hand sides, each B-number appears at most once.
- Several B-numbers on ONE A-number IS legal, and "no 1-on-1 result exists - only a block by TWO OR MORE of
  your creatures counts" asks for exactly that: n such attackers need 2n blockers, and with fewer you choose
  WHICH ONE to stop. Two of them and two blockers -> both B-numbers on ONE, or "BLOCKS: none"; one on each is
  illegal and is the worst answer.
- The parenthesis is the computed result, first strike and deathtouch already applied; never override it.
FIRST LINE OF EVERY BLOCK ANSWER: find the header's "you would be at N". "BLOCKS: none" is allowed in exactly
one case - every offered block reads "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" AND N is 10 or more. The test is N,
not the size of the attack: at "you would be at 6" you block even if only 2 damage is coming. When the header
says the attack IS LETHAL, survival comes first - assign blockers so the damage still getting through is LESS
than your printed life, biggest "deals N" attackers first, whatever their parentheses say. Then the ladder:
 0. "(neither dies)" - TAKE IT; nothing dies and the damage is gone.
 1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" - TAKE IT, at every life total, always.
 2. "(both die)" - TAKE IT unless the attacker it kills is worth less than your blocker.
 3. "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" - the chump line, the only rung N decides: take it at N of 9 or less
    or when the attack is lethal, above 9 leave that attacker unblocked. One blocker stops ALL of a
    non-trampler's damage however big. TRAMPLE: a parenthesis ending "...N tramples to your face" buys only
    (attacker power minus N) life - only when the attack IS lethal, and never two blockers on one trampler.
A B-ROW LISTING SEVERAL A-NUMBERS: the ladder also picks WHICH attacker it blocks - read its own list and
take its BEST rung, 0 before 1 before 2 before 3, never the biggest attacker and never the one the header's
"best case" assignment names.
KATILDA'S B-ROW: not assigned to a "(both die)" or rung-3 line while N is 10 or more - she is every Human's
mana ability and a token is never worth her - but at N of 9 or less she blocks like everything else, and her
A-row is the same: a tag naming a blocker that kills her means she stays home.

YOUR CARDS
- INTREPID ADVERSARY ({1}{W}, 3/1, lifelink): ETB pay {1}{W} any number of times, each a valor counter giving
  ALL your creatures +1/+1. PAY as many times as you can afford with a board out; "don't add any counter" only
  with no spare mana.
- SIGARDA ({1}{G}{W}{W}, 4/4 flying trample): Humans get +1/+1. Best card here; cast on curve, and she needs
  TWO separate white sources.
- KATILDA ({G}{W}, 1/1): protection from Werewolves, and your Humans each tap for one mana of their own colour
  - she is a Human, so she appears in your "Mana available" total. Her "{4}{G}{W}, {T}: +1/+1 counter on each
  creature you control" is MAIN 2 ONLY: after attackers are declared, three or more creatures out, spare mana.
- LUMINARCH ASPIRANT ({1}{W}, 1/1): free +1/+1 counter each combat. PUT IT ON A CREATURE THAT CAN CONNECT,
  NOT THE BIGGEST ONE - counters are worth zero on a creature stopped every turn by the same blocker (a FOG
  BANK, which prevents all combat damage to and from itself; a Shield Sphere; any wall reading "(neither
  dies)"). THE TEST IS THE SIZE PRINTED ON THE ROW: if your pick reads 6/6 or larger, choose another,
  preferring a flier, and never the Aspirant while another creature can attack.
- RANGER CLASS ({1}{G}): cast for the 2/2 Wolf at once. Level 2 ({1}{G}) counters an attacker each combat,
  Level 3 ({3}{G}) casts creatures off the top; level with SPARE mana only.
- BRUTAL CATHAR ({2}{W}, 2/2, daybound): ETB exiles an opponent creature until the Cathar leaves. Every row it
  appears on then prints "{if this leaves the battlefield: their <name> comes back from exile}": while that
  clause is there its death hands them a creature back, so it is never an even trade. Send it ONLY on "(you
  kill it, your attacker lives)" or with no blocker listed - "(both die)" keeps it home - and block with it
  only on rung 0 or rung 1. Night side MOONRAGE BRUTE: 3/3, first strike, Ward-Pay 3 life; its ward ask is
  mis-labelled 'TARGET CHOICE ... "this spell costs 3 life more" ability', costs YOU nothing, and EXACTLY ONE
  ROW READS "[opponent's stack]" - that row is the answer.
- TOVOLAR'S HUNTMASTER ({4}{G}{G}, 6/6, daybound): ETB two 2/2 Wolves. Night side PACKLEADER: 7/7 making TWO
  more Wolves every time it enters OR ATTACKS, plus "{2}{G}{G}: another Wolf or Werewolf of yours fights their
  creature" - with it out, attacking is pure profit, so swing every turn.
- FATEFUL ABSENCE ({1}{W}, instant): destroys a creature or planeswalker (they get a Clue). Your ONLY answer
  to a PLANESWALKER, so against a CREATURE it is your LAST answer - with a Brutal Cathar in hand, cast the
  CATHAR instead. STOP: when the option says "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN right now", DO NOT CAST IT.
- ELITE SPELLBINDER ({2}{W}, 3/1 flier): its hand look is a TAX, not an answer - they may still cast the
  exiled card for {2} more. Exile "Exquisite Blood" or "Sanguine Bond" above everything; with neither listed,
  take IDYLLIC TUTOR, which searches for an enchantment and finds them.
- TEFERI ({2}{W}{U}), your ONLY blue card: one Deserted Beach pays one pip, so you need a SECOND white source
  first, and you never contort your mana for him. His +1 is worth 2 life unless a pick is live now, and it is
  a three-stage cascade you cannot back out of: STAGE 1 ("Choose an option for Teferi ...", rows "choose a
  land" / "Decline - do nothing") is THE ONLY WINDOW THAT WILL LET YOU DECLINE, so take the gate only when you
  can already name an untapped permanent of THEIRS or a tapped one of YOURS you want back; STAGE 2 (the
  polarity pair, both verbs printed in its brackets) and STAGE 3 (the target list) have NO pass row, and a
  target printed "[tapped]" is a wasted pick. Tapping one of theirs does not survive the turn, so on your turn
  the tap half only removes a BLOCKER - to stop an ATTACKER, tap it on THEIR turn. With no attack of your own,
  take the -2 whenever your line reads "0 are creatures".
- LAIR OF THE HYDRA taps for {G} and for {1}{G} becomes a 1/1 Hydra until end of turn - PASS that row on sight
  when it prints "[Upkeep offer: ...]", "[this land is TAPPED: ...]" or "[repeat: activated this turn ...]",
  and otherwise animate in MAIN 1 only, with mana left after your creature, and only when its A-line would
  read "[no creature they control can block this attacker]".

MULLIGAN - AN ODDS COMPARISON, NOT A CHECKLIST
The ask prints "having already taken N mulligans you will bottom N cards on a keep (keeping M)". READ THE M
FIRST, before the cards. You run 25 lands (14 green, 15 white; only the four Overgrown Farmlands make both)
and your cheapest spells cost TWO, so a fresh seven is a two-lander about as often as anything else and a
mulligan rarely fixes lands.
- M of 5 or less: with even ONE land, KEEP - the bottom ask sheds the uncastable cards. Only ZERO lands buys a
  fresh look. Colour, two-drops and curve are bottom-ask questions, never ship reasons.
- M of 6: keep unless the hand is zero lands, all lands, or ONE land under "Playing every land in this hand
  would not cover any spell in it". "would cover the cost of:" naming a card is a KEEP.
- M of 7, the untouched seven: TWO OR MORE LANDS AND AT LEAST ONE SPELL is the floor - KEEP. ALL LANDS or ZERO
  LANDS: ship. ONE LAND: keep if the coverage line names a card, ship if it covers nothing.
- THE CARRIED PLAN IS NOT EVIDENCE ABOUT THIS HAND: the ask hands back the plan you wrote about the hand you
  already threw away, so "mulligan for lands" returns at every look. Each look is a NEW seven with its own
  "counted by the engine: N lands, M spells" header - decide from that header, never from the plan or your own
  recount.
- "would not cover any spell in it" IS A ONE-LAND TEST AND NOTHING ELSE; at TWO OR MORE lands it is not a
  ship. Its bracketed reason has two families: the COUNT family, "(1 land is not enough for your cheapest
  spell at mana value 2)", which at ONE land is the whole test, and the COLOUR family, "(... no spell in it is
  castable off {W}{U} alone ...)", which is NOT A SHIP at any land count clearing the floor - it only says
  your lands make one colour pair and your spells want another.
- THE "Mana sources among those lands" LINE COUNTS ONLY THE FACE EACH PATHWAY IS SHOWING (Branchloft under {G}
  not {W}, Barkchannel under {G} not {U}, Hengegate under {W} not {U}), but you choose the face when you play
  it, so ADD each Pathway's other colour before pricing a keep; that can only turn a ship into a keep.
- THE FORBIDDEN THOUGHT: "no white source / no green source, so this hand is dead." IT IS NOT - a one-colour
  opener is ordinary and the other colour arrives within a turn or two, so a mono-green opener casting Ranger
  Class on turn 2 and a mono-white opener casting Luminarch Aspirant are both KEEPS. "No one-drop" is never a
  reason since you run none. Between hands that clear the floor, prefer more lands and the cheap threats.

BOTTOMING AND CLEANUP DISCARD
On a keep, "You will be left with a N-card hand": keep first the lands that cover your cheapest spells (up to
four), then those cheap spells, then the rest by cost, losing the most expensive spell you cannot reach first;
at N of 4 or fewer that is two lands and two cheap spells.
CLEANUP DISCARD is the same order read from the end - answer "PUT: n, m" with the numbers you SEND, most
expensive first: the priciest spell your lands cannot reach in two turns, then a duplicate of a spell already
in hand, then a land beyond the fourth once your battlefield line prints four or more. Never your only
two-drop, and never Aspirant, Adversary, Ranger Class or Tracker while a cheaper card is left to send.

THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW ("pass now, and do not ask me again unless the board changes ...") is a pass with one
extra effect: when you have ALREADY decided to take no action AND the prompt carries "[you declined this exact
list N times already this turn]", answer HOLD instead of "Cast nothing right now". That N counts identical OPTION
LISTS, not boards, so it is never itself a reason to pass.

MATCHUPS
- COUNTERSPELLS / OPEN MANA: count the entries on their battlefield line with NO "[tapped]" and call it U. U
  of 2 or less, or their hand at 3 or below -> cast your BEST creature now, and a second the same turn if the
  rows allow. U of 3 or more with their hand at 5 or more -> deploy the CHEAPEST creature first and hold the
  expensive one. NEVER hold everything; the deploy floor still applies.
- LIFEGAIN / FLIERS: kill or exile a lifelinker on sight, since you cannot outrace their life total; against
  fliers your only maindeck answer is Fateful Absence, and your ground creatures are not offered as blockers
  for a flier at all, so do not spend both copies on ground creatures you could have blocked.
- INFECT / POISON: the prompt prices these swings in counters, not life ("Poison counters (you): 4 of 10").
  Poison never resets and nothing here removes it, so no life-race arithmetic applies: block infect attackers
  even when the block loses the creature and even at a healthy life total, and kill or exile [infect]
  creatures on sight. KATILDA is the one exception - at 7 of 10 poison or less she stays out of infect
  chump-blocks, at 8 or more she blocks like the rest.
- A LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER: the attackers prompt prints "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield:
  theirs" and you cannot answer it - nothing here destroys an enchantment. READ THEIR LINE FOR THE SECOND NAME.
  * BOTH "Sanguine Bond" and "Exquisite Blood" -> a LOOP without limit: one point of damage you deal, or one
    wall gaining them 3, ends the game. ATTACK: none every combat, FLIERS INCLUDED - "[no creature they control
    can block this attacker]" is exactly why it kills you. Block every "[lifelink]" attacker you are offered a
    block for, and cast the rest of your hand as blockers.
  * ONE of the two names -> race it with EVASION. An A-line reading "[no creature they control can block this
    attacker]" is free in both directions, since no block means no blocking trigger and no life gained: SEND
    every such creature, every combat, at every life total. A "blocking trigger" tag is a PRICE, not a
    prohibition - read the header's "They have N untapped creatures able to block", never re-derive N, and the
    surplus above N lands its full power. The tag prints both halves ("they gain 3, and their converter takes
    3 off you"), so an extra attacker past N is worth declaring when the power it adds beats TWICE that gain
    number. Hold ONLY the creature whose WHOLE blocker list both stops it AND pays them.

SITUATIONS
- One blocker, its row listing "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" on a 2/2 and "(both die)" on a bigger
  attacker -> block the 2/2; the bigger name is not the better rung.
- "You would be at 6" and the only block offered is "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> take it; at "you
  would be at 14" with the same offer -> BLOCKS: none.
- An A-line tag reads "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" and the attacker is a real card -> it stays
  home; a Wolf token goes anyway as free bait.
