INTRUDERS OF THRABEN - WBU TOKEN COMBO

THE DECK. Intruder Alarm beside Thraben Doomsayer or Bloodline Keeper, tapped for a token again
and again: any creature entering untaps all creatures, so the tap comes straight back and one
maker builds an army in one window. Removal buys the turns; the tokens end the game.

ORDER OF OPERATIONS. 1. Land, first, every turn. 2. A token-maker (Keeper before Doomsayer).
3. Intruder Alarm once one maker is down, ahead of a SECOND maker and of Lightning Greaves.
4. Tap makers to the stop, preferably on the OPPONENT'S turn. 5. Attack with everything except one
untapped maker. Two things reorder it: 0 creatures of yours against 2+ of their attackers (sweeper
first), and lethal already on the board (attack, do not build).

--- ANSWER FORMAT ---
- "CHOICE:" is bare, on its own line; "ANSWER: CHOICE: 0 (pass)" is not read as an answer at all
  and the built-in AI plays the window. Reasoning, CHOICE line, PLAN.
- A CHOICE names a NUMBERED ROW ON THIS WINDOW; a printed row is payable now, and a card with no
  "Cast" row is not castable now, whatever your plan says.
- THE FIRST CHOICE LINE RUNS AND PROSE NEVER TAKES IT BACK. Only a line STARTING "Correction:",
  "Re-evaluating:", "Actually," or "On reflection" plus a SECOND full CHOICE line replaces it -
  both halves or nothing. "Wait," and "Correct Plan:" do not count.
- EVERY GATE IN THIS GUIDE IS READ BEFORE THE CHOICE LINE, NEVER AFTER IT. A row's own numbers -
  "M of yours", "they control N creatures", "at 0 this does nothing", "this equips nothing" - decide
  whether that row's NUMBER may be written at all. A refusal you write after the CHOICE line, or in
  the PLAN, does not run; the row you already named does.
- Your carried PLAN is older text; where it and a "{right now:}" clause disagree, the clause wins.
--- LAND DROP AND MARSH FLATS ---
- Any "Play <land>" line listed: take one, every turn, before anything.
- The drop is NOT spent by a spell or a Flats crack; "Land drop: NOT yet used this turn" outranks
  your own memory.
- Prefer a land entering UNTAPPED (Sea, Tundra, Scrubland, Plains, Swamp) when it makes a spell
  castable this turn; Arcane Sanctum always enters tapped, Isolated Chapel unless you control a
  Plains or Swamp.
- Marsh Flats makes NO mana until cracked: crack it the FIRST window listed, either turn; that is
  not your land drop.
- WHICH FETCH: take the row tagged "(you cannot make {W} right now)", and among several the one
  supplying the pip your hand needs TWICE. Sea = {U}{B}, Tundra = {W}{U}, Scrubland = {W}{B}.
- No such tag: still a DUAL, never a basic - "adds {W} or {B}" beats "adds {B}". A colour printed
  at 0 comes first; {U} is scarce (10 of 24 lands).
--- WHAT TO CAST WHEN SEVERAL ARE LISTED ---
A priority order over CARDS, not choice numbers. Take the highest entry present on this menu.
 P0 Damnation {2}{b}{b} - your line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and the row's K is 2+.
    Ahead of any maker, Tutor or Alarm.
 P1 Bloodline Keeper {2}{b}{b} / P2 Thraben Doomsayer {1}{w}{w} - no maker on your battlefield.
 P3 Intruder Alarm {2}{u} - no Alarm on your battlefield; once ONE maker is down this beats a
    second maker and the Greaves.
 P4 Idyllic Tutor {2}{w} - no Alarm on the battlefield or in hand, live bracket only.
 P5 Lightning Greaves {2} - only when the row does NOT read "this equips nothing".
 P6 removal, by its own rule. P7 Vision Skeins {1}{u}.
 P8 "Cast nothing right now" - correct when nothing above is on the menu.
P8 IS NOT INACTION: rows reading "destroys 0 of their creatures", "at 0 this does nothing" or "a
second copy changes nothing" are a menu with nothing on it.
--- HOLDS AND PASSES ---
- "0. Pass priority" is always available, and is the answer whenever the stop is reached or all
  rows are dead.
- Your instants ARE offered on the opponent's turn at every phase, the BEST window for Tragic Slip
  and both edicts - you see their board after they commit.
- Take "Hold priority: pass now, and do not ask me again" at the FIRST dead window of the turn,
  and answer a reopened run the same way every time.
--- THE TOKEN LINES AND THE STOP ---
Rows: "Create human with Thraben Doomsayer [cost: Tap]", "Create vampire with Bloodline Keeper",
"Create vampire with Lord of Lineage".
POWER CANNOT MEASURE THIS LOOP: new tokens are summoning sick, so the "not summoning sick" power
number does not move. M = the "of which" number on YOUR battlefield line
("activated this turn N times" is NOT M); L = "Opponent life:"; C = the "of which" number on
THEIRS.
- THE STOP IS L + C + 3. Below it, take the line every time it returns; at or past it, "CHOICE: 0
  (pass)" whatever else is listed. Against a lifegain board add their last gain to the stop a
  second time, and read L again in the ATTACK window.
- TAKE THE REPEAT ROW ("repeated N times, then stop") ONCE, with N = (L + C + 3) - M, in its own
  format: CHOICE: 2 (Create human with Thraben Doomsayer x17). A take with no number is re-asked
  and the window spent; the next window is a pass. N of 0 or less is a pass - not "x1", not "x0".
- Carry all four numbers, every blank filled from the screen, never the bracket text itself:
    PLAN: L <opponent life>, C <their creature count>, stop <L+C+3>; M is <M> now;
    this window: <x<N> or pass>
  "this window:" names the action the CHOICE line named; a Damnation menu takes its own plan line.
- Recompute the stop from THIS window's row every time; if your numbers disagree, the arithmetic
  is right.
- "[RE-ASK] ... your own PLAN puts you at <M> with your stop at <S>" -> "CHOICE: 0 (pass)": not
  the same count, not a smaller count, not a new stop invented to fit it.
- THE STOP ALSO GOVERNS THE PLAIN "[cost: Tap]" CREATE ROW, which prints no stop clause. Once M is
  at or past the stop, EVERY Create row is a pass.

--- TIMING THE CHAIN ---
- Tokens made now cannot attack until your next turn. FIRE ON THE OPPONENT'S TURN, where the lines
  are also offered: a token made there blocks that turn, swings on your next, and dodges part of a
  sweeper. Fire on your own turn only when your attackers are ALREADY out.
- Your OWN upkeep is the worst window against a log showing Damnation, Supreme Verdict or
  Starstorm: pass there.
- Nothing untaps in your untap step while the Alarm is out, so "Your creatures that can attack:"
  holds what you made on THEIR turn and have not spent, never what you made this turn.
- ALARM IN HAND AND CASTABLE THIS TURN: the maker WAITS UNTAPPED until it resolves, or it sits
  tapped all their turn. Land, Alarm, then tap; with no castable Alarm, tap freely.
- BUT THE ROW DOES NOT WAIT WITH IT: pass a Create row TWICE in one turn and it is retired for the
  turn, Alarm or no Alarm. On an Alarm turn the FIRST Create window may be passed, the SECOND not.
--- DAMNATION ---
It destroys ALL creatures and nothing else - not an enchantment, artifact, planeswalker or land.
The row counts for you: "destroys N of their
creatures (K without a restriction against attacking), M of yours", and "all of them carry a
restriction" is K = 0. FIRST, BEFORE THE CHOICE LINE, read M and N off that row: M at 4 or more,
or N at 0, and Damnation's number is not written this window - answer another row. Only if that
comparison passes do you go on. Whenever it is listed this is the ONLY plan line:
    PLAN: Damnation: N <theirs>, K <able to attack>, M <mine>; <cast / hold>
- M at 4+: NEVER, whatever N says. Four bodies is a board you win with.
- M at 0 with K at 2+: cast it, above a maker, the Tutor and the Alarm; at your life 10 or less,
  "hold" is not an option.
- N bigger than M with K at 1+: cast it. K at 0 on Perimeter Captain or Pride Guardian: cast it.
- Any other K at 0, or N exactly 1 while a Tragic Slip or live edict on the menu kills the one
  that matters: take the one-for-one and keep the Damnation.
- M bigger than N, or N at 0: never, whatever the plan says. Two Damnation rows: the cheaper one.
--- TRAGIC SLIP ---
-1/-1 kills toughness 1 only; with Morbid live, -13/-13 kills anything. THE DECISION IS AT THE
CAST ROW - the target ask has NO pass row, so the card is spent on one of the names listed.
- "a creature died this turn, so Morbid applies": cast it; take the biggest power.
- "kills 0 of the 3 legal targets at -1/-1": cast nothing; it costs {B} and kills something later.
  "{kills: <name>}": cast it.
- On the target ask take the row ending "- DIES"; "- SURVIVES" means the card is wasted.

--- THE TWO EDICTS ---
You NEVER pick the victim; the only choice is whether to cast. Read N off the row: "they control N
creatures - <name> is sacrificed" at N = 1, "they choose which one" at 2+, "at 0 this does
nothing" at 0. N not exactly 1: no edict. A creature on the stack is not on the battlefield and
does not change N.
- Tribute to Hunger {2}{b}: they sacrifice, YOU gain its toughness in life. At N = 1 it is the
  cast, ahead of a Slip on a toughness-2 creature.
- Devour Flesh {1}{b}: the player you NAME sacrifices AND gains the life; use it at the opponent
  only when Tribute is not listed and N is exactly 1.
- Decline an edict on an N = 1 body printing "[defender]" or power 0 - unless its text gives their
  board mana, counters or +X/+X, which makes it their engine.
- Never cast an edict when their life is at or below the life it gives them.
- "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER ... theirs - Sanguine Bond" on screen: an edict at the opponent swings
  both ways. Cast nothing.
- NAMING "Yourself" IS A LIFE GAIN, and here a big one: you sacrifice one of your own creatures,
  your choice, and gain its toughness - under Fateful hour your 1/1 Humans are 3/3. Take it
  whenever the LIFE, not their board, is killing you; give up a token, never a maker.
--- "THAT WOULD KILL YOU" AT A PRIORITY WINDOW ---
"ON THE STACK: N damage to you - you would be at X; that would KILL YOU" is the ONLY fact in that
window: it resolves in the step you are standing in, so do not plan around a main phase you will
not reach. Take the row that moves the number - gain the life (Devour Flesh naming Yourself) or
kill the source. A hold is NEVER the answer here.
--- LIGHTNING GREAVES ---
Equip costs {0} and is re-offered every window: take it ONCE per Greaves, on Bloodline Keeper if
out, else Thraben Doomsayer; "{attached: Lightning Greaves #1}" is the done-signal. Later
re-offers: a "[cost: Tap]" Create line if listed, else pass; equip again only when that creature
died or a new unequipped maker arrived. "this equips nothing" is a decline even as the only cast
row, on an empty board or any other.
--- INTRUDER ALARM AND IDYLLIC TUTOR ---
- One copy does the whole job; "[second copy: ... changes nothing]" is LEGAL, not USEFUL. On an
  empty battlefield the Alarm does NOTHING. It is symmetric - it untaps THEIR creatures too.
- Idyllic Tutor finds an ENCHANTMENT, and the only one here is Intruder Alarm - it CANNOT find a
  creature. Only one bracket is a cast: "still in your library, not on your battlefield or in your
  hand" -> LIVE, cast it and name Intruder Alarm; "a copy of one you already control or hold" and
  "none left in your library" -> DEAD. An Alarm in HAND is as dead as one on the battlefield.
- THE ONE TIME A DEAD ROW IS A PLAY: against counterspells in their log (Cancel, Essence Scatter,
  Dream Fracture, Fall of the Gavel) they counter the FIRST spell you cast, so with mana for TWO
  cast the SPARE first (a dead Tutor, a second Alarm, a Skeins) and the maker SECOND in the same
  main phase. ONE TEST: the maker must be a numbered row ON THIS SAME MENU.
--- CARD FACTS THE PROMPT DOES NOT SPELL OUT ---
- Thraben Doomsayer {1}{w}{w} (2/2), "{T}: put a 1/1 white Human token". FATEFUL HOUR: at YOUR
  life 5 or less every OTHER creature you control gets +2/+2, printed as the result - "Human #1
  (5/5) (printed 1/1)". Add those numbers before answering "ATTACK: none": that sum is LETHAL or
  it is nothing.
- Bloodline Keeper {2}{b}{b} (3/3) flying, "{T}: put a 2/2 black flying Vampire token" - your best
  creature. Take "Transform:backside with Bloodline Keeper [cost: {b}]" whenever listed: it
  becomes Lord of Lineage, a 5/5 giving every OTHER Vampire +2/+2 and still making a token on the
  same tap. It needs {B} and five Vampires, and the Alarm untaps CREATURES, not lands, so leave a
  black source untapped - off four lands the Keeper taps you out.
- Vision Skeins {1}{u} - EACH player draws two. Your dig, not your default: cast it only with no
  maker and no castable Alarm this window, and count "Your hand (N cards)" first - at 6 or more it
  ends in a Cleanup discard. Never cast it against a card that draws them extra cards or punishes
  drawing (Howling Mine, Underworld Dreams).
--- ATTACKING - FIVE CHECKS IN ORDER ---
Stop at the first check that answers; CHECK 2 only trims the list.
CHECK 0 - CRACK-BACK. What you send is TAPPED and cannot block on their turn. "CRACK-BACK NEXT
TURN: ... you would be at X or lower" - READ X. At 0 or below: "ATTACK: none", unless your
sendable power is at or above their PRINTED life. No CRACK-BACK line: the check is free.
CHECK 1 - LIFEGAIN AND WALLS, by the names on their line; Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood are
enchantments you cannot remove, so waiting them out is not a plan.
 A - only PERIMETER CAPTAIN or PRIDE GUARDIAN: "ATTACK: none" until removal takes the last of
  them off that line.
 B2 - BOTH SANGUINE BOND AND EXQUISITE BLOOD ("their life LOOP is in play"): one point they gain
  chains to your zero before your damage. "ATTACK: none" while any untapped creature of theirs can
  BLOCK what you send - the gate is BLOCK LEGALITY, not their count. When "[no creature they
  control can block this attacker]" covers EVERY attacker and the total says lethal, send them all.
 B - exactly ONE of those two: A = the LENGTH of "Your creatures that can attack:", B = their
  UNTAPPED creatures. Attack at A of 3 x B + 3 or more; below that, "ATTACK: none" and make tokens.
 Lethal overrides all three - power at or above their printed life, send it all - here and in B2
 only. Attacker tags cannot see life: "(neither dies)" counts deaths.
CHECK 2 - GANG BLOCKS. "(you kill it, your attacker lives)" prices ONE blocker; a "GANG BLOCK: ...
enough to kill this attacker" clause prices what happens. With that clause, or 2+ "untapped
creatures that could block this one": a TOKEN goes anyway, a CARD stays home (any maker, or
anything wearing the Greaves).
CHECK 3 - THE COUNT. ONE BLOCKER BLOCKS EXACTLY ONE ATTACKER, so a 5/5 flier blocks ONE of yours
and the rest connect - but outnumbering protects no individual creature; THEY pick the matchups.
"ATTACK: none" on the count alone only when their untapped count is EQUAL TO OR HIGHER THAN what
you send and no tag reads "you kill it, your blocker lives".
CHECK 4 - SEND EVERYTHING on "Your creatures that can attack:"; that list already dropped what
cannot legally attack.
 - KEEP ONE UNTAPPED MAKER HOME, every attack, while the Alarm is out: a tapped creature untaps
   only when a creature ENTERS, which only an untapped maker causes. Or fire the chain to its stop
   in your main phase FIRST.
 - With no maker out, what you send stays tapped until one arrives: send it when the attack
   advances your clock, but do not plan a block with it.
 - Never send a LONE maker, or your only creature, into "(both die)" or "(your attacker dies)":
   that is "ATTACK: none".
--- BLOCKING ---
"INCOMING THIS COMBAT: ... D unblocked damage - you would be at X; this KILLS you" prints on EVERY
window of their combat, so an instant or a token line in their upkeep is priced against it first.
"this KILLS you" ALSO SAYS WHETHER BLOCKING SAVES YOU: "you would be at N AT BEST (no assignment
of your blockers does better)". THAT N IS THE VERDICT, never your own arithmetic: at 1 or more,
blocking SURVIVES - block by the LETHAL rule and spend every blocker. Only when that clause is
absent, or its N is 0 or lower, is "BLOCKS: none" right.
 STEP -1 - ON THE LOOP BOARD (both Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood on THEIR side) a block that
  gains them life is LETHAL, and the header's best case does not price the loop. Block only
  attackers whose rows carry no life-gain clause; if all do, "BLOCKS: none".
 STEP 0 - A "B" ROW IS A BLOCKER, and its "may block A1, A2" list is the legality list. At LETHAL
  your FIRST line is "BLOCKS: B1:A<largest deals N>". "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" is a
  free kill unless STEP -1 says otherwise.
 STEP 0b - THE HEADER'S NAMED ASSIGNMENT IS A LIFE-MAXIMISER, NOT ADVICE. Take it ONLY when the
  header also says "LETHAL"; on "NOT lethal", ignore the name and go to STEP 1.
 STEP 1 - Spend a TOKEN (Human, Vampire - no mana cost on the row) before any CARD; makers block
  LAST, after every token.
 LETHAL RULE - at LETHAL, or unblocked damage of your life minus 4 or more, ASSIGN TO THE BIGGEST
  ATTACKERS FIRST AND SPEND EVERY BLOCKER: sort the "deals N" numbers and work down, makers
  included.
 MENACE - "[menace]" is not blocked by one creature at all: two blockers on it, or none.
 OTHERWISE makers do NOT block: "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" or "(both die)" on a
  Doomsayer or Keeper is declined while "you would be at N" is above 5.
--- MULLIGAN AND BOTTOMING ---
Read "(keeping N)" first: each look is a fresh seven shaved by one more card at the SAME land
odds, so a mulligan buys a look, never a fix. Colour never ships a hand.
- At (keeping 5) or lower: ONE land in front of you is a KEEP. Only ZERO lands, or all lands, ships.
- On the untouched seven or at (keeping 6), use "counted by the engine: N lands, M spells" - read
  that number, never recount. TWO OR MORE LANDS AND A SPELL: keep, full stop, even under "would
  not cover any spell in it" - that coverage line decides ONE-land hands only. ONE LAND: "would
  cover the cost of: <card>" keeps, "would not cover any spell in it" ships. ZERO LANDS or ALL
  lands: ship. SIX OR MORE LANDS with one spell: keep.
- BOTTOMING: read "You will be left with an N-card hand" and BUILD the best N in this order,
  bottoming the leftovers - 1. lands covering the cheapest spell you keep (at N of 3, two lands
  and one spell; at N of 5 or 6, three or four lands and the spells they cover, up to FOUR mana);
  2. a token-maker; 3. Idyllic Tutor or Vision Skeins; 4. removal; 5. Intruder Alarm LAST.
- A LAND THAT MAKES MANA IS NEVER THE BOTTOM while a card outside that order is in hand: Lightning
  Greaves is the leftover and goes under. Marsh Flats makes no mana, so a hand whose only other
  land is a Flats is one-source.
--- DISCARD AND SACRIFICE ASKS ---
A forced sacrifice ask (annihilator, or an edict naming you): give up a land, then a spare copy,
then a token; never the Alarm, your only maker or Damnation.
Cleanup discard: a listed "copy 2 of 2" / "copy 2 of 3" / "copy 3 of 3" is always the discard. A row
carrying its own verdict ("dead right now: 0 legal targets", "spare: you control N lands") is the
send; one carrying none is not thereby live. NEVER your only maker, Alarm or Damnation.
--- SITUATIONS AND THE RIGHT ANSWER ---
- Your line reads 0 creatures and Damnation reads "destroys 4 ... (4 without a restriction against
  attacking), 0 of yours": cast it, above a maker and the Alarm. "destroys 0 ...": hold it.
- A maker is out and Intruder Alarm is listed: cast it, ahead of a second maker and the Greaves.
  Alarm in hand and castable with a Create row listed first: pass that first Create window, cast
  the Alarm, then tap.
- Opponent life 22, their line 3 creatures, yours 4, repeat row listed: stop 28, N 24 - "CHOICE: 2
  (Create human with Thraben Doomsayer x24)", then pass.
- At 2 life with 25 Humans able to attack, "ON THE STACK: 3 damage to you ... that would KILL
  you", Devour Flesh on row 1: cast it naming Yourself - a 3/3 Human under Fateful hour gains 3.
- Their only creature is Wall of Omens (0/4) [defender], Tribute to Hunger listed: cast nothing;
  but if its text pumps their others, cast the edict.
