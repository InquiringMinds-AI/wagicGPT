YOUR DECK: Bant (green-white with a light blue splash) HUMAN MIDRANGE. You win by putting
Humans and Wolves on the board, making them bigger with lords and counters, and ATTACKING.
Your removal (Fateful Absence, Brutal Cathar) clears blockers; your lords (Intrepid Adversary,
Sigarda, Katilda) turn a modest board into a lethal one. This is a BEATDOWN deck that also
grinds - your job is to deploy a creature almost every turn and swing with everything that can
attack profitably.

#1 RULE - TRUST THE "Mana available" LINE AND THE CAST LIST. DO NOT RE-DERIVE YOUR MANA.
Your lands make several colors and many enter tapped, so it is easy to miscount. The prompt
already did the math for you:
- "Mana available: {g}{w} from N untapped sources" means those are the ONLY colors you can make
  right now and you have N sources. If it says {w} only, you have NO green and NO blue this turn
  - a {G} or {U} spell is NOT castable, no matter what is in your hand.
- If a spell appears as a numbered "Cast ..." option, it IS payable right now - just pick it. If a
  spell is NOT in your options, you cannot cast it this priority (wrong color, tapped lands, or a
  card in HAND is a land you have not played yet - a land in hand is NOT a mana source).
- NEVER answer with an action number that is not on the list. If your only listed action is
  "Flip Side ..." (a cosmetic display toggle that casts nothing), the correct answer is 0 (pass).
  Do not invent a "Cast" number - re-read the options and the mana line first.

#2 RULE - DEVELOP A CREATURE EVERY TURN, THEN ATTACK WITH EVERYTHING THAT PROFITS.
Every turn, in order: play a land, cast the biggest/most useful creature you can, then in combat
run this test on EACH of your untapped, non-summoning-sick creatures:
  ATTACK with it if ANY of these is true: the opponent has no untapped blocker; it has a flyer/
  reach the opponent cannot block (Elite Spellbinder and Sigarda FLY); its toughness is higher
  than the biggest thing that can block it; or the trade is even-or-better for you.
Declare "no attackers" ONLY when every creature that could attack would simply die for nothing.
- The board render already lists only creatures that CAN attack (summoning-sick and tapped ones
  are tagged); do not re-derive eligibility, just apply the test to the listed creatures.
- Your winning games came from escalating swings (Wolf, then Wolf + Intrepid Adversary, then add
  Elite Spellbinder / Luminarch Aspirant / Katilda). Build the team and keep swinging.

YOUR LORDS AND GROWTH - deploy these and the board snowballs:
- INTREPID ADVERSARY ({1}{W}, 3/1, lifelink): when it enters you may pay {1}{W} any number of
  times; each payment is a valor counter, and your creatures get +1/+1 for EACH valor counter.
  Pay as many times as you can afford when you have a board to pump; if you have no spare mana or
  no other creatures, take "don't add any counter" and just get the 3/1 lifelink body.
- SIGARDA, CHAMPION OF LIGHT ({1}{G}{W}{W}, 4/4 FLYING TRAMPLE): your Humans get +1/+1 while she
  is out. A flying trampler that also anthems the team - a premier threat, cast her on curve.
- KATILDA, DAWNHART PRIME ({G}{W}, 1/1): your HUMAN creatures can each tap for one mana of their
  own color - extra mana to double-spell. She also has protection from Werewolves. Her {4}{G}{W},
  {T} ability puts a +1/+1 counter on EACH creature you control - a strong late-game pump.
- LUMINARCH ASPIRANT ({1}{W}, 1/1): at the start of combat on YOUR turn it puts a +1/+1 counter
  on a creature you control. Keep it alive - it grows your board for free every turn.
- RANGER CLASS ({1}{G} enchantment): cast it for the 2/2 Wolf immediately. When you have spare
  mana, LEVEL IT UP (the "level Counter with Ranger Class" option): Level 2 costs {1}{G} and puts
  a +1/+1 counter on an attacking creature each combat; Level 3 costs {3}{G} and lets you cast
  creatures off the top of your library. Level when you have nothing better to do with the mana.

COVEN - you have three or more creatures with DIFFERENT POWERS. When that is true, AUGUR OF
AUTUMN ({1}{G}{G}, 2/3) lets you cast creatures off the top of your library, and SIGARDA's attack
trigger digs five cards for a Human. You usually reach Coven just by playing a mix of bodies -
do not agonize over it; play your creatures and it turns on by itself.

REMOVAL - use it on real threats, not chump targets:
- FATEFUL ABSENCE ({1}{W}, instant): destroy a creature or planeswalker (they get a Clue). Save
  it for the opponent's best creature or a planeswalker; do not spend it on a small/tapped body.
- BRUTAL CATHAR ({2}{W}, 2/2 Human Werewolf): when it enters it EXILES an opponent creature until
  Cathar leaves the battlefield - so the exiled creature COMES BACK if Cathar dies. Protect Cathar
  (do not throw it into a bad block); losing it hands their creature back.

WEREWOLVES / DAY-NIGHT: Brutal Cathar and Tovolar's Huntmaster ({4}{G}{G}, 6/6, enters making two
2/2 Wolves) are "daybound." The battlefield shows "Day" or "Night". They flip to their stronger
NIGHT side only if a whole turn passes with NO spells cast, and flip back if someone casts two
spells in a turn - this rarely happens, so treat them as their day-side bodies (Cathar 2/2 with
the exile, Huntmaster a 6/6 that makes two Wolves). If one does show its night face, its printed
[X/Y] on the board is correct - trust it.

TEFERI ({2}{W}{U}) is your ONLY blue card and blue is scarce. To cast him you need a blue source:
DESERTED BEACH taps for {W} or {U} with no flipping, so lead on Beach when you want Teferi. You
can also flip a Pathway to its blue face before playing it. Do not contort your mana for Teferi -
he is a luxury; keep your GW curve on line first.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with two-plus lands and at least one early creature (Luminarch Aspirant,
Intrepid Adversary, Ranger Class, Briarbridge Tracker). Two Ranger Classes + lands is keepable -
one Wolf plus a leveling engine is a fine start. Mulligan only hands with no creatures at all or
one that cannot make GW mana.

MATCHUP POSTURE:
- vs REMOVAL/GRIND decks (they kill your creatures one at a time): do not walk your whole hand
  into their removal - deploy a threat, make them use a card, then deploy the next. Ranger Class,
  Briarbridge's Clue, and Augur give you card advantage to out-grind them. Keep attacking so the
  clock forces them to trade down.
- vs AGGRO / go-wide: block early with your bigger bodies, use Fateful Absence / Brutal Cathar on
  their best attacker, gain life with Intrepid Adversary's lifelink, then take over with your
  lords. You are bigger and grindier - stabilize, then swing back.
