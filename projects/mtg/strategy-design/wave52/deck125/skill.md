# wave-52 strategy-writing-skill proposals - deck125 seat

Only what this seat's evidence supports. Each proposal names the corpus fact that produced it.

## S1 (NEW amendment) - a rule whose trigger is a PRICE must name the row's own printed number, not
## a number the pilot computes from the board

**Evidence.** `deck125 vs162` seq 97 (t20, 3 life). The Forced Fruition rule read "cast a counter
ONLY when that price is below your life minus 1" - a correct rule stated as arithmetic over two
facts the pilot has to find and combine (how many Fruitions, how many punishers). The row itself
printed the finished number: `[DRAW PRICE: casting this draws YOU 7 cards (their Forced Fruition),
and their Underworld Dreams deals you 7]`. The reply cast the counter and died in that window.

**Proposal.** When the engine prints a computed price on the row, the guide sentence must quote the
CLAUSE and compare it against one board number, never re-derive the price. This is the same lesson
the skill already records for the sweeper header's second number and for `leaves N`; the corpus now
has it a third time, on a clause the guide had not yet been re-keyed to. Suggested wording of the
amendment: *a rule that needs a quantity the render already prints is written as "read X off the
row and compare it with Y", and the guide never restates the formula that produces X.*

## S2 (NEW amendment) - when a render literal is RETIRED, every guide that keyed a rule to it loses
## that rule, silently, and the corpus will show the rule going from N/N to 0/N

**Evidence.** Cross-seat but visible from here: lane K replaced `[already owned: X on your
battlefield]` with `[second copy: you already control X; both stay on the battlefield - no legend
rule]`. deck126's Chromatic Lantern rule declined 9 of 9 second-copy rows in wave 51 and took 5 of 5
in wave 52. deck125 had no sentence for the new tag at all and got lucky (its second copies - Staff,
Elixir - are genuinely worth casting, 5/5 correct by accident rather than by rule).

**Proposal.** Add to the skill's release discipline: an engine change that alters a printed literal
carries an obligation to grep every guide for the OLD string and re-key it in the SAME step, and the
review carry-list should record the expected before/after counts so the re-key is falsifiable rather
than assumed. The wave-52 carry-list already asked reviewers to do the re-key; what it did not ask
for was the count of meetings with the NEW literal, which is the only thing that shows whether the
re-keyed rule fires.

## S3 (evidence for an existing doctrine, no new amendment) - the "read the row, not the total"
## family is the guide's most reliable construction

**Evidence.** On this seat, every rule of that shape held at its ceiling this corpus: sweeper at the
header's second number 0, 220/220 declined; land drop, 98/98 taken; `X = 3` off the printed row,
11/11; Revelation from a `max affordable X` of 1 or 2, 0/18; TARGET asks with 2+ candidates decided
by the printed engine text then `[flying]` then power, 5/5. The rules that broke this corpus (the
Staff tap-out gate, 4/11; the Elixir gate, 2/11) are the two that ask the pilot to compare the row
against a price he must find on a DIFFERENT line (his hand). Recorded as a measured contrast, not a
proposal to move either rule.
