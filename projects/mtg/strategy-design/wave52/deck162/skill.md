# deck162 -> strategy-writing skill: proposals from wave-52 evidence

## S7 - A RULE WHOSE ALTERNATIVES ARE A LIST BREAKS ON THE WINDOW WHERE THE LIST IS EMPTY
**Evidence, both of deck162's only rule breaks this corpus.** RULE 2's brake reads: *"With a
Howling Mine already on your battlefield line, a second row reading 'converters on your
battlefield: 0' is the brake: take a wall, a punisher, a Liliana's Caress, or 'Cast nothing right
now' instead"*. Both breaks (`162v130` s11, `162v123` s8) came from an ask with **exactly two
rows**: the second Mine, and `Cast nothing right now`. Three of the four named alternatives were
not on the menu; the fourth - the one that was - is the least imperative-sounding item in the
list and sits last. Where the list had a body in it, the brake held (13/13 casts at K >= 1, and
five permitted K=0 casts including two Masters under a named exception the reply cited by name).

**Proposal.** The skill should require any rule offering a MENU OF ALTERNATIVES to state what
happens when **none of the positive alternatives is on the screen**, in its own sentence, with
the do-nothing option promoted rather than trailing: *"A two-row ask whose only spell is <the
forbidden one> is the brake firing, not the brake failing - row 2 IS the answer."* An
alternatives list implicitly promises that one of its items will be available; the window where
that promise fails is exactly the window where the rule is worth the most, and the pilot reads an
empty list as licence.

## S8 - AN EXCEPTION THE MODEL CITES BY NAME IS AN EXCEPTION THAT IS WORKING - MEASURE THAT, NOT
JUST THE BREAKS
**Evidence.** deck162's Master-of-the-Feast carve-out (*"With NO punisher out, cast it ONLY when
the opponent's battlefield line reads 'of which N are creatures' with N of 1 or more"*) was hit
twice at K=0 and both takes were legal (`v146` s17, their line "of which 1 is a creature";
`v123` s10, "of which 3 are creatures"). The s10 reply reads: *"Currently have 0 converters, so
Master of the Feast is cast only for its body to block the opponent's threats, as per Rule 2
exception. Do not cast draw engines yet as K=0."* The rule, its condition, its literal and its
scope all came back in the pilot's own words.

**Proposal.** The skill's review method currently counts rule BREAKS. It should also count
**named citations** - windows where the reply quotes a rule's name, its condition or its literal
- because a cited exception is evidence the whole surrounding rule is being read, and because a
rule with citations and no breaks is the strongest exit signal a guide can produce. Cheap to
measure (grep the replies for the rule's own words) and it distinguishes "no breaks because the
rule works" from "no breaks because no window arose".
