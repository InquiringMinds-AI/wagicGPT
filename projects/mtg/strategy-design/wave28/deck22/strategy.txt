Deck identity: MONO-RED GIANTS - a tribal ramp/midrange BEATDOWN. You win by curving
cheap enablers and cost-reducers into big Giants, then swinging with all of them -
doubled and buffed by your payoffs. You ARE the beatdown against control and midrange;
against faster aggro you deploy a body every turn and race with bigger creatures. Every
turn ask: did I add a threat, and did I attack with every Giant that can attack? If not,
you are losing to your own passivity.

YOUR MANA IS SIMPLE - EVERY SPELL CASTS OFF MOUNTAINS ALONE. There are no color or
fixing decisions. If a Cast option is listed, it is payable right now; trust it. Your
Giants get CHEAPER as you play Stinkdrinker Daredevils - the option line already shows
the reduced cost, so just cast what is offered.

RULE #1 - UNIVERSAL AUTOMATON IS A GIANT. It is a {1} 1/1 with CHANGELING, which means
it is EVERY creature type, INCLUDING Giant. Treat it as a one-mana Giant, always:
  - It is "another Giant" that turns ON Blind-Spot Giant (which otherwise cannot attack
    or block). Automaton on turn 1 -> Blind-Spot Giant can attack the turn it lands.
  - It counts for Borderland Behemoth (+4/+4 per other Giant), for Sunrise Sovereign's
    +2/+2 trample anthem, and it is a Giant source for Calamity Bearer's damage-doubling.
  - So CAST Universal Automaton on turn 1 whenever you have it. NEVER keep it home as a
    do-nothing 1/1, and NEVER mulligan or bottom it away thinking "it's just a 1/1" - it
    is the cheapest enabler in the deck and it makes the rest of your hand work.

THE ENGINE - how the deck wins (deploy in this order when you have the pieces):
  1. Turn 1-2: cast Universal Automaton and/or Stinkdrinker Daredevil (the enablers).
  2. Turn 3+: cast a Giant every turn. With another Giant already out, Blind-Spot Giant
     (4/3) can attack immediately.
  3. Land your PAYOFFS and the deck snowballs:
     - Calamity Bearer (3/4): DOUBLES all damage from your Giants - combat damage AND
       Inferno Titan's pings. Once it is out, your attacks and Titan are lethal fast.
       Get it down and KEEP it - it is a payoff, not a trade-fodder body.
     - Sunrise Sovereign (5/5): gives your OTHER Giants +2/+2 and trample - a board-wide
       anthem. Cast it and swing wide.
     - Inferno Titan (6/6): when it ENTERS and each time it ATTACKS, deal 3 damage split
       among up to 3 targets (aim ALL 3 at the opponent's FACE for reach, unless a
       blocker must die first). With Calamity Bearer out it deals 6. {R}: +1/+0 - pump
       it with spare mana for extra reach.
     - Borderland Behemoth (4/4 base, trample): gets +4/+4 for each OTHER Giant you
       control, so it is huge on a developed board. A finisher, not a blocker.

ATTACK FLOOR - RUN THIS EVERY COMBAT (this is how you win; you lose games by NOT
attacking). Look at the offered attacker list and DECLARE every GIANT on it, every turn,
UNLESS that Giant must stay home this turn to block an attacker that would otherwise
kill you. Your Giants are: Universal Automaton, Blind-Spot Giant, Skyraker Giant,
Calamity Bearer, Inferno Titan, Sunrise Sovereign, Borderland Behemoth. Do not leave a
4/3 or bigger sitting home while you chip with a 1/1 - that has lost close games.
  - EXCEPTION 1 - MOGG SENTRY STAYS HOME. Mogg Sentry is NOT a Giant and is a 1/1 when
    you attack with it (it only grows when the OPPONENT casts a spell). It is a BLOCKER
    and ambusher - do NOT declare it as an attacker.
  - EXCEPTION 2 - STINKDRINKER DAREDEVIL (1/3) stays back to block; attack with it only
    when that 1 extra damage is lethal-relevant or nothing can block it.
  - "No attackers" is correct ONLY when every Giant you could declare would die for zero
    gain or is needed to block lethal.

INFERNO TITAN TARGETING: on enter/attack you pick targets for 3 damage (6 with Calamity
Bearer). Default: send it all at the opponent's face to close the game. Only spend some
on a creature when that creature will block your lethal or is a real threat you must
remove now.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2+ Mountains and at least one early play - a Universal
Automaton, a Stinkdrinker Daredevil, or a Giant you can cast by turn 3-4. Universal
Automaton COUNTS as an early Giant play (see Rule #1) - do NOT mulligan a hand just
because "Blind-Spot Giant needs another Giant"; the Automaton in your hand IS that
Giant. Ship only hands with 0-1 lands, or all lands, or nothing castable before turn 4.
When you keep and must bottom a card, bottom your MOST EXPENSIVE excess or a duplicate
bomb - NEVER bottom Universal Automaton, Stinkdrinker Daredevil, or your only early
Giant; those are what let the hand function.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recap):
  - You have Universal Automaton and a Blind-Spot Giant -> cast the Automaton first; now
    the Blind-Spot Giant can attack. Deploy and swing.
  - Calamity Bearer is on your board -> your combat and Inferno Titan damage is DOUBLED;
    push for lethal, attack with everything.
  - A 4/3+ Giant is offered as an attacker and the opponent cannot profitably block or
    kill it -> ATTACK with it. Do not sit behind it.
  - Mogg Sentry is on the board and the opponent has attackers -> keep it back to block
    (it grows +2/+2 when they cast a spell), never send it in.
  - Fresh 7 with 2-3 Mountains + Automaton/Stinkdrinker + a Giant -> KEEP. That is your
    good hand, not a mulligan.
