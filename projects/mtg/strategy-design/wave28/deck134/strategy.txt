YOUR DECK: blue-green Urza tron control. You are ALWAYS the defense here - every opponent is
faster than you. You do not race. You WIN LATE, and you win late BY DOING SOMETHING EVERY TURN:
build your mana with the Urza lands, keep yourself alive with fogs, walls and lifegain, LOCK the
opponent out of combat with Stonehorn Dignitary + Ghostly Flicker, then grind them out with
Mulldrifter flyers (and Compelling Argument mill as the slow backup). A turn where you pass with
mana up is a turn you lose the game - there is always a land to fetch, a spell to dig with, or a
threat to deploy.

YOUR MANA IS SIMPLE. Unknown Shores and Prophetic Prism each make mana of ANY color, so you can
always pay the green (fogs, Pulse), white (Stonehorn), and black (Dinrova) pips - never decline
or skip a card thinking you "lack the color." If a Cast option is listed, you CAN pay for it.

#1 RULE - CRACK EXPEDITION MAP. The moment "search card with Expedition Map [cost {2}, Tap,
Sacrifice]" appears in your list, TAKE IT - do not pass. Search for whichever Urza land you do
NOT yet control: Urza's Mine, Urza's Power-Plant, or Urza's Tower. Controlling all THREE turns
your 3 Urza lands into ~7 mana, which powers your whole deck. If you already have all three Urza
lands, fetch any land to make your land drop. The ONLY time to pass the Map crack is when you
control all three Urza lands AND already made your land drop AND need the {2} this turn for a
counter or a threat. "Sacrifice" is how the Map works - do not refuse it.

THE LOCK - HOW YOU BEAT AGGRO. Stonehorn Dignitary's enter makes the opponent SKIP their next
combat phase - they cannot attack you at all. Ghostly Flicker exiles and returns two of your own
permanents, RE-TRIGGERING their enter effects. So: cast Stonehorn, then EVERY ONE OF YOUR TURNS
cast Ghostly Flicker targeting Stonehorn Dignitary - the opponent skips combat again, forever.
Once this is going you cannot lose to attacks; take your time and win. Do NOT attack with the
1/4 Stonehorn for 1 damage - its job is the ETB, keep it home and re-blink it.

GHOSTLY FLICKER TARGETS (it needs TWO). Pick two of these, best first: Stonehorn Dignitary
(opponent skips combat), Mulldrifter (draw 2), Mnemonic Wall (return an instant/sorcery from
your graveyard), Prophetic Prism (draw a card). NEVER target a plain land with Flicker - blinking
a land does nothing and wastes the whole spell. If only ONE value permanent is out, a land is an
acceptable second target, but the FIRST target is always a value ETB.

KEY CARDS - what each actually does:
- Mulldrifter: HARD-CAST it {4}{U} for the 2/2 FLYING body plus draw 2 - the flyer is your best
  blocker and your main clock. Choose "evoke" {2}{U} (draw 2, then it dies, no body) ONLY when
  you cannot afford {4}{U} or you are safe and just want the cards. Under attack or at low life,
  hard-cast for the body. You can Flicker Mulldrifter later to draw 2 more.
- Moment's Peace: FOG - it prevents ALL combat damage this turn. When the opponent's attack would
  drop you to 8 or less, leave {1}{G} untapped on their turn and cast Moment's Peace to take ZERO
  damage. It comes back: you can cast it AGAIN from your graveyard (flashback) to fog a second
  time. This is your main way to survive while your engine sets up.
- Pulse of Murasa: GAINS YOU 6 LIFE (and returns a creature or land to a hand). At 10 life or
  less, cast it for the life. Prefer returning your OWN creature or land (a Mulldrifter to recast
  is great); the 6 life is the point even when the only return is an opponent's card.
- Mnemonic Wall: 0/4 defender - a great blocker AND its enter returns an instant/sorcery from
  your graveyard (get back a Counterspell, a fog, a Pulse). Flicker it to return another one.
- Counterspell / Condescend: save these for the opponent's most dangerous spell or a board wipe
  (Ritual of Soot kills your board - counter it). Leave {U}{U} (or {X}{U}) untapped on their turn
  when you are holding one. For Condescend, choose the LARGEST X you can pay so they cannot pay it.
- Compelling Argument: mills the opponent 5. This is a slow BACKUP win - only real once your
  Flicker + Mnemonic Wall loop lets you mill many times. Do not build your whole plan on it early;
  cycle it ({U}, draw a card) if you just need a card.
- Dinrova Horror / Mystical Teachings: Dinrova bounces a permanent + makes them discard (tempo vs
  their best threat); Mystical Teachings tutors any instant (grab Counterspell or a fog). Use them.

CLOSE THE GAME. Once you are not about to die - lock online, or life stabilized above 10 - you
must WIN, not sit. Attack every turn with your Mulldrifter flyers, keep flickering for cards, and
grind the opponent down. A game left even until the turn cap is a LOSS for you. Deploy and press.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-5 lands with at least one blue source (Island, Thornwood Falls, Remote Isle,
Unknown Shores, or Prophetic Prism). Count Unknown Shores and Prophetic Prism as any-color
sources - never ship a hand for "no green" or "no white." An Expedition Map plus 2 lands is a
keep (the Map finds your third land). Ship 0-1 or 6-7 land hands.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> do):
- "search card with Expedition Map" is listed -> crack it, fetch the missing Urza land.
- You control Stonehorn and can cast Ghostly Flicker on your turn -> blink Stonehorn; they skip
  combat again.
- The opponent's attack would put you to 8 or less and you have Moment's Peace + {1}{G} open ->
  fog it, take zero damage.
- Your life is 10 or less and Pulse of Murasa is castable -> cast it for the 6 life.
- Mulldrifter is offered and you are under pressure -> hard-cast {4}{U} for the flyer, not evoke.
- Ghostly Flicker is castable and Stonehorn / Mulldrifter / Mnemonic Wall / Prophetic Prism is on
  your board -> flicker the value permanent(s), never a bare land.
- A board wipe or their best threat is on the stack and you have Counterspell/Condescend -> counter it.
