# deck134 -> strategy-writing-skill proposals (wave 28)

deck134 is the FIRST seat to complete a full guide loop (guideless Step-0 wave-27 -> first-guided
wave-28) and produce a "teaches validated, record flat" result. It teaches something about the
LIMITS of the guide instrument. One primary proposal, tagged SINGLE-SEAT but flagged as a likely
NEW TERMINAL CATEGORY for the synthesis agent.

## Proposal 1 (NEW: a terminal category distinct from new-work-exhaustion) - the
CONSTRUCTION-TERMINAL seat: teaches VALIDATE and the record does NOT move.

Evidence: wave-27 decomposed deck134's 0/6 as ~half-construction/half-decisions and wrote a guide
targeting the decision half (Map crack, Stonehorn+Flicker lock, blink targets, evoke gate, fog/
lifegain). Wave-28 validated EVERY guide-addressable teach independently of the record:
- Map cracked at first payable window in 6/6 (was ~10 passes/game); tron 3/3 in 4/6 (was 1/6).
- Mulldrifter hard-cast 100%, used as blocker (was evoked under pressure).
- Fog/Pulse fired on their triggers; ~25 "held" fog offers were correct own-turn restraint.
- The lock teach even landed COGNITIVELY (the pilot's PLAN line states the intent verbatim).
And the record stayed 0/6, damage dealt stayed ~0. The two teaches that did NOT convert did so for
NON-guide reasons: the lock is an untutorable 2-card combo that never reached a castable hand in 6
games (construction), and the deck has no clock so its flyers correctly block and never attack, so
under -T 3000 cap-adjudication it cannot win on life.

Proposed rung (for the reviewer methodology, not the deck guide): when a Step-0 loss decomposes
into a CONSTRUCTION half and a DECISION half, the guide can only be judged against the DECISION
half - so VALIDATE EACH TEACH INDEPENDENTLY OF THE WIN/LOSS RECORD (a fully-successful guide can
sit under an unchanged 0/6). If, after one guided corpus, the teaches validate but the record is
flat AND the residual losses trace to construction (no clock / untutorable combo / cap-adjudication
scores a clockless deck a loss), the seat is CONSTRUCTION-TERMINAL: the guide instrument has
reached its ceiling in ONE corpus and further guide iteration is wasted motion. Route to the ROSTER
OWNER (construction/pool/format lane), not to another guide revision. This is a DIFFERENT
termination shape than the deck18/deck59 "new-work-exhaustion after a validation perfecta" - there
the guide works AND wins; here the guide works AND the deck still cannot win. Both are terminal;
only one is a success.

## Proposal 2 (corollary, rule-3 family): a deck whose taught win-condition is a DRAW-DEPENDENT
multi-card combo with no tutor is a guide-teach the guide cannot guarantee gets EXERCISED.

Evidence: the Stonehorn+Flicker lock is deck134's entire anti-aggro plan and the guide's teach #2,
but across 6 games neither piece ever became a castable option - they only appeared as library
reveals from card-selection spells. A guide can teach "assemble the lock" perfectly and it will
never fire if the pieces don't arrive. Implication for guide-writing: when a deck's taught engine
is a combo it cannot search, the guide should be honest that it is a HIGH-VARIANCE plan and carry a
non-combo survival line as the primary (deck134's guide already leads defense with fogs/Pulse/
blockers, which is correct) - but recognize in the review that the combo teach's non-conversion is
a CONSTRUCTION fact, not a teaching failure to iterate on. This reinforces the Proposal-1 rung:
separate "teach did not fire because the model ignored it" (guide-fixable) from "teach did not fire
because the cards never came" (construction).

## Everything else: the skill worked as written.
The wave-27 skill produced a guide that validated all its decision teaches on first deployment -
the strongest possible evidence the Step-0-bis rules (engine-derived card facts, reasoning-tax-
ranked teaching points, dominant-idiosyncrasy-as-rule-1, the option-anchored named crack line) are
sound. The ONE residual model slip (fog on own main phase 2, vs136 seq39) is a single execution
noise event against a correct instruction, not a guide defect. No strategy.txt revision this wave.
