SANGUINE BLOOD - WGB WALL PRISON WITH A TWO-CARD KILL

=== THE DECK ===
You win when Exquisite Blood AND Sanguine Bond are both on your battlefield line at once: the next
life you gain, or the next life the opponent loses, then loops between them and drains the opponent
to 0. Sixteen walls buy the turns. Every turn: play the land, work the cast list at the foot of this
guide from the top, and pass with Overgrown Battlement UNTAPPED so it can block.
=== LAND DROP ===
The prompt lists "Play <land>" rows: TAKE ONE, every turn, at any life total, on any board. "Play no
land right now" is never your answer while a land is listed. Take the colour your hand needs.
=== MANA ===
"Mana available: N" is a FLOOR for the GENERIC part of a cost only: Overgrown Battlement adds {G}
per defender but the line "counts it as one source, not as its mana", so your real generic mana is N
plus one per defender past the first.
THE COLOURED PIPS ARE A SEPARATE COUNT AND THE BATTLEMENT PAYS NONE OF THEM. It makes {G} only - not
the {B} in Sanguine Bond {3}{B}{B}, Exquisite Blood {4}{B} or Tribute to Hunger {2}{B}, not the {W}
in Idyllic Tutor {2}{W}. Before planning a coloured spell read the source sub-list under the mana
line and COUNT the untapped cards holding that pip; Sanguine Bond needs TWO {b} sources. A hand card
with NO "Cast" row is not castable this window - never name one.
=== THE WIN BUTTON ===
Read your battlefield line at every decision - NO HEADER ANNOUNCES A COMPLETE PAIR. The "LOOP HALF
PENDING" block prints only while a half is MISSING, so its absence is not "no loop". If BOTH
"Exquisite Blood" and "Sanguine Bond" are on your line, take the first of these the menu offers:
 - "Cast Tribute to Hunger" at 1 creature or more (a wall of theirs counts; a Fog Bank is 2 life,
   and 2 is enough). This is the win you press on your own turn with no creature of your own.
 - A block by Pride Guardian (3 life, always), or by ANY wall while Perimeter Captain is out (2 life
   per blocking defender). Without a Captain, a Battlement's or Wall of Omens' block gains nothing.
 - "Deal 1 Damage with Staff of Nin targeting a player" - pick the OPPONENT.
 - Attack with a lifelink Vampire, blocked or not.
One life is enough, and a wall cast instead is a turn given away. A PRICE ON THE ROW IS NOT A REASON
TO DECLINE IT: while its own "you would be at N" is 1 or more you survive paying and the loop ends
the game in that window. When "DRAW FORECAST: ... = N life" prints an N at or above your life, that
draw step is lethal - no later window exists this turn.
THE LOOP NEEDS A STARTER AND IT IS ALWAYS A CARD OF YOURS: Exquisite Blood reads "whenever an
OPPONENT loses life", so damage dealt to YOU triggers nothing. With both names out and none of these
available (no creature on their line; no Staff, Sorin or Vampire of yours) CAST SOMETHING EVERY
TURN: Staff of Nin, then Sorin, then Wall of Omens, then any wall.
Three rows are NOT live cast rows: a Tribute reading "at 0 this does nothing"; a row tagged "[second
copy: you already control <that same name>"; a Tutor tagged "[finds only ... a copy of one you
already control or hold". When every cast row is one of those take the hold row; otherwise, with
both names on your line, "Cast nothing right now" is never your answer.
=== ASSEMBLING THE PAIR ===
Exquisite Blood {4}{B}: whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much.
Sanguine Bond {3}{B}{B}: whenever you gain life, the opponent loses that much.
THE PAIR IS TWO DIFFERENT NAMES; a second copy of either does not complete it. Read both names off
your own battlefield line; a COUNTERED copy is not on it, so cast the next copy. SANGUINE BOND ALONE
IS ALREADY A CLOCK - every point you gain comes off their total; EXQUISITE BLOOD ALONE DOES NOTHING
YOU CAN START. Holding neither, the Bond is the half to find and to cast.
 1. The turn either half appears in your menu, CAST IT - not after a wall, not once you can afford
    both.
 2. One half on your battlefield and the other on neither your hand nor battlefield line: "Cast
    Idyllic Tutor {2}{w}" is your best play. If the other IS on your hand line with a Cast row, that
    row is the play and the Tutor is not.
 3. The Tutor puts the enchantment in HAND, not play, so cast it the FIRST turn it is listed.
 4. On the search list, read your battlefield line AND hand line first: the name printed on either
    is the one you have, so pick the OTHER; holding neither, pick SANGUINE BOND. If their line
    prints "[named: <half>]", take the OTHER half - that one costs 3 life to cast.
 5. Four Tutors, two of each half; if one is countered or exiled, tutor the next copy.
=== TRIBUTE TO HUNGER IS AN EDICT, NOT A KILL SPELL ===
The OPPONENT picks which of their creatures dies and picks their worst; you gain that creature's
toughness, so never plan it "on their best creature". The row reads "they control 4 creatures - they
choose which one", or at exactly one it NAMES victim and life ("<name> is sacrificed, you gain 2"),
or "at 0 this does nothing".
CAST IT when any of these is printed:
 - Both enchantment names are on your battlefield and the row reads 1 creature or more: this wins.
 - The row NAMES the victim and it is NOT tagged [defender]: their choice is your choice, so this is
   clean removal - cast it IN THAT WINDOW, at any life total, ahead of both halves, the Tutor and
   every wall. "is sacrificed" means it IS their only creature now, not that it may become the only
   one later. Sharpest case: a victim reading "{T}: Put a ... token" or "{T}: Create", or a 1/1 that
   destroys a land each turn - printed power does not measure it.
 - The header prints damage you cannot block ("none of your creatures can block", or a "CRACK-BACK
   NEXT TURN" total with no untapped blocker of yours) and your life is 12 or less: ANY live Tribute
   row, including "they choose which one", outranks another wall, a Lantern and a Staff.
 - Their only creature is one your board cannot block ([flying] or similar): Tribute takes it at any
   life total, in the first window offered.
 - "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN: ... that would KILL you": a row that REMOVES an attacker or ADDS a blocker
   outranks everything this window. The half you skip stays in hand.
 - You need the life to survive the turn.
HOLD IT when the row names a victim tagged [defender] (Shield Sphere, Fog Bank, a wall), unless your
life is 10 or less or both halves are out: a creature that cannot attack is not what is killing you,
and "remove their blocker" is never a reason - nothing of yours attacks except a Vampire.
ONE TRIBUTE ANSWERS ONE CREATURE, AND A SECOND WINDOW THE SAME TURN IS NOT A SECOND CREATURE - the
same row returns with the same verdict while your own spell resolves. THE ROW SAYS SO ITSELF: "but
an effect already on the stack is aimed at that same creature; if it is gone when this resolves they
control 0 creatures and this does nothing" OUTRANKS the "is sacrificed, you gain N" in front of it -
take the hold row, never a second copy.
THE ZERO STOP: never cast it on "at 0 this does nothing" - it sacrifices nothing and taps you out.
=== BLOCKING IS HOW THIS DECK DEALS DAMAGE ===
The combat prompt's general hint about trades says your guide overrides it; THIS IS THAT OVERRIDE.
Your walls have 0 power, so no block wins a trade - blocks are how you gain life.
STEP 1 - READ THE PARENTHESIS ON EVERY B-LINE FIRST. Per attacker it says your blocker LIVES
("neither dies", "you kill it", "both die") or DIES ("your blocker dies, attacker lives"); that
outranks the "[deals 0 ... but it STOPS all N damage]" tag beside it.
 - A blocker whose EVERY offered attacker reads "your blocker dies" stays OUT of your BLOCKS line,
   unless one of three is printed: an enchantment of yours on your battlefield line, the header's
   "you would be at N" at 8 or less, or the blocker is a lifelink Vampire. "both die" is NOT that
   line - take it. Offered a "dies" attacker and a "lives" attacker, take the LIVES one even when
   the dies one has the bigger "deals N".
 - ONCE AN ENCHANTMENT OF YOURS IS OUT, STEP 1 IS OFF AND EVERY BLOCKER BLOCKS, on any line. The
   B-line prints "{blocking trigger: you may gain 2, and if you do your converter takes 2 off
   them}" - that clause IS the deck's damage on a turn with no Tribute, ping or Vampire, so "BLOCKS:
   none" is never your answer while it shows. Same at "you would be at 8" or less. ONE LIMIT: with
   no enchantment out, when every attacker is already blocked by a blocker that lives, an extra
   "dies" blocker only feeds them a body.
STEP 2 - assign every blocker STEP 1 did not bench, in every combat:
 - One blocker per blockable attacker FIRST, leftovers onto the biggest; blockers may gang on one
   attacker and each triggers its own life gain. Never stack the board on one attacker while another
   blockable one walks through; an attacker no blocker of yours can block is damage you must count.
 - MENACE NEEDS TWO OR NONE: "[menace - only a block by TWO OR MORE of your creatures counts]". One
   body stops NOTHING. Two menace attackers and two blockers: both on ONE - one each is two illegal
   blocks and the engine throws out your whole answer.
 - Among attackers that all let that blocker live, READ THE "deals N" OFF EACH A-LINE AND TAKE THE
   BIGGEST; the life gain is identical either way. [deathtouch] kills your wall regardless.
 - Perimeter Captain gains 2 for EACH defender that blocks; Pride Guardian gains 3 whenever it
   blocks. The life arrives when blockers are DECLARED, so a wall that dies has paid it.
WHEN THE HEADER SAYS "this KILLS you", NOTHING ABOVE APPLIES: there is no next combat, so give each
blocker the BIGGEST "deals N" it may legally block and ignore lives/dies and unpairable menace.
"BLOCKS: none" is right only when EVERY blocker falls under STEP 1, and at a header of 8 or less
STEP 1 benches nobody - so there it is right only when no blocker is offered. You never concede.
=== ATTACKING ===
All sixteen creatures are Defenders and can never attack; an empty attacker list means ATTACK: none
at no cost. A Vampire token has lifelink, so every point it connects with is life gained.
THE COLLAPSED TAG reads "they have N untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest X",
sometimes with "- GANG BLOCK: ... each result above is a LONE blocker only". The parenthesis prices
ONE blocker, GANG BLOCK prices what happens, and your Vampires are 1/1s, so any two bodies eat one.
 - A GANG BLOCK clause, or N of 2 or more: ATTACK: none for that Vampire; keep it as a blocker. Same
   when N is 1, the parenthesis reads "your attacker dies", and you have exactly ONE Vampire.
 - N is 1 with no GANG BLOCK clause: COUNT YOUR OWN A-LINES. If more Vampires are offered than N the
   surplus connects whatever the parenthesis says - send them ALL. THEY choose which attacker each
   blocker stops, so it is a count of bodies getting through, not a promise about WHICH Vampire
   lives. An attacking creature does not block.
 - No blockers listed, or "[no creature they control can block this]": send every Vampire.
THE WIN BUTTON OUTRANKS EVERY STOP BUT THE LIGHTMINE ONE: with BOTH names on your battlefield send
EVERY Vampire whatever the tag says - lifelink pays you for damage dealt to a BLOCKER, and that
starts the loop.
ONE STOP, NAMED IN THE ATTACK TOTAL HEADER: "Lightmine Field fires on the declaration, before any
combat damage" deals the NUMBER OF ATTACKERS to EACH attacker: ATTACK: none, or take Sorin's -2
emblem and send ONE 2/1 Vampire per turn. Same stop off the log - if your last attacker died before
combat damage, ATTACK: none until that permanent leaves their line. THIS ONE STOP HOLDS WITH BOTH
NAMES OUT: a Vampire that dies on the declaration deals nothing and gains nothing, so no loop
starts - take the emblem first, or win off a Staff ping or a Tribute instead.
=== WHAT TO CAST WHEN SEVERAL THINGS ARE LISTED ===
Take the highest entry that appears in your menu. A row tagged "[DRAW PRICE: ... you would be at
X]" is barred here unless it is entry 1: that price is paid per CAST, and a wall bought with life
against a punisher board is life spent on nothing.
 0. SURVIVAL FLOOR, above everything: a Tribute row naming a single non-defender victim, or any row
    that removes an attacker or adds a blocker while "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN: ... that would KILL you"
    is printed. Cast it THIS window, at any life total. THE CRACK-BACK BINDS BELOW LETHAL TOO: while
    it prints any number and you control NO untapped blocker, a row that neither adds a blocker nor
    removes an attacker is not worth "casting this taps you out" - the wall or the Tribute is.
 1. Exquisite Blood or Sanguine Bond - whichever NAME is missing from your battlefield line, at any
    "leaves N", including "casting this taps you out". A copy of a name already there is not this
    entry; "[second copy: ... no legend rule]" means the copy is LEGAL, not worth casting. NOTHING
    BELOW ENTRY 1 IS AN ANSWER WHILE AN ENTRY-1 ROW IS PRINTED - not a wall, a Lantern, a Tutor,
    "leaves N", or a life total. BOTH NAMES MISSING AND BOTH ROWS PRINTED: TAKE SANGUINE BOND.
    "CASTING THIS TAPS YOU OUT" IS A MANA NOTE, NOT A WARNING. The one price that outranks this
    entry is the row's own "[NAMED BY THEIR Silverquill Silencer: ... this KILLS you]": then take
    the other half if its row carries no such price, else go down.
 2. Idyllic Tutor, when either half is missing from BOTH your hand line and your battlefield line. A
    missing name on your HAND line with a Cast row is entry 1, not this. The Tutor is dead when
    tagged "[finds only an enchantment card - every enchantment left in your library is a copy of
    one you already control or hold]" - then go down. The other form, "still in your library ...
    <name>", IS this entry.
 3. Perimeter Captain - it turns every future block by every wall into 2 more life.
 4. Any other wall: Wall of Omens (it replaces itself), Overgrown Battlement, Pride Guardian.
 5. Staff of Nin.  6. Sorin - but with SANGUINE BOND ALONE on your line SORIN COMES FIRST: his
    Vampire's lifelink gain is life YOU gain, so the Bond converts it; the Staff's damage is not.
 7. Chromatic Lantern, when nothing from 1-6 is on the menu AND no Lantern is on your battlefield
    line. IT MOVES ABOVE ENTRY 4 IN EXACTLY ONE SHAPE: a half or Tutor on your HAND line whose
    coloured pips your untapped SOURCES cannot pay - count the {b}/{w} sources under the mana
    line first. A low "Mana available:" is not that shape: if the pips are already there the Lantern
    buys nothing and the wall is right. Once a Lantern is on your line, finished.
 8. If BOTH names are already on your battlefield, STAFF OF NIN and SORIN move to the TOP of this
    list - their ping and their Vampires are the only triggers you can make without the opponent.
 9. Tribute to Hunger at "they control N creatures - they choose which one", or at a [defender]
    victim when you want the life more than the card.
10. "Cast nothing right now" - correct only when no entry above appeared.
Every castable card has an entry here. Keep casting while the menu keeps offering.
=== WRITING THE ANSWER ===
ONE "CHOICE:" LINE PER REPLY, AND IT IS THE ONE THAT RUNS. Write your PLAN line, then one CHOICE
line, stop.
THE NAME IN THE PARENTHESES IS WHAT THE ENGINE EXECUTES when it disagrees with the number: copy the
name off the row you mean, character for character, and check the number against it. A NUMBER
OUTSIDE THE PRINTED ROWS IS A REFUSAL and spends the window; attacking is a LATER decision, reached
by "Cast nothing right now (combat comes next this turn)".
=== THE HOLD ROW ===
The last row of nearly every casting menu is "Hold priority ... pass now, and do not ask me again".
It costs NOTHING and does not end your turn. TAKE IT instead of "Cast nothing right now" whenever
the menu prints "NO LIVE CAST ROW ON THIS MENU" or "[you declined this exact list N times already
this turn]". Decline only when you WANT asking again - holding Tribute for a creature yet to attack.
A LOYALTY ROW OF SORIN'S, A STAFF PING ROW, OR ANY OTHER ACTIVATED ROW IS A LIVE ROW, and the hold
row's "you give up no cast" is not about them: taking it here throws that activation away for the
rest of the turn. NEVER HOLD ON A MENU THAT PRINTS ONE. If your PLAN line names an activation, the
CHOICE number on the next line is that row - a plan you answer with a pass never happens. "I will
activate it in my second main" is not open to you: the row itself says a hold taken in your first
main covers your second while these rows stand.
=== STAFF OF NIN AND SORIN ===
Staff of Nin {6}: an extra card at each of your upkeeps, and "Deal 1 Damage ... targeting a player"
each turn. Aim it at the OPPONENT, every upkeep - the one upkeep option you always TAKE. With both
halves out it wins on the spot. It is damage, not a trigger, so the Bond alone does not convert it.
Sorin {2}{W}{B}: cast when offered, then "+1: create a 1/1 vampire" every turn; Vampires have
lifelink. HIS -2 EMBLEM BEATS A 1-DAMAGE BOARD: it is PERMANENT and makes every Vampire a 2/1. TAKE
IT ONCE, in the first window that prints it, whenever anything on their line kills a 1/1 on sight.
Take the +1 every turn after. THE -6 ROW ("destroy up to three and reanimate") IS YOUR ONLY SWEEPER
AND IT KEEPS WHAT IT KILLS - the three come back under YOUR control, so it removes their board and
hands you the attackers your walls never were. The turn it is printed and three or more creatures
are on their line, TAKE IT over the +1.
=== THE SILENCER TAG ===
When their battlefield line prints "[named: <card>]" after one of their creatures, every time YOU
cast that exact name you LOSE 3 LIFE and THEY DRAW A CARD. BOTH HALVES ON ONE MENU, ONE ROW CARRYING
THAT PRICE: cast the UNTAGGED half - unless the untagged one is EXQUISITE BLOOD and the tagged one
is SANGUINE BOND, and then above 12 life you pay the 3 and cast the BOND: Blood alone starts
nothing. A half that COMPLETES the pair this turn: pay and cast. One that
does NOT: skip at 12 life or less. A Lantern, Tutor or wall is never worth 3 life.
=== DRAW PUNISHERS ===
With only a "DRAW FORECAST: ... = N life" and no per-row price, the damage is metered by your DRAW
STEP and hand size is the multiplier: empty your hand, cheapest permanents first. Once the ROWS
carry "[DRAW PRICE: ...]" it is metered by your CASTS instead and emptying your hand is suicide.
=== CLEANUP DISCARD ===
Above seven cards at end of turn the ask prints a NUMBERED hand and how many to send; you choose the
hand you KEEP. Send in this order: Chromatic Lantern, Staff of Nin, a duplicate wall, a land beyond
the sixth, Sorin, then a single wall, cheapest first. A Tribute goes under last, and NEVER while a
"{T}: Put a ... token" / "{T}: Create" creature is in play.
=== MULLIGAN ===
The question is ODDS, never a number the hand must meet. 24 of 60 cards are lands, most making two
colours, and sixteen are walls, so almost any two-land hand acts on turn one or two. "No early play"
and "no combo piece" are not reasons to ship.
 - Two or more lands and a coverage line naming even one card: KEEP, at any price.
 - Two or more lands and "would not cover any spell in it": KEEP on a seven. On a six or smaller
   with exactly two lands, ship only if the hand also holds no one-mana spell.
 - Zero or one land: SHIP on a seven and on a six. At "would keep 5 cards" or smaller, keep a
   one-land hand whose coverage line names a card, and ship only a hand with no land at all.
 - Seven lands and no spell ships at any price; six lands and one spell is a keep, whichever spell.
BOTTOMING, at any hand size: keep the lands covering your cheapest kept spells (a {B} land first),
then one of Sanguine Bond / Exquisite Blood / Idyllic Tutor, then your cheapest wall. Send a
duplicate wall first, then a Lantern or Staff of Nin, then your most expensive spell, then a surplus
land; a combo piece goes under last.
