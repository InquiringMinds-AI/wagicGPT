# deck152 - proposals for the strategy-writing skill (wave 48)

S-1. **A POSTURE THAT SAYS "RACE" MUST NAME THE BOARD ON WHICH RACING IS THE LOSS.** Amendment 78
made a posture rule select among the options the price rules allow. deck152's converter posture
("race it with your evasion") did exactly that - it sent only the unpriced flier - and the flier
killed the seat, because on a board holding BOTH loop halves the unpriced damage IS the price.
So: a posture rule for a matchup mechanic names the mechanic's ESCALATED form (the second card,
the pair, the emblem) and states the verdict there, in the render's vocabulary (both names on
the battlefield line), before it states the default posture. The escalated form is usually the
one that ends games, and it is the one the render is least likely to describe (R12).

S-2. **A LETHAL CHECK BELONGS BEFORE THE FIRST SPEND OF THE TURN, NOT ONLY BEFORE THE ATTACK.**
Amendment 71 put the lethal carve-out above absolutes that could forbid a win; amendment 56 put
a race gate above the attack floor. deck152 lost a game it had won on the screen because the
lethal was spent in UPKEEP (a 6-mana pump) and MAIN 1 (a creature cast by tapping two attackers)
- both before any attack rule was read. Every deck whose mana can come from creatures, or whose
activated abilities tap attackers, carries the lethal count as the first check of the TURN, keyed
to the printed sum and the opponent's `of which 0 are creatures`, and says it outranks the
deploy floor for that one turn.

S-3. **WHEN A DECK'S CREATURES ARE MANA SOURCES, THE GUIDE OWNS THE PAYMENT.** The engine's
auto-payer taps whatever is untapped, and the mana line names the creatures it will use. A guide
for such a deck (Katilda, Overgrown Battlement decks, any lord that grants "{T}: add") carries a
rule keyed to the `Those sources, one per untapped card:` list: a creature named there is tapped
by the next cast. Until E-3's row clause ships this is the only surface that says so, and it is
a header line, not the row (#89 preference (b)).

S-4. **THE FIRST GUIDE IN THE POOL WITHOUT A QUICK-REFERENCE LIST WAS THE ONE THAT LOST TO A
COLLISION.** deck152 was the only guide of the three without a DECIDING SITUATIONS list (amendment
24's contract). Its converter posture and its price rule lived in prose 250 lines apart. The
list is where both branches of a rule sit next to each other (#98) and where a matchup mechanic's
escalated form gets its one line; added this wave with every existing prohibition restated.
Definition-of-Done: every guide has the list, and a reviewer inheriting a guide without one adds
it before tuning anything else (#81's coverage diff applied to the guide's own structure).

S-5 (measurement). **Report `put 1/1 counters` / any tap-cost activation by PHASE, the way Hive
animations are reported for deck146.** 9 Katilda activations this corpus, 8 in Upkeep; the phase
distribution was the finding, and no obedience metric would have shown it.
