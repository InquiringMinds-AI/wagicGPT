# deck35 — proposals for the strategy-writing skill (Step 0-ter validation seat)

All items single-seat; tagged. Most are CONFIRMATIONS of existing Step 0-ter rungs with a clean
worked example, plus one sharpening. Layer-routing respected (engine items are in notes.md).

## S1 [CONFIRM — the 0-attacker durdle instrument earned its adoption]
The instrument (adopted wave-17 as a MEASUREMENT) delivered a clean, unambiguous DIED verdict this
wave: **0/6 zero-attacker games (was 2/6)**, and the two former durdle-losses changed character
(49 LOSS→WIN, 62 durdle→race). The instrument read the guide's success in one line, independent of
the 4/6 record — exactly its intended job. Reinforcement rung: when a guide's headline is an
"attack every turn" countermand, the zero-attacker count going to 0 is STRONGER evidence the
countermand fired than any win-rate move. Keep it as the first thing tabulated for such decks.

## S2 [CONFIRM + SHARPEN — killing a bad-CHOICE class does not kill the TAX at that seam]
Step 0-ter rule 4 (tax-relocation) and the wave-17 "died-quiet is a re-check candidate" rung both
predicted this, and deck35 is a textbook case: FC1's wrong block CHOICES died, but the wasted
REASONING at the block seam did NOT leave with them — it became the corpus's DOMINANT residual and
produced the only fallback (62 s16, 11,169 chars → unparsed). **Sharpening:** after a bad-choice
class at a seam is validated dead, RE-MEASURE reply length at that same seam before calling it
settled — the tax can outlive the choice error and is often now the top failure. (The instrument
for choices — the durdle count — says nothing about the tax; they are separate gauges.)

## S3 [SHARPEN Step 0-ter / guide-writing — a "trust the rendered number" rule must be TWO-SIDED]
deck35's guide told the pilot to trust ITS OWN rendered [X/Y] and not recompute the lord stack. The
model obeyed that perfectly (multi-anthem buff math is now clean). But the block-seam blow-up came
from the UNGUIDED side: the model distrusted the OPPONENT's rendered [X/Y] and the "deal up to N"
damage annotation, re-derived a 10/12 aura creature's power from its enchantments, and spiraled when
its OWN (wrong) arithmetic disagreed with the CORRECT annotation. **Lesson:** a one-sided trust rule
leaves the model free to distrust the other side and re-open the very computation the annotation
exists to close. When you write "trust the rendered number," scope it to BOTH boards and to any
damage/effect annotation the representation provides — not just the pilot's own creatures. (Applied
in this wave's revision.)

## S4 [CONFIRM — losses live in the off-case, and the off-case can be CONSTRUCTION, not guide]
Step 0-ter rule 6 again: the guide fixed the durdle; both residual losses are the deck being
out-raced (turbo-Affinity) or out-sized (a 10/12 aura giant) with its blue evasion color-screwed
out of hand. A single-deck guide cannot fix a construction axis (the premier win-con Shifting Sliver
was uncastable for lack of blue in TWO corpora). Rung: when a validated guide's remaining losses all
trace to matchup speed/size or a color/curve the guide cannot change, say so and route to
construction/notes — do NOT invent guide prose to "fix" a variance/construction loss (that is the
over-broad-countermand risk of rule 3 in reverse). Guide steers priority; it cannot draw an Island.
