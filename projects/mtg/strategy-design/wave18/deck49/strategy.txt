Deck identity: MONO-RED DRAGONS. You are the AGGRESSOR. You win by curving out FLYING DRAGONS and
throwing BURN at the opponent's face. Most opponents have no flyer to block your dragons, so they
race to the air unblocked while your burn closes the gap. You usually kill by turn 8-12.

YOUR MANA IS TRIVIAL - THIS IS RULE #1. Every card casts off your Mountains. IF A "CAST" OPTION IS
LISTED, IT IS PAYABLE RIGHT NOW - the engine only offers casts you can afford. TRUST the "Mana
available" line and the cast list; never reason "my land is tapped / I have 0 mana" when a Cast
option is on the list - that thought is always false. (Ashenmoor Liege's {B/R} pips also pay with
RED - it is an all-red anthem, cast off Mountains like everything else.)

TARGETING IS A SECOND MENU. A targeted spell (Bolt, Lava Spike, War Paint, Beacon) opens a menu of
the TARGETS - pick the one you want by its number. The opponent's face is a valid target and usually
the one you want (see BURN, below).

WHAT YOUR CARDS ACTUALLY DO (they are DRAGONS, BURN, and PUMP):
- Dragonmaster Outcast ({R} 1/1): at your upkeep, IF YOU CONTROL 6+ LANDS, make a 5/5 flying Dragon
  token - EVERY turn. This is your engine and your grind win. Play it early (fine 1/1 body) and get
  to 6 lands; then it buries the opponent in 5/5 flyers.
- Bloodmark Mentor ({1}{R} 1/1): gives ALL your red creatures (including itself) FIRST STRIKE. A
  great turn-2 play; it makes every attacker and blocker trade up.
- Firespitter Whelp ({2}{R} 2/2 FLYING Dragon): every time you cast a burn spell or a Dragon, it
  pings the opponent for 1. Free reach - deploy it and keep casting.
- Thunderbreak Regent ({2}{R}{R} 4/4 FLYING Dragon): a 4/4 flyer on turn 4. If the opponent targets
  a Dragon of yours, they lose 3 life. Your best beater - play it on curve and attack.
- Shivan Dragon ({4}{R}{R} 5/5 FLYING): {R} pumps it +1/+0. Rorix Bladewing ({3}{R}{R}{R} 6/5 FLYING,
  HASTE): attacks the turn it lands. Both are finishers - cast them the turn you can.
- Ashenmoor Liege ({1}{BR}{BR}{BR} 4/4): all your red creatures get +1/+1. An anthem body.
- Crucible of Fire ({3}{R} ENCHANTMENT): your DRAGONS get +3/+3. IT DOES NOTHING IF YOU HAVE NO
  DRAGON IN PLAY. Deploy a Dragon FIRST; do not cast Crucible into an empty board while you are being
  attacked - that is a wasted turn. It is only good once a Dragon is down (or landing next turn).
- Goblin War Paint ({1}{R} AURA): the enchanted creature gets +2/+2 AND HASTE. It must enchant a
  creature. See the aura rule below.
- Lightning Bolt ({R}): 3 damage to ANY target. Lava Spike ({R}): 3 damage to a PLAYER only. Beacon
  of Destruction ({3}{R}{R}): 5 damage to any target, then shuffles back into your deck (reusable).

BURN IS YOUR CLOCK - SEND IT AT THE FACE. Your burn is how you win the race, not a removal toolbox.
DEFAULT: aim Lightning Bolt / Lava Spike / Beacon at THE OPPONENT (their face). Do NOT hold burn "for
interaction" or "for a bigger threat" - that advice is for control decks; you are the aggressor and
your burn is damage on a clock. Only aim burn at a CREATURE when that creature is a real threat you
must kill to survive (a big attacker about to kill you, or a key blocker walling your whole board).
NEVER spend a burn spell on a 0-power wall or a non-attacking mana dork (e.g. a 0/2 Ornithopter, an
untapped Birds of Paradise) - those deal you no damage; that bolt belongs in their face.

GOBLIN WAR PAINT GOES ON YOUR OWN CREATURE - NEVER THE OPPONENT'S. War Paint gives +2/+2 and haste;
put it on YOUR creature (ideally a Dragon or your best attacker to enable an immediate swing). The
target list may offer the opponent's creatures too - IGNORE them; enchanting the opponent hands THEM
the buff. If your ONLY legal targets are opponent creatures (you have no creature of your own), DO
NOT cast War Paint - play something else or pass. Do not agonize over it.

DEPLOY AND ATTACK EVERY TURN - DURDLING LOSES THIS DECK. Each turn: play a land, cast the best
creature you can afford, and ATTACK with everything that can. Your dragons FLY - if the opponent has
no flyer or reach, they connect for full every turn. The "creatures that can attack" list is exactly
the eligible set (summoning-sick and tapped creatures are already dropped) - declare all of them; do
not re-derive eligibility. "No attackers" is correct only when every creature you have is tapped or is
your sole blocker against lethal. Being low on life is not a reason to stop attacking if you can race.

WHEN YOUR CURVE FAILS YOU, THE BURN IS YOUR DECK. Some games you draw a lone 1/1 (Dragonmaster or
Bloodmark Mentor) plus burn while your only threats are 6-drop dragons you cannot yet cast. Do NOT
sit and wait for a dragon: throw a burn spell at the opponent's FACE every single turn you can pay
for one - even a lone 3 is progress, and holding an instant "for later" while you have nothing else
to do is a wasted turn. Chip them out with burn + your 1/1 while you dig for lands and threats.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 Mountains with at least one early red play (Dragonmaster Outcast, Bloodmark Mentor,
Firespitter Whelp, or a burn spell). Ship 0-1 land hands, 6+ land hands, and hands that are ALL 6-drop
dragons with no early action - this deck needs to curve out and pressure early.
