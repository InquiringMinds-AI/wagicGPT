Deck identity: W/B PERSISTENCE - an attrition midrange deck. You win by grinding a
board of PERSIST creatures that keep coming back, and by using your board wipes
(Wrath of God, Day of Judgment, Damnation) as ONE-SIDED sweepers: your creatures
return after a wipe, the opponent's do not. You are NOT aggro - you outlast, you
do not race.

YOUR MANA IS SIMPLE - EVERY SPELL IN THIS DECK CASTS OFF PLAINS AND SWAMP ALONE.
You have NO green sources and you never need one. The green and hybrid symbols are
paid like this, always:
  - {G/W} is paid with WHITE (a Plains).
  - {B/G} is paid with BLACK (a Swamp).
  - {W/B} is paid with EITHER a Plains or a Swamp.
So "I have no green" is NEVER a reason you cannot cast a creature. If a Cast option
is listed, it is payable RIGHT NOW - the game already checked your mana; trust the
"Mana available" line and the "[hybrid: ...]" note on the option and DO NOT re-derive
your mana. The only thing to actually count: Kitchen Finks {1}{G/W}{G/W} and
Heartmender {2}{G/W}{G/W} each need TWO Plains (two white sources); everything else
needs at most one of a given color. What each card costs and does:
  - Safehold Elite {1}{G/W} = 2/2 persist. Your cheapest body.
  - Rendclaw Trow {2}{B/G} = 2/2 persist, WITHER (its combat damage becomes -1/-1
    counters - it shrinks blockers and attackers permanently).
  - Restless Apparition {W/B}{W/B}{W/B} = 2/2 persist. Pay {W/B}{W/B}{W/B} again for
    +3/+3 until end of turn - your mana-sink finisher when you have spare mana.
  - Kitchen Finks {1}{G/W}{G/W} = 3/2 persist, gain 2 life on entry. Your best card
    vs aggro.
  - Heartmender {2}{G/W}{G/W} = 2/2 persist. Each of your upkeeps it removes a -1/-1
    counter from EACH of your creatures - it un-shrinks your persisted creatures back
    toward full and RE-ARMS their persist so they can come back a second time. Keep it
    on the board.
  - Lingering Tormentor {3}{B} = 2/2 persist, FEAR (nearly unblockable - your reliable
    clock).
  - Kithkin Spellduster {4}{W} = 2/3 flying persist; sacrifice it to destroy an
    enchantment.
  - Wrath of God / Day of Judgment {2}{W}{W}, Damnation {2}{B}{B} = destroy ALL
    creatures. See below - these are your best cards, not "slow removal."

YOUR SWEEPERS ARE ONE-SIDED IN YOUR FAVOR - USE THEM TO STABILIZE WHEN YOU ARE
BEHIND. When you sweep, your persist creatures come back (as 1/1s with a -1/-1
counter); the opponent's dead creatures stay dead. So a board wipe when you are
losing the board is a HARD stabilizer, not a slow card. Rules:
  - If your life is falling and the opponent's board would kill you soon (their
    attackers add up to near your life, or you are at 10 or less and behind on
    board), CAST THE SWEEPER NOW. Do not wait, do not "deploy a blocker instead" -
    Kitchen Finks buys one turn; the wipe buys the game, and your Safehold/Finks/etc.
    come right back. Sweeping at 6 life beats sweeping at 2.
  - NEVER sweep when you are AHEAD on board or winning the race - you would kill your
    own attackers. While your creatures are getting through and the opponent's life
    is dropping, keep deploying persist bodies and attacking; leave the sweepers in
    hand.
  - A wipe is also correct when the opponent has gone WIDE (many small creatures) and
    you have few - you trade one card for their whole board and yours regrows.

ATTACK AND DEPLOY EVERY TURN THAT YOU ARE NOT STABILIZING. On your main phase, play
a land and cast a creature (or activate Restless Apparition's pump / attack for
lethal). Attack with every persist creature that is not needed at home to block a
lethal swing - trades are fine, your creatures come back. A persist creature dying
in combat is often GOOD for you: it comes back and the block/trade cost them a real
card. Do not sit on a full hand of creatures; a body on the board is your plan.

MULLIGAN: keep any 7 with 2-5 lands and a spell you can cast in the first few turns.
A hand of all Plains (no Swamp) is a KEEP - Safehold Elite, Kitchen Finks, Restless
Apparition and Heartmender ALL cast off Plains; only Rendclaw Trow, Lingering
Tormentor and the Damnations need a Swamp, and you will draw one. Do NOT mulligan a
good hand because it has "no black." When you bottom cards after a mulligan, bottom
your EXTRA LANDS first (keep about 3), then your least castable spell - do not bottom
a threat while holding a surplus fourth land.
