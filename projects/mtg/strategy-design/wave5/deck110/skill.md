# Proposed revisions to the strategy-writing skill (from deck110, wave 5)

Two small, evidence-backed additions. The skill is already strong on the re-equip/waste-loop
pattern (Step 2 "Waste loops"; the Step-5 three-part re-offer fix); these refine, not replace.

## P1. The re-offer three-part fix has a proven CEILING — say so, and route the residual to representation

Step 5's "When the engine RE-OFFERS a completed action" block prescribes a DONE-SIGNAL cue +
count brake + release. deck110 now has a clean natural experiment showing the CUE part fails
even when it works perfectly as *perception*: at `1783980343` seq84 the pilot READ the
two-power-number cue, VERBALIZED the correct conclusion ("already attached... waste of mana"),
and still chose the re-offered equip. The cue is not the weak link — the re-offer itself is.

Proposed edit: add one sentence to that Step-5 block: "This three-part guard is a STOPGAP with
a known ceiling: when the corpus shows the pilot correctly READING the done-signal in its reply
and STILL taking the re-offered action, instruction has failed at full strength — record it as a
representation defect (marker on the option line / suppress the re-offer) and stop escalating
guide wording." This keeps a reviewer from spending wave N+1 making the cue louder (the exact
trap the skill warns about elsewhere) once the cue is provably being read.

## P2. Count-brakes that cap a REPEATABLE action must key on the countable resource, not the turn

The skill's re-offer fix recommends "take at most ONE Equip in a whole turn" as the robust
count brake. deck110 wave-5 falsified the flat-per-turn form for the multi-copy case: with two
Cranial Platings in play, TWO equips in one turn are correct (attach each). A flat "one per
turn" brake forbids the second legit attach. The brake still works, but it must key on the
countable that bounds legitimacy (unattached copies of the equipment), not on the turn.

Proposed edit: in the Step-5 re-offer block, change the count-brake guidance from "a COUNTABLE
per-turn brake (e.g. one Equip per turn)" to "a COUNTABLE brake keyed to the resource that
bounds legitimacy — one activation per UNATTACHED copy / per unused charge, not a flat per-turn
cap, which mis-fires when the deck runs multiples." One-line change; prevents a brake that is
simultaneously violated AND wrong.

## Not proposed

- No change to the overshoot-audit method — it worked exactly as written here (the Lightmine
  exception was found by the Step-0 "audit the #1 rule for overshoot / locally-obedient
  globally-bad" lens, and confirmed against `auto=` per the mechanism-check rule).
- No change to the negative-framing ruling — the terminal DO-NOT -> positive recap conversion
  and the two sanctioned constructs (the mana falsity-fact; the one bare NEVER on Lightmine with
  its Galvanic-Blast redirect) mapped cleanly with no friction.
- No change to turnaround-preservation mode — the frozen-diff + freeze-check discipline fit a
  5/6 deck perfectly; it is why this revision stayed tiny.
