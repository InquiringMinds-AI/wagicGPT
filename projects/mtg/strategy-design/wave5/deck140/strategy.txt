Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck: 14 board wipes, almost no
creatures, three finishers (Rakdos's Return, Staff of Nin, Blightsteel Colossus).
You win BY DOING, every turn: sweep their creatures, deploy a finisher, then grind
their life to 0 with your reach. You out-survive every deck in this pool - the only
way you LOSE is by never closing. So closing is job #1.

THE #1 RULE - RAKDOS'S RETURN IS YOUR KILL SHOT, HELD FOR LETHAL:
You have only two Rakdos's Return and it is your only burst reach - it deals X
damage to the opponent's FACE and makes them discard X cards. Do NOT spend it
early for small damage; a Rakdos's Return fired for X=3 is a kill you threw away.
Instead, run this check EVERY turn it is in hand: X = (your untapped lands) minus 2.
If that X is at or above the opponent's life, cast it NOW and pick the biggest X -
you win this turn. Your Staff of Nin pings the opponent down every turn, so this
lethal X arrives on its own; wait for it. Fire it below lethal ONLY when the
opponent has 3+ cards in hand AND is attacking you - then the discard earns it.

THE X MENU (Rakdos's Return, Black Sun's Zenith): the menu now lists the BIGGEST X
FIRST. "1. X = 12", "2. X = 11", ... , last line "X = 0". Pick OPTION 1 - it is the
largest X. Before sending, confirm the line reads the biggest number shown. (For
Black Sun's Zenith, biggest X is free - it shuffles back into your library.)

DEPLOY WHEN SAFE - every turn the opponent has no creatures (or only creatures too
small to kill you within two turns), cast the FIRST of these that appears, the turn
it appears, never "later":
 1. Cast Staff of Nin {6} - draws you a card every upkeep AND pings 1 damage a turn;
    it is your repeating clock. Cast it the first turn you can afford it.
 2. Cast Blightsteel Colossus {12} - 11/11 trample infect, indestructible; it
    attacks by itself and one unblocked hit is 11 poison = dead opponent. Cast it
    the turn you reach 12 mana. Your only job is to cast it.
 3. Cast Venser's Journal {5} - gain life per card in hand every upkeep; best vs aggro.
 4. Cast Lightmine Field {2}{w}{w} - only vs a deck that ATTACKS; skip it vs a
    creatureless deck (it does nothing if no one attacks).
 5. Cast Elixir of Immortality {1} - cast with spare mana; activate ({2},{T}: gain 5)
    when you are below 10 life or your library is under 10 cards.
With no opponent creatures you are stable NOW - deploying does not stop you sweeping
next turn. (Rakdos's Return is NOT on this list - it is your kill shot, held per THE
#1 RULE.)

STAFF OF NIN IN PLAY - take its "Deal 1 Damage" option EVERY turn, at the OPPONENT's
face. Target a creature only if its toughness is shown as 1 (1 damage kills it);
anything toughness 2 or more (Guttersnipe is 2/2) survives the ping and wastes your
clock, so hit the face instead.

WHICH SWEEPER (only when the opponent HAS creatures - match the board):
- Exactly ONE creature: Cruel Edict {1}{b} - they sacrifice it.
- 2+ creatures, ALL toughness 2 or less: Pyroclasm {1}{r}.
- 2+ creatures, any toughness 3+: Wrath of God {2}{w}{w} or Damnation {2}{b}{b} -
  destroys everything.
- Indestructible / protection / regeneration, or a single huge creature: Black Sun's
  Zenith - pick option 1 (biggest X); X at or above their biggest toughness kills
  everything, and it shuffles back, so spend X freely.
Sweep as soon as they have 2+ creatures, or any board that kills you within two
turns. You carry 14 sweepers - one per board, never saved while you take damage.

INTERFACE FACT: every "Cast" option listed is already affordable - lands tap by
themselves and "Mana in your pool: (none)" is normal. The thought "I have no mana"
or "my lands are tapped" is ALWAYS FALSE when a cast is offered. "Cast nothing" is
correct ONLY when every offered spell is a sweeper or Cruel Edict AND the opponent
has no creatures - otherwise cast something.

LANDS: play a land EVERY turn; never pick "Hold <land>".
MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2, 3, or 4 lands. Never mulligan for "too many
sweepers" - sweepers are what this deck does.

OVERRIDE of the general strategy: you have NO instants - never pass your turn to
"keep mana open"; nothing in your deck casts on the opponent's turn. Cast on your
own main phase, every single turn.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize these - they decide your games):
- Your untapped lands minus 2 is at or above the opponent's life and Rakdos's Return
  is in hand -> cast it, pick option 1 (biggest X), and win this turn.
- Opponent's board is clear and Staff of Nin or Blightsteel is castable -> cast it
  the first turn offered; that is your clock starting.
- Staff of Nin resolves its "Deal 1 Damage" -> send it at the opponent's face.
- Opponent has one big or hexproof creature -> Cruel Edict makes them sacrifice it.
- Opponent has 2+ creatures with toughness 3+ -> Wrath of God or Damnation, not
  Pyroclasm (Pyroclasm's 2 damage leaves the big ones alive).
