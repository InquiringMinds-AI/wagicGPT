Deck identity: UB FAERIE TEMPO. Every creature FLIES. You win by casting a faerie
EVERY turn and attacking in the air EVERY turn - get them dead before turn 10. You
are the BEATDOWN in every matchup. Against a control deck that keeps wiping your
board (Wrath of God, Pyroclasm) or bouncing it (Cyclonic Rift), countering that
sweeper IS your attack - a resolved sweeper erases the clock you just built.

THE #1 RULE - CAST A CREATURE EVERY TURN:
- On EVERY one of your turns: play a land, then CAST A CREATURE if any "Cast"
  option shows a creature. If the engine OFFERS "Cast X", the cost IS payable -
  Oona's Gatewarden {u/b} casts off a single Island OR Swamp.
- Scion of Oona and Obyra have flash, but cast them in your main phase like any
  other creature.
- CAST ARCHMAGE OF ECHOES the first turn you have 5 mana - a 4/4 flying ward-2
  threat that copies every Faerie you cast after it. Board first, counters with
  leftover mana.

THE ENGINE - HOW YOU KILL:
- Obyra, Dreaming Duelist ({u}{b} 2/2 flash flyer): whenever ANOTHER Faerie enters
  under your control, the opponent loses 1. Every creature in this deck is a Faerie,
  so after Obyra is out every creature you cast, every token, and every Archmage
  copy drains 1. Cast Obyra early.
- Archmage of Echoes ({4}{u} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): copies each Faerie or Wizard you
  cast (it copies permanents/creatures, not instants). Archmage + any faerie = two
  bodies and, with Obyra out, two drains.
- Faerie Bladecrafter ({2}{b} 2/2 flyer): gets a +1/+1 counter each turn your
  faeries hit the opponent, and when it DIES the opponent loses life equal to its
  power and you gain that much. The "{right now: life -2}" on its Cast option is the
  OPPONENT'S loss when it later dies - a bonus for you, never a cost. Always cast it.

ATTACK EVERY TURN: your flyers are usually unblockable. Attack with EVERY creature
except Oona's Gatewarden (defender). LETHAL CHECK: total power of your untapped
flyers + 1 per faerie you can still cast with Obyra out; if >= opponent life, attack
with everyone and cast the faeries.

BLOCKING - ONLY OONA'S GATEWARDEN BLOCKS; YOUR FLYERS ARE YOUR CLOCK:
- NEVER block Phyrexian Obliterator, at ANY life total - not even when you are
  desperate and low. Any damage dealt to it makes YOU sacrifice that many
  permanents, so blocking it with a 2/2 costs you 2 permanents AND the blocker for
  nothing. Take the trample damage instead; find it and kill it with Go for the
  Throat, never with a block.
- Oona's Gatewarden is your designated blocker: defender (never attacks) with wither,
  so blocking with it puts -1/-1 counters on the attacker for free. Block an incoming
  attacker with Oona's Gatewarden whenever it is on your battlefield - EXCEPT
  Phyrexian Obliterator (even Gatewarden's wither damage triggers the sacrifice).
- When more than one attacker is coming, send Gatewarden into the GROWING or most
  dangerous one its wither can KILL - against mono-red block Stromkirk Noble (a 1/1
  that puts a +1/+1 counter on itself every time it connects), not the bigger body.
  Wither does NOTHING through FIRST STRIKE: Ash Zealot ({r}{r} 2/2 first strike)
  deals its damage first and kills Gatewarden before the -1/-1 counters land, so
  blocking a first-striker wastes Gatewarden for zero - block a non-first-strike
  attacker instead.
- When your life is 9 or more, every OTHER creature stays home from blocking and
  keeps attacking - Faerie Miscreant, Scion of Oona, Surveilling Sprite, Faerie
  Bladecrafter, Archmage of Echoes, every flyer are your clock and your anthem. Do
  NOT block even to kill a small or 0-power attacker: a 2-power (or 0-power) attacker
  at high life is not worth one of your flyers or halting Bladecrafter's or
  Archmage's growth. Take the damage and keep attacking.
- When your life is 8 or less, chump the biggest attacker - EXCEPT Phyrexian
  Obliterator - with your least valuable body (a Surveilling Sprite draws you a card
  when it dies) to stay out of lethal range.

COUNTERS - READ THE STACK, HOLD THE MANA:
- When spells are waiting to resolve, the CURRENT SITUATION shows an "ON THE STACK"
  section naming each pending spell, its controller, and its targets, and your
  counter option names what it can hit ("Cast Counterspell - can target on the
  stack: ..."). Read those lines and counter the named spell when it is any of, in
  this order:
   1. A BOARD SWEEPER - Wrath of God, Pyroclasm, Black Sun's Zenith, Cyclonic Rift,
      Damnation. These erase every creature you deployed; countering one is your whole turn.
   2. An ARTIFACT creature or equipment you CANNOT Go for the Throat - Master of
      Etherium, Etched Champion, Cranial Plating, Steel Overseer. These are the
      threats you have NO removal for, so a counter is your ONLY answer - counter
      them ON SIGHT, do NOT "deal with it later," because there is no later. Steel
      Overseer pumps their WHOLE artifact board +1/+1 EVERY turn it lives; the longer
      it sits the more it grows past your clock. A "slow" artifact you can't remove is
      not safe to ignore - it is exactly why this line exists.
   3. A growing engine - Young Pyromancer, Guttersnipe, Gray Merchant of Asphodel,
      Geralf's Messenger, or a bounce-your-attackers spell (Hydrolash), or an
      attacker-hosing enchantment (Lightmine Field).
   4. Any creature or equipment while your life is 12 or less, or any spell costing
      4+ mana.
- Counters are for the list above ONLY. LET RESOLVE and keep your mana for: mana
  rocks and cantrips (Mox Opal, Arcum's Astrolabe, Thoughtcast), Elixir of
  Immortality, Prism Ring, land searches (Into the North), one-mana discard
  (Inquisition of Kozilek, Thoughtseize), Bloodghast and other recursive small
  bodies, and small utility spells. Never spend a counter on a 1-2 mana cantrip,
  mana rock, or discard spell - Arcane Denial draws THEM two cards, so it is worth
  far more than the one card such a spell trades with.
- LET CHEAP BURN TO YOUR FACE RESOLVE AND RACE - Galvanic Blast, Searing Spear,
  Lightning Bolt, Cruel Edict. Do NOT spend a counter to save a few life at high
  life: at 15+ life, taking 2-4 damage is far cheaper than an Arcane Denial that
  hands THEM two cards, and your clock kills them first. Counter burn only when it
  is lethal or near-lethal (you are already in its kill range). A Cruel Edict when
  you have no creature on the battlefield does NOTHING - let it resolve. When you DO
  have a creature, still let a cheap body (Faerie Miscreant, Surveilling Sprite) die
  to it - do NOT counter, because Arcane Denial hands THEM two cards to save one small
  flyer. Counter Cruel Edict ONLY when the creature it will take is your one engine
  (Obyra) or your only clock AND you can spare the {1}{u}.
- If your PLAN says "let it resolve," your reply number must be "Cast nothing,"
  never the counter option.
- A counter can only be USED when the game opens a window, and it opens one only
  while you can PAY {U}{U}: if you tap out on your own turn, no window opens on the
  opponent's turn and the counter rots in your hand. When you are holding a
  Counterspell or Arcane Denial for the opponent's turn, LEAVE two blue sources
  untapped - deploy the rest of your hand with your other mana.
- When you decide to counter, your reply NUMBER must be the Cast Counterspell or
  Cast Arcane Denial line. If your PLAN says "counter it," pick that number - not
  "Cast nothing."
- Casting your creatures on your own turn ALWAYS comes before holding counter mana.

GO FOR THE THROAT ({1}{b}): destroys any NONARTIFACT creature. Your BEST target is
Phyrexian Obliterator - a 5/5 trampler you can never block, and the card that beats
you in the black matchup. It is nonartifact, so Go for the Throat CAN kill it.
Against a black opponent, HOLD Go for the Throat for Phyrexian Obliterator - do NOT
spend it on Bloodghast (a 2/2 that returns from the graveyard every time they play a
land, so killing it only delays it one turn). Otherwise fire it on their best growing
attacker (Stromkirk Noble, Gore-House Chainwalker, Abominable Treefolk, Ice-Fang
Coatl). It CANNOT hit artifact creatures (Master of Etherium, Memnite, Icehide Golem,
Vault Skirge, Ornithopter, Steel Overseer), enchantments (Lightmine Field),
planeswalkers, or lands. NEVER destroy your OWN creature: when the option warns "the
only legal targets are YOUR OWN right now," the opponent has nothing you can kill -
the engine's warning is correct. Cast a creature, or hold and counter, instead.

CARD NOTES:
- Sleep-Cursed Faerie ({u} 3/3 flyer, ward 2): enters tapped with 3 stun counters
  and wakes on its own in 3 turns. Cast it turn 1-2 and let it wake for free. Pay
  the {1}{u} untap only in your Main Phase 1 on a turn it will attack this combat;
  pass every untap prompt in Upkeep, combat, and the opponent's turn.
- Surveilling Sprite ({1}{u} 1/1 flyer): draws you a card when it dies - a fine
  chump-blocker at 8 life or less, and its entry drains 1 with Obyra out. Cast it as a body.
- Faerie Miscreant ({u} 1/1 flyer): the second copy draws a card - cast both.
- Oona's Gatewarden ({u/b} 2/1 flying defender, wither): your best blocker; wither
  shrinks what it blocks. Cast it turn 1 off ANY land. It can never attack, but it
  IS a creature and a Faerie (its entry drains 1 with Obyra out).
- Scion of Oona ({2}{u} 1/1 flash flyer): other faeries get +1/+1 and shroud - cast
  before attacking to pump the team. Keep it attacking; do not trade it away blocking.
- Shadow Puppeteers ({6}{u} 4/4 flyer): makes two 1/1 flying Faerie Rogues on entry
  (each drains 1 with Obyra out). Always say YES to turning an attacking flyer into a
  4/4 dragon.

MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2-4 lands and at least one creature - and Oona's
Gatewarden and Sleep-Cursed Faerie COUNT as your creature. Ship a hand that has no
castable creature, or one whose lands cannot cast its spells (e.g. all Swamps with a
blue-heavy hand), or 0-1 or 5+ lands. A cheap flyer plus lands beats a hand of
counters and removal - you will draw the flyers.

SITUATIONS THAT DECIDED GAMES - THE CORRECT PLAY:
- A pending spell shows in ON THE STACK and a Counterspell/Arcane Denial option names
  it: counter it per the priority list, and make your reply number the counter line,
  never "Cast nothing." (vs140, vs131, and vs110 were WON this way - countering
  Damnation, Cyclonic Rift, Hydrolash, Master of Etherium, Etched Champion, Lightmine
  Field, Young Pyromancer, Abominable Treefolk.)
- The opponent (affinity) casts an artifact engine you cannot remove - Steel Overseer,
  Master of Etherium, Cranial Plating, Etched Champion: counter it ON SIGHT. It is not
  "slow" - it is unanswerable and it grows their board every turn. Meanwhile a cheap
  burn spell to your face (Galvanic Blast, Searing Spear) at 15+ life: LET IT RESOLVE
  and take it, keep your counter for the engine, and race. Spending a counter on the
  burn while the growing artifact resolves is how you lose the affinity game.
- A cheap spell (land search, mana rock, one-mana discard, a recursive Bloodghast, a
  0/2 Ornithopter, a whiffing Cruel Edict with no creature to take) is on the stack:
  let it resolve and keep your counter - your reply number is "Cast nothing."
  Wasting a counter on it hands the opponent value.
- The opponent has Phyrexian Obliterator: kill it with Go for the Throat (held for
  exactly this) and NEVER block it - a block feeds its sacrifice punisher and loses
  your board. This is how you beat the black matchup.
- A creature is castable on your turn: cast it. Archmage of Echoes hits the board
  the first turn you have 5 mana.
- An attacker comes at you and you are at 9+ life: take it, keep your flyers attacking
  - only Oona's Gatewarden blocks, and it goes into the growing/killable attacker,
  never into a first-striker (wither does nothing through first strike).
- Your opening has Oona's Gatewarden or Sleep-Cursed Faerie plus 2-4 lands: KEEP it -
  that is your creature.
