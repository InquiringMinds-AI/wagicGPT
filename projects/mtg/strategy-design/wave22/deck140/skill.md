# Skill proposals — deck140 wave 22 (rotated-in seat: what re-validation taught about guide aging)

Routing: skill (method) proposals only. Engine/harness/card items are in notes.md. The deck-specific
guide diff is in strategy.txt.

## PROPOSED RUNG 1 (NEW, strong single-seat evidence + generalizable) — "Rotating a converged deck
back in AGAINST A CHANGED POOL is a NEW-LEAK HUNT, not a re-validation of the obeyed lines."

Evidence: deck140 converged in wave 15 (6 corpora of correct SWEEP-TRIGGER / #1-Rakdos-hold /
sweeper-selection) and was rotated in for wave 22 against a pool with TWO new opponents (109 Mono-R
Aggro, 102 Tergrid). Re-validating the obeyed lines was trivial and near-zero-value — they were
already proven across six prior corpora and were obeyed again everywhere they fired. **100% of the
wave's real guide work came from the seam the OLD pool never exercised:** Tergrid's Archon of Cruelty
forced deck140 to discard, and the guide had NO rule for forced self-discard because no prior-pool
opponent had that mechanic. deck140 pitched Damnation then Pyroclasm (its two live sweepers) and lost
a game it was winning at 23 life.

Proposed rung text (for the rotation-discipline / Step-0 section):
> When a CONVERGED deck is rotated back in and the OPPONENT POOL has changed, do not spend the wave
> re-confirming the obeyed lines (they are proven; a changed pool cannot un-prove them, only change
> matchup difficulty). Spend it hunting the seams the NEW opponents exercise that the OLD pool never
> did — new mechanics aimed at the seat (forced discard/sacrifice, mill, specific evasion, lifegain,
> combo) are where a long-frozen guide is UNDEFENDED, because a frozen guide only defends the leaks
> its historical opponents produced. Diff the new pool's decks for mechanics absent from the seat's
> prior corpora; those are the wave's candidate new rules.

Why it generalizes: rotation exists to stop re-reviewing proven seats, but "proven against pool P" is
not "proven." A pool swap is a fresh adversary set, and the frozen guide's coverage is exactly the
union of its past opponents' threats. The rung tells the reviewer where to point attention on
rotation-in so the cycle actually earns its cost.

## PROPOSED RUNG 2 (sharpening of the existing crutch-lifecycle rung) — "A long-frozen guide is where
DEMOTION DEBT accumulates; re-audit EVERY belief-kill against the CURRENT printed representation on
rotation-in, because representation shipped WHILE the guide sat frozen."

Evidence: deck140's deployed guide is wave-11 vintage. Between then and now the `(creatures: N)` count
became printed representation, yet the guide still carried the FULL tapped-still-counts and
artifacts-don't-lower-C walkthroughs (double coverage — the count carries "how many"). A guide that is
actively revised each wave demotes these as the fixes ship; a FROZEN guide silently accrues the debt.
This wave demoted both to one-line marker-anchored nods and KEPT the shroud targeting-fact (the count
does not carry "how your spell interacts") — the per-belief demotion is not uniform, exactly the
wave-8 deck140 ruling.

Proposed rung text (append to the crutch-lifecycle rung):
> The crutch-lifecycle audit is MOST OVERDUE on a guide that has been frozen for several waves: every
> representation fix that shipped while it sat frozen is un-applied demotion debt. On rotation-in,
> re-audit every interim crutch against what the representation LITERALLY surfaces TODAY (not what it
> surfaced when the guide was written), and demote/retire per-belief — a count dissolves "how many"
> beliefs but not "how the spell interacts."

This is not new doctrine; it is a targeting instruction (WHERE the debt concentrates) for an existing
rung. Route as an adopt-if-convergent sharpening, not a standalone rung, if the synthesis agent
prefers.
