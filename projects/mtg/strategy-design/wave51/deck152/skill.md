# deck152 - skill proposals (wave 51)

S-1. **A PER-CARD EXCEPTION IS WRITTEN FOR BOTH SIDES OF COMBAT AT ONCE.** #148 keyed Katilda's
B-row to the parenthesis and the header N; the A-row twin was left to the generic stop rule, and
the next corpus broke it on exactly that card (`152 vs146` s12: a 1/1 mana engine sent into a
`(your attacker dies, their blocker lives)` tag on turn 6). A creature the guide singles out on
defence is singled out on offence in the same paragraph - the parenthesis strings are the same
family (`your attacker dies` / `your blocker dies`), so the second sentence costs one line (152-G).

S-2. **"THE ONLY ANSWER" IS A CARD-FACT CLAIM AND GETS #145's GREP.** deck152's Spellbinder bullet
said its exile was "the ONLY answer this deck has to an enchantment"; the primitive's
`canplayfromexile` + `changecost(colorless:2)` (Oracle: "its owner may play it ... costs 2 more")
makes it a tax, and `152 vs126` s49 shows the exiled Bond cast from exile. Rule: any guide sentence
that calls a card an "answer", "removal" or "the only way" is checked against the primitive's
`moveto`/`transforms` clause for a return path, and written as the effect the engine delivers
("buys turns") when one exists. Extends #145 from class words to effect words.
