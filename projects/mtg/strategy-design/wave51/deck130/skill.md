# deck130 - skill proposals (wave 51)

S1. A RENDER SHIP THAT COMPLETES A ROW FAMILY RETIRES THE GUIDE'S "THIS FAMILY IS SILENT" SENTENCE
THE SAME WAVE, AND THE REVIEWER'S #139 COLUMN FLIPS. D50-5 taught "ability rows print NO result" one
wave ago; lane E printed the result 428 times at this seat. The sentence was false on every window
of the corpus and the guide kept it until this pass (D51-2). Proposal: #139's per-family table is
re-run at every corpus for every family a lane touched; a family that flipped from silent to printed
is a mandatory edit in the same wave's revision, before any behavioural finding is scored against it.

S2. WHEN THE PILOT'S PLAN QUOTES THE MECHANISM AND BREAKS THE RULE, THE CONTRADICTION IS THE NEXT
PARAGRAPH, NOT THE MISSING FACT (#102 second failure, proven). 130v126 seq 20's plan explained
Sanguine Bond correctly and attacked anyway; the guide's ATTACK FLOOR said "send EVERY creature
listed" one paragraph below the WALL CHECK. #102(b) says look for a contradiction elsewhere at the
second failure - here it was adjacent, and the fix was ordering (the wall check became the floor's
FIRST case), not text. Proposal: at a second identical failure the reviewer reads the paragraph
AFTER the rule for an imperative that outranks it in the pilot's reading order.

S3. A PAYMENT THAT COSTS LIFE IS A ROW FACT THE GUIDE KEYS TO THE `{leaves 0 of your N}` CLAUSE, NOT
TO A LIFE NUMBER. D50-12's "life 3 or less" floor failed three times in one game (D51-3) because the
pilot's plan never priced the Talisman; the row's own "leaves 0 of your 1" plus the sources line is
the trigger a pilot can see. General: any hidden cost (pain lands, Talismans, phyrexian mana) is
taught at the string that reveals which source will pay, with a dictated "pays: life L -> L-1".

S4. AN "ALWAYS" ON A CREATURE'S ROLE IS #135'S ABSOLUTE-ABOUT-A-CLASS. "Blastminer alone: ATTACK:
none - always" was written for boards where his ability had work; on an opponent at 0 lands it
forfeited seven damage (D51-6). Proposal (confirms #135): role rules for utility creatures state the
job the creature is being kept for, so the pilot can see when the job is gone.
