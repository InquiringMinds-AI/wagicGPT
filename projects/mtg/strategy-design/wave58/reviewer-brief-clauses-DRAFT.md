## 1. Rotation (restored from the exit-era briefs, waves 28-35)
Per-deck rotation state is carried in this brief's header and re-argued every wave:
  deckN — HOLD | EXIT-ELIGIBLE (named gate) | EXIT projected wave-M | CONSTRUCTION-CEILINGED.
Rotation is UNSENTIMENTAL. Rotation arguments must be adherence/maturity-shaped (do the guide's
rungs fire and hold; do the seat's items belong to the deck or to the engine), never
record-shaped (win rate is unattributable under fair hands). Exit gates: HL1 retroactive card-fact
audit of the whole guide; every open falsifier adjudicated PASS or retired. Engine items a seat
surfaces are the WAVE's product, not the deck's debt: they do not block that deck's exit.
Synthesis carries the projection forward; the orchestrator cuts the next entrant from the
never-cycled queue (109 valid-format) as a Step-0 guideless first pass.

## 2. The null outcome is the expected steady state
A guide that needs no edit is returned UNCHANGED and reported as such ("deckN: no edit — every
rung fired and held; N/M windows"). This is the normal result for a converged guide, not a
failure of the review. The four per-deck files remain, but strategy.txt may be byte-identical to
the live guide and edit-texts.md may be empty. Reviewers are judged on the quality of their
adjudication, not on the number of edits.

## 3. An edit needs a falsifier, and dies without one
Every guide edit ships with a falsifiable prediction the NEXT corpus adjudicates (the existing
"every added line paid for by a cited seq" is necessary, not sufficient: a cited seq is an
anecdote). An edit whose prediction reads UNTESTED for two consecutive corpora is retired by the
boundary pass. An edit that re-keys a literal the render changed is a MAINTENANCE edit, listed
separately and not counted as a strategy change for rotation.

## 4. Skill amendments need two seats
A strategy-writing-skill amendment requires the same rule to have been violated or needed at two
or more seats in the corpus (or an owner ruling). Single-seat observations go to the seat file as
evidence for a future amendment, not into the skill. Synthesis reports the amendments it declined
under this rule.

## 5. Guide size is not a target
The 41-71 KB band is a ceiling, not a level to fill. A guide that shrinks is not a regression.
