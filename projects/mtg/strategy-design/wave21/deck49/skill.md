## VALIDATED rung — "either is fine — COMMIT" + tighten-the-exception DISSOLVES a co-firing fork; it does not merely get parse-absorbed

The wave-20 rung said: when two guide imperatives co-fire on one board state with no tiebreak, the
weak executor deliberates to truncation; the fix is (a) "either is fine — pick one and COMMIT" + a
soft board-state lean when both answers are fine, and (c) scope an over-broad EXCEPTION to the
degenerate board it misfires on. The wave-20 seam broke the zero-fallback streak with 3 unparses (5-9k
chars) at the develop fork.

**This corpus the deployed tiebreak was tested against the SAME seam type in all 6 games and resolved
every one in 719-1202 chars (a clean deploy commit, 0 oscillation, 0 truncation).** The method point
worth recording for the skill:

1. **A co-firing-imperative fix must be judged by REPLY LENGTH at the seam, not just by fallback
   count.** Answer-first (the wave-21 protocol) would make a still-oscillating spiral PARSE, masking a
   failed fix behind a 0-unparse count. The discriminator is length: if the fork were merely
   parse-absorbed, the seam replies would be long-but-parsed (5k+ chars, like the wave-20 spirals);
   because they are SHORT (sub-1200), the fork was genuinely dissolved. **Always length-check the
   seam, or you will credit answer-first for a guide fix (or miss a guide fix answer-first is hiding).
   This corpus separated the two: develop seam SHORT (guide dissolved it); combat/card-value pockets
   LONG-but-parsed (answer-first absorbing a still-live spiral).** This is a durable addition to the
   Step-0 method: co-firing-fork verification = seam reply length, post-answer-first.

2. **The soundness check held.** The wave-20 rung soundness-checked the soft lean against what the
   recovered turns actually did. This corpus: the lean ("no board threat -> deploy") matched the
   actual commit in 6/6 forks, and all 6 were correct aggressive plays (opp at 17-20, no lethal burn).
   The lean is confirmed sound, not just non-harmful.

3. **The tightened exception is validated HARMLESS but its narrow-correctness is UNEXERCISED.** Burn-
   face 13/13, 0 creature-targeted (cleaner than wave-20). But no must-kill-blocker spot arose, so the
   claim "the tightening does not over-correct into never-kill-any-blocker" is confirmed only on the
   over-correction side (no wrongful face-aim), not on the exception's own trigger. Method note: a
   scope-tightening edit needs BOTH the misfire board AND the true-fire board in a validation corpus
   to be fully discharged; single-corpus repeated-pool validation may exercise only one side. Track
   the exception's true-fire (a genuine key blocker) as an OPEN validation thread, same discipline as
   the wave-19 off-case thread deck49 already carried and discharged.
