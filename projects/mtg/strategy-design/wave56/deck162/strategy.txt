DRAW AND DIE - UB FORCED-DRAW PUNISH

The opponent's own draw step is your kill. Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler and Ob Nixilis each
deal the opponent 1 damage EVERY time that opponent draws a card - and they stack, so three of them
out means 3 damage per card. Howling Mine, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's Puzzle Box and Forced
Fruition make the opponent draw MORE cards. Put the damage on the battlefield FIRST, then hand them
cards. Every turn you do one of three things: land a punisher, put a wall in front of their
creatures, or - once a punisher is out - stack another draw engine on top. You are never idle and
you never wait to stabilize.

=== RULE 0 - PLAY A LAND EVERY TURN YOU ARE OFFERED ONE. NOTHING OUTRANKS THIS ===
When a "Land drop:" question is put to you and one of its options plays a land, PLAY THE LAND.
The land drop is its OWN decision - the prompt says so on its own line. It costs no mana, it is
not part of your casting decision, and taking it does not spend anything you were going to cast
this turn: you play the land AND cast the spell, on the same turn, in that order. "I am casting
Underworld Dreams this turn, so I will hold the land" and "I will play it next turn when I know
what I need" are the two sentences that lose this deck games before turn six - both are false.
Your punishers cost three to five mana and every land you skip is a turn they arrive late.
Never answer "Play no land right now" or "Hold ..." while any land is playable, and when several
are offered take the one whose colour your hand needs (black for the punishers first).

=== RULE 0b - TRUST THE "Mana available" LINE. DO NOT RE-DERIVE YOUR MANA ===
"Mana available: N total (... colours you can make: {u}{b})" gives you two facts and both are
correct: N is what you can spend this window, and the colour list names which COLOURED pips you
can pay. GENERIC IS NOT A COLOUR - Underworld Dreams {B}{B}{B} needs three black sources, but
Howling Mine {2} and Fog Bank {1}{U} take any source for their generic. Never spend a decision
asking "do I have generic mana"; there is no such thing as not having it. And if a spell is
printed as a numbered "Cast ..." option, it IS payable - the game has already checked. Pick it.

=== RULE 0c - COUNT THE BODIES BEFORE YOU CAST ANYTHING. THIS OUTRANKS RULE 1 ===
RULE 4's survival arithmetic is run at the BLOCK window, and by then the mana is gone. Run this
one at every "which card do you cast now" ask, before you read Rule 1, off three numbers that are
already printed:
  L  = your printed life.
  N  = your own battlefield line's "of which N are creatures".
  M  = their battlefield line's "M of them without a restriction against attacking".
If M is greater than N and L is 10 or less, THIS TURN IS A BODY TURN. Take every creature row on
the menu you can afford in ONE window, cheapest per body first, before any noncreature row -
Shield Sphere {0} first because it is free, then Fog Bank, then Master of the Feast. A five-mana
planeswalker that taps you out is the wrong card on a body turn however loudly Rule 1 reads: the
punisher is damage you collect over turns you are still alive for.
READ THE "{leaves N of your M untapped mana sources untapped}" CLAUSE ON EACH ROW AND SPEND THE
WHOLE WINDOW. Two bodies out of one ask is the answer that survives; one body and a walker is not.
WHAT THIS COST: at 4 life with five untapped sources against four untapped attackers and ZERO
creatures of its own, this seat was offered Ob Nixilis {3}{b}{b} "{leaves 0 of your 5 ...}",
Master of the Feast {1}{b}{b} (5/5) "{leaves 2 of your 5 ...}" and Shield Sphere {0} on the SAME
menu. Master plus Sphere was affordable together and is two blockers. It took Ob Nixilis, tapped
out, and the next menu had one row left. Three attackers for 7 arrived, one 0/6 blocked one of
them, and it died at 0 with the opponent on 11 - and its own PLAN on that reply said "Cast
Master of the Feast" while the row it took had already made that impossible.

=== RULE 1 - THE PUNISHER GOES FIRST. ALWAYS. READ YOUR CAST LIST TOP TO BOTTOM ===
Your three punishers are UNDERWORLD DREAMS {b}{b}{b}, FATE UNRAVELER {3}{b}, OB NIXILIS, THE
HATE-TWISTED {3}{b}{b}. Every time you are asked "which card do you cast now", scan the numbered
options for those three names FIRST.
If ANY of the three is in the list, CAST IT THIS ASK. Not after the Howling Mine, not after the
Master of the Feast, not in a moment - THIS ask, this reply. It goes ahead of every other card in
your deck, at every life total, on every board.
An option that is printed is an option you can pay for; the game has already checked.
Mana runs out mid-turn: cast the punisher while you can still afford it, then spend what is left.
The thought "it doesn't strictly matter which order I cast these in" is ALWAYS FALSE for this deck.
The order is the single most important thing you decide.
ONE named exception: when your life is 8 or less AND the opponent's battlefield shows a creature,
"Cast Fog Bank {1}{u}" goes ahead of the punisher for that one ask - then cast the punisher with
what is left, or on your next turn.
WHICH PUNISHER, WHEN TWO ARE OFFERED: TAKE THE ONE THAT IS ALSO A BODY. Fate Unraveler (3/4) is
the only PUNISHER of the three that is also a body - it punishes AND it blocks.
Underworld Dreams and Ob Nixilis are not creatures and will never stop a single point of damage.
Master of the Feast (5/5 flying) is not a punisher at all: its own text makes the OPPONENT draw,
so it is a draw ENGINE with a body, it is entry 5 on the cast list, and RULE 2's brake still
governs it. It never goes ahead of a punisher.
Read your own battlefield line's "of which N are creatures" number and their battlefield line's
"of which M are creatures, M of them without a restriction against attacking". When your N is 0
and their M is 1 or more, the punisher with a body goes first, every time - the damage race is
the same either way and only one of the two answers also holds the board.
WHAT THIS COST: at 16 life with zero creatures against three attackers, this seat was offered
Underworld Dreams and Fate Unraveler in the same ask and took Underworld Dreams. It never blocked
once, cast Fate Unraveler four turns later at 3 life, and lost at turn 12 - the only game it
lost this corpus.

=== RULE 2 - HOW MANY DRAW ENGINES YOU MAY HAVE OUT ===
Howling Mine, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's Puzzle Box, Forced Fruition and Master of the Feast all
give the OPPONENT cards - their card text says "each player" or "each opponent". They are damage
only while a punisher is on the battlefield to convert those cards into damage.
EVERY draw-engine row prints its own count: "{feeds: the opponent draws N extra card(s) per turn;
converters on your battlefield: K - <names>}", or "converters on your battlefield: 0 (nothing of
yours punishes their draws or discards yet - the cards you hand them are free until a converter
is out)". Read K off the row you are about to take. It counts Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler,
Ob Nixilis AND Liliana's Caress for you, and it is the whole arithmetic of this rule - not the
punishers in your hand, not the one that was countered last turn. ONE qualifier is yours to add:
when the only name after K is Liliana's Caress, K counts only while the "Opponent hand size:"
line reads 7 or more - every extra card a Mine, a Dictate or a Puzzle Box hands them past seven
is a card they discard at their cleanup, and Caress makes each discard 2 life, a converter that
needs no draw trigger; at a hand of 6 or less a lone Caress converts nothing yet, and the row's
K is read as 0. WHAT THIS BOUGHT: with Caress out and no punisher, two Mines, a
Dictate and a Puzzle Box took a full-handed opponent from 20 to 8 in three turns (2, 2+2,
2+2+2 at their cleanups) before the first Underworld Dreams landed.
- K OF 1 OR MORE (the Caress qualifier passed): cast every draw engine you are offered, every
  turn, as fast as you can - each one multiplies the damage. This is where the deck wins. Stack Mines,
  Dictates and Puzzle Boxes without limit, with ONE ordering rule that never lapses: if a second
  punisher (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Ob Nixilis) is ALSO in this ask's list, cast the
  punisher FIRST and the engines with whatever mana is left. Rule 1 outranks this rule at every
  life total and on every board, whether or not a punisher is already out. A punisher you priced
  out of your own turn is a punisher that never resolves.
- K OF 0: one Howling Mine is enough, and the FIRST Howling Mine is cast at K of 0 - its row
  reads "converters on your battlefield: 0" too, and that is fine for that one card: it draws
  you into the punisher and is the engine you convert later.
  THE ROW TELLS YOU WHICH MINE IT IS, AND IT IS THE ONLY THING YOU HAVE TO READ. A SECOND Mine's
  row carries the tag "[second copy: you already control Howling Mine; both stay on the
  battlefield - no legend rule]". A FIRST Mine's row does not carry it. So: a "Cast Howling Mine"
  row with NO "[second copy:" tag on it is your first Mine - CAST IT THIS ASK. At K of 0. On a
  two-row menu whose other row is "Cast nothing right now". On your turn 4 and on your turn 11.
  The brake below is about the tagged row and nothing else, and "converters on your battlefield:
  0" on an untagged Mine row is not a brake, it is the reason you are casting it.
  ONE COLLISION, AND THE PUNISHER WINS IT: when the same ask offers your first Mine AND an
  Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler or Ob Nixilis, and the row prices say you cannot pay for
  both this turn, take the PUNISHER and the Mine next window. "Cast it this ask" is about a
  menu where nothing better is competing for the same sources, and RULE 1 outranks it.
  With a Howling Mine already on your
  battlefield line, a second row reading "converters on your battlefield: 0" is the brake: take
  a wall, a punisher, a Liliana's Caress, or "Cast nothing right now" instead; Master of the
  Feast, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's Puzzle Box and Forced Fruition all wait for the punisher.
  THE BRAKE HAS ONE RELEASE, AND IT IS READ OFF YOUR HAND LINE. Before you answer, scan "Your
  hand (N cards):" for UNDERWORLD DREAMS, FATE UNRAVELER or OB NIXILIS. If one of those three
  names is IN YOUR HAND, the converter is one turn away and the engine you cast now is drawing
  into it, not feeding a game you are not in: take the engine row. If none of the three is in
  your hand, the brake stands - "Cast nothing right now", every time, however much mana is idle.
  The hand line is the whole test; do not substitute "I will draw one".
  THIS IS WHAT THE THREE TAKES IN THIS CORPUS LOOKED LIKE. Two of them - Dictate of Kruphix at
  K of 0 on turn 8 with UNDERWORLD DREAMS in hand, and again on turn 9 with OB NIXILIS and
  UNDERWORLD DREAMS in hand - were RIGHT, and the first of them is why the draw forecast read
  "their next draw step draws 5 cards" five turns later and ended the game at 20 to 0. The third
  was the break: the same Dictate row on turn 21 with a hand of three Islands, a Puzzle Box and a
  Master of the Feast and NO punisher name in it.
  THE WINDOW THIS BREAKS IN HAS EXACTLY TWO ROWS, AND THE OTHER ONE IS "Cast nothing right now".
  Both breaks in the last corpus looked like this: "1. Cast Howling Mine {2} [second copy: you
  already control Howling Mine ...] {feeds: ... converters on your battlefield: 0 ...}" and
  "2. Cast nothing right now" - nothing else on the menu. That is not a menu with no answer on
  it; row 2 IS the answer, and it is the answer this rule was written to produce. A two-row ask
  whose only spell is a second engine at K of 0 is the brake firing, not the brake failing:
  "Cast nothing right now" costs you a turn of mana you had no better use for, and the second
  Mine costs you the game. Take row 2 and say so in one clause.
  WHAT THIS COST, both ways: a second Mine went down on turn 6 under "converters on your
  battlefield: 0" - the opponent drew into two Vanishing Verses and a Soul Shatter, stripped
  every punisher as it landed, and won from 20 life; and in another game the FIRST Mine sat in
  hand for four windows at K of 0 while the plan said "stack engines once a punisher is out" -
  the first Mine is not what that sentence is about.
THE BRAKE IS ABOUT ROWS THAT PRINT "{feeds:", AND ONLY THOSE. Every card that hands the opponent
a card prints "{feeds: the opponent draws N extra card(s) per turn; converters on your
battlefield: K ...}" on its own row. A row WITHOUT that clause gives them nothing, so no K, no
punisher and no "wait for a converter" sentence has anything to say about it: Liliana's Caress,
Fog Bank, Shield Sphere, a punisher and a land are all free of this rule. Cast them when they
are offered. WHAT THIS COST, six windows in one game: a two-row ask whose only spell was
"Cast Liliana's Caress {1}{b}" was answered "Cast nothing right now" on turns 4, 4, 6, 6, 8 and
8 - two lands untapped every time, no third black source until turn 10, and the plan sentence
reading "cast Underworld Dreams first" about a spell that needed a source the seat did not have.
Six turns of mana bought nothing and the game ended 0 to 38. "Cast nothing right now" is the
answer to a menu of GIFTS, never to a menu of one cheap permanent that gives them nothing.
THE SENTENCE THAT KEEPS DOING THIS IS "as soon as possible". "Cast Underworld Dreams or Fate
Unraveler as soon as possible" is a plan for a LATER window; it is not a reason to decline the
row in front of you. A punisher you cannot pay for is not competing for these two mana - the
engine would have printed its row if it were. So when the only spell on the menu is a Liliana's
Caress, a Fog Bank or a Shield Sphere and your plan sentence names a punisher that is NOT one of
the numbered rows, cast the row and leave the sentence alone. WHAT THIS COST, three more windows
in this corpus after the six above: two-row asks whose only spell was "Cast Liliana's Caress
{1}{b}" answered "Cast nothing right now" on turns 4, 4 and 5, each under a plan reading "cast
Underworld Dreams or Fate Unraveler as soon as possible".
AND YOU CANNOT HOLD MANA ON YOUR OWN MAIN PHASE FOR YOUR OWN SORCERY-SPEED CARD. Underworld
Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Ob Nixilis, the Mines, the Dictates and the Caress are all sorcery
speed: if one of them were castable this window the engine would have printed it as a row.
It is not on the menu, so no amount of untapped mana reaches it this turn - "keep mana open
for Underworld Dreams" on your own main is a sentence that costs a turn and buys nothing.
The thought "drawing more cards is the priority, I need to find my win condition" is ALWAYS FALSE
for this deck - a card you hand the opponent with no punisher out is a card that kills you.
Read K off the row - not the punishers in hand, not the one that was countered last turn. K of 0
and a Mine already there: the wall, the Caress or "Cast nothing".
ONE exception to the brake, and only this one: MASTER OF THE FEAST is a 5/5 flying body as well
as a gift. With NO punisher out, cast it ONLY when the opponent's battlefield line reads "of
which N are creatures" with N of 1 or more - the body is worth the card only when there is
something for it to block. At "of which 0 are creatures" it is a gift like the others and waits.
It still sits behind Rule 1, behind Shield Sphere and behind Fog Bank.
CHECK YOUR OWN PLAN SENTENCE BEFORE YOU ANSWER. If your PLAN says "stack draw engines", "deploy
draw engines", "cast Master of the Feast for board presence" or "once a punisher is on the
battlefield" while the row you are taking prints "converters on your battlefield: 0", the plan is
describing a board you do not have and the answer it leads to is wrong - this seat wrote each
of those sentences while casting a second Mine, a second Master, a Forced Fruition and a Puzzle
Box into an empty line, and handed a control deck twenty cards. Count the names; then answer.

=== RULE 3 - THE WALLS. PUT THEM DOWN THE TURN THEY APPEAR ===
Shield Sphere and Fog Bank are your entire defence - this deck has no removal except Ob Nixilis's
-2 and it gains no life. Both walls are cheap permanents with no downside.
- "Cast Shield Sphere {0}" costs ZERO mana. Take it every single time it is listed, whatever else
  you are doing, including with a full board and no other play. A 0/6 that blocks anything.
- "Cast Fog Bank {1}{u}": if the opponent's battlefield line shows ANY creature, cast Fog Bank the
  first ask it is offered. Fog Bank prevents ALL combat damage dealt to it and by it, so it blocks
  the same attacker every combat and takes no damage from it. It is your answer to a big ground
  creature. Removal still answers it, so a Fog Bank in your hand blocks nothing - put it on the
  battlefield.

THE WHOLE CAST ORDER, ONE LIST. At every "which card do you cast now" ask, read your numbered
options top to bottom and take the FIRST line that matches:
0. RULE 0c's body turn - M greater than N and your life 10 or less: every creature row you can
   afford this window, cheapest per body first, and nothing else. Then stop.
1a. Fate Unraveler - whenever your own battlefield line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and
   theirs shows 1 or more "of them without a restriction against attacking".
1b. Underworld Dreams or Ob Nixilis - otherwise, or when 1a's condition does not hold.
2. Peer into the Abyss, when a punisher is already on your battlefield.
3. Shield Sphere (it is free).
4. Fog Bank, when the opponent controls a creature.
5. Any Howling Mine, Dictate of Kruphix, Teferi's Puzzle Box, Forced Fruition or Master of the
   Feast - when the row's "converters on your battlefield:" number is 1 or more, or it is your
   first Howling Mine (K of 0 and no Howling Mine on your line yet).
6. Liliana's Caress - at K of 0 exactly as readily as at K of 1, and BEFORE the engines, not
   after them. WHEN A CARESS ROW AND AN ENGINE ROW ARE ON THE SAME MENU AND YOU CAN ONLY PAY FOR
   ONE, THE CARESS WINS - its row says so itself, because it prints no "{feeds:" clause and the
   engine's prints "converters on your battlefield: 0". WHAT THIS COST: a three-mana turn 5 menu
   of "1. Cast Liliana's Caress {1}{b}" and "2. Cast Master of the Feast {1}{b}{b} ... {feeds: ...
   converters on your battlefield: 0}" was answered first "Cast nothing right now" and then, on
   the very next window, Master of the Feast - the gift taken and the converter left in hand,
   against an opponent line reading "0 creatures" where the Master's 5/5 body blocked nothing. Its row prints no "{feeds:" clause because it hands them nothing; RULE 2's brake
   is about rows that DO print one. A Caress already on the battlefield is what lets a later
   Mine's row read "converters on your battlefield: 1" at all.
7. "Cast nothing right now".
Then take the ask again if the game offers it - you can cast more than one spell in a turn.

=== RULE 4 - BLOCKING. YOUR WALLS BLOCK; THAT IS WHAT THEY ARE FOR ===
The combat prompt prints a general hint about trades and about taking damage while ahead on
life. It also says your strategy guide overrides it. THIS SECTION IS THAT OVERRIDE: run the
numbered rules below and nothing the hint says.
Every blocker line tells you what its block does: a "STOPS all ..." clause naming the damage
that block erases, and a parenthesis naming who dies. Both are true. Read both and act on them.

SURVIVAL COMES FIRST, AND IT IS ARITHMETIC - RUN IT BEFORE THE NUMBERED RULES BELOW.
Look at the header. If it says the attack IS LETHAL ("you would be at -2 - LETHAL"), the first
question is not which block is free, it is which assignment leaves you alive.
 STEP 1. Each attacker prints its own "deals N". Add them all up - call that TOTAL.
 STEP 2. Your printed life is L. You must block enough that TOTAL minus what you stop is LESS
   than L. At 0 you lose; 0 is not survival.
 STEP 3. With FEWER blockers than attackers you do not get to pick the pretty block. Assign each
   blocker to the biggest "deals N" it is allowed to stop, whatever its parenthesis says, and
   then check step 2 again.
 STEP 4. If more than one assignment survives, take the one that leaves you at the HIGHEST life.
   Surviving at 4 and surviving at 1 are not the same answer: at 1 their next attacker of any
   size kills you. A free kill on a 3-power lifelinker while a 6/6 Wolf walks past you is a
   LOSS you chose, even though the free kill is real.
 STEP 5. If NO assignment survives, block the biggest "deals N" you can and play on.
Only when the header does NOT say LETHAL, or once you have already secured the largest surviving
margin, do the numbered rules below decide anything.

Run this over the offered block lines, every combat, in this order:
0. HOW MUCH GETS THROUGH IF I TAKE THIS BLOCK? Before rung 1, read the header's "you would be at
   N" and read every attacker's "deals N". When ONE blocker is offered several attackers, the
   free kill is only free if what walks past you is survivable. Subtract the damage you would
   stop from the total: if the number left would put your printed life at 8 OR LESS, block the
   BIGGEST "deals N" you are allowed to block instead, even when that line kills your blocker
   and even when a free kill on a small attacker is sitting right there. A free kill on a 2/2
   while a 4/4 walks past and drops you to 4 is a loss you chose two turns early. Only when the
   damage getting past you leaves your life ABOVE 8 does rung 1 below decide anything.
1. A line ending "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" - take that block. Free kill, every time.
2. A line ending "(neither dies)" - take that block. One wall per attacker, spending your walls
   on the attackers with the biggest "deals N" numbers first. A second wall added to an attacker
   that is already blocked stops nothing extra and wears your walls out - never stack two walls
   on one attacker while another attacker is going unblocked.
3. When ONE blocker is offered several attackers, prefer an attacker whose parenthesis lets
   your blocker LIVE over one that kills it, even when the dying line stops a bigger number -
   a wall that survives erases damage again next combat. Take a dying line instead only when
   leaving that attacker unblocked would put your printed life at 8 or below; then block the
   biggest "STOPS all" number you are offered.
4. A line ending "your blocker dies, attacker lives" - take it when the header says the attack
   IS lethal, when your life is 8 or less, or when it is the only line that blocker has.
5. A wall you leave home erases nothing and is being saved for no one. "BLOCKS: none" is right
   only when every blocker you control is already assigned, or every line offered is rule 4 at
   a healthy life total.
Fate Unraveler (3/4) and Master of the Feast (5/5) block well - use them on a "you kill it,
your blocker lives" line when they are not needed untapped for a lethal swing.

=== HOW THE KILL ACTUALLY HAPPENS ===
1. Punisher on the battlefield.
2. Draw engines on top of it. Howling Mine and Dictate of Kruphix each add ONE opponent card per
   draw step, so each adds 1 damage per punisher per turn. Two punishers plus two Mines is 4 damage
   a turn before you attack.
3. TEFERI'S PUZZLE BOX {4} is your best multiplier: each draw step the opponent bottoms their whole
   hand and redraws that many cards, so their hand size becomes damage every single turn. Read the
   number on the "Opponent hand size:" line - with one punisher out, Puzzle Box turns that number
   into that much damage every turn.
4. FORCED FRUITION {4}{u}{u}: whenever the opponent casts a spell, they draw SEVEN cards. With one
   punisher out that is 7 damage per spell they cast; with two it is 14. Cast it the turn you can,
   once a punisher is out. If it is not in your hand, nothing above changes.
5. PEER INTO THE ABYSS {4}{b}{b}{b} finishes. It makes the target draw half their library and lose
   half their life. Cast it when a punisher is on your battlefield - the draws convert into damage
   at 1 per punisher per card, and it draws them more than half their library. It then asks a SECOND
   question with
   two options: "target opponent" and "target controller". Answer "target opponent". NEVER answer
   "target controller" - that one points the spell at YOU; the opponent is always the answer.

=== ATTACKING ===
Read each attacker's own "their untapped blockers" tag and answer from it. The tag is the
engine's computed result - do not argue with it, and do not answer ATTACK: none out of a
general wish to keep your board.
NO "their untapped blockers" TAG AT ALL ON A LINE MEANS NOTHING THEY CONTROL CAN BLOCK IT.
That is free, unopposed damage and the answer is SEND, every time, at every life total - it is
the one attack in this deck that has no downside to weigh. "I will keep it back as a blocker"
is not a reason here: they have no creature that can be blocked, or none that can block this
one, which is exactly why the tag is empty. Only the low-life rule at the bottom of this
section overrides it, and only for your LAST untapped body.
SEND IT when every outcome on that creature's tag reads "(you kill it, your attacker lives)".
That line says your creature SURVIVES and theirs does not: there is no trade to fear and no
punisher to lose. Either they decline the block and take the damage, or they block and hand
you a free kill. "I might trade them away" is FALSE against that tag - the tag already told
you the attacker lives. This is the commonest way this deck throws away free damage, and it
costs you the turns your punishers are buying.
DO NOT SEND Fate Unraveler into a tag that CONTAINS "your attacker dies, their blocker lives"
anywhere in its list, and do not send it into "(both die)" either: Fate Unraveler is a punisher
first, and dead it stops the draw damage that wins you the game, for a creature they simply
replace. The tag also does not tell you what the BLOCKER gains - a lifelink blocker hands them
life on top of the trade. Below the lethal check, both of those lines mean ATTACK: none for
Fate Unraveler.
A "(blocking trigger:" CLAUSE OR A "(lifelink:" TAIL IS ALSO A PRICE, AND SOMETIMES IT IS THE
WHOLE GAME. This deck gains no life and has no removal for enchantments, so life THEY gain is
progress you cannot undo. Two tags carry that price:
  "(neither dies (blocking trigger: they may gain 2))" - the block costs you nothing and pays
  them; a tag containing one is NOT an all-free tag, and the send rung above does not fire.
  "(you kill it, your attacker lives (lifelink: they gain 1))" - you get the kill and they get
  the life. Against 1/1 lifelink tokens their controller remakes every turn, that trade is
  theirs, not yours.
AND WHEN THE PROMPT ALSO PRINTS "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs", EVERY
ONE OF THOSE NUMBERS IS DOUBLE AND POINTED AT YOU. That line means life THEY gain also comes
off YOUR total. Three 1/1 lifelink blockers eating one attacker is 3 life to them and 3 life
off you, and their controller may hold a second enchantment that turns that loop into your
death in a single combat. So: with that converter line on the screen, send ONLY creatures whose
A-line prints no "(blocking trigger:" and no "(lifelink:" anywhere in it. If no attacker
qualifies, the answer is ATTACK: none and you win on the draw step instead - your punishers
deal their damage whether or not you attacked, and that is the whole point of this deck.
ONE FATAL NAME IN THE LIST IS A STOP, HOWEVER MANY FRIENDLY NAMES SIT BESIDE IT. A tag reading
"Tovolar's Huntmaster (6/6) (your attacker dies, their blocker lives); Wolf #2 (2/2) (you kill
it, your attacker lives); Wolf #3 (2/2) (you kill it, your attacker lives)" is a STOP. Two of
those three are good for you and it does not matter: THEY choose which one blocks, and they
choose the Huntmaster. Price the WORST name on the list, always. SEND requires that EVERY name
on the tag reads "(you kill it, your attacker lives)".
AND A LOW LIFE TOTAL IS A REASON TO KEEP IT HOME, NOT A REASON TO SWING - AND THIS APPLIES TO
WHICHEVER CREATURE IS YOUR LAST UNTAPPED BODY, NOT ONLY TO FATE UNRAVELER. Count the creatures
on YOUR battlefield line that are untapped and not summoning sick. If that count is ONE and
your printed life is 8 or less, that creature does not attack, whatever its tag says and even
when its tag is empty - it is the only thing standing between you and the next combat, and an
attacker is tapped for their whole turn. Master of the Feast is the card this rule is usually
about: a 5/5 flier is your best blocker exactly when you are low, and swinging it for 5 while
they hold three ground creatures is trading your life for damage you did not need. The punisher
damage arrives on THEIR draw step whether or not you attacked; that is what buys the turns.
"I have to do something" is not a rule. The rule is the tag and the count.
MASTER OF THE FEAST AND THE COLLAPSED TAG. When a creature's tag does NOT list their blockers
one by one but instead reads "they have N untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest
X (you kill it, your attacker lives)", that parenthesis prices ONE blocker only - the biggest -
and the footnote under it says the numbers are computed "before gang-blocks". N is how many
creatures can block that attacker, and any two of them may block it TOGETHER. So read N, not
the parenthesis:
  N = 1  -> the parenthesis is the whole story; use it.
  N >= 2 -> a gang block is available and your attacker's toughness is measured against the
            SUM of their bodies, not against the biggest one. Unless the lethal check has
            cleared, the answer for that creature is ATTACK: none.
  THE ENGINE OFTEN PRICES THE GANG FOR YOU, IN THE SAME TAG: "- GANG BLOCK: their 2 biggest
  together deal 5, enough to kill this attacker; each result above is a LONE blocker only".
  When you see that clause, it is the answer - ATTACK: none for that creature below the lethal
  check - and the friendly parenthesis in front of it is the LONE-blocker case only. Do not
  argue with it because they might block with just the big one; they might not, and the clause
  told you what happens when they do not.
Master of the Feast is a 5/5 flier and your fastest clock, but it does not untap while Intruder
Alarm or a similar effect is out (its battlefield line will say so), and three 2/2 tokens gang
it to death for nothing. Do not swing it into an N >= 2 board.
The one time everything goes: the printed opponent life is at or below the total power of every
attacker you are sending - then run the lethal check below, swing with everything, and end it.

=== LETHAL CHECK - RUN IT EVERY TURN ===
Count the punisher names on your battlefield (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Ob Nixilis) - call
that P. FIRST, THE BURST YOU CONTROL: if Ob Nixilis is on your battlefield with 2 or more loyalty
and any creature of theirs is on his -2 list, that -2 is 2 x P damage NOW - at or above their
printed life, take it and stop counting. Then the slow count: your Howling Mines, Dictates and Puzzle Boxes - each Mine or Dictate is 1 extra
opponent card per turn, a Puzzle Box is their hand size. Multiply the extra cards by P, add 1 x P
for their normal draw, and add the power of your unblocked attackers. If that total is at or above
the number printed as the opponent's life, attack with everything including Fate Unraveler and cast
every draw engine in your list this turn. You do not need to survive another turn - they take the
damage on their own draw step.
Worked example: P = 2 (Underworld Dreams and one Fate Unraveler). You control two Howling Mines and
a Dictate of Kruphix, so 3 extra cards. 3 extra + 1 normal = 4 cards; 4 x P = 8 damage on their
draw step. An unblocked Master of the Feast adds 5, for 13. If the opponent's printed life is 13 or
less, swing with everything and hold nothing back.

=== MULLIGAN - AN ODDS COMPARISON, NOT A CHECKLIST ===
The prompt counts the hand for you on its own line - "Your hand (7 cards), counted by the engine:
3 lands, 4 spells" - read that number, never count the hand yourself.
The question is always: is this seven more likely to win than a fresh six? Weigh it on the deck
facts. You run 26 lands, so a third land by turn three and a fourth by turn five are both likely,
and a six you ship is most often a two-lander again. Your punishers cost three to five, so you
need to reach four or five mana on time - that is what the land count is for.
- TWO OR MORE LANDS AND AT LEAST ONE SPELL: KEEP. Not a close call. ALL LANDS: ship - it casts
  nothing.
- ONE LAND ON THE UNTOUCHED SEVEN: ship it. Your cheap plays are Shield Sphere {0} and Fog
  Bank/Howling Mine at two, and none of them advances the plan; everything that wins costs three
  or more, so one land means four or five turns of waiting against a deck that is attacking you,
  and a fresh seven costs you only one card.
- ONE LAND AT THE SECOND LOOK ("(keeping 6)"): now the ship costs TWO cards for the same land
  odds, and the bottom ask lets you shave the six yourself. Keep it when the "would cover the
  cost of:" line names a card or the hand holds a punisher you can reach on the third land
  (Fate Unraveler at four); ship it when the coverage line reads "would not cover any spell in it"
  and no punisher is in the hand. This seat shipped a one-land six holding three Underworld Dreams, a Shield Sphere and a
  Fog Bank, found a three-lander with two punishers at (keeping 5) and won - the ship was a fair
  bet there because none of the six was castable before the third land.
- ONE LAND AT "(keeping 5)" OR LOWER: keep. Each further look is a fresh seven shaved by yet
  another card at the same land odds; the bottom ask builds the hand (lands first, then the
  cheapest spells they cover, punishers over engines).
- ZERO LANDS: ship, always.
Tiebreakers between hands that already clear the land count, never a reason to ship one that
does: prefer a hand holding a punisher or a Fog Bank; prefer a hand whose "Playing every land in
this hand would cover the cost of:" line names at least one card. Colour is a tiebreaker and
never a gate: 18 of your 26 lands make black, 18 make blue, and ten of them make both. A hand
with no cheap play is never a reason to mulligan - this deck contains no one-mana card.
"I will keep whatever I draw next" is the sentence that runs a chain to four cards - the next
seven is no likelier to have lands than this one, and it will be one card smaller. Decide each
look on its own header and the land branches above; a carried plan that says "mulligan for
lands" is about a hand you already threw away.
WHEN YOU KEEP AFTER A MULLIGAN THE GAME ASKS WHICH CARDS TO BOTTOM. Bottom your most EXPENSIVE
spell first: Forced Fruition, Peer into the Abyss, Teferi's Puzzle Box, a second copy of a draw
engine - in that order. KEEP in this order, so any N is buildable: Shield Sphere first (it costs
ZERO mana, free defence on any turn no matter what your lands are doing), then your only
punisher, then lands up to four, then spells by cost - a Sphere or your last punisher goes under
only when nothing below it in the order is left.
LANDS: bottom one when the hand you keep would still hold FOUR OR MORE lands. A five-land,
two-spell keep has a land to spare and the spell is worth more; a four-land keep does not, and
below that a land is never the right card to lose.
CLEANUP DISCARD IS THE SAME ORDER, READ FROM THE END. When your hand is above seven at end of turn
the ask prints your hand as a NUMBERED list ("your hand has N cards and your maximum hand size is
7"; the log calls it "Cleanup discard (hand N, limit 7)") and you answer "PUT: n, m" - the numbers
ONLY, nothing in brackets after them - of the cards you SEND, most expensive first: Forced
Fruition, Peer into the Abyss, Teferi's Puzzle
Box, a second copy of a draw engine already on your battlefield line, then a land when that line
already prints six or more lands. Never Shield Sphere and never your only punisher - they go only
when nothing else is left to send. Read the PUT line back against your own plan sentence before
you send it: a plan that says "cast Underworld Dreams next turn" with its number in the PUT line
is the plan discarding itself.

THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW - TAKE IT INSTEAD OF A SECOND "Cast nothing right now".
Many menus carry a last row reading "Hold priority for the rest of this turn: pass now, and do not
ask me again unless the board changes (any change re-opens this window; you give up no cast)
{taking this row skips the rest of this turn's identical windows}". It is a PASS - the same pass
as "Cast nothing right now" - with one extra effect: it stops the game asking you this identical
list again for the rest of this turn.
THE BRAKE HAS TWO EXEMPTIONS, AND THEY ARE NOT LANE BREAKS. A decline is CORRECT, and this brake
is not what decided it, when the only non-pass row on the menu carries
"[legendary: you already control one ...]" or "[second copy: you already control ...]" and its
{feeds:} clause reads "converters on your battlefield: 0" - a duplicate legend that would die on
arrival, or a second copy of a symmetric card, is not a play in that window whatever the brake
says. WHAT THIS LOOKED LIKE: three own-turn declines this corpus were flagged by the brake's own
key and all three were right - two were a second Ob Nixilis with one already out, one was a
Liliana's Caress at zero converters on turn 4.

THE TEST IS ONE LITERAL. When the brake above has told you to pass AND the prompt carries the line
"[you declined this exact list N times already this turn]", answer the HOLD row, not "Cast nothing
right now". You have already given this list your answer; the row exists to make that answer stick.
When that line is absent - this is the first time you have seen this list this turn - answer
"Cast nothing right now" as usual, because the next window may be a different list.
FORCED FRUITION IS A FEEDER, NOT A CONVERTER. It makes THEM draw four per spell; it deals no
damage by itself. Holding it in hand does not release the brake - only Underworld Dreams, Fate
Unraveler, Ob Nixilis or a Liliana's Caress on your battlefield line converts a card into life
loss. WHAT THIS BROKE: at `vs125` seq 15 this seat cast Dictate of Kruphix on a row reading
"converters on your battlefield: 0" with only Forced Fruition and two Islands in hand - it handed
the opponent an extra card a turn for four turns before a converter arrived.
The row costs you nothing the brake would have let you take: it says so on its own face, and the
engine re-opens the window the moment anything on the board changes - including the moment you
draw the punisher that releases the brake. WHAT THIS COST: one game answered the SAME two-row
Dictate menu "Cast nothing right now" eight to ten times a turn for eleven turns - 42 windows
carrying the declined line, the HOLD row on every one of them, and not one take.

=== DECIDING SITUATIONS ===
- Their battlefield line shows BOTH "Sanguine Bond" and "Exquisite Blood" -> any damage you
  deal them and any life they gain repeats without limit. ATTACK: none, whatever the tags say,
  until one is gone; the punishers still fire on their draw step.
- Your life is 10 or less, your own line reads "of which 0 are creatures" (or fewer than theirs)
  and their line reads "M of them without a restriction against attacking" with M larger: this is
  RULE 0c's BODY TURN. Every creature row you can afford in this window, cheapest per body first
  (Shield Sphere {0} is always affordable), and nothing else - not the punisher, not the walker.
- A "Cast Howling Mine" row carries NO "[second copy:" tag and your battlefield line shows no
  Howling Mine: that is your FIRST Mine. Cast it this ask, even at "converters on your
  battlefield: 0", even when the menu has only two rows.
- Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler or Ob Nixilis is anywhere in your cast list: cast it this ask,
  ahead of everything else (RULE 0c's body turn is the one thing that outranks this). TWO of the three offered in the same ask, and your own battlefield
  line reads "of which 0 are creatures": take Fate Unraveler, the one with a body.
- Two spells are both payable and one is a punisher: the punisher goes first, then spend the rest.
- Teferi's Puzzle Box, Forced Fruition or a Dictate is in the SAME list as a punisher: the
  punisher is number 1 in the cast order and the Box is number 5. Cast the punisher this ask.
  A Puzzle Box with nothing to convert its cards is a gift, however big the number looks.
- A "Land drop:" question offers a land: play it. Every turn, no exceptions, whatever you plan
  to cast afterwards.
- An attacker's A-line prints NO "their untapped blockers" tag at all: send it - nothing they
  control can block it.
- An attacker's tag reads only "(you kill it, your attacker lives)" - EVERY name on it, with no
  "(blocking trigger:" and no "(lifelink:" anywhere in it: send that attacker.
- The prompt prints "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs": send only attackers
  whose tag prints no "(blocking trigger:" and no "(lifelink:". If none qualifies, ATTACK: none
  - the punishers still fire on their draw step.
- You control exactly ONE untapped creature and your life is 8 or less: it stays home.
- ONE blocker, several attackers, and taking the free kill would leave your life at 8 or less:
  block the biggest "deals N" instead, whatever its parenthesis says.
- An attacker's tag lists several blockers and ONE of them reads "your attacker dies, their
  blocker lives": ATTACK: none for that creature. They pick the blocker and they pick that one.
- An attacker's tag reads "they have N untapped creatures that could block this one" with N of
  2 or more, or prints a "GANG BLOCK: ... enough to kill this attacker" clause: ATTACK: none for
  that creature unless the lethal check has cleared.
- The block header says the attack IS LETHAL: add up every "deals N", assign your blockers to the
  biggest ones until what still gets through is below your life, and among the assignments that
  survive take the one leaving you the MOST life - then use the numbered rules for what is left.
- The engine row prints "converters on your battlefield: 0" and a Howling Mine is already on
  your line: cast a wall or a Liliana's Caress - or "Cast nothing right now".
- That row is one of only TWO on the ask and the other is "Cast nothing right now": take
  "Cast nothing right now". A menu with no good spell on it is not a menu that forces the bad
  one; this seat took the second Mine at K of 0 twice from exactly that two-row ask.
- No Mine on your line yet: the first Mine is cast at 0. A row whose K names only Liliana's
  Caress counts with their hand at 7 or more: the extra cards are discards, and discards are
  damage.
- Ob Nixilis's -2 is offered, any creature of theirs is on its list, and 2 x P (your punisher
  count) is at or above their printed life: take it - they draw two, and the game ends.
- The engine row prints "converters on your battlefield:" 1 or more (a lone Caress only with
  their hand at 7 or more): cast every Howling Mine, Dictate, Puzzle Box and Forced Fruition
  you are offered, every turn.
- "Cast Shield Sphere {0}" is in the list: take it.
- The opponent controls any creature and "Cast Fog Bank" is in the list: take it.
- A block line reads "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" or "(neither dies)": take that block.
- Peer into the Abyss resolves and asks target opponent or target controller: answer "target
  opponent".
- Ob Nixilis's -2 offers only your own creature as a target: pass.
- Ob Nixilis's -2 lists a token AND a creature whose text makes tokens: destroy the maker.
- The row prints "converters on your battlefield: 0", a Mine is already out, and your plan says
  "stack draw engines" or "for board presence": the plan is wrong - cast the wall or nothing. Master of the Feast goes only at
  "of which 1" or more creatures on THEIR line.
- The lethal check clears: attack with everything, Fate Unraveler included.
