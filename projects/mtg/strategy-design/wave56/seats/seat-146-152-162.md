**Mechanism, code-verified.** The clause lane D left in place, `restriction{can play land}`, is a
**no-op string**. `AbilityFactory::parseRestriction` (`src/MTGAbility.cpp:1069-1099`) recognises
only `myturnonly`, `opponentturnonly`, `assorcery` and the `<my|opponent><phase>only` family; every
other text returns `ActivatedAbility::NO_RESTRICTION`, and `parseCastRestrictions` (`:106-136`) then
gates nothing. `assorcery` is the token that enforces both `player == observer->currentPlayer` and
`cPhase == MTG_PHASE_FIRSTMAIN || MTG_PHASE_SECONDMAIN` - i.e. exactly the missing rule.
**E-2** below. I did NOT guide around this: the bug is a gain, teaching the pilot to exploit it
would be a rule that dies with the fix, and deck152's edit teaches the rules-correct main-phase
line instead.
