# deck152 -> strategy-writing skill proposals (wave 56)

## S5 (STRONG) - when a render gains a row, every guide rule that taught the OLD ROUTE to the same
outcome becomes a decision tax, and the reviewer must go looking for it by ROUTE, not by defect.
EVIDENCE. deck152's guide taught a two-step recipe ("take Flip Side FIRST - once, then Play Land")
because that was the only way to reach a Pathway's other face. D8 added a direct row. Nothing about
the old recipe was FALSE afterwards - the toggle still works - so a truth-audit would have kept it;
it was simply one decision more expensive than the row beside it. `Flip Side` was rendered 109
times and taken 0 times corpus-wide, i.e. the pilot had already stopped following its own guide.
PROPOSE: add to the reviewer instrument a **zero-take audit of every literal the guide NAMES**. A
guide-named row with a corpus take count of 0 is either a rule the pilot is ignoring (a guide
problem) or a route the render has superseded (a guide problem of the other kind). Both need an
edit; neither shows up in a prediction table, because predictions are written about breaks.

## S6 (MED) - the anti-anchor clause needs the losing row named, not just the winning one.
EVIDENCE. deck152's HOLD block named the row to TAKE and the literal that triggers it, and the take
rate went 4.5% -> 16.8% - but at the declined-note N>=2 it is 2 of 35, and **32 of the 33 misses
answered one specific competing row**, `Cast nothing right now`, which sits two positions above
HOLD. deck146, whose block is otherwise identical, is at 5 of 20; the difference is not the rule.
PROPOSE: an anti-anchor clause states BOTH sides - "X is the answer here" and "Y is the answer for
<the other case>, and this is not that case". A rule that only promotes its own row loses to menu
position; a rule that also demotes the specific row the pilot is reaching for does not.

## S7 (MED) - `WHAT THIS COST` entries should be re-dated, not accumulated.
EVIDENCE. deck152's mulligan section carries two `WHAT THIS COST` paragraphs marked "a fourth
corpus running" and "in two corpora running" for a break that has now not recurred for two corpora
(P5 passed in wave 55 and had zero windows to fail in wave 56 - 6 keeps, 0 mulligans, 0 colour-
family renders). They are ~700 bytes of a 65 KB budget spent on a closed lane.
PROPOSE: a `WHAT THIS COST` entry carries the corpus it was measured in, and the skill states that
an entry whose lane has passed for two consecutive corpora is DELETED (the rule stays, its
cautionary tale goes). I did not delete these two this wave - the lane had no windows in wave 56,
which is UNTESTED rather than passed, and I would not spend the deck's only untested lane on a byte
saving. Flagged for synthesis to rule on.
