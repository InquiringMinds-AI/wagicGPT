Deck identity: MONO-GREEN ENCHANTRESS. Your enchantments are a DRAW ENGINE and your auras are
PUMP - none of them deal damage or destroy anything. You win one of two ways: (A) VOLTRON -
stack pump auras onto ONE creature, give it TRAMPLE, and swing for a huge unblockable-through
hit; or (B) GO WIDE - Fists-of-Ironwood Saproling tokens grown by Gaea's Anthem + Primal Rage.
You usually kill by turn 8-13. Your mana is trivial - EVERY spell is green off your Forests
(plus Birds/Druid dorks). There is never a mana problem to agonize over; if a Cast option is
listed, it is payable now.

THE #1 RULE - YOUR CARDS DRAW AND PUMP, THEY DO NOT KILL. NEVER cast an aura "to kill" or "to
trade with" an opponent's creature - they only make bodies bigger. What each card ACTUALLY does:
- Argothian Enchantress ({1}{g} 0/1, SHROUD): whenever you cast an enchantment spell, DRAW A
  CARD. This is your engine. She is the most important card in the deck - cast her EARLY, and cast
  her BEFORE you pour out your enchantments, so every enchantment after her refills your hand.
- Yavimaya Enchantress ({2}{g} 2/2): gets +1/+1 for EACH enchantment on the battlefield (yours
  AND the opponent's). In this deck she becomes a 5/5, 6/6, 7/7 fast - a real beater. Pump her.
- Web ({g} AURA): enchanted creature gets +0/+2 AND REACH (can now block flyers). It adds NO
  power - it is a draw-trigger and a reach-granter, not a way to grow your clock. Cheap.
- Fists of Ironwood ({1}{g} AURA): when it enters, make TWO 1/1 green Saproling tokens; the
  enchanted creature gains TRAMPLE (no stat boost). The two tokens are the real payoff.
- Blanchwood Armor ({2}{g} AURA): +1/+1 for EACH Forest you control. You run ~26 Forests, so this
  is routinely +5/+5 or more. Your best single pump.
- Ancestral Mask ({2}{g} AURA): +2/+2 for each OTHER enchantment on the battlefield. In a deck
  full of enchantments this is enormous (it made a 0/1 Birds into a 9/10 in a game you won).
- Primal Rage ({1}{g} ENCHANTMENT, no target): ALL your creatures have TRAMPLE. Global. A SECOND
  copy does nothing - if Primal Rage is already on your battlefield, do not agonize over a copy in
  hand; it is redundant. Cast a real threat instead.
- Gaea's Anthem ({1}{g}{g} ENCHANTMENT, no target): ALL your creatures get +1/+1. Global anthem.
- Briar Patch ({1}{g}{g} ENCHANTMENT, no target): every creature that attacks YOU gets -1/-0.
  Defensive - it shrinks the opponent's attackers, it does not help you attack.
- Birds of Paradise ({g} 0/1 flying): mana dork, taps for any color. Druid of the Cowl ({1}{g}
  1/3): mana dork for {g}. Canopy Spider ({1}{g} 1/3, REACH): a blocker that can block flyers.

SEQUENCE THE ENGINE - CAST ARGOTHIAN ENCHANTRESS FIRST, THEN DUMP ENCHANTMENTS:
- The turn you can, cast Argothian Enchantress. AFTER she is down, every enchantment you cast
  (Web, Fists, Primal Rage, Gaea's Anthem, Blanchwood, Ancestral Mask, Briar Patch) draws you a
  card. Casting your cheap enchantments BEFORE her throws away free cards - do not do it.
- She has SHROUD: she cannot be targeted, so you can NEVER put your own auras on her, and the
  opponent cannot remove her. Protect the engine - do not attack with her (she is a 0/1).

AURA TARGETING - ENCHANT YOUR OWN CREATURE:
- Web, Fists, Blanchwood Armor and Ancestral Mask must target a creature. When the option is
  labeled "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN," just pick the creature you want to grow. When it
  also lists the opponent's creatures, IGNORE them - enchanting the opponent gives THEM the pump.
- If your ONLY legal target is an opponent's creature (you have no creature of your own yet), do
  NOT cast the aura. Deploy a body first - a mana dork, Canopy Spider, Yavimaya, or make Saprolings
  with Fists. Best target for a big pump: Yavimaya Enchantress or a Saproling you intend to swing.
- Pick a target that carries POWER. Blanchwood/Ancestral/Gaea's add power; Web does not. Stacking
  auras on a 0-power body (Birds, Argothian) mostly buys evasion or toughness, not a clock - if you
  need to close, grow a creature that already has power (Yavimaya, a Saproling) or one that flies
  and can carry a real power boost.

YOUR WIN CONDITION - MAKE ONE BIG TRAMPLER AND ATTACK:
- Pick one creature (Yavimaya, a Saproling, or a pumped dork), stack Blanchwood Armor / Ancestral
  Mask / Gaea's Anthem on it, and make sure it has TRAMPLE (Primal Rage or Fists). A single 7/7+
  trampler ends games - trample means it deals its excess through any blocker to the face.
- When you have a large trampler and the opponent cannot block it profitably, that is LETHAL math -
  do not re-derive the aura stack. The battlefield line ALREADY shows the final boosted number -
  e.g. "Saproling (6/6) (printed 1/1)" means it IS a 6/6 right now, Forests and auras already
  counted. TRUST that printed [X/Y] and SWING; do NOT recount Forests or re-add anthems/auras.

YOU CANNOT ALWAYS WIN THIS TURN - DO NOT HUNT LETHAL THAT ISN'T THERE (AHEAD OR BEHIND):
- Some turns your board simply cannot deal enough THIS turn - and this is just as true when you
  are AHEAD with a big board as when you are behind. If your attackers add up to (say) 6 and the
  opponent is at 13, the exact lethal is not there this turn - you are NOT missing a trick. Do NOT
  re-read the board over and over hunting for a combo or an extra 7 damage you do not have. Make
  the best single play (add a body, pump ONE threat, draw off Argothian, block to survive) and
  pass. You are a turn-8-13 deck: winning over TWO turns is the normal plan, not a failure. Spinning
  on lethal-this-turn wastes the turn and can even lose the reply to a run-on.
- Being behind and at low life is normal against faster decks. The plan is: BLOCK to stay alive,
  keep drawing off Argothian, grow ONE real threat, and win a turn or two later. You do not have to
  find lethal this exact turn - you have to not die and keep building.

ATTACK EVERY TURN - DURDLING LOSES THIS DECK:
- Your losses were fast decks racing you while your board sat home under auras. Every turn, attack
  with every creature that has real power and is not needed to block.
- The "creatures that can attack" list (A1, A2, ...) is the EXACT and ONLY eligible set. You may
  declare ONLY creatures printed on that list, by their A-number or exact name. A creature you WISH
  could attack is not eligible just because you want the damage - if it is not on the list it
  CANNOT attack this turn (almost always summoning-sick because you made it this turn, or tapped).
  This holds EVEN when you feel you must attack to win: naming a creature that is absent from the
  list does not add it to combat, it throws the whole attack away. If the list is empty or holds
  only your 0-power engine/dorks, answer exactly ATTACK: none.
- Do NOT attack with 0-power engine/dork creatures: Argothian Enchantress (0/1) and un-pumped
  Birds of Paradise (0/1) deal 0 - keep them home. Your Saprolings, Yavimaya, and pumped creatures
  are your attackers.
- "No attackers" is correct ONLY when every creature with power is tapped/summoning-sick or is your
  sole blocker against lethal. Being at low life is not a reason to stop attacking if you can race.

SURVIVING AGGRO AND FLIERS WHILE THE ENGINE SETS UP:
- Against faster decks (fliers, aggro) do not just race - stabilize. Canopy Spider (reach) and any
  creature wearing Web (reach) can BLOCK FLYERS. Briar Patch shrinks every attacker against you.
  Block to survive, keep drawing off Argothian, and out-card them into your big trampler.
- Protect the engine on DEFENSE too. The block prompt tells you the total incoming damage and
  whether it is lethal. If it says NOT lethal and you are ahead or comfortable, TAKE the damage -
  do NOT chump-block with Argothian Enchantress or an un-pumped Birds (your 0/1 engine/dorks) just
  to absorb a few points. The prompt marks them "deals 0 - only absorbs damage": a 0/1 that keeps
  drawing you cards is worth far more than soaking 1-2 life you can afford. Chump the engine ONLY to
  stop ACTUAL lethal.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands (Forest / a dork counts) with at least one green spell. An opener with
Argothian Enchantress or a mana dork plus a Forest is ideal. Ship 0-1 or 6+ land hands, or an
all-expensive hand with no early play. Decide KEEP or MULLIGAN from what the hand HAS (mana +
a green spell) - do not plan out future turns to answer it.
