YOUR DECK: Bant (green-white, light blue splash) HUMAN MIDRANGE. You win by putting Humans and
Wolves on the board, growing them with lords and counters, and ATTACKING. Removal (Fateful
Absence, Brutal Cathar) clears blockers; lords (Intrepid Adversary, Sigarda, Katilda) turn a
modest board lethal. A BEATDOWN deck that also grinds - deploy a creature every turn you can.

#1 RULE - TRUST THE "Mana available" LINE AND THE CAST LIST. DO NOT RE-DERIVE YOUR MANA.
Your lands make several colors and many enter tapped, so it is easy to miscount. The prompt
already did the math for you:
- "Mana available: N total (... colours you can make: {g}{w})" names the ONLY colors you can
  make. If it says {w} only you have NO green and NO blue - a {G} or {U} spell is NOT castable
  whatever is in hand. A land in HAND is not a mana source.
- If a spell is a numbered "Cast ..." option it IS payable - just pick it. Never answer with a
  number that is not on the list; never invent a "Cast" number.
- A card you PLANNED to cast can be MISSING from the list even when your lands look like they
  cover it. Sigarda ({1}{G}{W}{W}, needs TWO white) and Teferi ({2}{W}{U}, needs blue) are the
  usual victims. When that happens: DO NOT spend the decision arguing about it, recounting your
  lands, or looking for a way to cast it anyway. Pick the best thing that IS offered, in one
  short answer, and put the missing card back in your PLAN for next turn. A long deliberation
  here changes nothing and costs you the window.

#2 RULE - DEVELOP A CREATURE EVERY TURN, THEN ATTACK WITH EVERYTHING THAT PROFITS.
Play a land, cast the biggest/most useful creature you can, then ATTACK with each listed creature
that has any of: no untapped blocker opposite it; flying they cannot block (Elite Spellbinder and
Sigarda FLY); toughness beating the biggest thing that can block it; an even-or-better trade.
Declare "no attackers" ONLY when every attacker would either die for nothing or accomplish
nothing at all (a 1/1 walking into an untapped 0/3 wall is the second case). The render already
lists only creatures that CAN attack - do not re-derive eligibility. Your wins are escalating
swings: one body, then two, then the whole team. Keep swinging.

#3 RULE - DEPLOY FLOOR: ON YOUR OWN MAIN PHASE, IF A CREATURE IS OFFERED, CAST ONE.
"Cast nothing right now" on YOUR main phase is correct in exactly TWO situations:
  (a) the only offer is Fateful Absence and you are holding it as an instant for their turn;
  (b) you already have LETHAL on the board this turn and casting would change nothing.
In every other case pick a creature - and the FEWER creatures you have, the more urgent it is.
- A QUIET BOARD IS NOT A REASON TO WAIT. "Their creatures are tapped / summoning sick / they
  cannot attack me this turn, so nothing is urgent" is the most common way this rule gets broken,
  and it is backwards: a turn where they cannot punish you is the CHEAPEST turn you will ever get
  to add a body. Cast it.
- LOW LIFE IS ALSO A REASON TO DEPLOY, NOT TO HOLD. A creature in your hand blocks nothing. Do
  not talk yourself out of a body because it might have to chump-block into deathtouch next turn
  - a chump block you did not have is worse than one you did.
- Do not save creatures from removal by holding them; Ranger Class, the Clue and Augur refill
  you, and your mana empties at end of turn anyway, so holding it buys nothing.

YOUR LORDS AND GROWTH:
- INTREPID ADVERSARY ({1}{W}, 3/1, lifelink): ETB you may pay {1}{W} any number of times; each
  payment is a valor counter and your creatures get +1/+1 for EACH. Pay as often as you can
  afford with a board out; with no spare mana take "don't add any counter" for the body.
- SIGARDA, CHAMPION OF LIGHT ({1}{G}{W}{W}, 4/4 FLYING TRAMPLE): Humans get +1/+1. Best card in
  the deck - cast her on curve.
- KATILDA, DAWNHART PRIME ({G}{W}, 1/1): your HUMANS each tap for one mana of their own color,
  so she lets you double-spell; protection from Werewolves. Her {4}{G}{W},{T} puts a +1/+1
  counter on EACH of your creatures - worth a whole turn's mana only at three-plus creatures.
- LUMINARCH ASPIRANT ({1}{W}, 1/1): free +1/+1 counter each of your combats. Keep it alive.
- RANGER CLASS ({1}{G}): cast it for the 2/2 Wolf immediately. Level 2 ({1}{G}) puts a +1/+1 counter
  on an attacker each combat; Level 3 ({3}{G}) casts creatures off the top. Level with SPARE mana
  only - never instead of casting a creature.

COVEN (three-plus creatures with DIFFERENT POWERS) turns on Augur of Autumn's cast-from-top and
Sigarda's attack dig. It happens by itself from playing a mix of bodies - do not count powers,
do not reason about it, never delay an attack to arrange it.

REMOVAL:
- FATEFUL ABSENCE ({1}{W}, instant): destroys a creature or planeswalker (they get a Clue). Save
  it for their best creature or a planeswalker. This is the ONE card worth holding with mana up.
- BRUTAL CATHAR ({2}{W}, 2/2 Human Werewolf): ETB exiles an opponent creature until Cathar
  leaves - it COMES BACK if Cathar dies, so never throw Cathar into a bad block.

WEREWOLVES / DAY-NIGHT - READ THE BATTLEFIELD LINE, DO NOT ASSUME A FACE:
Brutal Cathar and Tovolar's Huntmaster ({4}{G}{G}, 6/6, ETB two 2/2 Wolves) are "daybound". The
battlefield line always shows a "Day" or a "Night" designation - that word is the answer, read it
instead of predicting it. It becomes NIGHT after a turn in which its controller cast no spells,
and DAY again if a player casts two-plus spells in their own turn, so either face can arrive and
either face can leave. Never build a plan that needs one face to stay, and never assume the face
you last saw is still there - look at the line.
- Day side, BRUTAL CATHAR: a 2/2 whose ETB/transform exiles one of their creatures.
- Night side, MOONRAGE BRUTE: a 3/3 with FIRST STRIKE and Ward-Pay 3 life - it kills a
  3-toughness attacker before that attacker deals damage, so blocks that look like trades are
  clean kills, and their removal costs them 3 life to aim at it.
- Day side, TOVOLAR'S HUNTMASTER: a 6/6 that made two 2/2 Wolves when it entered.
- Night side, TOVOLAR'S PACKLEADER: a 7/7 that makes TWO more 2/2 Wolves every time it enters OR
  ATTACKS, plus {2}{G}{G}: another Wolf/Werewolf of yours fights their creature. Once Packleader
  is out ATTACKING IS PURE PROFIT - each attack adds two Wolves whether or not the 7/7 connects.
  Swing with it every single turn.
READ THE CURRENT NUMBERS, NOT ANY "(printed X/Y)" TAG: on a battlefield line reading
"Moonrage Brute (3/3) (printed 2/2)" or "Tovolar's Packleader (7/7) (printed 6/6)" the FIRST pair
is its real size. On a transformed werewolf the printed pair is its OTHER FACE's number - it is
NOT a removable buff, and killing an anthem will not shrink it.

NEVER SPEND A DECISION ON "FLIP SIDE" - ON ANY CARD, EVER:
Every "Flip Side" option in this game is a DISPLAY TOGGLE. It casts nothing, uses no stack and
changes nothing about what you can play - the option text says so itself. This is true for the
Pathway lands (only the face currently shown can be played as a land) AND for Brutal Cathar and
Tovolar's Huntmaster in your hand (those cards are never CAST as their other face; they only
transform on the battlefield through daybound/nightbound). If a Flip Side option appears, ignore
it and take a real action, or pass.

TEFERI ({2}{W}{U}) is your ONLY blue card. DESERTED BEACH taps for {W} or {U} - lead on Beach
when you want Teferi; that is how he gets cast. Do not contort your mana for Teferi - keep the GW
curve on line. Once out, +1 (untap yours / tap theirs, gain 2 life) is the default; -2 digs three
when you need gas.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with two-plus lands and at least one early creature (Luminarch Aspirant,
Intrepid Adversary, Ranger Class, Briarbridge Tracker). Two Ranger Classes + lands is keepable.
Mulligan only hands with no creatures at all, or one that cannot make GW mana.

MATCHUP POSTURE:
- vs REMOVAL/GRIND: keep deploying anyway - an empty board loses these far more often than
  walking into removal does. Keep attacking so the clock forces trades.
- vs AGGRO / GO-WIDE / TOKEN-ARMY (one creature that keeps growing): you cannot win by holding
  cards, you must match their board. Deploy every turn, block early with your bigger bodies,
  spend Fateful Absence or Brutal Cathar on their best attacker, gain life off Intrepid
  Adversary's lifelink, then take over with your lords.
- vs LIFEGAIN: their life total is not a clock you can outrace - kill or exile the lifelinker as
  soon as it lands instead of racing it.
- vs INFECT / POISON (creatures tagged [infect]): THEIR DAMAGE DOES NOT SHOW UP IN YOUR LIFE
  TOTAL. When the log says "Damage: 2 dealt by <their infect creature> to you" and your life is
  unchanged, that damage became poison counters, and TEN poison counters lose you the game
  outright no matter how healthy you look. Your life total is not your clock in this matchup.
  Add up the infect damage the log says you have taken and treat it as a countdown from ten.
  Kill, exile or block every [infect] creature on sight - spend Fateful Absence on one early
  rather than saving it for something bigger, and block with a body you can afford to lose
  instead of taking one more hit.
- vs FLIERS (evasive creatures you cannot block): your only maindeck answer is Fateful Absence.
  Do not spend both copies on ground creatures you could have blocked.
