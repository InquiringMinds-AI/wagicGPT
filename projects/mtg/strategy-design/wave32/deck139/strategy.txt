YOUR DECK: Simic-green RAMP + MUTATE midrange. You ramp with cheap creatures and land-fetchers,
then MUTATE a big "Apex" onto a creature you already control to fire a game-swinging trigger and
build one huge threat. You are the CONTROL against aggro (put bodies down, survive, then take over
with a mutated bomb) and the BEATDOWN against slow decks. Re-check every turn off the board: if
your life is falling while theirs is not, your first job is to STOP DYING - deploy bodies. In BOTH
roles you cast a creature every turn.

MULLIGAN - YOUR BIGGEST LEAK. READ THE HAND LINE; DO NOT REASON ABOUT MANA.
Count LANDS by the printed tag: every card whose line contains "(land:" IS a land. Count GREEN
SOURCES the same way: every land whose braces contain {G} IS a green source. All five of these
contain {G} and all of them count: Forest, Stomping Ground (land: taps for {R}{G}), Temple Garden
(land: taps for {W}{G}), Overgrown Tomb (land: taps for {B}{G}), Breeding Pool (land: taps for
{U}{G}).
  ANSWER "Keep this hand" when BOTH are true: (1) 2 or more cards contain "(land:" and at least
  one of those contains {G}; AND (2) at least one of Arboreal Grazer, Pollywog Symbiote, Dryad of
  the Ilysian Grove, Migratory Greathorn, Gemrazer, Beanstalk Giant // Fertile Footsteps.
  ANSWER "Mulligan" only when you count 0 or 1 lands, or 6-7 lands, or nothing from (2).
THE FLOOR IS THE NUMBER PRINTED IN FRONT OF YOU. The prompt says "having already taken N
mulligans". If that sentence appears at all, this is your LAST look: answer "Keep this hand".
There is no second mulligan. Writing "I will keep whatever I draw next" is how you end up with a
2-card hand - keep THIS one instead.
The thought "this hand has zero lands" is ALWAYS FALSE when any card shows "(land:".
The thought "this hand has zero green sources" is ALWAYS FALSE when any land shows {G}.
The thought "Stomping Ground / Temple Garden / Overgrown Tomb / Breeding Pool cannot make green
because I must pay 2 life" is ALWAYS FALSE: the 2 life is an enters-the-battlefield choice about
tapped-or-untapped, and the land taps for both its colours either way.
Lands in your HAND are mana - you play one every turn.
When asked to put cards on the BOTTOM, name exactly as many as the line asks for, worst first
(spare duplicate lands, then the most expensive Apex you cannot cast). Write the PUT: line and
STOP - the PUT: line is what happens, so do not re-argue it below it.

DEPLOY CHOICE WHEN BEHIND - THE BODY, NOT THE ENGINE.
When two or more "Cast" lines are offered AND your life is 10 or less AND the opponent shows more
creatures than you: cast the offered creature with the HIGHEST TOUGHNESS, preferring one whose
line shows [reach] or [flying] if any opposing attacker shows [flying]. Pollywog Symbiote (1/3) is
your discount ENGINE, not your stabilizer - at 10 or less life and behind on board, "Cast Gemrazer
(4/4)" beats "Cast Pollywog Symbiote (1/3)" every time, even though Pollywog makes your next
mutate cheaper. A discount you do not live to use is worth nothing. Above 10 life, or when you are
even or ahead on creatures, cast Pollywog first - that is when the engine is right.

#1 RULE - THE APEX BOMBS ARE PAYOFFS ONLY WHEN YOU MUTATE THEM. Snapdax, Illuna, Nethroi, Vadrok,
Brokkos, Everquill Phoenix, Gemrazer and Migratory Greathorn all say "Whenever this creature
MUTATES, <do something>." A HARDCAST gives you ONLY a body and its keywords - the trigger does
NOTHING. So when a non-Human creature is on your battlefield, take the mutate line.
- CHECK THE LINE YOU ARE ABOUT TO TAKE. A mutate line always contains the words "with its mutate
  cost". "Cast Illuna, Apex of Wishes {2}{g}{u}{r}" is a HARDCAST; "Cast Everquill Phoenix with
  its mutate cost {3}{r}" is the mutate. Before you answer, re-read your chosen option and confirm
  the words "mutate cost" are in it if you meant to mutate.
- IF NO LINE FOR THAT CARD SAYS "with its mutate cost", THE MUTATE IS NOT AVAILABLE THIS TURN
  (usually a missing coloured pip - Snapdax needs {W}{W}, Illuna needs {U}{U}). Then either
  hardcast it because you need the body, or cast something else and mutate later. Say which one
  you are doing; do not write "I will mutate it" over a hardcast line.
- ALWAYS ANSWER "mutate over". Putting the Apex on top is how you get its P/T, its evasion and its
  trigger. Never answer "mutate under" with an Apex.
- GEMRAZER (mutate {1}{G}{G}, 4/4 reach trample): destroys an opponent's artifact or enchantment.
  MIGRATORY GREATHORN (mutate {2}{G}, 3/4): fetches a basic land - on mutate ONLY, never on a
  normal cast. These two are your cheapest and most reliable mutates; green is your deepest
  colour, so take them early and often. Both are worth mutating even with no trigger target - the
  merged body is the point.
- SNAPDAX (mutate {2}{BR}{W}{W}, 3/5 double strike): 4 damage to an opponent's creature or
  planeswalker AND gain 4 life. Your best anti-aggro play; the 4 life happens even with nothing
  to shoot.
- ILLUNA (mutate {3}{RG}{U}{U}, 6/6 flying trample): digs until it hits a nonland permanent and
  puts it onto the battlefield or into hand.
- NETHROI (mutate {4}{GW}{B}{B}, 5/5 deathtouch lifelink): returns creatures from your graveyard
  to the battlefield - read the target list you are given and take everything it lets you take.
- BROKKOS (mutate {2}{UB}{G}{G}, 6/6 trample): may be cast FROM YOUR GRAVEYARD using its mutate.
- VADROK (mutate {1}{WU}{R}{R}, 3/3 flying first strike) and EVERQUILL PHOENIX (mutate {3}{r}, 4/4
  flying): mutate these for the BODY. Vadrok's trigger wants a noncreature card of mana value 3 or
  less in your graveyard and this deck has none; Everquill's makes a small artifact, not a
  creature.

#2 RULE - UNDER AGGRO, A FRESH BODY BEATS A MUTATE. Mutating grows one creature but adds NO new
body; hardcasting adds a blocker. When you are being attacked and need more blockers, CAST A
CREATURE NORMALLY. Save mutate for when your life is safe, or when the trigger (Snapdax's
4-damage-and-gain-4 especially) is itself the stabilizer.

CAST FLOOR: every one of your main phases, if a "Cast <something>" line is offered, TAKE ONE.
Every listed line is already payable - the thought "I have no untapped lands left to pay" is
ALWAYS FALSE when a Cast line is listed. "Cast nothing right now" is the answer ONLY when the sole
options are Apexes you cannot mutate and do not need as bodies.

RAMP FIRST, THEN THE BOMBS:
- ARBOREAL GRAZER ({G}, 0/3 reach): on enter, put a LAND FROM YOUR HAND onto the battlefield
  tapped - ramp, a wall, and a fine mutate host. Play it turn 1 when you can.
- DRYAD OF THE ILYSIAN GROVE ({2}{G}, 2/4): extra land drop each turn, and all your lands tap for
  every colour. Dryad is what turns Snapdax's {W}{W} and Illuna's {U}{U} from impossible into
  castable - cast her on curve every time and keep her as a host.
- BEANSTALK GIANT // FERTILE FOOTSTEPS: cast Fertile Footsteps {2}{G} to fetch a basic; the Giant
  is */* = your land count. NISSA WHO SHAKES THE WORLD: your Forests make double green - and with
  Dryad out every land is a Forest - and +1 turns a land into a 3/3 haste-vigilance body.

READING THE MUTATE FLOW (follow the labels in order, one answer each):
1. Take "Cast <card> with its mutate cost {..}".
2. "1. Cast Card Normally / 2. mutate [cast for the MUTATE cost: merge onto one of your non-Human
   creatures ...] / 3. Decline" -> answer "mutate". "Cast Card Normally" here throws it away.
3. With POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE on your battlefield you are then asked "DISCARD ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS"
   listing your HAND -> pitch a spare land, never a creature you want. Two Pollywogs ask twice.
   (Pollywog makes every mutate spell cost {1} less, and the discount keeps working even while
   Pollywog is the UNDER card of a merged pile - a strong reason to mutate onto it once stable.)
4. "mutate over / mutate under" -> "mutate over".
5. "TARGET CHOICE for <card>" listing YOUR BATTLEFIELD -> pick the host whose abilities you want
   (Dryad's ramp, Pollywog's discount) over a vanilla 0/3.
Afterwards the pile renders as ONE line tagged "[mutated pile - ONE merged creature ...]" - it is
one creature with every card's abilities; count it as one.

ATTACK FLOOR - run this on each listed attacker, one line at a time: send it if its POWER is 1 or
more AND any of: the opponent has no untapped creature that can block it / it survives every block
shown / it trades up or kills a blocker. Then answer and stop.
- Any attacker whose line reads "[deals 0 - this attack deals no damage to the opponent]" NEVER
  attacks. That tag is printed for you; read it and skip that line.
- "no attackers" is the answer ONLY when every listed attacker carries that tag or dies for zero
  gain. Otherwise declare EVERY attacker that passes the test, in one reply.

BLOCK FLOOR: block when the line says "you kill it, your blocker lives", when the block is free
("neither dies"), or when the damage would otherwise put you below 6 life. Read the parenthetical
the render gives you and believe it - including "[summoning sick ... but CAN block]", which means
that creature blocks normally.
- POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE STAYS BACK OFF BLOCKS unless the block stops lethal - it dies to almost
  everything and it is your discount engine.
- A blocker facing deathtouch or first strike dies without killing anything - take that block only
  to stop lethal damage.
- Only a creature whose line shows [reach] or [flying] can block an attacker tagged [flying]. Your
  Arboreal Grazers and Gemrazer have reach; Pollywog and Dryad do not.
- An attacker tagged [infect] does NOT reduce your life - it gives you poison counters, and 10
  poison counters lose you the game at any life total. Your poison count is not printed anywhere,
  so against an [infect] board block the infect attackers FIRST, even while your life looks safe.

DECIDE FAST - ON EVERY DECISION. Write your CHOICE / ATTACK / BLOCKS / PUT line, two or three
sentences of reason, and STOP. Re-reading the board a second and third time inside one answer does
not change the answer and it burns the clock; the mulligan and bottoming asks are where this costs
you most. Double strike (Snapdax) and trample (Gemrazer/Illuna/Nethroi/Brokkos) mean a blocked
trampler assigns lethal to the blocker and the rest to the player; a blocked creature WITHOUT
trample deals nothing to the player.
