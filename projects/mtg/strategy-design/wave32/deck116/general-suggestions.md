### Why PASS, with the evidence that could have argued otherwise

Three candidate core edits presented themselves at this seat. Each is routed elsewhere, and the
routing reason matters more than the verdict:

**1. "Deploy a body before you cast a search spell."** This is the seat's #1 leak (6 declines of
an offered creature/enabler across 24 cast windows, 5 of them in losses). It reads like a general
truth and is therefore tempting as a core line. **Rejected:** it is archetype-specific and would be
actively wrong at a control seat — deck134's whole identity is declining bodies, and deck146/158
hold removal for a reason. The core already carries the neutral framing; the deck-specific
"prefer the body over the tutor" belongs in the guide, where it is now rule #5. This is the
Step-0-bis rule-4 COUNTERMAND shape (a per-deck reversal of a generic reflex), which the skill
already names as the sanctioned, cheaper route.

**2. "A card that is put onto the battlefield is not CAST; cast triggers do not fire."** The pilot
stated the opposite belief explicitly (`vs139 s12`: *"As soon as he enters, his 'When you cast'
trigger resolves, drawing four cards"* — the engine correctly fired nothing). This is a genuine
rules misconception, and the cheat-into-play / reanimate / "put onto the battlefield" family is
broad enough that a core sentence is imaginable. **Rejected as PREMATURE, single-family:** this is
the first deck in the campaign whose identity is put-onto-the-battlefield, so the observation has
exactly one witness family. Per the skill's standing rule (HL8 lineage: *do NOT mint the core
sentence off one more witness — promote on cross-FAMILY agreement*), it goes in the guide as
rule #3 and is tagged in `skill.md` (S-116c) as a promotion candidate. Promote if a reanimator,
a Show-and-Tell shell, or any second cast-trigger-gated deck rotates in and reproduces it.

**3. "Do not trade away the permanent your plan depends on."** deck116 chump-blocked its own
enabler to death twice; deck148 (wave-31) traded off its ungeared lord; deck122 attacked away its
blockers. Three seats, three archetypes, one shape — which looks like core material.
**Rejected, and this is the interesting one:** wave-31 HL3 already ruled on it and routed it to a
GUIDE rung (PAYOFF-PERMANENT PRESERVATION as a named teach category, placed IN the blocking-seam
block). The core cannot know WHICH permanent is the payoff; a core sentence would have to say
"protect your important permanents," which is a JUDGMENT surface the model already applies badly
and would not change a single number. The per-deck naming is the whole content. **Do not promote —
this is a case where cross-seat convergence is real and the core is still the wrong layer.**
