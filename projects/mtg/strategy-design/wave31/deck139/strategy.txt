YOUR DECK: Simic-green RAMP + MUTATE midrange. You ramp with cheap creatures and land-fetchers,
then MUTATE your big "Apex" bombs onto a creature you already control to fire game-swinging
triggers and build one huge threat. You are usually the CONTROL against aggro (deploy blockers
early, survive, then take over with a mutated bomb) and the BEATDOWN against slow decks (ramp
into an Apex, mutate it, and race). Read the opponent: if they are faster than you, your first
job is to STOP DYING - put bodies down to block - and save your mutate value for when you are
stable. Do not sit on a hand of expensive Apexes while an aggro deck kills you.

MULLIGAN - THIS IS YOUR BIGGEST LEVER. RUN THIS COUNT, THEN COMMIT.
Every one of these ELEVEN cards is a LAND and every one of them makes GREEN mana: Forest,
Stomping Ground, Temple Garden, Overgrown Tomb, Breeding Pool. Sacred Foundry, Steam Vents,
Island, Mountain, Plains and Swamp are lands too - they just do not make green. A card printed
"(land: taps for {R}{G})" IS a land AND IS a green source; read the braces, not the name.
KEEP the hand when BOTH are true, and answer "Keep this hand":
  (1) you count 2 or more LANDS of any kind, at least one of them from the green list above; AND
  (2) you see at least ONE of: Arboreal Grazer, Pollywog Symbiote, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove,
      Migratory Greathorn, Gemrazer, or Beanstalk Giant // Fertile Footsteps.
MULLIGAN only when you count 0 or 1 lands, or 6-7 lands, or zero cards from list (2).
HARD FLOOR: you may take at most TWO mulligans in a game. On your third look you KEEP whatever
you have and play it out - a 5-card hand that develops beats a 4-card hand that is prettier, and
a 3-card hand loses on the spot.
The thought "this hand has no lands" is ALWAYS FALSE when any card in it is printed "(land: ...)".
The thought "I have no green source" is ALWAYS FALSE when any card in it shows {G} in its braces.
Lands in your HAND are mana - you play one every turn; a hand does not need lands already on the
battlefield to be keepable.
When you are told to put cards on the BOTTOM, name only as many cards as the line asks for, worst
first (spare duplicate lands, then the most expensive Apex you cannot cast).

HOW MUTATE WORKS (this is the whole deck - learn it once):
- MUTATE = casting a creature "for its mutate cost" onto a NON-HUMAN creature you already
  control on the battlefield. Every creature in this deck is non-Human, so any of your creatures
  can be the host. The two cards MERGE into ONE creature.
- The MERGED creature has the P/T and NAME of whichever card is ON TOP, PLUS ALL abilities of
  both cards. It keeps the host's summoning-sickness status (if the host was already in play a
  turn, the merged creature can attack immediately).
- "mutate OVER" puts the card you are casting ON TOP. "mutate UNDER" leaves the host on top.
  DEFAULT = mutate OVER with your Apex/bigger creature, so the merged creature gets the big P/T
  and evasion. Only mutate UNDER if the host already has the bigger, better P/T you want to keep.
- The mutate TRIGGER fires either way (over or under) - it belongs to the card that mutated.

#1 RULE - THE APEX BOMBS ARE PAYOFFS ONLY WHEN YOU MUTATE THEM. Snapdax, Illuna, Nethroi,
Vadrok, Brokkos, Everquill Phoenix, Gemrazer and Migratory Greathorn all say "Whenever this
creature MUTATES, <do something powerful>." If you HARDCAST one (pay its full cost) you get ONLY a
body and its keywords - the trigger does NOTHING. So when a host is on your battlefield, take the
mutate line.
- CHECK THE LINE YOU ARE ABOUT TO TAKE. A mutate line always contains the words "with its mutate
  cost". "Cast Illuna, Apex of Wishes {2}{g}{u}{r}" is a HARDCAST; "Cast Everquill Phoenix with
  its mutate cost {3}{r}" is the mutate. Before you answer, re-read your chosen option and confirm
  the words "mutate cost" are in it if you meant to mutate.
- IF NO LINE FOR THAT CARD SAYS "with its mutate cost", THE MUTATE IS NOT AVAILABLE THIS TURN
  (usually you are short a coloured pip - Snapdax needs {W}{W}, Illuna needs {U}{U}). Then either
  hardcast it as a plain body because you need the body, or cast something else and mutate later.
  Say which one you are doing; do not write "I will mutate it" over a hardcast line.
- SNAPDAX (mutate {2}{BR}{W}{W}, 3/5 double strike): on mutate, deal 4 damage to an opponent's
  creature/planeswalker AND gain 4 life. Your best anti-aggro play.
- ILLUNA (mutate {3}{RG}{U}{U}, 6/6 flying trample): on mutate, dig until you hit a nonland
  permanent and put it onto the battlefield or into hand - a free extra card/threat.
- NETHROI (mutate {4}{GW}{B}{B}, 5/5 deathtouch lifelink): on mutate, return creatures with
  total power 10 or less from your graveyard to the battlefield.
- VADROK (mutate {1}{WU}{R}{R}, 3/3 flying first strike): on mutate, recast a noncreature card
  of mana value 3 or less from your graveyard for free.
- BROKKOS (mutate {2}{UB}{G}{G}, 6/6 trample): you may even cast it FROM YOUR GRAVEYARD using its
  mutate ability - a recurring threat.
- GEMRAZER (mutate {1}{G}{G}, 4/4 reach trample): on mutate, destroy an opponent's artifact or
  enchantment. MIGRATORY GREATHORN (mutate {2}{G}, 3/4): on mutate, fetch a basic land onto the
  battlefield. Its land-search fires on mutate ONLY, not on a normal cast. These two are your
  CHEAPEST and most reliable mutates - green is your deepest colour, so take them early and often.

#2 RULE - UNDER AGGRO, A FRESH BODY BEATS A MUTATE. Mutating grows one creature but adds NO new
body; hardcasting adds a blocker. When you are being attacked and need more blockers to survive,
CAST A CREATURE NORMALLY. Save mutate for when your life is safe or when the trigger (Snapdax's
4-damage-and-gain-4 especially) is itself the stabilizer. Mutate to REMOVE/RECUR value or to
close, not for vanity size.

CAST FLOOR: every one of your main phases, if a "Cast <something>" line is offered and you can
pay for it, TAKE ONE. "Cast nothing right now" is the answer ONLY when the sole options are
Apexes you cannot mutate and do not need as bodies. A castable 3/4 is worth more than any plan
you are saving mana for.

RAMP FIRST, THEN THE BOMBS. Your early plays enable everything:
- ARBOREAL GRAZER ({G}, 0/3 reach): when it enters, put a LAND FROM YOUR HAND onto the
  battlefield tapped - a ramp step AND a 0/3 wall that blocks the ground and is a fine mutate
  host. Play it turn 1 when you can.
- DRYAD OF THE ILYSIAN GROVE ({2}{G}, 2/4): extra land drop each turn + all your lands tap for
  every color. Dryad is what turns Snapdax's {W}{W} and Illuna's {U}{U} from impossible into
  castable - cast her on curve every time you draw her, and keep her as your mutate host.
- MIGRATORY GREATHORN, BEANSTALK GIANT // FERTILE FOOTSTEPS (cast Fertile Footsteps {2}{G} to
  fetch a basic; Beanstalk Giant is */* = your land count), NISSA WHO SHAKES THE WORLD (Forests
  make double green; +1 turns a land into a 3/3 haste-vigilance body that is still a land).

READING THE MUTATE FLOW (the render labels each step - just follow it in order):
- The cast menu offers the mutate line as "Cast <card> with its mutate cost {..}". Take it to
  start the mutate; a line without those words is the hardcast (no trigger - see #1).
- You may then be asked a SECOND time: "1. Cast Card Normally  2. mutate [cast for the MUTATE
  cost: merge onto one of your non-Human creatures ...]  3. Decline". If you took the mutate cost,
  answer "mutate" here. Answering "Cast Card Normally" at this step throws the mutate away.
- If POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE is in play, casting a mutate spell makes you draw then discard. The game
  asks "DISCARD ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS" listing your HAND - pitch your LEAST useful card (a spare
  land), never a creature or spell you want. (Pollywog also makes your mutate spells cost {1}
  less, and its discount keeps working even while Pollywog is the UNDER card of a merged pile - a
  strong reason to mutate onto it.)
- Next you are asked "mutate over / mutate under" - choose OVER (Apex/bigger card on top) unless
  the host already has the P/T you want to keep.
- Then the mutate TARGET question, titled "TARGET CHOICE for <the card you are mutating>",
  listing creatures on YOUR BATTLEFIELD - pick the host whose abilities you want (Dryad's ramp,
  Pollywog's discount) over a vanilla 0/3.
- After it resolves, your battlefield shows the pile as ONE line tagged "[mutated pile - ONE
  merged creature ...; N card(s) underneath: <names>]". It is ONE creature with the abilities of
  every card in the pile - count it as one.

ATTACK FLOOR - run this test on each listed attacker, one line at a time:
send it if its POWER is 1 or more AND any of: the opponent has no untapped creature that can
block it / it survives every block shown / it trades up or kills a blocker. Then answer and stop.
- ARBOREAL GRAZER IS 0/3. A 0-power creature deals ZERO damage to the opponent no matter how many
  you send. Grazers stay home and BLOCK; they attack only when they are your last bodies and the
  attack is somehow lethal. Three Grazers attacking is three damage of nothing.
- A creature marked "[summoning sick - cannot attack this turn]" CAN STILL BLOCK. When you count
  the opponent's available blockers, count every creature of theirs that is NOT marked "[tapped]".
- "no attackers" is the answer ONLY when every listed attacker dies for zero gain.

BLOCK FLOOR: block when the line says "you kill it, your blocker lives" or when the damage would
otherwise put you below 6 life. Read the parenthetical the render gives you and believe it.
- POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE STAYS BACK OFF BLOCKS. It is your mutate discount engine; keep it alive on the
  battlefield and mutate onto it. Chump it only to stop lethal damage.
- A blocker facing deathtouch or first strike dies without killing anything - the render says "your
  blocker dies, attacker lives". Take that block only to stop lethal damage.

DECIDE FAST - ON EVERY DECISION, NOT JUST COMBAT. Write your CHOICE / ATTACK / BLOCK / PUT line,
give two or three sentences of reason, and STOP. Do the math ONCE and commit. Re-reading the board
a second and third time inside one answer does not change the answer and it burns the clock: games
are time-capped, a slow seat gets adjudicated behind, and a long answer risks truncating into no
action. Your merged creatures are big; attack with them once they can. Double strike (Snapdax) and
trample (Gemrazer/Illuna/Nethroi/Brokkos) mean a blocked trampler assigns lethal to the blocker and
the rest to the player; a blocked creature WITHOUT trample deals nothing to the player.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> do):
- Opening hand with 2+ lands (one green) and any of Grazer/Pollywog/Dryad/Greathorn/Gemrazer/
  Beanstalk -> KEEP THIS HAND.
- You have already mulliganed twice -> KEEP whatever you are looking at.
- You can cast an Apex and you control a non-Human creature -> take the line that says "with its
  mutate cost", then "mutate", then "mutate over".
- No line for that Apex says "with its mutate cost" -> the mutate is off this turn; hardcast it
  only if you need the body, and call it a hardcast.
- You are under attack and short on blockers -> cast a creature NORMALLY for the extra body.
- Snapdax is castable FOR ITS MUTATE COST and the opponent has a threatening creature/planeswalker
  -> mutate Snapdax: it kills the threat AND you gain 4 life.
- You are asked to "DISCARD ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS" (Pollywog) listing your HAND -> discard a spare
  land.
- You are asked "mutate over / mutate under" -> choose OVER, unless the host already has the P/T
  you want to keep.
- Arboreal Grazer is castable and you have a spare land in hand -> cast it; ramp + a wall + a
  future mutate host.
- Dryad is on your battlefield -> take your extra land drop every turn; she is your best host and
  your colour fixing for the {W}{W} and {U}{U} mutates.
- You are flooding on lands -> play Fertile Footsteps / Beanstalk for value; use Nissa +1 to turn a
  spare land into a 3/3 body.
- A game is drifting to the turn cap even -> that is a LOSS risk; mutate a bomb and PRESS to close.
