Deck identity: "Etched Affinity" is a fast URB artifact-aggro deck. Almost
every card is an artifact, so your creatures come down for free or nearly
free and your cheap payoffs get huge. You are the BEATDOWN in nearly every
matchup: dump artifacts, EQUIP Cranial Plating, and kill the opponent by
turn 4-6. Play fast and pressure their life total. Only shift to defense if
you are clearly getting raced and have no equipped attacker.

#1 RULE - EQUIP CRANIAL PLATING. THIS WINS THE GAME.
Cranial Plating gives the creature it is attached to +1/+0 for EACH artifact
you control - usually +4 or +5. An UNEQUIPPED Plating sitting on the
battlefield does NOTHING. Casting Plating is only step one; you MUST then
equip it.
- When you see a priority action "Equip with Cranial Plating targeting X
  [cost: {1}]", TAKE IT. Equipping is almost always your best play. Do not
  pass (0) while a Plating is unequipped and you have {1} open.
- To equip you need {1} of mana untapped, so DO NOT TAP OUT. Every turn,
  leave one land untapped so you can equip.
- ONE equipped Plating is plenty. Do NOT cast a second or third Plating just
  to "use mana" - a spare copy in play is a dead card. Cast creatures
  instead, or hold the Plating and keep mana up to equip the first one.
- Equip Plating onto your best carrier, in this order:
  1. Etched Champion (with 3+ artifacts it has protection from all colors -
     colored decks CANNOT block or kill it, so it is an unstoppable beater).
  2. A flyer - Vault Skirge or Ornithopter - evasion the opponent can't block.
  3. Signal Pest (can't be blocked except by fliers/reach - also evasive).
  A pumped evasive creature ends the game in 2-3 swings.

DEPLOY CREATURES EVERY TURN. You cannot win with no board.
Your cheap threats: Memnite (0 mana 1/1), Ornithopter (0 mana 0/2 flier),
Signal Pest (1 mana), Vault Skirge (1 mana flier lifelink), Steel Overseer
(2), Etched Champion (3), Master of Etherium (3). Play out your creatures
early and often. A hand full of Platings and burn with no creatures is a
LOSS - get bodies down first, THEN equip and attack.

KEY CARDS:
- Signal Pest: ALWAYS attack with it, even at 0 power. Its battle cry pumps
  every OTHER attacker +1/+0, and it can't be blocked except by fliers. It
  is free damage and free team pump - never hold it back.
- Steel Overseer: TAP IT every one of your turns ({T}: +1/+1 counter on all
  your artifact creatures). The counters are permanent and stack, so an
  Overseer that lives 2-3 turns snowballs your whole team. Protect it.
- Master of Etherium: its power/toughness = your artifact count, AND it gives
  your other artifact creatures +1/+1. A game-ending anthem + body. Cast it
  once your board is developed.
- Mox Opal: with 3+ artifacts it taps for any color. Free mana - use it to
  cast an extra spell or to keep a land open for equipping.
- Thoughtcast: draw 2, and affinity makes it cost as little as {U}. Cast it
  to refuel when your hand is nearly empty. Don't cast it if you'd rather be
  equipping or attacking this turn.
- Galvanic Blast: 2 damage, but 4 damage with metalcraft (3+ artifacts),
  which you almost always have. It is your reach and removal - HOLD it. Point
  it at the OPPONENT'S FACE for the kill, or kill a blocker in the way of
  your equipped attacker. NEVER target yourself. Don't burn a small creature
  early when you could save 4 to the face.

METALCRAFT: controlling 3+ artifacts turns on Galvanic Blast (4 dmg), Etched
Champion (protection), and Mox Opal (mana). Darksteel Citadel is an artifact
LAND and counts. You reach 3 artifacts almost immediately - assume metalcraft
is ON.

COMBAT: attack every turn you can profitably. You are the aggressor. Send
evasive creatures (fliers, Signal Pest, protected Etched Champion) in freely -
they rarely get blocked. Don't attack with a 0-power creature that has no
Plating and no battle cry benefit (a lone unequipped Ornithopter does nothing).

MISTAKES TO AVOID (seen in real games with this deck):
- Casting Cranial Plating and NEVER equipping it - the single biggest way this
  deck loses. Equip it.
- Tapping out so you have no {1} left to equip. Keep a land open.
- Casting a 2nd/3rd Plating instead of a creature - redundant Platings are
  dead cards. Develop threats.
- Firing Galvanic Blast early at a small creature instead of holding 4 damage
  for the face or a key blocker.
- Sitting back passively - this deck races and wins the race. Attack.
