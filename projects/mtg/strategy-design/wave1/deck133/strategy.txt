Deck identity: mono-black midrange. You attack from TWO angles at once - strip
their HAND with discard, kill their creatures with cheap removal - while your
own black permanents pile up "devotion" (each {B} in a permanent's mana cost)
for one big payoff: Gray Merchant of Asphodel. You grind the opponent out of
resources, drain their life, and win late. You are not a fast deck; you are the
deck that still has cards when theirs run out.

WHO YOU ARE BY MATCHUP (decide this first, every game):
- vs AGGRO / BURN (lots of cheap creatures, direct-damage spells): YOU ARE THE
  CONTROL. Survive. Kill their creatures with removal, drain life back with Gray
  Merchant, wall the board with Phyrexian Obliterator. Do NOT just chip in with
  one small attacker while they race you - stabilize first.
- vs CONTROL (counterspells, card draw, few creatures): YOU ARE THE BEATDOWN.
  Strip their counters and removal with discard, jam your threats, and ATTACK.
  Do not sit back and durdle - a control deck beats you if the game goes long.
- vs MIDRANGE / other creature decks: grind. Trade one-for-one, remove their
  best threat, and win with Gray Merchant's drain plus Phyrexian Arena's cards.

GRAY MERCHANT OF ASPHODEL - your payoff. It drains the opponent for your
devotion to black (count the {B} symbols in the mana costs of YOUR permanents;
LANDS DO NOT COUNT) and you gain that much life.
- RULE: NEVER cast Gray Merchant as your only black permanent - that drains for
  just 2. Build a black board FIRST (Bloodghast BB, Geralf's BBB, Obliterator
  BBBB, the Lilianas, Yawgmoth all add pips), THEN cast it for a 5+ drain.
- A big Gray Merchant swings the life totals and wins the race against aggro.
- Late game, use Liliana the Last Hope's -2 to return Gray Merchant to hand and
  drain them a second time.

DISCARD (Thoughtseize, Inquisition of Kozilek, Collective Brutality) - cast it
EARLY, turn 1 if you can, before they get to use the card. When you choose which
card to take, TAKE THE MOST DANGEROUS CARD YOU CANNOT ANSWER LATER:
- vs control: take COUNTERSPELLS, SWEEPERS, and card-draw ENGINES so your
  threats resolve.
- vs aggro: take BURN aimed at your face (you have removal for their creatures,
  but nothing for burn to the face) and their strongest creature.
- NEVER strip their weakest card. A mana rock or mana dork (things like
  Astrolabe, Boreal Druid) or a tiny vanilla creature is almost never the right
  take when a sweeper, counter, bomb, or burn spell is in their hand.
- Inquisition only takes a card of mana value 3 or less. Thoughtseize takes
  anything but costs you 2 life - don't Thoughtseize when you are low vs aggro.

FATAL PUSH - an INSTANT. Destroys a creature of mana value 2 or less (4 or less
if one of your permanents left play this turn - a cracked fetchland or a dying
Bloodghast enables this). HOLD IT. Do not fire it on your own main phase unless
you have to. Wait for their scariest cheap creature, or their attack step.

YOUR THREATS:
- Bloodghast (2/1) CAN'T BLOCK, and returns from your graveyard every time you
  play a land. Haste only when the opponent is at 10 or less. It is a relentless
  attacker and free devotion/sacrifice fodder that always comes back - it is
  also your BEST card to discard to Collective Brutality's escalate or Yawgmoth
  (you get it back with a land drop). Never rely on it to block.
- Geralf's Messenger (3/3) enters tapped, drains 2 when it enters, and has
  undying (comes back bigger when it dies - draining 2 AGAIN). Perfect fodder
  for Yawgmoth: sacrifice it, it returns, drains again.
- Phyrexian Obliterator (5/5 trample, costs BBBB = 4 devotion by itself).
  Whenever anything damages it, that source's controller sacrifices that many
  permanents. Opponents cannot profitably block it or burn it. Drop it vs aggro
  as a wall they cannot remove and a fast clock.

YOUR ENGINES:
- Yawgmoth, Thran Physician: pay 1 life + sacrifice another creature to put a
  -1/-1 counter on a creature and draw a card. Strong removal + card draw, but
  ONLY with fodder (Bloodghast, Geralf's, tokens). Do NOT cast it when you have
  no other creature to feed it - it does nothing.
- Liliana of the Veil: +1 makes BOTH players discard (use it when their hand is
  better than yours). -2 forces a player to sacrifice a creature - use it to
  kill a threat too big for Fatal Push.
- Liliana, the Last Hope: +1 gives a creature -2/-1 - kills X/1s and shrinks
  attackers, excellent vs aggro and tokens. -2 returns a creature (rebuy Gray
  Merchant). Protect her loyalty with +1 when under attack.
- Phyrexian Arena: draws you an extra card each turn for 1 life. Great in the
  grind; go easy on it when you are low vs aggro.

SEQUENCING:
- Play a land BEFORE your spells (it also returns Bloodghast).
- Early: land + discard their best card, or hold cheap removal.
- Mid: deploy black threats, remove theirs, build devotion.
- Late: Gray Merchant for a big drain, then attack to close. Rebuy it with
  Liliana if the game grinds on.

DO NOT: cast Gray Merchant with no other black permanents out; strip their
weakest card with discard when a game-swinging one is there; fire Fatal Push
early or at sorcery speed when you could hold it; cast Yawgmoth with no fodder;
race a faster aggro deck instead of removing their creatures and draining life.
