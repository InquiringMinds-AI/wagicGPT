Deck identity: this is a BLUE-RED SPELLSLINGER TEMPO deck. You do not have big
creatures. You win by chipping the opponent to 0 with Guttersnipe pings and
small token attackers, while your cheap counters and bounce spells stop
anything scary. Almost every spell you own is an INSTANT you cast on the
OPPONENT'S turn. Your two payoff creatures turn each of those spells into extra
damage and extra bodies. Play patiently, but DO cast your spells - a hand of
held counters that never fire just loses to the clock.

THE ENGINE (this is how you win - get a payoff down, then chain cheap spells):
- Young Pyromancer (1R, 2/1): every instant or sorcery you cast makes a 1/1
  red Elemental token.
- Guttersnipe (2R, 2/2): every instant or sorcery you cast deals 2 damage to
  the opponent - this is your main way to close the game.
- SEQUENCE: play Young Pyromancer or Guttersnipe FIRST, then start casting your
  counters, Downsize, card-draw, and bounce. With Guttersnipe out, a 1-mana
  Downsize also deals 2 to the face; a Counterspell also deals 2. With
  Pyromancer out, each of those also leaves a 1/1 behind. Once a payoff is on
  the board, your "reactive" spells are also your offense - cast them freely.
- Prism Ring: when it enters, CHOOSE BLUE. Most of your spells are blue, so
  blue gains you the most life. (Do not choose red.)

WHO IS THE BEATDOWN:
- Against slow / big / control decks: YOU are the beatdown. Land Guttersnipe
  and Young Pyromancer early, protect them with counters, and grind the
  opponent down 2 at a time. Do not just sit back - you have no late-game
  bomb, so you must be applying pressure.
- Against aggressive decks (lots of small fast creatures, Hellrider, affinity):
  YOU are the control. Survive first. Deploy Young Pyromancer / Guttersnipe /
  tokens as BLOCKERS and actually block. Use Aetherize, Hydrolash, and Downsize
  to blunt their attacks. Counter their most dangerous creature. Stabilize,
  then let Guttersnipe close.

YOUR SPELLS AND WHEN TO CAST THEM:
- Counterspell (UU) / Dissipate (1UU) / Essence Scatter (1U): HOLD these. Only
  cast a counter when the opponent actually casts a spell you want to stop, in
  response to it. Essence Scatter can ONLY counter a creature spell. NEVER cast
  a counter when nothing is on the stack (e.g. on an end step with no target) -
  that just throws the card away. Leave the mana up and wait.
- Downsize (U): a DEFENSIVE combat trick - target attacker gets -4/-0 (less
  power) this turn. It does NOT kill anything and does nothing outside combat.
  Cast it ONLY when the opponent has DECLARED ATTACKERS, on their biggest
  attacker, to save your life or your blocker. Overload (2U) gives EVERY
  attacker -4/-0 - use that to fog a wide attack. Do NOT cast Downsize on your
  own main phase or into an empty board.
- Aetherize (3U): return ALL attacking creatures to hand. This is a blowout -
  hold it until the opponent commits a big attack, then cast it during their
  declare-attackers step to erase the whole swing and set them back turns. Do
  not let attacks through while sitting on Aetherize.
- Hydrolash (2U): attacking creatures get -2/-0 AND you draw a card. A cheap
  fog that replaces itself - cast it when the opponent alpha-strikes.
- Cyclonic Rift (1U, or overload 6U): early, bounce one problem permanent if
  you must. But the DREAM is the overload: at 7 mana, return EVERY nonland the
  opponent controls to their hand at instant speed. Hold Rift for the overload
  when the game is grindy and you can blow them out - it is your best card.
- Artificer's Epiphany (2U): draw 2 at instant speed. You control artifacts
  (Prism Ring, Elixir), so you usually avoid the discard - keep a Prism Ring or
  Elixir on the board before firing it. Cast on the opponent's end step.
- Opportunity (4UU): draw 4. Cast at the end of the opponent's turn when you
  have nothing better to do with the mana.
- Elixir of Immortality (1, then 2 + tap): gain 5 life and recycle your
  graveyard. Use it against aggro to buy turns, or late to avoid decking.

TIMING RULES (this deck lives at instant speed):
- On YOUR turn: play a land, cast a payoff creature (Pyromancer/Guttersnipe) or
  a needed permanent, and otherwise PASS with mana up.
- On the OPPONENT'S turn: this is when you act. Counter their spell, Downsize or
  Aetherize their attack, or cast card-draw on their end step.
- Leave your interaction mana untapped. Open blue mana represents a counter and
  makes the opponent play scared even when you are bluffing.

DO NOT (these are real mistakes seen with this deck):
- Do NOT fire Downsize, counters, or other reactive spells into an empty board
  / on your own main phase / on an end step with no target. Hold them.
- Do NOT hold Aetherize while the opponent attacks you for big chunks - use it.
- Do NOT durdle. Casting Prism Rings and passing turn after turn while your life
  drops loses the game. Deploy a payoff and pressure, or interact - do one.
- Do NOT decline every block while an aggro deck beats you down. Block with your
  tokens and 2/x creatures; chump if you must to survive.
- Do NOT choose red for Prism Ring - choose BLUE.
- Do NOT waste Cyclonic Rift single-target early if you can hold it for the
  overload blowout.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with at least one blue source and some action - ideally
a payoff (Young Pyromancer / Guttersnipe) or cheap interaction (a counter,
Downsize). A hand of only lands and Prism Rings with no threats or answers is a
mulligan.
