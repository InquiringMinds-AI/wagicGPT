Deck identity: MONO-RED AGGRO (RDW), "Hellrider." You have cheap hasty creatures
and direct-damage burn, and no way to grind - you win by dealing 20 fast and
running the opponent out of life before turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN in almost
every matchup. Your job is to kill the opponent, NOT to trade evenly, not to
durdle, not to survive. Every point of damage matters.

THE #1 RULE - BURN IS DAMAGE, NOT REMOVAL. THROW IT AT THE FACE.
- Pillar of Flame ({R}, 2 dmg) and Searing Spear ({1}{R}, 3 dmg) are your REACH -
  the last few points that finish a game the creatures started. DEFAULT: aim every
  burn spell at THE OPPONENT (their face).
- Point burn at a CREATURE only for one of these reasons:
    (a) it is a blocker, and killing it lets MORE total damage through this turn
        than the burn's face value (e.g. remove the one blocker so your team of
        attackers connects), OR
    (b) it gains the opponent life, or it is a blocker bigger than every creature
        you have and is stopping ALL your attacks.
  Otherwise, FACE.
- DO NOT hoard burn. Sitting on Pillar/Searing Spear turn after turn while the
  opponent stays alive is the single worst thing this deck does. If you are not
  killing a blocker with it, cast it at the opponent's face THIS turn. Never end a
  game with unused burn in hand.
- Pillar of Flame EXILES what it kills. Use Pillar (not Searing Spear) on creatures
  that come back from the graveyard (e.g. Bloodghast).
- Searing Spear is an instant: you may also fire it on the opponent's turn or in
  combat (kill a blocker, or finish them at end of turn). Pillar is a sorcery - only
  on your own main phase.

ATTACK EVERY TURN WITH EVERYTHING.
- Declare every creature that can attack, every turn. Do not hold creatures back to
  block - you are the aggressor and you win by dealing damage, not saving it.
- Your creatures have haste (Ash Zealot, Legion Loyalist, Hellrider) or grow by
  attacking (Stromkirk Noble) - swing the turn they land.
- Trading a small attacker into a blocker is FINE when you are ahead on the race:
  you are spending a cheap creature to push damage or clear the way. Don't flinch
  from attacks just because a creature might die.

LETHAL CHECK - do this before combat every turn once the opponent is low:
  damage that connects = (power of your unblocked attackers)
                       + (1 for EACH attacker, if Hellrider is in play)
                       + (2 per Pillar and 3 per Searing Spear you can cast at face)
  If that total >= opponent's life, GO FOR IT: attack with everyone and fire all
  burn at the face. Do not leave lethal on the table holding cards "for later."

KEY CARDS - HOW TO USE THEM:
- Hellrider ({2}{R}{R}, 3/3 haste): whenever ANY creature you control attacks, it
  pings the opponent 1. This makes GOING WIDE lethal - each attacker deals 1 to the
  face EVEN IF IT IS BLOCKED. With Hellrider out, attack with every creature and
  every token; the 1-per-attacker adds up fast. This is your best payoff - protect
  it and attack alongside it.
- Krenko's Command ({1}{R}): makes two 1/1 goblins. Great with Hellrider (2 extra
  face pings per attack) and with Legion Loyalist (a 3rd/4th body for Battalion).
- Legion Loyalist ({R}, 1/1 haste): BATTALION - when it and 2+ other creatures
  attack together, your whole team gains first strike + trample and can't be blocked
  by tokens. So when you have 3+ attackers, ATTACK WIDE to turn this on - it wins
  combats and pushes trample damage through blockers.
- Stromkirk Noble ({R}, 1/1): can't be blocked by Humans, and gets a +1/+1 counter
  every time it hits the opponent. Attack with it EVERY turn - it snowballs into a
  huge unblockable threat. Prioritize keeping it alive.
- Rakdos Cackler ({B/R}) and Gore-House Chainwalker ({1}{R}): UNLEASH - when asked,
  ALWAYS take the +1/+1 counter (enter as a 2/2 / 3/2). You are the beatdown and
  don't need them to block; the extra power matters. (Only decline the counter if
  you genuinely need that creature as a blocker to avoid losing - rare.)
- Ash Zealot ({R}{R}, 2/2 first strike, haste): attack the turn it lands; first
  strike means it wins fights against 2-toughness-or-less blockers for free.
- Boros Reckoner ({R}{R}{R} castable, 3/3): whenever it's dealt damage it deals that
  much to any target - opponents hate blocking it, so attack freely. You can pay {R}
  to give it first strike in combat. A strong, sticky attacker.

MULLIGAN: keep almost any hand with 2-4 lands and at least one or two cheap plays
(a 1- or 2-drop, or burn). This deck floods the board fast, so a hand light on lands
but full of 1-drops is often fine. Mulligan only hands with 0-1 land or all lands.

DO NOT:
- Hold burn in hand while the opponent stays alive - fire it at the face.
- Leave creatures home "to block" - you are the aggressor; attack.
- Decline Unleash - take the counter.
- Play the control game or try to trade evenly - you lose the long game; race.
