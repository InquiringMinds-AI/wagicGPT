# Proposals for the strategy-writing skill, from seat deck123 (wave 44)

Only what this seat's evidence supports. Two proposals, one retirement note.

## PROPOSAL S1 - A GUIDE SECTION WITH MORE THAN TWO CONDITIONS MUST BE AN ORDERED LADDER
WITH A STOP, NOT A PARAGRAPH OF EXCEPTIONS. The executor resolves prose by narrative fit, not
by rule order.
Evidence: deck123's COMBAT section carried five attack conditions in prose - "send all of
them", the Lightmine Field exception, the all-(neither dies) hold, the count hold, the
lifegain-wall hold, and the release. In ONE corpus the same section produced BOTH failure
directions: it attacked 12 creatures into the wall board it explicitly forbids (twice, losing
the game), and it held 11 attackers back against one blocker in a window where two of its own
release clauses applied. Neither failure is a missing fact - every fact was in the section and
in the render. The wave-43 edit that added the wall clause added it as a SIXTH paragraph.
Ask: where a section has three or more branches, the skill should require (a) numbered checks
in decision order, (b) "stop at the first one that answers", and (c) the highest-cost branch
FIRST. A new finding appended to such a section must be placed by cost, not by chronology.

## PROPOSAL S2 - WHEN A GATE FAILS BECAUSE THE EXECUTOR OUT-ARGUES IT WITH ARITHMETIC, THE
REPAIR IS TO NAME AND REFUSE THE ARITHMETIC, NOT TO RESTATE THE GATE LOUDER.
Evidence: both deck123 wall-board attacks quote Perimeter Captain and Sanguine Bond by name and
then compute a damage sum ("we deal 30 and they are at 11, so we win") that is correct in
isolation and fatal in context. A restatement of "ATTACK: none when these names appear" was
already present and was read. What was missing was a sentence saying that a favourable damage
sum is NOT an exception, and why (you do not survive to collect it).
Ask: the skill's Step 5 wording rules should carry a named pattern - THE COUNTER-ARGUMENT
CLAUSE. When log evidence shows the executor overriding a gate with a specific line of
reasoning, quote that reasoning's SHAPE in the guide and refuse it by name. A gate the executor
can talk itself past needs its escape route closed, not its volume raised.

## RETIREMENT NOTE - the wave-43 "spend EVERY blocker" teach did its job and should not be
re-derived. It held 2/2 here. What replaced it as the live gap is WHICH attacker, which is a
different lesson (arithmetic-of-survival vs. count-of-blockers) and should be tracked as its
own rung rather than as a second corpus of the same one.

## NOT PROPOSED (evidence too thin)
- Anything about mulligan wording. One fair-hand corpus, 6 keeps, 0 mulligans at this seat.
  The skill's amendment-21 note that mulligan teaches were untestable is now merely PARTLY
  discharged: the seam is live, but no seat here has produced a mulligan to learn from.
