# Core-prompt / render proposals from seat deck123 (wave 44)

LAYER ROUTING: engine defects are in the seat file's ENGINE list (H1, H2, M3, M4, L5) and are
NOT restated as prompt text here. What follows is core-prompt wording only.

## G1 (MED) - the attackers prompt's standing paragraph should name what the tags do NOT cover.
Current text: `A "their untapped blockers" tag lists each of their currently untapped creatures
that could legally block that attacker, with the computed 1-on-1 result if it does - before
gang-blocks, pump or combat tricks. They choose whether to block. Do not re-derive these
outcomes; use them.`
The exclusion list is "gang-blocks, pump or combat tricks". It is incomplete in a way that has
now killed a seat twice: the tag also does not cover any LIFE consequence of the block. A
Perimeter Captain that blocks reads `(neither dies)` and gains its controller 2 life per
blocking defender, which two Sanguine Bonds convert into 4 off the attacker per defender.
Proposed addition, one clause: `... - before gang-blocks, pump, combat tricks, or any life
gained or lost by the block itself.`
This is a TRUST-PRESERVING change, not a doubt-teaching one: the tag stays authoritative for
what it measures, and the sentence tells the pilot what it measures. (The render fix that would
make this clause unnecessary is H2.)

## G2 (LOW) - the "This is not your last chance to act this turn" paragraph is correct and is
being used to defer, then never acted on.
deck123 answered "Cast nothing right now" in main phase 1 and again in main phase 2 of the same
turn, with more than one option on the menu both times, 25 times across the six games. The paragraph
is doing its job (it prevents panic-casting) and no change is proposed to it. Recorded only so
a future wave does not read the pass-pass pattern as a prompt defect.

## G3 (LOW) - `Land drop:` phrasing is now excellent and load-bearing; do not shorten it.
Both variants appeared - `Land drop: NOT yet used this turn - you can still play a land. The
land drop is its OWN decision ... so its absence from the choices below does not mean it is
gone.` and `Land drop: you have no land you could play right now (none playable from your hand
or any other zone).` The second form is what let this seat correctly answer "Play no land right
now" and correctly stop planning around a drop it did not have (deck123 vs130 seq 7, mana-screw
game). No change; flagged as a keep.
