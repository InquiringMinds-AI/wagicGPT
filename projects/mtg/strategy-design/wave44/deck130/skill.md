# Proposals for the strategy-writing skill, from seat deck130 (wave 44)

Only what this seat's evidence supports. Two proposals.

## PROPOSAL S3 - A GUIDE CLAUSE THAT TELLS THE EXECUTOR TO READ AN ANNOTATION MUST SAY WHAT TO
DO WITH BOTH HALVES OF IT. A "read further" teach with a one-sided conclusion converts a
under-use into an over-use.
Evidence: wave-43's EDIT 6 told deck130 to read the WHOLE `{X pricing:}` annotation instead of
stopping at the headline, and concluded "losing your own Blastminer to kill two of their
creatures is a trade you take". The pilot now demonstrably reads the tail clause - and used it
twice to justify casting Starstorm at an X the same annotation said would kill its own Rorix
Bladewing, in both cases while it was ahead and holding lethal on board. The annotation prints
THEIRS and YOURS; the guide graded only THEIRS. The failure moved, it did not close.
Ask: the skill should require that any "read the whole annotation" teach enumerate a verdict
for EVERY field the annotation prints, including a DO-NOT case, before it ships. A reviewer
proposing such a clause should be asked: what does the executor do when the other half of this
annotation says the opposite?

## PROPOSAL S4 - A GUIDE MUST BE CHECKED FOR SELF-CONTRADICTION ACROSS SECTIONS, AND WHEN TWO
CLAUSES DISAGREE THE ONE NEAREST THE DECISION WINS - SO THE QUICK-REFERENCE LIST IS THE ONE
THAT MUST BE RIGHT.
Evidence: deck130's rule #2 step 4 sets a hard 6-or-less floor for face damage, its CROSS-CHECK
paragraph restates it, and its DECIDING SITUATIONS list at the bottom said "otherwise fire it
at the opponent and move on, do not agonise". The pilot fired 9 times at opponent life 17-27
while their life was visibly rising. Three clauses, one contradiction, and the terse
recognise -> do line beat two paragraphs of doctrine.
Ask: add a Definition-of-Done check - every DECIDING SITUATIONS / quick-reference line must be
traced back to the rule it summarises and verified to agree with it. This is cheap (the lists
are short) and it caught a game-losing contradiction that had survived at least one wave.

## NOT PROPOSED
- A general teach about inverted menus. That is an engine item (H1 in the seat file); the guide
  guard shipped here is a stopgap and should be RETIRED, not generalised, once the menu is
  fixed. Recording it as skill doctrine would be teaching doubt of a render that is about to
  become trustworthy.
