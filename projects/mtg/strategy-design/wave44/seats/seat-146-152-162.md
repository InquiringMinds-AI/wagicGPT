## ENGINE / RENDER ITEMS

**E-1 (MED) — the attack tag prices lifelink but not block-triggered life gain.**
`Pride Guardian #1 (0/3) (neither dies)` hides 3 life; `Perimeter Captain (0/4) (neither dies)`
hides 2 per defender block. The convention already exists for lifelink:
`Vampire (1/1) (both die (lifelink: they gain 1))`. Repro: deck146 vs deck126 `attackers` seq 44,
51, 56 — the opponent's life went 13 -> 42 on blocks whose every tag read "(neither dies)".
Fix shape: extend the same parenthesis to any `@combat(blocking)` life trigger on the blocker.

**E-2 (HIGH) — the collapsed blocker summary prints only the favourable outcome.**
Repro: deck162 vs deck123 `attackers` seq 13:
`[their untapped blockers: they have 6 untapped creatures that could block this one, biggest
Bloodline Keeper #1 (3/3) (you kill it, your attacker lives)]`. Three 2/2 Vampires then gang-blocked
and killed the 5/5. Every word is true and the footnote does say "before gang-blocks", but the only
OUTCOME shown is favourable on a board where the unfavourable one is trivial. Guides are all written
against the ENUMERATED form of this field and none of them mentions the collapsed form.
Fix shape: price the collapse ("any 2 of them together deal 4, enough to kill it"), or at minimum
tag it "1-on-1 only — N blockers can gang this attacker". The collapse threshold should be recorded
where reviewers can see it.

**E-3 (HIGH) — `stale_echo` discards valid answers on sub-asks whose options carry no card name.**
All 6 corpus fallbacks are on this seat: 5 `stale_echo` + 1 `retracted_choice`.
- 146 vs126 seq 35 — Silverquill Command MODE list; reply `CHOICE: 6 (Silverquill Command)`.
  The parenthetical names **the ask's own source card**; no mode label contains those words, so the
  echo pass finds no match and an in-range index is discarded. This one is a **false stale**.
- 152 vs125 seq 52, 53, 62 — Teferi sub-asks (`choose a land` / `Decline`; `choose your land` /
  `choose opponent land`); replies named "Staff of Nin". Genuine staleness, but the ask states no
  effect (G-7) and the land rows carry no mana text (G-3).
- 162 vs123 seq 17 — damage-assignment among three identically-named Vampires; reply named
  "Cast Fate Unraveler". Genuine.
Subclass rate: **57 mode-label asks corpus-wide, 4 fallbacks = 7.0%**, vs 0.29% overall.
Fix shape (engine): `AIPlayerGPT.cpp` already strips a `pendingSource` prefix for the
`"<X> targeting <Y>"` family; add the symmetric case — when the echo's significant words match the
**ask's own source card name**, treat it as a source prefix, not a stale echo. That alone recovers
the 146 vs126 seq 35 record. The remaining four are prompt-side (G-6, G-7).

**E-4 (LOW) — singular loyalty loss carries no "(now N)".** Repro above; see G-10.

**E-5 (LOW) — land target rows carry no mana text.** `Tundra [land] [opponent's battlefield]`
(deck152 vs deck125 seq 64, 71, 85, 102). Relevant because Teferi's +1 asks the model to pick a
land to tap or untap with nothing to pick on. See G-3.

**NOT bugs — checked, so nobody re-dockets them:**
- `[doesn't untap during its controller's untap step]` on deck123's board is CORRECT: deck123 runs
  Intruder Alarm ("Creatures don't untap during their controllers' untap steps"). Master of the
  Feast's own primitive is `abilities=flying` + `@each my upkeep:draw:1 opponent`, 5/5, no such
  clause — the tag comes from the Alarm and the render is right.
- The Hive animation state IS rendered: an animated Hive prints `(3/3) [menace]` and is counted in
  "of which N are creatures". The 12 redundant animations are a guide gap, not a render gap.
