## Proposal 1 (rule-2 corollary, NEW face of Step 0-bis) — SPELL/BURN-HEAVY newcomer: the dominant
## idiosyncrasy is the TARGET SUB-MENU seam, and it must be taught as a first-pass rule.
Step 0-bis rule 3 currently names two faces of "the dominant idiosyncrasy": a belief that VETOES a
real option (decks 17/21 — hybrid mana, hold-burn) and card-text FABRICATION (deck62 — capability
table). deck49 is a THIRD face: a deck whose plays are mostly TARGETED SPELLS (burn + auras), where
the dominant idiosyncrasy is neither a belief nor a fabrication but an INTERFACE seam — after the
model commits to a targeted cast, the engine opens a SEPARATE target sub-menu the weak model does not
model. In this corpus that seam produced ALL 7 stale_echoes and the two unparsed loops, and the top
tax spikes. Proposed addition: "When a newcomer's deck is spell/aura/burn-heavy (most of its plays
target something), expect the reasoning-tax and stale_echo cluster to sit at the TARGET SUB-MENU, not
at card-text. Teach the flow as an explicit rule: 'choosing a targeted cast opens a second menu that
lists the TARGETS; pick the target by number; you cannot Cast-nothing after committing; do not re-name
the spell.'" (single-seat → WATCH.)

## Proposal 2 (rule-4/aura corollary) — a beneficial AURA offered only on opponent creatures is a LOOP
## TRAP; teach the explicit off-ramp on the first pass.
deck62 established "never enchant the opponent" for pump auras. deck49 sharpens the failure MODE: when
a +stats/haste aura (Goblin War Paint) is offered with ONLY opponent-creature targets, the model does
not just mis-target — it LOOPS to unparsed_reply hunting for a use (deck17 s18, deck135 s22, ~13k tax
each, both fallbacks). Proposed sharpening to the aura rule: pair "never enchant the opponent" with the
explicit terminal instruction "if your only legal aura targets are opponent creatures, DO NOT cast it —
pick Cast-nothing / play something else; do not agonize," because for a weak model the absence of a good
target is a loop source, not a silent skip. (Converges with deck62 at 2 seats → promote from WATCH.)

## Convergence to record (no new rule) — "MANA IS TRIVIAL / trust the cast list" is now 3-seat.
deck17 (hybrid off mono-Islands), deck62 (mono-green off Forests), deck49 (mono-red off Mountains) all
had their #1 tax epicenter in the "I have no mana / my land is tapped → I can't cast" false-belief, and
all three take the same rule-1 corrective ("if a Cast option is listed it is payable; trust the Mana-
available line; do not recount your lands"). This is a stable, converged first-pass rule-#1 for any
mono-color or simple-mana newcomer — record it as the default, not a per-seat discovery.
