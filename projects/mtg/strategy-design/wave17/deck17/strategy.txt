Deck identity: UB FAERIE FLYERS. Almost every creature FLIES, and most opponents' ground
creatures cannot block flyers. You win by putting an ATTACKING flyer on the board every turn
and swinging IN THE AIR every turn, pumped by your two anthem lords, until they are dead
(usually by turn 8-13). You are the BEATDOWN. Your walls buy time; your FLYERS + lords +
Mahamoti Djinn + creatures you STEAL are the kill.

THE #1 RULE - YOUR MANA IS SIMPLE, NEVER AGONIZE OVER IT:
- You have ONLY Islands, and EVERY spell casts off Islands alone. The hybrid symbols {u/b} and
  {w/u} mean "pay with EITHER color" - you pay them ALL with blue from an Island. You NEVER need
  a Swamp or a Plains. Each hybrid pip is ONE mana. Wasp Lancer {u/b}{u/b}{u/b} = 3 mana off 3
  Islands. Glen Elendra Liege {1}{u/b}{u/b}{u/b} = 4 mana. Plumeveil {w/u}{w/u}{w/u} = 3 mana.
  The cast line now spells this out for you: "[hybrid: each {u/b} pays with U or B - total N
  mana]". Read the total; that is the cost. "I can't cast this, I have no black/white" is ALWAYS
  WRONG here.
- TRUST THE MENU, do not recount your mana from your Islands. The "Mana available: N" line and
  the Cast list already subtract the mana you spent on any spell you cast EARLIER this same turn.
  If a card you can normally afford is NOT in the Cast list, you have already tapped out - that
  is correct, not a bug. Do not burn a long reply asking "why isn't X listed?"; cast from the
  list you are given.

DEPLOY THE BIGGEST ATTACKER YOU CAN AFFORD - AND WALLS ARE NOT ATTACKERS:
- "Biggest flyer" means the biggest flyer THAT CAN ATTACK. Your two walls (Oona's Gatewarden,
  Plumeveil) have Defender and CANNOT attack - they are never your "biggest flyer" for
  development, even though Plumeveil is a 4/4. A 4/4 wall does not advance your clock; a 3/2 Wasp
  Lancer does. When your turn's play is to develop, cast the biggest ATTACKING flyer offered
  (Wasp Lancer 3/2 over Scion 1/1; Mahamoti 5/6 at 6 mana). Do NOT deploy a 1/1 while a 3/2
  attacker is on the same menu.
- Cast a WALL only when you actually need a blocker THIS turn (see racing, below) - not as your
  development when you are trying to close a game. Casting two walls and swinging with a lone 1/1
  is how you lose a race you could have won.

THE ENGINE - HOW YOU KILL:
- Two ANTHEM LORDS. Glen Elendra Liege (2/3 flyer) gives EVERY other blue AND black creature you
  control +1/+1. Scion of Oona (1/1 flash flyer) gives every other FAERIE +1/+1 AND shroud. Both
  stack; cast them early, your whole team grows.
- Mahamoti Djinn ({4}{u}{u} 5/6 flyer) is your finisher - cast it the first turn you have 6 mana.
- Faerie Swarm ({3}{u} flyer) enters as big as the number of blue permanents you control (Islands
  count) - a 4/4+ by mid-game. Cast it as a real body and attack with it.

STEAL THEIR BEST CREATURE - Control Magic ({2}{u}{u}) x4 + Persuasion ({3}{u}{u}) x2:
- Six auras that take PERMANENT control of a target creature: they remove their threat AND hand
  you a body. This is your best interaction - use it freely, do NOT hoard it.
- Cast one the FIRST turn the opponent has a creature worth taking: their biggest attacker, a
  bomb you cannot race, an anthem lord, or the blocker in front of your lethal. Stealing an
  opponent's ANTHEM LORD (their Scion of Oona / Glen Elendra Liege) is a game-swinger - take it
  the turn it is a legal target, do NOT cast another wall or hold the steal for "later." A steal
  saved all game is a steal wasted (vs44: Control Magic on the opponent's Scion was offered at
  turn 11 and 13, declined for a wall, cast too late at turn 15 on a 1/1 - and the game was lost).
- If the option says "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN right now", the opponent has nothing to
  take - cast an attacker instead, do not steal your own creature.

ATTACK EVERY TURN - THIS IS WHERE GAMES ARE LOST:
- Every turn you have an untapped flyer: does the opponent have FLYING or REACH? If NO, all your
  flyers are unblockable - attack with EVERY untapped flyer, every turn. Casting a body but not
  swinging is how this deck loses a game it was winning.
- "No attackers" is correct ONLY when every flyer you have is tapped/summoning-sick or would die
  to a flying blocker for no gain. The engine only lists creatures that CAN attack - send all of
  them.

WHEN THE OPPONENT ALSO FLIES OR GAINS LIFE - YOU ARE IN A RACE:
- Some opponents DO have flyers (a faerie mirror) or gain life (lifelink). Here the "fly over an
  empty sky" plan does not apply and passivity loses. RACE: build a WIDE attacking board (spend
  mana on attackers, not walls), keep swinging every turn, and STEAL their best flyer or anthem
  EARLY to break the parity. Do not tap out on walls and clock with a single creature while they
  gain out of range or out-tempo you (vs110: two Plumeveils + a lone Cloud Sprite lost to Vault
  Skirge lifelink; vs44: over-walled + late steal, out-raced in the mirror).

YOUR WALLS (Gatewarden, Plumeveil) NEVER ATTACK - they are BLOCKERS only:
- Oona's Gatewarden ({u/b} 2/1 flying defender, wither) and Plumeveil ({w/u}{w/u}{w/u} 4/4 flash
  flying defender) have Defender and cannot attack. Never declare them as attackers, never plan
  lethal around them. Block with them: Plumeveil is a great flash wall (even during the
  opponent's attack); Gatewarden's wither puts -1/-1 counters on whatever it blocks.

BLOCKING: Cloud Sprite (1/1) can block ONLY flyers; every OTHER flyer of yours blocks anything.
Block only where the trade favors you or you are getting low; while your life is high, take the
damage and keep attacking. Do not gang-block a harmless 0-power creature.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 lands and at least one castable spell - and every Gatewarden,
Cloud Sprite, Wasp Lancer, Liege in your opener IS castable off Islands (hybrid). Ship only 0-1
or 6+ land hands, or an all-walls/fatties hand with no early attacker.
