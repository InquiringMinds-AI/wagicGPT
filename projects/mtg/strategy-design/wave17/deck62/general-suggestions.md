# deck62 general suggestions (wave 17)

1. **Target-annotation label is a WIN — keep and generalize.** The new menu label "Cast X - the
   only legal targets are YOUR OWN right now" (and the "legal targets right now: <names>" variant)
   materially killed deck62's #1 guideless failure (enchant-the-opponent gift): the model now
   picks own creatures every time. This engine-side steering is doing work the guide used to carry
   alone. Recommend the same own-vs-opponent annotation stay on for every targeted aura/pump across
   the corpus — it converts a deck-knowledge burden into a representation the model reads directly.

2. **The PLAN stale-intent caveat is doing its job here too.** "(note: the actions your plan names
   are no longer among the options available right now...)" appears at deck62 seams (e.g. vs135 s17)
   and precedes correct re-derivation. Consistent with the corpus-wide unparsed halving. No change.

3. **First-guide validation is the right lens — do NOT chase the 4/6.** Two of deck62's four wins
   were close/adjudicated and its two losses were matchup floors (mono-flyer tempo, 8-turn affinity)
   that no guide prose fixes. The guide's real result is the tax collapse; keep grading these seats
   on tax + class-death, per Step 0-ter, and resist reading win-rate swings as guide quality.

4. **Blanchwood is a general "printed-P/T distrust" exemplar.** Any aura/anthem whose bonus is
   COMPUTED ("+1/+1 per Forest", "+2/+2 per other enchantment") triggers the model to recount from
   sources even when the final [X/Y] is printed. If more decks show this, a corpus-wide
   representation nudge (annotate computed P/T as final) beats per-deck trust-lines. Held as N3.
