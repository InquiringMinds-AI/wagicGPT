# deck35 → strategy-writing skill: proposed deltas

Mostly CONFIRMATIONS of existing Step 0-bis rungs from a third/fourth independent guideless seat,
plus one concrete new reviewer instrument. Nothing here touches the core.

## Confirmations (guideless-first-pass doctrine held)
- **Rule (d) COUNTERMAND a generic core line — reconfirmed, now the dominant lever again.** deck35's
  game-losing class is the core's generic "preserve life / trade favorably / block the threat"
  reflex actively taxing an aggro swarm. Like deck21 (hold-instant override) and deck17, the #1
  guide rule had to be an OVERRIDE of a core instinct, not a fact-add. Rule (d) now has 3-seat
  support.
- **Rule (e) write the ATTACK/DEPLOY FLOOR on the first pass — reconfirmed (4th newcomer seat).**
  The attack-floor-does-not-transfer class recurred yet again; a guideless aggro deck WILL durdle
  unless the first guide forbids it. Do not defer to a revision.
- **Rule (a) reasoning-tax = saliency — reconfirmed and sharpened.** Every multi-thousand-char
  reply clustered at ONE seam (blocking combat-math), and that seam is the same one the deck should
  mostly be declining. Fixing the strategic instinct (rarely block) removes the misplay AND the tax
  together — the tax pointed straight at the load-bearing rule.
- **deck62 S4 "trust the rendered number, don't recompute the stack" — CONVERGES here (now 3-seat:
  deck17 mana-line, deck62 aura stack, deck35 lord stack).** Strong candidate to graduate from an
  engine-deck instantiation to a general Step 0-bis guide-authoring line: any deck whose board
  math is derived (auras, lords, anthems, counters) gets a "trust the rendered [X/Y]/keyword tags"
  line paired with the representation ledger.

## New reviewer instrument (proposed sub-lesson under Step 0-ter/review-discipline)
**For an AGGRO/aggressive deck, the durdle failure has a countable SIGNATURE: games with ZERO
`attackers` records (and/or an empty own-battlefield despite many creatures cast).** deck35's two
clean losses had 0 attackers records and ended with the opponent at 20 life; its wins had 4-5. A
reviewer should tabulate `attackers`-decision COUNT per game and the own-creature count over time
for any deck the guide labels "attack every turn" — a 0-attacker game is proof the durdle fired,
independent of the win/loss and cheaper to read than the prose. Sibling to deck17's
"reconcile looks-disobeyed against the eligible set": here the eligible set was empty *because the
model chumped it away*, so also check WHY the attack step was never reached (traded-away vs
tapped vs summoning-sick).

## Buff-math nuance (report the ACTION, not the arithmetic or the verbal model)
deck35 computed team power correctly off the rendered line (8/11/12) WHILE stating a wrong rule
("lords buff future slivers only"). Lesson for the "does the model understand X" watch: **a correct
number does not prove comprehension and a wrong verbal statement does not prove a misunderstanding —
score the ACTION taken.** Here the action was right (buff-math verdict PASS) despite the wrong
verbal model; the guide addresses the verbal tic cheaply without a representation change. This
extends method-headline-3's "read RESULT not INTENT" to the comprehension-watch case.
