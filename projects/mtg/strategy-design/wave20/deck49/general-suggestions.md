## G-49a (carry, ESCALATED) — reasoning-tax spiral CROSSED the token cap -> truncation-unparse (CORE)
Wave-19: 13/142 replies >=600 words, ALL parsed (0 fallbacks). Wave-20: the SAME class (combat/
card-value + stale-plan spirals) crossed the 2048-ish token cap at the develop-fork seam and produced
3 TRUNCATED unparses (deck27 decode-repetition loop; deck62 progressing spiral; both ~260-300 lines).
This is the wave's key CORE signal: the tax did not grow in frequency, it grew in CONSEQUENCE — a
verbose-but-parsed spiral became an unparsed truncation once it hit a fork the guide did not
adjudicate. Two general-prompt/decode levers (both CORE, cross-deck — I only propose, synthesis
decides):
  (a) A COMMIT brake in the general prompt: "once you have weighed both sides of a choice, state your
     CHOICE line — do not re-derive the same two options again." The deck49 guide now carries a
     deck-local version at its specific seam, but the tendency is universal (seen at other seats' giant
     replies). This is the strongest anti-truncation lever and belongs in core, not per-deck.
  (b) A decode-time REPETITION guard: deck27's tail repeats one paragraph ~10x verbatim. A repetition
     penalty / early-stop on n-gram repeat would end the loop before the cap and could be salvaged.
     This is a decode/harness item (route to notes), NOT a prompt change.
Do not double-layer: the deck49 guide dissolves the TRIGGER (the fork); (a)+(b) address the LOOP
itself. Both are needed; neither substitutes for the other.

## G-49b (NEW, MEDIUM) — the develop-seam fork is a CROSS-DECK shape, not deck49-specific
The "deploy a body vs throw removal/burn at the face, no tiebreak, model paralyzes" fork will recur in
any aggro-with-reach deck (Zombies, Goblins, burn). deck49's fix (tighten the over-broad exception +
"either is fine, commit") is a template. Flagged for synthesis as a candidate general method: when an
aggro guide states BOTH "deploy every turn" and "burn goes face," add an explicit early-turn tiebreak
so the two do not compete into a truncation. Corroborates other seats' giant-reply pockets.
