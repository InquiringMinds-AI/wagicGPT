# Skill proposal (wave 24, deck59 — guideless Step-0 on a persist/recursion deck)

## PROPOSAL — a SIXTH idiosyncrasy face for rule 3 (Step-0-bis): the SYMMETRIC EFFECT that is ASYMMETRIC IN YOUR FAVOR via recursion (the under-fired inverse of the deck102 face). Tag: single-seat, pending a second recursion/symmetric-payoff deck.

Rule 3's face list currently ends at FIVE (belief-that-VETOES 17/21; card-text
FABRICATION 62; target-SUB-MENU seam 49; SYMMETRIC-cost card read as one-sided 102;
alternative-cost dual-option 137). deck59 adds a distinct sixth shape that is the clean
INVERSE of the deck102 (fourth) face:

- **deck102 (fourth face):** a symmetric card (Pox/Smallpox) the pilot OVER-fires because
  it reads the self-cost as if the card were one-sided disruption. The number's SIGN is
  ignored; the fix is a scoped FIRE-GATE that stops it firing when the symmetry hurts you.
- **deck59 (this proposal):** a symmetric MASS-REMOVAL (Wrath of God / Day of Judgment /
  Damnation) that the pilot UNDER-fires because it does NOT see that the deck's own
  recursion (persist) makes the symmetric effect ONE-SIDED IN ITS FAVOR — your creatures
  come back, the opponent's do not. The pilot treats the sweeper as generic "slow removal"
  and declines it in exactly the spot it wins the game (deck109 s12: at 6 life facing
  lethal, "Day of Judgment is too slow and risky," played a blocker instead, swept only at
  2 life — too late — and lost). The fix is the MIRROR of the deck102 gate: a scoped
  FIRE-GATE that TELLS the pilot to fire the symmetric effect WHEN a board-readable
  condition holds (behind on board / being raced / opponent gone wide), plus the WHY (your
  board recurs), and the closed exception (NEVER when ahead — you would kill your own
  attackers).

**Why it is its own face, not just "teach the card":** the two errors share a mechanism —
the pilot mis-reads a SYMMETRIC card as if the symmetry did not apply to the side it helps
or hurts — but they need OPPOSITE guide levers (a stop-gate vs a go-gate), and the go-gate
must carry the recursion WHY or the weak executor keeps pattern-matching "board wipe = slow
/ kills my stuff = bad." A bare capability line ("Wrath destroys all creatures") does NOT
countermand the reflex; only "your creatures COME BACK, so the wipe is one-sided FOR YOU,
cast it when behind" does. This is also the rule-4 "COUNTERMAND a core reflex" move applied
to combat math: the core's correct-in-general "don't overcommit into a sweeper / a wipe
kills your board" prior is anti-teaching for a deck whose board survives its own wipes.

**Authoring template (matches the annotate/teach, never-suppress doctrine):** rule #1 stays
the capability table; add a scoped rule keyed to a board-readable trigger — "when [behind /
raced / opponent wide], cast the symmetric effect NOW because YOUR side recovers and theirs
does not; NEVER when ahead." Pair with a representation note (the block-outcome annotation
should mark that a persist blocker returns — see deck59 notes.md) so the recursion value is
visible at the combat seam too.

Promote from single-seat to a named rule-3 face if a second deck whose OWN engine inverts a
symmetric effect (another recursion/persist deck, an undying deck, a "you gain what they
lose" symmetric payoff) reproduces the UNDER-fire. Until then: tagged single-seat, encoded
in deck59's guide rule #2.
