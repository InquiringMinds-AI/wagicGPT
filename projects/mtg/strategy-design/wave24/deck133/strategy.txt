Deck identity: mono-black DEVOTION MIDRANGE. You win by casting a black creature
every turn, removing what races you, and finishing with Gray Merchant of
Asphodel's life drain - the drain is your only damage that ignores blockers and
flyers, AND it gains you that much life, so it is both your finisher and your
panic button. Half your games you win by BEATS instead (recurring Bloodghasts +
Phyrexian Obliterator) when Gray Merchant does not arrive - that is normal. Vs a
control/counter deck the beats ARE the win: discard buys the tempo, Phyrexian
Obliterator closes it, and Gray Merchant can still be countered by a topdeck - so
keep attacking, do not wait for the drain.

CAST SOMETHING EVERY TURN, BUT RANK BY IMPACT - YOU ALWAYS HAVE THE MANA. Every
cast the list offers is already paid for; the game taps your lands automatically.
Each turn, take the highest thing on this list that is offered:
  1. Gray Merchant when it wins OR saves you (see its block below). If it does
     NEITHER right now (the shown drain N is less than the opponent's life AND
     your own life is above 10), do NOT cast him yet - take a creature/Obliterator
     from lower on this list to build devotion; a bigger drain a turn later wins
     more, and a small Merchant into an open-mana control deck just gets countered.
  2. Remove a creature that is attacking you (Fatal Push; Collective Brutality
     -2/-2; Liliana of the Veil -2).
  3. Cast a creature/threat (Obliterator, Geralf's Messenger, Bloodghast).
  4. Inquisition of Kozilek - FREE, cast it whenever it is your best remaining
     play to strip a threat or a burn spell.
  5. Thoughtseize - costs YOU 2 life. Cast it only turns 1-3, or later ONLY to
     take a specific card you can SEE will kill you (a sweeper, a burn spell, a
     bomb). If the opponent's hand shows 0 cards, or holds nothing that threatens
     you, DO NOT cast Thoughtseize - its 2 life is a race you lose. Prefer
     Inquisition or a creature.
Choose FROM THE NUMBERED LIST ONLY: before you write a plan, confirm the card
you name appears in the list. If it does not, cast the best card that IS listed.

ROLE CHECK - run it every turn, straight off the board and life totals:
- BEATDOWN (their board is empty, or your creatures are bigger): deploy the
  curve below and attack with everything.
- DEFENDER (your life is falling while theirs is not, or they have flyers or an
  unkillable creature you cannot block): your job is REMOVAL + WALL + DRAIN, the
  line that won you the Faerie matchup AND the red-burn matchup last run:
  1. Kill their attacker: Fatal Push, or Collective Brutality's "creature gets
     -2/-2" mode, or Liliana of the Veil -2.
  2. Cast PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR (5/5 trample) and BLOCK with it - damaging it
     forces that player to sacrifice that many permanents, so it eats their
     attacker AND their board. It is your wall.
  3. Protect your life: play a Swamp and a creature instead of Thoughtseize
     (2 life); skip Phyrexian Arena (1 life/turn); crack a fetch only on a turn
     you need the land.
  4. Close - or stabilize - with the Gray Merchant drain (no attack needed).
  You still cast a creature every turn in BOTH roles.

VS RED/BURN AGGRO (Pillar of Flame, Searing Spear, Legion Loyalist, Goblins):
strip their BURN with your turn-1/2 discard first (a burn spell taken is damage
removed from YOUR face), Fatal Push their haste creature, use Collective
Brutality's -2/-2 mode as removal on their attacker, then block with Geralf's
Messenger (undying = a repeatable wall) and go WIDE with your recurring
Bloodghasts. You do not need Gray Merchant to win this - beats close it. If they
go WIDE with many small creatures (a Goblin swarm), discard barely helps: your
answer is PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR as a wall and Collective Brutality -2/-2, not
more discard - land a body fast, an edict only removes one of them.

GRAY MERCHANT OF ASPHODEL {3}{B}{B}: he is your finisher AND your stabilizer -
you gain the life the opponent loses. The option shows "{right now: drains N}" -
that N is the FINAL drain: it ALREADY counts Gray Merchant's own devotion. Use
that shown number DIRECTLY - do NOT add anything to it, and do NOT try to count
your own devotion by hand, you will miscount; the option already did the counting
for you.
EVERY turn he is in the list, cast him NOW if EITHER holds:
  - N >= the opponent's life  ->  you win this turn, cast him and win.
  - your own life is 10 or less  ->  you gain N life; cast him to survive
    (do NOT spend that mana on removal instead - stabilizing beats one kill).
If NEITHER holds, WAIT (impact-list rule 1) - cast a creature instead and drain
bigger later. Liliana, the Last Hope -2 returns a dead Gray Merchant to hand -
recast for a second drain (you did exactly this last run).

DEPLOY CURVE (substitute the closest creature you hold):
- Turn 1: land + Thoughtseize or Inquisition of Kozilek.
- Turn 2: land + Bloodghast.
- Turn 3: land + Geralf's Messenger (enters, drains 2; undying returns it bigger).
- Turn 4: land + Phyrexian Obliterator.
- Turn 5+: keep casting a threat every turn by IMPACT (Obliterator, Geralf's,
  Bloodghast, Yawgmoth). Cast Gray Merchant only when he wins or saves you.
Two spells in one turn is GOOD when the list offers both.

BLOODGHAST {B}{B} (2/1): cast it from hand - landfall returns it only from the
GRAVEYARD, free off every land you play once it has died. It CANNOT BLOCK - as
defender its jobs are +2 devotion for the drain, Yawgmoth fodder, Collective
Brutality escalate fuel, and the BEST card to give up to a forced sacrifice or
discard (an edict, Cruel Edict, a forced-discard prompt) - it returns from the
graveyard on your next land, so when the game FORCES you to lose a card, a
Bloodghast is the one you can always best afford. It gains haste when the
opponent is at 10 or less.
Attack with it when you are the beatdown; as defender, an attack that just trades
into a blocker can wait - Gray Merchant does that damage through the air.

FATAL PUSH (instant, {B}): destroys a creature of mana value 2 or less - 4 or
less if a permanent of yours left play this turn (cracked fetch, dead Bloodghast).
Fire it at the creature actually attacking you, not a harmless 0/2 or a mana dork.
It cannot hit a creature of mana value 3+ unless your revolt is on, so crack a
fetch first if you need the bigger kill. Holding Push never stops your creature cast.

COLLECTIVE BRUTALITY {1}{B}: has a "target creature gets -2/-2" mode - that is
your creature REMOVAL, use it on their attacker when you are defending or racing
aggro (it kills toughness-2 creatures and shrinks bigger ones for the block).
Other modes: take an instant/sorcery from their hand, or "opponent loses 2, you
gain 2". Escalate by discarding Bloodghast - your next land returns it.

LILIANA OF THE VEIL +1 (each player discards a card): this is SYMMETRIC - it
empties YOUR hand too. Use +1 only when the opponent holds MORE cards than you,
or your own hand is already dead cards you cannot cast. When you are the DEFENDER
being beaten down, +1 throws away your OWN answers (removal, Gray Merchant) into
your own discard - prefer the -2 edict below, or just hold Liliana and tick her
up later. Do not +1 yourself out of the cards that were going to save you.
LILIANA OF THE VEIL -2: this is a SACRIFICE (edict), not targeted - the opponent
picks which creature dies, so it is best when they have ONE creature (their
attacker). It removes only ONE creature, so it is weak against a wide token board.
Because it is a sacrifice, it kills creatures you cannot target - including
protection-from-black or hexproof creatures like Etched Champion.

FETCHLANDS (Polluted Delta, Bloodstained Mire, Verdant Catacombs, Marsh Flats):
crack the moment "Put in Play... targeting Snow-Covered Swamp" appears - the 1
life is the card working, and it turns Fatal Push's revolt on. When defending and
low on life, crack only on a turn you actually need the land.

YAWGMOTH: cast with another creature out, then each turn: pay 1 life, sacrifice
Bloodghast or Geralf's Messenger (both come back), -1/-1 counter on THEIR
creature, draw a card. You piloted this perfectly last run - keep it.
GERALF'S MESSENGER: undying returns it bigger and drains 2 again.
PHYREXIAN ARENA: 1 life every upkeep for a card - skip casting it when defending.

ATTACK with every untapped creature when you are the beatdown. Obliterator
(5/5 trample) attacks AND blocks safely - no one fights it profitably.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with a creature, or a discard spell AND a creature.
Ship 0-1 land hands, 6+ land hands, and hands that are all discard spells with no
creature - discard alone never wins, and a hand of only discard + one land is a
ship against fast aggro.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> play):
- Your life is falling while theirs holds -> you are the DEFENDER: remove their
  attacker and land Obliterator; stop casting discard.
- You are at 10 or less and Gray Merchant is listed -> cast him to gain N life,
  not removal.
- The opponent's hand shows 0 cards (or nothing that kills you) -> cast a creature
  or Inquisition, NOT Thoughtseize; its 2 life is not worth an empty hand.
- Gray Merchant is listed but N < their life and your life is above 10 -> cast a
  creature to build devotion; drain bigger next turn.
- Their creature has protection from black / is unkillable by Push -> Liliana of
  the Veil -2 (a sacrifice) removes it; or block/trade around it.
- They flood the board with small creatures -> Phyrexian Obliterator as a wall and
  Collective Brutality -2/-2, not more discard; an edict removes only one.
- N >= their life on the Gray Merchant option -> cast him, you win now.
