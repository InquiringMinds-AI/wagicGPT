# Wave-42 deploy-ready guide edits (consolidated verbatim from the six seat reports)

## deck162 (seat 126+162)
D2 — replace the MotF carve-out paragraph in Rule 2 with:
ONE exception to the brake, and only this one: MASTER OF THE FEAST is a 5/5 flying body as
well as a gift, and it is the only card behind this brake that defends you. With NO punisher
out, cast it whenever the opponent's battlefield line shows a creature - the body is worth the
one card you hand over. It still sits behind Rule 1, behind Shield Sphere and behind Fog Bank.
Everything else - Dictate, Puzzle Box, Forced Fruition, extra Mines - waits behind the brake at
every life total, with a creature on their board or without one.

D3 — replace the last sentence of the ATTACKING section:
MASTER OF THE FEAST attacks only when you can spare it. Before you send it, read the opponent's
battlefield line: if they control any creature and your walls do not already cover every one of
them, MASTER OF THE FEAST STAYS HOME - an attacking Master is tapped on their turn and blocks
nothing, and a 5/5 blocker erases more damage than 5 unblocked damage buys you. Send it when
their board shows no creature, when your walls already cover every attacker they have, or when
the lethal check clears.

D4 — add after "The one time it goes: ..." in the ATTACKING section:
Never send Fate Unraveler into a "their untapped blockers" tag that reads "your attacker dies,
their blocker lives". That tag is the engine's own computed result - do not argue with it. Fate
Unraveler is a punisher first: dead, it stops the draw damage that wins you the game. If the
lethal check has not cleared and that tag is on its line, the answer is ATTACK: none.

D5 — replace Rule 4 rung 2:
2. A line ending "(neither dies)" - take that block. One wall per attacker, spending your walls
   on the attackers with the biggest "deals N" numbers first. A second wall added to an attacker
   that is already blocked stops nothing extra and wears your walls out - never stack two walls
   on one attacker while another attacker is going unblocked.

## deck126 (seat 126+162)
D6 — replace the "Two surviving lines are equal; take either." sentence in the Rule-5
assignment paragraph with:
When two or more attackers all let that blocker live, take the one with the BIGGEST "deals N"
number on its A-line. The life gain is the same either way, so the only thing left to decide is
how much damage you stop - stop the bigger number.

D7 — replace the all-dying fallback sentence ("Only when every attacker offered...") with:
Only when every attacker offered to that blocker kills it do you put it on the attacker with the
biggest "deals N" number on its A-line - read the number off the line, do not judge by name, by
size in words, or by which one you blocked last turn.

D8 — add after Rule 6's "attack with every Vampire offered, every turn" sentence:
ONE stop, and read it off your own narration: if the last time you attacked it says your attacker
took damage and died before combat damage - "<something> dealt N damage to Vampire" then "Your
Vampire died" - the opponent controls a permanent that kills anything you send, and the next
Vampire dies to it exactly the same way. Answer ATTACK: none and keep the Vampire on your
battlefield instead: it is a lifelink blocker, and with both enchantments out one block wins the
game outright. Attack again only once that permanent is gone from their battlefield line.

## deck123 (seat 123) — EDITs A-H
A — replace RULE 1's header through "Each token is a free body. There is no cost to weigh."
(DELETE that sentence) with:
=== RULE 1 - THE TOKEN LINES, AND WHEN TO STOP TAKING THEM ===
These are the three lines, exactly as you will see them. A second copy of the same card adds a
handle - "Thraben Doomsayer #1", "Thraben Doomsayer #2" - same line, same rule:
  "Create human with Thraben Doomsayer [cost: Tap]"
  "Create vampire with Bloodline Keeper [cost: Tap]"
  "Create vampire with Lord of Lineage [cost: Tap]"
BEFORE you take one, read your creature count. Use the FIRST source that is on the screen:
  1. the option line's own note, "[repeat: ... you control M creatures]" - M is your count.
  2. no such note on the line? Then your battlefield line: "Your battlefield (K permanents
     listed, of which M are creatures)" - that M is your count, printed in every window.
     When the count is one it reads "of which 1 is a creature".
The note belongs to ONE COPY and ONE TURN: each copy's first two activations of a turn carry no
note, so with two makers out four activations a turn have none. The battlefield line always does.
Then, and only then:
  - M is 11 or less: take the line. Take it again every time it comes back.
  - M is 12 or more: answer 0 (pass). Take nothing from this list, this window.
The 12 counts creatures ON YOUR BATTLEFIELD. It does not reset when a new turn starts, it does
not reset when the "[repeat:]" note disappears, and "I have tapped it 0 times this turn" is not
a reason to start again. Twelve creatures already standing is the whole of the reason to stop.
ONE exception: if the opponent has attackers on the board whose damage would take you to 0 this
turn, keep making tokens past 12 - each new one is another blocker, and a board you never get
to use is worth nothing.

B — replace the first two DECIDING SITUATIONS bullets with:
- A line ending in "[cost: Tap]" is in your list AND your creature count is 11 or less: take it,
  this window, whatever your mana says, on either player's turn. Count 12 or more: answer 0.
- You just took that line and it appears again: read the count again first. 11 or less, take it
  again. 12 or more, stop - and it stays stopped on later turns and after the "[repeat:]" note
  has gone.

C — replace the wave-41 EDIT-7 paragraph ("One exception to \"there is no cost\"...") with:
Timing: the tokens you make CANNOT attack until your next turn, so making them early in your own
turn buys you nothing this turn and leaves them standing through the opponent's whole turn. Fire
the chain on the OPPONENT'S turn - the lines are offered there, in their upkeep, their main phase
and their attack step. A token made then blocks that same turn and attacks on your very next turn,
and a sweeper cast on their turn catches fewer of them.
Fire on your OWN turn only when the creatures you are about to attack with are already on the
board, or when the opponent has no untapped land left.

D — append to KEY CARDS, Bloodline Keeper:
  Transforming needs {B} and five or more Vampires. Casting the Keeper off exactly four lands
  taps you out, and Intruder Alarm untaps CREATURES, not lands - so the "Transform:backside with
  Bloodline Keeper [cost: {b}]" line will not appear again that turn. When you can cast the
  Keeper and still leave one black source untapped, do it: Lord of Lineage gives every OTHER
  Vampire +2/+2, which turns eleven 2/2 tokens into eleven 4/4s.

E — insert in RULE 5 immediately after the "Cast Devour Flesh {1}{b}" paragraph:
Only a creature ON THE BATTLEFIELD can be sacrificed. A creature spell listed under "ON THE
STACK, waiting to resolve" is not there yet - cast an edict at it and the opponent sacrifices
nothing and you have thrown the card away. Read the opponent's battlefield line: if it says "of
which 0 are creatures", no edict does anything, whatever is on the stack.
Never cast an edict when the opponent's life is at or below the life it gives them. Killing
their last creature and moving them from 1 life to 4 costs you a game you had already won.

F — replace the "Both cards let the SACRIFICING player pick, so against two or more..." text:
Both cards let the SACRIFICING player pick, so with TWO OR MORE of their creatures listed no
edict is ever the play: they hand you their worst. A board of two Fate Unravelers (3/4) and a
Fog Bank (0/2 defender) gives up the Fog Bank and gains 2 life, and both Unravelers stay. Count
the number printed after "of which" on the OPPONENT'S battlefield line. If it is not exactly 1,
take Tragic Slip, Damnation or a token activation instead.

G — replace COMBAT blocking's second bullet:
 - Add up the damage of every attacker with no blocker on it. If that total is your life total
   minus 4 or more, put a blocker on EVERY attacker - biggest attacker first, Thraben Doomsayer
   and Bloodline Keeper included - and keep assigning until no attacker is left unblocked. A
   token-maker you keep alive at 1 life makes tokens for a game you already lost.

H — add under COMBAT after "With no such permanent on their board, send everything.":
One more hold-back: do not send a lone Thraben Doomsayer or Bloodline Keeper into a board that
can block and kill it. The maker is the engine; your tokens are the attack. Send it once you
have tokens attacking beside it.
