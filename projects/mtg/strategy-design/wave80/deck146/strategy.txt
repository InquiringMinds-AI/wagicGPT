YOUR DECK: Orzhov dungeon MIDRANGE - removal, bodies that VENTURE, two planeswalkers, a manland.
BEATDOWN vs slow decks, CONTROL vs aggro.
ENGINE, in order: a land every turn -> a cheap body or removal on curve -> a walker you can pay for
-> venture whenever a venture body attacks -> close with attacks, Kaya's -3, Lolth's Spiders and the
drains.

THE WHOLE CAST ORDER. At every "which card do you cast now" ask on your turn, read the entries top
to bottom and take the FIRST that matches; stop there. Never sit on removal.
 1. Removal (Vanishing Verse, Soul Shatter) when their line shows a creature or walker you can hit -
    for Verse, only when its target list names one of THEIRS with power above 0 and no "[defender]";
    Soul Shatter leaves this entry on "at 0 this does nothing" or "tied at MV 0" - tokens, and their
    real threats stay.
 2. LOLTH or KAYA if listed. THE COUNTER TEST FIRST, off two printed facts: their graveyard line or
    the log names a card of theirs that counters spells, AND "Their untapped sources" reads 2 or
    more -> SKIP THIS ENTRY THIS WINDOW; take the cheapest entries below that leave sources up, and
    take her on a later menu whose row leaves sources for a second row you also take. Past that test
    - and a "[legendary: ...]" row only under the loyalty test in PLANESWALKERS - cast her NOW, AND
    USE A LOYALTY ABILITY THE SAME TURN: a walker is never summoning sick and never enters tapped,
    so one is legal at once.
 3. Nadaar, Barrowin or Triumphant Adventurer - a body that ventures.
 4. Silverquill Silencer, when no entry-3 body is listed.
 5. Silverquill Command, when its "{modes live right now: ...; dead ...}" clause names a mode
    you want BEFORE the semicolon - at 0 creatures of your own, "return creature" standing there
    is that mode.
 6. Emeria's Call, when listed.
 7. Pelakka Predation, when their hand size is 3 or more.
 8. Acererak; 8b. Silverquill Command with nothing live before the semicolon (a card for 1 life).
 9. "Cast nothing right now".
Entry 9 is the LAST line, not a tiebreaker: unless the engine marked a row dead ("at 0 this does
nothing"), the reason a row does not match must be a fact on THIS screen. Take the ask again if
offered - more than one spell a turn is legal.

SPELLS
- VANISHING VERSE ({W}{B}, instant): exiles ONE MONOCOLORED permanent cleanly, no death trigger.
  ITS TARGET LIST IS THE ONLY TRUTH - fire at the best name on "legal targets right now: X, Y";
  "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN" -> do not cast it. A "[defender]" or a power of 0
  never attacks you: NEVER SPEND VERSE ON ONE. It is this deck's only answer to a monocoloured
  ENCHANTMENT or ARTIFACT, and those beat you.
- SOUL SHATTER ({2}{B}, instant): they SACRIFICE their highest-mana-value creature or walker. It
  does not target, so it answers hexproof, indestructible and artifact creatures Verse cannot. THE
  ROW NAMES WHAT DIES ("{right now: they sacrifice X (MV n, their highest)}"); N tied names is the
  list they choose ONE from. A NAMED PERMANENT IS A REAL KILL AT ANY MANA VALUE, 0 included; only
  "at 0 this does nothing" takes it off the list.
- KAYA THE INEXORABLE ({3}{W}{B}): her -3 EXILES ANY nonland permanent - multicolored, colorless,
  artifact, planeswalker - what Verse cannot touch. SHE ENTERS AT 5 AND DIES AT 0: each -3 spends three
  of five and can cost the second -3 this deck otherwise never gets.
  THE -3 LADDER, top first, stopping at the first name the list offers: a PLANESWALKER of theirs -
  nothing else here answers one; a noncreature permanent of theirs making cards, damage or life
  every turn; the body the CRACK-BACK line prices at 4 OR MORE - "N from 1 attacker nothing you can
  legally block" first, else the top of their power line, flier or trampler first. EVERY OTHER BOARD
  IS THE +1, one naming nothing on that ladder included: a "[defender]", a power of 0, a lord, an
  anthem, a token, a body priced at 3 or less. She keeps the counters and takes a real name the turn
  one lands. ONE LINE OUTRANKS THAT: the crack-back line says the attack KILLS you and exiling a
  listed body stops it - then -3 it at any size. Every row on her -3 list reading
  "[your battlefield]" -> take the +1 or pass.
- SILVERQUILL COMMAND ({2}{W}{B}, SORCERY - your main phases only): choose TWO of four modes.
  * The CAST ROW's live/dead clause is the truth about which modes exist; the mode ask re-lists dead
    pairs UNMARKED, so carry the cast row's dead list in.
  * TIMING: the +3/+3 ends this turn, so want it in combat -> cast in MAIN 1, before attackers;
    "pump now, attack next turn" and any main-2 pump is wrong. After combat, or with nothing able
    to attack, pair sacrifice with draw.
  * TARGET: asked to pick ONE creature, pick YOUR OWN - it gets the +3/+3.
  * DRAW DIRECTION: that player draws AND loses 1. Point it at YOURSELF; at them only to finish.
- SILVERQUILL SILENCER ({W}{B}): it fires only when they CAST the named card, so name one they can
  still cast. Every row prints "{copies not yet in a public zone: N of theirs}"; the list is SORTED
  BY THAT N and that N is the decision. Take the highest N, ties toward a counterspell or a sweeper,
  then the missing half of a pair on their line (Sanguine Bond out -> Exquisite Blood) and anything
  Pelakka showed you; a body last, an N of 0 never. "{visible now: ...}" says where SPENT copies sit
  and never raises a row.
- PELAKKA PREDATION ({2}{B}): a discard of mana value 3 OR MORE only - a bomb, not a curve. ON ITS
  REVEAL LIST take the card that will be CAST: a sweeper ("Destroy all", "Each ... sacrifices") or a
  counterspell ahead of ANY creature, however big - the huge body is one they never reach. Their
  "of which N are creatures" three or more above yours -> not a play: cast the biggest body you can
  afford, or removal for their engine.
- NEVER CAST AGADEEM'S AWAKENING: it returns CREATURE CARDS only, so no X returns Kaya or Lolth and
  the cast taps you out for nothing. It is a land here.

LANDS AND MANA. Shineshadow Snarl taps for {W} OR {B}. If a Cast option is listed you CAN pay for it
- never decline a spell for its colour. PLAY A LAND EVERY TURN: a land entering TAPPED still goes
down - the drop does not carry over. Snarl enters TAPPED unless you reveal a Plains or Swamp; Hive
enters TAPPED once you control two or more other lands.
- A PATHWAY IS ONE CARD WITH TWO LAND FACES (Brightclimb taps {W}, Grimclimb taps {B}) offered as
  two rows on one menu, both entering untapped. TAKE THE FACE MAKING THE COLOUR YOUR HAND IS SHORT
  OF, not the one your carried plan names: read the "[cannot pay now: ...]" brackets and "Mana
  sources among those lands". No black source in play and a {b} spell in hand -> the BLACK face is
  the only right row; the white face is how this deck loses to its own mana.
- EMERIA'S CALL, AGADEEM'S AWAKENING AND PELAKKA PREDATION ARE LANDS (Emeria taps {W}; Agadeem and
  Pelakka Caverns tap {B}); their row "<land> with <spell> -> PLAY THIS AS A LAND" costs no mana.
  Play a real land first and hold the land-backed card; take its land row when the alternative is no
  drop or its spell face is dead. Pelakka Caverns shows no 3-life menu.
- The hand header counts these three TWICE: the LAND number is true, the SPELL number one too high
  per such card. "Mana sources among those lands" counts the back face already: never discard one
  while it is a colour's only source.
- THE 3-LIFE MENU AFTER AN EMERIA OR AGADEEM: ANSWER "tap" BY DEFAULT. Pay only when BOTH hold: (a)
  a card in hand needs exactly one more mana than your untapped sources make, and a casting window
  this turn can still take it; (b) the CRACK-BACK line's damage number is below the N in "{this
  payment puts you at N}", or no CRACK-BACK line is printed. NEVER AT 3 LIFE OR LESS. 3 life is a
  turn under any ping, drain or punisher of theirs, and a source you do not spend THIS turn buys
  nothing: (a) failing ends the row - "an extra source is good anyway" is not a condition.
- HIVE OF THE EYE TYRANT: {3}{B} - FOUR mana - for a 3/3 menace attacker until end of turn. A Hive
  you controlled as the turn began IS NOT SUMMONING SICK: animate in your own main phase and it
  ATTACKS THIS TURN. AFFORDABILITY: FOUR OTHER untapped sources - its own mana never pays for it.
  Before answering "becomes beholder" run these in order, first that fires wins.
  CHECK 0 - Add the power of every untapped creature of yours that could attack, plus 3. At or above
    their printed life -> ANIMATE and swing, at any phase.
  CHECK 0b - Their line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and your line shows 0 able to attack ->
    ANIMATE at any phase (your only clock), unless the row is tagged [this land is TAPPED] or that
    mana pays a cast you make this turn.
  CHECK 1 - "Phase:" says Upkeep and CHECK 0 did not fire -> PASS.
  CHECK 2 - that Hive already carries a (P/T) on your line, or its ROW prints TAPPED in any form ->
    PASS: a tapped Hive becomes a 3/3 that cannot attack or block and the mana is gone.

COMBAT - ATTACKING TAPS YOUR BLOCKERS for their whole turn (Nadaar has vigilance and attacks free).
- BLOCKED DAMAGE IS ZERO, BOTH WAYS: a blocked creature without trample deals the PLAYER nothing -
  your 1/1 in front of their 5/5 stops all 5, a cover line counting N of your bodies
  against N of their attackers is TRUE, and sending fewer attackers than their untapped blockers
  guarantees nothing.
- LETHAL IS THE FIRST CHECK, ABOVE EVERY RULE BELOW: the ATTACK TOTAL line reads "That IS lethal",
  or their printed life is at or below the power you can send -> SEND EVERY BODY LISTED, never a
  chosen few. A blocking trigger that gains them life, a wall, a converter line and a carried plan
  are all under it.
- An A-line printing "[no creature they control can block this attacker]" -> SEND IT, at every life
  total, ahead of every rule below except CHECK A. No tag, wall or converter rule reaches it.
- THEIR WALKER WHEN THE SWING IS NOT LETHAL: ATTACK TOTAL reads "NOT lethal" and one attacker's
  power reaches a listed walker's "[N loyalty left]" -> send that one as A#>W#, first when a DRAW
  PUNISHERS or converter line names it. What it makes EVERY turn beats damage to a life total you
  cannot reach this turn.
- CHECK 0 - "[doesn't untap during its controller's untap step]" taps it for the REST OF THE GAME.
  Exemptions: that tag also contains "vigilance" -> send always; the header reads "They have 0
  untapped creatures able to block" AND an untapped creature of yours remains after the declaration
  -> send those that get through. Else ATTACK: none.
- CHECK A - printed life 8 or less and exactly ONE creature on your line carrying neither "[tapped"
  nor "[summoning sick" -> it does not attack, whatever its tag says, an empty tag included. Nadaar
  is exempt.
- CHECK B - an all-"(neither dies" tag: sending exactly ONE creature -> ATTACK: none (wall damage
  does not carry between turns); with more, send only when your attackers OUTNUMBER their untapped
  blockers, or it has flying or menace. THEY pick which attacker each blocker stops: a surplus
  protects the swing, not a card.
- A tag collapsing several blockers into one line prices ONE blocker; a "GANG BLOCK:" clause prices
  your toughness against the SUM. Such a clause, or a tag counting 2 or more blockers -> that
  creature STAYS HOME if it is a CARD; a dungeon GOBLIN TOKEN goes anyway, free bait. One blocker,
  no such clause -> the parenthesis is the story.
- ANY tag name reading "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" -> that creature stays home while
  the team attacks (tokens still go). Price the WORST name; other names, outnumbering them, a
  friendlier A-line and "[held back ...]" do not cancel it.
- ANY blocker printing "(blocking trigger: they gain N)", "(blocking trigger: they may gain N)" or
  "(lifelink: they gain N)" -> that swing PAYS them: send only what gets through and win with
  removal, Kaya and the drains. A tag line that KILLS one is no exception, and prices ADD across a
  gang block. No such clause -> their walls are free to attack. LETHAL outranks it.
- "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" printed AND any priced clause on a tag ->
  that price comes off YOUR life, so that attacker STAYS HOME however many you have; only no-blocker
  bodies go. A plan saying "net 0", "net zero", "drained from them" or "the game ends first" is
  wrong.
- THE RENDER DID YOUR COMBAT MATH: "Barrowin (5/5) (printed 3/3)" hits for 5: add the rendered
  numbers, never rebuild from base stats.

BLOCK LADDER - one parenthesis per offered block, and the parenthesis is the answer. SURVIVAL FIRST:
the header says the attack IS LETHAL -> assign blockers to the biggest "deals N" attackers until the
damage still coming through is below your printed life, whatever the parentheses say; only then
spend spare blockers below.
ONE BLOCKER PER ATTACKER: a second body on one attacker is one more attacker unblocked; a "GANG
BLOCK:" clause prices the KILL, never the life that block costs you. The header NAMES an assignment
-> take it; a life number alone -> one blocker on each attacker, biggest "deals N" first, and stop.
 0. "(neither dies" -> TAKE IT (a price clause after those words still applies, and only a lifegain
    price makes this block worse than none).
 1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" -> TAKE IT, always, at every life total.
 2. "(both die)" -> TAKE IT unless the attacker it kills is worth less than your blocker.
 3. "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> the chump line, the ONLY one the forecast decides. Read
    "you would be at N": block at 10 or less, or when the header says lethal; above 10 leave it
    unblocked. "...N tramples to your face" does not stop that N - chump a trampler only at lethal.
Blocked damage is ZERO (see COMBAT), so "I take the damage either way" and "preserve it for future
blocks" are false at N of 10 or less. "BLOCKS: none" is right only when every block offered is rung
3 above your floor.

VENTURE. Adventurer ventures on attack, Nadaar on enter AND attack, Acererak and Barrowin on enter.
- ACERERAK IS NOT A BODY: he bounces himself to hand on entry unless Tomb of Annihilation is done,
  so his printed 5 power is in no damage math and he reaches no combat. He IS a {2}{B} repeatable
  venture: cast him with spare mana only, never over a row that leaves a creature on your
  battlefield.
- WHICH DUNGEON: whenever the three-dungeon list appears - first venture and after each completion -
  answer LOST MINE OF PHANDELVER by NAME (numbers are not fixed); every Lost Mine room is
  upside and none costs life. TOMB OF ANNIHILATION only while your printed life is 5 or more ABOVE
  theirs; MAD MAGE only in a slow grind for cards.
- ROOM BRANCHES print each room's effect ("{room effect: ...}"). DECIDE ON THE EFFECT TEXT ALONE:
  the room that MAKES A BODY or DRAINS them beats a Treasure, a counter or a scry. Never work out
  which room you are on.

PLANESWALKERS - USE THEM THE TURN THEY LAND AND EVERY TURN AFTER; an idle walker is wasted and "she
only just came down" is no reason to wait.
- LOLTH: -3 first, THE TURN SHE ARRIVES whenever a crack-back line is aimed at you. Her two 2/1
  Spiders have REACH - they BLOCK A FLIER - and menace, blockers this deck buys no other way; every
  creature of yours that dies puts a counter back on her. LOLTH'S 0 HAS A LIFE FLOOR OF 6: printed
  life 6 or less -> no 0; pass, or -3.
- KAYA: USING HER IS NOT SPENDING HER - her +1 is a USE. Her -3 runs the ladder in SPELLS.
- A SECOND KAYA OR LOLTH IN HAND IS A LOYALTY RESET FOR A SPENT COPY AND NOTHING ELSE. A cast row
  tagged "[legendary: you already control ...]" is LIVE: casting it bins ONE of the two copies and
  YOU CHOOSE WHICH at the legend-rule ask. DECIDE BY THE LOYALTY ON YOUR BATTLEFIELD LINE. Below 3,
  so it cannot pay a -3: CAST THE SECOND COPY, bin the spent one, and -3 the fresh one the same turn
  - the only way this deck fires a second -3, and skipping the row as "legendary, useless" loses the
  game. At 3 or more the cast buys nothing and costs a card and the mana: take the next cast-order
  entry instead.

THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW ("Hold priority - pass now ...") is a DURABLE pass answering every later
window carrying these rows, NOT the one-window pass. Read the "[hold check: ...]" line: "every row
above was also on the menu at the last window" -> take HOLD once you have decided on no action;
"N rows above are new" or "first window ... at this seam" -> decline this window only.
NEVER hold a list offering a Kaya or Lolth loyalty ability or the Hive animation - holding gives them
up while the rows stand. With no numbered pass row on a priority list, the one-window pass is
"CHOICE: 0 (pass)".
A RUN OF DECLINES IS THE FAILURE MODE: once a decline row says you have declined this exact list 2
or more times this turn and nothing on it becomes payable this turn, TAKE HOLD.

MULLIGAN. THE ENGINE COUNTS YOUR HAND FOR YOU - the land/spell count, "Mana sources among those
lands", "Cheapest spell in this hand", "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of".
Those counts are the whole decision; never recount. Where you are is the "(keeping N)".
- (keeping 5) or lower with even ONE land -> KEEP; shipping buys one card fewer at the same land
  odds. Zero lands, or all lands, is the only fresh look worth a card.
- On the untouched 7 or at (keeping 6), a mulligan is right for exactly three hands: ZERO lands; an
  all-land hand (6-7); or ONE land under "Playing every land in this hand would not cover any spell
  in it" WITH the bracketed COUNT reason. That is a ONE-LAND TEST AND NOTHING ELSE - at TWO OR MORE
  lands never a ship. Any other hand KEEPS, one land under "would cover the cost of:" included.
- COLOUR IS NEVER A REASON TO SHIP, AT ANY LOOK: 14 of your 21 lands make white, 15 black, 8 either.
  Every bracketed COLOUR reason is what this forbids.
- A CARRIED PLAN IS ABOUT A HAND ALREADY THROWN AWAY - decide from this header. Never ship for too
  many lands or for lacking a one-drop.

CLEANUP DISCARD ("your maximum hand size is 7"). Answer "PUT: n, m" with the numbers you SEND. SCAN
in this order, stopping at the first hit - never row 1 because it is first:
 (1) Highest printed mana value among cards NEITHER LAND NOR LAND-BACKED. Emeria's Call, Agadeem's
     Awakening and Pelakka Predation are LAND-BACKED and not eligible; the row prints them as plain
     sorceries and says nothing of it.
 (2) A row printing "{you already control one: <name>}", whatever its mana value - unless the copy
     you control is a walker below 3 loyalty, making the one in hand a reset (see KAYA) - or a
     second copy of a card whose first copy you keep; between two "{dead right now: ...}" rows, the
     higher mana value.
 (3) A LAND, once your line prints four or more ("{spare: you control N lands already}"); below four
     the PUT line holds spell numbers only.
 (4) Otherwise the HIGHEST PRINTED MANA VALUE left - read the {..} cost off each row.
Never send your only removal while their line shows a creature or walker.

WHEN THEY COUNTER AND GAIN LIFE (Cancel, Supreme Verdict, Elixir of Immortality, Staff of Nin in
their log): Elixir and Staff are COLORLESS ARTIFACTS - never on Verse's list, and Soul Shatter does
nothing to them, so KAYA'S -3 IS THE ONLY ANSWER once her list names no walker of theirs. The
counter test in THE WHOLE CAST ORDER keeps her out of open blue mana.

SANGUINE BOND AND EXQUISITE BLOOD. Either name on Verse's target list or Kaya's -3 list -> take it,
above any creature. That is a REMOVAL rule and never a Silencer name. With BOTH out the drain loops
without limit: ATTACK: none whatever the tags say until one is gone, and a "[lifelink]" attacker of
theirs ends the game blocked or not - "NOT lethal" is not about that chain.

LEGEND RULE. The row you PICK is the one that DIES: pick the copy tagged "[summoning sick" or with
fewer counters.
