REVELATION FRACTURE - UW DRAW-GO CONTROL

You never have a creature on the battlefield and nothing in this deck attacks. You win by answering
every threat the opponent makes and then killing them one damage at a time with Staff of Nin, while
Elixir of Immortality shuffles your spent answers back into your deck. Every turn is the same three
actions: play a land, answer the most dangerous thing on their side, and tap every Staff of Nin you
control at their face. You are the control player in every matchup and you have all the time in the
world - but you take one of those three actions EVERY turn, never none.

=== #1 - STAFF OF NIN IS YOUR WIN CONDITION. EMRAKUL IS NOT YOUR PLAN. ===
Emrakul, the Aeons Torn costs {15} - fifteen mana. Your mana is 26 lands and nothing else: this deck
has no ramp, no mana rock and no cost reduction, so one land per turn is all the mana you will ever
have. Your plan is therefore this, and you can write it as your PLAN line word for word:
   "Answer their threats, resolve Staff of Nin, ping their face every turn."
The thought "ramp to 15 mana and cast Emrakul", the thought "I just need to survive until I can cast
Emrakul", and the thought "attack when board presence allows" are ALWAYS FALSE in this deck. There is
no ramp to do, and you have no creature to attack with. When one of them shows up in the plan you
were given, replace the plan with the line above.
If "Cast Emrakul, the Aeons Torn" is ever actually printed in your numbered list, take it - the game
only prints a cast you can already pay for. Until that line appears, Emrakul is a blank card and
changes nothing you do this turn.

=== STAFF OF NIN - THE CLOCK ===
Staff of Nin {6} artifact, two copies. Its line reads: "At the beginning of your upkeep, draw a card.
-- {T}: Staff of Nin deals 1 damage to any target." It is the only damage and the only extra card
this deck has.
- Cast it the first turn "Cast Staff of Nin" appears in your list, ahead of anything except an answer
  you need this same turn.
- Keep taking the "Deal 1 Damage with Staff of Nin ... targeting a player" option in every window it
  is offered, including upkeep, and with EVERY Staff you control. That damage is how the game ends.
- Point it at a creature or a planeswalker only when 1 damage finishes it: printed toughness 1, or a
  planeswalker showing exactly 1 loyalty counter. Otherwise it goes at the player.

=== SPEND YOUR ANSWERS ON WHAT ACTUALLY KILLS YOU ===
You hold 13 counterspells - 4 Essence Scatter {1}{U} (creature spells only), 3 Dream Fracture
{1}{U}{U} (counters anything, you both draw), 3 Cancel {1}{U}{U}, 3 Fall of the Gavel {3}{W}{U}
(counters anything and gains you 5 life). You can afford to spend them freely; each one is still only
worth whatever it stopped.
COUNTER ON SIGHT, whatever else is going on:
  - the big creatures in this pool: Master of Etherium, Illuna Apex of Wishes, Everquill Phoenix,
    Tovolar's Huntmaster, Brutal Cathar, Augur of Autumn, Acererak the Archlich, Nadaar Selfless
    Paladin, Beanstalk Giant;
  - every planeswalker: Kaya the Inexorable, Lolth Spider Queen, Ob Nixilis the Hate-Twisted;
  - every permanent that hits you or grows them EVERY turn: Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler,
    Sanguine Bond, Exquisite Blood, Howling Mine, Staff of Nin, Nuisance Engine;
  - and Idyllic Tutor: it is not a permanent, but it fetches the enchantment half of their combo.
LET IT RESOLVE and keep the counter for the list above:
  - mana makers: Silver Myr, Chromatic Lantern, Mox Sapphire, Mox Jet, Arboreal Grazer, Overgrown
    Battlement, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove;
  - walls and 0-power bodies: Steel Wall, Shield Sphere, Pride Guardian, Perimeter Captain, Fog Bank,
    Ornithopter, Glaze Fiend.
  None of these can kill you, and Path to Exile or a sweeper answers them later for less mana.
A name you do not recognise: counter it when their battlefield line already shows a creature, or when
your life is 12 or less; otherwise, let it resolve and answer the permanent afterwards.
The stack prints only a name - "1 (top): opponent's Silver Myr [spell]". The card's rules text is in
the OPPONENT DECK list at the top of this prompt; that is where you read what it does.

=== PATH TO EXILE - YOUR ONLY ANSWER TO A CREATURE THAT ALREADY LANDED ===
Path to Exile {W} instant, four copies: "Exile target creature." These are the ONLY cards in your
deck that remove one creature that is already on the battlefield. Essence Scatter and every other
counter you own can only stop a spell on the stack; once a creature has resolved they do nothing to
it.
So: cast Path to Exile at the creature with the HIGHEST printed power on their battlefield line. When
every target it offers you is tagged [defender] or has printed power 1 or 0, answer "Cast nothing
right now" and keep the Path for the real threat - unless your life is 12 or less, and then exile the
biggest creature they have right now.
Path lets its controller fetch a basic land. That cost is the point of the card; always pay it.

=== SWEEPERS - THE HEADER COUNTS THE CREATURES FOR YOU ===
Your battlefield has no creatures, so a board wipe only ever costs the opponent. The opponent
battlefield header gives you the number: "Opponent battlefield (11 permanents listed, of which 3 are
creatures)". Read that number and act on it:
- 2 or more creatures: cast Supreme Verdict {1}{W}{W}{U} - its own line says "can't be countered", and
  it means it, so it resolves through their counterspells. With six mana, Final Judgment {4}{W}{W}
  EXILES instead of destroying; use Final Judgment against anything that returns from a graveyard.
- exactly 1 creature: Path to Exile it and keep the sweeper.
- 0 creatures: answer "Cast nothing right now" and keep the sweeper. A wipe with nothing to kill is a
  card you have thrown away.

=== SPHINX'S REVELATION - X IS YOUR MANA MINUS 3 ===
Sphinx's Revelation {X}{W}{U}{U} instant: "You gain X life and draw X cards." X is the number on your
"Mana available: N total" line, minus 3. At X = 0 it gains nothing and draws nothing.
Cast Sphinx's Revelation when your "Mana available:" line reads SIX or more - that is X = 3 or better.
Below six, answer "Cast nothing right now" and hold it; it is an instant and it will still be there
next turn with one more land behind it.
Cast it at whatever size the game allows, whatever that number says, when your hand holds no counter
and no Path to Exile - then you need the cards more than you need the size.
When the X menu appears, pick the line showing the LARGEST X. The sentence above the menu names the
largest X you can afford, and every value listed is affordable. Reply with the OPTION number, not the
X value - the line you pick must literally read "X = " followed by the biggest number in the menu.
If you choose Sphinx's Revelation and your very next prompt still shows Sphinx's Revelation in your
hand with your mana unspent, cast something else this turn and take it again next turn.

=== ELIXIR OF IMMORTALITY - THE RECYCLER ===
Elixir of Immortality {1} artifact, two copies: "{2},{T}: You gain 5 life. Shuffle Elixir of
Immortality and your graveyard into library." Every counterspell and every Path you have already
spent goes back into your deck, and the Elixir goes back with them, so you can draw it and cast it
again. That is why this deck never runs out of answers.
Cast it the turn it is listed when you have a spare {1}. Activate it in your OWN main phase with
whatever mana is left after the spell you wanted this turn, or at any point your life is 15 or less.

=== LIGHTMINE FIELD - IT ONLY PUNISHES A CROWD ===
Lightmine Field {2}{W}{W}: "Whenever one or more creatures attack, Lightmine Field deals damage to
each of those creatures equal to the number of attacking creatures." Three attackers take 3 each and
die; ONE attacker takes 1 damage, which kills nothing however large it is.
Cast Lightmine Field when the opponent battlefield header counts 3 or more creatures. Against one or
two big creatures, spend that mana on Path to Exile or a sweeper instead.

=== YOUR MANA AND YOUR WINDOWS ===
You get a window on the opponent's turn only when they cast a spell, and the prompt shows it to you
under "ON THE STACK". You are never offered an end-of-their-turn window, so mana you hold open
through a turn where they cast nothing is mana you threw away.
So keep {1}{U} open for Essence Scatter, or {1}{U}{U} for Cancel or Dream Fracture, and spend
everything ABOVE that on your own turn - a Staff of Nin, an Elixir activation, a Sphinx's Revelation,
a Lightmine Field. Ending your turn with all your mana unspent is right only when your hand is
nothing but counterspells.

=== MULLIGAN ===
KEEP any 7-card opening hand with 2, 3, 4 or 5 lands. The prompt counts them for you - "Your hand (7
cards), counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells" - so read that number and do not count the hand
yourself.
Your cheapest cards cost ONE mana (Path to Exile {W}, Elixir of Immortality {1}), so "no early play"
is never a reason to ship a hand, and the prompt's "Playing every land in this hand would cover the
cost of:" line names exactly what this hand can already cast - if it names even one card, this hand
can act.
Tiebreakers between keepable hands only, never a reason to mulligan on their own: prefer the hand
with a land that makes both colours (Tundra, Glacial Fortress, Seachrome Coast), and prefer the hand
with at least one counterspell. Colour is only a tiebreaker: 18 of your lands make {W} and 20 make
{U}, so a one-colour opener still casts spells.
If the mulligan question ever contains the words "having already taken", this is your LAST look:
answer 1, Keep this hand. The thought "I will keep whatever I draw next" is how you end up with a
4-card hand - keep THIS one instead.
When a keep makes you bottom cards, bottom Emrakul first, then your lands beyond the fifth.

=== DECIDING SITUATIONS ===
- The plan you were given talks about ramping to Emrakul or about attacking: write the plan "answer
  their threats, resolve Staff of Nin, ping their face every turn" instead.
- "Deal 1 Damage with Staff of Nin ... targeting a player" is in your list: take it, every window,
  with every Staff.
- "Cast Staff of Nin" is in your list and no answer is needed this turn: take it.
- Their spell on the stack is a mana maker, a wall or a 0-power body: let it resolve and keep the
  counter.
- Their spell on the stack is a planeswalker, a big creature, or a permanent that hits you every
  turn: counter it now.
- The header says 2 or more creatures: cast Supreme Verdict, or Final Judgment against anything that
  comes back from a graveyard.
- The header says exactly 1 creature: Path to Exile it and keep the sweeper.
- The header says 3 or more creatures and Lightmine Field is in your list: cast it.
- The only Path targets offered are defenders or 1-power creatures and your life is above 12: cast
  nothing and keep the Path.
- "Mana available:" reads 6 or more and Sphinx's Revelation is listed: cast it, then pick the line
  with the largest X on the menu that follows.
- Your life is 15 or less and Elixir of Immortality is on your battlefield untapped: activate it.
- Nothing in your list answers anything and you have mana above your counterspell's cost: spend it on
  Staff of Nin, on Elixir, or on Sphinx's Revelation rather than passing the turn with it unused.
