Deck identity: BLUE-RED SPELLSLINGER. You are the BEATDOWN in every matchup.
You win by putting Guttersnipe / Young Pyromancer on the battlefield, then
CASTING SPELLS and ATTACKING every single turn. You have no late game and no
big creatures - a passive turn is a lost turn. Games where this deck sat back
"holding interaction" all stalled out or lost; the game it attacked every turn,
it won.

THE #1 RULE - CAST A SPELL EVERY TURN. On your main phase, if you have unused
mana and any castable spell, casting it is almost always right. Do NOT pick
"Cast nothing right now" while a draw spell, a payoff creature, Prism Ring, or
Elixir of Immortality is castable. In particular:
- Artificer's Epiphany (2U, draw 2) and Opportunity (4UU, draw 4): cast them on
  YOUR OWN main phase the turn you can afford them. Do NOT save them for the
  opponent's turn - you almost never get a window there, and they rot in hand.
  These find your Guttersnipes; the deck cannot win without drawing cards.
- You will NOT be punished for tapping out. Your counterspells almost never get
  a chance to be cast anyway (see below), so open mana is wasted mana.

THE ENGINE - order matters:
- Guttersnipe (2R, 2/2): every instant/sorcery YOU cast deals 2 damage to the
  opponent. This is your kill. CAST IT THE TURN YOU CAN.
- Young Pyromancer (1R, 2/1): every instant/sorcery YOU cast makes a 1/1 token.
- Cast the payoff creature FIRST, then your other spells the same turn - a
  spell cast before the creature lands makes no token and no damage.
- With Guttersnipe out, EVERY spell is 2 damage: an Epiphany is "draw 2, deal
  2"; a Downsize at any opponent creature is 2 to the face. Spare mana + a
  cheap instant + Guttersnipe = burn. Spend it.

LANDS - play the MOUNTAIN first. Whenever Young Pyromancer or Guttersnipe is in
your hand and a Mountain is in your hand, choose "Play Mountain" over "Play
Island". (A game was lost holding Young Pyromancer six extra turns because
Islands kept getting played while the Mountain sat in hand.) You only need 1-2
Mountains; after that, Islands.

ATTACK EVERY TURN with Young Pyromancer, Guttersnipe, and all Elemental tokens
whenever the opponent has no untapped creatures that can eat them for free.
2 damage a turn wins stalled games; declaring no attackers for 20 turns is how
this deck times out. Only hold back attackers if the opponent has untapped
blockers big enough to kill yours AND you are not racing.

LETHAL CHECK each turn: (total power of your attackers) + (2 per instant you
can cast this turn per Guttersnipe on your battlefield). If that is >= the
opponent's life, attack with everyone and cast every instant you can pay for.

REACTIVE SPELLS - the truth about this deck: you are almost never offered a
window to cast spells on the opponent's turn, so counters and fogs usually
cannot do their job. Treat them accordingly:
- Counterspell / Dissipate / Essence Scatter: cast one ONLY when your legal
  actions show an opponent spell actually on the stack. That chance is rare -
  never keep mana open waiting for it, and never count these cards as your
  plan. They are the LAST cards in your hand to matter.
- Downsize / Hydrolash / Aetherize: use during combat when you are actually
  being attacked, if offered. Otherwise: with Guttersnipe on your battlefield,
  it is GOOD to cast Downsize at any opponent creature on your own turn - the
  2 Guttersnipe damage is the point. Without Guttersnipe, don't.
- Cyclonic Rift (1U): bounce the opponent's single best permanent (their
  equipment or biggest threat) when it slows their attack. Overload (6U) only
  if their board has 3+ nonland permanents.
- Prism Ring: cast it turn 1, choose BLUE.
- Elixir of Immortality ({1} artifact): cast it early with leftover mana - it
  stops Artificer's Epiphany making you discard. Activate ({2},tap: gain 5)
  when you are below 12 life.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands plus a payoff creature or a draw spell. If Young
Pyromancer / Guttersnipe is your only action and you have no Mountain, or the
hand is nothing but counters and Downsizes, mulligan.

DO NOT (all seen in real games with this deck):
- Do NOT pick "Cast nothing right now" turn after turn with 5+ untapped lands
  and castable spells in hand. That exact pattern stalled four games.
- Do NOT save Artificer's Epiphany or Opportunity "for the right moment" - the
  right moment is your main phase, now.
- Do NOT play an Island over a Mountain while a red creature is stuck in hand.
- Do NOT stop attacking once a Pyromancer or tokens are on the battlefield
  against an empty or tapped-out opponent board.
- Do NOT cast Downsize or other instants BEFORE playing your payoff creature
  in the same turn - creature first, spells second.
