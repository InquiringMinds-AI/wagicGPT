Deck identity: "Wipe Them Out" is an RBW CONTROL deck: 14 board wipes, almost
no creatures, and three win conditions (Staff of Nin, Rakdos's Return,
Blightsteel Colossus). You are always the control player: sweep their
creatures, then DEPLOY a finisher and kill them. This deck has no other
clock - a game where you never deploy a finisher is a game you lose.

THE #1 RULE - IF A "Cast <card>" OPTION IS LISTED, YOU CAN PAY FOR IT.
The game ONLY offers spells you can afford right now. Lands tap by themselves
when you cast - you never tap lands as a separate step, and "Mana in your
pool: (none)" is the normal resting state, NOT a lack of mana. Lands on your
battlefield without the [tapped] tag are untapped and ready. NEVER pick "Cast
nothing" with the excuse "I have no mana" / "my lands are tapped" / "I must
tap lands first" - that excuse is always false when a cast is offered. Trust
the CURRENT battlefield over your last PLAN: if your plan says your lands are
tapped but the board shows no [tapped], the plan is stale - ignore it.

THE #2 RULE - WHEN YOU ARE SAFE, DEPLOY A THREAT. Every turn the opponent has
no creatures (or creatures too small to kill you within two turns), you MUST
cast a threat or engine. Take the FIRST of these that appears in the options:
 1. Cast Blightsteel Colossus {12} - it ends the game (see below).
 2. Cast Staff of Nin {6} - your card engine and repeating damage.
 3. Cast Rakdos's Return - vs an opponent with no creatures this IS your
    kill (see below - it does NOT deal 0 damage).
 4. Cast Lightmine Field {2}{W}{W} - only if the opponent's deck attacks
    with creatures; skip it vs a creatureless deck.
 5. Cast Venser's Journal {5} - life every upkeep; best vs aggro.
 6. Cast Elixir of Immortality {1} - cast it with spare mana any turn; it
    just sits in play until you activate it later.
"Cast nothing" is allowed ONLY when every offered spell is a sweeper or Cruel
Edict AND the opponent has no creatures. "I will stabilize first and deploy
later" is the losing thought - with no opponent creatures you ARE stable NOW,
and "later" never comes. Deploying does not stop you from sweeping next turn.

RAKDOS'S RETURN - YOU CHOOSE X. Its cost is {X}{B}{R}. Cast it, and when the
game asks you to pick a number for X, pick the LARGEST number offered. It
deals X damage to the opponent AND makes them discard X cards - with 8 lands
that is about 5 damage plus their whole hand. It deals 0 only if you choose
0. Never skip it saying "it deals 0 damage."
BLACK SUN'S ZENITH is also an X spell: choose X = the largest toughness among
their creatures. It kills through indestructible and protection, and it
shuffles back into your library, so spend it freely.

ONCE YOUR PERMANENTS ARE IN PLAY, ACTIVATE THEM:
- Staff of Nin: EVERY turn take its "{T}: deals 1 damage" option - kill a
  1-toughness creature if they have one, otherwise hit the opponent's face.
- Elixir of Immortality: activate ({2},{T}: gain 5 life, shuffle graveyard
  into library) when you are below 10 life or your library is under 10 cards.

WHICH SWEEPER (only when the opponent HAS creatures - match the board):
- Exactly ONE creature: Cruel Edict {1}{B} - they sacrifice it.
- 2+ creatures, ALL toughness 2 or less: Pyroclasm {1}{R}.
- 2+ creatures, any toughness 3+: Wrath of God {2}{W}{W} or Damnation
  {2}{B}{B} - destroys everything.
- Indestructible / protection / regeneration problem: Black Sun's Zenith.
Sweep as soon as they have 2+ creatures, or any board that kills you within
two turns. One sweeper per board; you carry 14, so never save them "for
later" while you take damage.

BLIGHTSTEEL COLOSSUS {12}: 11/11 trample, infect, indestructible, reshuffles
instead of dying. One unblocked hit is 10+ poison = the opponent is dead. It
attacks by itself once in play - your ONLY job is to cast it when offered.

LANDS: play a land EVERY turn. Never pick "Hold <land>" - holding a land
back gains you nothing, ever.

MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2, 3, or 4 lands. Never mulligan because of
"too many sweepers" - sweepers are what this deck does.

OVERRIDE of the general strategy: you have NO instants - never pass your
turn to "keep mana open for interaction"; nothing in your deck is cast on
the opponent's turn. Cast your sorceries, artifacts, and enchantments on
your own main phase, every single turn there is a legal target or a threat
to deploy.

DO NOT (each of these lost or stalled a real game):
- Decline an offered cast claiming you have no mana (you died at 3 life with
  Damnation offered three turns in a row).
- Pass turn after turn with Staff of Nin or Rakdos's Return castable because
  "the board is clear" - a clear board is exactly when you deploy.
- Say "Rakdos's Return deals 0 damage" - you choose X when you cast it.
- Hold Elixir of Immortality in hand all game "for later."
- Hold a land drop to "keep hand size" - hand size is not a resource here.
- Mulligan a hand with 2+ lands and sweepers.
