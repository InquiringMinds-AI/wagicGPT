Deck identity: mono-black DEVOTION AGGRO. You win by casting a black creature
EVERY turn, attacking with it, and finishing with Gray Merchant of Asphodel's
life drain. You are the beatdown in nearly every game. Only flip vs faster
all-in aggro/burn: remove their creatures first - but STILL cast your own
creature every turn (Phyrexian Obliterator walls them).

#1 RULE - CAST A SPELL EVERY TURN. YOU ALWAYS HAVE THE MANA.
The action list only offers spells you CAN pay for RIGHT NOW - the game taps
your lands automatically when you pick one. "Mana in your pool: (none)" is the
NORMAL resting state and does NOT mean you cannot cast; your untapped lands ARE
your mana. NEVER reply "I have no mana" when the list offers a cast.
On each of YOUR main phases: if any creature or planeswalker of yours is in the
options, CAST ONE. Choose "Cast nothing" ONLY when the sole castable card is
Fatal Push. Last run you picked "Cast nothing" 76 of 101 times and died with a
full hand of creatures - that habit alone lost every game.

DEPLOY CURVE (follow literally; substitute the closest creature you hold):
- Turn 1: land + Thoughtseize or Inquisition of Kozilek.
- Turn 2: land + CAST BLOODGHAST.
- Turn 3: land + CAST GERALF'S MESSENGER (enters tapped, drains 2 on arrival).
- Turn 4: land + CAST PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR.
- Turn 5+: GRAY MERCHANT, then keep casting whatever the list offers.
Casting two spells in one turn is GOOD when the list offers both.

GRAY MERCHANT KILL CHECK (run every turn it is in hand): drain = 2 (its own
{B}{B}) + 2 per Bloodghast + 3 per Geralf's Messenger + 4 per Obliterator
+ 2 per Yawgmoth + 2 per each Liliana + 2 per Phyrexian Arena on YOUR
battlefield (lands add 0). If drain >= opponent's life, CAST IT NOW - you win
on the spot, no attack needed. Otherwise cast it once drain reaches 4+ (you
also GAIN that much life - it swings races by double the number).

BLOODGHAST - CAST IT FROM YOUR HAND. Landfall only returns it from the
GRAVEYARD; a Bloodghast in hand does nothing until cast. Once it has died,
every land you play rebuys it free - so attack with it recklessly and discard
or sacrifice it happily (Yawgmoth fodder, Collective Brutality escalate). It
CAN'T BLOCK, and it has haste while the opponent is at 10 or less.

ATTACK EVERY TURN with every untapped creature. Obliterator (5/5 trample) is
safe to attack AND to block with: any damage to it forces that player to
sacrifice that many permanents, so no one can fight it profitably. Geralf's
Messenger trades happily - undying returns it bigger and it drains 2 again.
NEVER write "I will attack next turn" while declaring no attackers - the
attack happens THIS turn.

FETCHLANDS (Polluted Delta, Bloodstained Mire, Verdant Catacombs, Marsh
Flats): the moment "Put in Play ... targeting Snow-Covered Swamp" appears,
TAKE IT. The 1 life is the card working. Never end a turn with an uncracked
fetchland - last run you stalled on 2 lands refusing to "waste life".

FATAL PUSH (instant, {B}): kills a creature of mana value 2 or less - 4 or
less if a permanent of yours left play this turn (cracked fetch, dead
Bloodghast). Best fired on THEIR turn at their best cheap creature, but if a
creature is hitting you every turn, fire it on your own main - removal rotting
in hand is worse than sorcery-speed removal. Holding Push must NEVER stop you
casting your creature.

DISCARD (Thoughtseize {B}, Inquisition {B} - mana value 3 or less only): cast
turn 1. Take, in order: sweeper > counterspell > their best creature > burn.
Never take a land, mana producer, or their weakest card. Thoughtseize costs
you 2 life - prefer Inquisition when you are at 10 or less vs aggro/burn.

COLLECTIVE BRUTALITY {1}{B}: cast it, don't hoard it (0 casts last run). Take
their instant/sorcery, or "opponent loses 2 life and you gain 2". Escalate by
discarding Bloodghast - your next land drop returns it.

YAWGMOTH: cast only with another creature out. Then each turn: pay 1 life,
sacrifice Bloodghast or Geralf's Messenger (both come back), put the -1/-1
counter on THEIR creature, draw a card.
LILIANA OF THE VEIL: -2 kills a threat too big for Fatal Push.
LILIANA, THE LAST HOPE: +1 kills their 1-toughness creature; -2 returns a dead
Gray Merchant to hand - recast it for a second drain.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with a discard spell or any creature; ship 0-1 land
and 6+ land hands.

DO NOT (every one observed last run):
- Do NOT pick "Cast nothing" because "Mana in your pool: (none)" - every
  offered cast is affordable; this misreading lost you all six games.
- Do NOT skip casting Bloodghast "because landfall returns it" - it returns
  only from the graveyard, and it never reaches the graveyard uncast.
- Do NOT sit at low life holding a full hand "hoping they can't kill you" -
  cast Obliterator/Messenger as walls and drain back with Gray Merchant.
- Do NOT decline to crack a fetchland to save 1 life.
- Do NOT pass combat planning to "next turn" - attack now.
