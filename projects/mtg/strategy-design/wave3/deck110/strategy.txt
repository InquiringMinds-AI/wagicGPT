Deck identity: "Etched Affinity" is a fast artifact-aggro deck. Your cards cost
0-3 mana. You are the BEATDOWN in every matchup: dump your whole hand onto the
battlefield in the first few turns, equip Cranial Plating, attack every turn,
and finish with Galvanic Blast. Kill by turn 5-6. A turn where you cast nothing
is a lost turn.

#1 RULE - IF "Cast X" IS IN YOUR OPTIONS, YOU CAN PAY FOR IT. CAST IT.
The game only lists actions you can legally pay RIGHT NOW - it checks your
mana for you and taps your lands automatically when you cast. "Mana in your
pool: (none)" is NORMAL and does NOT mean you lack mana; untapped lands ARE
your mana. Do not reason about colors or whether you "can produce" mana - the
list already did that.
- NEVER pick "Cast nothing right now" while a creature, your first Cranial
  Plating, or Thoughtcast is on the list. Pick the creature.
- Memnite and Ornithopter cost {0} - literally free. Cast them the moment you
  see them, always.
- Act NOW, not next turn. If your plan says "cast X", pick the number that
  casts X in THIS reply. "I will do it next turn" repeated every turn is how
  this deck loses.
- It is fine to tap out to deploy creatures. Board presence beats saved mana.

THE ENGINE - CRANIAL PLATING:
Plating gives its creature +1/+0 for EACH artifact you control (usually +4 or
more). Sequence:
1. CAST Cranial Plating the first turn it appears in your options - even if
   you cannot equip the same turn. It must be on the battlefield first.
2. Next priority you see "Equip with Cranial Plating targeting X [cost: {1}]",
   TAKE IT once.
3. Equip ONCE. If your creature's power already shows the bonus - e.g.
   "Memnite (5/1) (printed 1/1)" - the Plating is ALREADY attached: do NOT
   equip it again, pass (0). Re-equipping the same creature wastes your mana
   every turn.
4. Ignore the "attach with Cranial Plating [cost: {b}{b}]" line - it is a
   worse copy of the {1} Equip. Never pay {B}{B} for it.
Best carrier order: 1) Etched Champion (protection from all colors with 3+
artifacts - they cannot block or kill it), 2) Vault Skirge or Ornithopter
(fliers), 3) Signal Pest, 4) Memnite. One equipped Plating wins; do not cast
a 2nd copy while the 1st is unequipped.

ATTACK EVERY TURN with every creature. Signal Pest always attacks even at 0
power - battle cry gives every OTHER attacker +1/+0 and it can't be blocked
except by fliers/reach. Etched Champion with 3+ artifacts is unblockable by
colored creatures - always send it.

LETHAL CHECK - run this every turn:
damage = total power of your attackers (+1 each if Signal Pest attacks too)
       + 4 per Galvanic Blast in hand (metalcraft: 3+ artifacts = 4 damage).
If damage >= opponent's life: attack with everyone and fire every Blast at
the opponent's face. When the opponent is at 8 or less, ALWAYS point your
Blasts at their face - never save them. Earlier, Blast may kill a blocker
stopping your equipped attacker; otherwise hold it - but holding it past
opponent-at-8 is a misplay.

OTHER KEY CARDS:
- Steel Overseer: take "Counter with Steel Overseer [cost: Tap]" EVERY one of
  your turns - permanent +1/+1 counters on all your artifact creatures.
- Master of Etherium: power = your artifact count and it pumps your whole
  team +1/+1. Cast it on sight.
- Thoughtcast: affinity means it costs as little as {U}. Cast it whenever you
  have 2 or fewer other cards worth casting - refuel, do not hoard it.
- Mox Opal / Darksteel Citadel / Glimmervoid: lands and free mana that also
  count as artifacts for metalcraft (3+ artifacts). You hit metalcraft almost
  immediately - assume it is ON.

MULLIGAN: keep 2+ lands and 2+ creatures. Ship hands with 0-1 land or no
creatures.

DO NOT (all seen in real games with this deck):
- Do NOT pick "Cast nothing" because you think you have no mana or the wrong
  colors. If the cast is listed, you can pay it. This single error lost games
  from 20 life with a full hand.
- Do NOT hold Cranial Plating in hand for "next turn" - it sat uncast for 10+
  turns while 0-power creatures attacked for nothing. Cast it NOW.
- Do NOT re-equip a creature that already has the Plating attached.
- Do NOT skip a 4-damage Galvanic Blast at the face when the opponent is at 8
  or less "to keep mana for equipping" - the Blast is the win.
- Do NOT describe casting a spell in your plan and then pick the "Cast
  nothing" number. Your chosen number must match your plan.
