Deck identity: UB FAERIE TEMPO. Nearly every creature FLIES. You win by casting
faeries EVERY turn and attacking in the air EVERY turn. You are the BEATDOWN in
every matchup: your job is to get the opponent dead before turn 10, not to sit
on a hand of spells.

THE #1 RULE - CAST A CREATURE EVERY TURN. NO EXCEPTIONS:
- On EVERY one of your turns: play a land, then CAST A CREATURE if any "Cast"
  option shows a creature. Picking "Cast nothing right now" while a creature is
  castable is how this deck loses - your games stall for 10+ turns at 1 damage a
  turn while your win conditions rot in hand.
- If the engine OFFERS "Cast X", the cost IS payable - never refuse it because
  you think you lack a color. Oona's Gatewarden costs {U/B}: hybrid mana, ONE
  mana of EITHER blue or black. A single Island casts it.
- Scion of Oona and Obyra have flash, but treat them as NORMAL creatures: cast
  them in your main phase. Do NOT hold them in hand waiting for a better moment -
  that moment never comes and you waste whole turns.
- CAST ARCHMAGE OF ECHOES the first turn you have 5 mana. It is a 4/4 flying,
  ward-2 threat that doubles every faerie you cast after it. It wins the game
  from the battlefield and does NOTHING from your hand. Do not "hold up
  counterspells" instead of casting it - board first, counters with leftovers.

THE ENGINE - HOW YOU ACTUALLY KILL:
- Obyra, Dreaming Duelist ({U}{B} 2/2 flash flyer): every OTHER faerie that
  enters drains the opponent 1. Cast Obyra early, then every faerie is damage.
- Archmage of Echoes ({4}{U} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): copies each Faerie or Wizard
  CREATURE spell you cast (it does not copy instants). Archmage + any faerie =
  two bodies, and two Obyra drains.
- Faerie Bladecrafter ({2}{B} 2/2 flyer): gets a +1/+1 counter whenever your
  faeries hit the opponent, and when it DIES the opponent loses life equal to
  its power (you gain that much). Cast it and ATTACK with it every turn;
  trading or dying is fine - that death drain is real damage.

ATTACK EVERY TURN: your flyers are usually unblockable. Attack with EVERY
creature except Oona's Gatewarden (defender, can't attack) and anything needed
to block a lethal swing back. Chip damage adds up only if it happens every turn.
LETHAL CHECK each combat: total power of your untapped flyers + 1 per faerie you
can still cast with Obyra out; if that is >= opponent life, attack with everyone
and cast the faeries.

INTERACTION - FIRE IT, DON'T DIE WITH IT (you lost games with Go for the Throat
and Counterspell still in hand):
- Go for the Throat: vs aggro, kill their best attacker IMMEDIATELY - especially
  any creature that GROWS (+1/+1 counters, like Stromkirk Noble). Do not wait
  for something bigger; the growing 1/1 becomes a 4/4 that kills you.
- Counterspell / Arcane Denial: counter the FIRST opponent spell that costs 4 or
  more mana, or any creature with power 4 or more (a 13/13 Abominable Treefolk
  must NEVER resolve). Cheap 2-drops: let them through and race in the air.
- If your life is 10 or less: spend ALL interaction NOW on whatever is hitting
  you. Saving it for later means there is no later.
- Do not counter small utility spells; two counters held all game = two wasted cards.

CARD NOTES:
- Sleep-Cursed Faerie ({U} 3/3 flyer, ward 2): enters tapped with 3 stun
  counters and wakes up by itself in 3 turns. Cast it turn 1-2 and LEAVE IT
  ALONE. NEVER pay {1}{U} to untap it on the turn you cast it (it cannot attack
  yet - the mana is pure waste, seen repeatedly). Pay the untap ONLY in main
  phase 1 when it will attack THIS combat.
- Faerie Miscreant ({U} 1/1 flyer): the second copy draws a card - cast both.
- Oona's Gatewarden ({U/B} 2/1 flying defender, wither): best blocker; wither
  shrinks what it blocks. Cast it turn 1 off ANY land; it can never attack.
- Scion of Oona ({2}{U} 1/1 flash flyer): other faeries get +1/+1 and shroud
  (can't be targeted). Cast before attacking to pump the team.
- Shadow Puppeteers ({6}{U} 4/4 flyer, ward 2): makes two 1/1 flyers on entry.
  When asked, ALWAYS say YES to turning an attacking flyer into a 4/4 dragon.

MULLIGAN: keep ANY hand with 2-4 lands and at least one creature. Also KEEP
3+ lands with removal/counters and no creature - half your deck is cheap flyers,
you will draw them; a 6-card hand is worse than a slow 7. Ship only 0-1 land or
5+ land hands.

MISTAKES SEEN IN PLAY - DO NOT REPEAT:
- Choosing "Cast nothing" turn after turn while Archmage of Echoes was castable
  (it was never cast in six games - it must hit the battlefield).
- Refusing to cast Oona's Gatewarden off Islands for four turns ({U/B} is
  castable with blue OR black - trust the offered option).
- Dying at 4 life with Go for the Throat and Counterspell unspent in hand.
- Letting a huge creature resolve with two counterspells in hand.
- Paying {1}{U} to untap Sleep-Cursed Faerie the turn it entered, or after
  combat when it had nothing to do.
- Mulliganing a 4-land hand with a creature and removal into a 1-land hand.
