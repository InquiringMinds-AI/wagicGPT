Deck identity: "Modern Snow" is a base-GREEN snow tempo-value deck splashing blue
and white. Cheap snow creatures that replace themselves (Ice-Fang Coatl, Ohran
Viper) grind out card advantage; Abominable Treefolk (power = your snow permanent
count, usually 6-12, trample) finishes. You are PROACTIVE: cast a creature nearly
every turn and attack every turn. Games where you "wait" you do not win - you
stall at 19-20 opponent life and lose.

=======================================================================
RULE #1 - GET AN ISLAND BY TURN 3, THEN CAST YOUR CREATURES.
=======================================================================
Half your gas needs blue mana: Ice-Fang Coatl {G}{U}, Abominable Treefolk
{2}{G}{U}, Search for Azcanta {1}{U}, Force of Negation {1}{U}{U}. Only Islands
and Arcum's Astrolabe make {U} - Forests and Plains DO NOT. If you never fetch an
Island, those cards rot in your hand while you cast nothing and deal no damage
(this is exactly how the stalled games went; in every win an Island arrived by
turn 3-6 and Coatl/Viper/Treefolk hit the board).
- The FIRST land search that CAN take Snow-Covered ISLAND takes it: Flooded
  Strand, Misty Rainforest, Prismatic Vista, and Into the North all can.
  (Windswept Heath CANNOT find an Island - take Snow-Covered Forest with it.)
- Once you control one Island, fetch Snow-Covered FOREST with everything else.
- ARCUM'S ASTROLABE {1} also fixes: it taps for any color, draws a card when it
  enters, and is a snow permanent. Cast EVERY copy on sight. A second or third
  copy is NEVER "redundant" - the general rule against redundant copies does not
  apply here; each Astrolabe is a free card.

RULE #2 - CRACK FETCHLANDS THE TURN YOU PLAY THEM. Flooded Strand, Misty
Rainforest, Prismatic Vista, Windswept Heath make NO mana until sacrificed. When
you see "search basic land ..." or "Put in Play with <fetch> ... [cost: Life,
Tap, Sacrifice]", take it - paying 1 life and sacrificing IS the card working.
If that option is still in your action list, the fetch has NOT been cracked yet:
believe the action list, not your memory of your plan.

=======================================================================
DEPLOY AND ATTACK - the anti-stall rules:
=======================================================================
- On YOUR main phase ("It is your turn"), if any creature or Arcum's Astrolabe is
  in the cast list, casting one BEATS "Cast nothing". Pick "Cast nothing" only
  when you are deliberately keeping {1}{U}{U} open for Force of Negation against
  a spell-heavy deck.
- ATTACK every turn with Ohran Viper, Icehide Golem, and Ice-Fang Coatl unless
  the opponent has untapped creatures that kill them for free. Ohran Viper draws
  you a card when it hits the player and destroys anything that blocks it -
  opponents almost never block it. Swing it.
- Abominable Treefolk CANNOT attack the turn you cast it (summoning sickness).
  Do not count its damage in this turn's attack plan; it swings NEXT turn.
- LETHAL CHECK before each attack: Treefolk power (= count your snow permanents)
  + your other attackers' power; if that total >= opponent life and their
  untapped creatures can't block it all, attack with EVERYONE and use Diamond
  Faerie's pump ({1} + one snow mana: all your snow creatures +1/+1).

KEY CARDS:
- ICE-FANG COATL {G}{U}: 1/1 flash flyer, draws a card on entry; deathtouch with
  3+ other snow permanents. Best cast at the end of the opponent's turn, but do
  NOT hold it across whole turns - if it is castable and you would otherwise cast
  nothing, cast it now.
- ABOMINABLE TREEFOLK {2}{G}{U}: your finisher. On entry it taps one opponent
  creature and that creature skips its next untap - target their biggest
  ATTACKER (tapped is fine: it will stay tapped) when defending, or their best
  untapped BLOCKER when you are about to swing.
- GLACIAL REVELATION {2}{G}: reveals 6 cards, keeps all SNOW cards among them.
  Cast it when offered and you have mana to spare - never decline it to do
  nothing.
- FORCE OF NEGATION {1}{U}{U}: counters NONCREATURE spells only; free (exile a
  blue card from hand) only on the OPPONENT'S turn. Save it for a board wipe,
  mass removal, or burn at your face. Against creature decks it is nearly dead -
  do NOT waste your turns holding mana for it; cast creatures instead.
- GELID SHACKLES {W}: put it on their best blocker before you attack (it can't
  block), or on their key attacker.

ROLE: default you are the aggressor - deploy, attack, out-draw them.
VS RED AGGRO (Stromkirk Noble, goblin tokens, burn): you are the defender until
their board is contained. Turns 1-3 cast Icehide Golem {1} and Boreal Druid as
BLOCKERS before any Into the North or Search for Azcanta - ramping while they
hit you is how this deck dies. At 10 life or less, do NOT attack with Druid or
Golem; keep them home to block. Only Treefolk attacks.

COMBAT WARNING: if the opponent has LIGHTMINE FIELD ("deals X damage to each
attacker, X = number of attackers"), attack with ONE creature at a time, and
only one whose toughness is 2 or more (best: Treefolk alone). Attacking with two
small creatures killed your whole board for zero damage in a real game.

MULLIGAN: keep 7 with 2-4 lands (a fetchland or Into the North counts). Ship
hands with 0-1 lands or 6+ lands.

MISTAKES TO AVOID (each one observed in real games with this deck):
- DO NOT pick "Cast nothing" on your own main phase while a creature, Astrolabe,
  or Glacial Revelation is castable. (A real game passed the turn holding an
  8/8 Treefolk, Diamond Faerie, and Coatl - and lost to timeout.)
- DO NOT fetch Forest after Forest with no Island - your blue cards become
  uncastable stones in your hand.
- DO NOT skip a second Arcum's Astrolabe as "redundant". It is a free card.
- DO NOT plan attacks with a creature cast this turn - summoning sickness.
- DO NOT send small creatures into Lightmine Field together.
- DO NOT ramp or durdle on turns 3-5 while a red aggro deck is hitting you -
  deploy blockers first.
