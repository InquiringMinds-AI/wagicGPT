SELESNYA (GW) ADVENTURES -- go-wide beatdown. You win by curving out cheap creatures,
drawing extra cards off Edgewall Innkeeper, flooding the board with 1/1 lifelink Soldiers
(Emmara, Trostani, March, Castle), pumping them (Trostani, Venerated Loxodon, Unbreakable
Formation, Flourish), and swinging for the win around turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN.
Deploy a creature and ATTACK every turn. Your losses come from being too SLOW -- develop your
board fast and press damage; do not durdle on setup while an aggressive opponent races you.

ADVENTURE CARDS SHOW TWO CAST OPTIONS. Five of your cards are adventures (Faerie Guidemother,
Giant Killer, Lovestruck Beast, Shepherd of the Flock, Flaxen Intruder). Each appears TWICE:
  "Cast NAME // ADV {base cost} (P/T)"                    = the CREATURE (a real body on board)
  "Cast NAME // ADV with its adventure cost {..} {adventure spell: ...}" = the SPELL (its listed
     effect happens, then the card is EXILED so you may cast the creature LATER from exile)
The option line now TELLS you exactly what the adventure spell does (the {adventure spell: ...}
text) and the creature face shows its (P/T). Read them and pick the one you want.
DEFAULT: cast the CREATURE face (a body + an Innkeeper draw). Cast the adventure SPELL face only
when you want its effect right now. Per-face strategy:
  Faerie Guidemother = a 1/1 flyer, OR Gift of the Fae {1}{W}: +2/+1 & flying to a creature until
    end of turn -- a combat/evasion trick, use it to push lethal.
  Giant Killer = a 1/2 tapper, OR Chop Down {2}{W}: destroy a creature with power 4+ -- your removal
    for the opponent's big threats.
  Lovestruck Beast = a 5/5 that CANNOT ATTACK unless you control a 1/1 creature, OR Heart's Desire
    {G}: make a 1/1 white Human -- cast Heart's Desire to UNLOCK Lovestruck (or as a chump/body).
  Shepherd of the Flock = a 3/1, OR Usher to Safety {W}: return a permanent YOU control to hand --
    cast Usher ONLY to save your own creature from removal or re-use an ETB; NEVER just "for a body,"
    it bounces your own board for nothing.
  Flaxen Intruder = a 1/2, OR Welcome Home {5}{G}{G}: three 2/2 Bears (7 mana, rarely castable).

AN ADVENTURE CREATURE ON YOUR BATTLEFIELD IS A REAL BODY OF ITS PRINTED (P/T). When you see a
creature on your battlefield tagged [adventure] with a (1/1) or (1/2) -- e.g.
"Faerie Guidemother // Gift of the Fae (1/1) [flying, adventure]" -- that IS a live 1/1 creature.
The [adventure] tag only means the card is an adventure card; it does NOT mean it is "just a
face" or "not a real creature." It counts as a 1/1 for EVERYTHING: it satisfies Lovestruck
Beast's "you control a 1/1" requirement, it can attack and block, and it can tap for convoke.
Do not talk yourself out of a play by claiming a battlefield adventure creature "is not a real
1/1 body" -- it is exactly the body its (P/T) shows.

EDGEWALL INNKEEPER DRAW ENGINE. While Edgewall Innkeeper (1/1) is on your battlefield, casting
any adventure CREATURE (the five above) DRAWS YOU A CARD. Casting the adventure SPELL face does
NOT draw. Innkeeper is your card advantage -- deploy it turn 1-2 the moment you can and keep it
alive; then cast adventure creatures for the free cards.

CONVOKE CARDS -- Venerated Loxodon and March of the Multitudes. Convoke lets you TAP your
creatures to help pay. You are often offered TWO cast options for these: the PLAIN cast (its full
mana cost, e.g. "Cast Venerated Loxodon {4}{W}") and the convoke cast ("Cast X with its convoke
cost"). PREFER THE PLAIN CAST whenever you have enough untapped mana to pay the full cost -- it is
the reliable one and it leaves your creatures UNTAPPED to attack or block. Choose the convoke
option ONLY when you cannot cover the mana cost otherwise and need your creatures to help pay; when
you do, the engine handles which creatures tap (a separate deferred step) -- do not agonize over it.
IMPORTANT: if you pick a cast and the SAME card is STILL IN YOUR HAND on your next decision (it did
not resolve), do NOT keep re-picking that cast -- deploy a cheaper creature, attack, or move on;
re-casting an unresolved spell over and over wastes the whole turn while the opponent races you.
  Venerated Loxodon {4}{W} = a 4/4 that puts a +1/+1 counter on EACH creature that helped convoke
    it (the plain cast gives the 4/4 body). Cast it when you can, to add a body and pump the team.
  March of the Multitudes {G}{W}{W}{X} = make X 1/1 white lifelink Soldiers. Bigger X is better --
    a great go-wide + lifegain play.
  Bonus: tapping Emmara for convoke makes her trigger -- a free 1/1 lifelink Soldier.
  Note: creatures you tap to convoke are TAPPED and cannot attack or block THIS turn -- convoke on a
  turn you are pressing damage or need blockers only if the new board is worth the tapped bodies.

ATTACK EVERY TURN -- per-attacker test over the offered A-list. A creature ATTACKS if ANY of:
no untapped blocker can kill it / it kills or trades up / the opponent has no untapped creatures.
When the opponent has no untapped blocker, SWING WITH EVERYONE -- a 1/1 or 1/2 to the face is
real progress. Declare "no attackers" ONLY when every listed attacker would die for zero gain.
Do not spiral: pick the attackers and commit. EXCLUSION: the offered A-list already drops
ineligible creatures. TRUST IT BOTH WAYS: if Lovestruck Beast is MISSING it is because you
control no 1/1 (make a Human -- Heart's Desire / Castle Ardenvale -- to unlock it); if Lovestruck
Beast IS listed, the engine has ALREADY confirmed you control a 1/1, so its condition is met --
SWING it, do not re-derive the 1/1 rule or convince yourself it is ineligible and pass. A 5/5
into an opponent with only a small blocker or a defender kills or trades up -- send it.

BLOCKING -- read this before you panic under a big attack. Each of YOUR blockers may block AT MOST
ONE attacker (you CANNOT pile one creature onto several attackers); several of your creatures may
gang-block the same attacker. Answer with the offered B-to-A assignments and commit -- do not
re-derive the rules. THE OFFERED B-LIST IS AUTHORITATIVE: any creature shown as a blocker (a
B-line) is UNTAPPED and can block THIS turn -- even if the game log says it attacked earlier, or you
control another creature of the SAME NAME that is tapped. The B-line points at the specific untapped
copy; take the block it offers (especially a "you kill it, your blocker lives" block -- that is free
removal). Do NOT talk yourself out of an offered block by re-deriving a creature's tapped state from
the log. When you are facing LETHAL (their total attacking power >= your life): you do
not have to block everything, only enough to survive. Block to bring the damage through BELOW your
life total -- use your biggest creature to block-and-kill (it survives if its toughness beats their
power), and chump the rest with your smallest, most expendable bodies (1/1 Soldiers/Humans). Save
Lovestruck Beast and your real threats by having them block-to-kill rather than chump. If you have
Unbreakable Formation and mana, casting it (indestructible) before damage saves every blocker.

ROLE CHECK (rerun every turn on your life totals). Default = beatdown. If YOUR life is falling
while theirs is not (a fast aggro race -- e.g. mono-red), switch to LIFELINK STABILIZE: make
1/1 lifelink Soldiers (Emmara taps for one, Trostani makes two on entry, March makes X), block
with them and gain life. Do this EARLY -- most of your losses are races you tried to stabilize one
turn too late. When you are stabilizing (behind on the race), STOP feeding your small bodies into
the attack: hold Edgewall Innkeeper (your draw engine) and your 1/1s BACK as blockers -- a 1-power
swing that gives up a blocker is a bad trade when you are the one who dies first. Reserve Flourish/
Unbreakable Formation for a defensive turn (blockers survive, lifelink Soldiers live to gain more)
rather than an all-in swing you cannot afford. You STILL cast a creature every turn in both roles
-- never go passive; in stabilize mode that creature is a blocker, not an attacker.

PUMP AND CLOSE. Trostani Discordant gives your other creatures +1/+1 (an anthem -- cast it to
grow the whole team). Unbreakable Formation cast in your MAIN phase gives every creature a
+1/+1 counter, vigilance, AND indestructible until end of turn -- cast it before your alpha
strike, or in response to a board wipe to save your team. Flourish (Flower's back face,
{4}{G}{W}) gives all your creatures +2/+2 until end of turn. Before a lethal swing, TRUST the
printed [X/Y] on each creature -- it already includes anthems and counters; do not recompute
power from base stats.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with green AND white reachable (Temple Garden, Once Upon a Time, and
Flower all fix). Once Upon a Time is FREE if it is your first spell of the game -- cast it turn 1
to dig for lands and creatures. Note: Temple Garden asks "pay 2 life" or "tap" as it enters --
pay the 2 life to have it enter untapped when you want a play THIS turn; let it enter tapped only
if you have no turn-1/2 use for the mana.
