## REFINEMENT (small) -- distinguish "the corner is unstated" (edit) from "the model applies a stated corner inconsistently" (WATCH)

Wave-27 surfaced the failure mode the discipline must NOT be confused with. After the whole corner is stated,
the model may still apply it INCONSISTENTLY -- here it took the favorable block correctly 3x (vs22) but
declined it 2x (vs137), reverting to the old "never trade" frame on an already-corrected line. The tempting
error is to read those declines as "the corner needs MORE work" and add a fourth clause (louder, or a
speculative sub-case). That would be wrong twice over: (a) it re-opens a completed corner, resurrecting the
piecemeal pattern the discipline exists to kill; (b) it violates the parent rung's own tell -- a WRONG model
deviation from a RIGHT line is a MODEL slip (WATCH), never a guide edit.

**The refinement, one line for the skill:** *After you enumerate a corner's full rules-space, the corner is
CLOSED to edits. Subsequent model MISPLAYS inside that stated space are model-inconsistency WATCHES, not
evidence the corner is under-specified -- do not re-open a completed corner to "shout louder" at a rule the
model already has and sometimes ignores. The corner re-opens ONLY if a genuinely NEW axis-value appears
(a rules case the stated enumeration did not cover), which is what "cover the whole rules-space" is designed
to make rare.*

This gives the whole-corner discipline its natural stopping rule and protects the rotation verdict from being
deferred by model noise dressed up as new work. The tell that separates the two: is the observed play a NEW
rules case the enumeration missed (re-open, edit), or the SAME stated case the model executed wrong (WATCH)?
Wave-27's vs137 declines are unambiguously the latter -- the engine option literally read "you kill it, your
blocker lives," the stated favorable case, and the model still declined.
