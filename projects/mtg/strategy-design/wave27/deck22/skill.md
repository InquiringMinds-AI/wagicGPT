## NEW cross-cutting lesson: representation-lever <-> parser coupling

The d2 annotation fix produced a NEW, generalizable authoring lesson worth a standing skill
line: **an in-band render annotation added as a representation lever WILL be echoed by the
model into its structured answer.** vs131 s3 - the model copied `[changeling: counts as Giant]`
verbatim into its `CHOICE: 1 (...)` name parenthetical, which no longer matched the canonical
option text, and the name-anchored parser (c4) safe-fell to heuristic (1/146, benign).
Implication for BOTH lanes:
- Representation lane: annotations are consumed (good - it proves the render is read) but they
  are not free; expect them in replies.
- Parser lane: answer-matching must be annotation-tolerant - match on choice NUMBER +
  card-name PREFIX, or strip bracketed `[...]` tails before comparing echoed names. Routed to
  notes.md as an engine ledger item (parser, LOW, safe-direction).
This is the first observed case of a representation fix inducing a parser fallback - a
structural coupling the skill should name so future in-band annotations ship with a
parser-tolerance check.
