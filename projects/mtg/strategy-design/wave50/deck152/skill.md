# deck152 - proposals for the strategy-writing skill (wave 50)

S-1. **EVERY REPEATABLE SINK IN THE DECK GETS A SECTION BEFORE IT BITES, KEYED TO THE ROW'S
PRESENT FORMS.** #129 was written after deck130's Hammer and deck146's Hive had each cost a game;
deck152's Lair of the Hydra had no section at all and was offered 34 times this corpus, taken on a
`[this land is TAPPED ...]` row twice (`152 vs125` s16, `152 vs126` s24) and on a `[repeat:
activated this turn 1 times already]` row twice (`152 vs125` s59, s73). The engine's tags were
complete; the guide had nothing to key them to (#111's "name every present form" applies to a
row the guide never mentioned). Proposed rule, extending #129: the Step-1 deck read lists every
permanent with an activated ability the emitter can offer in Upkeep, and each gets at least a
two-line entry keyed to the row's tags (`[tapped ...]`, `[repeat: ...]`, `{paying this taps: ...}`)
before the first corpus, not after the first loss. Metric (#117): offers by phase and takes on
tagged rows, per sink.

S-2. **RE-KEY A CARD-SPECIFIC BLOCK RULE TO THE B-ROW PARENTHESIS AND THE HEADER N, NEVER TO
THE ATTACKER'S IDENTITY.** "Never swap Katilda with a 1/1 mana creature" was keyed to the wave-46
story; the failing windows were a 1/1 Goblin TOKEN (`152 vs146` s22) and a 3/3 Human token
(`152 vs123` s19) - the identity clause matched neither in the pilot's reading, and both replies
argued the block "saves life". #126 (re-key to the printed string) applies; the addition is the
pairing with the ladder's own number: a per-card exception to the ladder is written as "this
card's B-row + this parenthesis + N >= 10 -> not assigned", so it is evaluated by the same read
the ladder already demands.

S-3. **A CHANGE-OF-MIND REPLY IS SCORED BY ITS LAST CODED LINE AND READ FOR WHETHER THE REVISION
WAS RIGHT (SUPPORTING INSTANCE OF #116).** `152 vs126` s40: first line `ATTACK: A1, A5`, last
line `ATTACK: A1, A2, A3, A4, A5`, executed the last, lethal. The reasoning between correctly
distinguished Sanguine Bond alone from the pair and read the walls' tags for blocking triggers.
Two `attack_last_line_taken` on my seats, both revisions correct (`146 vs126` s18 went
`A1, A2` -> `none`, also right). Recorded as the corpus's specimen that the E1-era last-line
rule rescues a better answer at least as often as a worse one; no skill change.
