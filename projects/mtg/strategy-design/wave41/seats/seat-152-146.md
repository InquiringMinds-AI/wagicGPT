- BEFORE YOU WRITE "ATTACK:", RUN THE HOLD-BACK GATE - every attackers decision,
  first, and it is a NUMBER, not a feeling. Add up the current power (the first
  number in each "(P/T)") of every opposing creature that is not marked
  [defender] or "can't attack" - INCLUDING every one marked "[tapped - cannot
  attack or block this turn]", because that marker is about THIS turn and they
  all untap for theirs. Call that THEIR TOTAL POWER.
  If THEIR TOTAL POWER is BELOW your life total, the gate passes and this check
  is over: attack with every creature the list offers, including a sole

  If THEIR TOTAL POWER is your life total or MORE, keep your biggest ground
  creature HOME as a blocker and attack with the rest; if the list offers only
  ONE attacker, answer "ATTACK: none" - that one creature is your blocker.
  EXCEPTION, both directions: if the attack you can send RIGHT NOW is lethal
  (unblockable damage at or above their life), send everyone.
