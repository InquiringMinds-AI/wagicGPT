# Wave-43 review: consolidated deploy-ready guide edits

## deck125
W6 — insert immediately above the existing power-floor paragraph in === PATH TO EXILE ===:
BEFORE you use the power rule below, read each candidate's own rules text on the
opponent battlefield line. A creature whose text contains "{T}:", "Whenever ...
attacks", "At the beginning of", or "venture" DOES SOMETHING EVERY TURN, and printed
power 1 does not measure it. Path THAT creature the first window it is offered, at any
life total: it is the same danger the counter-on-sight list names, and Path is the only
answer you have once it has landed. Dwarven Blastminer (1/1, "{2}{R},{T}: Destroy
target nonbasic land"), Triumphant Adventurer (1/1, ventures on every attack) and
Luminarch Aspirant (1/1, a +1/+1 counter every combat) are this shape.
Only when NO candidate has such a line do you use the power rule:

W7 — append to === ELIXIR OF IMMORTALITY ===:
Read "Your library: N cards" at every decision. If N is 12 or less and an Elixir of
Immortality of yours is on the battlefield untapped, ACTIVATE IT IN THAT WINDOW, ahead
of a Staff ping and ahead of any spell. Shuffling your graveyard back is the only thing
that stops this deck from drawing its last card, and drawing from an empty library
loses the game outright at any life total. This rule has nothing to do with your life:
at 98 life with 17 cards left you are the player who is losing.

W8 — replace "EXCEPT that you never announce an X larger than N minus 2" with:
EXCEPT that you never announce an X larger than N minus 2, minus one more for EACH
Staff of Nin on your battlefield - every Staff draws you an extra card at your next
upkeep on top of your draw step. With two Staffs and a library of 17, the largest X you
may announce is 13, not 15.

W9 — replace the W1 hand-cap paragraph with:
Before you announce X, count the cards on your own "Your hand:" line and add X to that
number. Everything above 7 is discarded at end of turn: those are cards you paid mana
for and threw away. If the opponent battlefield line shows Liliana's Caress, Underworld
Dreams or Fate Unraveler, each discarded card ALSO costs you life on the way to the
graveyard. Announce the largest X that keeps (cards in hand + X) at 7 or less, even
when the menu offers more and even when your library is deep.

## deck126
D9 — append to the blocker-dies carve-out paragraph in === RULE #5 ===:
That carve-out is OFF whenever the combat header's "you would be at N" number is 8 or
less. At that life total the damage a body stops is worth more than the body, so block
with everything the list offers, whatever the parenthesis says.

D10 — replace the D6 surviving-line tiebreak sentence with:
When two or more attackers all let that blocker live, take the one with the BIGGEST
"deals N" number on its A-line that no other blocker of yours is already assigned to -
one blocker per attacker first, as above; this tiebreak only chooses among attackers
still unblocked.

## deck123
EDIT J — replace COMBAT attack section's "With no such permanent on their board, send everything.":
Before you send anything, read the "[their untapped blockers: ...]" tag on your own attacker
lines. If EVERY outcome in it reads "(neither dies)", you are not sending an attack - you are
feeding blockers. Count their untapped creatures. If they have as many untapped creatures as
you are sending, or more, answer ATTACK: none and keep building.
Two walls make this actively lethal to you, and their names are on their battlefield line:
PERIMETER CAPTAIN gains them 2 life for EVERY defender they block with, and PRIDE GUARDIAN
gains them 3 more. With SANGUINE BOND or EXQUISITE BLOOD also on that line, each point they
gain is a point you lose - a twelve-creature attack into six walls gained one opponent 26 life
and took 19 off you in a single turn. When their battlefield line names Perimeter Captain,
Pride Guardian, Sanguine Bond or Exquisite Blood, ATTACK: none is the answer until a token
activation, a Tragic Slip or a Damnation has removed the wall that gains them the life.
Send the army only when at least one attacker's tag says "you kill it, your blocker lives"
or their untapped creature count is lower than the number you are sending.

EDIT K — add as entry 5 of the cast list (renumber removal to 6, Vision Skeins to 7):
 5. "Cast Lightning Greaves {2}" - ONLY when a token-maker is already on your battlefield.
    Read your own battlefield line: "of which 0 are creatures" means the Greaves equips
    nothing and does nothing. Cast a removal spell or answer "Cast nothing right now" and
    keep the Greaves for the turn a maker lands.

EDIT L — replace EDIT E's first sentence (the stack-vs-battlefield teach) with:
Before any edict, run this in order: (1) find "of which N are creatures" on the OPPONENT'S
battlefield line. N is the only number that matters. (2) If N is not exactly 1, do not cast
an edict - not at 0, not at 2 or more. (3) A creature listed under "ON THE STACK, waiting to
resolve" is NOT on the battlefield and does not change N. Naming a creature spell on the
stack in your PLAN is the mistake itself: at N=0 the opponent sacrifices nothing and you have
thrown away the card.

EDIT M — append to the EDIT-G block-every-attacker bullet:
This rule spends EVERY blocker you have, including your last one. One blocker on one attacker
still stops that attacker's whole damage - "I only have one, so it does not matter" is how you
go from 10 life to 2 in one combat step. Assign blockers until you run out of blockers or run
out of unblocked attackers, whichever comes first.

## deck130
EDIT 6 — replace rule #2 step 0 with:
  0. FIRST read the "{X pricing:" annotation on the Cast Starstorm line, and read the WHOLE of
     it - the "At X=..." clause after the first sentence, not just the first number. Find the
     largest X you can afford and the list of THEIRS it kills at that X. If that list names TWO

     a trade you take.

  ONE exception to the 6-or-less floor, and only this one: LETHAL THIS TURN. Add up every

  Nine life to a Siege-Gang Commander with three Goblins and a loaded Spellbomb is lethal, not
  a face-burn mistake.

## deck152 AND deck146 (both guides)
S1 — insert as a third bullet after the R3a/R3b "(neither dies)" bullets:
- A "their untapped blockers" tag that contains "your attacker dies, their blocker lives"
  is a per-creature STOP, not a note about the whole attack. Leave THAT creature home even
  when the rest of the team is attacking - "attack with everything that profits" is decided
  one A-line at a time, and a creature that dies to a blocker they already control profits
  nothing. Send it anyway only when the lethal check clears: the printed opponent life is at
  or below the total power of everything you are sending.

S2 — append to the R3a/R3b outnumber bullet:
  The commonest way this goes wrong is a list with ONE name on it: one attacker never
  outnumbers one untapped blocker. If your only attacker's tag is all "(neither dies)",
  the answer is ATTACK: none - it deals nothing to their life total and it is tapped out
  of blocking on their turn.

S3 — append immediately after S2:
  ONE named exception: SHIELD SPHERE. It takes a permanent -0/-1 counter every time it
  blocks, so a "(neither dies)" line against Shield Sphere is not a wasted swing - it is
  how you kill it. Send into a Shield Sphere whether or not you outnumber them, and read
  the proof off their battlefield line as its printed toughness falls: 0/6, 0/5, 0/4.
