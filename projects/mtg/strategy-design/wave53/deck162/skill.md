# deck162 -> strategy-writing skill proposals (wave 53)

## S-1. A SURVIVAL GATE LIVES AT THE WINDOW THAT SPENDS THE RESOURCE, NOT AT THE WINDOW WHERE
## THE LOSS BECOMES VISIBLE.

deck162's arithmetic is correct and complete - `SURVIVAL COMES FIRST, AND IT IS ARITHMETIC - RUN
IT BEFORE THE NUMBERED RULES BELOW`, four numbered steps - and it sits under `RULE 4 - BLOCKING`.
At `162v152` s11 (turn 10, **4 life**, five untapped sources, own line `of which 0 are creatures`,
their line `4 of them without a restriction against attacking`) the seat was offered
`Ob Nixilis {3}{b}{b} {leaves 0 of your 5 ...}`, `Master of the Feast (5/5) {leaves 2 of your 5
...}` and `Shield Sphere {0} {leaves 5 of your 5 ...}` on ONE menu. Master + Sphere were
affordable together - two blockers. It took Ob Nixilis, tapped out, and s12's menu had two rows
left. At s14 the block window ran the survival arithmetic perfectly and the reply concluded
*"There is no way to survive this combat"*. The gate fired at the only window where it could not
help.

**Rule.** For every guide gate phrased as survival, ask which ASK KIND spends the resource the
gate needs, and put the gate there - the block window inherits a board the cast window bought.
Concretely: a survival check is written against the numbers printed on the CAST prompt (your
life, your own header's `of which N are creatures`, their header's `M of them without a
restriction against attacking`) and it becomes numbered step 0 of the cast order (#165), not a
paragraph under a different heading (#158).

## S-2. CLOSING A RULE'S "EMPTY CASE" CREATES A MIRROR FAILURE ON ITS **EXEMPT** CASE UNLESS BOTH
## BRANCHES GET IMPERATIVES IN THE SAME EDIT.

Wave 52 shipped #185 at this deck: *"a two-row ask whose only spell is a second engine at K of 0
is the brake FIRING, not the brake failing: row 2 IS the answer"* - three imperatives, a
WHAT-THIS-COST, its own paragraph. The rule's exemption (the FIRST Howling Mine is cast at K of
0) stayed a half-sentence with none. Result this corpus: second Mines declined **3/3** at K=0 and
taken **4/4** at K>=1 - the brake works perfectly - while the untapped, untagged FIRST Mine was
declined on a two-row menu **11 times across three games**, arriving four turns late twice
(`162v130` s9/s10/s12/s13; `162v146` s6/s7/s10/s11/s15). Wave 52's over-brake count was 1.

**Rule.** #183's diagnostic is run on the EDIT, not only on the guide: after writing a fix for
one branch, strip the headings and count the imperatives on each branch of the pair. Where the
branches are "the rule fires" and "the rule is exempt", both need the same register and both need
the printed discriminator named. Here the discriminator is exact and free - a second copy's row
carries `[second copy: you already control Howling Mine; both stay on the battlefield - no legend
rule]` and a first copy's row does not - so the exemption costs one sentence keyed to a tag that
rendered 11 times with 4 takes, all legal.
