YOUR DECK: Orzhov (white-black) dungeon MIDRANGE - removal, bodies that VENTURE INTO THE DUNGEON,
two planeswalkers, a manland. BEATDOWN vs slow decks, CONTROL vs aggro.
ENGINE, in order: a land every turn -> a cheap body or removal on curve -> a planeswalker you can
pay for -> venture whenever a venture body attacks -> close with attacks, Kaya's -3, Lolth's
Spiders and the drains. Life you pay buys a cast THIS window.

THE WHOLE CAST ORDER. At every "which card do you cast now" ask on your turn, read the entries top
to bottom and take the FIRST that matches; stop there. Never sit on removal.
 1. Removal (Vanishing Verse, Soul Shatter) when their line shows a creature or planeswalker you
    can hit - for Verse, only when its target list names one of THEIRS with power above 0 and no
    "[defender]"; Soul Shatter leaves this entry when its row says "at 0 this does nothing" or
    "tied at MV 0" - tokens, and their real threats stay.
 2. LOLTH or KAYA if listed. THE COUNTER TEST FIRST, off two printed facts on this screen: their
    graveyard line or the log names a card of theirs that counters spells, AND "Their untapped
    sources" reads 2 or more -> SKIP THIS ENTRY THIS WINDOW; take the cheapest entries below that
    leave sources up, and take her on a later menu whose row leaves sources for a second row you
    also take that turn. Past that test - and a "[legendary: you already control ...]" row only
    under the loyalty test in PLANESWALKERS - cast her NOW, AND USE A LOYALTY ABILITY THE SAME TURN:
    a walker is never summoning sick and never enters tapped, so she arrives at full loyalty and one
    ability is legal at once on your own main phase, asked at its own window after the cast.
 3. Nadaar, Barrowin or Triumphant Adventurer - a body that ventures.
 4. Silverquill Silencer, when no entry-3 body is listed.
 5. Silverquill Command, when its "{modes live right now: ...; dead ...}" clause names a mode
    you want BEFORE the semicolon.
 6. Emeria's Call, when the option is listed.
 7. Pelakka Predation, when their hand size is 3 or more.
 8. Acererak; 8b. Silverquill Command with nothing live before the semicolon (a card for 1 life).
 9. "Cast nothing right now".
Entry 9 is the LAST line, not a tiebreaker: unless the engine marked a row dead ("at 0 this does
nothing"), the reason a row does not match must be a fact on THIS screen. Take the ask again if
offered - you may cast more than one spell a turn.

SPELLS
- VANISHING VERSE ({W}{B}, instant): exiles ONE MONOCOLORED permanent cleanly - no death trigger.
  ITS TARGET LIST IS THE ONLY TRUTH - fire at the best name on "legal targets right now: X, Y";
  "the only legal targets are YOUR OWN right now" -> do not cast it - exile is not a bounce. A body
  printing "[defender]" or a power of 0 never attacks you: NEVER SPEND VERSE ON ONE. Hold it - Verse
  is this deck's only answer to a monocoloured ENCHANTMENT or ARTIFACT, and those beat you.
- SOUL SHATTER ({2}{B}, instant): they SACRIFICE their highest-mana-value creature or planeswalker.
  It does not target, so it answers hexproof, indestructible and artifact creatures Verse cannot.
  THE ROW NAMES WHAT DIES ("{right now: they sacrifice X (MV n, their highest)}"); a row
  offering N tied names is the list they choose ONE from. A NAMED PERMANENT IS A REAL KILL AT ANY
  MANA VALUE, 0 included; only "at 0 this does nothing" takes it off the list.
- KAYA THE INEXORABLE ({3}{W}{B}): her -3 EXILES ANY nonland permanent - multicolored, colorless,
  artifact, planeswalker - what Verse cannot touch. SHE ENTERS AT 5 AND DIES AT 0: each -3 spends
  three of her five counters and can cost the second -3 this deck otherwise never gets.
  THE -3 LADDER, top first, stopping at the first name the list offers: a PLANESWALKER of theirs -
  nothing else here answers one; a noncreature permanent of theirs making cards, damage or life
  every turn; the body the CRACK-BACK line prices at 4 OR MORE - "N from 1 attacker nothing you
  control can legally block" first, else the top of their power line, flier or trampler first.
  EVERY OTHER BOARD IS THE +1, a board naming nothing on that ladder included: a "[defender]", a
  power of 0, a lord, an anthem, a token, a body priced at 3 or less. She keeps the counters and
  takes a real name the turn one lands. ONE LINE OUTRANKS THAT: the crack-back line says the attack
  KILLS you and exiling a listed body stops it - then -3 it at any size.
  Every row on her -3 list reading "[your battlefield]" -> take the +1 or pass.
- SILVERQUILL COMMAND ({2}{W}{B}, SORCERY - your main phases only): choose TWO of its four modes.
  * The CAST ROW's live/dead clause is the truth about which modes exist ("return creature" before
    its semicolon = return is live). The mode ask re-lists dead pairs UNMARKED, so carry the cast
    row's dead list into the ask.
  * TIMING: want the pump in combat -> cast in MAIN 1, before attackers; the +3/+3 ends this turn,
    so "pump now, attack next turn" and any main-2 pump is wrong. After combat, or with nothing
    able to attack, pair sacrifice with draw.
  * TARGET: asked to pick ONE creature, pick YOUR OWN - that one gets the +3/+3.
  * DRAW DIRECTION: that player draws AND loses 1. Point it at YOURSELF; at them only when that 1
    life finishes them.
- SILVERQUILL SILENCER ({W}{B}): it fires only when they CAST the named card, so name one they can
  still cast. Every row prints "{copies not yet in a public zone: N of theirs}" and the list is
  SORTED BY THAT N - that N is the decision. Take the highest N, breaking ties toward a counterspell
  or a sweeper, then the missing half of a pair on their line (Sanguine Bond out -> Exquisite Blood)
  and anything Pelakka Predation showed you; a body last, an N of 0 never. "{visible now: ...}" says
  where SPENT copies sit and never raises a row.
- PELAKKA PREDATION ({2}{B}): a discard of mana value 3 OR MORE only - a bomb, not a curve.
  ON ITS REVEAL LIST take the card that will actually be CAST: a sweeper ("Destroy all", "Each
  ... sacrifices") or a counterspell ahead of ANY creature, however big - the huge body is one they
  never reach. Their "of which N are creatures" at least three above your creature count ->
  not a play: cast the biggest body you can afford, or removal for their engine.
- DO NOT CAST AGADEEM'S AWAKENING - EVER. It returns CREATURE CARDS only, so no X returns Kaya or
  Lolth and the cast taps you out for nothing. It is a land here.

LANDS AND MANA. Shineshadow Snarl taps for {W} OR {B} by itself. If a Cast option is listed you CAN
pay for it - never decline a spell for its colour. PLAY A LAND EVERY TURN: a land entering TAPPED
still goes down, because the drop does not carry to the next turn. Snarl enters TAPPED unless you
reveal a Plains or Swamp; Hive enters TAPPED once you control two or more other lands.
- A PATHWAY IS ONE CARD WITH TWO LAND FACES (Brightclimb taps {W}, Grimclimb taps {B}) offered as
  two rows on the same menu, and both enter untapped. TAKE THE FACE THAT MAKES THE COLOUR YOUR HAND
  IS SHORT OF, not the one named in your carried plan: read the "[cannot pay now: ...]" brackets and
  "Mana sources among those lands". No black source in play and a {b} spell in hand -> the BLACK
  face is the only right row; the white face is how this deck loses to its own mana.
- EMERIA'S CALL, AGADEEM'S AWAKENING AND PELAKKA PREDATION ARE LANDS (Emeria taps {W}; Agadeem and
  Pelakka Caverns tap {B}); their row "<land name> with <spell name> -> PLAY THIS AS A LAND" costs
  no mana. Play a real land first and hold the land-backed card; take its land row when the
  alternative is no land drop or its spell face is dead. Pelakka Caverns shows no 3-life menu.
- The hand header counts these three TWICE on purpose: the LAND number is true, the SPELL number is
  one too high per such card. "Mana sources among those lands" counts the back face already: never
  discard one while it is a colour's only source.
- THE 3-LIFE MENU AFTER AN EMERIA OR AGADEEM: ANSWER "tap" BY DEFAULT. Pay only when BOTH hold:
  (a) a card in hand needs exactly one more mana than your untapped sources make, and a casting
  window this turn can still take it; (b) "{this payment puts you at N}" is above the power total on
  their line. NEVER AT 3 LIFE OR LESS.
- HIVE OF THE EYE TYRANT: {3}{B} - FOUR mana - for a 3/3 menace attacker until end of turn.
  A Hive you controlled as the turn began IS NOT SUMMONING SICK: animate it in your own main phase
  and it ATTACKS THIS TURN; it needs no haste and no window gives it one.
  AFFORDABILITY: FOUR OTHER untapped sources - its own mana never pays for it. Before answering
  "becomes beholder" run these in order, stopping at the first that fires.
  CHECK 0 - Add the power of every untapped creature of yours that could attack, plus 3, and weigh
    that sum against their printed life. At or above their life -> ANIMATE and swing,
    whatever the phase.
  CHECK 0b - Their line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and your line shows 0 able to attack ->
    ANIMATE at any phase (your only clock), unless the row is tagged [this land is TAPPED] or that
    mana pays a cast you make.
  CHECK 1 - "Phase:" says Upkeep and CHECK 0 did not fire -> PASS.
  CHECK 2 - that Hive already carries a (P/T) on your line, or its ROW prints TAPPED in any form
    -> PASS: a tapped Hive becomes a 3/3 that neither attacks nor blocks and the mana is gone.

COMBAT - ATTACKING TAPS YOUR BLOCKERS for your opponent's whole turn (Nadaar has vigilance and
attacks free). Never block with a creature you attack with.
- A-line prints "[no creature they control can block this attacker]" -> SEND IT, at every life
  total, ahead of every rule below except CHECK A. No tag, wall or converter rule reaches it.
- CHECK 0 - "[doesn't untap during its controller's untap step]" taps it for the REST OF THE GAME.
  Exemptions: that tag also contains "vigilance" -> send always; the header reads "They have 0
  untapped creatures able to block" AND an untapped creature of yours remains after the declaration
  -> send those that get through. Otherwise send only when the power you send is at or above their
  printed life THIS turn; else ATTACK: none.
- CHECK A - printed life 8 or less and exactly ONE creature on your line carrying neither "[tapped"
  nor "[summoning sick" -> it does not attack, whatever its tag says, an empty tag included. Nadaar
  (vigilance) is exempt.
- CHECK B - an all-"(neither dies" tag: sending exactly ONE creature -> ATTACK: none (wall damage
  does not carry between turns); with more, send only when your attackers OUTNUMBER their untapped
  blockers, or it has flying or menace. THEY choose which attacker each blocker stops, so a surplus
  protects the swing, not a card.
- A tag collapsing several blockers into one line prices ONE blocker; a "GANG BLOCK:" clause prices
  your toughness against the SUM. GANG BLOCK clause present, or the tag counting 2 or more blockers
  -> that creature STAYS HOME if it is a CARD; a dungeon GOBLIN TOKEN goes anyway, free bait. One
  blocker, no GANG BLOCK clause -> the parenthesis is the story.
- ANY tag name reads "your attacker dies, their blocker lives" -> that creature stays home while the
  team attacks (tokens still go). Price the WORST name; other names, outnumbering them, a friendlier
  A-line and "[held back, it CANNOT block ...]" do not cancel it.
- ANY blocker prints "(blocking trigger: they gain N)", "(blocking trigger: they may gain N)" or
  "(lifelink: they gain N)" -> that swing PAYS them: send only what gets through and win with
  removal, Kaya and the drains. A tag line that KILLS one of them is no exception, and prices ADD
  across a gang block. No such clause -> their walls are free to attack.
- "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" printed AND any priced clause on a tag ->
  that price comes off YOUR life, so that attacker STAYS HOME however many you have; only
  no-blocker bodies go. A plan saying "net 0", "net zero", "drained from them" or "the game ends
  before that damage matters" is wrong.
- LETHAL OVERRIDES EVERY STOP ABOVE: their printed life at or below the total power of what you
  send -> send it all.
- THE RENDER DID YOUR COMBAT MATH: "Barrowin of Clan Undurr (5/5) (printed 3/3)" hits for 5 now -
  add the rendered numbers, never rebuild them from base stats.

BLOCK LADDER - one parenthesis per offered block, and the parenthesis is the answer. SURVIVAL
FIRST: the header says the attack IS LETHAL -> assign blockers to the biggest "deals N" attackers
until the damage still coming through is below your printed life, whatever the parentheses say;
only then spend spare blockers on the ladder.
 0. "(neither dies" -> TAKE IT (a price clause after those words still applies, and only a
    lifegain price makes this block worse than none).
 1. "(you kill it, your blocker lives)" -> TAKE IT, always, at every life total.
 2. "(both die)" -> TAKE IT unless the attacker it kills is worth less than your blocker.
 3. "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> the chump line, the ONLY one the forecast decides.
    Read "you would be at N": block at 10 or less, or when the header says lethal; above 10 leave it
    unblocked. "...N tramples to your face" does not stop that N - chump a trampler only at lethal,
    never two blockers on one.
ONE BLOCKER STOPS ALL of a non-trampler's damage: a blocked non-trampler deals you ZERO, so "I take
the damage either way" and "preserve it for future blocks" are false at N of 10 or less. "BLOCKS:
none" is right only when every offered block is rung 3 above your floor.

VENTURE. Adventurer ventures on attack, Nadaar on enter AND attack, Acererak and Barrowin on enter.
- ACERERAK IS NOT A BODY: he bounces himself to hand on entry unless Tomb of Annihilation is
  complete, so he is never at declare-attackers or declare-blockers and his printed 5 power is in no
  damage math. He IS a {2}{B} repeatable venture: cast him with spare mana only.
- WHICH DUNGEON: whenever the three-dungeon list appears - first venture and after each completion
  - answer LOST MINE OF PHANDELVER by NAME (option numbers are not fixed). Every Lost Mine room is
  upside and none costs you life. TOMB OF ANNIHILATION only while your printed life is 5 or more
  ABOVE theirs; MAD MAGE only in a slow grind for raw cards.
- ROOM BRANCHES print each room's effect ("{room effect: ...}"). DECIDE ON THE EFFECT TEXT ALONE:
  the room that MAKES A BODY or DRAINS them beats a Treasure, a counter or a scry. Never work out
  which room you are on.

PLANESWALKERS - USE THEM THE TURN THEY LAND AND EVERY TURN AFTER; an idle walker is wasted and
"she only just came down" is never a reason to wait.
- LOLTH: -3 first, THE TURN SHE ARRIVES whenever a crack-back line is aimed at you. Her two 2/1
  Spiders have REACH - they BLOCK A FLIER - and menace; they are blockers this deck cannot buy any
  other way, and every creature of yours that dies puts a counter back on her, so chump-blocking
  with them refuels her. LOLTH'S 0 HAS A LIFE FLOOR OF 6: printed life 6 or less -> do NOT take the
  0; pass, or -3.
- KAYA: USING HER IS NOT SPENDING HER - her +1 is a USE. Her -3 runs the ladder in SPELLS.
- A SECOND KAYA OR LOLTH IN HAND IS A LOYALTY RESET FOR A SPENT COPY AND NOTHING ELSE. A cast row
  tagged "[legendary: you already control ...]" is LIVE: casting it bins ONE of the two copies and
  YOU CHOOSE WHICH at the legend-rule ask, so the survivor is whichever you keep. DECIDE BY THE
  LOYALTY ON YOUR BATTLEFIELD LINE. Below 3, so it cannot pay a -3: CAST THE SECOND COPY, bin the
  spent one, and -3 the fresh one the same turn - the only way this deck fires a second -3, and
  skipping the row as "legendary, useless" loses the game. At 3 or more the cast buys nothing and
  costs a card and the turn's mana: take the next entry on THE WHOLE CAST ORDER instead.

THE HOLD-PRIORITY ROW ("Hold priority - pass now ...") is a DURABLE pass - it answers every later
window carrying these same rows - and it is NOT the one-window pass. Read the "[hold check: ...]"
line: "every row above was also on the menu at the last window" -> take HOLD once you have decided
on no action; "N rows above are new" or "first window I have asked you at this seam" -> decline this
window only. NEVER hold a list offering a Kaya
or Lolth loyalty ability or the Hive animation - holding gives them up while the rows stand. With no
numbered pass row on a priority list, the one-window pass is "CHOICE: 0 (pass)": it is legal.
A RUN OF DECLINES IS THE FAILURE MODE: once a decline row says you have already declined this exact
list 2 or more times this turn, and nothing on it will become payable this turn, TAKE HOLD.

MULLIGAN. THE ENGINE COUNTS YOUR HAND FOR YOU - the land/spell count, "Mana sources among those
lands", "Cheapest spell in this hand", "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of".
Those counts are correct and are the whole decision; never recount. Where you are is the ask's
"(keeping N)" number - each hand is a fresh 7.
- (keeping 5) or lower with even ONE land -> KEEP; shipping buys one card fewer at the same land
  odds. Zero lands, or all lands, is the only fresh look worth a card.
- On the untouched 7 or at (keeping 6), a mulligan is right for exactly three hands: ZERO lands; an
  all-land hand (6-7); or ONE land under "Playing every land in this hand would not cover any spell
  in it" WITH the bracketed COUNT reason. That line is a ONE-LAND TEST AND NOTHING ELSE - at TWO OR
  MORE lands never a ship. Any other hand is a KEEP, one land under "would cover the cost of:" too.
- COLOUR IS NEVER A REASON TO SHIP, AT ANY LOOK: 14 of your 21 lands make white, 15 black, 8
  either. Every bracketed COLOUR reason is what this forbids.
- A CARRIED PLAN IS ABOUT A HAND ALREADY THROWN AWAY - decide from this header and the price line.
  Never ship for too many lands, for lacking a one-drop, or for colour.

CLEANUP DISCARD ("your maximum hand size is 7"). Answer "PUT: n, m" with the numbers you SEND. SCAN
in this order, stopping at the first hit - never row 1 because it is first:
 (1) Highest printed mana value among cards that are NEITHER LAND NOR LAND-BACKED. Emeria's Call,
     Agadeem's Awakening and Pelakka Predation are LAND-BACKED and not eligible; the row will not
     say so - all three print as plain sorceries.
 (2) A row printing "{you already control one: <name>}", whatever its mana value - unless the
     copy you control is a walker below 3 loyalty, which makes the one in hand a reset (see KAYA).
 (3) A second copy of a card whose first copy you keep; between two "{dead right now: ...}" rows,
     the higher printed mana value.
 (4) A LAND, once your line prints four or more ("{spare: you control N lands already}"); below
     four lands the PUT line holds spell numbers only.
 (5) Otherwise the HIGHEST PRINTED MANA VALUE left - read the {..} cost off each row.
Never send your only removal while their line shows a creature or a planeswalker.

WHEN THEY COUNTER AND GAIN LIFE (Cancel, Supreme Verdict, Elixir of Immortality, Staff of Nin in
their log): Elixir and Staff are COLORLESS ARTIFACTS - never on Verse's list, and Soul Shatter does
nothing to them, so KAYA'S -3 IS THE ONLY ANSWER once her list names no planeswalker of theirs, and
she is the card this deck cannot afford to lose to a counter. The counter test in THE WHOLE CAST
ORDER is what keeps her out of open blue mana.

SANGUINE BOND AND EXQUISITE BLOOD. Either name on Verse's target list or on Kaya's -3 list -> take
it, above any creature. That is a REMOVAL rule and never a Silencer name. With BOTH out the drain
loops without limit: ATTACK: none whatever the tags say until one is gone, and a "[lifelink]"
attacker of theirs ends the game blocked or not - the combat header's "NOT lethal" is not about
that chain.

LEGEND RULE. The row you PICK is the one that DIES: pick the copy tagged "[summoning sick" or
carrying fewer counters.
