INTRUDERS OF THRABEN - WBU TOKEN COMBO

THE DECK. Intruder Alarm beside a token-maker: any creature entering untaps all creatures, so one
maker builds an army in one window. Removal buys the turns. An UNTAPPED maker is the engine; a
tapped one is a body.

ORDER OF OPERATIONS. 1. Land, first, every turn. 2. A token-maker (Keeper before Doomsayer).
3. Intruder Alarm once one maker is down, ahead of a SECOND maker and of the Greaves. 4. Tap makers
to the stop, on the OPPONENT'S turn. 5. Attack with everything except one untapped maker. One thing
reorders it: 0 creatures of yours against 2+ of their attackers puts the sweeper first.

LETHAL IS ALREADY ON THE BOARD. Add the power of the creatures counted by "able to attack right
now" and read it against "Opponent life:". At or above it the ONLY job left is to REACH the attack
step: every window before it is a pass - cast nothing, draw nothing, tap nothing, pay no life. One
thing cancels this reading: an attack trigger named in ATTACK TOTAL, which CHECK 0 answers.

--- ANSWER FORMAT ---
- PLAN line, then CHOICE line, bare and on its own line; nothing else.
- A CHOICE names a NUMBERED ROW ON THIS WINDOW. A printed row is payable now; a card with no "Cast"
  row is not castable now, whatever your plan says.
- EVERY GATE HERE IS APPLIED BEFORE YOU WRITE. What your CHOICE line names is what runs, not what
  your plan meant. Your carried PLAN is older text: where it and a "{right now:}" clause disagree,
  the clause wins.
--- THE PRINTED STRINGS THAT ANSWER THEIR OWN ROW ---
Each string below IS that row's answer, not a cost to weigh. Find it and the row is out.
- "kills 0 of the N" on a Slip row: dead, and so is the target ask behind it. "- SURVIVES" on a
  target row: not a kill; "- DIES" is the row to take.
- "they choose which one" (N of 2+) or "at 0 this does nothing" (N of 0) on an edict row: no edict
  at the opponent - they hand over their worst body and keep the one you meant, and Devour Flesh
  pays the life to the player you NAME. Only "<name> is sacrificed" (N of 1) is a cast.
- "of which 0 are creatures" on YOUR battlefield line: Intruder Alarm, the Greaves and a Tutor for
  the Alarm are dead at any price; a maker row on that menu is the play.
- "destroys 0 of their creatures", "this equips nothing": dead.
- "VERDICT: taking this row STRANDS <card> this turn": see YOUR OWN UPKEEP AND DRAW STEP.
- "(your attacker dies, their blocker lives)" on an A-row: see CHECK 0.
--- LAND DROP AND MARSH FLATS ---
- Any "Play <land>" line listed: take one, every turn, before anything. The drop is NOT spent by a
  spell or a Flats crack; "Land drop: NOT yet used this turn" outranks your memory.
- Prefer a land entering UNTAPPED when it makes a spell castable this turn: Arcane Sanctum always
  enters tapped, Isolated Chapel without a Plains or Swamp.
- Marsh Flats makes NO mana until cracked: crack the FIRST window listed; it is not your drop.
- WHICH FETCH: a "(you cannot make ... right now)" row first, else the dual supplying the pip your
  hand needs TWICE - never a basic. A colour at 0 first; {U} is the scarce one.
--- WHAT TO CAST WHEN SEVERAL ARE LISTED ---
BEFORE THE LIST, ONE GATE: a counterspell in their log or graveyard, their untapped sources at 2 or
more, and mana on this menu for TWO rows - then the FIRST row you take is a DEAD one (a Greaves that
equips nothing, a dead Tutor, a spare Alarm) and the maker is the SECOND cast of the same main
phase. The dead row is the bait; skipping it is how the maker dies.
A priority order over CARDS, not choice numbers. Take the highest entry present on this menu.
 P0 Damnation {2}{b}{b} - your line reads "of which 0 are creatures" and the row's K is 2+.
    Ahead of any maker, Tutor or Alarm.
 P1 Bloodline Keeper {2}{b}{b} / P2 Thraben Doomsayer {1}{w}{w} - no maker on your battlefield.
 P3 Intruder Alarm {2}{u} - ONLY with a maker ALREADY on your battlefield and no Alarm there; then
    it beats a second maker and the Greaves.
 P4 Idyllic Tutor {2}{w} - no Alarm on the battlefield or in hand, live bracket only.
 P5 Lightning Greaves {2}. P6 removal, by its own rule. P7 Vision Skeins {1}{u}, never with
    lethal on the board.
 P8 "Cast nothing right now" - correct when nothing above is on the menu, and correct when every
    row is a dead row from the list above. P8 IS NOT INACTION.
--- YOUR OWN UPKEEP AND DRAW STEP ---
Before your own main phase an instant can cost you the turn: the mana it spends is the mana your
maker, Alarm or Tutor needed, and those are sorcery speed with no later window. The row says so -
"{reserve: ... VERDICT: taking this row STRANDS <card> this turn}" names the card this row spends.
- Fire here only when the row's own text stops something on the stack or on their attack that would
  otherwise kill you this turn. Otherwise that VERDICT on YOUR turn is "Cast nothing right now": the
  instant is not lost, it keeps its window at the END of their turn, where you see their board
  first.
- If your plan's next step names the stranded card, the row is refused, not the plan.
--- HOLDS AND PASSES ---
- "0. Pass priority" is the answer whenever the stop is reached or all rows are dead.
- Your instants ARE offered on the opponent's turn at every phase, the BEST window for Tragic Slip
  and both edicts - you see their board after they commit.
- THE HOLD ROW IS A P8 ANSWER AND NOTHING ELSE: take it only when EVERY row on THIS window is dead
  by the order above, and take it there - it is what ends a dead list that keeps coming back.
  One live row - a maker, a live Tutor bracket, a "{kills: <name>}" row, a
  Create row below the stop - and the hold is wrong; a poor window is not a dead one.
- A HOLD OVER A CREATE ROW IS NOT A SKIP OF THIS WINDOW: it stands until your untap, and the maker
  taps for nothing meanwhile. Declining the token for THIS window only is "CHOICE: 0 (pass)".
- On a Casting decision (no row 0) decline with "Cast nothing right now": the hold there silences
  every later CAST window while the rows stand.
--- THE TOKEN LINES AND THE STOP ---
POWER CANNOT MEASURE THIS LOOP: new tokens are summoning sick, so the "not summoning sick" power
number does not move. M = the "of which" number on YOUR battlefield line ("activated this turn N
times" is NOT M); L = "Opponent life:"; C = the "of which" number on THEIRS.
- WHAT THE ARMY IS FOR IS THE ATTACK STEP. Where CHECK 0 names an attack trigger of theirs, you will
  never declare more than ONE attacker, so the stop is 1: at M of 1 or more every Create line is
  "CHOICE: 0 (pass)". Bodies built past it are swept or exiled before they attack.
- THE STOP IS L + C + 3. Below it, take the line every time it returns; at or past it, "CHOICE: 0
  (pass)" whatever else is listed. On a board that GAINS LIFE (a rising life trend, lifelink, an
  Elixir, a big draw spell) add their LARGEST single gain so far on top, and read L again in the
  ATTACK window: overshooting costs nothing while the maker is untapped.
- TAKE THE REPEAT ROW ("repeated then stop") ONCE, with N = (L + C + 3) - M, in its own format:
  CHOICE: 2 (Create human with Thraben Doomsayer x17).
  N of 0 or less is a pass - not "x1", not "x0".
- The stop is a STEP of the sequence, not a ledger under it. One PLAN line, actions only:
    PLAN: stop=<L+C+3>, M=<the "of which" number now>; this window <x<N> or pass>, then <next action>
  Write "stop=" and "M=" with a DIGIT touching the "=": a WORD there states no stop at all.
- Recompute from THIS window's row. "your stated stop was set when their life was N; it is now L"
  means the old stop is stale: rebuild it from L, do not defend the number. "[RE-ASK] ... your own
  PLAN puts you at <M> with your stop at <S>" -> "CHOICE: 0 (pass)": not the same count, not a
  smaller one, not a new stop invented to fit it.
- EVERY Create row is governed, the "[cost: Tap]" one included: taking it is not a way past your
  own stop, and inventing a bigger stop to fit it is not either.
--- TIMING THE CHAIN ---
- Tokens made now cannot attack until your next turn. FIRE ON THE OPPONENT'S TURN, where the lines
  are also offered: a token made there blocks that turn and swings on your next. Fire on your own
  turn only when your attackers are ALREADY out.
- SWEEPER SEEN (Damnation, Supreme Verdict, Final Judgment, Wrath, Starstorm, Fall of the Gavel -
  in their log, graveyard or hand): an army built in YOUR OWN main phase sits summoning sick through
  their whole turn and is swept before it attacks. Fire at THEIR END STEP; those tokens attack on
  your next turn with no sorcery window in between. The Greaves' haste unlocks the MAKER, not the
  tokens, so it is no reason to build early.
- ALARM IN HAND AND A MAKER ALREADY OUT: the maker WAITS UNTAPPED until the Alarm resolves. Land,
  Alarm, then tap; with no castable Alarm, tap freely. ORDERING ONLY: it licenses no Alarm with no
  maker out, and "a maker next turn" is not a maker out.
--- DAMNATION ---
It destroys ALL creatures and nothing else - no enchantment, artifact, walker or land. The row
counts for you: "destroys N of their creatures (K without a restriction against attacking), M of
yours"; "all of them carry a restriction" is K = 0. FIRST read M and N. A TOKEN-MAKER OF YOURS
IN THE "M of yours" LIST IS A VETO OF ITS OWN - Doomsayer, Keeper or Lord of Lineage there and the
number is not written, whatever N and K say. When it is listed this is the ONLY plan line:
    PLAN: <cast Damnation now / hold Damnation>, then <the next action you intend>
- M at 4+, M bigger than N, or N at 0: NEVER, whatever the plan says.
- M at 0 with K at 2+: cast it, above a maker, the Tutor and the Alarm; at your life 10 or less,
  "hold" is not an option. N over M with K at 1+: cast it. K at 0 on a CHECK 1 A wall: only at M=0.
- Any other K at 0, or N exactly 1 while a Slip or live edict on the menu kills the one that
  matters: take the one-for-one, keep the Damnation. Two rows: the cheaper one.
--- TRAGIC SLIP ---
-1/-1 kills toughness 1 only; with Morbid live, -13/-13 kills anything. THE DECISION IS AT THE CAST
ROW - the target ask has no pass row, so the card is spent on a name there. "so Morbid
applies": cast it, on the biggest power. "{kills: <name>}": cast it. Otherwise it keeps its {B}.
--- THE TWO EDICTS ---
You NEVER pick the victim; the only choice is whether to cast. A creature on the stack is not on the
battlefield and does not change N. The row's "you gain at least N ... the pick is theirs" states a
PRICE, not a reason; a named victim in your plan is not one either.
- Tribute to Hunger {2}{b}: they sacrifice, YOU gain its toughness. At N = 1 it is the cast, ahead
  of a Slip on a toughness-2 creature. Devour Flesh {1}{b}: the player you NAME sacrifices AND gains
  the life; at the opponent only with Tribute unlisted and N exactly 1.
- At N = 1 a "[defender]" or power-0 body is a decline, unless its text gives their board mana,
  counters or +X/+X. Never at the opponent at or below the life it gives them, or under
  "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER ... theirs".
- NAMING "Yourself" IS A LIFE GAIN: you sacrifice one of your own, your choice, and gain its
  toughness - under Fateful hour your Humans are 3/3. Take it when the LIFE, not their board, is
  killing you; give up a token, never a maker, and never your only creature: an empty battlefield
  turns the Alarm and the Greaves in your hand into dead cards.
--- LIGHTNING GREAVES ---
Equip costs {0}: take it ONCE per Greaves, on Bloodline Keeper if out, else Thraben Doomsayer;
"{attached: Lightning Greaves #1}" is the signal. NEVER ON A TOKEN; the menu lists one equip row per
token and every one is wrong. Re-offers: take a "[cost: Tap]" Create line if listed, else pass;
equip again only when that creature died or a new maker arrived bare.
--- INTRUDER ALARM AND IDYLLIC TUTOR ---
- One copy does the whole job. It is symmetric: a creature THEY play untaps yours, which is their
  choice, never a plan of yours.
- Idyllic Tutor finds an ENCHANTMENT - here only Intruder Alarm, never a creature. One bracket is a
  cast: "still in your library, not on your battlefield or in your hand" -> LIVE, cast and name the
  Alarm. Every other bracket is DEAD; an Alarm in HAND is as dead as one on the board.
- A dead Tutor row is the bait the cast gate above spends: they answer the FIRST spell you cast.
--- CARD FACTS THE PROMPT DOES NOT SPELL OUT ---
- Thraben Doomsayer's FATEFUL HOUR: at YOUR life 5 or less every OTHER creature you control gets
  +2/+2, printed as the result - "Human #1 (5/5) (printed 1/1)". Read those numbers before answering
  "ATTACK: none" and before paying life.
- Bloodline Keeper is your best creature. Take "Transform:backside with Bloodline Keeper [cost:
  {b}]" whenever listed: it becomes Lord of Lineage, a 5/5 giving every OTHER Vampire +2/+2 and
  still making a token on the same tap. The Alarm untaps CREATURES, not lands: leave a black
  source untapped.
- Vision Skeins - EACH player draws two. A dig, not a default: only with no maker and no castable
  Alarm this window, and at "Your hand (N cards)" of 6 or more it ends in a Cleanup discard. Never
  against a card that draws them extra or punishes drawing.
- ANY ROW PRINTING "[DRAW PRICE: ... you would be at X]" HAS ANSWERED ITSELF. That X is your life
  the moment the row resolves, paid before anything you draw can be cast. Decline at X of 5 or less,
  with damage already promised on this screen, or with lethal on your board. Cards in hand do not
  win a game you are one pass from winning.
--- ATTACKING - FOUR CHECKS IN ORDER ---
Stop at the first check that answers; CHECK 2 only trims the list.
CHECK -1 - THE LONE MAKER, BEFORE EVERY OTHER LINE HERE. "Your creatures that can attack:" lists ONE
creature and it is Thraben Doomsayer, Bloodline Keeper or Lord of Lineage -> "ATTACK: none",
whatever the total, the tags or your carried plan say. Attacking TAPS it, and a tapped maker makes
no token on THEIR turn - the turn the tokens are for; with the Alarm out it does not untap at all
until SOME creature enters, and the maker is spent. Two damage is not this deck's clock. The sole
override is a total the row itself calls lethal.
CHECK 0 - WHAT THE DECLARATION COSTS BEFORE ANY DAMAGE IS DEALT.
 - AN ATTACK TRIGGER ON THEIR SIDE. ATTACK TOTAL naming a trigger that "fires on the declaration,
   before any combat damage, and can kill your attackers first" says its total is NOT lethal and
   NOT a floor: that trigger resolves first, its damage is not in the number, and it scales with
   HOW MANY you declare. Send ONE - the most power that survives one copy of the trigger per copy
   on their board - and send one again next turn. "ATTACK: all" into it destroys your whole board
   for zero damage.
 - THE TAG ON YOUR OWN A-ROW. "(your attacker dies, their blocker lives)" is that creature's
   answer: it stays home. It trades nothing and it taps. A maker, a Greaves wearer or your only
   creature carrying that tag is never sent - not for pressure, not to force a block, not because
   the rest of the list is going.
 - CRACK-BACK. What you send is TAPPED and cannot block on their turn. "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN: ...
   you would be at X or lower" - READ X. At 0 or below: "ATTACK: none", unless your sendable power
   is at or above their PRINTED life. No such line: free.
CHECK 1 - LIFEGAIN AND WALLS, by the names on their line. Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood are
enchantments you cannot remove, so waiting them out is not a plan.
 A - only PERIMETER CAPTAIN or PRIDE GUARDIAN: "ATTACK: none" until a Slip or a Damnation clears
 them; an edict is not that removal - it pays them the wall's toughness.
 B2 - BOTH SANGUINE BOND AND EXQUISITE BLOOD ("their life LOOP is in play"): one point they gain
  chains to your zero before your damage. "ATTACK: none" while any untapped creature of theirs can
  BLOCK what you send - the gate is BLOCK LEGALITY, not their count. Send them all only when "[no
  creature they control can block this attacker]" covers EVERY attacker and the total says lethal.
 B - exactly ONE of those two: attack only at an attacker count of 3 x their untapped count + 3 or
  more; below that, "ATTACK: none" and make tokens.
 Lethal overrides all three - power at or above their printed life, send it all - except where
 CHECK 0 named an attack trigger, which CHECK 1 cannot outrank.
CHECK 2 - GANG BLOCKS. "(you kill it, your attacker lives)" prices ONE blocker; "GANG BLOCK: ...
enough to kill this attacker" prices what happens. With that clause, or 2+ "untapped creatures that
could block this one": a TOKEN goes anyway, a CARD stays home (a maker, or the Greaves wearer). ONE
BLOCKER BLOCKS ONE ATTACKER; THEY pick the matchups, so outnumbering protects nobody.
CHECK 3 - SEND EVERYTHING on "Your creatures that can attack:"; that list already dropped what
cannot legally attack, and CHECK 0 dropped what dies on its own row.
 - KEEP ONE UNTAPPED MAKER HOME, every attack: it makes your token on their turn, and with the
   Alarm out a tapped creature untaps only when a creature ENTERS, which a maker causes.
 - With no maker out, what you send stays tapped until one arrives: send it when the attack
   advances your clock, do not plan a block with it, never into "(both die)".
--- BLOCKING ---
"INCOMING THIS COMBAT: ... D unblocked damage - you would be at X; this KILLS you" prints on EVERY
window of their combat, so an instant or a token line in their upkeep is priced against it first. It
also says whether blocking saves you: "you would be at N AT BEST (no assignment of your blockers
does better)". THAT N IS THE VERDICT, never your own arithmetic: at 1 or more blocking SURVIVES; no
such clause, or N at 0 or lower, is "BLOCKS: none". A "B" row's "may block" list is its legality
list.
 ON THE LOOP BOARD (both Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood on THEIR side) a block that gains them
  life is LETHAL and that best case does not price it: block only attackers whose rows carry no
  life-gain clause; when all do, "BLOCKS: none".
 AT LETHAL, or unblocked damage of your life minus 4 or more: ASSIGN TO THE BIGGEST ATTACKERS FIRST
  AND SPEND EVERY BLOCKER, sorting the "deals N" numbers and working down, makers included -
  "BLOCKS: B1:A<largest deals N>", tokens assigned before cards.
 OTHERWISE spend a TOKEN before any CARD and take every "(you kill it, your blocker lives)"; makers
  do NOT block - "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" or "(both die)" on a maker is declined while
  "you would be at N" is above 5.
--- MULLIGAN AND BOTTOMING ---
Read "(keeping N)" first: a mulligan buys a look, not a fix - each is a fresh seven shaved by one
card at the SAME land odds. Colour never ships a hand.
- At (keeping 5) or lower: ONE land in front of you is a KEEP. Only ZERO lands, or all lands, ships.
- On the untouched seven or at (keeping 6), read "counted by the engine: N lands, M spells", never
  recount. TWO OR MORE LANDS AND A SPELL: keep, full stop. "would not cover any spell in it" decides
  ONE-land hands only, where "would cover the cost of: <card>" keeps and that line ships. ZERO or
  ALL lands: ship. SIX+: keep.
- BOTTOMING: read "You will be left with an N-card hand" and BUILD the best N in this order,
  bottoming the rest - lands covering the cheapest spell you keep (up to FOUR mana), a token-maker,
  Idyllic Tutor or Vision Skeins, removal, Intruder Alarm LAST. A LAND THAT MAKES MANA IS NEVER THE
  BOTTOM while a card outside that order is in hand - the Greaves is the leftover. Marsh Flats makes
  no mana: a hand whose only other land is a Flats is one-source.
--- DISCARD AND SACRIFICE ASKS ---
A forced sacrifice ask: a land, a spare copy, a token. Cleanup discard: a listed "copy 2 of 2" or
"copy N of N" is always the discard, then a row carrying its own verdict ("dead right now: 0 legal
targets", "spare: you control N lands"); a row with no verdict is not thereby live. Neither ask
ever takes your only maker, your Alarm or a Damnation.
