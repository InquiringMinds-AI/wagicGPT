SELESNYA (GW) ADVENTURES — go-wide beatdown. You win by curving out cheap creatures,
drawing extra cards off Edgewall Innkeeper, flooding the board with 1/1 lifelink Soldiers
(Emmara, Trostani, March, Castle), pumping them (Trostani, Venerated Loxodon, Unbreakable
Formation, Flourish), and swinging for the win around turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN.
Deploy a creature and ATTACK every turn.

ADVENTURE CARDS SHOW TWO CAST OPTIONS — READ THIS FIRST. Five of your cards are adventures
(Faerie Guidemother, Giant Killer, Lovestruck Beast, Shepherd of the Flock, Flaxen Intruder).
Each appears TWICE in your list:
  "Cast NAME // ADV {base cost}"                  = the CREATURE (a real body on the board)
  "Cast NAME // ADV with its adventure cost {..}" = the ADVENTURE SPELL (an effect, then the
                                                    card is EXILED to cast as the creature LATER)
TRAP: the "with its adventure cost" option prints the CREATURE's (P/T) — e.g. "Heart's Desire
with its adventure cost {g} (5/5)". That (5/5) is NOT what you get now; it is the creature you
may cast LATER from exile. The adventure option does its SPELL effect only. Do NOT pick "with
its adventure cost" thinking you are deploying that body — you are not.

WHAT EACH FACE DOES (memorize; the option line does not tell you the effect):
  Faerie Guidemother {W} = 1/1 flyer. Gift of the Fae {1}{W} = target creature +2/+1 and flying
    until end of turn (a combat/evasion trick — use to push lethal).
  Giant Killer {W} = 1/2, can {1}{W}{T} to tap a creature. Chop Down {2}{W} = DESTROY target
    creature with power 4+ (your removal for big threats).
  Lovestruck Beast {2}{G} = 5/5, but CANNOT ATTACK unless you control a 1/1 creature.
    Heart's Desire {G} = make a 1/1 white Human (use it to UNLOCK Lovestruck, or as a body).
  Shepherd of the Flock {1}{W} = 3/1. Usher to Safety {W} = return a permanent YOU control to
    hand — cast this ONLY to save your own creature from removal or re-use an ETB; otherwise it
    bounces your own board for nothing. NEVER cast Usher to Safety just to "get a body."
  Flaxen Intruder {G} = 1/2. Welcome Home {5}{G}{G} = three 2/2 Bears (7 mana, rarely castable).

DEFAULT: cast the CREATURE face. Cast the adventure SPELL face only for its listed effect above.

EDGEWALL INNKEEPER DRAW ENGINE. While Edgewall Innkeeper (1/1) is on your battlefield, casting
any adventure CREATURE (the five above) DRAWS YOU A CARD. Casting the adventure SPELL face does
NOT draw. So play Innkeeper early and, with it out, cast adventure creatures for the free cards.
Innkeeper is your card advantage — deploy it turn 1-2 and keep it alive.

CONVOKE CARDS — Venerated Loxodon and March of the Multitudes. Convoke lets you TAP your
creatures to help pay. When "Cast X with its convoke cost" is offered, you CAN afford it — the
engine taps your creatures for you automatically (you do not choose which; a separate deferred
step handles it). Do NOT recompute mana or agonize; just pick the convoke option when you want it.
  Venerated Loxodon {4}{W} convoke = 4/4 that puts a +1/+1 counter on EACH creature that helped
    convoke it. Cast it AFTER you have a wide board of tapped-able creatures, to pump the team.
  March of the Multitudes {G}{W}{W}{X} convoke = make X 1/1 white lifelink Soldiers. Convoke to
    make X large. Great go-wide + lifegain.
  Bonus: tapping Emmara for convoke makes her trigger — a free 1/1 lifelink Soldier.

ATTACK EVERY TURN — per-attacker test over the offered A-list. A creature ATTACKS if ANY of:
no untapped blocker can kill it / it kills or trades up / the opponent has no untapped creatures.
When the opponent has no untapped blocker, SWING WITH EVERYONE — a 1/1 or 1/2 to the face is
real progress. Declare "no attackers" ONLY when every listed attacker would die for zero gain.
Do not spiral: pick the attackers and commit. EXCLUSION: the offered A-list already drops
ineligible creatures; if Lovestruck Beast is missing it is because you control no 1/1 — make a
Human (Heart's Desire / Castle Ardenvale) to unlock it.

ROLE CHECK (rerun every turn on your life totals). Default = beatdown. If YOUR life is falling
while theirs is not (a fast aggro race — e.g. mono-red), switch to LIFELINK STABILIZE: make
1/1 lifelink Soldiers (Emmara taps for one, Trostani makes two on entry, March makes X), block
with them and gain life. You STILL cast a creature every turn in both roles — never go passive.

PUMP AND CLOSE. Trostani Discordant gives your other creatures +1/+1 (an anthem — cast it to
grow the whole team). Unbreakable Formation cast in your MAIN phase gives every creature a
+1/+1 counter, vigilance, AND indestructible until end of turn — cast it before your alpha
strike, or in response to a board wipe to save your team. Flourish (Flower's back face,
{4}{G}{W}) gives all your creatures +2/+2 until end of turn. Before a lethal swing, TRUST the
printed [X/Y] on each creature — it already includes anthems and counters; do not recompute
power from base stats.

MULLIGAN: keep 2-4 lands with green AND white reachable (Temple Garden, Once Upon a Time, and
Flower all fix). Once Upon a Time is FREE if it is your first spell of the game — cast it turn 1
to dig for lands and creatures.
