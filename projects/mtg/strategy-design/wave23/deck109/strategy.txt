Deck identity: MONO-RED AGGRO (RDW), "Hellrider." Cheap fast creatures plus burn.
You win by dealing 20 fast - kill by turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN in every
matchup: attack every turn, cast something every turn, spend burn at the face.
You have no defensive plan - your only route to winning is a faster clock, so a
long game is a lost game. Your creatures are ATTACKERS, not blockers.

DEPLOY AND ATTACK - THE #1 RULE, run it every turn:
- Every main phase, if any "Cast" option shows a creature or Krenko's Command,
  take it - put a body on the board even if it cannot attack yet (it attacks
  NEXT turn). "Cast nothing" is for the rare turn when your ONLY castable option
  is an unaffordable card - NEVER pick it while a creature is in your Cast list.
  Even when you cannot assemble lethal or afford your bomb (a second Hellrider,
  Boros Reckoner) this turn, cast the biggest listed body instead: a 2/2 or 3/3
  now is damage next turn. Do not decline a listed creature because you are
  fixated on a card you cannot pay for - a stalled hand loses the race.
- Every combat, declare EVERY creature that can attack. Ash Zealot, Legion
  Loyalist, and Hellrider have haste - swing the turn they land. Trading a cheap
  attacker into a blocker is fine; you are racing, not preserving.
- COMMIT FAST at the attack step. Write your ATTACK line, then stop - do not
  re-derive the combat math over and over. Your ground creatures cannot block
  the opponent's fliers anyway, so a creature kept home does nothing; a creature
  swinging deals at least a chip of damage. When in doubt, attack.

MAIN PHASE 2 CHECKLIST - run it every turn after combat, in order:
1. LETHAL: (2 x each Pillar in hand) + (3 x each Spear in hand) >= opponent's
   life, with enough on the "Mana available:" line? Fire them ALL at "The
   opponent (player, life N)" NOW. The game ends this turn.
2. Otherwise, with mana left: cast Krenko's Command, then any creature in hand.
   Creatures cannot attack the turn they arrive (except haste) - cast them ANYWAY
   so they attack next turn.
3. End your turn with your mana SPENT; the top of your deck refills your hand.

BURN - FACE BY DEFAULT, WITH ONE EXCEPTION.
- Pillar of Flame (2 dmg, sorcery, exiles what it kills) and Searing Spear
  (3 dmg, instant) are your reach. Their cost is shown on the option line - pay
  it and FIRE. Pillar of Flame costs {R} = ONE red mana; Searing Spear costs
  {1}{R} = TWO. Do NOT confuse the two costs: if you have even one untapped red
  source and "Cast Pillar of Flame {r}" is listed, you CAN afford it - the
  option line's cost is the truth; never decline Pillar believing it costs two.
  DEFAULT target: "The opponent (player, life N)". Fire Searing
  Spear at the opponent on YOUR turn - it is damage on a clock; you have no
  reason to hold it (you have no counter-magic and nothing to wait for).
- EXCEPTION - kill these ON SIGHT with Pillar/Spear: a creature that MAKES TOKENS,
  triggers on every spell, or DRAINS/GAINS LIFE on a recurring trigger (Young
  Pyromancer, Guttersnipe, a Faerie that drains each time another Faerie enters,
  a lifegain creature). One such engine turns every opposing play into value or a
  blocker - 2-3 damage there is worth more than at the face. (Validated: Searing
  Spear correctly killed Obyra, Dreaming Duelist - a flier that drained you on
  every Faerie ETB - instead of hitting the face.)
- Also burn a blocker when killing it lets MORE total attack damage through this
  turn than the burn's face value. Otherwise: face. Pick "The opponent (player,
  life N)" or a card marked [opponent's battlefield] - never your own permanents.

BLOCKING - your creatures are ATTACKERS. When asked to declare blockers, the
prompt prints a line: "Unblocked, these attackers deal up to N - you would be at M
- NOT lethal" (or "- LETHAL"). OBEY THAT LINE - it is the real math:
- If it says NOT lethal: declare NO blockers. Take the hit and keep every creature
  untapped to swing next turn - a body kept home is a body that never attacks, and
  you win by racing. This holds even when M is low (e.g. "you would be at 3 - NOT
  lethal") - surviving at 3 and swinging beats trading away attackers.
- BLOCK only in these cases: (a) to KILL a token-maker or lifegain creature you
  could not burn this turn - gang-block Young Pyromancer or Guttersnipe with
  Goblins on sight; (b) ONLY when that line says LETHAL (M is 0 or less) - then
  chump with your least valuable creature to survive.
- NEVER block Phyrexian Obliterator, or ANY creature whose text punishes damage
  dealt to it - even to survive. Your blocker deals it combat damage and you pay
  the punisher (Obliterator: you SACRIFICE that many permanents). Take the hit and
  keep racing; blocking it costs you the game faster than the damage does.

PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR (opponent's 5/5 trample, black): any damage YOUR cards deal
to it forces YOU to sacrifice that many permanents. Your winning line: ALL burn
goes at "The opponent (player, life N)", attack only past it (not into it), NEVER
block it (your blockers deal it damage and you sacrifice - see BLOCKING above),
and when it attacks, take the hit and keep racing - you win the race from a healthy
life total; sacrificing your board loses it.

EVASIVE / UNTOUCHABLE BOARDS (faerie fliers, protection-from-red artifact creatures,
a green voltron creature that has grown out of burn range): Some opponents present a
board you CANNOT block (they fly over your ground creatures) and CANNOT kill (shroud,
protection from red, or simply more toughness than your burn deals - the engine won't
even offer a shroud/pro-red creature as a burn target). Do NOT wait to find an answer;
you have none, and you cannot out-grind them. Your ONLY route is to race their FACE
faster:
- Your ground creatures can never block their fliers, so holding a creature back
  gains you NOTHING. Swing with EVERY creature EVERY turn without exception - even
  when your attackers are shrunk (an opposing effect giving your attackers -1/-0,
  etc.) and each deals only 1: a chip of damage is strictly better than zero, and
  keeping bodies home only wastes them. Do not agonize over the math - attack.
- Play a land and deploy a body every turn, send EVERY burn spell at "The opponent
  (player, life N)", and keep swinging. If you fall behind on life, keep racing -
  trying to interact is a wasted turn you cannot afford.

KEY CARDS:
- Hellrider ({2}{R}{R}, 3/3 haste): each creature you control that ATTACKS pings
  the opponent for 1, even if blocked. Cast it the first turn you have 4 mana and
  attack with EVERYTHING after - wide attacks become lethal fast.
- Krenko's Command ({1}{R}): two 1/1 Goblins. Cast it the first turn you have 2
  spare mana - never bank it.
- Legion Loyalist ({R}, 1/1 haste): attacking with 2+ other creatures gives your
  team first strike + trample and can't-be-blocked-by-tokens. With 3+ attackers,
  always include it.
- Stromkirk Noble ({R}, 1/1): can't be blocked by Humans; grows +1/+1 each time
  it hits the opponent. Attack with it every turn - it snowballs.
- Rakdos Cackler / Gore-House Chainwalker: UNLEASH - ALWAYS take the "1/1 Counter"
  option (you weren't going to block anyway). Rakdos Cackler's {b/r} is a single
  hybrid mana; the option line spells out that a Mountain pays it - if "Cast Rakdos
  Cackler" is offered, you can afford it, so cast it.
- Boros Reckoner (3/3): any damage dealt to it bounces at any target - aim the
  bounce at the opponent or their best creature. Attack freely with it. Pay its
  "{RW}: first strike" ability ONLY when it is in combat against a creature with
  3+ power - otherwise pass (do not spend mana on it when nothing is attacking it).
- Ash Zealot ({R}{R}, 2/2 first strike haste): attack the turn it lands.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 lands and one or two 1-2 drops or burn.
Mulligan only 0-1 land or all-land hands.

SITUATIONS THAT DECIDE GAMES - recognize and act:
- Lethal on the table (attackers + Hellrider pings + burn >= their life): swing
  with everyone and fire all burn NOW.
- A creature in hand and open mana: cast it this turn so it attacks next turn -
  even if you cannot afford your bomb or assemble lethal, deploy the biggest body.
- Young Pyromancer / a token-maker / a life-drainer / a lifegain creature resolves:
  kill it with burn, or gang-block it with Goblins - do not let it live.
- Asked to block and the prompt says "NOT lethal": declare no blockers, keep
  attacking next turn. Only chump when it says LETHAL.
- Phyrexian Obliterator (or any damage-punisher) is attacking: take the hit, never
  block it - blocking it makes you sacrifice permanents.
- Opponent at 8 or less: every burn spell goes at their face.
