Deck identity: "Etched Affinity" is a fast artifact-aggro deck. Your cards cost
0-3 mana. Dump your whole hand onto the battlefield in the first few turns,
equip Cranial Plating, attack every turn, and finish with Galvanic Blast.
Kill by turn 5-6. A turn where you cast nothing is a lost turn. You are the
beatdown by default; against another fast attack deck, see THE RACE below.

#1 RULE - EQUIP EACH CRANIAL PLATING ONCE, THEN LEAVE THE EQUIP LINE ALONE.
The game re-offers "Equip with Cranial Plating" every turn even after it is
attached. An equip option tagged "(ALREADY attached to it - this would change
NOTHING)" is a wasted {1} - NEVER take it. Spend that mana on a creature or a
Blast, then attack. Equip each Cranial Plating you control ONCE (two equips in
one turn ONLY when you cast two Platings - attach each to your best carrier;
its power jumps twice). Equip again in a later turn only when your equipped
creature DIED or a strictly better carrier arrived. (Two power numbers - like
"Etched Champion (6/2) (printed 2/2)" - also mean a Plating is already on it.)

CASTING: every "Cast X" in your options is fully payable right now - the game
taps your lands for you, and "Mana in your pool: (none)" is normal. The thought
"I have no mana for this" is always false when the cast is listed. Memnite and
Ornithopter cost {0} - cast them the moment you see them, always. Cast a
creature every turn one is listed; it is fine to tap out.

THE ENGINE - CRANIAL PLATING gives its creature +1/+0 for EACH artifact you
control (your artifact count is shown on the board - usually +4 or more). It
adds POWER only, never toughness.
1. CAST Cranial Plating the first turn it appears - even if you cannot equip
   the same turn. One copy on the battlefield is enough; cast a creature
   before a 2nd Plating when you can only afford one of them.
2. EQUIP it with your first spare {1} - same turn if you can, otherwise the
   very next turn. An unattached Plating adds nothing but its artifact count.
3. Then run rule #1: equip each Plating once, done, attack.
Carrier order: 1) Etched Champion (with 3+ artifacts it has protection from
all colors - colored creatures cannot block or kill it), 2) Vault Skirge or
Ornithopter (fliers), 3) Signal Pest, 4) Memnite.

THE RACE - when the opponent is also fast aggro (red decks with Rakdos
Cackler, Ash Zealot, Legion Loyalist, Goblin tokens, Hellrider), win by going
WIDER, not taller: cast EVERY creature in your hand before spending mana on
Equip lines - two bodies beat one +4 bonus; they add damage AND can block.
Keep attacking, but when their attack on you would drop you to 8 life or
less, block with every creature you do not need for your own lethal next
turn - chump-blocking to stay alive wins the race. AT 8 LIFE OR LESS you must
KEEP a blocker back: a creature can only block if it is UNTAPPED, and attacking
taps it - so do NOT attack with a creature you are counting on to block the
swing back. If this turn's attack is not lethal, leave your best blocker home
untapped; send only the creatures you do not need on defense.

ATTACK EVERY TURN with every creature - including the turn after your best
attacker died: send the rest and rebuild. Signal Pest always attacks even at
0 power (battle cry gives every OTHER attacker +1/+0, and only fliers/reach
can block it). Etched Champion with 3+ artifacts walks past colored blockers
- always send it. One exception - LIGHTMINE FIELD: it deals damage to EACH
attacker equal to the number of attackers (doubled with two copies), and your
creatures have only 1-2 toughness while the Plating adds power, not toughness
- so an attacker DIES to Lightmine and deals nothing, however big its power.
When the opponent has Lightmine Field, do NOT attack; win by firing Galvanic
Blast at their face (4 damage with metalcraft). Attack into Lightmine only
with a creature whose toughness is greater than the Lightmine damage.

LETHAL CHECK - run it every turn:
damage = total power of your attackers (+1 each if Signal Pest attacks too)
       + 4 per Galvanic Blast in hand (metalcraft: 3+ artifacts = 4 damage).
If damage >= opponent's life: attack with everyone and fire every Blast at
the opponent's face. When the opponent is at 8 or less, Blasts always go to
the face. Earlier, a Blast may kill a blocker stopping your equipped attacker.

PLAYING AROUND COUNTERS - when the opponent's deck has blue (counterspells,
Arcane Denial, Faerie Miscreant fliers), do NOT lead with your one big threat
into their open mana. Deploy your CHEAP bodies first and go WIDE: Memnite,
Ornithopter, Signal Pest, Vault Skirge, Cranial Plating - a 1-for-1 counter
that eats a {0} Memnite is a bad trade for them, and a wide board beats one
countered bomb. Save Master of Etherium and a naked Etched Champion for a turn
you can follow another threat behind them, so a single counter never stops
your clock. Keep casting every turn - going wide IS how this deck beats
counters; never hold your hand back waiting for a "safe" window.

OTHER KEY CARDS:
- Steel Overseer: take "Counter with Steel Overseer [cost: Tap]" in Main
  Phase 1 EVERY turn it is untapped - permanent +1/+1 counters on ALL your
  artifact creatures beat one 1/1 attack, and the toughness they add helps
  survive pingers. Skip the tap only when Overseer itself must attack for
  lethal THIS turn.
- Master of Etherium: power = your artifact count, and it pumps your other
  artifact creatures +1/+1 (toughness too). Cast it on sight.
- Thoughtcast: affinity - it usually costs just {U}. Cast it whenever you
  have 2 or fewer other cards worth casting - refuel, do not hoard it.
- Mox Opal / Darksteel Citadel / Glimmervoid: free mana and lands; Mox Opal
  and Citadel also count as artifacts - you hit metalcraft (3+ artifacts)
  almost immediately, assume it is ON. (Glimmervoid is a LAND, not an
  artifact - it does not count.) A "Cast Card Normally with Mox Opal" or
  "Play Land with Mox Opal" line just makes mana with Mox Opal - your
  creatures and spells come first.

MULLIGAN: keep 2+ lands and 2+ creatures (Mox Opal counts as a land here).
Ship hands with 0-1 land or no creatures.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> play):
- An Equip line is tagged "(ALREADY attached... change NOTHING)" or the
  carrier shows two power numbers like "(6/2) (printed 2/2)": the Plating is
  attached - spend the mana on another creature or a Blast and attack.
- You hold castable creatures and an Equip line: cast the creatures first - two
  bodies beat one +4 bonus; the games this deck loses, it rode one creature
  while the opponent built five.
- The opponent's deck has blue and you hold one big threat plus cheap bodies:
  cast the cheap bodies first and go wide; do not walk Master of Etherium or a
  lone Etched Champion into open counter mana.
- You are at 8 life or less against a fast attacker: keep your best blocker
  home untapped this combat unless your attack is lethal right now.
- Your equipped creature died: keep declaring attackers with the rest and
  rebuild - the game this deck timed out, it stopped attacking with creatures
  still in play.
- The opponent has Lightmine Field: hold your creatures home and send Galvanic
  Blast at their face - your creatures die to it for nothing.
