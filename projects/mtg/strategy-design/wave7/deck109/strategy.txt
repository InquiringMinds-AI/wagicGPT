Deck identity: MONO-RED AGGRO (RDW), "Hellrider." Cheap fast creatures plus burn.
You win by dealing 20 fast - kill by turn 6-8. You are the BEATDOWN in every
matchup: attack every turn, cast something every turn, spend burn at the face.
You have no defensive plan - your only route to winning is a faster clock, so a
long game is a lost game. Your creatures are ATTACKERS, not blockers.

DEPLOY AND ATTACK - THE #1 RULE, run it every turn:
- Every main phase, if any "Cast" option shows a creature or Krenko's Command,
  take it - put a body on the board even if it cannot attack yet (it attacks
  NEXT turn). "Cast nothing" is for the rare turn when your ONLY castable option
  is an unaffordable card - NEVER pick it while a creature is in your Cast list.
  Even when you cannot assemble lethal or afford your bomb (a second Hellrider,
  Boros Reckoner) this turn, cast the biggest listed body instead: a 2/2 or 3/3
  now is damage next turn. Do not decline a listed creature because you are
  fixated on a card you cannot pay for - a stalled hand loses the race.
- Every combat, declare EVERY creature that can attack. Ash Zealot, Legion
  Loyalist, and Hellrider have haste - swing the turn they land. Trading a cheap
  attacker into a blocker is fine; you are racing, not preserving.

MAIN PHASE 2 CHECKLIST - run it every turn after combat, in order:
1. LETHAL: (2 x each Pillar in hand) + (3 x each Spear in hand) >= opponent's
   life, with enough on the "Mana available:" line? Fire them ALL at "The
   opponent (player, life N)" NOW. The game ends this turn.
2. Otherwise, with mana left: cast Krenko's Command, then any creature in hand.
   Creatures cannot attack the turn they arrive (except haste) - cast them ANYWAY
   so they attack next turn.
3. End your turn with your mana SPENT; the top of your deck refills your hand.

BURN - FACE BY DEFAULT, WITH ONE EXCEPTION.
- Pillar of Flame ({R}, 2 dmg, sorcery, exiles what it kills) and Searing Spear
  ({1}{R}, 3 dmg, instant) are your reach. DEFAULT target: "The opponent (player,
  life N)". Fire Searing Spear at the opponent on YOUR turn - it is damage on a
  clock; the hold-instants prior does not apply to it.
- EXCEPTION - kill these ON SIGHT with Pillar/Spear: a creature that MAKES TOKENS
  or triggers on every spell (Young Pyromancer, Guttersnipe and the like), or a
  lifegain creature. One 2/1 Young Pyromancer turns every opposing spell into a
  blocker - 2 damage there is worth more than 2 at the face.
- Also burn a blocker when killing it lets MORE total attack damage through this
  turn than the burn's face value. Otherwise: face. Pick "The opponent (player,
  life N)" or a card marked [opponent's battlefield] - never your own permanents.

BLOCKING - your creatures are ATTACKERS. When asked to declare blockers:
- If your life is ABOVE 8: declare NO blockers. Take the hit and keep every
  creature untapped to swing next turn - a body kept home is a body that never
  attacks, and you win by racing. A mana dork or 0-1 power attacker deals nothing
  worth blocking a real creature for.
- BLOCK only in these two cases: (a) to KILL a token-maker or lifegain creature
  you could not burn this turn - gang-block Young Pyromancer or Guttersnipe with
  Goblins on sight; (b) when the attackers you leave unblocked would drop you to 8
  life or less - then chump with your least valuable creature to survive.

PHYREXIAN OBLITERATOR (opponent's 5/5 trample, black): any damage YOUR cards deal
to it forces YOU to sacrifice that many permanents. Your winning line: ALL burn
goes at "The opponent (player, life N)", attack only past it (not into it), and
when it attacks, take the 5 and keep racing - you win the race from 20 life;
sacrificing your board loses it.

EVASIVE / UNTOUCHABLE BOARDS (faerie flyers, protection-from-red artifact creatures):
Some opponents (a UB faeries deck of flyers with SHROUD; an affinity deck with an
Etched Champion that has PROTECTION FROM RED) present a board you CANNOT block
(they fly over your ground creatures) and CANNOT burn (shroud/pro-red rejects your
targeting - the engine won't even offer them as burn targets). Do NOT wait to find
an answer; you have none. Your ONLY route is to race their FACE faster: play a land
and deploy a body EVERY turn without exception, send EVERY burn spell at "The
opponent (player, life N)", and swing with everything. If you fall behind on life
against these boards, keep racing - trying to interact is a wasted turn you cannot
afford.

KEY CARDS:
- Hellrider ({2}{R}{R}, 3/3 haste): each creature you control that ATTACKS pings
  the opponent for 1, even if blocked. Cast it the first turn you have 4 mana and
  attack with EVERYTHING after - wide attacks become lethal fast.
- Krenko's Command ({1}{R}): two 1/1 Goblins. Cast it the first turn you have 2
  spare mana - never bank it.
- Legion Loyalist ({R}, 1/1 haste): attacking with 2+ other creatures gives your
  team first strike + trample and can't-be-blocked-by-tokens. With 3+ attackers,
  always include it.
- Stromkirk Noble ({R}, 1/1): can't be blocked by Humans; grows +1/+1 each time
  it hits the opponent. Attack with it every turn - it snowballs.
- Rakdos Cackler / Gore-House Chainwalker: UNLEASH - ALWAYS take the "1/1 Counter"
  option (you weren't going to block anyway).
- Boros Reckoner ({R/W}{R/W}{R/W} - three Mountains cast it, 3/3): any damage
  dealt to it bounces at any target - aim the bounce at the opponent or their best
  creature. Attack freely with it. Pay its "{RW}: first strike" only when it is in
  combat against a 3-power creature.
- Ash Zealot ({R}{R}, 2/2 first strike haste): attack the turn it lands.

MULLIGAN: keep any hand with 2-4 lands and one or two 1-2 drops or burn.
Mulligan only 0-1 land or all-land hands.

SITUATIONS THAT DECIDE GAMES - recognize and act:
- Lethal on the table (attackers + Hellrider pings + burn >= their life): swing
  with everyone and fire all burn NOW.
- A creature in hand and open mana: cast it this turn so it attacks next turn -
  even if you cannot afford your bomb or assemble lethal, deploy the biggest body.
- Young Pyromancer / a token-maker / a lifegain creature resolves: kill it with
  burn, or gang-block it with Goblins - do not let it live.
- You are asked to block and you are above 8 life: declare no blockers, keep
  attacking next turn.
- Opponent at 8 or less: every burn spell goes at their face.
