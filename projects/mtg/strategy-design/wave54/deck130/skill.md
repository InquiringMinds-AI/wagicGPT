# deck130 — proposals for the strategy-writing skill (wave 54)

## S5 — A RULE KEYED TO A MARKER MUST CARRY THE BRANCH FOR THE MARKER'S ABSENCE, AND THE AUTHOR MUST MEASURE HOW OFTEN IT IS ABSENT
**Evidence.** deck130's X rung is built on `[<- most kills at any affordable X that costs you
nothing]`. That string rendered **0 times in the entire 42-log corpus**. The guide's fallback
("find the row whose YOURS reads none") assumes such a row exists; on `130v152` s17 none did, the
rule had no branch, and the pilot took `X = 1 {kills THEIRS: none; YOURS: Goblin x3}` at 9 life
against 25 and lost the game. Wave 53 recorded the marker as "taken 2/2" and predicted N/N again —
a rung scored PASS twice on a surface that has since stopped printing.
**Proposal.** When a rule's primary branch is keyed to an OPTIONAL annotation, the guide must (a)
state the condition under which it prints, and (b) carry a complete branch for its absence. And
the reviewer's denominator obligation grows a clause: report the marker's RENDER count, not only
its take rate. "2 of 2 taken" and "0 of 0 rendered" are the same number on paper and opposite
findings.

## S6 — DENOMINATOR HYGIENE: A SEAT'S RUNG COUNT MUST BE TAKEN AGAINST WHAT THE HAND ALLOWED
**Evidence.** deck130 cast land destruction at a two-land opponent three times in `130v146`
(s44 t20, s47 t22, s60 t32) — on the face of it three breaks of the land-plan gate, one of them
below the 8-life floor. Every one of those prompts reads `Your hand (1 card)` and the card was the
only legal cast. Scored as breaks they would have bought a guide edit that could not change a
single decision.
**Proposal.** Add to the per-rung method: before recording a break, read the hand line and the
menu. A rung break requires that a BETTER row existed on the same screen. Report "no alternative
existed" as its own verdict — it is not a PASS and it is not a FAIL, and folding it into either
one corrupts the trend the next wave reads.

## S7 — THE FIRST BOTTOMING CORPUS: RECORD THE ASK, NOT THE OUTCOME
**Evidence.** This is the first corpus at my seats with real `bottom` records (2, both deck123:
`123v126` s3 and `123v146` s3). Both were correct and one won its game — which tells us nothing
yet, because the guides' bottoming sections are PREFERENCE ORDERS and two samples cannot
discriminate an order. deck130 took 0 mulligans and 0 bottoms, fourth corpus.
**Proposal.** Until the sample supports it, the bottoming rung is reported as offers/ships/bottoms
with the ORDER the reply implies, and no verdict. The skill's #200 UNTESTABLE-AT-THIS-SEAT
machinery already exists for the mulligan half; extend it explicitly to bottoming, so a lucky
two-for-two does not read as a validated order in wave 55.
