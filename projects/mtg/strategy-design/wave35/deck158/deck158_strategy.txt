ORCS OF MORDOR - Rakdos (black-red) Orc aggro. You kill with ONE creature: the Orc Army token.

IDENTITY AND ROLE
You are the beatdown in every game and your kills land around turns 8-11. Default: cast your
amass spells in main phase 1, then attack with everything the list offers you. You do not switch
to defence; you keep attacking and you use the BLOCKING rules below when their attackers add up
to your life total.

THE ENGINE - THE ORC ARMY IS THE WIN CONDITION
Every amass card feeds the SAME token. You never choose which Army; the counters go on
automatically, and with no Army out the game creates a 0/0 Orc Army first. Its size is printed on
the battlefield line: "Orc army (9/9) (printed 0/0) [counters: 9x +1/+1]". Read that number and
swing with it. Each counter also narrates as "Counter added to Orc army: +1/+1 (now 9/9)" - the
number in the parentheses is the Army's new size, already correct.
Every amass card's cast line prints the exact result before you commit -
"Cast Mordor Muster {1}{b} {right now: Army 4/4 -> 5/5}" - and that number is CORRECT, Mauhur's
doubling and the new-token case included. Compare cards by that number, not by their card text.
- MARCH FROM THE BLACK GATE {1}{B} enchantment is the engine: amass 1 when it lands AND amass 1
  EVERY TIME your Army attacks. Best turn-2 play in the deck, and cast BOTH copies - a second
  March is a second counter on every attack. With a March out, attacking feeds the Army.
- MAUHUR, URUK-HAI CAPTAIN {B}{R} 2/2 menace: every +1/+1 counter event on your Army or Orcs adds
  one EXTRA counter. Cast him before your amass spells when both fit in one turn.
- GOTHMOG, MORGUL LIEUTENANT {3}{B} 3/3: amass 1, and your TOKENS gain deathtouch - so the Orc
  Army gains deathtouch. A deathtouch Army kills any blocker: attack into anything.

READING A CREATURE'S NUMBERS - USE THIS EVERYWHERE
A creature line prints (power/toughness). POWER is the FIRST number - the damage it deals.
TOUGHNESS is the SECOND number - the damage it takes to die. A (3/1) is a toughness-1 creature:
one damage kills it. A (1/3) is a toughness-3 creature: one damage does nothing to it.
Damage kills a creature only when the damage number is AT LEAST the SECOND number: 6 damage
does not kill an 8/8. Re-read the pair before every shot, every Foray, and every block.

SEQUENCING - AMASS IN MAIN PHASE 1, THEN ATTACK. THIS OVERRIDES THE GENERAL ADVICE.
The general prompt says cast sorceries and creatures in your SECOND main phase by default.
IGNORE THAT FOR AMASS CARDS: every counter added before combat is damage this turn. In MAIN
PHASE 1, in order: (1) play your land, (2) cast every amass card you can pay for - Foray of
Orcs, Swarming of Moria, Mordor Muster, March from the Black Gate, Dunland Crebain, Orcish
Bowmasters, Gothmog - (3) THEN declare attackers.

CASTING - TAKE THE CAST
When a "Cast <card>" line appears in your numbered list, cast it. The game has already checked
your lands and colours and it taps them for you; the "Mana available: N total" line at the top
of the board tells you how much you have. Read the list and pick.
Never re-count your mana against a listed cast: the thought "I cannot actually pay this" is
ALWAYS FALSE when the line is in your list - your Treasures and every source are already counted.
A cost's leading number is GENERIC mana ON TOP of the coloured pips: {2}{R} is THREE mana,
{3}{B} is FOUR, {4}{B}{B} is SIX. The thought "{2}{R} is two mana" is ALWAYS FALSE.

KEY CARDS
- FORAY OF ORCS {3}{R}: amass 2, then deal damage equal to the Army's power AFTER the amass.
  Its option line prints the exact result - "{right now: Army 7/7 -> 9/9, damage 9}" - and the
  Army numbers are CORRECT; use them as printed.
  That damage can only ever hit an opponent CREATURE. It never hits the opponent. When the
  opponent controls no creature, the printed "damage N" WILL NOT HAPPEN - cast Foray anyway for
  the amass alone, and count only the Army's new power toward lethal. Never add Foray's damage
  number to a face-damage total.
  Choosing the target: the damage kills a creature only when the printed "damage N" is AT LEAST
  that creature's TOUGHNESS - the second number of its (P/T). Kill the biggest creature the
  number actually kills. When N kills nothing on the list, put it on their biggest blocker
  anyway - the damage stays marked until end of turn and your Army's combat damage adds to it.
- SWARMING OF MORIA {2}{R}: a Treasure plus amass 2 - your cheapest big amass.
- MORDOR MUSTER {1}{B}: draw a card, lose 1 life, amass 1. Cast on sight - the life is the card
  doing its job.
- ORCISH BOWMASTERS {1}{B} 1/1: cast it in your MAIN PHASE like any other creature - you get no
  window on the opponent's turn and a Bowmasters in hand does nothing.
  ITS 1 DAMAGE. Bowmasters deals exactly 1. One damage kills a creature whose printed toughness
  is 1 and NOTHING ELSE - against a toughness of 2 or more it does nothing at all, however big a
  threat that creature is. So read the (P/T) of every opponent creature in the target list
  FIRST, checking the SECOND number of every pair: a (3/1) IS a toughness-1 creature and the
  shot kills it; a (1/3) is not.
  - Any opponent creature with a toughness of 1 in the list: SHOOT IT. Take the first one
    printed. A dead 1-toughness creature beats 1 point of face damage in every game this deck
    plays, and it is worth most against creatures marked [infect].
  - Only when NO opponent creature in the list shows a toughness of 1: shoot the opponent's face.
  "It is the bigger threat" is not a reason to shoot a creature with toughness 2 or more, and
  neither is [infect] - the damage bounces off it and the trigger is wasted. An infect creature
  with toughness 2 or more is a job for Feed the Swarm, never for the shot.
- EASTERLING VANGUARD {1}{B} 2/1: it amasses when it DIES, not when it enters - which is why its
  cast line prints no Army preview. That makes it your best blocker and best trade - putting it
  in front of anything turns it into a counter on the Army. Attack and block with it freely.
  It needs no setup: dying in combat IS the payoff. The thought "Vanguard is useless without a
  sac outlet" is FALSE - combat is the outlet. Cast it on sight and trade it.
- DUNLAND CREBAIN {2}{B} 1/1 flying: amass 2 on entry, and it chips in the air.
- SNARLING WARG {3}{B} 3/4 menace: a 4/4 while you control an Orc (your Army is an Orc) - the
  blocker that saves close races. OLOG-HAI CRUSHER {3}{R} 4/4 trample can block only while you
  control a Goblin or Orc; attacking with it is always fine.
- SAURON, THE LIDLESS EYE {3}{B}{R} 4/4: on entry steal an opponent creature until end of turn.
  It arrives UNTAPPED AND HASTY, so it attacks THIS TURN even if it was tapped when you took it -
  take their BIGGEST body and swing with it, leaving their board a blocker short the same turn.
  Its "{1}{B}{R}: creatures gain +2/+0" also drains 2; spend spare mana on it when the opponent
  is nearly dead.
- WITCH-KING, BRINGER OF RUIN {4}{B}{B} 5/3 flying: whenever it attacks the defender sacrifices
  their lowest-power creature - it clears chumps out of the Army's way. Attack with it always.
- UGLUK OF THE WHITE HAND {2}{B}{R} 3/3: whenever another of your creatures dies he gets one
  +1/+1 counter, or TWO counters if that creature was an Orc or a Goblin.
- GRISHNAKH, BRASH INSTIGATOR {2}{R} 1/1: amass 2, then steal a nonlegendary opponent creature
  whose power is at or below the Army's new power, untapped and hasty until end of turn. Cast
  him with an Army already out so the steal reaches a real body, and attack with what you take.
- ASSAULT ON OSGILIATH {X}{R}{R}{R}: the game offers whichever of its two faces is legal - the
  plain face when you control NO Army (it creates one with X counters), the "with its
  alternative cost" face when you DO (it adds X counters). Take the LARGEST X the menu offers
  you; option 1 is always the largest. It also gives your Orcs double strike and haste for the
  turn, so cast it in MAIN PHASE 1, before combat. Cast it only when the menu offers X of 2 or
  more.
- FEED THE SWARM {1}{B}: destroys a creature OR an enchantment, and you lose life equal to the
  destroyed permanent's mana value. Both surfaces print the price on every target - the cast line
  ("Ornithopter (costs you 0 life), Cathodion (costs you 3 life)") and the target menu that comes
  next. Read the price and pay it deliberately.
  Spend the card on what is actually killing you - a deathtouch or first-strike blocker that eats
  your Army, a lifelinker, a flier, an [infect] creature, the biggest blocker - and pay more life
  for that than you would for a cheaper target that is not in your way. When two targets are
  equally in your way, take the cheaper one.

COMBAT - ATTACKING
Keep declaring EVERY creature the attackers list offers you; the list already excludes creatures
that cannot attack, so do not re-derive eligibility. Attack with the Army whenever its blocker
line says "you kill it, your blocker lives", and once it has deathtouch attack into anything.
With March from the Black Gate out, attack with the Army every turn - each attack adds a counter
whether or not the damage gets through.

COMBAT - BLOCKING: ANSWER FROM THE B-LINES AND NOTHING ELSE
Your blockers are the numbered B-lines. A creature on your battlefield that has no B-line cannot
block this turn and the board line already says why ("[tapped - cannot attack or block this
turn]"); count only the B-lines you were given.
Each B-line ends with the attackers it may be assigned to - "B1. Orc army (1/1) - may block A1
(your blocker dies, attacker lives)". That list is complete: an attacker missing from it cannot
be blocked by that creature, usually because it flies. Assign only pairs the line permits.
Write your answer with the printed LABELS: "BLOCKS: B1:A2, B3:A1". Labels, never card names.
Then run this test, once, down the A-list:
1. Add up the "deals N" numbers of every attacker you are leaving unblocked.
2. If that total is your life total OR MORE - EQUAL counts: at exactly 0 life you LOSE - assign
   a blocker to the biggest attacker you legally can, and keep assigning until the unblocked
   total is below your life. Do this EVEN WHEN the annotation says "your blocker dies, attacker
   lives", and even when the blocker is the Orc Army: a body that keeps you alive is worth more
   than any creature you own.
3. If the unblocked total is already below your life total, block only where the annotation says
   "you kill it, your blocker lives" or "both die", and take the rest of the damage.
For step 2, Easterling Vanguard is your first chump: it is the ONLY creature in this deck that
amasses when it DIES, so its death pays you a counter. Every other creature already delivered
its amass when it ENTERED - Crebain and Bowmasters get you nothing back by dying. When you must
keep chumping past Vanguard, spend Dunland Crebain before Orcish Bowmasters: a live Bowmasters
keeps shooting every time the opponent draws.
When an attacker is marked [infect] the header replaces your life total with your POISON count
and tells you where 10 is. Run the same test against the poison numbers instead: infect damage
never moves your life total, poison never resets, and blocking is the only thing that stops it.

MULLIGAN - THE ENGINE COUNTS THE HAND FOR YOU. AT MOST TWO MULLIGANS, THEN KEEP.
Swamp is your only black source; this deck has no dual lands, and 22 of its 34 spells need {B}.
The opening-hand prompt does the counting: it prints "Mana sources among those lands, counted by
the engine: {B} n, {R} n" and "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of: ...".
Read those two lines and decide from them - do not recount the hand yourself.
- {B} of 1 or more, and two to five lands: KEEP.
- {B} 0: MULLIGAN - Mountains cannot cast the engine. The one exception: keep a Swamp-less hand
  of three or more lands whose covered-cost line already names Swarming of Moria or Foray of
  Orcs, because those are red and they still build the Army.
- After your SECOND mulligan, KEEP the hand you drew and play it out.
The covered-cost line names what your lands will pay for across the whole game, not this turn -
on turn one you still have exactly one land in play and one land drop.
When you bottom cards, you put back exactly one per mulligan you took: bottom your extra
Mountains first, then your most expensive spell.

DECIDING SITUATIONS
- A card is in your cast list: cast it. The mana is already checked.
- An amass card and an attack both available: amass FIRST in main phase 1, then attack bigger.
- March from the Black Gate out and your Army can attack: attack.
- Foray of Orcs with an opponent creature on board: cast it, read its printed damage number, and
  kill the biggest creature whose toughness that number covers.
- Foray with the opponent's board empty: cast it for the amass, and count no damage.
- Bowmasters entering or triggering: shoot the first opponent creature in the list whose SECOND
  (P/T) number is 1. Only if there is none, shoot the face.
- Sauron or Grishnakh entering with a big opponent creature: steal the biggest one their line
  allows and attack with it now.
- Their attackers' "deals N" numbers add up to your life or more: assign blockers by the B-lines
  until they do not - Vanguard first (its death feeds the Army), then Crebain, then Bowmasters.
- Their attackers are [infect]: run the same test against the poison count and the 10 threshold.
- Opponent's board empty or all tapped: run the lethal count, then attack with everything in the
  list. Your Army alone is often lethal.
