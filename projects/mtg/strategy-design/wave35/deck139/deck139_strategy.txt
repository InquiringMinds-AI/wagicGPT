YOUR DECK: Simic-green RAMP + MUTATE midrange. You ramp with cheap creatures and land-fetchers,
then MUTATE a big "Apex" onto a creature you already control to fire a game-swinging trigger and
build one huge threat. You are the CONTROL against aggro (put bodies down, survive, then take over
with a mutated bomb) and the BEATDOWN against slow decks. Re-check every turn off the board: if
your life is falling while theirs is not, your first job is to STOP DYING - deploy bodies. In BOTH
roles you cast a creature every turn.

EVERY CREATURE IN THIS DECK IS NON-HUMAN. There is no Human anywhere in your 60 cards. Pollywog
Symbiote is a Frog. Arboreal Grazer, Migratory Greathorn and Gemrazer are Beasts. Dryad of the
Ilysian Grove is a Nymph. Everquill Phoenix is a Phoenix. Snapdax, Illuna, Nethroi, Vadrok and
Brokkos are Dinosaurs / Cats / Nightmares / Beasts / Elementals. Beanstalk Giant is a Giant.
The board line does not print creature types, so you cannot check this yourself - it is written
here because it is a fact about your deck, not something to work out.
  The thought "my creature is a Human, so I cannot mutate" is ALWAYS FALSE.
  The thought "I have no non-Human creature on the battlefield" is ALWAYS FALSE whenever you
  control any creature at all.
AND YOU NEVER HAVE TO DECIDE IT: the game only prints a line saying "with its mutate cost" when a
legal non-Human host is ALREADY on your battlefield. If that line is in the list, the host exists
- take it. If that line is not in the list, the mutate is off this turn for some other reason.
Never write "the mutate option is illegal/unavailable" about a line the game just offered you.

MULLIGAN - READ THE ENGINE'S TWO COUNTS, THEN ANSWER.
The prompt prints, above your hand:
  "Your hand (7 cards), counted by the engine: 3 lands, 4 spells."
  "Mana sources among those lands, counted by the engine: {W} 1, {B} 1, {R} 1, {G} 3."
  "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of: ..."
Those numbers are computed from the cards you are holding. Read them; do not recount the hand and
do not argue with them.
  ANSWER "Keep this hand" when BOTH are true: (1) the land count is 2 or more AND the {G} number
  is 1 or more; AND (2) at least one of Arboreal Grazer, Pollywog Symbiote, Dryad of the Ilysian
  Grove, Migratory Greathorn, Gemrazer, Beanstalk Giant // Fertile Footsteps is in the hand.
  ANSWER "Mulligan" only when the land count is 0 or 1, or 6 or 7, or the {G} number is 0, or
  nothing from (2) is there.
ONE green source is enough. A {G} count of 1 is a KEEP, not a shortage.
The thought "this hand has zero lands" is ALWAYS FALSE when the printed land count is 1 or more.
The thought "this hand has zero green sources" is ALWAYS FALSE when the printed {G} number is 1
or more.
THE FLOOR IS THE NUMBER PRINTED IN FRONT OF YOU. The prompt says "having already taken N
mulligans". If that sentence appears at all, this is your LAST look: answer "Keep this hand".
There is no second mulligan. Writing "I will keep whatever I draw next" is how you end up with a
2-card hand - keep THIS one instead.
Lands in your HAND are mana - you play one every turn.
When asked to put cards on the BOTTOM, name exactly as many as the line asks for, worst first
(spare duplicate lands, then the most expensive Apex you cannot cast). Write the PUT: line and
STOP - the PUT: line is what happens, so do not re-argue it below it.

SHOCKLANDS - "pay 2 life" OR "tap". YOUR LIFE IS A RESOURCE AND THIS IS WHERE YOU SPEND IT.
Stomping Ground, Temple Garden, Overgrown Tomb, Breeding Pool, Sacred Foundry and Steam Vents ask
"1. pay 2 life [enters UNTAPPED] / 2. tap [enters TAPPED]". The 2 life is a choice about
tapped-or-untapped only: the land taps for both its colours either way, on this turn and every
later turn.
  ANSWER "tap" unless one of the next two lines applies.
  ANSWER "pay 2 life" ONLY when you will use that mana THIS TURN - that is, you are holding a
  card you intend to cast this turn and this land is the source that makes it payable.
  ANSWER "tap" always when your life is 12 or less, unless paying deploys a blocker THIS TURN
  against creatures already on their battlefield.
Six of these payments is twelve life - more than half your starting total, taken by your own
lands. Untapped mana you do not spend this turn is worth nothing; the 2 life is gone forever.

DEPLOY CHOICE WHEN BEHIND - THE BODY, NOT THE ENGINE.
When two or more "Cast" lines are offered AND your life is 10 or less AND the opponent shows more
creatures than you: cast the offered creature with the HIGHEST TOUGHNESS, preferring one whose
line shows [reach] or [flying] if any opposing attacker shows [flying]. Pollywog Symbiote (1/3) is
your discount ENGINE, not your stabilizer - at 10 or less life and behind on board, "Cast Gemrazer
(4/4)" beats "Cast Pollywog Symbiote (1/3)" every time, even though Pollywog makes your next
mutate cheaper. A discount you do not live to use is worth nothing. Above 10 life, or when you are
even or ahead on creatures, cast Pollywog first - that is when the engine is right.

#1 RULE - THE APEX BOMBS ARE PAYOFFS ONLY WHEN YOU MUTATE THEM. Snapdax, Illuna, Nethroi, Vadrok,
Brokkos, Everquill Phoenix, Gemrazer and Migratory Greathorn all say "Whenever this creature
MUTATES, <do something>." A HARDCAST gives you ONLY a body and its keywords - the trigger does
NOTHING. So when a line reading "with its mutate cost" is offered, TAKE IT.
- CHECK THE LINE YOU ARE ABOUT TO TAKE. A mutate line always contains the words "with its mutate
  cost". "Cast Illuna, Apex of Wishes {2}{g}{u}{r}" is a HARDCAST; "Cast Everquill Phoenix with
  its mutate cost {3}{r}" is the mutate. Before you answer, re-read your chosen option and confirm
  the words "mutate cost" are in it if you meant to mutate.
- IF NO LINE FOR THAT CARD SAYS "with its mutate cost", THE MUTATE IS NOT AVAILABLE THIS TURN
  (a missing coloured pip does this - Snapdax needs {W}{W}, Illuna needs {U}{U}). Then either
  hardcast it because you need the body, or cast something else and mutate later. Say which one
  you are doing; do not write "I will mutate it" over a hardcast line.
- OVER OR UNDER: THE BIGGEST PRINTED P/T GOES ON TOP. The merged pile has the printed power and
  toughness of its TOP card only; every card's abilities AND the mutate trigger work the same
  whether you answer "over" or "under". Decide which host you will pick (that question comes
  next), then compare printed numbers:
    the card you are CASTING is the bigger half -> answer "mutate over" (an Apex onto a
    Pollywog: over).
    your battlefield creature is the bigger half -> answer "mutate under" (Migratory Greathorn
    3/4 onto your 6/6 Brokkos pile: UNDER - "over" would shrink the 6/6 into a 3/4, and the
    land-fetch triggers either way).
  The thought "'mutate over' is always right" is ALWAYS FALSE when the host's printed P/T is
  bigger than the card you are casting.
- GEMRAZER (mutate {1}{G}{G}, 4/4 reach trample): destroys an opponent's artifact or enchantment.
  MIGRATORY GREATHORN (mutate {2}{G}, 3/4): fetches a basic land - on mutate ONLY, never on a
  normal cast. These two are your cheapest and most reliable mutates; green is your deepest
  colour, so take their mutate line every time it is offered - EXCEPT when "Cast Dryad of the
  Ilysian Grove" is in the same list and you cannot pay for both this turn: cast Dryad FIRST (she
  is the fixing that turns the {W}{W} and {U}{U} Apexes on), and take the mutate next turn - it
  keeps. Both are worth mutating even with no trigger target - the merged body is the point.
- SNAPDAX (mutate {2}{BR}{W}{W}, 3/5 double strike): 4 damage to an opponent's creature or
  planeswalker AND gain 4 life. Your best anti-aggro play; the 4 life happens even with nothing
  to shoot.
- ILLUNA (mutate {3}{RG}{U}{U}, 6/6 flying trample): digs until it hits a nonland permanent and
  puts it onto the battlefield or into hand.
- NETHROI (mutate {4}{GW}{B}{B}, 5/5 deathtouch lifelink): returns creatures from your graveyard
  to the battlefield - read the target list you are given and take everything it lets you take.
- BROKKOS (mutate {2}{UB}{G}{G}, 6/6 trample): may be cast FROM YOUR GRAVEYARD using its mutate -
  a discarded Brokkos is never lost, he comes back.
- VADROK (mutate {1}{WU}{R}{R}, 3/3 flying first strike) and EVERQUILL PHOENIX (mutate {3}{r}, 4/4
  flying): mutate these for the BODY. Vadrok's trigger wants a noncreature card of mana value 3 or
  less in your graveyard and this deck has none; Everquill's makes a small artifact, not a
  creature.

#2 RULE - UNDER AGGRO, A FRESH BODY BEATS A MUTATE. Mutating grows one creature but adds NO new
body; hardcasting adds a blocker. When you are being attacked and need more blockers, CAST A
CREATURE NORMALLY - and say so: "I am hardcasting for the extra blocker." That is the ONLY reason
to pass up an offered mutate line. "I cannot mutate" is never the reason.

CAST FLOOR: every one of your main phases, if a "Cast <something>" line is offered, TAKE ONE.
Every listed line is already payable - the thought "I have no untapped lands left to pay" is
ALWAYS FALSE when a Cast line is listed. "Cast nothing right now" is the answer ONLY when the sole
options are Apexes you cannot mutate and do not need as bodies.
- ARBOREAL GRAZER WITH NO LAND IN HAND IS STILL A CAST. The thought "Grazer does nothing without a
  land in hand, so it is not worth casting" is FALSE: a 0/3 reach wall is the body that keeps you
  alive against a wide board, and it is a mutate host. Cast it.

RAMP FIRST, THEN THE BOMBS:
- ARBOREAL GRAZER ({G}, 0/3 reach): on enter, put a LAND FROM YOUR HAND onto the battlefield
  tapped - ramp, a wall, and a fine mutate host. Play it turn 1 when you can.
- DRYAD OF THE ILYSIAN GROVE ({2}{G}, 2/4): extra land drop each turn, and all your lands tap for
  every colour. Dryad is what turns Snapdax's {W}{W} and Illuna's {U}{U} from impossible into
  castable - cast her on curve every time and keep her as a host.
- BEANSTALK GIANT // FERTILE FOOTSTEPS: cast Fertile Footsteps {2}{G} to fetch a basic; the Giant
  is */* = your land count. NISSA WHO SHAKES THE WORLD: your Forests make double green - and with
  Dryad out every land is a Forest - and +1 turns a land into a 3/3 haste-vigilance body. Cast
  Nissa when you are stable; at 10 or less life under attack a creature or a mutate comes first -
  Nissa adds no blocker the turn she lands.

READING THE MUTATE FLOW (follow the labels in order, one answer each):
1. Take "Cast <card> with its mutate cost {..}".
2. "1. Cast Card Normally / 2. mutate [cast for the MUTATE cost: merge onto one of your non-Human
   creatures ...] / 3. Decline" -> answer "mutate". "Cast Card Normally" here throws it away.
   Your creatures ARE the non-Human creatures this label means; see the top of this guide.
3. With POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE on your battlefield you are then asked "DISCARD ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS"
   listing your HAND -> pitch, in this order: a spare land / Brokkos (he mutates back out of your
   graveyard - discarding him costs nothing) / a duplicate of a card you still hold / the most
   expensive card you cannot cast soon. Two Pollywogs ask twice.
   (Pollywog makes every mutate spell cost {1} less, and the discount keeps working even while
   Pollywog is the UNDER card of a merged pile - a strong reason to mutate onto it once stable.
   Pollywog itself has no mutate and does not trigger off its own casting.)
4. "mutate over / mutate under" -> the biggest printed P/T goes ON TOP: casting the bigger card
   = "mutate over"; your battlefield creature is bigger = "mutate under" (see the OVER OR UNDER
   rule above).
5. "TARGET CHOICE for <card>" listing YOUR BATTLEFIELD -> pick the host whose abilities you want
   (Dryad's ramp, Pollywog's discount) over a vanilla 0/3. For Gemrazer and Greathorn, prefer a
   SMALL engine host (Pollywog, Dryad, Grazer) and go over; pick your big Brokkos/Apex pile as
   the host only when you answered "mutate under" at step 4 - never put a 3/4 on top of a 6/6.
Afterwards the pile renders as ONE line tagged "[mutated pile - ONE merged creature ...]" - it is
one creature with every card's abilities; count it as one.

ATTACK FLOOR - run this on each listed attacker, one line at a time: send it if its POWER is 1 or
more AND any of: the opponent has no untapped creature that can block it / it survives every block
shown / it trades up or kills a blocker. Then answer and stop.
- EVERY CREATURE THE GAME LISTS UNDER "Your creatures that can attack" CAN ATTACK. The thought
  "that one is summoning sick" is ALWAYS FALSE about a creature on that list - the game already
  removed the ones that cannot.
- Any attacker whose line reads "[deals 0 - this attack deals no damage to the opponent]" NEVER
  attacks. That tag is printed for you; read it and skip that line.
- "no attackers" is the answer ONLY when every listed attacker carries that tag or dies for zero
  gain. Otherwise declare EVERY attacker that passes the test, in one reply.

BLOCK FLOOR: block when the line says "you kill it, your blocker lives", when the block is free
("neither dies"), or when the damage would otherwise put you below 6 life. Read the parenthetical
the render gives you and believe it - including "[summoning sick ... but CAN block]", which means
that creature blocks normally.
- ONE CREATURE BLOCKS ONE ATTACKER. The prompt says it: "Assign each blocker to AT MOST ONE
  attacker (a creature cannot block two attackers)". You may put SEVERAL of your creatures on the
  SAME attacker; you may never put ONE of your creatures on several attackers. Never write the
  same creature's name twice in a BLOCKS line - only the first assignment happens and every other
  attacker you named hits you in full. If you have one blocker and four attackers, you are
  choosing WHICH ONE to stop; pick the one whose damage matters most and say so.
- POLLYWOG SYMBIOTE STAYS BACK OFF BLOCKS unless the block stops lethal - it dies to almost
  everything and it is your discount engine.
- A blocker facing deathtouch or first strike dies without killing anything - take that block only
  to stop lethal damage.
- Only a creature whose line shows [reach] or [flying] can block an attacker tagged [flying]. Your
  Arboreal Grazers and Gemrazer have reach; Pollywog and Dryad do not.

POISON - READ THE PRINTED NUMBER, NOT YOUR LIFE TOTAL.
Against an [infect] board the blocker prompt prints "Your life: 14. Your poison counters: 3 of 10."
and then, for that combat, "these attackers put up to N POISON COUNTERS on you - you would be at M
of 10 poison". Ten poison counters lose you the game at any life total, and they never go away.
  Read M. If M is 10 or more, this combat is LETHAL and your life total does not matter: put ONE
  blocker on EVERY attacker, biggest poison number first, until you run out of blockers. ANY
  block stops that attacker's ENTIRE poison - the blocker's power does not matter: a 0/3, a
  shrunken -2/1, any creature on your B-list stops its attacker completely. The thought "this
  blocker is too weak to block" is ALWAYS FALSE about a creature the B-list offers you. The only
  attacker that poisons you is one with NO blocker assigned.
  An attacker tagged [infect] does NOT reduce your life at all - blocking is the only thing that
  stops it. Do not price these blocks against your life total; price them against M.

DECIDE FAST - ON EVERY DECISION. Settle the decision, then answer in exactly the shape the HOW TO
REPLY block gives you, and STOP. Re-reading the board a second and third time inside one answer does
not change the answer and it burns the clock. Double strike (Snapdax) and trample
(Gemrazer/Illuna/Nethroi/Brokkos) mean a blocked trampler assigns lethal to the blocker and the
rest to the player; a blocked creature WITHOUT trample deals nothing to the player.

DECIDING SITUATIONS (recognize -> do):
- The printed land count is 2 or more and the printed {G} number is 1 or more, plus any of
  Grazer/Pollywog/Dryad/Greathorn/Gemrazer/Beanstalk -> KEEP THIS HAND.
- The mulligan prompt says "having already taken N mulligans" -> KEEP THIS HAND.
- "pay 2 life / tap" and you are not casting anything this turn off that land -> answer "tap".
- "pay 2 life / tap" at 12 life or less -> answer "tap".
- A line says "with its mutate cost" -> the host exists; take that line, then "mutate", then put
  the bigger printed P/T on top.
- "Cast Dryad of the Ilysian Grove" and a cheap mutate line both listed, mana for only one ->
  Dryad first; the mutate keeps for next turn.
- Casting Gemrazer or Greathorn and your only good host is a BIGGER pile (Brokkos, a merged
  Apex) -> answer "mutate under"; the trigger still fires and the big P/T stays on top.
- No line for that Apex says "with its mutate cost" -> the mutate is off this turn; hardcast only
  if you need the body, and call it a hardcast.
- At 10 or less life, behind on creatures, two Cast lines offered -> cast the highest toughness.
- Under attack and short on blockers -> cast a creature NORMALLY for the extra body, and say that
  is why.
- Only "Cast Arboreal Grazer" is offered and you hold no land -> cast it anyway.
- Snapdax castable FOR ITS MUTATE COST -> mutate it: 4 damage to their best creature AND 4 life.
- "DISCARD ONE OF YOUR OWN CARDS" -> a spare land; no land in the list -> Brokkos, then a
  duplicate.
- An attacker line reads "[deals 0 ...]" -> that creature stays home.
- A creature is on the "can attack" list -> it can attack; do not call it summoning sick.
- An opposing attacker shows [flying] -> block it only with [reach] or [flying].
- One blocker, several attackers -> assign it to ONE of them; never repeat its name.
- The poison line says you would be at 10 of 10 or more -> that combat is lethal; block EVERY
  attacker you have a blocker for, ignore your life total and your blockers' power.
- Dryad on your battlefield -> take the extra land drop every turn; she is your best host and your
  fixing for the {W}{W} and {U}{U} mutates.
- Flooding on lands -> cast Fertile Footsteps / Beanstalk; Nissa +1 turns a spare land into a 3/3.
- The game is drifting long and even -> that is a LOSS risk; mutate a bomb and PRESS to close.
