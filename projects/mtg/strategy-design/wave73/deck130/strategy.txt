YOUR DECK: mono-red LAND DESTRUCTION. Take their lands while they are still cheap to take, kill
every creature they deploy, then land one real body - Siege-Gang Commander or Rorix Bladewing - and
attack until they are dead. You are NOT a burn deck: every damage card is REMOVAL that can also hit
a face, and all of it totals 11 damage, which is not a clock. Every turn has a job - destroy a
land, kill a creature, or add a body. The kill is Rorix (6 a turn, flying) or Siege-Gang plus
Goblin sacrifices. Colour never justifies a mulligan or a declined spell: "Mana available:" is
correct.

=== ORDER OF OPERATIONS ===
Play a land every turn. At each "which card do you cast" ask take the FIRST entry below matching a
row on your menu and stop there, then take the ask again - more than one spell a turn is allowed.
 1. STARSTORM, when STARSTORM'S X below clears it. Sweep BEFORE you deploy: the affordable X
    shrinks with each spell cast first.
 2. A single-target damage spell (Pyrite Spellbomb, Spark Spray, Hammer of Bogardan) aimed by
    TARGETING below. A token-MAKER on their line (Thraben Doomsayer 2/2, Bloodline Keeper 3/3) is
    what this entry exists for and outranks entry 4.
 3. STONE RAIN, MOLTEN RAIN or LAY WASTE at one of their lands, under THE LAND PLAN's numbers.
 4. RORIX at six lands, or SIEGE-GANG at five - never into a board where 1 still applies.
 5. DWARVEN BLASTMINER or TALISMAN OF IMPULSE early, when nothing above matched.
 6. A cycling row, by CYCLING below, with mana you were not going to spend.
 7. "Cast nothing right now".
ONE OVERRIDE: before entry 3, subtract its cost from "Mana available:" - if what is left cannot
cast a creature your hand marks [castable now], cast THAT BODY FIRST and take the land later. Their
land keeps; a body in hand can be discarded, exiled or countered out of it.
By land count: 2 Blastminer / Talisman / Spellbomb; 3 Stone Rain or Molten Rain (never a Spellbomb
"first" with land destruction in hand); 4 a second land-kill plus a Blastminer activation; 5
Siege-Gang; 6+ Rorix. "Land drop: NOT yet used this turn" with a land in hand -> play it first,
ahead of any cycling row. Cycle in your SECOND MAIN PHASE only, never at your own upkeep.

=== YOUR OWN UPKEEP - SPEND NOTHING THAT YOUR MAIN PHASE NEEDS ===
Your own upkeep asks twice: a casting ask that CAN offer "Cast Starstorm", and a priority menu of
abilities and cycling rows. A card you cannot cast there says so on its own hand line; believe it.
Answer both windows CHOICE: 0 (pass) or the hold row, unless BOTH hold: the row is a Blastminer
land-kill, the Hammer
return under EMPTY-HAND, or a Starstorm that kills a creature THIS turn, AND after paying it your
remaining "Mana available:" still covers every ORDER OF OPERATIONS entry this board matches. A
CYCLING ROW IS NEVER ONE OF THEM. Do the subtraction before you answer: "Mana available:" minus
this row's cost, plus 1 when "Land drop:" reads NOT yet used - if that number cannot cast the
cheapest creature in your hand (with no creature there, the cheapest spell), pass. An upkeep row
that taps you out is the wrong answer.

=== DRAW PUNISHERS - READ BEFORE ANY OPTIONAL DRAW ===
The frame prints a summed line when they are out: "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield ... Every card
YOU draw costs you 1 life to theirs." That is N; no line, no punisher.
 - N is 1 and "Your life:" above 5 -> you may cycle.
 - N is 2 or more, OR N at or above your life minus 5 -> take NO optional draw: no cycling row, no
   Forgotten Cave cycle, no Spellbomb draw mode. Pass.
YOUR DRAW STEP IS MANDATORY AND NO ROW SKIPS IT: "DRAW FORECAST:" says what it costs, not that you
may skip it.
PUNISHERS PUNISH DRAWS AND NOTHING ELSE - casting, land drops, attacking, blocking and activating
cost you nothing. Killing a punisher outranks casting a creature; a 4-toughness one is Starstorm's
job, not Hammer's.

=== CYCLING - LAY WASTE AND STARSTORM ARE NOT CARD DRAW ===
A cycle is an option beginning "cycling with ", or a "cycling" row of a "Choose an option for"
menu. Costs: Spark Spray and Forgotten Cave {R}, Lay Waste {2}, Starstorm {3}.
 Forgotten Cave -> it enters tapped; while the land drop is unused, or a second land sits in hand,
   PLAY it; after that cycle it where a rule below sends you.
 Spark Spray -> cycle when its target list shows no opponent creature whose row reads "takes 1
   damage - DIES". A SURVIVES row is not one, nor is the face row.
 Lay Waste -> CAST it at a land; the gate is the MANA line, not a land count, so at "Mana
   available:" 4 or more it is castable. Cycle only when it is uncastable this turn AND you hold
   another land-kill, or once the land plan is dead. Never twice in one turn.
 Starstorm -> never cycle while the "Opponent battlefield" line names ANY creature. Release: "of
   which 0 are creatures" and no Forgotten Cave in hand -> cycle it; with a Cave in hand, cycle the
   Cave first.
SPARE MANA IS NOT A REASON: unspent mana costs you nothing, and a cycled Starstorm is a sweep you
no longer have. Cycle only where a rule above sends you.
"CHOOSE AN OPTION FOR <card>" COMPLETES THE CAST YOU JUST ANSWERED: "Cast Card Normally" is that
row; every other row abandons it, and "Decline - do nothing" is the way back.

=== TARGETING DAMAGE ===
Spellbomb, Spark Spray, Starstorm, Hammer and Siege-Gang's sacrifice list their choices; the face
row reads "The opponent (player, life N)". Top down:
 0. LETHAL FIRST. Read "Opponent life:" on every decision. If one row deals that number or more to
    a player - Hammer 3, Spellbomb 2, Spark Spray 1, each Goblin sacrificed 2 - take the face row
    NOW; nothing below applies. Kill counts count CREATURES only, so "{kills 0 of the 2 legal
    targets}" can still be lethal, and a repeatable cost counts once per payment you can afford:
    three Goblins at {1}{R} with 8 mana is 6 damage.
 1. An opponent PLANESWALKER, or a creature named on the DRAW PUNISHERS line -> aim there, ahead of
    casting any creature. Its row prints "[counters: Nx loyalty]": when N is at or below a spell's
    damage, that cast comes before any body.
 2. An opponent CREATURE whose printed CURRENT toughness the damage reaches -> aim there. Spark
    Spray kills 1 toughness, Spellbomb 2, Hammer 3. THE ROW'S VERDICT OUTRANKS THE VERB IN YOUR
    OWN PLAN: "- DIES" is the row you take, "- SURVIVES (toughness N)" the one you decline; if
    EVERY row says SURVIVES the answer one screen earlier was "Cast nothing right now".
    Exceptions: "SURVIVES (loyalty 3, 1 left)" is a planeswalker and IS a take, and after combat
    damage a row can flip.
 3. Only then the face, and only when "Opponent life:" is 6 or less; at 7 or more that row is not
    on your menu, and an empty opposing board is a reason to HOLD.
NEVER SPEND DAMAGE ON A SURVIVOR: chip damage on a body you cannot kill buys nothing and the card
is gone. Hold it for the next 2- or 3-toughness creature, or for a face row under step 0.
THE 6-LIFE FLOOR RELEASES ON TWO PRINTED NUMBERS - their line reading 0 creatures AND 2 or fewer
lands - and only once the log shows a land-kill of yours that resolved. Once released the face row
is the clock at any life total.
LIFEGAIN CROSS-CHECK: read the sign in "Opponent life trend: ... (+3 since turn 34)". A one-off
gain does not stop your burn; a gain that REPEATS at or above your damage per turn latches, and
every point then goes at creatures and planeswalkers unless step 0
fires. On a board that turns your life into their damage, treat every "paying this costs you N
life" bracket - the Talisman's own 1 among them - as lethal, not expensive.
HAMMER, SPELLBOMB AND SPARK SPRAY NEVER TOUCH A LAND.
A ROW TAGGED [your battlefield], or "Yourself (player, life N)", IS NEVER THE ANSWER; when it is
the only row, "CHOICE: 0 (pass)".

=== STARSTORM'S X ===
DECIDE WHETHER AT THE CAST ROW, NOT AT THE X MENU. That row prints the whole test: "kills THEIRS:
...; YOURS: ..." at each priced X, plus a marker like "[<- board sweep: THEIRS n / YOURS m]". RUN
DO NOT CAST ON THAT TEXT, THERE, and when it holds answer "Cast nothing right now" on that same
window. A marker saying the sweep takes more of YOURS than of THEIRS is a REFUSAL, not advice about
which X. Declining one screen later is the worst answer there is: the announcement has already
tapped your sources and every mana it paid is stranded for the turn.
DO NOT CAST when ANY holds: (a) the YOURS list at that X names Rorix or Siege-Gang, the only two
cards that end a game for you; (b) the THEIRS list names only "[defender]" creatures - carve-out,
if a GROUNDED attacker of yours is being held back by exactly those defenders, the sweep buys your
clock (never for Rorix, who flies over them). (c) paying it taps you out while a creature sits in
your hand - count the land drop, cast the body instead, and sweep on a later turn: one blocker
beats a sweep that kills one creature. Also do not cast on "even at X=N NOTHING on the board dies".
SURVIVAL OVERRIDE, which never lifts (c): add the printed power on their line; if that sum is at or
above "Your life:", take the longest THEIRS list whatever YOURS names - unless what it leaves alive
still totals your life or more, when it buys nothing and you keep the blocker.
ON THE X MENU: option 1 is the LARGEST X, never X=1, and any X your plan named is older than the
menu. A marked row ("[<- most kills ...]" / "[<- best trade: ...]") is the answer - a marker says
WHICH X, never WHETHER. Unmarked: take the largest "kills THEIRS" whose "YOURS:" reads "none", then
walk down any "same kills as X=N, for K less mana" run to its lowest. Never a row killing none.

=== THE LAND PLAN ===
Land destruction is TEMPO: worth a card while their battlefield shows FOUR OR FEWER lands and
nearly worthless after, so spend it EARLY and never hold for a better target.
WRITE THREE NUMBERS BEFORE EVERY SUCH CAST OR BLASTMINER ACTIVATION: "Their lands <N>; my life <L>;
their power <P>". N comes off their line's tail, collapsed handles counting in full ("Island #1-#4
x4" is four lands, one row); P adds the printed power of every creature on their line. The plan is
DEAD FOR THE GAME - and does not revive because the mana has nothing else to do - the moment ANY
holds: N is 5 or more; L is 8 or less; P is at or above L; "Opponent life:" is at or below the
damage you can point at a player this turn. Dead means "Cast nothing right now" and a
Cleanup discard: a board about to kill you is answered with removal or a blocker, never a land.
A PLAN LINE YOU WROTE LAST TURN NEVER REVIVES A DEAD RULE: when it names a land-kill these numbers
have killed, the numbers win.
WHICH LAND: prefer a nonbasic (Molten Rain deals 2 extra to its controller, and nonbasics fix their
colours); a tapped land is fine.

=== CARD FACTS THE PROMPT DOES NOT PRINT ===
DWARVEN BLASTMINER (1/1) is a machine, not a creature: "{2}{R}, Tap: destroy target NONBASIC land"
- basics are not legal targets, and he must be untapped. Activate only while THE LAND PLAN is
alive; with a body in "Your hand" the activation is OFF - that {2}{R} is the body's mana.
TALISMAN OF IMPULSE is ramp; its {1} mode is free, its coloured mode costs 1 life ("paying this
costs you 1 life"). At 3 life or less, and on any life-loop board, that row is "Cast nothing right
now".
SIEGE-GANG COMMANDER brings FOUR BODIES (a 2/2 plus three 1/1 Goblins) - board and blockers.
"{1}{R}, Sacrifice a Goblin: 2 damage" is REMOVAL while their board has creatures: take it when the
row reads "- DIES" or its "they would be at N" is 0 or less; at any other N the Goblin is a body.
IT BECOMES YOUR CLOCK when their line reads 0 creatures and "Mana available:" is 8 or more - each
{1}{R} is 2 to the face, payable three or four times in one turn, which beats every other damage
this deck has. A "[repeat: ... N times already]" tag is a count, not a refusal: keep paying while
the mana lasts.
RORIX BLADEWING is a 6/5 with FLYING and HASTE: cast him and attack that turn and every turn after.
HE IS LEGENDARY AND YOU RUN TWO - keep the second for the turn the first dies.
HAMMER OF BOGARDAN is a sorcery dealing 3; from the graveyard your upkeep offers "Put a card into
hand with Hammer of Bogardan [cost: {2}{R}{R}{R}]". Five to return plus three to recast is EIGHT
MANA for 3 damage - never let it push a Siege-Gang or a Stone Rain out of a turn, and with either
body in hand and the lands for it, pass on the return. THE EMPTY-HAND RULE wins games: when
"Your hand" reads "(0 cards): (none)" - or holds only a land spell the land plan has killed - and
"Mana available:" is 8 or more, TAKE THE RETURN EVERY UPKEEP and recast in main phase 1.
PYRITE SPELLBOMB: cast for {1} on a spare-mana turn, leave it loaded for their first
2-toughness creature; draw mode only when their line reads 0 creatures.
THEY COUNTER when "Their untapped sources:" reads 2 or more, "Opponent hand size:" is 1 or more, and
EITHER "Their graveyard" names Cancel, Essence Scatter, Dream Fracture or Fall of the Gavel OR the
log has ever read "was COUNTERED by" - the log is evidence a shuffle cannot erase. They answer the
FIRST spell of your turn: with mana for two, cast the CHEAP one FIRST as bait and the threat
SECOND. THE BAIT EXPIRES - once a spell of yours has resolved or been countered this turn the body
is your NEXT cast, and you hold a body for AT MOST ONE of your own turns. "Hold mana for their
turn" is not a play here - nothing but Starstorm is an instant.

=== COMBAT: ATTACKING ===
RACE CHECK first, and only when your attack list holds exactly ONE creature and it is Rorix or
Siege-Gang. Their turns to kill you = "Your life:" over the "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN" line's own "for
up to N" (that figure, never a power you add up); yours = "Opponent life:" over your attacker's
power. If THEIR number is smaller or equal, "ATTACK: none" - unless "for up to N" exceeds your life
PLUS that creature's toughness, when holding it home changes nothing: send it.
WALL CHECK, before the floor. A "[defender]" creature cannot attack but BLOCKS normally and is
named in the "their untapped blockers" tag. While those rows read "(blocking trigger: they may gain
2)", each attacker you declare hands them up to 2 life and your 1/1s and 2/2 kill nothing through a
0/4: write "Wall: <n> attackers x 2 = <2n> life to them; ATTACK: none", and kill Perimeter Captain
or Pride Guardian first.
ATTACK FLOOR, first case that applies:
 - Wall check fired -> "ATTACK: none".
 - Blastminer alone on the list -> he stays home while a nonbasic "[opponent's battlefield]" land
   sits on his target rows; otherwise read HIS OWN tag and SEND HIM unless a parenthesis says he
   dies ("(neither dies)" means he loses nothing).
 - A listed creature whose "their untapped blockers" tag prices it "(your attacker dies, their
   blocker lives)" -> that creature STAYS HOME: it is a body traded for nothing. Read this verdict
   FIRST, before the line below. For SIEGE-GANG and RORIX it is absolute at any life total.
 - Any other creature listed -> send EVERY creature listed except Blastminer. The list already
   dropped everything that cannot legally attack: do not re-check summoning sickness or tapped
   status, and never hold back because their creatures read "[tapped ...]".
 - Otherwise "ATTACK: none" under the crack-back exception.
BLOCKER TAGS: each parenthesis prices ONE blocker; GANG BLOCK measures your toughness against
the SUM.
 - A GANG BLOCK clause, or 2 or more blockers named -> that creature stays home. A GOBLIN token
   goes anyway; RORIX goes only when every blocker named is grounded; SIEGE-GANG never goes, its
   sacrifice ability needs it alive.
 - N is 1, no GANG BLOCK clause -> the parenthesis is the whole story, and "(your attacker dies,
   their blocker lives)" is a refusal.
CRACK-BACK EXCEPTION, outranking "send every creature listed": when "for up to N" is at or above
"Your life:" ("that would KILL you"), every creature you send is a blocker you lose - keep home
whatever blocks the biggest attackers; send only vigilance, or a Rorix whose 6 is lethal THIS
turn.

=== COMBAT: BLOCKING ===
Take any block where your creature kills theirs and lives, and a "(both die)" with a GOBLIN token
always. Do NOT take "(both die)" with SIEGE-GANG or RORIX - your only bodies; send a Goblin, or
take the damage while "you would be at N" is above 5. The one "(both die)" the Commander takes is
against a token-MAKER.
ONE BLOCKER STOPS THE WHOLE OF ONE ATTACKER'S DAMAGE; a second and third stop nothing more and die
with it. Read "you would be at N" first:
 - N above 5 and every row on that attacker reads "(your blocker dies, attacker lives)" -> "BLOCKS:
   none".
 - N at 5 or below -> ONE Goblin on the attacker with the largest "deals N", a second only on a
   DIFFERENT attacker; never two on one unless the row says "(both die)" for the pair.
WHEN THE HEADER SAYS LETHAL the arithmetic is the whole question, not the trade. An attacker tagged
"[NONE of your available blockers can block this ...]", or absent from your blocker's "may block"
line, is off the menu; among the rest block the LARGEST "deals N" even when your blocker dies.
A "[GANG BLOCK: ...]" header clause names the cheapest lethal group: use it when your life minus
that attacker's "deals N" is 5 or less, Goblins first.

=== MULLIGAN AND DISCARD ===
"counted by the engine: N lands, M spells" is the land count - never recount. "Playing every land
in this hand would cover the cost of: ..." is the mana check: if it names a card, this hand ACTS.
KEEP SIZE N is the number the mulligan row itself names ("a keep after this one would keep N
cards"); there may be no "(keeping N)" text anywhere, so use that row.
 - Two or more lands and at least one spell -> KEEP, unless the header reads 5 or more lands and
   exactly 1 spell: mulligan while N is 5 or more, keep at 4 or lower.
 - One land -> keep when the coverage line names a card; when it reads "would not cover any spell",
   mulligan while N is 5 or more and keep at 4 or lower.
 - Zero lands mulligans at any N; all lands and no spells mulligans while N is above 4.
BOTTOMING keeps in this ORDER: lands covering the cheapest spells you keep (three, two at 4 or
less); then one Starstorm; then your cheapest spells; then the rest by cost.
CLEANUP DISCARD ("PUT: n, m"): the priciest spell your lands cannot cast in two turns, then a
duplicate "(copy 2 of 3)", then a land beyond the third. Never the only Starstorm, never a castable
card while a duplicate is listed.

=== PLAN LINES AND THE HOLD ROW ===
A CARRIED PLAN NEVER OUTRANKS THIS WINDOW'S PRINTED VERDICT. "I said I would, so I will" is not a
reason to act: when the row your plan names is priced as losing a body ("your attacker dies, their
blocker lives"), as killing more of YOURS than of THEIRS, or as a rule here forbids, re-plan on the
numbers in front of you.
A PLAN line names the NEXT ACTION - a land to destroy, a creature to kill, a body to add. THERE IS
NO "THE GAME IS LOST": never write that no path exists, that you lack a win condition, or that you
pass to draw into one. When nothing else matches, the FLOOR is the plan: cast
Siege-Gang and sacrifice Goblins at their face while the mana lasts; with no Commander in reach the
floor is returning Hammer and pointing 3 at their face, and when "Opponent life trend" shows them
gaining 3 or more a turn that return is not a clock - spend the turn deploying instead.
Write that, and take the row that does it.
When the last menu row begins "Hold priority - pass now, and do not ask me again ...", take THAT
row at the FIRST such ask of the turn - "Cast nothing right now" answers one window, the hold row
all of them.

=== SITUATIONS ===
- Two non-defender creatures on their line and Starstorm in hand -> Starstorm, but only at an X
  that kills both and still leaves you the mana for a body; otherwise cast the body.
- Their line names ten or more creatures and your life is under half: the land plan is dead and the
  sweep is the only play.
