REVELATION FRACTURE - UW DRAW-GO CONTROL

=== THE DECK ===
You never have a creature and nothing here attacks. You kill them one damage at a
time with Staff of Nin; Elixir of Immortality shuffles your spent answers back into your library. 26 lands.

=== ORDER OF OPERATIONS ===
1. Play a land, every turn.
2. On THEIR turn: counter what is on the stack; otherwise take the hold row.
3. On YOUR turn CAST NOTHING BEFORE YOUR MAIN PHASE: upkeep and draw are for the Staff ping, an Elixir
   activation and a counter at the stack, nothing else - a sweeper, a Staff, a Lightmine Field and an
   Elixir CAST are main-phase-only, and mana spent earlier is mana that phase will not have. AT A
   PRE-MAIN WINDOW OFFERING NONE OF THOSE THREE, "Cast nothing right now" IS THE ANSWER, and no rule
   below lifts it.
   Then spend mana ABOVE the price of the cheapest counterspell in
   hand - a first Staff, an Elixir, a Path/sweeper/Lightmine Field the board calls for, a Revelation the
   three stops below allow.
4. End your turn with {1}{U} open for Essence Scatter, or {1}{U}{U} for Cancel or Dream Fracture.
Your PLAN line is what you intend in THIS window and the next, written fresh every window; the plan the
prompt quotes back is history, not an instruction. WHEN A ROW AND THE PLAN YOU WROTE DISAGREE,
THE ROW IS TRUE.

=== THE TAP-OUT GATE ===
Covers every mana you spend on your OWN turn: a Staff, an Elixir ACTIVATION, a Lightmine Field, a
Revelation. Not a counter cast at the stack, nor the Elixir CAST ({1} is always paid at once). Cast rows end
"{leaves N of your M untapped mana sources untapped}", "casting this taps you out" (N=0), or "{spends K of
your M ...}". THE PRICE is the cheapest COUNTERSPELL on your hand line: Essence Scatter 2, Cancel and Dream Fracture 3, Fall of the Gavel 5. Take the row only when what is
left is at least that; below it, "Cast nothing right now". A Revelation row prints no such clause: read its
"{X pricing: max affordable X=N (M mana total)}" and treat the cast as spending all M. THE GATE IS OPEN,
whatever N reads, in four shapes and only four:
 (a) a sweeper row whose "{right now: destroys/exiles K of their creatures}" reads K of 2 or more;
 (b) your life is 5 or less and the row gains life (Elixir activation, Revelation);
 (c) the cheapest counter in hand costs MORE than the row's own M;
 (d) the row is "Cast Staff of Nin" and NO Staff is on your battlefield line - the FIRST Staff is the
     only card in this deck that ends a game. A SECOND Staff is NOT this shape, and neither is a first
     one while a "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN" line reads "you would be at" 12 or less: answer THAT line
     first with the sweeper, Path or Revelation on the menu and cast the Staff a turn later.
With no counterspell in hand at all, the gate is open at any leaves-count. THE GATE ONLY EVER DECIDES
WHETHER YOU MAY AFFORD A ROW, NEVER WHETHER THE ROW DOES ANYTHING: a row whose own verdict reads zero - a
sweeper at "destroys 0", a Lightmine Field at "0 creatures able to attack" - is still not a play, with an
empty hand and an open gate. "Mana available:" means the number on that line, one exception: at "0 total (no
untapped sources) | Already in pool: ... (N mana ALREADY produced and floating)", what you can spend IN THIS
DECISION is N; mana kept open for a counterspell must be an UNTAPPED SOURCE, never a pool.

=== STAFF OF NIN - THE CLOCK AND THE WIN CONDITION ===
{6} artifact, two copies: "At the beginning of your upkeep, draw a card. -- {T}: Staff of Nin deals 1 damage
to any target." A "[second copy: ... no legend rule]" tag means both stay, each drawing and pinging. A SECOND STAFF DOUBLES THE CLOCK AND IT IS HOW A STABILISED GAME
ENDS: while fewer than two are on your battlefield line and one is in your hand, its cast row outranks a
Sphinx's Revelation for the same mana in the same window, and you never send it at cleanup.
READ "Your library: N cards" BEFORE EVERY PING. At 16 or less, with a "Life with Elixir of Immortality"
row anywhere on the same menu, TAKE THAT ROW and ping in the next window: the shuffle, not the 5 life,
stops you decking. Otherwise take
"Deal 1 Damage with Staff of Nin ... targeting a
player" in EVERY window offered, upkeep included, with EVERY Staff. THE TARGET IS ALWAYS THEIRS: a ping
row "targeting you" is NEVER your answer, at any life and under any paragraph on the screen.
- THE PING GOES AT THE FACE, two printed exceptions. While "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs
  - <names>" is printed and a ping row offers one of those names, PING THE PUNISHER with every Staff,
  every window, until its row stops printing "SURVIVES", then back to the face.
- A ping row reading "DIES" AND carrying "{removes N from the CRACK-BACK total": TAKE THAT ROW, at any
  life and whatever the body's text says - N off you EVERY turn beats 1 off them ONCE.
- Otherwise the ping leaves the face only for a body that 1 damage FINISHES (toughness 1, or 1 loyalty
  counter) and whose text reads "{T}:", "Whenever ... attacks" or "At the beginning of" - that body
  grows their board every turn and one ping ends it. Any other body: at the player.

=== EMRAKUL ===
Costs {15}. Until its own cast row is numbered it is blank.
- When the row IS printed and NO Staff of Nin is on your battlefield line, take it: the extra turn is
  real and it outranks the tap-out gate.
- WITH ANY STAFF OF YOURS ON THE BOARD, EMRAKUL IS NOT YOUR ANSWER, at any mana and any life, and neither
  HAVING NOTHING ELSE TO DO nor THEIR LIFE BEING LOW is an exception: a Staff already pinging IS the game
  ending and the cast taps you out of every counter. Same answer, no Staff needed, while
  "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER on the battlefield: theirs" is printed - that 15 comes straight back off you.
  Take the Staff ping instead, however many turns that reads.

=== THEIR CONVERTER, AND THEIR LOOP ===
A converter of THEIRS fires on life THEY GAIN, turning it into that much off you; life YOU gain is not in
it, so your Revelation, Elixir and Gavel are safe from it. A "LOOP" paragraph is the other
half and it reads the opposite way: life YOU LOSE chains too, without limit. WHILE A LOOP PARAGRAPH IS
PRINTED, PAY NO LIFE OF YOUR OWN FOR ANYTHING - not a self-ping, not a life cost on a cast row - at any
life, because the chain does not stop where that row's gloss stops. Life THEY lose
does not enter it: damage you point at THEM is always safe.

=== COUNTERSPELLS ===
13: 4 Essence Scatter (creature spells only), 3 Dream Fracture, 3 Cancel, 3 Fall of the Gavel (gains you 5).
Decide from the TYPE, MANA COST and (P/T) on the stack line:
- (creature P/T) printed POWER 2 or more: COUNTER IT.
- [defender] at any power, or printed power 0 or 1: LET IT RESOLVE -
  neither winning nor spare mana is a reason to counter one. UNLESS its "{target text:}" contains "{T}:",
  "Whenever ... attacks", "At the beginning of" or "venture", which act EVERY turn: then COUNTER IT
  whatever the P/T reads, [defender] included.
- (instant)/(sorcery) reading "Destroy target land": COUNTER at 5 of your lands or fewer, LET IT RESOLVE
  at 6 or more.
- (land), (artifact) with no P/T, or an (instant)/(sorcery) that only draws or searches: LET IT
  RESOLVE - above all a MANA ARTIFACT, whose {target text:} says "Add" and nothing else.
COUNTER ON SIGHT whatever the numbers: every planeswalker; every permanent that hits you or grows them EVERY
turn (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Sanguine Bond, Exquisite Blood, Howling Mine, Staff of Nin,
Liliana's Caress); Idyllic Tutor; and anything that makes
a player DRAW extra cards, whatever its type - that outranks the artifact line. Every counterspell you can
pay for has its own row, so COUNT THE ROWS: when exactly ONE is printed it is your LAST answer, spent only
on that list or on power 2 or more. WHICH ONE, when two or more rows can counter the same spell: the
CHEAPEST - one exception, and it outranks the price. Your printed life is 12 or less: take Fall of the
Gavel. An "INCOMING THIS COMBAT ... you would be at N" line tells you what this turn costs.

=== PATH TO EXILE ===
{W} instant, four copies: "Exile target creature." Your ONLY answer to a resolved creature. DECIDE AT THE
CAST ROW, NOT AT THE TARGET ASK - it prints "{removes: A, B, C}" and a "legal targets right now:" clause
giving each candidate's (P/T) and tags. In order:
1. "LIFE-TO-DAMAGE CONVERTER ... theirs" is printed and a candidate is [lifelink]: Path that one, at
   any power and any life.
2. A candidate whose own text reads "{T}:", "Whenever ... attacks", "At the beginning of" or "venture":
   Path it the first window offered, at any life total. "When this creature enters" is NOT that line.
3. Otherwise the highest printed power, [flying] outranking a ground body of equal power.
4. EVERY name on that clause [defender] or printed power 0 or 1: answer "Cast nothing right now" at THAT
   window, at any life above 12.
YOUR LIFE IS 12 OR LESS AND ANY CANDIDATE READS POWER 2 OR MORE: Path the biggest in that window.

=== SWEEPERS - THE HEADER COUNTS THE ATTACKERS ===
Supreme Verdict {1}{W}{W}{U} destroys; Final Judgment {4}{W}{W} EXILES - use it against recursion. The opponent header prints TWO numbers and only the SECOND
matters: "N are creatures, K of them without a restriction against attacking" (on the active player's board,
"K of them able to attack right now"). Walls are already off K and a [tapped] creature is still counted. A LAND OF THEIRS THAT ANIMATES IS IN NO COUNT ON THIS SCREEN, and no sweeper of
yours answers it. The "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN" paragraph names it and totals the damage; subtract that total
from your life before you spend a turn on anything else.
- K IS 0: Supreme Verdict and Final Judgment are NOT castable plays this turn, in either main phase;
  "Cast nothing right now" and keep them. This outranks being ahead or BEHIND, spare mana, a plan naming
  a wipe, the sweeper being your last card, and the phase. NO SURVIVAL CLAUSE IN THIS GUIDE REACHES K OF
  0, whatever your life reads.
- K of 2 or more: cast Supreme Verdict, or Final Judgment if anything of theirs recurs.
- K of 1, and that creature is [defender] or power 0-1 with none of the engine text the Path section names:
  cast NOTHING, no sweeper and no Path, at any life total.
- K of 1 with power 2 or more and Path in your list: take the Path, keep the sweeper.
- K of 1 with power 2 or more and no Path: cast the sweeper at power 4 or more; otherwise keep it unless
  your life is 10 or less and nothing else in your list answers it.

=== LIGHTMINE FIELD ===
{2}{W}{W}: "Whenever one or more creatures attack, Lightmine Field deals damage to each of those creatures
equal to the number of attacking creatures." Its row prints "{right now: they control N creatures able to attack - deals N to each}", so it kills every counted creature whose printed TOUGHNESS is N or less.
 - N of 0: "Cast nothing right now", always, a second copy included.
 - N of 1 or more with a counted creature at printed toughness N or less: CAST IT, subject only to the
   tap-out gate; every counted creature tougher: hold it, read it again next window.
SURVIVAL OUTRANKS THAT KILL TEST: when the printed power of their creatures able to attack totals at or
above your printed life, any row with N of 1 or more is a cast.

=== PUNISHER BOARDS ===
- "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" (Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler): every card
  you draw costs 1 life per such permanent and they stack. Revelation stop (1) covers it; take no
  other draw you are taking for the cards either, and take life from an Elixir, which draws none.
- "DISCARD PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs": Revelation stop (2) covers it.
- Forced Fruition {4}{U}{U}: every spell YOU cast draws you seven first (an Elixir activation and a land
  drop are not casts). Counter it on the stack; while it stands, cast nothing on your own turn beside a
  draw punisher, and nothing at all at "Your library:" of 14 or less.
- A COUNTERSPELL IS NOT AN OPTIONAL DRAW and no stop here reaches one: under Forced Fruition or a draw
  punisher, cast a counter off a "[DRAW PRICE: ... - you would be at K]" row reading K of 15 or more -
  and at any K above 1 when the spell countered has draw text of its own.

=== SPHINX'S REVELATION ===
{X}{W}{U}{U} instant: "You gain X life and draw X cards." X=0 does nothing. THREE PRINTED STOPS COME FIRST.
Answer "Cast nothing right now" while ANY is on the screen, at any mana, any X and any life:
 (1) a "DRAW PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" paragraph;
 (2) a "{... CLEANUP PRICE at X=N ...}" tail on the row;
 (3) their printed life is 10 or less AND a Staff of Nin is on YOUR battlefield line.
STOP (1) IS ARITHMETIC: with P such permanents printed, X cards drawn cost X times P life, so this gains X
and pays X times P - a NET LOSS at every X, and a badge naming "the largest X that leaves you alive" names
the smallest loss, never a gain.
THESE THREE ARE NOT HEURISTICS AND HAVING NOTHING ELSE TO DO IS NOT AN EXCEPTION: the answer stays "Cast
nothing right now" when the Revelation is the only row printed. THE LOW-LIFE
FLOOR LIFTS STOPS (2) AND (3), never (1), off two printed strings: your life is 6 OR LESS and no "DRAW
PUNISHERS on the battlefield: theirs" paragraph is printed. Then the row is your answer at ANY "max
affordable X" of 1 or more - ahead of the tap-out gate and the size floor - IN A MAIN PHASE OF YOUR OWN
TURN, NEVER AN UPKEEP OR DRAW STEP, and only while your life PLUS that X is ABOVE the "for up to N"
total on the printed "CRACK-BACK NEXT TURN" or "INCOMING THIS COMBAT" line. At or below that total the
cast buys nothing: answer "Cast nothing right now" and spend the mana in your main phase on the sweeper,
Path, Lightmine Field or first Staff step 3 names. With neither line printed the floor needs no test.
Same override, same test, when the printed power of their creatures able to
attack totals at or above your life. Then, in order:
- THE TAP-OUT GATE: take the row only when what it leaves covers the cheapest counter in your hand.
- THE SIZE FLOOR: cast only from a row whose "{X pricing: max affordable X=N ...}" reads 3 or more;
  X=1 and X=2 are both "Cast nothing right now" at any life above 10. Exception: your life is 10 or
  less and nothing else in your list is worth casting, then cast at the largest X offered. The
  "[<- best X for this cast: ...]" badge ranks X values and does not know this floor; the floor wins.
  Spare mana is never a reason, and a menu of only rows this guide already answers - a dead Lightmine
  Field, a sweeper at K=0, a Path at a [defender] - is an EMPTY menu.
THE X MENU. The ask prints "... X=K is the largest value on this menu the library pays for." THAT K HOLDS
BACK ONE DRAW ONLY - your draw step. EACH Staff of Nin on your battlefield line draws you one more card at
your next upkeep and that K does not count them, so announce K MINUS ONE PER STAFF, never larger; at 0 or
less take the "Decline" row. With no such sentence printed, use "library, minus 2, minus one per Staff". A
row's own "{library: ... - L-N left}" counts the library ALONE.
When option 1 carries a life bill naming a surviving X, announce that X - unless a DRAW PUNISHERS
paragraph is printed, and then take "Decline": no listed X is a gain. When it says no listed X leaves you
alive, answer "Cast nothing right now" and do not open the menu. Otherwise announce the largest X the
ceiling allows. A DECLINE HERE IS NOT FREE: the mana is paid and your sources stay
tapped, so the stops above are answered at the CAST row, before this menu opens.

=== ELIXIR OF IMMORTALITY ===
{1} artifact, two copies: "{2},{T}: You gain 5 life. Shuffle Elixir of Immortality and your graveyard into
library." CAST it the turn it is listed whenever you have a spare {1}. ACTIVATE it whenever your life is 15 or less OR "Your library: N cards" reads
16 or less - in THAT window, on a priority menu as much as on a casting menu, ahead of a Staff ping,
ahead of any spell and ahead of the hold row.
Above 15 life AND above 16 library, activate only
when the row leaves at or above the cheapest counter in hand, unless gate shape (a), (b) or (c) applies.

=== YOUR WINDOWS, AND THE HOLD ROW ===
A window with nothing under "ON THE STACK" has one right answer: decline it. TWO ROWS DECLINE AND THEY ARE
NOT THE SAME. "Cast nothing right now" closes THIS window only. "Hold priority - pass now, and do not ask me
again ..." closes every later window this turn whose rows have not moved - and a life total moving is not a
row moving, so a hold taken in your upkeep costs you your own main phase and their whole turn after
it. ON YOUR OWN TURN THE HOLD ROW IS NOT YOUR ANSWER: take "Cast nothing right now", every time,
whatever the menu and however dead the rows look. Only on THEIR turn is the hold row right. ONE OVERRIDE,
either turn: while the prompt carries "[you declined this exact list N times already this turn]", THAT ROW
IS THE ANSWER and "Cast nothing right now" is the wrong one - take it at the FIRST such window, and again
when the same rows come back. Only it stops the next identical ask.

=== LAND DROPS ===
When the prompt asks "Land drop:" and lists "Play <land>" options, TAKE ONE - every turn, at any life, with
any hand, however many lands you have, TAPPED OR NOT. "Play
no land right now" is never your answer while a land is listed. WHICH LAND: take an UNTAPPED row over a
TAPPED one; otherwise the colour you have fewest of.

=== CLEANUP DISCARD ===
Above seven the ask prints your hand as a NUMBERED list and says how many to send ("PUT: n, m"). Keep
counterspells you can pay for, and a Path. A STEP WHOSE CONDITION IS FALSE IS NOT A STEP: read
each step's clause against the board before you count it, and where the clause does not hold, skip that
step entirely and read on. Send in this order:
 1. Lightmine Field, unless the opponent header's K reads 3 or more.
 2. EVERY LAND on the list, while numbers still need sending, whenever your battlefield line reads "and N
    are lands" with N of 6 or more. Read N there; do not count your hand.
 3. A second Supreme Verdict or Final Judgment - and a LONE sweeper too when K reads 0 AND every creature
    they have cast is a wall.
 4. Emrakul, unless your battlefield line prints 13 or more lands.
 5. A Sphinx's Revelation beyond the first.
 6. A Staff of Nin, ONLY while TWO are already on your battlefield line.
 7. Only then a counterspell, MOST EXPENSIVE FIRST - Gavel before Cancel, Cancel before Scatter, reversed
    to Scatters first when every creature they have cast is a wall.
STEPS 1 TO 3 ARE A TIER, NOT A SEQUENCE - any order, and only when exhausted read on to 4-7. WHILE ANY LAND,
OR A LIGHTMINE FIELD STEP 1 WANTS, IS ON THE LIST, A COUNTERSPELL IS NOT YOUR ANSWER. THE ROW TAGS SORT THE
LIST: send "{dead right now:}" and "{spare:}" rows first, then untagged rows by the tier list, and a "{right
now:}" row LAST - and a LONE sweeper or Path is never sent for a "{dead right now:}" tag alone. HAND OVER SEVEN WITH A DISCARD PUNISHER PRINTED:
cleanup bills 2 life per card above seven - while your hand is above seven on
YOUR turn, cast one castable card per card you are over rather than "Cast nothing right now".

=== MULLIGAN ===
Read the engine's counts and its "Playing every land in this hand would cover the cost of: ..." line.
 - 2 to 5 lands and a coverage line naming a card: KEEP, at any price.
 - "would not cover any spell in it": ship a seven and a six; at five or smaller keep a land plus a spell
   castable in two draws.
 - ONE LAND: SHIP a seven and a six. At five or smaller keep it if it names a card.
 - Seven lands and no spell, or seven spells and no land: ship at any price.
BOTTOM IN THIS ORDER: Emrakul; then any "(copy 2 of 2 in your hand)" duplicate, a duplicate LAND included;
then a land beyond the fifth; then your most expensive spell; then the cheapest card that is neither a land
nor an answer. Keep the LANDS and at least ONE answer.
