| CS-011 | **Fixed — card script** | `@coinflipped` / Puppet's Verdict | The primitive was an indirect contraption: grant the TOP LIBRARY CARD a `@coinflipped` listener plus a do-nothing `flipacoin`, and compare flip-result globals. | CS-012 | **Confirmed — engine** | Targeted `@energizedof` payload | Reproducer built (`trigger_energized_targeted_counter.txt`): the trigger's StackAbility goes on the stack and RESOLVES WITHOUT ever opening/waiting on its target chooser — clicks before resolution are consumed as interrupt-declines, so the counter payload fizzles targetless. Not a suite artifact (the wait never happens, so GUI play cannot answer either), and not payload word order (leading `target(...)` form fails identically). ROOT CAUSE UNDERSTOOD: creature-ETB payloads (bare `auto=`, e.g. Flametongue Kavu) route through the SPELL flow, which acquires targets at cast; `@event` trigger payloads route through GenericTriggeredAbility::resolve, which has NO target-acquisition step — a one-shot payload resolves immediately with an empty chooser and fizzles. Systemic: every `@trigger:target(...)` payload in the corpus (Bomb Squad et al.) is likely equally dead. PROPOSED FIX (next session — touches all targeted triggers, needs full validation): when the payload carries an unfilled interactive action-TC (not a TriggerTargetChooser), wrap the clone in `MayAbility(must=true)` — the engine's own idiom for interactive triggered actions (the suspend cast-on-zero in Counters.cpp uses exactly this, and MayAbility::Update already gates on `validTargetsExist`). CS-005 (Circular Logic resolving before its payment menu) may be the same resolve-without-wait family. | Reproducer in `_potential_tests.txt`. |
